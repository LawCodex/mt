# Continental Shelf Act (Cap. 194)

**CHAPTER 194**

**CONTINENTAL SHELF ACT**

To make provision as to the exploration and exploitation of the continental shelf and for matters connected with those purposes.

29th July, 1966 ACT XXXV of 1966, as amended by Acts XIII of 1983 and I of 2002; and Legal Notice 410 of 2007.

Repealed by Act XXVIII of 2014.
