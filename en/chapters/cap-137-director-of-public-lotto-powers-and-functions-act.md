# Director of Public Lotto (Powers and Functions) Act (Cap. 137)

**CHAPTER 137**

**DIRECTOR OF PUBLIC LOTTO**

**(POWERS AND FUNCTIONS) ACT**

To remove certain doubts regarding the powers and functions of the Director of Public Lotto.

(4th April, 1953)\* Enacted by ACT IV of 1953.

## 1. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Director of Public Lotto (Powers and Functions) Act.

## 2. Declaration as to powers and functions of Director of Public Lotto. Cap. 122 (Repealed by Act XVI of 2018).

It is hereby declared that the powers and functions which by subsection (1) of section 2 of the Director of Public Lotto (Constitution of Office) Act were vested in the Director of Public Lotto comprise and, except for the purposes of criminal liability, shall be deemed to have at all times comprised all the powers and duties which, by or under the Public Lotto Ordinance and the Government Lotteries Act, 1948†, were conferred upon or assigned to the Collector of Imposts and Lotto.

*Cap. 70 (Repealed by Act XVI of 2018).*

\*See Government Notice No. 198 of 7th April, 1953. †Repealed by Act XXIV of 1981 (Chapter 292 of this Revised Edition).
