# Exportation of Certain Articles (Prohibition) Ordinance (Cap. 24)

**CHAPTER 24**

**EXPORTATION OF CERTAIN ARTICLES**

**(PROHIBITION) ORDINANCE**

To authorize the Minister responsible for the Police to prohibit in certain cases the exportation of certain articles.

(20th February, 1889)\* ORDINANCE IV of 1889 as amended by Legal Notice 46 of 1965 and Acts: LVIII of 1974, VIII of 1982, XIII of 1983 and Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

Repealed by Act II of 2015.

\*See Proclamation No. III of 1889.
