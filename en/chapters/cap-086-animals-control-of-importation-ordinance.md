# Animals (Control of Importation) Ordinance (Cap. 86)

**CHAPTER 86**

**ANIMALS (CONTROL OF IMPORTATION)**

**ORDINANCE\***

To make provision for the control of the importation of certain animals.

(6th February, 1935)† Enacted by ORDINANCE IV of 1935, as amended by Legal Notice 4 of 1963.

*Short title.*

l. This Ordinance may be cited as the Animals (Control of Importation) Ordinance.

## 2. Power of Minister to make regulations.

*Amended by: L.N. 4 of 1963.*

The Minister responsible for agriculture may, whenever he thinks it expedient so to do for the protection of animal husbandry in Malta, make and, when made, vary or repeal, regulations for prohibiting or otherwise restricting and controlling the importation of animals or of any specified kind of animals into Malta.

## 3. Offences and penalties.

*Amended by: L.N. 4 of 1963.*

If any person lands or attempts to land any animal in contravention of any regulation made by the Minister responsible for agriculture under the last preceding article, he shall be liable to the penalties imposed on persons importing or attempting to import goods the importation of which is prohibited by the Customs Ordinance and the animals in respect of which the offence is committed shall be forfeited.

*Cap.37.*

\*This Ordinance has been repealed by Act XV of 2025. †See Proclamation No. IV of the 6th of February, 1935.
