# Assignment of Statutory Ministerial Responsibilities Act (Cap. 561)

**CHAPTER 561**

**ASSIGNMENT OF STATUTORY MINISTERIAL**

**RESPONSIBILITIES ACT**

To provide for the assignment of statutory responsibilities to Ministers.

7th August, 2018

ACT XL of 2016, as amended by Legal Notice 265 of 2018, Act XXIX of 2019. and Legal Notice 503 of 2021. and Acts XL of 2023, XVII and XXXIII of 2024.

## 1. Short title.

(1) The short title of this Act is the Assignment of Statutory Ministerial Responsibilities Act.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:

"area of responsibility" means any business of the Government of Malta for which responsibility may be assigned to a Minister under article 82 of the Constitution, and includes the consolidated areas of responsibility that are listed in the Schedule;

"consolidated area of responsibility" means any of the areas of responsibility that are established under this Act and listed in the first column of the Schedule;

"Minister" means any Minister appointed in accordance with article 80 of the Constitution, and shall for the purposes of this Act, include the Prime Minister;

"related law" means any law which is listed in the second column of the Schedule against a consolidated area of responsibility;

"statutory title" means any title by means of which a Minister is identified, designated or assigned functions in a related law, and which is listed in the third column of the Schedule.

## 3. Assignment to Ministers of statutory responsibilities through the Schedule.

(1) When the President, acting in accordance with article 82 of the Constitution, assigns to a Minister an area of responsibility identical to a consolidated area of responsibility as listed in the first column of the Schedule, that Minister shall:

(a) be regarded as the Minister to whom each related law refers by means of the statutory title set out in that law and listed in the third column of the Schedule against the consolidated area of responsibility in question; and (b) assume the ministerial powers and duties assigned in that respect by the related law.

(2) Where an entry in the second column of the Schedule makes reference only to specific provisions or purposes of a related law, the ministerial powers and duties corresponding to that entry and assumed by the responsible Minister in terms of sub-article (1) shall be limited to those deriving from the specified provisions or purposes of the related law.

(3) Where -

(a) more than one law bears the same name, except in so far as the year of enactment may be concerned; and (b) one or more such laws make reference to the same statutory title,

the inclusion in the Schedule, with respect to that statutory title, of a reference to any one such law or to such laws in the plural shall be sufficient to enable the responsible Minister to assume, in terms of sub-article (1), the powers and duties assigned by all such laws with respect to that statutory title.

(4) Where -

(a) a Minister has, by virtue of sub-article (1), been assigned powers and duties pertaining to a statutory title that is set out in an Act and is listed in the Schedule; and

(b) a subsidiary law issued under that Act sets out or makes reference to the same statutory title,

the Minister shall assume any powers and duties assigned by the subsidiary law with respect to that statutory title, without the need for the subsidiary law to be listed in the Schedule.

(5) Where more than one subsidiary law issued under the same Act sets out a statutory title which is not mentioned in that Act, the inclusion in the Schedule of one such subsidiary law with respect to that statutory title shall be sufficient to enable the responsible Minister to assume, in terms of sub-article (1), the powers and duties assigned by all such subsidiary laws with respect to that statutory title.

## 4. Amendments to the Schedule.

(1) The Prime Minister may at any time, by Order in the Gazette, amend or substitute the Schedule to this Act.

(2) An Order amending or substituting the Schedule may apply retrospectively to any date which is so specified in the Order.

## 5. Publication of responsibilities assigned to Ministers.

(1) Whenever a Minister is assigned areas of responsibility in accordance with article 82 of the Constitution, the Prime Minister shall cause a notice listing those areas of responsibility to be published in the Gazette.

(2) A notice published in accordance with sub-article (1) may, in addition to areas of responsibility, list those departments, agencies, and other entities that have been placed under the Minister concerned; however the list of such departments, agencies and other entities shall be regarded as indicative rather than comprehensive.

## 6. Saving provision. Cap. 324.

Nothing in this Act shall impede the application of the Ministers (Delegation of Functions) Act.
