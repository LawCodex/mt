# Bishop of Gozo (Extension of Privileges) Ordinance (Cap. 15)

**CHAPTER 15**

BISHOP OF GOZO (EXTENSION OF PRIVILEGES) To make provision for removing certain doubts arisen in consequence of the separation of the islands of Gozo and Comino from the diocese of Malta and to make other provisions in connection therewith.

(30th June, 1865)\* ORDINANCE VIII of 1865, as amended by Legal Notice 46 of 1965; and Acts XXIV of 1975 and I of 1995.

Repealed by Act II of 2015.

\*See proclamation No. IV of 1865.
