# Judges and Magistrates (Salaries) Act (Cap. 175)

**CHAPTER 175**

**JUDGES AND MAGISTRATES (SALARIES) ACT**

There shall be paid to the holders of the offices specified in the first column of the Schedule, the annual salaries respectively specified opposite to each such office in the respective columns of the Schedule during the year to which each of the second to the third columns of the schedule refers or such greater amount as may be specified by an order of the Prime Minister published in the Gazette. The said salaries shall be payable as from the 1st day of January of each specified year.
