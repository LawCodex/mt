# President of Malta and other Officers (Salaries) Act (Cap. 186)

**CHAPTER 186**

**PRESIDENT OF MALTA AND OTHER OFFICERS**

**(SALARIES) ACT**

## 3. Salary of the holders of other offices.

(1) There shall be paid to the holder of the office specified in the first column of Schedule A the annual salary respectively specified in the respective columns of that Schedule during the year to which each of the second to the sixth columns of that Schedule refers. The said salaries shall be payable as from the 1st day of January of each specified year.

(2) There shall be paid in any year to the holders of the offices specified in the first column of Schedule B the salaries respectively specified opposite to such offices in the second column of that Schedule or such greater amount as may be specified by an order of the Prime Minister published in the Government Gazette.
