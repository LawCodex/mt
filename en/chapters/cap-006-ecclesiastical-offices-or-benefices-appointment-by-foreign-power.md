# Ecclesiastical Offices or Benefices (Appointment by Foreign Power) Ordinance (Cap. 6)

**CHAPTER 6**

**ECCLESIASTICAL OFFICES OR BENEFICES**

**(APPOINTMENT BY FOREIGN POWER)**

**ORDINANCE**

To make provision relating to the appointment to ecclesiastical offices or benefices by a foreign Power.

13th January, 1838\* ORDINANCE promulgated by PROCLAMATION No. I of 1838, as amended by Legal Notice 46 of 1965.

Repealed by Act II of 2015.

\*See Government Gazette No. 1415 of 17 January, 1838.
