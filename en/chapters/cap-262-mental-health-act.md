# Mental Health Act (Cap. 262)

**CHAPTER 262**

**MENTAL HEALTH ACT**

To provide for the treatment and care of mentally disordered persons and for purposes connected therewith or ancillary thereto.

14th September, 1981 ACT XVIII of 1976, as amended by Acts XIII of 1983 and VI of 2001; and Legal Notice 423 of 2007.

Repealed by Act XXII of 2012.
