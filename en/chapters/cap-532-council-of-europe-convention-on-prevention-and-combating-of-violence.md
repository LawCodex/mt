# Council of Europe Convention on Prevention and Combating of Violence against Women and Domestic Violence (Ratification) Act (Cap. 532)

**CHAPTER 532**

**COUNCIL OF EUROPE CONVENTION ON**

**PREVENTION AND COMBATING OF VIOLENCE**

**AGAINST WOMEN AND DOMESTIC VIOLENCE**

**(RATIFICATION) ACT**

To enable Malta to ratify the Council of Europe Convention on prevention and combating of violence against women and domestic violence.

17th June, 2014 ACT XIX of 2014.

Repealed by Act XIII of 2018.
