# Commissioner of Inland Revenue (Constitution of Office) Act (Cap. 121)

**CHAPTER 121**

**COMMISSIONER OF INLAND REVENUE**

(23rd August, 1948)\* Enacted by ACT XXX of 1948, as amended by Ordinance XXV of 1962; and Legal Notice 4 of 1963.
