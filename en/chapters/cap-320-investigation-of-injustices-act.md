# Investigation of Injustices Act (Cap. 320)

**CHAPTER 320**

**INVESTIGATION OF INJUSTICES ACT**

To make provision for the establishment and functions of one or more Commissions for the investigation of certain injustices, and for purposes connected therewith.

1st October, 1987 ACT XV of 1987, as amended by Act XVIII of 1989.

Repealed by Act XXI of 1995.
