# Aircraft (Application of Laws) Ordinance (Cap. 80)

**CHAPTER 80**

**AIRCRAFT (APPLICATION OF LAWS)**

**ORDINANCE\***

To apply to aircraft certain provisions of law.

(8th March, 1934)† ORDINANCE X of 1934, as amended by Legal Notice 4 of 1963; Act XI of 1973; Legal Notice 148 of 1975; and Act XXII of 1976.

## 1. Short title.

This Ordinance may be cited as the Aircraft (Application of Laws) Ordinance.

\*Repealed by Act VIII. 2010.61. †See Proclamation No. V of the 8th of March, 1934.
