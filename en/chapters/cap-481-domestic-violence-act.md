# Domestic Violence Act (Cap. 481)

**CHAPTER 481**

**DOMESTIC VIOLENCE ACT**

To make special provision for domestic violence and to make consequential and other amendments to the Criminal and Civil Codes.

28th February, 2006 ACT XX of 2005, as amended by Acts X of 2013 and VII of 2015.

Repealed by Act XIII of 2018.
