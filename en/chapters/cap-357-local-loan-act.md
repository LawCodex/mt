# Local Loan Act (Cap. 357)

**CHAPTER 357**

**LOCAL LOAN ACT**

To authorise a sum of thirty-six million Maltese liri to be raised in Malta by way of loan for the purpose of making good the deficit incurred by the Consolidated Fund to meet expenditure during the year 1991.

(12th May, 1992)\* Enacted by ACT II of 1992.

## 1. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Local Loan Act.

## 2. Power to raise loan.

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Government of Malta may raise in Malta by way of loan a sum of money not exceeding thirty-six million Maltese liri.

(2) For the purpose of raising the loan as aforesaid, the Minister responsible for finance is hereby authorised to issue stock in Malta under the provisions of the Local Loans (Registered Stock and Securities) Ordinance; and the loan made under the provisions of this article shall be made on such terms and conditions as the said Minister may approve.

*Cap. 161.*

## 3. Purpose of loan.

Any money borrowed under the authority of this Act shall be appropriated and applied for the purpose of making good the deficit incurred by the Consolidated Fund to meet expenditure during the year 1991.

\*See Government Notice No. 291 of 12th May, 1992.
