# Law of the Sea (Ratification) Act (Cap. 362)

**CHAPTER 362**

**LAW OF THE SEA (RATIFICATION) ACT**

To authorise the ratification of the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea.

(14th May, 1993)\* Enacted by ACT XII of 1993.

*Short title.*

l. This Act may be cited as the Law of the Sea (Ratification) Act, 1993.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Act the "Convention" means the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea signed at Montego Bay, Jamaica on 10th December, 1982.

## 3. Government authorised to ratify the Convention. Cap. 304.

For the purposes of the Ratification of Treaties Act, the ratification of the Convention by the Government is hereby authorised.

\*See Government Notice No. 320 of 14th May, 1993.
