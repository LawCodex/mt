# Encouragement of New Industries Act (Cap. 53)

**CHAPTER 53**

**ENCOURAGEMENT OF NEW INDUSTRIES ACT**

To repeal and re-enact with amendments the law relating to the encouragement of new industries.

(17th July, 1925)\* Act X of 1925, as amended by Acts: VI of 1947, VIII of 1950; Emergency Ordinances: XV of 1958, XXI of 1959; Ordinance XXV of 1962; Legal Notice 4 of 1963; Acts: X of 1963, XXVII of 1967; Legal Notice 148 of 1975; and Acts: XI of 1977, XIII of 1983, XXIV of 1995 and Legal Notice 408 of 2007.

Repealed by Act II of 2015.

\*See Government Notice No. 187 of the 17th of July. 1925.
