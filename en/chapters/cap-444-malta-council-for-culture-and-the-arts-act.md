# Malta Council for Culture and the Arts Act (Cap. 444)

**CHAPTER 444**

**MALTA COUNCIL FOR CULTURE AND THE ARTS ACT**

To provide for the establishment of a Council to be known as the Malta Council for Culture and the Arts to encourage and promote culture within a wide perspective of socio-economic activity, and to make provision with respect to matters ancillary thereto or connected therewith.

15th August, 2002 15th December, 2002 1st January, 2003 ACT V of 2002, as amended by Act XXXII of 2007; Legal Notice 426 of 2007; and Act XVIII of 2012.

Repealed by Act XV of 2015.
