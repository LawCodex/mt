# Employers Liability (Compulsory Insurance) Act (Cap. 241)

**CHAPTER 241**

**EMPLOYERS’ LIABILITY**

**(COMPULSORY INSURANCE) ACT**

To require employers to insure against their liability for personal injury to their employees and to provide for matters connected therewith.

\*

ACT XVI of 1974, as amended by Act XIII of 1983; Legal Notice 411 of 2007; and Act XXVI of 2014.

Repealed by Act II of 2015.

\*Never came into force.
