# Accountant General Ordinance (Cap. 160)

**CHAPTER 160**

**ACCOUNTANT GENERAL ORDINANCE**

To provide that certain rights and liabilities of the Treasurer shall be transferred to the Accountant General.

(31st July, 1959)\* Enacted by ORDINANCE VI of 1959.

## 1. Short title.

This Ordinance may be cited as the Accountant General Ordinance.

## 2. Transfer of rights and liabilities.

All rights and liabilities, whether vested or contingent, to which the Treasurer was entitled or subject immediately before the date of commencement of this Ordinance shall by virtue of this Ordinance be transferred to the Accountant General.

\*See Government Notice No. 451 of the 3lst July, 1959.
