# Animal Food and Feeding Stuffs Act (Cap. 183)

**CHAPTER 183**

**ANIMAL FOOD AND FEEDING STUFFS ACT**

To make provision with respect to the manufacture and sale of animal feeding stuffs and other matters incidental thereto.

\*

ACT IV of 1965, as amended by Acts XIII of 1985 and VIII of 1990; and Legal Notice 410 of 2007.

Repealed by Act II of 2015.

\*Never came into force.
