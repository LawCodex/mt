# Existing Laws (Reprint) Act (Cap. 180)

CHAPTER 180

EXISTING LAWS (REPRINT) ACT

31st May, 1963\*

ACT XXI of 1963, as amended by Legal Notice 46 of 1965; and Acts:

XXXI of 1966 and LVIII of 1974.
