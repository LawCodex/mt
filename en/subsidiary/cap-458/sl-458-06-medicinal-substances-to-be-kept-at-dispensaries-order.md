# Medicinal Substances to be kept at Dispensaries Order (S.L. 458.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.06**

**MEDICINAL SUBSTANCES TO BE KEPT**

**AT DISPENSARIES ORDER**

12th October, 1955 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 565 of 1955.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is List of Medicinal Substances to be kept at Dispensaries Order.

## 2. List of medicinal substances.

The list of medicinal substances which apothecaries are to keep in their dispensaries shall be that as is set out in the Schedule to this Order.
