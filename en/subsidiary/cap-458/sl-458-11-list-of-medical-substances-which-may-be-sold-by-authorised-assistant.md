# List of Medical Substances which may be sold by Authorised Assistant Apothecaries Order (S.L. 458.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.11**

LIST OF MEDICAL SUBSTANCES WHICH MAY BE SOLD BY AUTHORISED ASSISTANT

**APOTHECARIES ORDER**

20th September, 1968 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 664 of 1968.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is List of Medical Substances which may be Sold by Authorised Assistant Apothecaries Order.

## 2. List of medical substances.

The list of medical substances which may be sold by authorised assistant apothecaries is set out in the Schedule to this Order.
