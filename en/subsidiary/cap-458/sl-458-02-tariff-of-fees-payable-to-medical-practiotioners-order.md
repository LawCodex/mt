# Tariff of Fees payable to Medical Practiotioners Order (S.L. 458.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.02**

**TARIFF OF FEES PAYABLE TO MEDICAL**

**PRACTITIONERS ORDER**

13th December, 1938 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 486 of 1938, as amended by: Emergency Ordinance II of 1959; Legal Notice 1 of 1960; Act XIII of 1983; and Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Tariff of Fees payable to Medical Practitioners Order.

## 2. Tariff of fees.

The tariff of fees payable to medical practitioners shall be as set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## 42.

The rates as established by this tariff are binding only whenever there has been no previous agreement between practitioner and patient. In those cases in which minimum and maximum rates are established, if the amount asked by the practitioner be challenged, the fee shall be fixed by the Medical Council who will take in consideration all the circumstances of the case.

The fees for operations and other services not included in this tariff shall be assessed by the Medical Council according to circumstances, provided there has been no previous agreement.

The Medical Council may also assess fees due for medico-legal reports.
