# Pharmacy Licences (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 458.53)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.53**

**PHARMACY LICENCES (FEES) REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2008 LEGAL NOTICE 236 of 2008.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Pharmacy Licences (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

For the purposes of these regulations, the "Licensing Authority" and the "Medicines Authority" means the specific authority established under articles 3 and 4 respectively of the Medicines Act.

*Cap. 458.*

## 3. Fees.

(1) The licensee of each pharmacy shall by the end of any calendar year pay to the Licensing Authority, within thirty days of receipt of an invoice, a trading fee for the next following year which will be equivalent to the amount shown in the First Schedule to these regulations.

(2) Where a licence is to be issued for pharmacy trading to be carried out in Gozo, the relevant applicable fee shall be subjected to a reduction of 25%.

## 4. Transfer of a licence.

Where a licence is transferred by its holder to any other person or where, in relation to such licence, a change in premises occurs, the payable fee for the remaining part of the current calendar year shall be equivalent to the pro rata amount of fees shown in the Schedule.

## 5. Medicines Authority may verify information in hand.

In order to ascertain the applicable fee in relation to any pharmacy premises, the Medicines Authority may verify any such information with regard to the said premises as is provided by a licence holder or by an applicant for the issuing of a licence.
