# Register and Roll of Nurses (Special Parts) Regulations (S.L. 458.17)

SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.17

REGISTER AND ROLL OF NURSES

(SPECIAL PARTS) REGULATIONS

2nd November, 1984 LEGAL NOTICE 49 of 1984, as amended by Legal Notice 13 of 1994.

*\1.*

*Title.*

The title of these Regulations is Register and Roll of Nurses (Special Parts) Regulations.

*\2.*

*Special parts of the register of nurses. Amended by: L.N. 13 of 1994.*

For the register of nurses there shall be special parts which shall be designated as follows:

(a) register of nurses qualified in the nursing of patients suffering from mental illness. (registered nurses for the mentally sick);

(b) register of nurses qualified in the nursing of mentally handicapped patients. (registered nurses for the mentally handicapped);

(c) register of nurses qualified in the nursing of sick children. (registered nurses for sick children);

(d) register of nurses trained in adult nursing.

*\3.*

*Special parts of the roll of nurses.*

For the roll of nurses there shall be special parts which shall be designated as follows:

(a) roll of nurses qualified in the nursing of patients suffering from mental illness. (enrolled nurses for the mentally sick);

(b) roll of nurses qualified in the nursing of mentally handicapped patients. (enrolled nurses for the mentally handicapped);

(c) roll of nurses qualified in the nursing of sick children.

(enrolled nurses for sick children).
