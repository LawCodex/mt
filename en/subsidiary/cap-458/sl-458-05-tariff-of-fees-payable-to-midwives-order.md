# Tariff of Fees payable to Midwives Order (S.L. 458.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.05**

TARIFF OF FEES PAYABLE TO MIDWIVES ORDER 23rd July, 1954 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 315 of 1954, as amended by Emergency Ordinance II of 1959; Act XIII of 1983; and Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Tariff of Fees payable to Midwives Order.

## 2. Tariff of fees.

The tariff of fees payable to midwives shall be as set out in the Schedule to this Order.
