# Licensing Fees for Private Medical Premises Regulations (S.L. 458.26)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.26**

**LICENSING FEES FOR PRIVATE MEDICAL**

**PREMISES REGULATIONS**

1st January, 1998 LEGAL NOTICE 143 of 1998, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007 and Act XIII of 2015 and by Legal Notice 254 of 2025.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Licensing Fees for Private Medical Premises Regulations.

## 2. Levy of licence fee.

There shall be paid to the Superintendent of Public Health, in respect of the issue or renewal of a licence shown under Column 1 of the Schedule, the fee shown under Column 2 of the same Schedule.

## 3. Other licences, etc.

No licence issued under the provisions of these regulations shall be deemed to exempt from the requirements of any other licence, permit or authority imposed by any other regulation or other law.
