# Pharmacy Licence (Transfer) Regulations, 2014 (S.L. 458.58)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.58**

**PHARMACY LICENCE (TRANSFER)**

**REGULATIONS**

16th December, 2014 LEGAL NOTICE 463 of 2014.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Pharmacy Licence (Transfer) Regulations, 2014.

## 2. Interpretation.

For the purpose of these regulations -

*Cap. 458.*

"the Act" means the Medicines Act;

"Licensing Authority" means the Superintendent of Public Health in terms of article 3(1) of the Act;

*S.L 458.16*

"locality" shall have the same meaning as that assigned to it under the Pharmacy Licence Regulations;

"transfer of pharmacy licence" means the relocation of pharmacy premises from one place within a locality to another place within the same locality and which premises shall be named as the licensed premises from where such pharmacy may operate.

## 3. Application to transfer a pharmacy licence.

Any person who proves to the satisfaction of the Licensing Authority that a request for the transfer of a pharmacy licence to his or her premises was made and refused after the 6th July 1984 and before the 5th October 2007 and that such licence has been automatically revoked in such a manner and under such conditions under which the transfer would have been allowed had the Pharmacy Licensing Regulations as in force on the 1st July 2014 been in force at the time when the transfer was requested, shall be entitled to apply for the grant of a licence to the Licensing Authority within sixty days from the date of coming into force of these regulations.

## 4. Consideration of application and grant of licence.

(1) After receiving an application made in terms of regulation 3, the Licensing Authority may, after granting the applicant the right to make representations on such matters as it may seek to clarify, and after being satisfied that the said person fulfils the requirements of these regulations, proceed to approve such request for the grant of a pharmacy licence as from the date of the said approval and under such terms, conditions and limitations as it deems appropriate in the exercise of the powers conferred under article 71 of the Act.

(2) The Licensing Authority shall be entitled to authorise the issue of a pharmacy licence under these regulations notwithstanding that the licence which the applicant refers to has lapsed or had been cancelled for any reason other than as a result of the violation of any law.

(3) The issue of a pharmacy licence under these regulations may be made notwithstanding the provisions of regulations 4 and 6 of the Pharmacy License Regulations.

*S.L 458.16*

(4) The issue of a pharmacy licence under these regulations may be made subject to a condition that any claim of whatever nature arising out of or related to the original application for transfer or any claimed right to operate a pharmacy from any premises be withdrawn and renounced by the applicant unconditionally.
