# Pharmacies (Opening Hours) Rules (S.L. 458.28)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.28**

**PHARMACIES (OPENING HOURS) RULES**

9th November, 2010 LEGAL NOTICE 476 of 2010, as amended by Legal Notice 64 of 2022.

## 1. Title.

The title of these rules is the Pharmacies (Opening Hours) Rules.

## 2. Opening of pharmacies on weekdays.

(1) Any pharmacy in Malta and Gozo shall be open from at least 9.00 a.m. till 12.00 noon and from 4.00 p.m. till 7.00 p.m. from Monday to Saturday and may remain open for any period beyond these hours.

(2) On Saturday afternoon the managing pharmacist may not open his pharmacy as long as there is another pharmacy which is open in that locality as defined in the Second Schedule to the Local Government Act and according to a roster issued by the Licensing Authority:

*Cap. 363.*

Provided that in those localities where there is only one pharmacy, such pharmacy will be included in the roster of pharmacies for the neighbouring locality as the Licensing Authority may determine.

(3) It shall be the duty of the managing pharmacist who does not open his pharmacy on a Saturday afternoon to have clearly displayed on the door of his pharmacy the name and address of the pharmacy which is open in that locality, as defined in the Second Schedule to the Local Government Act, and according to the roster issued by the Licensing Authority:

*Cap. 363.*

## 3. Opening of pharmacies on Sundays and public holidays.

*Substituted by: L.N. 64 of 2022.*

It shall be the duty of the managing pharmacist to ensure that on Sundays and public holidays his pharmacy shall at least be open from 9.00 a.m. to 12.00 noon and from 4.00 p.m. to 7.00 p.m. strictly in accordance with the roster issued for this purpose by the Licensing Authority:

Provided that the provisions of this rule shall not apply for that pharmacy at the Malta International Airport.
