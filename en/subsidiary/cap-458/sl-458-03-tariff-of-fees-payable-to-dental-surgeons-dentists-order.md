# Tariff of Fees payable to Dental Surgeons/Dentists Order (S.L. 458.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.03**

**TARIFF OF FEES PAYABLE TO DENTAL**

**SURGEONS / DENTISTS ORDER**

9th October, 1951 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 491 of 1951, as amended by: Emergency Ordinance II of 1959; Act XIII of 1983; and Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Tariff of Fees payable to Dental Surgeons / Dentists Order.

## 2. Tariff of fees.

The tariff of fees payable to dental surgeons / dentists shall be as set out in the Schedule to this Order.
