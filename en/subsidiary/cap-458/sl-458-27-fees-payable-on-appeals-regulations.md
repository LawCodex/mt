# Fees payable on Appeals Regulations (S.L. 458.27)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.27**

**FEES PAYABLE ON APPEALS REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2001 LEGAL NOTICE 98 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007..

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees payable on Appeals Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable on appeals.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007. Cap. 464.*

The fees payable under article 36 of the Health Care Professions Act for an appeal from a decision of the Medical Council shall be one hundred and sixteen euro and forty-seven cents (116.47).
