# Qualified Person (Fees for Application) Regulations (S.L. 458.38)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.38**

**QUALIFIED PERSON (FEES FOR APPLICATION)**

**REGULATIONS**

4th May, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 260 of 2004, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Qualified Person (Fees for Application) Regulations.

## 2. Fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

There shall be paid to the Medicines Authority, in respect of an application for the interview of qualified persons, a fee of €116.47.
