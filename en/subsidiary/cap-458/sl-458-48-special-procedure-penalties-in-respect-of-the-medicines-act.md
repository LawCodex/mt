# Special Procedure (Penalties in respect of the Medicines Act) Regulations (S.L. 458.48)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.48**

**SPECIAL PROCEDURE (PENALTIES IN RESPECT**

**OF THE MEDICINES ACT) REGULATIONS**

31st October, 2006

LEGAL NOTICE 264 of 2006, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Special Procedure (Penalties in respect of the Medicines Act) Regulations.

## 2. Penalties.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007. Cap. 458.*

The Licensing Authority may demand payment of the following penalties for the breach of any of the following articles of the Medicines Act:

(a) articles 20, 24, 28, 37, 39, 41 and 43 ......... €23,293.73 (b) articles 54, 56, 58 and 61 .......................... €11,646.87 (c) articles 44, 45, 66(1), 71, 75(3), 75(4),

76(1), 81(1), 91 and 98 ............................. €9,317.49 (d) articles 59, 60, 65, 74(b), 75(1), 75(2), 84,

93, 94(1) and 96 ....................................... €6,988.12 (e) articles 31, 78, 85(1), 85(2) and 94(2) ....... €4,658.75 (f) articles 73(1) and 94(3) ............................ €2,329.37
