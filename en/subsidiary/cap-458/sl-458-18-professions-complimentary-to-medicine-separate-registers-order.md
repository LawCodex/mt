# Professions Complimentary to Medicine (Separate Registers) Order (S.L. 458.18)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.18**

PROFESSIONS COMPLIMENTARY TO MEDICINE

**(SEPARATE REGISTERS) ORDER**

30th August, 1985;

6th October, 1989;

14th July, 1992;

24th August, 2001 LEGAL NOTICES 26 of 1985, 121 of 1989, 64 of 1992 and 188 of 2001, consolidated.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Professions Complimentary to Medicine (Separate Registers) Order.

## 2. Separate registers to be kept.

Separate registers shall be kept by the Council for the Professions Complimentary to Medicine for the following professions:

acupuncturists;

clinical psychologists;

dental hygienists;

dental laboratory technicians;

medical laboratory analysts;

operating department assistants;

optometrists;

orthopists; and

perfusionists.
