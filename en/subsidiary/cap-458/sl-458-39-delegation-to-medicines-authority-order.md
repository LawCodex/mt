# Delegation to Medicines Authority Order (S.L. 458.39)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 458.39**

**DELEGATION TO MEDICINES AUTHORITY**

**ORDER**

11th June, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 334 of 2004.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation to Medicines Authority Order.

## 2. Delegation to Medicines Authority. Cap. 458.

The Licensing Authority has by this order delegated its functions under article 3(2)(m), (n) and (o) of the Medicines Act to the Medicines Authority.
