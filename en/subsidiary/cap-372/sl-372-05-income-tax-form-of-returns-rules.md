# Income Tax (Form of Returns) Rules (S.L. 372.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.05**

**INCOME TAX (FORM OF RETURNS) RULES**

25th November, 1994

LEGAL NOTICE 172 of 1994, as amended by Legal Notices 59 of 1997, 90 of 1998, 129 of 1999, 98 of 2000, 72 of 2001, 426 of 2004, 191 of 2005, 166 and 425 of 2007, and 296 of 2011.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is the Income Tax (Form of Returns) Rules.

## 2. Prescribed return.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2004; L.N. 296 of 2011. Cap. 372.*

(1) The returns to be made under article 10 of the Income Tax Management Act by or on behalf of an individual resident in Malta shall be made on the form contained in Schedule A.

(2) Any item on the form contained in Schedule A requiring an attachment to be filled in shall be made on such form as the Commissioner may require.

## 3. Sale of agricultural produce.

*Added by: L.N. 191 of 2005. Amended by: L.N. 296 of 2011. S.L. 123.80*

Any person who derives income from a qualifying sale as defined in the Sale of Agricultural Produce Rules, and to whom rule 2 applies in respect of such income, shall fill in and deliver such form as the Commissioner may require by the 30th June of the year following that in which the income was derived.

## 4. Incentives for job creation.

*Added by: L.N. 191 of 2005. Amended by: L.N. 296 of 2011. S.L. 325.06*

Any person who qualifies under the provisions of regulation 13 of the Business Promotion Regulations, and to whom rule 2 applies, shall fill in such form as the Commissioner may require and attach it to the income tax return for the relative year of assessment submitted to the Commissioner.
