# Removal of Secrecy (Details of Income) Rules (S.L. 372.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.10**

**REMOVAL OF SECRECY (DETAILS OF INCOME)**

**RULES**

12th May, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 60 of 1995, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is Removal of Secrecy (Details of Income) Rules.

## 2. Prescribed details. Cap. 372.

The details of income to which the provisions of article 4 of the Income Tax Management Act in regard to secrecy shall not apply in terms of subarticles (4) and (5) of the said article 4 shall be those contained in the First Schedule.

## 3. Prescribed form of statement and information. Cap. 372.

The statement to be submitted in accordance with paragraph (a) and any information to be given in accordance with paragraph (b) of article 4(5) of the Income Tax Management Act shall be in the form set out in the Second Schedule.

## 4. Applicability. Cap. 372.

Rules 2 and 3 shall apply to details of income contained in any return filed in accordance with article 10 of the Income Tax Management Act in respect of the year of assessment 1995 and subsequent years of assessment.
