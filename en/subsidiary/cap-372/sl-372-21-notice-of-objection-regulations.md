# Notice of Objection Regulations (S.L. 372.21)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.21**

**NOTICE OF OBJECTION REGULATIONS**

14th May, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 112 of 2002.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Notice of Objection Regulations.

## 2. Prescribed forms. Cap. 372.

The notice of objection referred to in article 33(2) of the Income Tax Management Act shall be made on the form prescribed in Schedule A in the case of an individual, and on the form prescribed in Schedule B in the case of a person other than an individual.
