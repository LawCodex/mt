# Income Tax (Further Return Form) Rules (S.L. 372.17)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.17**

INCOME TAX (FURTHER RETURN FORM) RULES 2nd November, 1999

LEGAL NOTICE 185 of 1999, as amended by Legal Notice 73 of 2001.

## 1. Title.

The title of these rules is the Income Tax (Further Return Form) Rules.

## 2. Interpretation.

For the purposes of these rules a Further Return Form shall be interpreted to mean an adjustment form in terms of the provisions of article 13 of the Income Tax Management Act.

*Cap. 372.*

## 3. Prescribed further return. Cap. 372.

The further returns to be made under article 13(3) and article 31(4) of the Income Tax Management Act -

(a) by or on behalf of an individual resident in Malta; or (b) by or on behalf of any body of persons other than an individual resident in Malta,

shall be made respectively on the Forms AF1 and AF2 contained in the First and Second Schedules.
