# Income Tax (Dividend Distribution to Shareholders) Rules (S.L. 372.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.03**

**INCOME TAX (DIVIDEND DISTRIBUTION TO**

**SHAREHOLDERS) RULES**

1st January, 1987

LEGAL NOTICE 69 of 1986, as amended by Legal Notice 25 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is Income Tax (Dividend Distribution to Shareholders) Rules.

## 2. Return. Cap. 372.

The return to be furnished by any person under article 24(1) of the Income Tax Management Act, in regard to dividends distributed by a company resident in Malta shall be according to Form I.T. 109A in the Schedule to these Rules.
