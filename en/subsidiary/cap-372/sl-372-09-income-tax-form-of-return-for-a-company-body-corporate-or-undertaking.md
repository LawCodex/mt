# Income Tax (Form of Return for a Company, Body Corporate or Undertaking) Rules (S.L. 372.09)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.09**

**INCOME TAX (FORM OF RETURN FOR A**

**COMPANY, BODY CORPORATE OR**

**UNDERTAKING) RULES**

24th March, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 36 of 1995, as amended by Legal Notices 29 of 1996, 60 of 1997, 91 of 1998, 97 of 2000, 89 and 227 of 2002, 425 and 462 of 2004, 190 of 2005, 425 of 2007, 105 of 2008, 297 of 2011 and 315 of 2012.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these rules is the Income Tax (Form of Return for a Company, Body Corporate or Undertaking) Rules.

## 2. Prescribed return.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2004; L.N. 297 of 2011. Cap. 372.*

(1) The return to be made under article 10 of the Income Tax Management Act by or on behalf of a company, a body corporate established by statute, or a trading or commercial undertaking operated by an entity (article 30) whether resident in Malta or not, shall be made on the form contained in Schedule A.

(2) Any item on the form contained in Schedule A requiring an attachment to be filled in shall be made on such form as the Commissioner may require.

## 3. Sale of agricultural produce.

*Added by: L.N. 190 of 2005. Amended by: L.N. 297 of 2011. S.L. 123.80*

Any company which derives income from a qualifying sale as defined in the Sale of Agricultural Produce Rules, and to which rule 2 applies in respect of such income, shall fill in and deliver such form as the Commissioner may require by the tax return date for the year of assessment in which the income was derived.
