# Income Tax Rules (S.L. 372.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.04**

**INCOME TAX RULES**

23rd September, 1994

LEGAL NOTICE 133 of 1994.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is the Income Tax Rules.

## 2. Rules under Income Tax Act to apply. Cap. 123. Cap. 372.

The rules issued under article 94 of the Income Tax Act as in force on the 22nd September, 1994, and any forms issued thereunder, shall, in so far as applicable, be applied and used for the purposes of and in connection with the Income Tax Management Act, as if the same were issued under article 57 of the Income Tax Management Act.

## 3. Limitation of scope. Cap. 372.

Where rules are made for any specified matter under article 57 of the Income Tax Management Act, rule 2 shall cease to apply to the matters governed by such rules.
