# Income Tax (Declaration of Secrecy) Rules (S.L. 372.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.01**

**INCOME TAX (DECLARATION OF SECRECY)**

**RULES**

18th January, 1949

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 32 of 1949, as amended by Legal Notices 127 of 1977 and 186 of 2021.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is Income Tax (Declaration of Secrecy) Rules.

## 2. Form of declaration. Cap. 372.

The declaration to be made and subscribed under article 4 of the Income Tax Management Act, by every person having any official duty or being employed in the administration of the said Act shall be in the form set out in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

*Substituted by: L.N. 127 of 1977. Amended by: L.N. 186 of 2021.*

(Rule 2)

**INCOME TAX MANAGEMENT ACT**

**Declaration in terms of article 4**

I .................................................. do solemnly and sincerely declare that I shall deal with and regard all information and all documents, returns and assessments, or copies thereof, relating to the Income Tax Act, as secret and confidential and that I shall not at any time communicate such information or the contents of such documents, returns and assessments or copies thereof to any person except as may be necessary for the purposes of the Income Tax Act, or as the Commissioner may otherwise direct.
