# Letting of Tenement (Forms) Rules (S.L. 372.27)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.27**

**LETTING OF TENEMENT (FORMS) RULES**

7th November, 2014

LEGAL NOTICE 393 of 2014, as amended by Legal Notices 148 of 2016 and 258 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

*Amended by: L.N. 148 of 2016.*

The title of these rules is the Letting of Tenement (Forms) Rules.

## 2. Form to accompany payment. Cap. 372.

*Substituted by: L.N. 258 of 2020.*

The payment to be made by a person in terms of article 42(4)(c) of the Income Tax Management At shall be accompanied by the form, duly filled in, that is published by the Commissioner for the purpose of these rules on the official website of the Commissioner for Revenue.

## 3. Prescribed form regarding payment made by an individual. Cap. 123.

The prescribed form indicating the gross rental income derived during the relevant period referred to in article 31D(6A) of the Income Tax Act which shall accompany a payment to be made by an individual, in terms of article 42(4)(c) of the Act, shall be the form in Schedule B.

## 4. Interpretation of "person". Cap. 123.

For the purposes of rule 2, the term "person" means a person who rents immovable property in terms of article 31D of the Income Tax Act and whose gross rental income from such property falls under the provisions of the said article.

## 5. Interpretation of "individual".

For the purposes of rule 3, the term "individual" means an individual who has not declared rental income derived from a tenement leased as a residence or garage during the relevant period in any return and exercises the option referred to in article 31D(6A) of the Income Tax Act.
