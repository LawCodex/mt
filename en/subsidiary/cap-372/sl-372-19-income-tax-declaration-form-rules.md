# Income Tax (Declaration Form) Rules (S.L. 372.19)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.19**

**INCOME TAX (DECLARATION FORM) RULES**

6th April, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 74 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notices 427 of 2004 and 425 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these rules is the Income Tax (Declaration Form) Rules.

## 2. Form to be used. Cap. 372.

The form to be made under article 12(4) of the Income Tax Management Act by individuals to whom the said article applies shall, for the year of assessment commencing on the 1st January, 2001 and for subsequent years of assessment, be according to Form DF in the Schedule.
