# Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts in connection with Appeals Regulations (S.L. 372.20)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 372.20**

**FEES PAYABLE ON THE FILING OF JUDICIAL**

**ACTS IN CONNECTION WITH APPEALS**

**REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 111 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts in connection with Appeals Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable on appeals.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007. Cap. 372.*

The fees payable under article 37(8) of the Income Tax Management Act on the filing of judicial acts in connection with appeals shall be thirty-four euro (34).
