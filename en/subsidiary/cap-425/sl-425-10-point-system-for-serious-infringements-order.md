# Point System for Serious Infringements Order (S.L. 425.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 425.10**

**POINT SYSTEM FOR**

**SERIOUS INFRINGEMENTS ORDER\***

22nd November, 2013

LEGAL NOTICE 406 of 2013.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Point System for Serious Infringements Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 425.*

"Act" means the Fisheries Conservation and Management Act;

"the Authority" means the Fisheries Control Directorate as established by the Act;

"IUU fishing" means fishing activities which are illegal, unreported or unregulated;

"illegal fishing" means fishing activities:

(a) conducted by national or foreign fishing vessels in the maritime waters under the jurisdiction of a state, without the permission of that state, or in contravention of its laws and regulations;

(b) conducted by fishing vessels flying the flag of states that are contracting parties to a relevant regional fisheries management organization, but which operate in contravention of the conservation and management measures adopted by that organization and by which those states are bound, or of relevant provisions of the applicable international law; or

(c) conducted by fishing vessels in violation of national laws or international obligations, including those undertaken by cooperating states to a relevant regional fisheries management organization;

"unreported fishing" means fishing activities:

(a) which have not been reported, or have been misreported, to the relevant national authority, in contravention of national laws and regulations; or

(b) which have been undertaken in the area or competence of a relevant regional fisheries management organization and have not been reported, or have been misreported, in contravention of the reporting procedures of that organization;

"unregulated fishing" means fishing activities:

(a) conducted in the area of application of a relevant regional fisheries management organization by fishing vessels without a nationality, by fishing vessels flying the flag of a state not party to that organization or by any other fishing entity, in a manner that is not consistent with or contravenes the conservation and management measures of that organization; or

\*Repealed by Legal Notice 364 of 2022.

(b) conducted in areas or for fish stocks in relation to which there are no applicable conservation or management measures by fishing vessels in a manner that is not consistent with state responsibilities for the conservation of living marine resources under international law;

"VMS data" or "vessel monitoring system data" means data on the fishing vessel identification, geographical position, date, time, course and speed transmitted by satellite-tracking devices installed on board fishing vessels to the fisheries monitoring center of its flag state.

## 3. Establishment of a penalty system.

(1) A penalty system shall apply for every person who is the holder of a fishing license issued pursuant to the Act for a Maltese fishing vessel and to the Master of such vessel so licensed.

(2) Any such license holder and Master who does not comply with any restriction or obligation relating to sea fishing contained in the Act or in any of the Conventions to which the Enforcement of Sea Fishing Conventions Order applies shall be guilty of an offence against the Act.

*S.L. 425.08*

(3) Any such license holder and Master having committed or having been held liable for an offence, that is shown to be serious in terms of the Act or the Convention from which it arises and which is listed as such in the Schedule to this Order, shall in addition to any other penalty to which he may be liable under any other law, be assigned points for the serious infringement.

## 4. Assignment of points.

(1) The number of points for serious infringements shall be assigned to the license holder and the Master of the vessel in accordance with the Schedule.

(2) When two or more serious infringements by the same natural or legal person holding the license or the Master of the vessel are detected in the course of one inspection, points shall be assigned in respect of each serious infringement concerned to the holder of the license and the Master of the vessel up to a maximum of twelve points.

(3) The holder of the fishing license and the Master of the vessel shall be informed that points have been assigned to them and of the number of points assigned.

(4) The points shall be assigned to the holder of the license and the Master of the vessel on the date stipulated in the decision of the Authority wherein the points are assigned.

(5) Where the Authority is properly notified by another Member State of a serious infringement committed by the holder of a Maltese fishing license issued pursuant to the Act or by the Master of a vessel so licensed, the Authority shall itself assign the appropriate penalty points to the holder of the fishing license, issued pursuant to the Act, and to the Master of such vessel so licensed.

## 5. Transfer of ownership.

Where the ownership of a fishing vessel is being transferred under any title whatsoever, the holder of the fishing license shall inform the prospective owner of the number of points which are still assigned to the fishing license by means of a certified copy obtained from the Authority.

## 6. Suspension and permanent withdrawal of a fishing license.

(1) When the total number of points assigned to the holder of a fishing license equals or exceeds eighteen points, the fishing license shall be automatically suspended for a period of two months.

(2) When the total number of points assigned to the Master of a Maltese fishing vessel licensed under the Act equals or exceeds eighteen points, the Master shall be automatically suspended from acting as Master on any fishing vessel for a period of two months.

(3) When the total number of points assigned to the holder of a fishing license equals or exceeds thirty-six points, the fishing license shall be automatically suspended for a period of four months.

(4) When the total number of points assigned to the Master of a Maltese fishing vessel licensed under the Act equals or exceeds thirty-six points, the Master shall be automatically suspended from acting as Master on any fishing vessel for a period of four months.

(5) When the total number of points assigned to the holder of a fishing license equals or exceeds fifty-four points, the fishing license shall be automatically suspended for a period of eight months.

(6) When the total number of points assigned to the Master of a Maltese fishing vessel licensed under the Act equals or exceeds fifty-four points, the Master shall be automatically suspended from acting as Master on any fishing vessel for a period of eight months.

(7) When the total number of points assigned to the holder of a fishing license equals or exceeds seventy-two points, the fishing license shall be automatically suspended for a period of one year.

(8) When the total number of points assigned to the Master of a Maltese fishing vessel licensed under the Act equals or exceeds seventy-two points, the Master shall be automatically suspended from acting as Master on any fishing vessel for a period of one year.

(9) When the total number of points assigned to the holder of a fishing license equals or exceeds ninety points, the fishing license shall be automatically permanently withdrawn.

(10) When the total number of points assigned to the Master of a Maltese fishing vessel licensed under the Act equals or exceeds ninety points, the Master shall be automatically permanently precluded from being a Master on any fishing vessel.

## 7. Effects of suspension and permanent withdrawal of fishing license.

(1) Where a fishing license is suspended or permanently withdrawn in terms of article 6, the Authority shall immediately inform the holder of the fishing license of this suspension or permanent withdrawal in writing.

(2) Where a Master is suspended or permanently precluded from being a Master on any fishing vessel in terms of article 6, the Authority shall immediately inform Master of this suspension or permanent preclusion in writing.

(3) Upon receiving the notification referred to in this article the holder of the fishing vessel and the Master of the vessel shall ensure that the fishing activity of the vessel concerned ceases immediately, and that it proceeds to the home port or to the port indicated by the Authority. During the voyage the fishing gear shall be properly lashed and stowed. The holder of the fishing license and the Master of the vessel shall ensure that any catch on board the fishing vessel is dealt with in accordance with the instructions of the Authority.

## 8. Deletion of fishing licenses from relevant lists.

(1) Where a fishing license is suspended or withdrawn permanently in accordance with this Order, the fishing vessel to which the suspended or permanently withdrawn fishing license relates shall be identified as being without a fishing license in the national register referred to in Article 15(1) of Regulation (EC) No 2371/2002, and shall also be identified in this way in the EU fishing fleet register referred to in Article 15(3) of Regulation (EC) No. 2371/2002.

(2) The Authority shall furthermore immediately update the list referred to in Article 116(1)(d) of Regulation (EC) No. 1224/2009 with an indication of all the points assigned, resulting suspensions and permanent withdrawals of fishing licenses, including the date on which they became applicable and their duration.

## 9. Illegal fishing during the suspension period or after the permanent withdrawal of a fishing license.

(1) Where a fishing vessel, the fishing license of which is suspended or has been permanently withdrawn in accordance with the provisions of this Order, carries out fishing activities during the suspension period or after the permanent withdrawal of the fishing license, such activities shall be deemed to constitute a serious infringement and the Authority shall immediately take the required enforcement measures stipulated in the Act to prevent the license holder and the Master of the relevant vessel from continuing to commit the serious infringement.

(2) Where a Master who is suspended or has been permanently precluded from acting as a Master of any fishing vessel, in accordance with the provisions of this Order, performs the duties of a Master of a fishing vessel during the suspension period or after the permanent preclusion, such activities will be deemed to constitute a serious infringement and the Authority shall immediately take the required enforcement measures stipulated in the Act to prevent the Master of the relevant vessel from continuing to commit the serious infringement.

(3) The fishing vessel referred to in this article may, where appropriate, be included in the EU IUU vessel list in accordance with Article 27 of Regulation (EC) No 1005/2008.

## 10. Deletion of points.

(1) Where the holder of the fishing license or the Master of the vessel does not commit, within a period of three years from the date of the last serious infringement, another serious infringement, all points assigned to the fishing license or to the Master, or both shall be deleted.

(2) Where a fishing license or Master, or both, have been suspended in accordance with article 6, the points on the basis of which the fishing license or Master or both were suspended shall not be deleted and any new points assigned to the holder of the fishing license or to the Master of the vessel shall be added to the existing points for the purpose of article 6.

(3) Where points have been deleted in terms of sub-article (1), the holder of the fishing license and the Master of the vessel shall be considered as if there had been no suspension, and nor had points been attached to the fishing licence or the Master.

(4) Where the total amount of points assigned to the holder of a fishing license or the Master of a vessel, or both, exceeds two, two points shall be deleted where:

(a) the fishing vessel which has been used in committing the infringement for which points were assigned uses thereafter VMS or records and transmits thereafter fishing logbook, transshipment and landing declaration data electronically without having a legal obligation to do so; or

(b) the holder of the fishing license or the Master, or both, of the vessel volunteers after the assignment of the points to participate in a scientific campaign for the improvement of the selectivity of the fishing gear; or (c) the holder of the fishing license is a member of a producer organization and the holder of the fishing license accepts a fishing plan adopted by the producer organization in the year following the assignment of points involving a reduction of 10% of the fishing opportunities for the holder of the fishing license; or (d) the holder of the fishing license joins a fishery covered by an eco-labeling scheme that is designed to certify and promote labels for products from well-managed marine capture fisheries and focus on issues related to the sustainable use of fisheries resources:

Provided that for each three-year period since the date of the last serious infringement, the holder of a fishing license and the Master of the vessel, or both, may avail themselves of one of the options under paragraphs (a), (b), (c) or (d), to reduce the number of points assigned only once, and provided that such reduction does not lead to the deletion of all points on the fishing license.

(5) Where penalty points are deleted in accordance with sub-article (4), the Authority shall inform the holder of the fishing licence and the Master of the vessel of that deletion and of the number of penalty points which still remain assigned against the said license or to the Master of the vessel.
