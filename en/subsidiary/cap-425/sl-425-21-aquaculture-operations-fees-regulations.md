# Aquaculture Operations Fees Regulations (S.L. 425.21)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 425.21**

AQUACULTURE OPERATIONS FEES REGULATIONS 4th August, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 211 of 2026.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Aquaculture Operations Fees Regulations.

*  S.L. 425.12.*

(2) The fees provided for in these regulations shall apply and be due in respect of services rendered by Government in terms of the Aquaculture Operation Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 425.*

"Act" means the Fisheries Conservation and Management Act;

*  S.L. 425.12.*

"applicant" means any natural or legal person who submits an application for a permit under the Aquaculture Operation Regulations;

"Director" means the Director General (Fisheries and Aquaculture) ex officio as head of the Fisheries and Aquaculture Department;

*   S.L. 425.12.*

"one-time administrative fee" means the fee that is payable only once for the processing of an application for a new permit in accordance with the Aquaculture Operation Regulations;

*   S.L. 425.12.*

"one-time approval fee" means the fee that is payable only once upon the approval by the Director of an application for a new permit in accordance with the Aquaculture Operation Regulations;

* S.L. 425.12.*

"permit holder" means the natural or legal person to whom a permit has been issued under the Aquaculture Operation Regulations and who is responsible for complying with all conditions, obligations and requirements attached to such permit.

*  S.L. 425.12.*

(2) Words and expressions used in these regulations which are also used in the Act and the Aquaculture Operations Regulations but which are not herein defined shall have the same meaning as assigned to them in the Act and, or in the Aquaculture Operations Regulations.

## 3. Fees due in relation to a new application for a permit. S.L. 425.12.

(1) An applicant for a permit in terms of articles 26 and 27 of the Act and in terms of regulation 4 of the Aquaculture Operations Regulations shall pay to the Director the fees specified in the First Schedule.

*    S.L. 425.12.*

(2) The one-time administrative fee and, where applicable, the one-time approval fee specified in the First Schedule shall be credited by the Director to the Aquaculture Fund established under the Aquaculture Operations Regulations.

## 4. Payment of annual fees and contributions for keeping the permit in force. S.L. 425. 12.

(1) Every permit holder in accordance with articles 26 and 27 of the Act and the Aquaculture Operations Regulations shall pay to the Director the annual contributions specified in the Second Schedule.

*   S.L. 425.12.*

(2) The contributions payable under this regulation shall be credited by the Director to the Aquaculture Fund established in accordance with regulation 2A of the Aquaculture Operations Regulations.

## 5. Default in payment.

The Director shall suspend any permit application, renewal or continuation process where any fee or annual contribution due in accordance with these regulations is not paid within the prescribed time.
