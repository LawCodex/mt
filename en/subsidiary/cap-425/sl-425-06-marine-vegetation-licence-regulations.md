# Marine Vegetation Licence Regulations (S.L. 425.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 425.06**

**MARINE VEGETATION LICENCE REGULATIONS**

23rd May, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 66 of 1997, as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007 and Act XIII of 2015.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Marine Vegetation Licence Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires -

*Cap. 425.*

"Act" means the Fisheries Conservation and Management Act;

"catching" includes cultivation;

"Fisheries Department" means the Department of Fisheries and Aquaculture;

"fishing" and "fishing boat" have the same meaning as is given to them in the Act;

"plants" and "plants found in the sea" include all types of marine vegetation.

## 3. Granting and renewal of licence.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007; XIII. 2015.157.*

A fee of one hundred and twenty-eight euro and twenty cents (€128.20) shall be paid shall be paid by a person engaged in fishing and in the cultivation of marine plants to the Fisheries Department for the granting of a licence, and for the renewal thereof each year thereafter, to use a fishing boat for the purpose of catching every description of plants found in the sea within such area of the bays or of the territorial waters of Malta as may be designated in the licence.

## 4. Prohibition and penalty.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

(1) No person shall cultivate or catch plants, or use a fishing boat for such purpose, unless he has been issued with, and is in possession of, a valid licence in terms of regulation 3.

(2) Any person who contravenes the provisions of these regulations shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (multa) of two hundred and thirty-two euro and ninety-four cents (€232.94).
