# Wholesale Fish Market Fees Regulations (S.L. 425.15)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 425.15**

WHOLESALE FISH MARKET FEES REGULATIONS 23rd April 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 178 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Wholesale Fish Market Fees Regulations.

## 2. Scope.

The fees and deductions provided for in these regulations shall apply and be payable in relation to services provided by the Government in accordance with the Fish Marketing Regulations at the wholesale fish market situated at Troubridge Street, Marsa.

*S.L. 425. 02.*

## 3. Applicable fees and deductions. S.L. 425. 02.

(1) The fees payable to Government for the services referred to under regulation 2 or under the Fish Marketing Regulations shall be those as found in Schedule I to these regulations.

(2) Deductions made by Government are to be made in accordance with Schedule I from the amounts paid by retailers according to the Fish Marketing Regulations, and any remaining balances shall be due by Government to the fisherman according to the relevant sale.
