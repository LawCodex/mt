# Agency Accreditation Regulations (S.L. 495.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 495.01**

**AGENCY ACCREDITATION REGULATIONS**

24th April, 2012

LEGAL NOTICE 125 of 2012,as amended by Act II of 2019.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Agency Accreditation Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 495.*

"accredited agency" has the same meaning as is assigned to it by the Adoption Administration Act;

*Cap. 495.*

"the Act" means the Adoption Administration Act;

"application" means the form set out in the Schedule;

"central authority" means the authority established by article 7 of the Act.

## 3. Provision of information indicated in the application.

Applicants shall provide the central authority with the information indicated in the application in the Schedule in relation to all requests made in terms of the Act.

## 4. Provision of assistance by the central authority.

The central authority shall make application forms readily available to the public at its premises and online on its website and shall assist applicants in filling in the application forms and in providing the required information as necessary.

## 5. Communications by the central authority.

The central authority shall make notifications and communicate decisions to applicants in writing or by email, as the case may be, and shall upon granting accreditation to an organisation, issue an Accreditation Certificate in accordance with article 12 of the Act.

## 6. Refusal of an application.

The central authority shall have the right to refuse an application for accreditation from an organisation according to article 13 of the Act:

Provided that the organisation shall have the right to appeal against such refusal as provided for in article 13(2) of the Act.

## 7. Revocation of accreditation.

The central authority shall have the right to revoke accreditation of an agency at any time in accordance with article 14 of the Act:

Provided that the agency shall have the right to appeal against such revocation as provided for in article 14 of the Act.

**GLOSSARY**

Private Organisation: For-profit or not-for-profit private (non-state) organisation

Public Organisation: An entity in which Government has a controlling stake and/or ownership

Parent Organisation: The organisation which owns and is legally responsible for the Adoption Agency

Responsible person of a Adoption The person who is legally responsible for Agency the Adoption Agency

Manager of a Adoption Agency The person who is authorised by the Responsible Person to manage, on a day to day basis, the Adoption Agency

Referees: The person managing the Adoption Agency (Manager) is to provide names and contact details of two referees.

Referees from the Parent Organisation or Adoption Agency, the Central Authority, Adoption Board, Board of Appeal and relatives/family members are excluded.

Referees should be able to attest to the professional integrity, competence and skill of the Manager, relevant to managing a Adoption agency.

Referees should know the Manager for at least two years.
