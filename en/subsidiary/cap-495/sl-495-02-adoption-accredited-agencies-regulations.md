# Adoption Accredited Agencies Regulations (S.L. 495.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 495.02**

**ADOPTION ACCREDITED AGENCIES**

**REGULATIONS**

27th November, 2015

LEGAL NOTICE 379 of 2015.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Adoption Accredited Agencies Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation. Cap. 495.

In these regulations, "the Act", means the Adoption Administration Act.

## 3. Records and documentation relating to adoption cases.

Without prejudice to the provisions of the Act, an agency shall deliver to the central authority all the records and documentation relating to the pre-adoption and adoption services that it has in its possession as follows:

(a) on the expiration of its period of accreditation if such agency fails to apply for renewal in terms of article 12(2) of the Act; and

(b) within the period of twenty working days from the day on which the decision of the central authority that the accreditation certificate should not be renewed has become res judicata.

## 4. Power to request documents.

The central authority may request any agency, including any agency which is no longer in possession of an accreditation certificate, to make available for inspection any document relating to pre-adoption and adoption services and the agency shall, by not later than ten working days from the date of request, make available any such document.

## 5. Continuance of adoption cases.

Notwithstanding the revocation, or expiry, of accreditation of an agency, the central authority may order such an agency to continue any adoption process commenced during its accreditation period where:

(a) the agency files a request for revocation in terms of article14(1)(a) of the Act; or

(b) the agency has not applied for the renewal of the accreditation certificate in terms of article 12(2) of the Act; or

(c) even though the agency has applied for the renewal of the accreditation certificate, a decision of the central authority on the renewal of the accreditation certificate is pending due to an ongoing investigation:

Provided that in each of the aforesaid cases the agency shall comply with the same conditions as specified by the central authority during the accreditation period, the conditions laid down in the Act and any other condition which the central authority deems adequate to specify:

Provided further that nothing in this regulation shall be construed as allowing the commencement of an adoption process.

## 6. Transfer of adoption cases.

An agency may, upon an authorisation in writing by the central authority, be permitted to transfer any adoption process to another accredited agency.
