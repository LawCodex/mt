# Designation of Central Authority in Adoptions Regulations (S.L. 495.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 495.03**

**DESIGNATION OF CENTRAL AUTHORITY**

**IN ADOPTION REGULATIONS**

19th June, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 191 of 2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is Designation of Central Authority in Adoptions Regulations.

## 2. Designation of Central Authority. Cap. 495.

The Social Care Standards Authority shall for all intents and purposes be designated as the Central Authority wherever it occurs in the Adoption Administration Act.
