LEGAL NOTICE 458 of 2020, as amended by Legal Notices 1 of 2021 and 59 of 2026.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Publication of Names of Citizens of Malta and Persons Deprived of Maltese Citizenship Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 188.*

"Act" means the Maltese Citizenship Act;

"applicant" means the person who applies for Maltese citizenship by means of naturalisation or registration, and includes dependent persons or persons who apply on behalf of another person;

"Minister" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act.

## 3. Publication of names of Maltese citizens by registration or naturalisation. 

*S.L.188.06. Amended by: L.N. 1 of 2020. Substituted by: L.N. 59 of 2026.*

The Minister shall publish every year in the Gazette the names of all the persons who within the previous twelve (12) calendar months have been granted Maltese citizenship by registration or naturalisation.

## 4. Publication of names of persons deprived of Maltese citizenship. 

*Amended by: L.N. 1 of 2020.*

The Minister shall as soon as practicable, ensure that the names of those persons deprived of Maltese citizenship is published by means of a notice in the Gazette.
