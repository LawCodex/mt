# Committee for Judges and Magistrates (Election of Members) Regulations (S.L. const.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION CONST.06**

**COMMITTEE FOR JUDGES AND MAGISTRATES**

**(ELECTION OF MEMBERS) REGULATIONS**

24th November, 2017

LEGAL NOTICE 329 of 2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Committee for Judges and Magistrates (Election of Members) Regulations.

## 2. Chief Justice to convene meetings of judges and magistrates.

Where the Commission for the Administration of Justice makes a call for the election of members of the Committee for Judges and Magistrates established by article 101B of the Constitution of Malta, the Chief Justice shall within the available time therein mentioned, convene the necessary meetings of the judges and the magistrates so that the necessary elections may separately take place.

## 3. Election by judges of the Superior Courts.

The election of two judges of the Superior Courts shall be conducted as follows:

(a) the Chief Justice and Judges who are members of the Commission for the Administration of Justice shall not be eligible to be candidates;

(b) the Chief Justice and each judge shall have one vote;

(c) the Chief Justice and each judge shall vote for one candidate;

(d) the two candidates obtaining the highest number of votes shall, provided that each of them shall have received the votes of at least one-third plus one of the valid votes cast, be declared elected members of the Committee. If only one of the said two candidates receives one-third plus one of valid votes cast only that candidate shall be declared elected;

(e) in the event that the share one-third plus one of valid votes cast is not achieved by the two candidates obtaining the highest number of votes or by any one of them the election in respect of any unfilled vacancy on the Committee for Judges and Magistrates shall be repeated amongst all unelected candidates and the requirement to obtain at least one-third plus one of valid votes cast shall not apply to the said election.

## 4. Where only two candidates are nominated.

Where at the start only two candidates are nominated, these shall deemed to have been elected members of the Committee for Judges and Magistrates.

## 5. Chief Justice shall preside over meeting etc.

The meeting and the election shall be presided by the Chief Justice and the counting of votes shall be carried out by him, assisted by one of the judges, during the meeting and in the presence of the judges.

## 6. Election of magistrates of the Inferior Courts.

The same rules aforesaid shall apply mutatis mutandis, for the election of two magistrates, so however that in that meeting the Chief Justice, who will preside and conduct the election, shall not be entitled to vote.
