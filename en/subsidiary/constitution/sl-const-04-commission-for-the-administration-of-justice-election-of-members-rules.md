# Commission for the Administration of Justice (Election of Members) Rules (S.L. const.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION CONST.04**

**COMMISSION FOR THE ADMINISTRATION OF**

**JUSTICE (ELECTION OF MEMBERS) RULES**

3rd May, 1994 LEGAL NOTICE 52 of 1994, as amended by Legal Notices 330 of 2017.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is Commission for the Administration of Justice (Election of Members) Rules.

## 2. Chief Justice to convene meetings of judges and magistrates.

Where the President of Malta makes a call for the elections as contemplated in paragraph (c) of sub-article (5) of article 101A of the Constitution of Malta, the Chief Justice shall within the available time therein mentioned, convene the necessary meetings of the judges and the magistrates, so that the necessary elections may, separately, take place.

## 3. Election by judges of the Superior Courts.

The election of the two judges of the superior courts shall be conducted as follows:

*Substituted by: L.N.330 of 2017.*

(a) the Chief Justice and each judge shall have one vote;

(b) the Chief Justice and each judge may vote for one candidate;

(c) the two candidates obtaining the highest number of votes shall, provided that each of them shall have received the votes of at least one-third plus one of the valid votes cast, be declared elected members of the Commission. If only one of the said two candidates receives one-third plus one of valid votes cast only that candidate shall be declared elected;

(d) in the event that the share one-third plus one of valid votes cast is not achieved by the two candidates obtaining the highest number of votes or by any one of them the election in respect of any unfilled vacancy on the Commission shall be repeated amongst all unelected candidates and the requirement to obtain at least one-third plus one of valid votes cast shall not apply to the said election.

## 4. Where only two candidates are nominated.

Where at the start only two candidates are nominated, these shall be deemed to have been elected members of the commission.

## 5. Chief Justice shall preside over meeting, etc.

The meeting and the election shall be presided by the Chief Justice and the counting of votes shall be carried out by him, assisted by one of the judges, during the meeting and in the presence of the judges.

## 6. Election by magistrates of inferior courts.

The same rules aforesaid shall apply, mutatis mutandis, for the election of the two magistrates, so however that, in that meeting, the Chief Justice, who will preside and conduct the election, will not be entitled to vote.
