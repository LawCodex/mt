# Boatmen s Fees Regulations (S.L. 73.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 73.01**

**BOATMEN’S FEES REGULATIONS**

16th September, 1957 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 572 of 1957, as amended by Legal Notice 408 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Boatmen’s Fees Regulations.

## 2. Agent to submit return to Comptroller of Customs.

The agent of any ship, the passengers whereof have embarked or disembarked by means other than dg]ajsas shall not later than twenty-four hours after the departure of the ship, furnish to the Comptroller of Customs a return showing the number of passengers so embarked or disembarked within each whole day during which the ship remained in harbour, with particulars in respect of each passenger as to whether he has embarked only or disembarked only or both.

## 3. Fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 408 of 2007.*

A fee of four cents (0.04) in respect of each passenger so embarking or disembarking and of eight cents (0.08) in respect of each passenger so embarking and disembarking or disembarking and embarking in any one day shall be charged and the total amount due shall be paid by the ship agent to the Comptroller of Customs at the time of his furnishing the return referred to in regulation 2, with a deduction of five per cent on the aforesaid amount, which the ship agent may retain as a remuneration for his services.

## 4. Comptroller of Customs to pay moneys accrued.

The Comptroller of Customs shall, every six months, pay to a representative of the boatmen licensed to ply their trade in the Grand Harbour and Marsamxett Harbour any moneys accrued to him from the payment of the fees referred to in regulation 3 and it shall be the responsibility of such representative to distribute the aforesaid moneys in equal proportions among the boatmen duly licensed as aforesaid during the relevant period.
