# Directive by the Prime Minister and Minister of Finance for the Issue of €39,599,347.78 5.1% Malta Government Stock 2014 (First Issue) (S.L. 469.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 469.04**

**DIRECTIVE BY THE PRIME MINISTER AND**

**MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF**

**€39,599,347.78 5.1% MALTA GOVERNMENT**

**STOCK 2014 (THIRD ISSUE)\***

30th July, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 374 of 2004, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Prime Minister and Minister of Finance for the Issue of €39,599,347.78 5.1% Malta Government Stock 2014 (First Issue).

## 2. Mode of issuing stock.

This Stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and/or entities are eligible to apply for this Stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

(a) In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when the Stock has been allotted, an application shall be made for the Stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

(b) On the 6th January, 2005 after the payment of the first dividend, this stock will be integrated with the existing issue 5.1% Malta Government Stock 2014 (III) and shall thereafter both be deemed to be one Stock.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the Stock shall be €39,599,347.78.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €2.55,0 for every €100 Stock will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 6th day of January and on the 6th day of July in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first pro-rata payment covering the period from the 14th day of August, 2004 to the 6th day of January, 2005 shall be made on the 6th day of January, 2005.

## 7. Redemption of Stock.

The Stock shall be redeemed at par on the 6th July, 2014.

\* This Directive has been revoked by Act XLV of 2016, but it is still partially applicable since the six-year period prescribed by law to claim funds referring to stocks that have already matured, has not yet expired.
