# Directive by the Prime Minister and Minister of Finance for the Issue of €46,587,467.97 5.1% Malta Government Stock 2022 (First Issue) (S.L. 469.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 469.03**

**DIRECTIVE BY THE PRIME MINISTER AND**

**MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF**

**€46,587.467.97 5.1% MALTA GOVERNMENT**

**STOCK 2022 (FIRST ISSUE)**

30th July, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 373 of 2004, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Prime Minister and Minister of Finance for the Issue of €46,587,467.97 5.1% Malta Government Stock 2022 (First Issue).

## 2. Mode of issuing stock.

This Stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and/or entities are eligible to apply for this Stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when the Stock has been allotted, an application shall be made for the Stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the Stock shall be €46,587,467.97.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €2.55,0 for every €100 Stock will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 16th day of February and on the 16th day of August in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first payment covering the period from the 17th day of August, 2004 to the 16th day of February, 2005 shall be made on the 16th day of February, 2005.

## 7. Redemption of Stock.

The Stock shall be redeemed at par on the 16th August, 2022.
