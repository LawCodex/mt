# Cyber Resilience Regulations (S.L. 591.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 591.06**

**CYBER RESILIENCE REGULATIONS**

11th September, 2026\* LEGAL NOTICE 238 of 2026.

## 1. Citation, scope and commencement.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Cyber Resilience Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 October 2024 on horizontal cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements and amending Regulations (EU) No 168/2013 and (EU) 2019/1020 and Directive (EU) 2020/1828 (Cyber Resilience Act).

(3) Regulations 3 to 7 and regulations 9 to 12 shall come into force on 11th December 2027.

*Administrative penalties.*

11.‡ (1) Part VIII of the Act shall apply to any breach under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and these regulations.

(2) Unless otherwise specified in these regulations, proceedings in relation to a breach of these regulations and of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 shall be instituted by the Authority and any administrative penalty imposed and, or decision taken by the Authority shall be subject to an appeal in accordance with Part IX of the Act.

(3) When deciding on the amount of the administrative penalty in each individual case, all relevant circumstances of the specific situation shall be taken into account and due regard shall be given to the following:

(a) the nature, gravity and duration of the infringement and of its consequences;

(b) whether administrative penalties have been already applied by the same or other market surveillance authorities to the same economic operator for a similar breach;

(c) the size, in particular with regard to microenterprises and small and medium sized-enterprises, including start-ups, and the market share of the economic operator committing the breach.

(4) At any stage of the proceedings in relation to a breach of these regulations and of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, the Authority may require any person or public authority who has violated any provision of these regulations or of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 to undertake in writing to refrain from such conduct and to take any remedial or other action as the Authority may specify.

(7) The initiation of proceedings by the Authority under these regulations shall be prescribed by the lapse of two (2) years from the date on which the breach is alleged to have been committed.
