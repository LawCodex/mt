# Micro and Small Business Representation Regulations (S.L. 512.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 512.02**

**MICRO AND SMALL BUSINESS**

**REPRESENTATION REGULATIONS**

4th January, 2013 LEGAL NOTICE 3 of 2013.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Micro and Small Business Representation Regulations.

## 2. Representation. Cap. 512.

For all intents and purposes relating to the Small Business Act, the right of representation of micro and small businesses as defined in Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC of the 6th May 2003, is hereby being vested in the entities representing employers in terms of article 2 of the Malta Council for Economic and Social Development Act defining the term ‘social partners’.

*Cap. 431.*
