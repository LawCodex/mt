# Designation of Central Authority in Foster Care Regulations (S.L. 491.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 491.01**

**DESIGNATION OF CENTRAL AUTHORITY**

**IN FOSTER CARE REGULATIONS**

19th June, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 192 of 2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is Designation of Central Authority in Foster Care Regulations.

## 2. Designation of Central Authority. Cap. 491.

The Social Care Standards Authority shall for all intents and purposes be designated as the Central Authority wherever it occurs in the Foster Care Act.
