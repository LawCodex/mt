## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Application of Part III of the Diplomatic Immunities and Privileges Act (International Organisation for Migration - IOM Malta) Order.

## 2. List of officers and servants.

The persons specified in the Schedule are officers and servants of the International Organisation for Migration in Malta.

## 3. Immunities and privileges. Cap. 191.

The officers and servants shall enjoy the immunities and privileges set out in Part III of the Second Schedule to the Diplomatic Immunities and Privileges Act during their term of office with the International Organisation for Migration in Malta.
