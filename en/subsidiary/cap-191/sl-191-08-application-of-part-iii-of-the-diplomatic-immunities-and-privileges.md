## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Application of Part III of the Diplomatic Immunities and Privileges Act (Global Centre for Maritime Sanctions Monitoring) Order.

## 2. Schedule of officers and employees.

The persons listed in the Schedule are officers and employees of the Global Centre for Maritime Sanctions Monitoring in Malta.

## 3. Privileges and immunities of officers and employees. Cap. 191.

The officers and employees shall enjoy the immunities and privileges established in Part III of the Second Schedule to the Diplomatic Immunities and Privileges Act during their term of office with the Global Centre for Maritime Sanctions Monitoring in Malta.
