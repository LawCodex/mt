## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Application of Part III of the Diplomatic Immunities and Privileges Act (Regional Marine Pollution Emergency Response Centre for the Mediterranean Sea - REMPEC) Order.

## 2.

The persons specified in the First Schedule are high officers of the Regional Marine Pollution Emergency Response Centre for the Mediterranean Sea (REMPEC) in Malta.

## 3. Immunities and privileges. Cap. 191.

The high officers shall enjoy the immunities and privileges set out in Part II and Part IV of the Second Schedule to the Diplomatic Immunities and Privileges Act during their term of office with the Regional Marine Pollution Emergency Response Centre for the Mediterranean Sea (REMPEC) in Malta.

## 4. Extension of immunities and privileges.

The wife or husband and children under the age of twenty-one of high officers specified in the First Schedule shall enjoy the immunities and privileges set out in Part IV of the Second Schedule to the Diplomatic Immunities and Privileges Act during the term of office of the high officer with the Regional Marine Pollution Emergency Response Centre for the Mediterranean Sea (REMPEC) in Malta.

*Cap. 191.*

## 5. Officers and servants.

The persons specified in the Second Schedule are officers and servants of the Regional Marine Pollution Emergency Response Centre for the Mediterranean Sea (REMPEC) in Malta.

## 6. Extension of immunities and privileges to officers and servants. Cap. 191.

The officers and servants shall enjoy the immunities and privileges set out in Part III of the Second Schedule to the Diplomatic Immunities and Privileges Act during their term of office with the Regional Marine Pollution Emergency Response Centre for the Mediterranean Sea (REMPEC) in Malta.
