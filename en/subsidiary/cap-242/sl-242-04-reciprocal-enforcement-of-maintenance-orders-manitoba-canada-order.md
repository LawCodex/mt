# Reciprocal Enforcement of Maintenance Orders - Manitoba (Canada) - Order (S.L. 242.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 242.04**

RECIPROCAL ENFORCEMENT OF MAINTENANCE ORDERS - MANITOBA (CANADA) - ORDER

20th November, 1984

LEGAL NOTICE 56 of 1984.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Reciprocal Enforcement of Maintenance Orders - Manitoba (Canada) - Order.

## 2. Reciprocal enforcement of maintenance orders. Cap. 242.

(1) The whole of the territory of Manitoba (Canada) is to be considered a reciprocating country for the purposes of the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act.

(2) The provisions of subarticle (1) shall be subject to the condition that, in the event of the benefits conferred by the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act being applied to maintenance orders made by the courts of Manitoba (Canada), similar benefits will in that country be applied to maintenance orders made by the courts of Malta.
