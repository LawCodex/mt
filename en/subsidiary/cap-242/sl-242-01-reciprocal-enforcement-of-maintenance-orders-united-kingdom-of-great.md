1st May, 1975

LEGAL NOTICE 49 of 1975.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Reciprocal Enforcement of Maintenance Orders - United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland - Order.

## 2. Reciprocal enforcement of maintenance orders. Cap. 242.

(1) The whole of the territory of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland is to be considered a reciprocating country for the purposes of the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act.

(2) The provisions of subarticle (1) shall be subject to the condition that, in the event of the benefits conferred by the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act being applied to maintenance orders made by the courts of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, similar benefits will in that country be applied to maintenance orders made by the courts of Malta.
