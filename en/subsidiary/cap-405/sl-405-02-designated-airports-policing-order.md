# Designated Airports (Policing) Order (S.L. 405.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 405.02**

**DESIGNATED AIRPORTS (POLICING) ORDER**

21st July, 1998

LEGAL NOTICE 213 of 2003.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Designated Airports (Policing) Order.

## 2. Designated airports requiring policing.

The Minister has designated the following airports as designated airports requiring policing for the purposes of Part V of the Airports and Civil Aviation (Security) Act:

(a) Malta International Airport, Gudja; and

(b) Xewkija Heliport, Gozo.
