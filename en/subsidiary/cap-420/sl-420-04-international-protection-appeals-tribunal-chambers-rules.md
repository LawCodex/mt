# International Protection Appeals Tribunal (Chambers) Rules (S.L. 420.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 420.04**

**INTERNATIONAL PROTECTION APPEALS**

**TRIBUNAL(CHAMBERS) RULES**

15th February, 2005

*Title.*

LEGAL NOTICE 47 of 2005, as amended by Legal Notice 46 of 2014. 1. The title of these rules is the International Protection Appeals Tribunal (Chambers) Rules.

## 2. Establishment of number of Chambers.

*Substituted by: L.N. 46 of 2014.*

The number of Chambers of the International Protection Appeals Tribunal shall be established by the Minister responsible for Immigration and one of the Chambers shall be known as the "First Chamber".

## 3. Assignment of cases between Chambers.

*Amended by: L.N. 46 of 2014.*

The assignment of cases between the Chambers of the International Protection Appeals Tribunal shall be made by the Secretary of the First Chamber by rotation in such manner that the Chambers shall, as far as possible, be assigned an equal number of cases.
