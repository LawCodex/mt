# Fees Payable for Lost or Destroyed Documents Regulations (S.L. 420.08)

SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 420.08

FEES PAYABLE FOR LOST OR DESTROYED

DOCUMENTS REGULATIONS

5th May, 2017

LEGAL NOTICE 134 of 2017.

*\1.*

*Title.*

The title of these regulations is the Fees Payable for Lost or Destroyed Documents Regulations.

*\2.*

*No fees.*

No charge whatsoever shall be imposed upon the first issuance of the following documents by the International Protection Agency:

(a) an application form;

(b) decision paper;

(c) interview transcript;

(d) asylum seekers’ document;

(e) other paper documents; or

(f) protection certificate (card).

*\3.*

*Fees.*

There shall be paid in respect of each item, and under the conditions outlined below, the following fees:

(a) Issue of an application form in replacement of the first application form which has been lost or destroyed ...............................................................................€5 (b) Issue of a decision paper in replacement of the first decision paper which has been lost or destroyed ................................................................................€5 (c) Issue of an interview transcript in replacement of the first interview transcript which has been lost or destroyed.................................................................€5 (d) Issue of an asylum seekers’ document in replacement of the first asylum seekers’ document which has been lost or destroyed .....................................................€5 (e) Issue of other paper documents in replacement of other first issued paper documents which have been lost or destroyed ............................................................... €5 per document

(f) Issue of a protection certificate (card) in replacement of the first protection certificate (card) which has been lost or destroyed .....................................................€5
