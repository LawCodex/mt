# Administrative Review Tribunal (Establishment of General Jurisdiction Section) Order (S.L. 490.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 490.03**

**ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW TRIBUNAL**

**(ESTABLISHMENT OF GENERAL**

**JURISDICTION SECTION) ORDER**

1st January, 2009

LEGAL NOTICE 349 of 2008.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Administrative Review Tribunal (Establishment of General Jurisdiction Section) Order.

## 2. Section of the Administrative Review Tribunal.

(1) There is hereby established a Section of the Administrative Review Tribunal to be known as the General Jurisdiction Section.

(2) All category of cases to be assigned to the Administrative Review Tribunal shall for the time being be assigned to the General Jurisdiction Section of the said Tribunal.
