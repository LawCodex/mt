# Filing of Acts before the Administrative Review Tribunal by Electronic Means Regulations (S.L. 490.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 490.05**

**FILING OF ACTS BEFORE THE**

**ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW TRIBUNAL**

**BY ELECTRONIC MEANS REGULATIONS**

1st October, 2015\*

LEGAL NOTICE 304 of 2015.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Filing of Acts before the Administrative Review Tribunal by Electronic Means Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 490.*

"Act" means the Administrative Justice Act;

"application" means the application made in terms of article 15(2) of the Act;

"by electronic means" shall mean online.

(2) Other words and expressions contained in these regulations shall have the same meaning assigned to them in the Act.

## 3. Running of the online system.

(1) There shall be an electronic mechanism by means of which applications referred to above may be filed online.

(2) Any person who wishes to file the said application through his legal representative, may file the said act online and pay the prescribed fee.

## 4. Responsibility of the Secretary and date when acts are deemed filed.

(1) The Secretary or persons delegated by him may receive the said application filed by electronic means.

(2) Applications filed by electronic means shall for all intents and purposes of law be deemed as if they have been filed directly in the Registry of the same Tribunal, provided that when the said applications are filed by electronic means outside registry opening hours as established by the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure, such acts shall be deemed to have been filed on the first following day of the opening of the said Registry.

*Cap. 12.*

\*see regulation 1(2) of these Regulations, as originally promulgated.
