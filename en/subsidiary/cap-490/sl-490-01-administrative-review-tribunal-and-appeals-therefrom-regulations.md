# Administrative Review Tribunal and Appeals therefrom Regulations (S.L. 490.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 490.01**

**ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW TRIBUNAL AND**

**APPEALS THEREFROM REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2009

LEGAL NOTICE 347 of 2008, as amended by Legal Notices 58 of 2015 and 508 of 2021.

## 1. Title.

*Substituted by:*

The title of these regulations is the Administrative Review Tribunal and Appeals therefrom Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 490.*

"the Act" means the Administrative Justice Act;

"Chairperson" means any Chairperson of the Administrative Review Tribunal appointed in terms of article 8(2) of the Act;

"Secretary" means the Secretary to the Tribunal;

"Tribunal" means the Administrative Review Tribunal.

(2) Other words and expressions contained in these regulations shall have the same meaning assigned to them in the Act.

## 3. Oath of office of a Chairperson.

(1) A Chairperson shall upon appointment take the following oath before the Court of Appeal:

"I .................................. (add name and surname) hereby declare that I will examine and decide the cases brought before me with impartiality, fairness and according to law. So help me God.".

(2) The said oath shall be signed by a Chairperson and shall also be dated. It shall be deposited with the Tribunal’s Secretary.

## 4. Oath of office of an Assistant.

(1) An Assistant shall upon appointment take the following oath before the Chairperson:

"I .................................. (add name and surname) hereby declare that I will assist the Chairperson of the Administrative Review Tribunal impartially, fairly and according to law. So help me God.".

(2) The said oath shall be signed by an Assistant and shall also be dated. It shall be deposited with the Tribunal’s Secretary.

## 5. Sittings of the Tribunal.

(1) The Tribunal shall hold sittings in Malta and in Gozo. (2) In Malta, the Tribunal shall hold its sittings in the building where the Courts of Justice ordinarily meet.

(3) In Gozo, the Tribunal shall hold its sittings in the building where the Court of Magistrates (Gozo) ordinarily meets.

## 6. Registry of the Tribunal.

(1) The Registry of the Civil Courts and Tribunals, Malta, shall be the Registry of the Tribunal in Malta.

(2) The Registry of the Civil Courts and Tribunals, Gozo, shall be the Registry of the Tribunal in Gozo.

## 7. Secretary of the Tribunal.

(1) When the Tribunal meets in Malta, the Registrar, Civil Courts and Tribunals, shall perform duties of Secretary of the Tribunal.

(2) When the Tribunal meets in Gozo, the Registrar, Civil Courts and Tribunals, Gozo shall perform the duties of Secretary of the Tribunal.

## 8. Application of

*S.L.12.21 to these regulations. Amended by: L.N. 508 of 2021.*

The provisions of regulations 5 to 11 of the Civil Procedure (Regulation of Registries, Archives and Functions of Director Courts (Gozo) and other Court Executive Officers) Regulations shall mutatis mutandis apply to the Tribunal and to the Secretary of the Tribunal.

## 9. Forms. Cap. 12.

The forms set out in Schedule B to the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure, where and to the extent applicable, shall mutatis mutandis, apply.

## 10. Tariffs.

The tariffs in the Schedule shall be levied by the Secretary to the Tribunal.
