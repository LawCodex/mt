# Assistants (Remuneration) Order (S.L. 490.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 490.02**

**ASSISTANTS (REMUNERATION) ORDER**

1st January, 2009

LEGAL NOTICE 348 of 2008, as amended by Legal Notice 269 of 2012 and 86 of 2019.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Assistants (Remuneration) Order.

## 2. Remuneration.

*Amended by: L.N. 269 of 2012; L.N. 86 of 2019,*

(1) An Assistant of the Administrative Review Tribunal shall be paid a remuneration of sixty euro per sitting.

(2) An Assistant of the Administrative Review Tribunal shall be paid a remuneration of two hundred euro for every report filed before the Tribunal.
