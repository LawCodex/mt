# Forms for the Valuation of Developed Land Order (S.L. 110.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 110.01**

DEVELOPED LAND VALUATION FORMS ORDER 23rd June, 1941 GOVERNMENT NOTICES 308 of 1941 and 34 of 1946, consolidated.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Developed Land Valuation Forms Order.

## 2. Forms. Cap. 110.

(1) For the purposes of article 5 of the Developed Land (Valuation) Ordinance, the form shall be as prescribed in the First Schedule to this Order.

(2) For the purposes of article 8 of the Developed Land (Valuation) Ordinance, the form shall be as prescribed in the Second Schedule to this Order.

(3) The said forms may be obtained from the Land Valuation Office.
