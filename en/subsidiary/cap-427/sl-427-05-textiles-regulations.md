# Textiles Regulations (S.L. 427.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.05**

**TEXTILES (IMPLEMENTATION)**

**REGULATIONS**

8th May, 2012\* LEGAL NOTICE 375 of 2012.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Textiles (Implementation) Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement the provisions of Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 September 2011 on textile fibre names and related labelling and marking of the fibre composition of textile products and repealing Council Directive 73/44/EEC and Directives 96/73/EC and 2008/121/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council.

*L.N. 246 of 2010.*

(3) Textile products which comply with the Textile Regulations, as promulgated by Legal Notice 246 of 2010, which are placed on the market before 8 May 2012 may continue to be made available on the market until 9 November 2014.

## 2. References to revoked regulations. L.N. 246 of 2010.

References to the Textiles Regulations, 2010, revoked by these regulations, shall be construed as references to Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011 on textile fibre names and related labelling and marking of the fibre composition of textile products and shall be read in accordance with the correlation tables in Annex X to the said Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011.

\*see regulation 1(3) of these Regulations, as originally promulgated.
