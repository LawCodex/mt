# Detergents (Implementation) Regulations (S.L. 427.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.06**

**DETERGENTS (IMPLEMENTATION)**

**REGULATIONS**

13th November, 2007 LEGAL NOTICE 363 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Detergents (Implementation) Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement the provisions of Regulation (EC) No. 648/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 31st March 2004 on detergents as amended by Regulation (EC) No. 907/2006 of 20 June 2006.

## 2.

The Malta Standards Authority shall be the competent authority within the meaning of Article 8(1) of Regulation (EC) No. 648/2004.

## 3.

The information specified in Article 11(2), (3), (4) and (5) of Regulation (EC) No. 648/2004 must appear in at least one of the following languages:

Maltese,

English,

Italian.
