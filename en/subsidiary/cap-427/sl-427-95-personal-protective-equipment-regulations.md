# Personal Protective Equipment Regulations (S.L. 427.95)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 345.18**

**PERSONAL PROTECTIVE**

**EQUIPMENT REGULATIONS**

21st April, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 186 of 2018.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Personal Protective Equipment Regulations.

## 2. Implementation provision.

(1) These regulations implement the requirements set by Regulation (EU) 2016/425 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2016 on personal protective equipment and repealing Council Directive 89/686/EEC.

(2) The procedures for the assessment and notification of conformity assessment bodies, and the monitoring of notified bodies shall be those prescribed in the Method for Designating Conformity Assessment Bodies Regulations.

*S.L. 427.38*

(3) With respect to Articles 8(6), 8(7), 8(10), 10(3), 10(4), 10(9), 11(2), 15(2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/426, the language used shall be Maltese or English.

## 3. Designation of notifying authority.

The Technical Regulations Division within the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority is the notifying authority within the meaning of Article 21 of Regulation (EU) 2016/425.

## 4. Penalties. Cap. 427.

The penalties applicable to any infringement related to Regulation (EU) 2016/425 shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.
