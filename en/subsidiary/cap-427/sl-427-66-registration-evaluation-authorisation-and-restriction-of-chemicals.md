# Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (Reach) (Implementation) Regulations (S.L. 427.66)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.66**

**REGISTRATION, EVALUATION,**

**AUTHORISATION AND RESTRICTION OF**

**CHEMICALS (REACH) (IMPLEMENTATION)**

**REGULATIONS**

12th February, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 61 of 2008, as amended by Legal Notice 186 of 2009.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH) (Implementation) Regulations.

## 2. Implementation.

These regulations implement the provisions of Articles 121, 125 and 126 of Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2006 concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH), establishing a European Chemicals Agency, amending Directive 1999/45/EC and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 793/93 and Commission Regulation (EC) No 1488/94 as well as Council Directive 76/769/EEC and Commission Directives 91/55/EEC, 93/67/EEC, 93/105/EC and 2000/21/EC.

## 3. Establishment of the competent authority.

The Malta Standards Authority is the competent authority within the meaning of Article 121 of Regulation (EC) No. 1907/ 2006.

## 4. Establishment of the competent authority.

The Malta Standards Authority shall be the authority responsible for the provisions prescribed in Regulation (EC) No. 1907/2006: Title XIV, Enforcement.

## 5. Penalties.

The penalties applicable for infringement of the provisions of Regulation (EC) No.1907/2006 shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.

## 6. Language requirements for Safety Data Sheets.

*Added by: L.N. 186 of 2009.*

With reference to Article 31(5) of Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 the safety data sheet shall be supplied in at least one of the following languages:

Maltese, English, Italian.
