# Construction Products (Implementation) Regulations (S.L. 427.83)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.83**

**CONSTRUCTION PRODUCTS**

**(IMPLEMENTATION) REGULATIONS**

25th November, 2011 LEGAL NOTICE 462 of 2011.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Construction Products (Implementation) Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement the provisions of Regulation (EU) No. 305/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2011 laying down harmonised conditions for the marketing of construction products and repealing Council Directive 89/106/EEC.

## 2. Definitions.

For the purposes of these regulations, the following definition shall apply:

the "Technical Regulations Division" means the Technical Regulations Division within the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority as established by the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act.

*Cap. 510.*

## 3. Establishment of the Product Contact Point for Construction. S.L. 419.08

The designated Product Contact Point for Construction within the meaning of Article 10 of Regulation (EU) No. 305/2011 shall be the same Product Contact Point designated by the Product Contact Point (Designation) Regulations.

## 4. Establishment of the competent authority.

The competent national authority within the meaning of Articles 11(7), 11(8), 12(2), 13(7), 13(9), 14(4) and 14(5) of Regulation (EU) No. 305/2011 shall be the Technical Regulations Division.

## 5. Designation of conformity assessment bodies.

(1) For the purposes of designation and notification under Chapters V and VII of Regulation (EU) No. 305/2011, conformity assessment bodies shall, in addition to the requirements set out in Regulation (EU) No. 305/2011, meet the requirements laid down in the Method for Designating Conformity Assessment Bodies Regulations.

*S.L. 427.45*

(2) The designating authority within the meaning of Chapter V and the notifying authority within the meaning of Chapter VII of Regulation (EU) No. 305/2011 shall be the Technical Regulations Division.

## 6. Establishment of the market surveillance authority.

The market surveillance authority within the meaning of Regulation (EU) No. 305/2011 shall be the Technical Regulations Division.

## 7. Languages provisions.

The information and documentation specified in Articles 7(4), 11(6), 11(8), 13(4), 13(9), 14(2) and 14(5) of Regulation (EU) No. 305/2011 shall be supplied in at least the Maltese or the English language.

## 8. Penalties.

The penalties and proceedings applicable for the infringement of any of the provisions of Chapter III of Regulation (EU) No. 305/2011 shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.

*Cap. 427.*
