# EU Fertilising Products (Implementing) Regulations (S.L. 427.104)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.104**

**EU FERTILISING PRODUCTS**

**(IMPLEMENTING) REGULATIONS**

16th April 2020\* 16th July 2022 † LEGAL NOTICE 389 of 2021.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the EU Fertilising Products (Implementing) Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2019/1009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 June 2019 laying down rules on the making available on the market of EU fertilising products and amending Regulations (EC) No 1069/2009 and (EC) No 1107/2009 and repealing Regulation (EC) No 2003/2003.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Regulation (EU) 2019/1009" means Regulation (EU) 2019/1009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 June 2019 laying down rules on the making available on the market of EU fertilising products and amending Regulations (EC) No 1069/2009 and (EC) No 1107/2009 and repealing Regulation (EC) No 2003/2003.

## 3. Designation of notifying authority. Cap. 510.

For the purpose of Regulation (EU) 2019/1009, the Technical Regulations Division established by article 19 of the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act shall be the notifying authority responsible for setting up and carrying out the necessary procedures for the assessment and notification of conformity assessment bodies and the monitoring of notified bodies, including compliance with Article 26 Regulation (EU) 2019/1009.

## 4. Language requirements.

Any information which shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2019/1009 shall be in at least the Maltese or English language:

Provided that any information which shall be provided in accordance with Articles 6(6), 6(7), 8(3), 8(4) and 9(2) shall be in at least the Maltese, English, or Italian language.

## 5. Method for designating conformity assessment bodies. S.L. 427. 45.

The procedures for the assessment, notification and monitoring of conformity assessment bodies shall be those prescribed in the Method for Designating Conformity Assessment Bodies Regulations.
