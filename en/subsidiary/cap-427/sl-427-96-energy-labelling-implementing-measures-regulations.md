# Energy Labelling (Implementing Measures) Regulations (S.L. 427.96)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.96**

**ENERGY LABELLING (IMPLEMENTING**

**MEASURES) REGULATIONS**

18th September, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 295 of 2018

## 1. Citation and implementation.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Energy Labelling (Implementing Measures) Regulations

(2) These regulations implement the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2017/1369 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2017 setting a framework for energy labelling and repealing Directive 2010/30/EU.

## 2. Definitions.

For the purpose of these regulations, the following definition shall apply:

the "Technical Regulations Division" means the Technical Regulations Division within the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority established under article 19 of the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act.

*Cap. 510.*

## 3. Establishment of the competent authority.

The Technical Regulations Division shall be the competent authority for the purposes of Regulation (EU) No. 2017/ 1369.

## 4. Penalties.

The penalties and proceedings applicable for infringement of any of the provisions of Regulation (EU) No. 2017/ 1369 shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.

*Cap. 427.*
