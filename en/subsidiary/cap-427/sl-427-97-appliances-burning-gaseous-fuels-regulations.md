# Appliances Burning Gaseous Fuels Regulations (S.L. 427.97)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.97**

**APPLIANCES BURNING GASEOUS**

**FUELS REGULATIONS**

12th December, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 429 of 2018.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Appliances Burning Gaseous Fuels Regulations.

## 2. Scope.

(1) These regulations implement the requirements set by Regulation (EU) 2016/426 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2016 on appliances burning gaseous fuels and repealing Directive 2009/142/EC.

(2) The procedures for the assessment and notification of conformity assessment bodies, and the monitoring of notified bodies shall be those prescribed in the Method for Designating Conformity Assessment Bodies Regulations.

*S.L. 427.45.*

(3) With respect to Articles 7(6), 7(7), 7(9), 9(3), 9(4), 9(9), 10(2), 14(4), 15(2), 15(3) of Regulation (EU) 2016/426, the language used shall be Maltese or English.

## 3. Designation of notifying authority. Cap. 427.

The Technical Regulations Division within the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority is the notifying authority within the meaning of Article 20 of Regulation (EU) 2016/426.

## 4. Penalties.

The penalties applicable to any infringement related to these regulations shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.

*Cap. 427.*
