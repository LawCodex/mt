# Energy Labelling of Household Lamps (Implementing Measures) Regulations (S.L. 427.79)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.79**

**ENERGY LABELLING OF HOUSEHOLD**

**LAMPS (IMPLEMENTING MEASURES)**

**REGULATIONS**

20th July, 2011\*

LEGAL NOTICE 342 of 2011.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Energy Labelling of Household Lamps (Implementing Measures) Regulations.

*S.L. 427.24*

(2) These regulations shall be interpreted in terms of the Indication by Labelling and Standard Product Information of the Consumption of Energy and other Resources by Energy Related Products Regulations, hereinafter referred to as "the Energy Labelling Regulations".

(3) These regulations implement the requirements of Commission Directive 98/11/EC of 27 January 1998 implementing Council Directive 92/75/EEC with regard to energy labelling of household lamps.

(4) These regulations constitute an implementing measure within the meaning of the Energy Labelling Regulations with regard to the energy labelling of household lamps.

## 2. Scope and application.

(1) These regulations shall apply to household electric lamps supplied directly from the mains (filament and integral compact fluorescent lamps), and to household fluorescent lamps (including linear, and non-integral compact fluorescent lamps), even when marketed for non-household use.

(2) Where an appliance can be taken apart by end users, for the purposes of this Directive the ‘lamp’ shall be any part emitting light.

(3) The following lamps shall be excluded from the scope of these regulations:

(a) those with a luminous flux of more than 6,500 lumens;

(b) those with an input power of less than 4 watts;

(c) reflector lamps;

(d) those marketed or commercialised primarily for use with other energy sources, such as batteries;

(e) those not marketed or commercialised primarily for the production of light in the visible range (400 to 800 nm);

(f) those marketed or commercialised as part of a product, the primary purpose of which is not illuminative. However, where the lamp is offered for sale, hire or hire purchase or displayed separately, for example as a spare part, it shall be included.

\*See regulation 1(5) of these Regulations as originally promulgated.

(4) For lamps referred to in sub-regulation (3), labels and fiches may be provided in accordance with these regulations, provided that harmonised measurement standards applicable to such lamps have been adopted and published in accordance with regulation 4(6).

## 3. Definitions.

In these regulation, except where the context otherwise requires, expressions used shall have the same meaning as in the Energy Labelling Regulations.

## 4. Energy label and information requirements.

(1) The technical documentation referred to in regulation 6(2)(a) of the Energy Labelling Regulations shall include:

(a) the name, trade mark and address of the supplier;

(b) a general description of the lamp, sufficient for it to be uniquely identified;

(c) information, including drawings as relevant, on the main design features of the model and in particular items which appreciably affect its energy consumption;

(d) reports of relevant measurement tests carried out under the test procedures of the harmonised standards referred to in regulation 4(6);

(e) operating instructions, if any.

(2) The label referred to in regulation 5(1) of the Energy Labelling Regulations shall be as specified in Schedule I.

The label shall be placed or printed on, or attached to, the outside of the individual packaging of the lamp. Nothing else placed or printed on, or attached to, the individual packaging of the lamp shall obscure it or reduce its visibility. Schedule I specifies how the label may be displayed in the case of very small packaging.

(3) The fiche referred to in regulation 5(2) of the Energy Labelling Regulations shall be as specified in Schedule II.

(4) In the circumstances referred to in regulation 8 of the Energy Labelling Regulations, and where the offer for sale, hire or hire purchase is provided by means of a printed communication, such as a catalogue, that printed communication shall include all the information specified in Schedule III.

(5) The energy efficiency class of a lamp, as specified on the label and the fiche, shall be determined in accordance with Schedule IV.

(6) The information required by these regulations shall be produced in accordance with harmonised standards, the reference numbers of which have been published in the Official Journal of the European Communities and for which Member States have published the reference numbers of the national standards transposing those harmonised standards.

(7) The harmonised standards referred to in sub-regulation (6)

shall be drawn up under a mandate from the Commission in

accordance with Directive 83/189/EEC.

Note

III. The input power (wattage) of the lamp, measured in accordance with the test procedures of the harmonised standards referred to in regulation 4(6).

IV. The average rated life of the lamp in hours, measured in accordance with the test procedures of the harmonised standards referred to in regulation 4(6). Where no other information on the life of the lamp is included on the packaging, this may be omitted.
