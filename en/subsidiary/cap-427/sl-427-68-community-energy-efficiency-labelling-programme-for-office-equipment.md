# Community Energy-Efficiency Labelling Programme for Office Equipment (Implementation) Regulations (S.L. 427.68)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.68**

**COMMUNITY ENERGY-EFFICIENCY**

**LABELLING PROGRAMME FOR OFFICE**

**EQUIPMENT (IMPLEMENTATION)**

**REGULATIONS**

12th September, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 208 of 2008.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Community Energy- Efficiency Labelling Programme for Office Equipment (Implementation) Regulations.

(2) These regulations apply to the requirements of Regulation (EC) No 106/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 January 2008 on a Community energy-efficiency labelling programme for office equipment as regards the use of the Common Logo, and the placing on the market of office equipment bearing the Common Logo.

## 2. National representative. S.L. 419.03

(1) The Director of Regulatory Affairs in the Malta Standards Authority, or his designate, as referred to in the Establishment of Directorates of the Malta Standards Authority Order, shall assume the functions of the national representative within the meaning of Articles 8 and 9 of Regulation (EC) No. 106/ 2008.

*S.L. 419.03*

(2) The Director of Market Surveillance in the Malta Standards Authority, or his designate, as referred to in the Establishment of Directorates of the Malta Standards Authority Order, shall assume the functions and responsibilities of market surveillance and control of abuse with in the meaning of Regulation (EC) No. 106/ 2008.

## 3. Contraventions. Cap. 427.

The provisions of articles 6, 12, 14, 23, 26, 29, and 32 of the Product Safety Act shall apply in respect of offences under Article 12, paragraphs 1 and 2, of Regulation (EC) No. 106/2008.
