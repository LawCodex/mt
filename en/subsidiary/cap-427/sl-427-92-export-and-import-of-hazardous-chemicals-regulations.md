# Export and Import of Hazardous Chemicals Regulations (S.L. 427.92)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.92**

**EXPORT AND IMPORT OF HAZARDOUS**

**CHEMICALS REGULATIONS**

24th January, 2017 LEGAL NOTICE 27 of 2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Export and Import of Hazardous Chemicals Regulations.

## 2. Scope.

These regulations implement the provisions of Regulation (EC) No. 649/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2012 concerning the export and import of hazardous chemicals, hereinafter referred to as "the EC Regulation".

## 3. Interpretation.

For the purposes of the EC Regulation, "the competent authority" means the Technical Regulatory Directorate (TRD) within Malta Consumer and Competition Authority (MCCAA).

## 4. Penalties. Cap. 427.

The penalties applicable for infringement of the provisions of the EC Regulation shall be those provided for in the Product Safety Act, Part IV.
