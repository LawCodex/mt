# Labelling of Tyres with respect to Fuel Efficiency and Other Parameters (Implementing) Regulations (S.L. 427.103)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.103**

**LABELLING OF TYRES WITH RESPECT TO FUEL**

**EFFICIENCY AND OTHER PARAMETERS**

**(IMPLEMENTING) REGULATIONS**

6th August, 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 323 of 2021.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Labelling of Tyres with respect to Fuel Efficiency and Other Parameters (Implementing) Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2020/740 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 May 2020 on the labelling of tyres with respect to fuel efficiency and other parameters, amending Regulation (EU) 2017/ 1369 and repealing Regulation (EC) No 1222/2009.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Regulation (EU) 2020/740" means Regulation (EU) 2020/740 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 May 2020 on the labelling of tyres with respect to fuel efficiency and other parameters, amending Regulation (EU) 2017/1369 and repealing Regulation (EC) No 1222/2009.

## 3. Proceedings and penalties.

With reference to Article 11(5) of Regulation (EU) 2020/ 740, the proceedings and penalties applicable for any infringement of the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2020/740 shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.

*Cap. 427.*
