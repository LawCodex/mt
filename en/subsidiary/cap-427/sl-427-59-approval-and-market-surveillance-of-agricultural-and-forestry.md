# Approval and Market Surveillance of Agricultural and Forestry Vehicles Regulations (S.L. 427.59)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.59**

APPROVAL AND MARKET SURVEILLANCE OF AGRICULTURAL AND FORESTRY VEHICLES

**REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2016\*

LEGAL NOTICE 18 of 2016, as amended by Legal Notice 69 of 2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Approval and Market Surveillance of Agricultural and Forestry Vehicles Regulations.

## 2. Scope.

*Amended by: L.N. 69 of 2017.*

These regulations implement:

(a) the requirements set by Regulation (EU) No 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 February 2013 on the approval and market surveillance of agricultural and forestry vehicles;

(b) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1322/ 2014 of 19 September 2014 supplementing and amending Regulation (EU) No 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to vehicle construction and general requirements for the approval of agricultural and forestry vehicles;

(c) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/68 of 15 October 2014 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 167/ 2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to vehicle braking requirements for the approval of agricultural and forestry vehicles;

(d) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/96 of 1 October 2014 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 167/ 2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards environmental and propulsion unit performance requirements of agricultural and forestry vehicles;

(e) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/208 of 8 December 2014 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to vehicle functional safety requirements for the approval of agricultural and forestry vehicles;

(f) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/504 of 11 March 2015 implementing Regulation (EU) No 167/ 2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to the administrative requirements for the approval and market surveillance of agricultural and forestry vehicles; and

\*see regulation 1(2) of these Regulations, as originally promulgated.

(g) the separate Directives listed in the Schedule.

## 3.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 510.*

"the Act" means the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act;

"end-of-series vehicle" means any vehicle that is part of a stock which cannot be made available on the market or can no longer be made available on the market, registered or enter into service owing to the entry into force of new technical requirements against which it has not been approved;

"national type-approval" means a type-approval procedure laid down by the national law of a Member State, the validity of such approval being restricted to the territory of that Member State;

"registration" means the administrative authorisation for the entry into service including for road traffic of a vehicle, involving the identification of the latter and the issuing to it of a serial number to be known as the registration number, be it permanently, temporarily or for a short period of time.

## 4. Approval authority.

(1) The Technical Regulations Division of the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority is the approval authority and the market surveillance authority within the meaning of Regulation (EU) No 167/2013.

(2) The Authority for Transport in Malta shall be the competent authority responsible for registration, national type-approval and end-of-series vehicles.

## 5. Penalties.

(1) The penalties applicable for the infringement of the provisions of Regulation (EU) No 167/2013 shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.

*Cap. 427.*

(2) The penalties applicable for any infringement related to registration, national type-approval and end-of-series vehicles, in accordance with regulation 4(2), shall be those provided for in the Authority for Transport in Malta Act.

*Cap. 499.*
