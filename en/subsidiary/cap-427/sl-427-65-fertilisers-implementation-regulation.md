# Fertilisers (Implementation) Regulation (S.L. 427.65)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.65**

FERTILISERS (IMPLEMENTATION) REGULATIONS 18th January, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 17 of 2008.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Fertilisers (Implementation) Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement the provisions of Regulation (EC) No 2003/2003 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13th October 2003 relating to fertilisers.

## 2. Establishment of the competent authority.

The Malta Standards Authority shall be the competent authority within the meaning of Regulation (EC) No. 2003/2003.

## 3. Labelling.

The information specified in Article 11 of Regulation (EC) No. 2003/2003 shall appear in at least one of the following languages:

Maltese,

English,

Italian.

## 4. Penalties.

The penalties applicable for infringement of the provisions of Article 36 of Regulation (EC) No. 2003/2003 shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.

*Cap. 427.*
