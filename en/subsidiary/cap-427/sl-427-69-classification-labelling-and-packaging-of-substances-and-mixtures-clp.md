# Classification, Labelling and Packaging of Substances and Mixtures (CLP) (Implementation) Regulations (S.L. 427.69)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.69**

**CLASSIFICATION, LABELLING AND**

**PACKAGING OF SUBSTANCES AND**

**MIXTURES (CLP) (IMPLEMENTATION)**

**REGULATIONS**

31st July, 2009

LEGAL NOTICE 214 of 2009.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Classification, Labelling and Packaging of Substances and Mixtures (CLP) (Implementation) Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement the provisions of articles 43, 44, 45, 47, 60, 61 and 62 of Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2008 on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures, amending and repealing Directives 67/548/EEC and 1999/45/EC, and amending Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006.

## 2. Establishment of the competent authority and enforcement authority.

(1) The Malta Standards Authority is the competent authority and enforcement authority within the meaning of Article 43 of Regulation (EC) No. 1272/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2008 on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures, amending and repealing Directives 67/548/EEC and 1999/45/EC, and amending Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 (hereinafter referred to as ''Regulation (EC) No. 1272/2008'').

(2) The Malta Standards Authority shall be the authority responsible for the provisions prescribed in Regulation (EC) No. 1272/2008, Title VI, Competent Authorities and Enforcement.

## 3. Penalties.

The penalties applicable for infringement of the provisions of Regulation (EC) No.1272/2008 shall be those provided for in the Product Safety Act, Part IV.
