# Mutual Recognition (Implementing) Regulations (S.L. 427.100)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.100**

**MUTUAL RECOGNITION (IMPLEMENTING)**

**REGULATIONS**

13th October, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 391 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is Mutual Recognition (Implementing) Regulations.

## 2. Scope.

These regulations implement the provisions of Regulation (EU) No. 2019/515 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 March 2019 on the mutual recognition of goods lawfully marketed in another Member State and repealing Regulation (EC) No. 764/2008.

## 3. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Director General" means the Director General (Technical Regulations) appointed by article 19 of the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act;

*Cap. 510.*

"Regulation (EU) No. 2019/515" means Regulation (EU) No. 2019/515 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 March 2019 on the mutual recognition of goods lawfully marketed in another Member State and repealing Regulation (EC) No. 764/2008.

## 4. Designation of the Product Contact Point. Cap. 510.

(a) The Technical Regulations Division established by article 19 of the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act shall be the Product Contact Point for the purpose of Article 9 of Regulation (EU) No. 2019/515.

*Information requirements by competent authorities.*

(b) To enable the Product Contact Point to submit online information in accordance with the provisions of Article 9(2) of Regulation (EU) No. 2019/515, the competent authorities shall provide their contact details, and information about their remedies and procedures to the Product Contact Point, in such form and manner and within such time-frames that the Director General shall require, provided that the competent authorities shall maintain this information up to date at all times.

(c) To enable the Product Contact Point to provide information in accordance with the provisions of Article 9(3) of Regulation (EU) No. 2019/515, the competent authorities shall respond to the request for information put forward by the Product Contact Point, in such form and manner and within such time-frames that the Director General shall prescribe.

## 5. National remedies by national competent authorities.

For the purpose of Article 5(12) and 9(2) of Regulation (EU) No. 2019/515, the national remedies shall be established by the relevant national competent authorities.

## 6. Language requirements for the mutual recognition declaration.

For the purpose of Article 4(1) of Regulation (EU) No. 2019/515, the mutual recognition declaration shall be drawn up in, or translated by the economic operator, in the English or Maltese language.
