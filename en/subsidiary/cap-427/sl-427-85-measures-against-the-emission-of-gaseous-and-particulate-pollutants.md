# Measures Against the Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants from Internal Combustion Engines to be Installed in Non-Road Mobile Machinery Regulations (S.L. 427.85)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.85**

**MEASURES AGAINST THE EMISSION OF**

GASEOUS AND PARTICULATE POLLUTANTS FROM INTERNAL COMBUSTION ENGINES TO BE INSTALLED IN NON-ROAD MOBILE

**MACHINERY REGULATIONS**

14th February, 2017 LEGAL NOTICE 55 of 2017.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Measures Against the Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants from Internal Combustion Engines to be Installed in Non-Road Mobile Machinery Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement the requirements set by Regulation (EU) 2016/1628 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 September 2016 on requirements relating to gaseous and particulate pollutant emission limits and type-approval for internal combustion engines for non-road mobile machinery.

## 3. Definitions.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 510*

"the Act" means the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act;

*Cap. 510*

"approval authority" means the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority established by article 3 of the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act.

## 4. Designation of competent authorities.

The Technical Regulations Division of the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority is the approval authority and the market surveillance authority within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2016/1628.

## 5. Penalties.

The penalties applicable to any infringement related to these regulations shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.

*Cap. 427.*

## 6. Revocation.

(1) Without prejudice to the transitional provisions stipulated in Article 58(1) to (4) of Regulation 2016/1628, the Measures Against the Emission of Gaseous and Particulate Pollutants from Internal Combustion Engines to be Installed in Non-Road Mobile Machinery Regulations, 2013, are hereby revoked.

*L.N. 83 of 2013.*

(2) Any references to the repealed regulations mentioned in sub-regulation (1) shall be construed as references to these regulations.
