# Cableways Installations (Implementing) Regulations (S.L. 427.98)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.98**

**CABLEWAYS INSTALLATIONS (IMPLEMENTING)**

**REGULATIONS**

16th April 2019

LEGAL NOTICE 79 of 2019.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is Cableways Installations (Implementing) Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2016/424 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2016 on cableway installations and repealing Directive 2000/9/EC.

## 2. Designation of notifying authority. Cap. 510.

The Technical Regulations Division established by article 19 of the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act shall be the notifying authority for the purposes of Article 23 of Regulation (EU) No. 2016/424.

## 3.

The procedures for the assessment and notification of conformity assessment bodies, and the monitoring of notified bodies shall be those prescribed in the Method for Designating Conformity Assessment Bodies Regulations.

*S.L. 427.45*

## 4. Penalties ans proceedings.

The penalties and proceedings applicable for infringement of any of the provisions of Regulation (EU) No. 2016/ 424 shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.

*Cap. 427.*
