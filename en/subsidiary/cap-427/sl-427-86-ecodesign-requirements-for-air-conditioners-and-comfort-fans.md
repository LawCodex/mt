# Ecodesign Requirements for Air Conditioners and Comfort Fans (Implementation) Regulations (S.L. 427.86)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 427.86**

**ECODESIGN REQUIREMENTS FOR**

**AIRCONDITIONERS AND COMFORT FANSS**

**(IMPLEMENTATION) REGULATIONS**

14th June, 2013

LEGAL NOTICE 156 of 2013.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Ecodesign Requirements for Air Conditioners and Comfort Fans (Implementation) Regulations.

## 2. Applicability or otherwise of these regulations.

These regulations implement the requirements set by Regulation (EC) No 206/2012 of the European Commission of 6 March 2012 implementing Directive 2009/125/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for air conditioners and comfort fans. Regulation (EC) No 206/2012 establishes eco-design requirements for the placing on the market of electric mains-operated air conditioners with a rated capacity of ≤ 12 kW for cooling, or heating if the product has no cooling function, and comfort fans with an electric fan power input ≤ 125W. In the implementation of the above-mentioned (EC) No 206/2012, these regulations shall apply to:

(a) appliances that use non-electric energy sources;

(b) air conditioners of which the condenser-side or evaporator-side, or both, do not use air for heat transfer medium.

## 3. Authority responsible to verify compliance. Cap. 510.

For the purpose of Articles 4 and 5 of Regulation (EC) No 206/2012, the Technical Regulations Division, as established by the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act, shall be the competent authority.

## 4. Applicable penalties. Cap. 427.

The penalties applicable for the infringement of the provisions of Regulation (EC) No 206/2012 shall be those provided for in Part IV of the Product Safety Act.
