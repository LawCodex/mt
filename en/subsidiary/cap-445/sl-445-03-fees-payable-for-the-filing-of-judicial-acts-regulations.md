# Fees payable for the Filing of Judicial Acts Regulations (S.L. 445.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 445.03**

**FEES PAYABLE FOR THE FILING OF JUDICIAL**

**ACTS REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 99 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Fees Payable for the Filing of Judicial Acts Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable for filing of judicial acts.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The fees payable under Cultural Heritage Act for the filing of judicial acts shall be three hundred and forty-nine euro and forty-one cents (€349.41).
