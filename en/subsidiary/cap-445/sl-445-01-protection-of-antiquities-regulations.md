# Protection of Antiquities Regulations (S.L. 445.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 445.01**

**PROTECTION OF ANTIQUITIES REGULATIONS**

21st November, 1932 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 402 of 1932, as amended by Government Notices 137 of 1935 and 338 of 1939.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Protection of Antiquities Regulations.

## 2. Discovery of sites or remains.

Any person discovering, whether in private tenements or in tenements belonging to the Government, such sites or remains as from their general appearance or from the nature of the objects found on or in such sites or remains would justify the suspicion that they are important in the sense of the Cultural Heritage Act, is required forthwith to report the discovery to the Director of the Museum and Secretary of the Antiquities Committee, or to the senior officer of the nearest Police Station, who will immediately forward it, or a record thereof, if the report is verbal, to the Director of the Museum and Secretary of the Antiquities Committee.

*Cap. 445.*

## 3. Inspection of sites or remains.

The Director of the Museum and Secretary of the Antiquities Committee shall immediately inspect such sites or remains or cause them to be inspected and shall report to the Minister the result of his investigations.

## 4. Sites to be regarded under Cultural Heritage Act. Cap. 445.

Such sites or remains are to be regarded as falling under the provisions of the Cultural Heritage Act, unless and until they are by the Minister declared to be free from the operation thereof by means of a letter directed to the person who shall have made the report, or, if necessary, to the owner of the tenement in which the sites or remains shall have been discovered.

*Amended by: G.N. 137 of 1935; G.N. 338 of 1939.*
