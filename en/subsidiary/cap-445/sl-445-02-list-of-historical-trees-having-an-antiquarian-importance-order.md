# List of Historical Trees having an Antiquarian Importance Order (S.L. 445.02)

SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 445.02

LIST OF HISTORICAL TREES HAVING AN

ANTIQUARIAN IMPORTANCE ORDER

19th July, 1933 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 269 of 1933.

*\1.*

*Title.*

The title of this Order is List of Historical Trees having an Antiquarian Importance Order.

*\2.*

*Protected trees.*

The more important trees to be protected by law are listed in the Schedule to this Order.

SCHEDULE

(Article 2)

List of trees protected by law

*\1.*

The group of ancient holm-oaks (Quercus Ilex) situated at Il-Ballut tal-Wardija at the foot of the Wardija hills facing the Pwales valley.

*\2.*

The group of ancient holm-oaks (Quercus Ilex) at Im[iebah, limits of Mellieħa.

*\3.*

The ancient olive-trees (Olea europaca) existing in the lands of Bidnija, close to Wardija.

*\4.*

The following trees at Buskett:

(a) the holm-oaks close to the main road;

(b) the large ash trees (Fraxinus excelsior) in the valley. The following trees in the Sant’ Antonio Gardens:

*\5.*

(a) the large pine-tree (Pinus halepensis) at the back of the Palace;

(b) the collossal tree (Gleditschia triachanthos, ar. inermis) and the old Indian coral tree (Erythrina indica), both in the private gardens;

(c) the group of pine trees close to the pumping station on the main road;

(d) the three huge Araucaria excelsa in the private gardens;

(e) the seven cypress trees (Cypressus sempervirens pyramidalis) and the four cypress trees (Cupressus sempervirens horizontalis) in the central part of the public gardens;

(f) the old olive trees in the said gardens.

*\6.*

The large pine trees in the Mall Gardens in San Filippo bastion at Floriana.
