# Consumer Claims Tribnal (Deposit of Acts) Order (S.L. 378.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 378.04**

**CONSUMER CLAIMS TRIBUNAL**

**(DEPOSIT OF ACTS) ORDER**

16th January, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 11 of 1996.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Consumer Claims Tribunal (Deposit of Acts) Order.

## 2. Deposit of acts.

The acts and records of the Consumer Claims Tribunal (Malta) shall be deposited and kept in the edifice of the Department of Consumer Affairs in Valletta, and the acts and records of the Consumer Claims Tribunal (Gozo) shall be deposited and kept in the building used by the Gozo Administrative Centre in Victoria, Gozo.
