# Consumer Claims Tribunal (Arbiter's Remuneration) Notice (S.L. 378.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 378.07**

**CONSUMER CLAIMS TRIBUNAL**

**(ARBITERS’ REMUNERATION) NOTICE**

7th September, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 169 of 2000, as amended by Legal Notices 425 of 2007 and 15 of 2023.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Notice is the Consumer Claims Tribunal (Arbiters' Remuneration) Notice.

## 2. Remuneration.

*Amended by: L.N. 15 of 2023.*

The remuneration payable to an arbiter of the Consumer Claims Tribunal as from the 1st January, 2023 at the rate of one hundred and fifty euro (€150) per case, whether decided upon, ceded or withdrawn.
