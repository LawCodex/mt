# Rail Passengers’ Rights and Obligations (Implementing) Regulations (S.L. 378.21)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 378.21**

**RAIL PASSENGERS’ RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS**

**(IMPLEMENTING) REGULATIONS**

22nd November, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 312 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Rail Passengers’ Rights and Obligations (Implementing) Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to implement the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2021/782 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2021 on rail passengers’ rights and obligations (recast).

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 378.*

"Act" means the Consumer Affairs Act;

"Director General" means the Director General (Consumer Affairs) as defined in the Act;

*  Cap. 510.*

"Office for Consumer Affairs" means the Office for Consumer Affairs as established under article 16(1) of the Malta Competition and Consumer Affairs Authority Act;

"person with disabilities" shall have the meaning assigned to it in Regulation (EU) 2021/782;

"person with reduced mobility" shall have the meaning assigned to it in Regulation (EU) 2021/782; and

"Regulation (EU) 2021/782" means Regulation (EU) 2021/782 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2021 on rail passengers’ rights and obligations (recast).

(2) Exclusively for the purposes of these regulations, "passengers", "person with disabilities" and "person with reduced mobility" shall be classified as "consumers" according to paragraph (iii) of the definition "consumer" in article 2 of the Act.

(3) Words and expressions which are not defined in these regulations shall have the same meaning assigned to them in Regulation (EU) 2021/782:

Provided that unless the context otherwise requires, words and expressions used in these regulations, which are not defined in Regulation (EU) 2021/782 and which are not herein defined, shall have the same meaning assigned to them in the Act.

## 3. Designated national enforcement body.

The Office for Consumer Affairs is hereby being designated as the national enforcement body for the purpose of Regulation (EU) 2021/782.

## 4. Functions and powers of the Director General.

For the purpose of discharging his functions under these regulations, the Director General shall have the same functions and powers prescribed under the Act.

## 5. Penalties and proceedings.

(1) For the purpose of Article 35 of Regulation (EU) 2021/782, the penalties in relation to any infringements of Regulation (EU) 2021/782 or of these regulations shall be those penalties established in Part XIIA of the Act.

(2) Part IIA of the Act shall apply to proceedings in relation to any infringements of Regulation (EU) 2021/782 or of these regulations.
