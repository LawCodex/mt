# Notice for the purposes of the interpretation of "trader" in the Consumer Affairs Act (S.L. 378.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 378.06**

**NOTICE FOR THE PURPOSES OF THE**

**INTERPRETATION OF "TRADER" IN THE**

**CONSUMER AFFAIRS ACT**

1st October, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 109 of 1997, as amended by Act XXXIV of 2014.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Notice is Notice for the purposes of the interpretation of "trader" in the Consumer Affairs Act.

## 2. Corporations considered as traders.

*Amended by: XXXIV. 2014.36. Cap. 378.*

Each public corporation mentioned hereunder, which is a corporation which provides goods or services to consumers for a fee, shall be considered as from the 1st October, 1997 as a trader for the purposes of the Consumer Affairs Act:

Jobsplus

Malta Development Corporation

Malta Drydocks

Telemalta Corporation

Malta Freeport Corporation Limited

Water Services Corporation.
