# Consumer Claims Tribunal (Designation of Places) Order (S.L. 378.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 378.02**

**CONSUMER CLAIMS TRIBUNAL**

**(DESIGNATION OF PLACES) ORDER**

16th January, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 9 of 1996, as amended by Legal Notice 14 of 2023.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Consumer Claims Tribunal (Designation of Places) Order.

## 2. Designation of places.

*Substituted by: L.N. 14 of 2023.*

The office of the Department of Consumer Affairs in Valletta, the office used by the Gozo Administrative Centre in Victoria, Gozo, and the office used by the Office of the Permanent Secretary for Inclusion, Voluntary Organisations and Consumer Rights in Mrieħel have been designated as the places where the Consumer Claims Tribunal shall hold its sittings.
