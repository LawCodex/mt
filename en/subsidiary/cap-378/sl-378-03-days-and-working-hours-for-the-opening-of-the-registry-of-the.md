# Days and Working Hours for the Opening of the Registry of the Consumer Claims Tribunal Regulations (S.L. 378.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 378.03**

DAYS AND WORKING HOURS FOR THE OPENING OF THE REGISTRY OF THE CONSUMER CLAIMS

**TRIBUNAL REGULATIONS**

16th January, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 10 of 1996.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Days and Working Hours for the Opening of the Registry of the Consumer Claims Tribunal Regulations.

## 2. Filing of acts.

The Registry of the Consumer Claims Tribunals shall be open for the filing of any acts from Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays, from 9.00 a.m. to 12 noon.
