# Display of Price-Lists in Bars and Kiosks Regulations (S.L. 378.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 378.05**

DISPLAY OF PRICE-LISTS IN BARS AND KIOSKS

**REGULATIONS**

27th June, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 97 of 1997, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Display of Price-Lists in Bars and Kiosks Regulations.

## 2. Display of price-lists.

(1) Every owner or other person responsible for the running of any bar or kiosk shall ensure that price-lists of meals, snacks and other items of food or drink shall be prominently displayed, in case of a bar at the entrance, and in the case of a kiosk on the outside of such kiosk in a manner which is easily noticeable by the customers.

(2) For the purposes of this regulation:

(a) the term "bar" also includes school tuck-shops and snack bars situated within factory premises;

(b) the term "kiosk" also includes any movable sales outlet, in which case the price-list shall be fixed in a prominent place thereof.

## 3. Offences.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

Any person who acts in contravention of, or fails to comply with, these regulations shall be guilty of an offence and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (multa) of not less than one hundred and sixteen euro and forty-seven cents (116.47) but not more than four hundred and sixty-five euro and eighty-seven cents (465.87).
