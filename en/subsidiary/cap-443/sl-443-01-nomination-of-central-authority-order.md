# Nomination of Central Authority Order (S.L. 443.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 443.01**

NOMINATION OF CENTRAL AUTHORITY ORDER 1st August, 2006

LEGAL NOTICE 163 of 2006, as amended by Legal Notice 295 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Nomination of Central Authority Order.

## 2. Central authority. Cap. 443.

The State Advocate is hereby nominated as the central authority for the purposes of the Legal Procedures (Ratification of Conventions) Act.
