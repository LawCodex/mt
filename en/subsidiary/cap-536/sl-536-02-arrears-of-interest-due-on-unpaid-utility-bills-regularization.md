# Arrears of Interest due on Unpaid Utility Bills (Regularization) Regulations (S.L. 536.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 536.02**

**ARREARS OF INTEREST DUE ON UNPAID**

**UTILITY BILLS (REGULARIZATION)**

**REGULATIONS**

1st September, 2014\*

LEGAL NOTICE 302 of 2014.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Arrears of Interest due on Unpaid Utility Bills (Regularization) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, "regularization period" means the period commencing from the 1st September 2014 till the 31st October 2014 both dates included.

## 3. Partial exemption from payment. S.L. 423.01 S.L. 423.03

Whosoever, on the date of the coming into force of these regulations, has unpaid dues on the utility bill and interest is due on the unpaid utility bill under regulation 66(3) of the Electricity Supply Regulations and regulation 30 of the Water Supply Regulations, shall be granted a partial exemption from the payment of arrears relative to the interest due on the bill upon the fulfilment of the terms and conditions set out in these regulations.

## 4. Regularization period.

The partial exemption from the payment of interest shall be granted to whosoever, during the regularisation period, submits to ARMS Limited the applicable form referred to in regulation 5.

## 5. Interest applicable.

(1) Whosoever submits a valid form in terms of these regulations and pays all amounts due on his utility bills within the regularisation period shall only be charged for 25% of the interest due.

(2) Whosoever submits a valid form in terms of these regulations and pays all amounts due on his utility bills within one month from the closure of the regularisation period shall only be charged for 50% of the interest due.

(3) Whosoever submits a valid form under this regulation and pays all amounts due on his utility bills within two months from the closure of the regularisation period shall only be charged for 75% of the interest due.

\*see regulation 1(2) of these Regulations as originally promulgated.
