# Enemalta Contracts (Removal of Restrictive Conditions) Order (S.L. 536.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 536.01**

**ENEMALTA CONTRACTS**

**(REMOVAL OF RESTRICTIVE CONDITIONS)**

**ORDER**

27th August, 2014

LEGAL NOTICE 302 of 2014, as amended by Legal Notice 406 of 2014.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Enemalta Contracts (Removal of Restrictive Conditions) Order.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 536.*

"Act" means the Enemalta (Transfer of Assets, Rights, Liabilities and Obligations) Act;

*Cap. 272.*

"Corporation" means the Enemalta Corporation established by the Enemalta Act;

"Company" means the public limited liability company Enemalta plc, registered under the laws of Malta and bearing company registration number C65836;

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Energy.

## 3. Removal of restrictive conditions.

All restrictive conditions which limit the use or the transfer of land included in the public deeds listed in the Schedule to this Order, which restrictive conditions were imposed by Government on the Corporation in the said public deeds by virtue of the application of article 3(1)(e) of the Disposal of Government Land Act and which public deeds transferred land to the Corporation which was subsequently transferred to the Company by virtue of the provisions of article 5 (a) of the Act and of the other provisions of the Act to which the said article 5(a) refers, are hereby removed:

*Cap. 268.*

Provided that:

(a) where land which is the subject matter of the public deeds listed in the Schedule is land which on the date of the issue of this Order is directly used for the storage of petroleum or of petroleum products or for the conveyance of petroleum or of petroleum products to a storage facility, any condition in the said public deeds, however worded, requiring that the said land shall be used for the purposes of the said storage or conveyance of petroleum or of petroleum products shall not be deemed to be a condition imposed by the Government on the Corporation in the said public deeds by virtue of the application of article 3(1)(e) of the Disposal of Government Land Act and this Order shall not apply in respect of such condition; and

*Cap. 268.*

(b) any condition in any public deed listed in the Schedule which requires the consent of the Commissioner of Land for the transfer of any land which forms the subject matter of the said public deeds shall not be deemed to be a condition imposed by the Government on the Corporation in the said public deeds by virtue of the application of article 3(1)(e) of the Disposal of Government Land Act and this Order shall not apply to such condition.

*Cap. 268.*
