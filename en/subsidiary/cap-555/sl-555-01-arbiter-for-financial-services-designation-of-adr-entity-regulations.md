# Arbiter for Financial Services (Designation of ADR Entity) Regulations (S.L. 555.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 555.01**

**ARBITER FOR FINANCIAL SERVICES**

(DESIGNATION OF ADR ENTITY) REGULATIONS 9th May, 2017 LEGAL NOTICE 137 of 2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Arbiter for Financial Services (Designation of ADR Entity) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"ADR" means Alternative Dispute Resolution.

## 3. Appointment of ADR Entity.

The Minister for Finance hereby appoints the Office of the Arbiter for Financial Services as Malta’s ADR entity for financial services which qualifies and is to be considered as the ADR entity for financial services falling within the scope of Directive 2013/11/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on alternative dispute resolution for consumer disputes.
