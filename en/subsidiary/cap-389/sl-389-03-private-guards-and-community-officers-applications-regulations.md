# Private Guards and Community Officers (Applications) Regulations (S.L. 389.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 389.03**

PRIVATE GUARDS AND COMMUNITY OFFICERS

**(APPLICATIONS) REGULATIONS**

21st May, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 115 of 2002, as amended by Legal Notice 316 of 2010.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Private Guards and Community Officers (Applications) Regulations.

## 2. Exemption.

*Amended by: L.N. 316 of 2010. Cap. 389.*

Public officers belonging to a grade or category in salary scale 11 to 20, both inclusive, or equivalent categories may be exempted from the provisions of article 10(a)(iv) of the Private Guards and Community Officers Act.
