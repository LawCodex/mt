# Community Officers Services Regulations (S.L. 389.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 389.04**

**COMMUNITY OFFICERS SERVICES**

**REGULATIONS**

10th September, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 256 of 2002.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Community Officers Services Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 389.*

"Act" means the Private Guards and Community Officers Act;

"Community Officers services" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in article 2 of the Act;

*Cap. 10.*

"street" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Code of Police Laws.

## 3. Community Officer shall have the power to give orders to the driver.

Whenever a community officer, during any activity or on a particular occasion in any street, in any locality for which a permit, licence or an authorization has been issued by the Local Council or by any other competent authority, or because of urgent circumstances, deems it necessary that the access and movement of vehicles should be controlled, he may direct and give any legitimate order to the drivers of such vehicles.
