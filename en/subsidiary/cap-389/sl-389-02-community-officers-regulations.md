# Community Officers Regulations (S.L. 389.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 389.02**

**COMMUNITY OFFICERS REGULATIONS**

27th August, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 161 of 1996, as amended by Legal Notices 216 of 1999, 79 of 2001, 402 of 2003, 425 of 2007, 402 of 2011 and 261 of 2012.

### 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Community Officers Regulations.

## PART I — Preliminary

### 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 389.*

"Act" means the Private Guards and Community Officers Act;

"Commissioner" means the Commissioner of Police;

"continuing training course" means any training course as is referred to in regulation 4(2) intended to continue to train and update community officers in the performance of their duties;

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for the Police.

## PART II — Qualifications and Training Courses

### 3. Qualifications for community officers.

*Amended by: L.N. 216 of 1999; L.N. 79 of 2001; L.N. 261 of 2012.*

No person shall be qualified to be licensed as a community officer in accordance with the provisions of the Act unless such person has attained the age of eighteen years and satisfies the Commissioner that he has obtained a standard of education equivalent to a school leaving certificate of either a secondary school or a trade school:

Provided that this provision shall not apply to persons who have completed a period of service of at least twenty-five years with the Police or armed forces or the prison services:

Provided further that the equivalence to a school leaving certificate as aforesaid shall not be required in the case of any person who satisfies the Commissioner that he has successfully completed an approved training course as specified in regulation 4, which training course was commenced prior to the 1st December, 1999:

Provided further that in the case of a person who is thirty years and over, a standard of eduation equivalent to a school leaving certificate of either a secondary school or a trade school shall not be required of him if he has successfully completed an approved training course conducted by the Employment Training Agency or an authorised person or agency as provided for in regulation 5.

### 4. Training courses certification.

*Amended by: L.N. 402 of 2003; L.N. 402 of 2011.*

Any person applying for a licence to act as a community officer shall submit to the Commissioner a certificate showing that he has successfully completed such training courses as may be approved in accordance with the provisions of these regulations:

Provided that this provision shall not apply to persons who have completed a period of service with the police or armed forces or the prison services, in Malta or abroad.

### 5. Approved training courses.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

(1) Any person desiring to provide training courses for the purposes of article 23 of the Act shall make an application therefore to the Minister.

(2) Such application shall contain the following information:

(a) the individual or individuals who will be effectively running the courses;

(b) the duration of the course;

(c) a detailed breakdown of the syllabus content;

(d) assessment criteria;

(e) the premises where training will be provided; and

(f) such other information as the Minister may require. (3) Having considered an application under subregulation (2) the Minister may approve the provision of such training courses.

(4) Such approval shall be valid for a period of one year and may be subject to such terms and conditions as the Minister may deem fit to impose and may be withdrawn at any time where:

(a) any of such terms and conditions or any of the provisions of these regulations has not been complied with; or

(b) circumstances arise which could have justified a refusal of the application; or

(c) for any other reason which he may deem appropriate. (5) Unless withdrawn in accordance with the provisions of subregulation (4) such approval may be renewable from year to year.

(6) Courses approved under this regulation shall as a minimum provide adequate instruction and training in the topics shown in the First Schedule.

(7) For the purposes of subregulation (3) such courses shall be subject to inspection as the Minister may direct.

(8) Any person whose application to provide training courses is approved in accordance with this regulation, shall be authorised to issue any certificate required under regulation 4.

(9) The authorisation under this regulation to a person to provide training courses shall include the authorisation to provide continuing training courses.

(10) Any person authorised to provide training courses under this regulation may not refuse to provide such training to any person seeking to be licensed as a community officer or to attend a continuing training course.

(11) A fee of two hundred and thirty-two euro and ninety-four cents (232.94) shall be payable on application under subregulation (1) and on renewal under subregulation (5).

## PART III — Miscellaneous

### 6. Licence of community officers agency.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

(1) An application for a licence of a community officers agency shall be made to the Commissioner in the form set out in the Second Schedule.

(2) There shall be payable to the Commissioner for the issue of a licence of a community officers agency the following application fees:

(a) an application fee of fifty-eight euro and twenty-three cents (58.23) for the issue of a licence to a person applying to offer his own services as an individual; and

(b) an application fee of two hundred and thirty-two euro and ninety-four cents (232.94) in any other case.

(3) On the renewal of such licence there shall also be paid to the Commissioner the following fees:

(a) fifty-eight euro and twenty-three cents (58.23) per annum in the case of a person offering his own services as an individual; and

(b) in any other case a fee of eleven euro and sixty-five cents (11.65) per annum for every community officer employed, subject to a minimum fee of two hundred and thirty-two euro and ninety-four cents (232.94) and a maximum fee of one thousand and one hundred and sixty-four euro and sixty-nine cents (1,164.69) per annum.

### 7. Licence of community officer.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007; L.N. 402 of 2011.*

(1) An application for a licence of a community officer shall be made to the Commissioner in the form set out in the Third Schedule.

(2) There shall be payable to the Commissioner in relation to a licence of a community officer an application fee of twenty-three euro and twenty-nine cents (23.29) on application.

### 8. Indemnity insurance.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

(1) Every person who, not being a person who is employed with a community officers agency for the purpose of such employment, is required to be covered by an indemnity insurance in terms of article 21 of the Act, and shall be so covered throughout the currency of the insurance policy as shown hereunder:

(a) for loss or damage caused by the negligence of such person or any of his employees, to an amount of not less than one hundred and sixteen thousand and four hundred and sixty-eight euro and sixty-seven cents (116,468.67); such amount shall be net of any excess under the said insurance policy;

(b) for any loss or damage caused by theft or any attempts thereof committed by such person or any of his employees, to an amount which is equivalent to the replacement value of the actual theft;

(c) for death or bodily injury resulting from the negligence of such person or his employee, to an amount of not less than two hundred and thirty-two thousand and nine hundred and thirty-seven euro and thirty-four cents (232,937.34).

(2) The provisions of subregulation (1) shall be without prejudice to any other cover for indemnity insurance which such person may be required to provide in the provision of community officer services.

(3) Unless special circumstances exist, an insurance company shall, for the purposes of the said article 21 of the Act, be deemed to be a company licensed under the provisions of the Insurance Business Act.

*Cap. 403.*

### 9. Identity cards.

(1) The Commissioner shall issue an identity card to every person licensed as a community officer. The identity card shall remain the property of the Commissioner.

(2) Such card shall bear a photograph of the licensee and shall be valid for a period of five years from the date of its issue. If the licence of the community officer in relation to whom the card was issued is suspended, withdrawn or not renewed the identity card shall be deemed to have expired on the happening of such an event.

(3) The identity card shall be returned to the Commissioner on the expiry of its validity as specified in subregulation (2).

(4) Every person licensed as a community officer shall, while on duty, wear the identity card in a conspicuous place.

FIRST SCHEDULE

(Regulation 5(6))

Training Course

*Course Objectives*

Successful completion of the course will enable a participant to:

(a) become a motivated and proficient private guard;

(b) recognise the functions and inherent duties and be able to perform such duties in a reliable, effective and well-organised manner.

*Methodology*

Following closely the provisions contained in the Private Guards and Community Officers Act, and other relevant legislation, a series of interconnected modules introduce the keynote subject-matter, skills required, threats and vulnerabilities.

Tuition will include presentations, demonstrations, discussions and participation in project exercises.

Quality of direction, tuition and presentation is to be assured through the participation of qualified specialists or acknowledged experts in all the fields of the keynote subjects.

*Syllabus*

Introduction

Purpose, Objectives and Outline of the Course

Introduction all round

Briefings covering Course procedures

*The Operating Environment*

Introduction to the Private Guards and Community Officers Act

Duties and obligations

Introduction to the Local Government Act with special reference to obligations of Local Council administration Introduction to other relevant legislation

Personal and interpersonal skills

Appearance and Image
