# Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts in connection with Appeals Regulations (S.L. 383.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 383.01**

**FEES PAYABLE ON THE FILING OF JUDICIAL**

**ACTS IN CONNECTION WITH APPEALS**

**REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 112 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts in connection with Appeals Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable on appeals.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007. Cap. 383.*

The fees payable under article 12(3) of the Controlled Companies (Procedure for Liquidation) Act on the filing of judicial acts in connection with appeals shall be one hundred and sixteen euro and forty-seven cents (€116.47).
