# Energy Performance of Buildings (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 623.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 623.03**

**ENERGY PERFORMANCE OF BUILDINGS**

**(FEES) REGULATIONS**

16th November, 2009

LEGAL NOTICE 308 of 2010, as amended by Act XXV of 2015 and legal Notice 409 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Energy Performance of Buildings (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: XXV. 2015.45.*

In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires:

"applications" means a request made by a registered EPB assessor for the registration of an Energy Performance of Building Certificate;

*S.L. 623.01*

"EPB assessor" has the meaning assigned to it in the Energy Performance of Buildings Regulations.

## 3. Applicability.

These regulations shall apply to all applications for the registration of Energy Performance of Buildings Certificates submitted by an EPB assessor to the Building and Construction Authority.

## 4. Fee to be paid.

There shall be paid a fee of seventy-five euro (€75) to the Building Regulation Office, in respect of the registration of Energy Performance of Buildings Certificates, submitted by registered assessors.
