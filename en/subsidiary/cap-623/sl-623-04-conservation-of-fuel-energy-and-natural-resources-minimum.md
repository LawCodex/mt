# Conservation of Fuel, Energy and Natural Resources (Minimum Requirements on the Energy Performance of Buildings) Regulations (S.L. 623.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 623.04**

**CONSERVATION OF FUEL, ENERGY AND**

**NATURAL RESOURCES (MINIMUM**

**REQUIREMENTS ON THE ENERGY**

**PERFORMANCE OF BUILDINGS REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2016

LEGAL NOTICE 434 of 2015 as amended by Legal Notice 409 of 2021.

## 1. Citation and commencement.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Conservation of Fuel, Energy and Natural Resources (Minimum Requirements on the Energy Performance of Buildings) Regulations.

(2) The provisions of these regulations shall come into force on 1st January, 2016:

Provided that these regulations shall only apply to buildings the application of which has been submitted to the Malta Environment and Planning Authority on or after the 1st January, 2016.

## 2. Minimum requirements for the conservation of fuel, energy and natural resources.

The Conservation of Fuel, Energy and Natural Resources (Minimum Requirements on the Energy Performance of Buildings), published by the Building and Construction Authority in August 2015, as Documents F - Part 1: Minimum Energy Performance Requirements for buildings in Malta and Part 2 Minimum Energy Performance Requirements for Building Services in Malta\*, shall for all intents and purposes be deemed applicable for new and renovated buildings, as the case may be, from 1st January 2016.
