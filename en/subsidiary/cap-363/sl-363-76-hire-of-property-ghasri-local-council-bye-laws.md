# Hire of Property (Ghasri Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.76)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.76**

HIRE OF PROPERTY (G}ASRI LOCAL COUNCIL)

**BYE-LAWS**

4th October, 2001

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 25/14/2001, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Hire of Property (G]asri Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-laws unless the context otherwise requires:

"Council" means the G]asri Local Council;

"property" means the building of the Council, the equipment, furnishings and any other objects, movable or immovable, housed inside its premises and which is the property of the Council or which falls under its custody.

*Hire of property.*

3.1 Without prejudice to the provisions of the financial regulations and procedures, the Council shall have the right to hire property to third parties and to other Local Councils, and commercial or non-commercial organisations.

3.2 In the case of hire of any property, the fee due shall be as indicated in the Schedule.

3.3 The hire of property shall be made by means of a contract valid for a period not exceeding one year, which may be renewed for any further period not exceeding one year:

Provided that the Council shall have the right to hire the property for any day at the established rate in the Schedule.

## 4. Terms and conditions of hire.

When hiring the property, the Council may impose any terms and conditions which it deems proper in the circumstances so as to safeguard its interests and ensure the proper use of the said property.

## 5. Termination of contract before the stipulated time.

Any lease made in terms of these Bye-Laws may be terminated as soon as any of the terms and conditions of the contract is breached.
