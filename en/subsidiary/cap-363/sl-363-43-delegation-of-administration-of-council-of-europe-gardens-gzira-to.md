# Delegation of Administration of Council of Europe Gardens Gzira to Gzira Local Council Order (S.L. 363.43)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.43**

**DELEGATION OF ADMINISTRATION OF**

**COUNCIL OF EUROPE GARDENS G|IRA TO**

**G|IRA LOCAL COUNCIL ORDER**

7th April, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 61 of 2000.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Administration of Council of Europe Gardens G\\ira to G\\ira Local Council Order.

## 2. Delegation of functions.

The land described in article 3 of this Order in terms of the Lease Agreement for the Administration and Use of Council of Europe Gardens G\\ira by G\\ira Local Council signed on the 8th March, 2000, is hereby being specified for the purposes of the delegation of functions mentioned in article 33(1)(m) of the Local Government Act, as form the date of the publication of this Order.

*Cap. 363.*

## 3. Description.

The land referred to in article 2 consists of an area of lands, shown bordered in red on plan L.D. 185/99 attached to the Lease Agreement mentioned in article 2.
