# Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Isla Local Council Order (S.L. 363.132)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.132**

**DELEGATION OF ADMINISTRATION OF**

**FACILITIES TO ISLA LOCAL COUNCIL ORDER**

6th July, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 176 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Isla Local Council Order.

## 2. Delegation of functions. Cap. 363.

The property described in article 3 of this Order is hereby being specified for the purposes of the delegation of functions mentioned in article 33(1)(m) of the Local Government Act, as from the date of the publication of this order.

## 3. Description.

The property in the locality of Isla referred to in article 2 of the order consists of Gardjola Gardens in Bastion Street Senglea as shown bordered on the attached plan to the Lease Agreement for the Administration and Use of the Gardjola Gardens in Bastion Street Senglea, by Isla Local Council signed by the Director Land on behalf of the Government of Malta and the Mayor and the Executive Secretary of the Isla Local Council on the 7th February 2000.
