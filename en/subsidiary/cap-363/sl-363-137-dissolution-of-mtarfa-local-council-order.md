# Dissolution of Mtarfa Local Council Order (S.L. 363.137)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.137**

**DISSOLUTION OF MTARFA LOCAL COUNCIL**

**ORDER**

23rd April, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 123 of 2008.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Dissolution of Mtarfa Local Council Order.

## 2. Dissolution of Mtarfa Local Council. Cap. 363.

The Mtarfa Local Council is hereby being dissolved due to a persistent non-regard to the provisions of article 22(b) of the Local Councils Act.
