# Control of Pigeons (Munxar Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.236)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.236**

**CONTROL OF PIGEONS (MUNXAR LOCAL**

**COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

23rd September, 2022

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 42/04/2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Control of Pigeons (Munxar Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"Council" means the Munxar Local Council;

"domestic pigeons" means pigeons which are kept in a residential place or in some other place for the purpose of recreation, hobby or sport;

"locality" means the locality of Munxar as indicated in the Second Schedule to the Act;

"wild pigeons" means pigeons which are not domestic pigeons.

## 3.1. The feeding of wild pigeons is prohibited.

The feeding of wild pigeons by any person in any part of the locality is prohibited.

## 3.2.

It is also prohibited for a person to voluntarily leave food in places which are accessible to wild pigeons.

## 3.3.

The provisions of this bye-law shall not apply to those persons who keep or take care of domestic pigeons.

## 4. Abandoned immovable property.

The owners of abandoned immovable property within the locality shall ensure that the said property be sealed shut in such a manner that no wild pigeons can go in or make nests therein.

## 5. Penalties.

Any person who fails to comply with any provision of these Bye-Laws shall be deemed to have committed a contravention and shall, on conviction, be liable, unless a higher penalty is applicable under any other law:

(a) for a first contravention, to a fine (ammenda) of forty-six euro and fifty-nine cents (€46.59);

(b) for a second or subsequent contravention, to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88); and

(c) in the case of a continuous contravention, to a fine (ammenda) of eleven euro and sixty-five cents (€11.65) for each day that the contravention continues.
