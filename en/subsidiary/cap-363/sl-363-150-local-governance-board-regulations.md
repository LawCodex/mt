# Local Governance Board Regulations (S.L. 363.150)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.150**

**LOCAL GOVERNANCE BOARD REGULATIONS**

2nd February, 2010

LEGAL NOTICE 63 of 2010.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Local Governance Board Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"the Board" means the Local Governance Board established by means of these regulations;

"the Department" means the Department for Local Government;

"the Director” means the Director for Local Government;

"Elected representative" includes any Mayor, Deputy Mayor, Councillor or any elected representative in any Regional Committee or Administrative Committee;

"Executive Secretary" includes the Secretary to any Regional Committee, Local Council or Administrative Committee;

"Local Government" means any Regional Committee, Local Council or Administrative Committee;

"maladministration" includes decisions taken by any elected representative or any official of any Regional Committee, Local Council or Administrative Committee that are taken after an unreasonable time without any justification, non consistent procedures adopted under similar circumstances, the provision of wrong information or documentation, or decisions that are not according to law or statutory procedures, any action the implementation of which causes undue inconvenience or any abuse of discretion;

"the Minister" means the Minister responsible for Local Government;

"official" includes any official of any Regional Committee, Local Council or Administrative Committee.

## 3. Establishment of a Local Governance Board.

There shall be established a Board to be called the Local Governance Board having its constitution and functions as established by these regulations.

## 4. Constitution.

(1) The Board, which shall be established by the Minister, shall be constituted a representative of the Minister who shall be the Chairperson and two other members, with the possibility of appointing up to two substitute members.

*Conflict of interest.*

(2) No person shall be appointed on the Board if he holds office in the Department, the Local Councils Association, the Association of Maltese Local Council Executive Secretaries, any Regional Committee, Local Council or Administrative Committee, in order to avoid any real or potential conflict of interest.

*Board Secretary.*

(3) The Board’s Secretary shall be an officer in the public sector.

*Oath of office.*

(4) The members and Board Secretary shall, on appointment, prescribe to the oath of office before a Commissioner for Justice, in the form prescribed in the Schedule.

## 5. Functions of the Board.

The Board shall have the functions to investigate and decide on any issue referred to it regarding:

(a) any alleged abuse or bad decision or maladministration committed by any elected representative, Executive Secretary, or any other official, which offence is not one of a criminal nature;

(b) any alleged breach of the Code of Ethics for Elected Representatives in force at the time of the alleged breach;

(c) any complaint by any member of the public against any Elected Representative, Executive Secretary or any official with respect to any maladministration;

(d) any complaint from any public official or public or private entity against any Elected Representative, Executive Secretary or any official of any maladministration;

(e) any complaint from a member of the public, public official or any public or private entity against any decision of a local government on any decision which he deems to constitute any irregularity, injustice or abuse not being of a criminal nature.

## 6. Method of lodging a complaint.

Any person or entity that lodges a complaint shall address such complaint to the Chairperson, Board of Local Governance.

## 7. Powers of the Board.

(1) In executing its functions, the Board may adopt such procedure that it deems fit.

(2) The Board Secretary shall write down the decisions taken by the Board members and make a summary of the discussion without the need to identify the members and their opinions. The decision shall be taken by a simple majority.

(3) Any member of the Board, may, if he does not agree with the final decision of the Board, submit to the Board a minority report wherein he gives his reasons for not agreeing with the final decision of the Board.

(4) In carrying out its investigation, the Board shall have the power to -

(a) convene persons to appear before it and give information on oath in any place, day and time as it may determine;

(b) cause any person having any document relevant to the investigation to present that document;

(c) inspect in any manner and in any place which it may determine, and keep extracts from any books, accounts or documents in a printed or any other manner.

## 8. Board decisions to be communicated to the Director.

(1) The decision of the Board together with the recommendation on the action to be taken shall be communicated to the Director.

(2) The Director may also request a clarification from the Board before any action is taken.
