# Consumption of Alcoholic Beverages in Public Places (Gżira Local Council) Bye- Laws (S.L. 363.182)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.182**

**CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES**

**IN PUBLIC PLACES (GŻIRA LOCAL COUNCIL)**

**BYE-LAWS**

29th September, 2017

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 21/3/2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Consumption of Alcoholic Beverages in Public Places (Gżira Local Council) Bye- Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"containers of alcoholic beverages" includes bottles, glasses, cans and or any other container intended for the serving of any beverages;

"Council" means the Gżira Local Council;

"designated areas" means those areas designated by the competent authorities for the placement of tables and chairs exclusively for the consumption of food and beverages;

"locality" means the locality of Gżira as delineated in the Act;

"public place" means the streets, pavements, open spaces, squares, gardens, shores and promenade within the Gżira locality;

## 3. Prohibition of loitering with alcoholic beverages.

No person is permitted to loiter in any public place holding or having on his person any alcoholic beverages.

## 4. Prohibition of consumption of alcohol in public places.

No person is permitted to consume alcohol in public places other than in the designated areas.

## 5. Notice and provision of containers.

Any commercial establishment that operates in the locality of Gżira, selling beverages, shall affix a notice in Maltese as well as in English as shown in Schedule A, which notice shall be placed in a conspicuous place and be easily legible by the patrons of such commercial establishment.

## 6. Penalties.

(a) Any owner of an establishment, who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70) for such contravention, and to a further fine (ammenda) of fifteen euro (€15) for every day during which any of the provisions of these Bye- Laws is breached.

(b) Any person who is in breach of any of the provisions of these bye-laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine of seventy euro (€70).

## SCHEDULE

(Bye-Law 5)

THE CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES OUTDOORS IS PROHIBITED BY LAW.

THE CARRYING OF ALCOHOL IN THE STREETS OTHER THAN IN SEALED UNOPENED CONTAINERS IS PROHIBITED BY LAW
