# Consumption of Alcoholic Beverages in Public Places (San Pawl il-Baħar Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.228)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.228**

**CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES IN**

**PUBLIC PLACES (SAN PAWL IL-BAĦAR LOCAL**

**COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

7th May, 2021

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 55/05/2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Consumption of Alcoholic Beverages in Public Places (San Pawl il-Baħar Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"containers of alcoholic beverages" includes bottles, glasses, cans, and any other container intended intended for the serving of any beverages;

"Council" means the San Pawl il-Baħar Local Council;

"locality" means the locality of San Pawl il-Baħar as shown in the Second Schedule to the Act;

"mentioned spaces" covers those spaces mentioned by the competent authorities to occupy with tables and chairs to serve and consume food and beverages;

"public space" means the streets, pavements, open spaces, squares, gardens, shores and promenade within the locality but excluding non-urban open spaces which are not in the vicinity of residential areas.

## 3. Prohibition of loitering with alcoholic beverages.

No one is permitted to loiter in any public place holding or having on his person any alcoholic beverages.

## 4. Prohibition of consumption of alcohol in public places.

No one is permitted to consume alcohol in public places other than in the designated areas.

## 5. Notice and provision of containers.

Any commercial establishment that operates in the locality of San Pawl il-Baħar, selling beverages shall affix a notice in Maltese as well as in English as shown in the Schedule, which shall be provided from the local council, which notice shall be placed in a conspicuous place and be easily legible by the patrons of such commercial establishment. Anyone organising an activity in San Pawl il-Baħar has to conform with these Bye-Laws and obtain the necessary permits from the San Pawl il-Baħar Local Council.

*Penalties.*

6.1 Any owner of an establishment who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70) for each contravention, and to a further fine (ammenda) of fifteen euro (€15) for every day during which any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws is breached.

6.2 Any person who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70).

## 7. Exemption.

The San Pawl il-Baħar Local Council on special occasions shall have the right to give exemptions to these Bye-Laws, which are to be given in writing after an application is filed citing the conditions of these Bye-laws to be exempted in such special occasions.
