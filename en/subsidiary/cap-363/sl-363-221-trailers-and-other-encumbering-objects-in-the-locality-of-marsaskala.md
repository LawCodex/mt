# Trailers and Other Encumbering Objects in the Locality of Marsaskala (Marsaskala Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.221)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.221**

TRAILERS AND OTHER ENCUMBERING OBJECTS IN THE LOCALITY OF MARSASKALA (MARSASKALA

**LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

10th July, 2020

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 35/02/2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is Trailers and Other Encumbering Objects in the Locality of Marsaskala (Marsaskala Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"the Council" means the Marsaskala Local Council;

"encumbering object" means any object which although not necessarily deemed to be waste, causes obstruction or nuisance or inconvenience in any part of the locality, or any other object of a size or quantity or accumulation, when considered holistically, causes a nuisance, or which may lead to deterioration or defacement of a public place;

"locality" means the locality of Marsaskala as defined in the Act;

"sea craft" means any boat, ship, yacht or every description of vessel used in navigation, whether it works with oars, sails or by other means, or whether self-propelled or not, and it includes barges, oil rigs, pontoons, seaplanes and any other crafts and similar vessels;

"trailer" means any vehicle without mechanical power of its own designed to be drawn by a motor vehicle and intended for the purpose of the carriage of boats and other sea crafts or for the conveyance of goods.

*Restrictions on trailers and other encumbering objects.*

3.1 No person shall in any part of the locality, place or leave on land, any trailer or other encumbering object, including any skip which is not meant for domestic use, and no person shall leave or park, whether temporarily or otherwise, or leave unattended any trailer, loaded or not in any part of the locality save in an area that is designated as a site for the parking of trailers or on private land.

3.2 Notwithstanding the provisions of these Bye-Laws, any person may place any trailer in any part of the locality to unload therefrom any boat or other sea-crafts:

Provided that, such trailer shall be removed immediately once the objects that would be conveyed thereon would be removed.

## 4. Means of identification.

Any trailer or other encumbering object which passes through or is parked within the locality shall have a clear means of identification which identifies the person responsible for the said trailer or encumbering object.

## 5. Fishermens’ zones.

Sites identified as fishermen’s zones shall only be used in accordance with the relative permits and in accordance with any law in force that pertains to fishermens’ zones and the granting of permits. Any other use shall be subject to these Bye-Laws.

## 6. Penalties.

Any person who contravenes any provision of these Bye- Laws, shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70) for such contravention together with a further ammenda of ten euro (€10) for each day during which such contravention of these Bye-Laws would have ensued.

## 7. Removal.

The penalty referred to in Bye-Law 6 shall be without prejudice to any other right of action which the Council or any other authority may take to remove, deposit, or dispose or any trailer or any other encumbering object or any accessory thereto in terms of the provisions of the Clamping and Removal of Motor Vehicles and Encumbering Objects Regulations, or any other law, and without prejudice to any other penalty or fine (ammenda) which may be imposed under any other law.
