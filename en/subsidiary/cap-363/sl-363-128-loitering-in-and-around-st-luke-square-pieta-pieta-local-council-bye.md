# Loitering in and around St. Luke Square, Pietà (Pietà Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.128)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.128**

LOITERING IN AND AROUND ST. LUKE SQUARE PIETÀ (PIETÀ LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS 10th August, 2006

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 47/1/2006, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Loitering in and around St. Luke Square, Pietà (Pietà Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"Council" means the Pietà Local Council;

"locality" means the locality of Pietà as shown in the Second Schedule to the Act;

"loitering" means the persistent presence of any person for no apparent reason, however excluding persons who are carrying out a duty in St. Luke Square, Pietà, or in the vicinty thereof.

## 3. No loitering.

No person shall loiter in St. Luke Square, Pietà or in the vicinity thereof.

## 4. Penalty.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

Any person who is found loitering in St. Luke Square, Pietà or in the vicinity thereof, shall be guilty of a contravention and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (69.88).
