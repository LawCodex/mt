# Beverages in Glass Containers and Consumption of Alcohol in Public Places (San Giljan Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.113)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.113**

**BEVERAGES IN GLASS CONTAINERS AND**

CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOL IN PUBLIC PLACES (SAN ĠILJAN LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS

1st August, 2008

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 52/1/2008, as amended by Local Council Bye-Law 52/11/2019.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Beverages in Glass Containers and Consumption of Alcohol in Public Places (San Ġiljan Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.C.B.L. 52/11/ 2019.*

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"Council" means the San Ġiljan Local Council;

"designated areas" means those areas designated by the competent authorities for the placement of tables and chairs exclusively for the consumption of food and beverages;

"glass container" includes glass bottles, glasses, and, or any other glass container intended for the serving of any beverages;

"locality" means the locality of San Ġiljan as delineated in the Act;

"public place" means all the streets within the San Ġiljan locality;

"streets" means all the streets within the San Ġiljan locality.

## 3. Prohibition of loitering with glass containers.

No person is permitted to loiter in any public place holding or having on his person any glass container.

## 4. Prohibition of carrying of alcohol in public places.

No person is permitted to carry alcohol in the public place other than in a sealed unopened container.

## 5. Prohibition of consumption of alcohol in public places.

No person is permitted to consume alcohol in public places other than in the designated areas.

## 6. Notice and provision of containers.

Any commercial establishment situated within any street or public place in the locality selling beverages, shall -

*Amended by: L.C.B.L. 52/11/ 2019.*

(a) affix a notice in Maltese as well as in English as shown in Schedule A, which notice shall be placed in a conspicuous place and be easily legible by the patrons of such commercial establishment; and

(b) provide containers made of a material other than glass if such beverage is to be consumed in any public place. 7. Any person who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of sixty- five euro (€65.00) for such contravention, and to a further fine (ammenda) of ten euro (€10.00) for every day during which any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws is breached.
