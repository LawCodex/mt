# Controlled Parking Schemes (Residents and Commercial) Regulations (S.L. 363.80)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.80**

CONTROLLED PARKING SCHEMES (RESIDENTS

**AND COMMERCIAL) REGULATIONS**

26th November, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 293 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notices 425 of 2007, 200 of 2009, 76 of 2011, 157 of 2013 and 345 of 2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Controlled Parking Schemes (Residents and Commercial) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 345 of 2018.*

In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires - "commercial vehicle" means any vehicle carrying goods of more than 3.5 tonnes weight and includes any truck, van, pickup, mechanical horse, trailer, concrete mixer, bowser and crane; passenger transport vehicles, including any coach, bus, minibus, mini-van and taxi; car-hire vehicles, whether chauffeur driven or rented; commercially registered vehicles; towed vehicles, including boats on a trailer, other trailers and caravans;

"controlled zone" means a zone where the holder of a residents’ permit issued to him in respect of his vehicle by the Local Council of a locality within which the zone lies, lives, and in respect of which there apply such parking conditions as are set out in these regulations;

*Cap. 363.*

"Local Council" means a local government council established in terms of the Local Government Act, and "Council locality" shall be construed as a locality governed by a Local Council;

"non-permitted vehicle" means a vehicle which is not a permitted vehicle in a particular Council locality;

"occupied space" means the space occupied by a non-permitted vehicle for a restrictive prescribed period as may be indicated in a controlled zone during a prescribed period and which, along with any other space within the controlled zone, may not be used again by the same vehicle during such prescribed period before the expiry of the corresponding stay-away prescribed period;

"parking disc" means a cardboard device as indicated in the Fourth Schedule containing a revolving disc which shows, through a window in the outer cover, a twelve-hour clock with indicators for segments of 15 minutes each inscribed on the inner disc, or any other automatic parking disc indicating the correct auto park time;

"permitted vehicle" means a vehicle which belongs to or is normally driven by a person holding a residents’ permit, and which may be parked without any restriction within normal lawful limits at any time and for any duration in a controlled zone or zones in respect of which the residents’ permit is issued;

"prescribed period" means such period as is prescribed in each controlled zone as relating to -

(a) the number of minutes a non-permitted vehicle may park in an occupied space without incurring any contravention, also referred to as the restrictive prescribed period;

(b) the number of minutes such non-permitted vehicle shall stay away from the occupied space before returning to the same controlled zone, also referred to as the stay-away prescribed period;

(c) the days of the week and the times thereof during which restricted parking for non-permitted vehicles applies;

"residents’ permit" means a permit as indicated in the Second Schedule issued to a resident of a controlled zone in respect of a registered vehicle, which is not a commercial vehicle or a motor-cycle, which is owned or driven by such resident, and which renders his vehicle a permitted vehicle;

"traffic sign" means a traffic sign as indicated in the Third Schedule and containing the following elements, namely an indication "P" that the sign relates to parking in a timed parking zone, indicating the zone, the parking time allowed and the no-return time, the prescribed period of restricted parking and an indication that all other days and times are unrestricted, the kerbside length of the restricted areas, a note about the non-applicability to particular holders of a residents’ permit of one or more controlled zones, a graphical representation of the parking/ disc, the coat-of arms of the Local Council or Local Councils of the locality or localities.

## 3. Controlled zones.

*Amended by: L.N. 200 of 2009.*

(1) There may be established any one or more controlled zones within any Council locality as may be indicated in the First Schedule within which -

(a) a resident of such zone to whom a residents’ permit has been issued in respect of his vehicle as shown in the Second Schedule, may always park such permitted vehicle without any restriction as to occupied space and prescribed periods;

(b) a resident of such zone to whom a residents’ permit has been issued in respect of his vehicle as shown in the said Second Schedule, may always park such permitted vehicle either within such zone or any other zone within the same Council locality without any restriction as to occupied space and prescribed periods;

(c) the driver of a non-permitted vehicle may not park such vehicle during any prescribed period indicated in the said First Schedule and as shown by way of example in the Third Schedule unless he abides by the provisions of these regulations.

(2) The driver of any motor-cycle, whether such vehicle pertains to the holder of a residents’ permit or not, may always park his vehicle within a zone in an unrestricted manner.

## 4. Residential permit.

(1) The Local Council shall issue a residents’ permit to those bona fide residents and owners of validly registered vehicles residing in a controlled zone within the Council locality that satisfy the criteria for eligibility established by an ad hoc order of either the Minister responsible for transport or the Minister responsible for local government duly communicated to the Local Council or the Local Councils.

(2) Such permit, as indicated in the Second Schedule, shall essentially indicate the coat-of arms and the locality of the Local Council issuing it, the registration number of the permitted vehicle and the controlled zone or zones for which it is applicable. A permit number shall also be included.

(3) The residents’ permit shall be fixed close to the road licence on the left-hand side of the windscreen of the vehicle having the same registration number as that shown on the permit.

(4) For the purposes of these regulations a resident is the owner or driver of a vehicle living in premises within a controlled zone for 186 days or more in a year, being the premises normally declared as such in the current electoral register.

(5) Upon proof being given that a person who has not received a permit is a bona fide resident of a controlled zone and satisfies the criteria duly established as prescribed in sub-regulation (1), the Local Council shall forthwith issue him with a permit.

(6) A residents’ permit shall be valid for two years, and it may be renewed at the Licensing and Testing Department at any time after its expiry together with the road licence when its renewal is next due.

(7) For the purpose of these regulations, a disabled person’s Blue Sticker issued by the National Commission Persons with Disability, or any other internationally recognised signs indicating a disability, shall be considered to be equivalent to a residents’ permit.

## 5. Parking of non-permitted vehicles. S.L. 65.11

Subject to the provisions of regulations 78 to 80 of the Motor Vehicles Regulations, no person shall park any motor vehicle in a controlled zone within the prescribed period unless - (a) such vehicle is a permitted vehicle whose owner or driver has a valid residents’ permit issued by the Local Council of a locality where such owner or driver resides, and such permit is fixed on the left-hand side of the windscreen in such a position as to be visible from the front;

(b) such person driving or owning a non-permitted vehicle conforms with the specific provisions for a controlled zone as may be indicated in the First Schedule, on a traffic sign and on the backside on the outer cover of the parking disc.

## 6. Use of parking disc.

*Amended by: L.N. 345 of 2018.*

(1) Whenever the driver of a non-permitted vehicle parks such vehicle within a controlled zone, whether during a prescribed period or at any other time, he shall set the parking disc to show as correctly as possible the time of his leaving the vehicle parked in an occupied space.

(2) The parking disc, either as portrayed in the Fourth Schedule or the automatic one, shall be displayed on the surface of the dashboard in a clear and visible position from the outer side the windscreen.

(3) A non-permitted vehicle shall not remain parked during a prescribed period for a time exceeding the restrictive prescribed period indicated in the First Schedule for such controlled zone and shown on the traffic sign affixed in the area.

(4) A non-permitted vehicle shall not return to the same controlled zone during a prescribed period for a time exceeding the stay-away prescribed period indicated in the First Schedule for such controlled zone and shown on the traffic sign affixed in the area.

## 7. Offences.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

Any person who, during a prescribed time and in a controlled zone -

(a) parks or is the owner of a permitted vehicle which is parked and on which a residents’ permit for that zone or any other permitted zone, a Blue Sticker issued by the National Commission Persons with Disability, or any other internationally recognised sign indicating a disability, are not clearly displayed, or

(b) parks or is the owner of a non-permitted vehicle which is parked and inside which a parking disc is not clearly displayed, or

(c) parks or is the owner of a vehicle which is parked and on which neither any valid residents’ permit nor a parking disc are displayed, or

(d) parks or is the owner of a non-permitted vehicle which is parked and has an incorrect parking time indicated on the parking disc, or

(e) parks or is the owner of a non-permitted vehicle which is parked for a period exceeding the restrictive prescribed period, or

(f) returns to park or is the owner of a non-permitted vehicle which is again parked in the same controlled zone within the stay-away prescribed period,

shall be guilty of an offence and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of not less than twenty-three euro and twenty-nine cents (23.29) and not exceeding fifty-eight euro and twenty-three cents (58.23) for any of the offences mentioned in this regulation.
