# Control of Solid Discharge by Horses (General Application) Regulations (S.L. 363.86)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.86**

**CONTROL OF SOLID DISCHARGE BY HORSES**

**(GENERAL APPLICATION) REGULATIONS**

15th April, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 67 of 2002, as amended by Legal Notices 425 of 2007 and 426 of 2012.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Control of Solid Discharge by Horses (General Application) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires - "Act" means the Local Government Act;

*Cap. 363.*

"authorised bag" means a bag authorised by the Department designed as shown in Schedule B and supplied by the Council, which is to be tied to the horse and to the karrozzin so as to collect and keep the horse droppings;

"closed skip" means a closed recipient, be it on wheels or not, metal or otherwise, provided by the Council in the locality normally used by the karrozzin, and such skip shall be marked to be used specifically for the disposal of horse solid discharge, and shall be placed in a site as may be authorised by the Council;

"Council" means Local Council as defined in the Act;

"Department" means the Department for Local Councils;

"karrozzin" means a horse drawn cab licensed for the conveyance of persons and pulled by a horse;

"locality" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Local Government;

"street" includes any road, alley, square, bridge, quay or other place of public passage or access.

## 3. Application for authorised bag.

(1) Without prejudice to any other licence required for the use of a karrozzin on the road, a person shall apply to the Council of the locality where the karrozzin normally is in use to be provided with such authorised bag for a fee of twenty three euro twenty-nine cents (€23.29).

(2) The design, the measurements, the colour and the material used in the production of the authorised bag shall be those indicated in Schedule B.

(3) The authorised bag shall at all times be tied to the horse and to the karrozzin so as to avoid horse droppings from falling onto to the road.

(4) The application for the authorised bag shall be submitted on the form prescribed in Schedule A and the authorised bag shall be provided by the Council after the application has been submitted.

## 4. Use of authorised bag.

Any person who has a karozzin under his control in any street shall at all times ensure that the authorised bag is in good condition, and also is securely tied to the horse and to the karozzin so as to avoid at all times solid discharge on the street.

## 5. Disposal of solid waste in the provided skips.

The solid waste collected in the authorised bag shall be placed in a plastic bag and disposed of by the person who has the karozzin under his control, in a closed skip provided for this purpose by the Council where normally the karozzin is in use.

## 6. Penalties and fines.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

Any person who is in breach of any of the provisions of these regulations shall be guilty of a contravention and shall be liable, if found guilty, unless a higher penalty is applicable under any other law:

(a) for the first contravention, to a fine (ammenda) of twenty-three euro and twenty-nine cents (23.29);

(b) for the second or subsequent contravention, to a fine (ammenda) of forty-six euro and fifty-nine cents (46.59).
