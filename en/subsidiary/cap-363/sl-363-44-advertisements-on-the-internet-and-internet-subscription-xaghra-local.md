# Advertisements on the Internet and Internet Subscription (Xaghra Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.44)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.44**

**ADVERTISEMENTS ON THE INTERNET**

**AND INTERNET SUBSCRIPTION**

**(XAG}RA LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

7th May, 2000

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 63/1/2000, as amended by Legal notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Advertisements on the Internet and Internet Subscription (Xag]ra Local Council) Bye- Laws.

*Interpretation.*

2.1 In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"advertisement" includes any commercial or non-commercial promotion;

"Council" means the Xag]ra Local Council;

"person" means the person who has been authorised by the Council to link with the Council’s Web-page under the provisions of bye-law 3.2;

"Web-page" means access to information and database via the Internet, known as the World Wide Web (WWW), which Web-page may be advertised on the so-called Web Search Engines.

2.2 In these Bye-Laws, any expression which is defined in the Act shall have the same meaning as is assigned to it in the Act.

*Official Council Web-page.*

3.1 The Council shall have a Web-page on the Internet which shall include all information on the Council and the locality which the Council may choose to include.

3.2 There shall be one official Web-page dedicated exclusively to the Xag]ra Local Council:

Provided that the Council may authorise any person to include or link such Web-page on or to another Web-page, either at a charge or at no charge, as the Council may decide.

*Authorisation in writing.*

3.3 The authorisation referred to in bye-law 3.2 shall be provided in writing by the Council.

*Penalties. Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

3.4 Every person who is found guilty of having breached any provision of bye-laws 3.2 and 3.3 shall be liable to a fine (ammenda) of forty-six euro and fifty-nine cents (€46.59) together with two euro and thirty-three cents (€2.33) for every day during which the Council’s Web-page is accessible on the offender’s Web-page without the written authorisation.

*Authorisation for advertising on the Council’s Web-page.*

4.1 The Council may contract, in accordance with the regulations and procedures applicable according to this Act, with commercial and non-commercial entities to advertise the Council’s Web-page at a charge.

4.2 The contract mentioned in bye-law 4.1 is valid for a period of one year which may be renewed for a further period of one year. The Council reserves the right to renew such contract, and if such renewal is refused, it shall give in writing the reasons for its refusal to any person concerned.

## 5. Subscription rates.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

The Council is introducing a scheme of Internet membership at the rate of €11.65 per year and provision of service at the rate of forty-seven cents (0.47) for every fifteen minute period. Non-members will be charged at the rate of ninety-three cents (0.93) for every fifteen minute period.
