# Delegation of Function to Birgu Local Council (Water Services Corporation Utility Bills) Order (S.L. 363.124)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.124**

**DELEGATION OF FUNCTION TO BIRGU LOCAL**

**COUNCIL (WATER SERVICES CORPORATION**

**UTILITY BILLS) ORDER**

17th June, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 229 of 2005.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Function to Birgu Local Council (Water Services Corporation Utility Bills), Order.

## 2. Delegation to take effect.

The Minister has directed that Birgu Local Council shall be delegated the function to collect, on behalf of the Water Services Corporation, payments of utility bills issued by the Water Services Corporation.
