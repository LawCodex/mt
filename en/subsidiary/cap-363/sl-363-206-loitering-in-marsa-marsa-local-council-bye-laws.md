# Loitering in Marsa (Marsa Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.206)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.206**

**LOITERING IN MARSA**

**(MARSA LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

21st June, 2018

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 34/15/2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Loitering in Marsa (Marsa Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

''the Act'' means the Local Government Act;

''Council'' means the Marsa Local Council;

*Cap. 363.*

''locality'' means the locality of Marsa as shown in the Second Schedule to the Local Government Act;

''loitering'' means the persistent presence of any person for no apparent reason;

*Cap. 363.*

''street'' shall have the same meaning as given to it in the Local Government Act.

## 3. No loitering.

No person shall loiter within any street in Marsa.

## 4. Penalties.

Any person who is found loitering in any street in Marsa, shall be guilty of a contravention and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-five euro (€65) for such contravention, and to a further fine (ammenda) not exceeding fifteen euro (€15) for every day during which one of the provisions of these Bye-Laws is breached.
