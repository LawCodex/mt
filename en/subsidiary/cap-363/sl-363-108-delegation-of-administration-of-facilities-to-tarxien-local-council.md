# Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Tarxien Local Council Order (S.L. 363.108)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.108**

**DELEGATION OF ADMINISTRATION OF**

**FACILITIES TO TARXIEN LOCAL COUNCIL**

**ORDER**

27th February, 2004

LEGAL NOTICES 106 and 265 of 2004, consolidated.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Tarxien Local Council Order.

## 2. Delegation of functions. Cap. 363.

The properties described in the Schedule are hereby being specified for the purposes of the delegation of functions mentioned in article 33(1)(m) of the Local Government Act, as from the dates therein specified.

## SCHEDULE

(Article 2)

**Description of property**

As from 27th February, 2004:

The property in the locality of Tarxien referred to in article 2 consists of a public garden, including football ground and public convenience in Saint Mary Street, Tarxien as shown bordered red on plan P.D. 132/2000 attached to the Lease Agreement for the Administration and Use of Public Garden, including football ground and public convenience in Saint Mary Street, Tarxien, by the Tarxien Local Council signed by the Director Land on behalf of the Government of Malta and the Mayor and Executive Secretary of the Tarxien Local Council on the 25th September, 2003.

As from 7th May, 2004:

The property in the locality of Tarxien referred to in article 2 consists of the premises known as Dar tal-Kejka in Xintill Street, Tarxien as shown bordered red on plan P.D. 144/93/A attached to the Lease Agreement for the Administration and Use of Premises known as Dar tal-Kejka, in Xintill Street, Tarxien, by the Tarxien Local Council signed by the Director Land on behalf of the Government of Malta and the Mayor and Executive Secretary of the Tarxien Local Council on the 28th September, 2000.
