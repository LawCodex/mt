# Delegation of Administration of Garden of Serenity to Santa Lucija Local Council Order (S.L. 363.42)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.42**

DELEGATION OF ADMINISTRATION OF GARDEN OF SERENITY TO SANTA LU~IJA LOCAL

**COUNCIL ORDER**

29th February, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 33 of 2000.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Administration of Garden of Serenity to Santa Luċija Local Council Order.

## 2. Delegation of functions.

The land described in article 3 of this Order in terms of the Lease Agreement for the Administration and Use of Garden of Serenity at Santa Luċija by the Santa Luċija Local Council signed on the 10th March, 1999, is hereby being specified for the purposes of the delegation of functions mentioned in article 33(1)(m) of the Local Government Act, as from the date of the publication of this Order.

*Cap. 363.*

## 3. Description.

The land referred to in article 2 consists of an area of lands, including any building thereon, at Santa Lu`ija, shown bordered in red on plan L.D. 158/81/1 attached to the Lease Agreement mentioned in article 2.
