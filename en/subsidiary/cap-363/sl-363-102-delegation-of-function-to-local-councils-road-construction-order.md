# Delegation of Function to Local Councils (Road Construction) Order (S.L. 363.102)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.102**

**DELEGATION OF FUNCTION TO LOCAL**

**COUNCILS (ROAD CONSTRUCTION) ORDER**

8th August, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 197 of 2003, as amended by Legal Notices 403 of 2003 and 148 of 2004; and Act XV of 2009.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Function to Local Councils (Road Construction) Order.

## 2. Delegation to take effect.

*Amended by: XV. 2009.49.*

The delegation by the Authority for Transport in Malta to complete the agreed works on the roads listed in an Agreement entered into between each Local Council and the said Authority shall take effect in relation to each Council in terms of the Agreement referred to in the published deed as from the effective date of delegation shown against the relative Local Council in the Schedule.
