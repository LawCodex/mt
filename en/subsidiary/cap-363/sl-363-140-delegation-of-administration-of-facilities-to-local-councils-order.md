# Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Local Councils Order (S.L. 363.140)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.140**

**DELEGATION OF ADMINISTRATION OF**

**FACILITIES TO LOCAL COUNCIL ORDER**

3rd October, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 245 of 2008.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Local Councils Order.

## 2. Delegation of functions. Cap. 363.

The property described in articles 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 of this Order is hereby being specified for the purposes of the delegation of functions mentioned in article 33(1)(m) of the Local Government Act, as from the date of the publication of this Order.
