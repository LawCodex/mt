# Protection of Wied id-Dis Bridge (Gharghur Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.83)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.83**

**PROTECTION OF WIED ID-DIS BRIDGE**

**(G}ARG}UR LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

15th February, 2002

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 24/1/2002, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Protection of Wied id- Dis Bridge (G]arg]ur Local Council) Bye-Laws.

*Interpretation.*

2.1 In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires - "Act" means the Local Government Act;

*Cap. 363.*

"advertisement" means any type of advertisement, including any commercial or non-commercial promotion, and includes also any other notice, notification or message printed or in writing;

"Council" means the G]arg]ur Local Council;

"locality" means the G]arg]ur locality;

"street furniture" includes any object which may be placed or fixed to the ground or placed or fixed to the wall or to any other part of the Bridge;

"the Bridge" means the bridge over Wied id-Dis within the locality of G]arg]ur, and includes the walls, fixtures and the road which runs over the bridge;

"vehicle" includes any motor car, motor cycle or any other vehicle which is used as a means of transport, whether it is a motor vehicle or not, but does not include bicycle or scooter.

## 3. Prohibitions.

No person shall, without prior written authorisation from the Council:

(a) ascend or abseil, by means of ropes or other means, at any part of the Bridge;

(b) make any type of ties to the Bridge with ropes or by any other means;

(c) fix any board, banner, sticker or advertisement to any part of the Bridge;

(d) fix or place any street furniture to or on the Bridge;

(e) park any vehicle on the Bridge.

## 4. Penalties.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

Any person who contravenes or fails to comply to any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall be guilty of a contravention and shall be liable, if found guilty, unless a higher penalty is applicable under any other law:

(a) for the first contravention to a fine (ammenda) of forty-six euro and fifty-nine cents (€46.59);

(b) for the second or subsequent contravention, to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88); and

(c) in the case of a continuing contravention, to a fine (ammenda) of eleven euro and sixty-five cents (€11.65) for every day during which the contravention continues.

## 5. Civil damages.

The imposition of a penalty under these Bye-Laws shall be without prejudice to the right of the Council to recover all civil damages caused by the commission of any contravention under these Bye-Laws, including the right to carry out the repairs at the expense of the guilty person.
