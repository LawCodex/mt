# Delegation of Function to Local Council (Trenching Works) Order (S.L. 363.95)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.95**

**DELEGATION OF FUNCTION TO LOCAL**

**COUNCILS (TRENCHING WORKS) ORDER**

15th October, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 314 of 2002.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Function to Local Councils (Trenching Works) Order.

## 2. Delegation of function. L.N. 441.06

(1) Each Local Council is hereby delegated the function of issuing a licence under regulation 4(1) of the Trenching Works Requiring Permit by Local Councils Regulations, when such proposed works are minor works, and the surface area of the excavation does not exceed 5.00m2 and 1.5m in depth.

(2) Only hand or pneumatic hand tools are to be used during excavation. No mechanical excavators are to be used on this work.
