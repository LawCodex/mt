# Street Furniture on Public Pavements (San Ġiljan Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.166)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.166**

**STREET FURNITURE ON PUBLIC PAVEMENTS**

**(SAN ĠILJAN LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

18th December, 2011\* LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 52/11/2011.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Street Furniture on Public Pavements (San Ġiljan Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires - "the applicant" means that person who applies to the Local Council under the provisions of bye-law 3;

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"furniture" means any benches or any other furniture movable or immovable;

"the Council" means the San Ġiljan Local Council;

"pavements" mean any public pavements including the promenade and the sea shore.

## 3. Prohibition of street furniture on public pavements.

Subject to any permit, authorisation, or licence required under any law, no person shall put up any furniture on pavements in the locality of San Ġiljan without the written autorization of the Council.

3.1 Such authorisation may be granted after a written application to the Council.

## 4. Penalty.

Any person who is in breach of one of the provisions of these Bye-Laws, or any condition of the authorization given by virtue of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of twenty three euro and thirty cents (€23.30) for such contravention.

## 5. Council shall be responsible for street furniture.

The Council reserves the right to remove any furniture set up without the authorization or in breach of any conditions imposed by the Local Council, at the expenses of the person contravening any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws.

\*See bye-law 1.2 of these Bye-Laws, as originally promulgated.
