# Rental of Garages in Civic Centre (Ta’ Kerċem Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.167)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.167**

**RENTAL OF GARAGES IN CIVIC CENTRE**

**(TA’ KERĊEM LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

17th February, 2012\* LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 30/01/2012, as amended by Local Council Bye-Law 30/1/2013.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Rental of Garages in Civic Centre (Ta’ Kerċem Local Council) Bye-Laws.

*Interpretation.*

2.1 In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires - "Act" means the Local Government Act;

*Cap. 363.*

"tenant" means any signatory who is renting the garage;

"the Council" means the Ta’ Kerċem Local Council;

"owner’’ means the Ta’ Kerċem Local Council.

2.2 In these Bye-Laws, any expression which is defined in the Act shall have the same meaning as is assigned to it in the Act.

## 3. Authorisation for rental.

The owner shall sign a contract with the tenant, where the owner gives and grants upon rental in favour of the tenant who accepts in taking the garage and under the same title of rent without its own air space.

## 4. Upgrading of premises.

The lessee shall be solely responsible to pay for expenses of any nature related to upgrading or maintenance of the premises and any such upgrading or maintenance remains in the property to the benefit of the lessor, who shall not be bound to reimburse the lessee for such expenses incurred.

*Call for tender.*

5.1 These garages shall only be rented after a public call for tenders.

*Validity of contract.*

5.2 The contract mentioned in bye-law 3 is valid for a period of five (5) years. After these five (5) years, another call for tenders shall be issued.

## 6. Rates of rental.

*Substituted by: L.C.B.L. 30/1/ 2013.*

The rates of garage rental in the call for tenders shall not be less than:

(a) five hundred euro (€500) for one (1) car garage, for one year;

(b) nine hundred (€900) for three (3) car garage, for one year.

\*See bye-law 1.2 of these Bye-Laws, as originally promulgated.
