# Catering Establishments in Outdoor Space (Tas-Sliema Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.227)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.227**

CATERING ESTABLISHMENTS IN OUTDOOR SPACE (TAS-SLIEMA LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS

7th June, 2021

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 59/04/2021.

## 1.1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is Catering Establishments in Outdoor Space (Tas-Sliema Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2.1. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"ashtrays" means receptacles approved for the disposal of waste relating to cigarettes including but not limited to cigarette butts and placed in a public place;

"catering establishment" shall include any establishment which provides any type of food or beverage to clientele;

"Council" means the Tas-Sliema Local Council;

"outdoor space" means the areas outside the catering establishment including the pavement and outside tables and chairs.

## 2.2.

In these Bye-Laws, any expression which is defined in the Act shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act.

## 3.1. Cleaning and clearing.

Every catering establishment shall be responsible for the cleaning and clearing of any waste deposited in the outside space of their establishment by their customers or by their establishment.

3.2 Every catering establishment servicing clientele in an outdoor space shall put an adequate number of ashtrays in their outside area and shall be responsible for the cleaning and clearing of the ashtrays as well as the appropriate disposal of waste placed in ashtrays.

3.3 Every catering establishment shall ensure that at no time shall ashtrays overflow or be at risk of overflowing.

## 4. Penalties.

If the catering establishment is considered to be in violation of the provisions of these Bye-Laws, it shall be deemed to have committed a contravention and shall on conviction be liable to the punishment of a fine (ammenda) of not less then fifty euro (€50), and in case of a continuing offence to a further fine (ammenda) of not less than ten euro (€10) per day on which the offence continues after conviction.
