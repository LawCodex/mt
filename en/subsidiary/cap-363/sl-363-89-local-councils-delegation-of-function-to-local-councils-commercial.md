# Local Councils (Delegation of Function to Local Councils) (Commercial Actitivities Regulation) Order (S.L. 363.89)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.89**

LOCAL COUNCILS (DELEGATION OF FUNCTION

**TO LOCAL COUNCILS) (COMMERCIAL**

**ACTIVITIES REGULATION) ORDER**

1st January, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 93 of 2002.

## 1. Citation.

(1) The title of this order is the Local Councils (Delegation of Function to Local Councils) (Commercial Activities Regulation) Order.

*Cap. 441.*

(2) This order shall come into force on the 1st January, 2002 and shall have effect in respect of licences that need to be paid on or after the 1st January, 2002 issued by the competent authorities as established in the Trading Licences Act.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this order unless the context otherwise requires:-

*Cap. 441.*

"Act" means the Trading Licences Act;

*Cap. 363.*

"Council" means Local Council as established in the Local Government Act;

"locality" shall have the same meaning assigned to it by the Local Government Act;

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Local Government;

"regulatory authority" shall have the same meaning assigned to it by the Act.

## 3. Regulatory authority licences renewal.

(1) The Minister has directed that Councils shall be delegated the function to collect on behalf of the regulatory authority payment for the renewal of licences pertaining to the regulatory authority leviable under the Trading Licences Act.

*Cap. 441.*

(2) The licences fees mentioned in subarticle (1) shall be paid to the Council of the locality where the relative licence is registered.
