# Local Councils (Street Naming) Regulations (S.L. 363.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.10**

**LOCAL COUNCILS (STREET NAMING)**

**REGULATIONS**

12th December, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 176 of 1995, as amended by Legal Notices 208 of 1997, 255 of 2014, and 33 and 65 of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Local Councils (Street Naming) Regulations.

## 2. Naming of streets within a locality.

*Amended by: L.N. 255 of 2014; L.N. 33 of 2016. Cap. 363.*

The naming or renaming of streets, other than arterial and distributor roads determined by the Structure Plan as specified in article 33(2) of the Local Government Act, shall take place after the Local Council of that locality submits its recommendation to the Minister responsible for the Electoral Office who shall, prior to directing the alteration of the name of any street or giving or approving a name to a new street in terms of article 115 of the Code of Police Laws, refer such recommendation to the appropriate Street Naming Committee for its advice.

*Cap. 10.*

## 3. Naming of arterial and distributor roads.

*Amended by: L.N. 255 of 2014; L.N. 65 of 2016. Cap. 363.*

The naming or renaming of arterial and distributor roads determined by the Structure Plan, national monuments, national parks or gardens, industrial estates, ports, airports and other national territories, establishments, buildings and items listed in the Fourth Schedule to the Local Government Act, and as specified in article 33(2) of the same Act, shall be directed by the Minister responsible for the Electoral Office in terms of article 115 of the Code of Police Laws, who shall act on the recommendation of the appropriate Street Naming Committee after such committee consults all the Local Councils of the localities bordering on the area to be so named.

*Cap. 10.*

## 4. Criteria to be adopted.

*Amended by: L.N. 208 of 1997.*

(1) A Local Council or a Street Naming Committee, as the case may be, shall not submit a recommendation for the alteration of the name of any street within any locality or of any road or area specified in regulation 3 unless a valid reason for such change subsists.

(2) The naming or renaming of streets, roads and any other specified area after persons may only take place after their decease.
