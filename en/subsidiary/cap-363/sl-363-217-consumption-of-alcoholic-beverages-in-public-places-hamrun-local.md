# Consumption of Alcoholic Beverages in Public Places (Ħamrun Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.217)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.217**

**CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES IN**

**PUBLIC PLACES (ĦAMRUN LOCAL COUNCIL)**

**BYE-LAWS**

6th September, 2019

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 27/08/2019.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Consumption of Alcoholic Beverages in Public Places (Ħamrun Local Council) Bye- Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"alcoholic recipient" includes bottles, tins, cans, tumblers and/or any glass recipient intended to contain or serve (alcoholic) beverages;

"the Council" means the Ħamrun Local Council;

"locality" means Ħamrun as shown in the Second Schedule to the Act;

"mentioned spaces" covers those spaces mentioned by the competent authorities to occupy with tables and chairs to serve and consume food and beverages;

"public space" means streets, roads, pavements, squares, public gardens and parks that make part of the limits of Ħamrun.

## 3. Prohibition of loitering with alcoholic beverages.

No one is permitted to loiter in any public space while holding or in possession of alcoholic beverages.

## 4. Prohibition of carrying alcohol in public spaces.

No one is permitted to carry alcoholic beverages unless in a sealed recipient.

## 5. Notice and provision of recipients.

All commercial establishments in Ħamrun selling beverages shall affix a notice in Maltese as well as in English as shown in the Schedule, which notice shall be placed in a conspicuous place and be easily legible by patrons of such commercial establishment.

## 6. Penalties.

(a) Any owner of any establishment who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70) for such contravention, and to a further fine (ammenda) of fifteen euro (€15) for every day during which any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws is breached.

(b) Any person who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70).

## 7. Exemption.

The Ħamrun Local Council holds the rights to exempt from these conditions on special and/or particular occasions.

## SCHEDULE

(Bye-Law 5)

THE CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES OUTDOORS IS PROHIBITED BY LAW.

THE CARRYING OF ALCOHOL IN THE STREETS OTHER THAN IN SEALED UNOPENED CONTAINERS IS PROHIBITED BY LAW.
