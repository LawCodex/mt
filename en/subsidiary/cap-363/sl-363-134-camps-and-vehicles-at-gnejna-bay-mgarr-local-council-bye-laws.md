# Camps and Vehicles at Gnejna Bay (Mgarr Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.134)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.134**

**CAMPS AND VEHICLES AT {NEJNA BAY**

**(M{ARR LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

21st August, 2007

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 38/2/2007, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Camps and Vehicles at {nejna Bay (M[arr Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"the Council" means the M[arr Local Council;

"sand" means the sand at {nejna Bay as shaded in the attached site plan;

"tent" includes all type of tents of any fabric or canvas, camps or any temporary structure and excludes umbrellas;

"vehicle" means any motor vehicle, any two, three or four wheeled vehicle or trailer used for the carriage of boats and any other moving object and any caravan.

## 3. Prohibition to install a tent or to place vehicles.

Subject to any permit, authorisation, or licence required under any law, no person shall install a tent or park or place a vehicle on the sand.

## 4. Penalty.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

Every person who is in breach of one of the provisions of these Bye-Laws, shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (69.88) for such contravention, and to a further fine (ammenda) of eleven euro and sixty-five cents (11.65) for every day during which one of the provisions of these Bye-Laws is breached.
