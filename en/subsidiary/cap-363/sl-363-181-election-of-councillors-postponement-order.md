# Election of Councillors (Postponement) Order (S.L. 363.181)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.181**

ELECTION OF COUNCILLORS (POSTPONEMENT) ORDER

9th January, 2015

LEGAL NOTICE 23 of 2015.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Election of Councillors (Postponement) Order.

## 2. Postponement of 2015 elections of councillors.

Elections for councillors which fall due to be held on the second Saturday of March 2015 are hereby postponed and shall be held on the 11th April 2015 for the purpose of being held on the same day when an abrogative referendum is to be held in terms of the Referenda Act.

*Cap. 237.*
