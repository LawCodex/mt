# Consumption of Alcoholic Beverages in Public Places (Isla Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.226)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.226**

**CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES IN**

**PUBLIC PLACES (ISLA LOCAL COUNCIL)**

**BYE-LAWS**

23rd April, 2021

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 04/03/2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Consumption of Alcoholic Beverages in Public Places (Isla Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"alcoholic recipient" includes bottles, tins, cans, tumblers and/ or any glass recipient intended to contain or serve (alcoholic) beverages;

"Council" means the Isla Local Council;

"locality" means the locality of Isla as shown in the Second Schedule to the Act;

"mentioned spaces" covers those spaces mentioned by the competent authorities to occupy with tables and chairs to serve and consume food and beverages;

"public space" means streets, roads, pavements, squares, public gardens and parks that make part of the limits of Isla.

## 3. Prohibition of loitering with alcoholic beverages.

No one is permitted to loiter in any public space while holding or in possession of alcoholic beverages.

## 4. Prohibition of carrying alcohol in public spaces.

No one is permitted to carry alcoholic beverages unless in a sealed recipient.

## 5. Advert and provision of recipients.

All commercial establishments in Isla selling beverages shall affix a notice in Maltese as well as in English as shown in the Schedule, which notice shall be placed in a conspicuous place and be easily legible by patrons of such commercial establishment.

## 6.1. Penalties.

Any owner of any establishment who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70) for each contravention, and to a further fine (ammenda) of fifteen euro (€15) for every day during which any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws is breached.

6.2 Any person who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70).

## 7. Exemption.

The Isla Local Council holds the rights to exempt from these conditions on special and/or particular occasions.

## SCHEDULE

(bye-law 5)

## 1.

THE CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOL BEVERAGES OUTDOORS IS PROHIBITED BY LAW.

## 2.

THE CARRYING OF ALCOHOL IN THE STREETS OTHER THAN IN SEALED UNOPENED CONTAINERS IS PROHIBITED BY LAW.
