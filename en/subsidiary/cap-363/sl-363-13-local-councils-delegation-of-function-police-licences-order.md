# Local Councils (Delegation of Function) (Police Licences) Order (S.L. 363.13)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.13**

LOCAL COUNCILS (DELEGATION OF FUNCTION) (POLICE LICENCES) ORDER

1st January, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 204 of 1996, as amended by Legal Notice 89 of 2011.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Local Councils (Delegation of Function) (Police Licences) Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires -

*Cap. 363.*

"Council" means a Local Council established under the Local Government Act;

"locality" has the same meaning assigned to it by the Local Government Act;

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Local Government.

## 3. Renewal of Police licences.

*Amended by: L.N. 89 of 2011. S.L.128.01*

(1) The Minister has directed that Councils shall be delegated the function to collect, on behalf of the Commissioner of Police, payment for the renewal of Police licences leviable under the Police Licences Regulations, referred to in subarticle (2).

(2) The Police licences referred to in subarticle (1) are those payable under items 1, 9 to 11, 25, item 44(f) insofar as it relates to hawkers at a fixed site either at the Valletta open air market or outside City Gate, items 47 to 49, 52 to 55, item 87(a), item 88(a) and (b), items 90, 93, 99, 103 to 105, 109 and 110 under Part I, and rental value licences payable under item 1 of Part II, with the exception of licences payable in relation to clubs, of the First Schedule to the above-mentioned regulations.

(3) The licence fees mentioned in subarticle (2) shall be paid to the Council of the locality where the relative licence is registered.
