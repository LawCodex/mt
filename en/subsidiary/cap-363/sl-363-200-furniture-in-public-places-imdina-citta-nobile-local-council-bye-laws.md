# Furniture in Public Places (Imdina (Città Nobile) Local Council) Bye-Laws. (S.L. 363.200)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.200**

**FURNITURE IN PUBLIC PLACES (L-IMDINA**

**(CITTÀ NOBILE) LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

6th April, 2018

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 2/09/2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is Furniture in Public Places (Imdina (Città Nobile) Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:-

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"the applicant" means that person who applies to the Local Council under the provisions of bye-law 3.1;

"the Council" means the Imdina (Città Nobile) Local Council;

"furniture" means any benches or any other furniture movable or immovable;

"open public places" mean any public pavements in the locality and in public places, including pavements, squares, gardens and the ditch.

## 3. Authorisation for street furniture on public pavements.

No person shall put up any furniture on pavements in the locality of Imdina (Città Nobile) without the written authorization of the Council.

3.1 Subject to any permit, authorization, or licence required under any law, such authorization may be granted after a written application to the Council.

## 4. Penalties.

Any person who is in breach of one of the provisions of these Bye-Laws, or any condition of the authorization given by virtue of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of twenty three euro and thirty cents (€23.30) for such contravention.

## 5. Reservation.

The Council reserves the right to remove any furniture set up without the authorization or in breach of any conditions imposed by the Local Council, at the expense of the person contravening any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws.
