# Local Councils (Delegation of Street Lighting Installation) Order (S.L. 363.91)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.91**

**LOCAL COUNCILS (DELEGATION OF STREET**

**LIGHTING INSTALLATION) ORDER**

17th May, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 114 of 2002, as amended by Legal Notices 20, 43, 105 and 113 of 2003; and Acts XXXIV of 2014 and XXV of 2015.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Local Councils (Delegation of Street Lighting Installation) Order.

## 2. Delegated functions. Cap. 363.

(1) In terms of article 33(1)(n) of the Local Government Act -

(a) the installation of all new street lighting systems, and (b) the maintenance of all street lighting installations,

except those listed in the First Schedule to this Order, shall be a function which is hereby delegated to each Local Council in relation to installations in its locality.

(2) The aforementioned delegation shall take effect in relation to each Council as from the date shown against that Local Council in the Second Schedule:

Provided that when it is indicated that such effective date is to be established, such date shall be established by Order of the Minister.

## 3. Installations, etc., to be in accordance with regulations and manual.

*Substituted by: XXXIV. 2014.32. Amended by: XXV. 2015.41. Cap. 545.*

All street lighting installations shall be installed or maintained, as the case may be, strictly in accordance with the regulations made in terms of the Regulator for Energy and Water Services Act and also in accordance with the standards, specifications, work-methods and procedures established by the "Street Lighting Devolution Manual" – Document Reference No. SLDM – 01, published with the approval of the Regulator for Energy and Water Services, as amended from time to time, hereinafter referred to as the "Manual".

## FIRST SCHEDULE — Article 2(1) — Items excluded from the competence of Local Councils

Clause 9.2 of the Manual mentioned in regulation 3 of these regulations shall determine the items and works which are excluded from the competence of Local Councils.

The proprietary database, including the plans and other technical information, produced by Enemalta Corporation in terms of Clause 9.2(iv) of the said Manual, shall be considered as the official platform from which the localisation, enumeration, categorization and statistical information related to street lighting systems can be obtained.
