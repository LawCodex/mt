# Beverages in Glass Containers and Consumption of Alcohol in Public Places (Għarb Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.229)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.229**

**BEVERAGES IN GLASS CONTAINERS AND**

CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOL IN PUBLIC PLACES

**(GĦARB LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

7th May, 2021

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 23/06/2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Beverages in Glass Containers and Consumption of Alcohol in Public Places (Għarb Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"Council" means the Għarb Local Council;

"designated areas" means those areas designated by the competent authorities for the placement of tables and chairs exclusively for the consumption of food and beverages;

"glass container" includes glass bottles, glasses and or any other glass container intended for the serving of food and beverages;

"locality" means the locality of Għarb as delineated in the Second Schedule to the Act;

"public space" means the streets, squares, belvederes, open space and gardens within the Għarb locality excluding designated areas.

## 3. Prohibition of loitering with glass containers.

No one is permitted to loiter in any public place holding or having on his person any glass container.

## 4. Prohibition of carrying of alcohol in public places.

No person is permitted to carry alcohol in public places other than in a sealed unopened container.

## 5. Prohibition of consumption of alcohol in public places.

No person is permitted to consume alcohol in the streets or in public places other than in the designated areas.

## 6. Notice and provision of containers.

Any commercial establishment situated within any street or public place selling beverages shall,:

(a) affix a notice in Maltese as well as in English as shown in the Schedule to these Bye-Laws, which notice shall be placed in a conspicuous place and be easily legible by the patrons of such commercial establishment;

(b) provide containers made of a material other than glass if such beverage is to be consumed in any public place; and

(c) ascertain the regular cleaning of the area within five(5) meters of the commercial establishment and collection of refuse from bins in the same distance.

## 7. Penalties.

Any person who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70) for such contravention, and to a further fine (ammenda) of fifteen euro (€15), for every day during which any of the provision of these Bye-Laws is breached.

## SCHEDULE

(bye-Law 6(a))

## 1.

THE CONSUMPTION OF BEVERAGES IN GLASS CONTAINERS OUTDOORS IS PROHIBITED BY LAW.

## 2.

THE CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOL IN PUBLIC PLACES IS PROHIBITED BY LAW.

THE CARRYING OF ALCOHOL IN PUBLIC PLACES OTHER THAN IN SEALED UNOPENED CONTAINERS IS PROHIBITED BY LAW.
