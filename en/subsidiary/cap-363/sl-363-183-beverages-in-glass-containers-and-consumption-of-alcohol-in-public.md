# Beverages in Glass Containers and Consumption of Alcohol in Public Places (Victoria Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.183)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.183**

**BEVERAGES IN GLASS CONTAINERS AND**

CONSUMPTION OF ALCOHOL IN PUBLIC PLACES (VICTORIA LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS

29th September, 2017

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 11/4/2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Beverages in Glass Containers and Consumption of Alcohol in Public Places (Victoria Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"Council" means the Victoria Local Council;

"designated areas" means those areas designated by the competent authorities for the placement of tables and chairs exclusively for the consumption of food and beverages;

"glass container" includes glass bottles, glasses, and, or any other glass container intended for the serving of any beverages;

"locality" means the locality of Victoria as delineated in the Act;

"public place" means the streets, squares, belvederes, open spaces and gardens within the Victoria locality, such as Villa Rundle, excluding designated areas.

## 3. Prohibition of loitering.

No person is permitted to loiter in any public place holding or having on his person any glass container.

## 4. Prohibition of carrying of alcohol.

No person is permitted to carry alcohol in public places other than in a sealed unopened container.

## 5. Prohibition of consumption of alcohol.

No person is permitted to consume alcohol in the streets or public places other than in the designated areas.

## 6. Commercial establishments.

Any commercial establishment situated within any street or public place selling beverages, shall:

(a) affix a notice in Maltese as well as in English as shown in the Schedule, which notice shall be placed in a conspicuous place and be easily legible by the patrons of such commercial establishment; and

(b) provide containers made of a material other than glass if such beverage is to be consumed in any public place. 7. Any person who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70) for such contravention, and to a further fine (ammenda) of fifteen euro (€15) for every day during which any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws is breached.

*Penalties.*
