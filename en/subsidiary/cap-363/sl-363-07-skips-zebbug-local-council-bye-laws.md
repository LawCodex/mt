# Skips (Zebbug Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.07**

**SKIPS (ŻEBBUĠ LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

21st August, 1995

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 7/1/95, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Skips (Żebbuġ Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

"bin on wheels" means any recipient on wheels that may be closed by means of a lid, used for the deposit of domestic refuse, supplied by or under the authority of the Council and placed in a public place within the Żebbuġ Locality boundary, but does not include skip;

"commercial and/or industrial waste or refuse" means any waste, originating from any shops, factories, enterprises, companies and other entities operating in commerce and industry and includes all bulky refuse in general, but does not include domestic waste or refuse;

"Council" means the Żebbuġ Local Council;

"domestic refuse" means household waste originating from private dwellings including flats or residential homes but does not include commercial and/or industrial waste;

"skip" means any open or closed skip supplied by or under the authority of the Council in a public place within the Żebbuġ locality boundary, and which includes any other recipient of this kind, used for the deposit of commercial and/or industrial waste, but does not include bin on wheels.

*Placement of skips/ bins on wheels.*

3.1 No person, other than the Council, shall have the power to place any skip or bin on wheels in any public place within the Żebbuġ locality boundary.

3.2 The Council may however authorise in writing any person to place a skip or bin on wheels during such times and at such places and under such terms and conditions as may be stipulated in the written authority by the Council annexed in Schedule A.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

3.3 There shall be payable on application to the Council by the person granted an authority under this bye-law a fee of:

3.3.1 one euro and sixteen cents (1.16) per day for every skip;

3.3.2. twenty-three cents (0.23) per day for every bin on wheels.

3.4 Any person who contravenes any provision of this bye-law or who fails to comply with any of the conditions stipulated in the written authority, shall be guilty of a contravention.

Misuse of skips. 4. No person shall -

4.1 cause any damage to any skip or bin on wheel or obliterate any markings printed on or affixed thereon; or scribble or make any mark on any skip or bin on wheels without the consent of the Council or of the person to whom authority has been granted to place a skip or bin on wheels by virtue of bye-law 3 as the case may require; or

4.2 after using the skip or bin on wheels leave its lid open or ajar; or

4.3 use the skip or bin on wheels for any purpose other than that it is intended for; or

4.4 when the skip or bin on wheels is full, deposit any refuse or waste in a manner that the lid of the skip or bin on wheels will not close properly or will remain ajar; or

4.5 when depositing such waste or refuse, spill and leave spilled any waste or refuse outside the skip or bin on wheels; or

4.6 throw or deposit any waste or refuse near or around the skip or bin on wheels; or

4.7 deposit in any bin on wheels any waste, refuse, object or substance which is not of a domestic nature; or deposit in any skip any commercial or industrial waste for which authority was not granted under the written authority in accordance with these bye-laws; or

4.8 deposit in the skip or bin on wheels any bulky article or liquid or any refuse, object or substance which the skip is not intended to receive; or 4.9 remove or misplace the skip or bin on wheels from where it is placed unless, in the case of a skip or bin on wheels placed under the authority of the Council by a person, such person shall have obtained authority in writing to do so from the Council; or 4.10 deposit waste or refuse in a skip or bin on wheels which is not supplied by the Council but placed by a person to whom authority has been granted by the Council in accordance with these bye-laws; or 4.11 after the 1st January 1997, deposit in a bin on wheels any domestic refuse which is not contained in a closed bag, of a type and material approved by the Council; or

4.12 in the case of a skip, deposit therein industrial and/ or commercial waste or refuse which does not originate from within the Żebbuġ locality boundary, unless under the authority in waiting of the Council and against registration or payment as the case may be of a fee as shown in Column 2 of Schedule B; or 4.13 when authorised by the Council to deposit waste or refuse in a particular skip or bin on wheels deposit any waste or refuse in another skip or bin on wheels. 5.1 Any person who engages in or operates a commercial or industrial activity within the Żebbuġ locality boundary and whose field of operation falls under one of the establishment categories shown under Column 1 of Schedule B, may register with the Council in order to be granted authority to deposit commercial and/ or industrial waste in the skips supplied by the Council in the Żebbuġ locality boundary.

5.1.2 Without prejudice to the provisions of bye-law 5.1, every person in Category 1 of Schedule B can dispose of commercial and/ or industrial waste in a bin on wheels.

*Registration of commercial and industrial establishments.*

5.2 For each registration made under bye-law 5.1 a fee as shown in Column 2 of Schedule B shall be payable to the Council.

5.3 The Council may, for a valid reason given in writing refuse to register a person under bye-law 5.1.

5.4 No person shown in Column 1 of Schedule B shall throw or deposit commercial and/or industrial waste in the skips supplied by the Council in the Żebbuġ locality boundary unless payment of the fee due under Column 2 of Schedule B is made on registration.

*Offences and penalties. Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

6.1 Without prejudice to the provisions of bye-laws 6.2 and 6.3 any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of bye-law 4 shall be deemed to have committed a contravention and shall, on conviction, be liable, unless a higher penalty is applicable under any other law -

6.1.1 for a first contravention to a fine (ammenda) of forty-six euro and fifty-nine cents (46.59), and

6.1.2 for a second or subsequent contravention to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (69.88).

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

6.2 In the case of a contravention under bye-law 3, a person shall be liable on conviction to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (69.88), and in the case of a continuing offence, to a further fine (ammenda) of eleven euro and sixty-five cents (11.65) for each day for each day during which the offence continues.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

6.3 In the case of a contravention under bye-law 5.4 a person shall be liable on conviction to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (69.88).

6.4 The imposition of a penalty under this bye-law shall be without prejudice to the right of the Council to recover any civil damages caused by the commission of any contravention under these Bye-Laws.

## 7. Termination of authority.

The Council may terminate the authority given or cancel the registration made under these bye-laws if it is satisfied that any of the terms and conditions under which the said authority was granted or registration made has not been complied with, or if there is a substantial change in circumstances which in the opinion of the Council render it necessary to withdraw the authority or cancel the registration. In this case the Council shall not be obliged to refund any fee that would have already been paid to the Council under Schedule B.
