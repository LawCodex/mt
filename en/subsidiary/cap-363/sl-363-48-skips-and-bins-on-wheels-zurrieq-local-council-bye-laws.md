# Skips and Bins on Wheels (Zurrieq Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.48)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.48**

**SKIPS AND BINS ON WHEELS**

**(|URRIEQ LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

14th May, 2000

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 67/5/2000, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Skips and Bins on Wheels (|urrieq Local Council) Bye-Laws.

*Interpretation.*

2.1 In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"authorised person" means any person holding a permit or his representative and any resident of any street or part thereof who is the only person authorised in accordance with bye-law 3.1;

"bin on wheels" means any recipient on wheels that may be closed by means of a lid, supplied by or under the authority of the Council and placed in a public place within the |urrieq locality boundary, for the deposit of domestic refuse but does not include skip;

"commercial and/or industrial waste or refuse" means any waste, originating from any shops, factories, enterprises, companies and other entities operating in commerce and industry and includes all bulky refuse in general;

"Council" means the |urrieq Local Council;

"domestic refuse" means all type of waste not being of a commercial nature but household waste originating from private dwellings including flats or residential homes;

"skip" means any open or closed skip supplied by the Council or under its authority in a public place by a person duly authorised by the Council within the |urrieq locality boundary, and which includes any other recipient of this kind, used for the deposit of commercial and/or industrial waste, but does not include bin on wheels.

*Placement of skips and bins on wheels.*

3.1 No person, society, organisation, authority, or any other enterprise without the authority of the Council, shall have the power to place any skip or bin on wheels in any public place within the |urrieq locality boundary.

3.2 The Council may however, authorise in writing any person to place a skip or bin on wheels during such times and at such places and under such terms and conditions as may be stipulated in the written authority by the Council annexed in Schedule A.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

3.3 When a written authority is granted, there shall be payable to the Council -

3.3.1 in the case of a skip, a fee of one euro and sixteen cents (1.16) per day for every skip;

3.3.2 in the case of a bin on wheels, twenty-three cents (0.23) per day for every bin.

3.4 Any person who contravenes any provision of this bye-law or who fails to comply with any of the conditions stipulated in the written authority, shall be guilty of a contravention.

*Misuse of skips and bins on wheels.*

4.1 A person shall be deemed to be guilty of a contravention if:

4.2 he causes any damage to any skip or bin on wheels or obliterate any markings printed on or affixed thereon; or scribble or make any mark of any sort on any skip or bin on wheels without the consent of the Council or of the person to whom written authority has been granted to place a skip or bin on wheels by virtue of bye-law 3 as the case may require; or

4.3 after using the skip or bin on wheels he leaves its lid open or ajar, or deposits any refuse in such manner as to impede the lid from closing properly or will remain ajar; or

4.4 he uses the skip or bin on wheels for any purpose ether than that it is intended for, as described in bye-law 4.2, or deposits any waste or refuse which the skip or bin on wheels is not intended to receive; or 4.5 he deposits any waste or refuse outside, around or near the skip or bin on wheels; or

4.6 he removes or misplaces the skip or bin on wheels from where it is placed by the Council within the space indicated by appropriate road markings which shall include the letter "SKIP", or by any person duly authorised without having obtained authority in writing to do so from the Council; or

4.7 he deposits waste or refuse in a skip or bin on wheels which is not supplied by the Council but placed by a person to whom written authority has been granted by the Council in accordance with these Bye-Laws; or

4.8 he deposits in a bin on wheels any domestic refuse which is not contained in a closed bag, of a type and material approved by the Council; or

4.9 he deposits waste or refuse in a skip or bin on wheels other than that for which written authority has been issued by the Council; or

4.10 he deposits any waste or refuse whether of a commercial and/or industrial or domestic nature, which waste or refuse has not originated from within the |urrieq locality boundary.

*Registration of commercial and industrial establishments.*

5.1 Any person who, wholly or partially, engages in or operates a commercial or industrial activity within the |urrieq locality boundary and whose field of operation falls under one of the establishment categories shown under Column 1 of Schedule B, shall register with the Council in order to be granted authority to deposit commercial and/or industrial waste in the skips supplied by or authorised by the Council in the |urrieq locality boundary.

5.2 Without prejudice to the provisions of bye-law 5.1, every person in Category 1 of Schedule B can dispose of commercial and/ or industrial waste in a bin on wheels.

5.3 For each registration made under bye-law 5.1 a fee as shown in Column 2 of Schedule B shall be payable to the Council.

5.4 The Council may, for a valid reason given in writing, refuse to register a person under bye-law 5.1.

5.5 The applicant shall only commence to make use of the skip or bin on wheels after the application has been approved by the Council and after he would have received such approval in writing. Should the applicant make use of the skip or bin on wheels prior to the receipt of such approval, he shall be guilty of a contravention.

*Offences and penalties. Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

6.1 Subject to the provisions of bye-laws 6.2 and 6.3, any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of bye-law 4 shall be deemed to have committed a contravention and shall, on conviction, be liable, unless a higher penalty is applicable under any other law -

6.1.1 for a first contravention to a fine (ammenda) of forty-six euro and fifty-nine cents (46.59), and

6.1.2 for a second or subsequent contravention to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (69.88).

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

6.2 In the case of a contravention under bye-laws 3 and 5.5, a person shall be liable on conviction to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (69.88).

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

6.3 In the case of a continuing offence a person shall be liable on conviction to a further fine (ammenda) of eleven euro and sixty-five cents (11.65) for each day during which the offence continues.

6.4 The imposition of a penalty under this bye-law shall be without prejudice to the right of the Council to sue for civil damages caused by the commission of any contravention under these Bye-Laws.

## 7. Termination or cancellation of registration.

The Council may terminate the authority given, and, or cancel the registration made under these Bye-Laws if it is satisfied that any of the terms and conditions under which the said authority was granted or registration made, has not been complied with, or if there is a substantial change in circumstances which in the opinion of the Council renders it necessary to withdraw the authority or cancel the registration. In this case the Council shall not be obliged to refund any fee that would have already been paid to the Council under Schedule B.
