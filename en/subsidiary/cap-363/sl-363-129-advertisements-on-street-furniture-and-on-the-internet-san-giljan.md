# Advertisements on Street Furniture and on the Internet (San Ġiljan Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.129)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.129**

**ADVERTISMENTS ON STREET FURNITURE AND**

*Substituted by: L.C.B.L. 52/03/ 2020.*

ON THE INTERNET (SAN ĠILJAN LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS

17th August, 2006

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 52/2/2006, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007 and Local Council Bye-Law 52/03/2020.

## 1. Citation.

*Substituted by: L.C.B.L. 52/03/ 2020.*

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Advertisements on Street Furniture and on the Internet (San Ġiljan Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.C.B.L. 52/03/ 2020.*

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires -

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"advertisement" means any type of promotion whether commercial or otherwise, and includes a notice;

"applicant" means the person who applies to the Council under the provisions of bye-law 3.2;

"contractor" means the person who has been authorised by the Council to display, fix or place any advertisement on street furniture under the provisions of bye-law 3.1;

"the Council" means the San Ġiljan Local Council;

"street furniture" means any object, movable or immovable, which is the Council’s property or is under its responsibility and which although would not substantially form part of the road, pavement, garden or other public area, would be placed in such area or fixed to it, or even placed on it, and includes dustbins, skips, bins on wheels, benches, planters, boundary walls, walls, balusters, columns, railings, bus shelters, tree pits, verges, central reservations, roundabouts, central strips, traffic islands, and u-shape barriers.

"Web-page" means access of information through Internet access described as World Wide Web (WWW), which web-page forms part of the Web Search Engines.

*Authorization to advertise on street furniture.*

3.1 Subject to any permit, authorization, or licence required under any law, no person shall display, fix or place any advertisement on any street furniture within the San {iljan locality boundary without the written authorization of the Council.

3.2 The authorization may be granted either after a written application to the Council or else after a public call made by the Council for applications to be submitted:

Provided that the authorization shall be deemed void if applicant fails to comply with the requirements established in the guidelines issued, or which may be issued from time to time by the Council in terms of article 33(1)(g) of the Act:

Provided further that the guidelines shall be incorporated in the contract document which shall regulate the authorization granted by the Council to the contractor in terms of bye-law 3.3.

3.3 Following a public call by the Council, the authorization of the Council shall take the form of a contract in accordance with the regulations and procedures applicable to Local Councils.

Such a contract shall be valid for a period of one year which may be renewed for a further one year period by means of a contract. In every case the Council reserves the right to refuse to renew the contract after presenting valid reasons in writing to the person concerned.

3.4 The Council may, on awarding the contract mentioned in bye-law 3.3, authorise the contractor to use the space where advertisements may be displayed on street furniture, even by selling the mentioned space to third parties. In such a case, third parties would not require the Council’s authorization:

Provided that the contractor shall not use or sell the space for any advertisement that is prohibited by the contract, or at a higher price than that established by the Council by the contract or with conditions different from those stipulated in the original contract.

3.5 For every authorization granted by the Council according to these Bye-Laws, there shall be paid a fee as established in the Schedule:

Provided that for the authorization granted by the Council following a public call, the fee entitled to be paid to the Council is that offered in the accepted tender document.

3.6 The Council may grant authorization in terms of these Bye- Laws to voluntary organizations that operate from the locality, parochial and philanthropic organisations or non-governmental organizations listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Act, after the Council receives a written request so as to advertise activities or public notices without payment.

3.7 Notwithstanding the authorization granted under these Bye-Laws, the person to whom authorization is given is personally responsible for any permits or licences necessary under any other law.

3.8 Any authorization granted under these Bye-Laws is subject, amongst other conditions, to a condition that the advertisement shall be removed within one week from the expiry date of the authorization. In any case to the contrary, the person responsible for removing such advert shall be guilty of a contravention.

## 3.9.

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 52/03/ 2020.*

As part of the conditions in such contract as mentioned in these bye-laws, the Council reserves the right establish the site where these adverts will be placed but also the type of material and the shape and type of such furniture.

## 3.10.

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 52/03/ 2020.*

The contractor will be responsible for the upkeep and condition of all structures erected in the streets, gardens or other public places and has to make sure that these are not an obstacle or create any danger to both pedestrians and vehicles.

## 4. Penalty.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007. Substituted by: L.C.B.L. 52/03/ 2020.*

Every person who is in breach of one of the provisions of these Bye-Laws or any condition of the authorization given by virtue of these Bye-Laws, shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of fifty euro (€50) for such contravention, and to a further fine (ammenda) of not less than three euro (€3) and not more than fifteen euro (€15) for every day during which one of the provisions of these Bye-Laws or any condition of the authorization is breached.

*Reservation. Amended by: L.C.B.L. 52/03/ 2020.*

5.1 The Council reserves the right to remove any advertisement affixed without authorisation or any advertisement which is not removed by the authorised person within the time-limit stipulated in these Bye-Laws, at the expense of the person contravening any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws or infringing any condition of the authorisation or contract given by virtue of these Bye-Laws.

## 5.2.

The Council can contract, according to the dispositions and procedures in the Act, with entities both commercial and non-commercial so that these entities can place their adverts on the Local Council Web-page.

## 5.3.

The contract mentioned in bye-law 5.2 will be valid for a period of one (1) year and can be renewed for another year (1).
