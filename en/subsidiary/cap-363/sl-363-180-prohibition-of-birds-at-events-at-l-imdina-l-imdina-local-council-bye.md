# Prohibition of Birds at Events at L-Imdina (L-Imdina Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.180)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.180**

**PROHIBITION OF BIRDS**

**AT EVENTS AT L-IMDINA**

**(L-IMDINA LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

7th November, 2014

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 2/04/2014.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is Prohibition of Birds at Events at L-Imdina (L-Imdina Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"birds" means birds of any species;

"the Council" means the L-Imdina Local Council;

"events" means a gathering of any nature taking place within the locality of L-Imdina.

## 3. Prohibition of the release of birds.

Persons shall be prohibited from releasing birds at events held in the locality of L-Imdina.

## 4. Penalty.

Any person who is in breach of the provisions of bye-law 3 shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70.00) for such contravention.
