# Advertisements on the Internet (Zurrieq Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.45)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.45**

**ADVERTISEMENTS ON THE INTERNET**

**(|URRIEQ LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

7th May, 2000

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 67/2/2000, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Advertisements on the Internet (|urrieq Local Council) Bye-Laws.

*Interpretation.*

2.1 In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"advertisement" includes any commercial or non-commercial promotion;

"Council" means the |urrieq Local Council;

"person" means the person who has been authorised by the Council to link with the Council’s Web-page under the provisions of bye-law 3.2;

"Web-page" means access to information and database via the Internet, known as the World Wide Web (WWV), which Web-page may be advertised on the so-called Web Search Engines.

2.2 In these Bye-Laws, any expression which is defined in the Act shall have the same meaning as is assigned to it in the Act.

*Official Council Web-page.*

3.1 The Council shall have a Web-page on the Internet which shall include all information on the Council and the locality which the Council may choose to include.

3.2 There shall be one official Web-page dedicated exclusively to the |urrieq Local Council:

Provided that the Council may authorise any person to include or link such Web-page on or to another Web-page, either at a charge or at no charge, as the Council may decide.

*Authorisation on writing.*

3.3 The Council shall authorise in writing the person mentioned in bye-law 3.2.

*Penalties. Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

3.4 Every person who is found guilty of having breached any provision of bye-laws 3.2 and 3.3 shall be fined sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88) together with two euro and thirty-three cents (€2.33) for every day during which the Council’s Web-page is accessible on the offender’s Web-page without the written authorisation.

*Authorisation for advertising on the Council’s Web-page.*

4.1 The Council may contract, in accordance with the regulations and procedures applicable according to the Act, with commercial and non-commercial entities to advertise the Council’s Web-page at a charge.

4.2 The contract mentioned in bye-law 4.1 is valid for a period of one year which may be renewed for a further period of one year.
