# Skips (Hamrun Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.05**

**SKIPS (}AMRUN LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

16th September, 1994

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 27/1/94, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Skips (}amrun Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Definitions.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Council" means the }amrun Local Council;

"domestic refuse" means household waste from private dwellings including flats or residential homes, but does not include waste or refuse of a commercial or industrial nature;

"skip" means any skip supplied by or under the authority of the Council and placed in a public place within the }amrun locality boundary, and includes skips intended to be used by the public for the deposit of domestic refuse.

## 3.1. Placement of skips.

No person, other than the Council, shall have power to place any skip in any public place within the }amrun locality boundary.

3.2 The Council may however authorise in writing any person to place a skip during such times and at such places and under such other terms and conditions as may be stipulated in the written authority annexed in the Schedule.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

3.3 There shall be payable on application to the Council by the person granted an authority under this bye-law a fee of ninety-three cents (0.93) per day for every skip.

3.4 Any person who contravenes any provision of this bye-law, or who fails to comply with any of the conditions stipulated in the written authority, shall be guilty of a contravention.

## 4. Misuse of skips.

No person shall -

4.1 cause any damage to any skip or obliterate any markings printed on or affixed to any skip; or

4.2 after using the skip, leave its lid open; or

4.3 use the skip for any purpose other than that it is intended for; or

4.4 when the skip is full, deposit any refuse in a manner that the lid of the skip will not close properly; or

4.5 when depositing such refuse, spill any refuse outside the skip; or

**4.6 throw or deposit any refuse near the skip; or**

4.7 deposit in the skip any refuse, object or substance which is not of a domestic nature; or

4.8 deposit in the skip any bulky article, or liquid, or any refuse, object or substance which the skip is not intended to receive; or

4.9 remove the skip from where it is placed; or

4.10 after the 1 January, 1996, deposit in a skip any refuse which is not contained in a closed bag, of a type and material approved by the Council.

*Offences and penalties. Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

5.1 Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of bye-law 4 shall be deemed to have committed a contravention and shall, on conviction, be liable, unless a higher penalty is applicable under any other law:

5.1.1 for a first offence to a fine (ammenda) of forty-six euro and fifty-nine cents (€46.59); and

5.1.2 for a second or subsequent offence to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88).

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

5.2 In the case of a contravention under bye-law 3, a person shall be liable, on conviction, to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88), and in the case of a continuing offence, to a further fine (ammenda) of eleven euro and sixty-five cents (€11.65) for each day during which the offence continues.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

5.3 The imposition of a penalty under this bye-law shall be without prejudice to the right of the Council to recover any civil damages caused by the commission of the contravention under these Bye-Laws.

## 6. Termination of authority.

The Council may terminate the authority given in terms of these Bye-Laws if it is satisfied that any of the terms and conditions under which the said authority was given has not been complied with or if there is a substantial change in circumstances which in the opinion of the Council render it necessary to withdraw the authority.
