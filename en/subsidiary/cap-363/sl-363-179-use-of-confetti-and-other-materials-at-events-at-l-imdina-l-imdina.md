# Use of Confetti and Other Materials at Events at L-Imdina (L-Imdina Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.179)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.179**

**USE OF CONFETTI AND OTHER MATERIALS AT**

**EVENTS AT L-IMDINA (L-IMDINA LOCAL**

**COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

7th November, 2014

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 2/03/2014.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is Use of Confetti and Other Materials at Events at L-Imdina (L-Imdina Local Council) Bye- Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

"applicant" means the person who applies to the Local Council under the provisions of bye-law 3;

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"confetti" means small pieces of paper or other materials;

"the Council" means the L-Imdina Local Council;

"other materials" means any substance of whatever form and nature.

*Prohibition of confetti and other materials.*

3.1 Subject to any permit, authorization, or licence required under any law, no person shall throw confetti or other materials at an event taking place in the locality of L-Imdina without the written autorization of the Council.

3.2 Such authorization may be granted after a written application to the Council.

## 4. Penalty.

Any person who is in breach of the provisions of bye-law 3, or any condition of the authorization given by virtue thereof shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70.00) for such contravention.

## 5. Council shall be responsible for cleaning.

The Council reserves the right to clean up the confetti or other materials thrown at an event without the authorization or in breach of any conditions imposed by the Local Council, at the expenses of the person contravening any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws.
