# Dissolution of Paola Local Council Order (S.L. 363.33)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.33**

**DISSOLUTION OF PAOLA LOCAL COUNCIL**

**ORDER**

7th October, 1999

LEGAL NOTICE 168 of 1999.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Dissolution of Paola Local Council Order.

## 2. Dissolution of Paola Local Council. Cap. 363.

The Paola Local Council is hereby being dissolved due to a persistent non-regard to the provisions of article 22(c) of the Local Councils Act.
