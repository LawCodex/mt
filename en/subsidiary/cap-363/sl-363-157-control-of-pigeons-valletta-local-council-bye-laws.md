# Control of Pigeons (Valletta Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.157)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.157**

**CONTROL OF PIGEONS**

**(VALLETTA LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

21st April, 2011\*

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 1/3/2011.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Control of Pigeons (Valletta Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires - "Act" means the Local Government Act;

*Cap. 363.*

"the Council" means the Valletta Local Council;

"domestic pigeons" means pigeons which are kept in a residential place or in some other place for the purpose of recreation, hobby or sport;

"locality" means the Valletta locality as shown in the Second Schedule to the Act;

"wild pigeons" means pigeons which are not domestic pigeons. 3.1. The feeding of wild pigeons in any part of the locality is prohibited.

*Feeding of wild pigeons by any person is prohibited.*

## 3.2.

It is also prohibited for any person to, voluntarily, leave food in places which are accessible to wild pigeons.

## 3.3.

The provisions of this article do not apply to those persons who keep or take care of domestic pigeons.

## 4. Abandoned immovable property.

The owners of immovable property situated within the locality and which is abandoned shall ensure that the said property be sealed in such a manner that no pigeons can go in or make their nest therein.

## 5.1. Penalty.

Any person who fails to comply with any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall be deemed to have committed a contravention, and shall, on conviction, be liable, unless a higher penalty is applicable under any other law:

5.1.1 for a first contravention, to a fine (ammenda) of forty-six euro (€46);

5.1.2 for a second or subsequent contravention, to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70); and

5.1.3 in the case of a continuing contravention, to a fine (ammenda) of twelve euro (€12) for each day that the contravention continues.

\*See bye-law 1.2 of these Bye-Laws, as originally promulgated.
