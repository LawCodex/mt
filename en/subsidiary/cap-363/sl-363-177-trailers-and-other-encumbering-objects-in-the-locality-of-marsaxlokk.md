# Trailers and Other Encumbering Objects in the locality of Marsaxlokk (Marsaxlokk Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.177)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.177**

TRAILERS AND OTHER ENCUMBERING OBJECTS IN THE LOCALITY OF MARSAXLOKK

**(MARSAXLOKK LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

17th June, 2014\*

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 36/01/2014.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Trailers and Other Encumbering Objects in the locality of Marsaxlokk (Marsaxlokk Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires - "Act" means the Local Government Act;

*Cap. 363.*

"the Council" means the Marsaxlokk Local Council;

"encumbering object" means any object which, although it may not necessary be considered as waste, causes obstruction or nuisance or inconvenience in any part of the locality or any other object or any other object of a size or quantity or accumulation which, as taken by itself, causes a nuisance, or which may lead to deterioration or defacement of a public place;

"locality" means the locality of Marsaxlokk as defined in the Act;

"trailer" means any vehicle without mechanical power of its own designed to be drawn by a motor vehicle and intended for the purpose of the carriage of boats and other sea craft or for the conveyance of goods.

*Restrictions on trailers and other encumbering objects.*

3.1 No person shall in any part of the locality, place or leave any encumbering object, including any skip which is not destined for domestic use, and no person shall leave or park, whether for a temporary period or otherwise, or leave unattended any trailer, loaded or not in any part of the locality save in an area that is designated by the Council as a site for the parking of trailers or on public land, with the prior authorisation of the Council.

3.2 No part of these Bye-laws shall be construed as restricting the transit of trailers within the locality:

Provided that such trailer shall be removed immediately once the objects that would be conveyed thereon are removed.

## 4. Means of identification.

Any trailer or encumbering object which passes through or is parked within the locality of Marsaxlokk shall have a means of identification which leads to the person responsible for the same trailer or encumbering object.

*Applicability.*

5.1 These Bye-laws shall not be applicable to a site or sites, as shown in the Schedule to these Bye-Laws, identified and approved by the Council for this purpose and as long as conditions imposed are observed.

\*see bye-law 1.2 of these Bye-laws, as originally promulgated.

5.2 Sites identified by the Council shall only be used after an application has been filed to the Council and the same Council approves such application.

## 6. Fishermen’s zones.

Sites identified as fishermen’s zone shall only be used as granted in relative permits and according to the any legislation in force relating to fishermen’s zones and the granting of permits. Any other use shall be subject to these Bye-Laws.

## 7. Penalties.

Any person who acts in contravention of any provision of these Bye-Laws, shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70) for such contravention together with a further fine (ammenda) of ten euro (€10) for each day during which such contravention of these Bye-laws would have ensued.

## 8. Removal.

The penalty referred to in bye-law 7 shall be without prejudice to any right of action which the Council or any other authority may take to remove, deposit, or dispose of any trailer or any other encumbering object or any accessory thereto in terms of the provisions of the Clamping and Removal of Motor Vehicles and Encumbering Objects Regulations, or any other law, and without prejudice to any other penalty or fine (ammenda) which may be imposed under any other law.
