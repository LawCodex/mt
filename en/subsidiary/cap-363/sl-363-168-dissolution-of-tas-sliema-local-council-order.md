# Dissolution of Tas-Sliema Local Council Order (S.L. 363.168)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.168**

DISSOLUTION OF TAS-SLIEMA LOCAL COUNCIL ORDER

9th February, 2012

LEGAL NOTICE 61 of 2012.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Dissolution of Tas-Sliema Local Council Order.

## 2. Dissolution of Tas-Sliema Local Council. Cap. 363.

The Tas-Sliema Local Council is hereby being dissolved due to a persistent non-regard to the provisions of the Local Councils Act, after formal notice dated 4 January 2012 has been given by the Prime Minister.
