# Local Councils and Regional Councils (Delegation of Enforcement) Order (S.L. 363.41)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.41**

**LOCAL COUNCILS AND REGIONAL COUNCILS**

**(DELEGATION OF ENFORCEMENT) ORDER**

25th February, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 32 of 2000, as amended by Legal Notices 38, 44, 45, 84, 88, 94, 114, 120, 133, 196, 227 and 256 of 2000, 20 and 294 of 2001, 137, 296, 315 and 326 of 2002, 210 of 2003, 45, 266, 293, 431 and 518 of 2004, 29, 224, 273 and 405 of 2005, 321 of 2007, 265 and 266 of 2008, and 2, 314, 347, 417 and 492 of 2011, 207 of 2019 and 285 of 2022 and Act XXIX of 2019.

## 1. Citation.

*Amended by: L.N. 2 of 2011. Substituted by: L.N. 285 of 2022.*

The title of this Order is the Local Councils and Regional Councils (Delegation of Enforcement) Order.

## 2. Delegated functions.

*Amended by: L.N. 20 of 2001; L.N. 2 of 2011. Cap. 363.*

(1) In terms of article 33(1)(n) of the Local Government Act, hereinafter referred to as "the Act", the enforcement of -

(a) Bye-Laws made, or which may from time to time be made, by each Local Council under the provisions of article 34(1) of the Act;

*Cap. 291.*

(b) contraventions for such of the offences listed in the Schedule to the Commissioners for Justice Act which are shown under the First Schedule to this Order,

shall be a function which is hereby being delegated to each Local Council in relation to contraventions committed in its locality or to the Joint Committee or Regional Council with regards to contraventions that are committed in any locality falling under its responsibility according to this Order.

(2) The aforementioned delegation shall take effect in relation to each Local Council or Regional Committee as from the date shown against that Local Council or Joint Committee or Regional Council in the Second Schedule:

Provided that when it is indicated that such effective date is to be established, such date shall be established by Order of the Minister.

(3) The Minister may by Order withdraw the delegation given to any Local Council or Joint Committee or Regional Council and such Order shall come into effect as from the date shown in the Second Schedule:

Provided that when that date is established, any notice of contravention issued by a Local Council shall remain valid and any proceedings before a tribunal or court shall be continued in the name of that Local Council until all proceedings in respect of that contravention have been exhausted.

## 3. Responsibility for arterial and distributor roads, etc.

*Amended by: L.N. 20 of 2001; L.N. 2 of 2011.*

In terms of article 33(2) of the Act, the responsibility for arterial and distributor roads and the items shown in the Third Schedule is being delegated to Local Councils or Joint Committee or Regional Council solely for the purposes of enforcement as provided in article 2(1) of this Order.

## 4. Applicability of article 36 of the Act.

(1) Subject to the provisions of sub-article (2) of this article, article 36(2), (3), (4) and (5) of the Act shall, in addition to Bye-Laws, apply to such of the offences listed in the Schedule to the Commissioners for Justice Act which are shown under the First Schedule to this Order.

*Cap. 291.*

(2) For the purposes of article 36(3) of the Act, Commissioners for Justice shall, with effect from the 17th day of April, 2000, sit in the Local Tribunals so designated in terms of article 4(11) of the Commissioners for Justice Act.
