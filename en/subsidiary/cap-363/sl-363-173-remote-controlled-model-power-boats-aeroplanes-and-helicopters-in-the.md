# Remote-Controlled Model Power Boats, Aeroplanes and helicopters in the locality of Marsaxlokk (Marsaxlokk Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.173)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.173**

REMOTE-CONTROLLED MODEL POWER BOATS,

**AEROPLANES OR HELICOPTERS IN THE**

**LOCALITY OF MARSAXLOKK**

**(MARSAXLOKK LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

27th November, 2012

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 36/06/2012.

*Citation and commencement.*

1.1 The title of these Bye-Laws is Remote-Controlled Model Power Boats, Aeroplanes and helicopters in the locality of Marsaxlokk (Marsaxlokk Local Council) Bye-Laws.

1.2 In accordance with article 35(4) of the Act, these Bye- Laws shall come into force upon their publication in the Gazette.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

"applicant" means a person who makes a request to the Council in accordance with these bye-laws and includes a company, club and association;

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"the Council" means the Marsaxlokk Local Council;

"locality" means the locality of Marsaxlokk as defined in the Act;

"Remote-Controlled Model Power Boats, Aeroplanes and Helicopters" means model power boats, or aeroplanes or helicopters controlled by means of a remote control or an auto pilot but excludes wind powered and electrical powered models.

## 3. Prohibition.

No person may, anywhere within the locality, including in any beach, make use of or operate Remote-Controlled Model Power Boats, Aeroplanes or Helicopters without the prior written authorisation of the Council given in terms of these Bye-Laws.

## 4. Authorisation.

The Council may grant a written authorisation for the use or operation of Remote-Controlled Model Power Boats, Aeroplanes or Helicopters within the locality when the Council is satisfied that the use or operation is intended as part of a philanthropic activity or part of another activity that benefits the economy of the country or of the locality:

Provided that such authorisation may be subject to such conditions as the Council may deem fit to impose.

## 5. Fines.

Any person who acts in contravention of any provision of these Bye-Laws, or who contravenes any condition imposed in a permit granted in terms of these Bye-Laws shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of not more than seventy euro (€70) for such contravention.
