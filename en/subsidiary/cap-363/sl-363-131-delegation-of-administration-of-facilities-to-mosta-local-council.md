# Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Mosta Local Council Order (S.L. 363.131)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.131**

**DELEGATION OF ADMINISTRATION OF**

FACILITIES TO MOSTA LOCAL COUNCIL ORDER 1st June, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 148 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Mosta Local Council Order.

## 2. Delegation of functions. Cap. 363.

The property described in article 3 of this Order is hereby being specified for the purposes of the delegation of functions mentioned in article 33(1)(m) of the Local Government Act, as from the date of the publication of this order.

## 3. Description.

The property in the locality of Mosta referred to in article 2 of the Order consists of underground shelter at Pjazza Rotunda, Mosta as shown bordered on plan P.D. 2007\_9 attached to the Lease Agreement for the Administration and Use of the underground shelter in Pjazza Rotunda Mosta, by Mosta Local Council, signed by the Director Land on behalf of the Government of Malta and the Mayor and Executive Secretary of the Mosta Local Council on the 27th February, 2007.
