# Dwejra Environmental Education Centre (San Lawrenz Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.202)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.202**

DWEJRA ENVIRONMENTAL EDUCATION CENTRE (SAN LAWRENZ LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS 15th May, 2018

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAWS 54/11/ 2018

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is Dwejra Environmental Education Centre (San Lawrenz Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:-

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"the agreement" means the agreement entered into between MEPA and San Lawrenz Local Council dated 27th September 2013;

"the Board" means the person or group of persons appointed by the Council in terms of bye-law 3;

"the Centre" means the Dwejra Environmental Education Centre;

"the Council" means the San Lawrenz Local Council;

"reasonable prices" means those prices which are normally charged by establishments which sell similar products.

## 3.1. Establishment of the Board.

The Council shall establish a Board which shall consist of three (3) persons who shall be entrusted with the administration of the Centre.

## 3.2.

The Council shall elect the Board in the first sitting of the Council of every legislature.

## 3.3.

Members of employees of the Council shall not be able to form part of the Board.

## 3.4.

Members of the Board shall hold office for the term of the legislature of the Council.

## 3.5.

The Board shall report directly to the Council and all decisions of the Board must be confirmed by the Council.

## 3.6.

The Council reserves the right to change the composition of the Board during the legislature.

## 4.1. Right to charge an entrance fee.

The Council shall have the right to run the Centre as well as to charge an entrance fee to the Centre according to the established rates by virtue of bye-law 7.

## 4.2.

The right of the Council to charge a fee shall subsist until the Agreement is in force.

## 5. Right to sell informative material and souvenirs.

In the course of managing the Centre, the Council shall have the right to sell informative material of any sort as well as souvenirs at reasonable prices.

## 6. Promotion of the Centre.

The Council may use any possible means in order to publicly promote the Centre.

## 7. Fees.

The entrance tickets for the Centre shall be against payment according to the Schedule.

## 8. Other permits and licences.

These Bye-Laws shall be without prejudice to any other permit or licence required under any other law.

## SCHEDULE

(Bye-Law 7)

Adults €2.00

Children (under 12 years of age) €1.00
