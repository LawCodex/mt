# Unsolicited Mail (Tas-Sliema Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.223)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.223**

**UNSOLICITED MAIL (TAS-SLIEMA LOCAL**

**COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

8th February, 2021

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 59/01/2021.

*Citation.*

1.1 The title of these Bye-Laws is Unsolicited Mail (Tas- Sliema Local Council) Bye-Laws.

*Interpretation.*

2.1 In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"Council" means the Tas-Sliema Local Council;

"unsolicted mail" means unwanted or unsolicited commercial advertising or commercial promotional material received through the post sometimes referred to as junk mail.

2.2 In these Bye-Laws, any expression which is defined in the Act shall have the same meaning as is assigned to it in the Act.

*Unsolicited mail.*

3.1 Any unsolicited mail must be delivered into letterboxes and not left on any doorstep, pavement, any part of building or garden or container even if such container is intended for unsolicited mail.

3.2 Any person delivering such unsolicited mail shall ensure that the mail is put entirely in the letterboxes of the recipient and that such mail shall not be visible from the outside.

3.3 If the letterbox is full and the person delivering unsolicited mail cannot ensure that the mail is put entirely in the letterboxes of the recipient, such person shall not leave or deliver the unsolicited mail in the letterboxes.

3.4 The person delivering unsolicited mail must respect any sign or sticker stating that unsolicited mail must not be left in a particular residence, commercial premises or block of apartments or any other building.

*Penalties.*

4 Any person who is in breach of any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws, or who fails to abide by any condition made by virtue of these Bye-Laws, shall be deemed to have committed a contravention and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of not more than seventy euro (€70) for such contravention.
