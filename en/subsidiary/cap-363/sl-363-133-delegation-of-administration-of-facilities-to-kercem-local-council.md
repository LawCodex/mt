# Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Kercem Local Council (S.L. 363.133)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.133**

**DELEGATION OF ADMINISTRATION OF**

**FACILITIES TO KER~EM LOCAL COUNCIL**

**ORDER**

6th July, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 177 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Ker`em Local Council Order.

## 2. Delegation of functions. Cap. 363.

The property described in article 3 of this Order is hereby being specified for the purposes of the delegation of functions mentioned in article 33(1)(m) of the Local Government Act, as from the date of the publication of this order.

## 3. Description.

The property in the locality of Ker`em referred to in article 2 of the order consists of site in Triq Wenzu Mintoff, Ker`em, as shown bordered on plan P.D.2005\_100 attached to the Lease Agreement for the Administration and Use of Government Property in Ker`em, by the Ker`em Local Council, signed by the Director Land on behalf of the Government of Malta and by the Mayor and the Executive Secretary of the Ker`em Local Council on the 25th February, 2007.
