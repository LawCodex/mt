# Loitering in Specific Streets in Ta' Xbiex (Ta' Xbiex Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.135)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.135**

LOITERING IN SPECIFIC STREETS IN TA’ XBIEX

**(TA’ XBIEX LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

21st September, 2007

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 61/4/2007, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Loitering in Specific Streets in Ta’ Xbiex (Ta’ Xbiex Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"Council" means the Ta’ Xbiex Local Council;

"locality" means the locality of Ta’ Xbiex as shown in the Second Schedule to the Act;

"loitering" means the persistent presence of any person for no apparent reason, however excluding persons who are carrying out a duty in the specific streets in Ta’ Xbiex as shown in the Schedule.

## 3. No loitering.

No person shall loiter in specific streets in Ta’ Xbiex as shown in the Schedule.

## 4. Penalty.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

Any person who is found loitering in specific streets in Ta’ Xbiex as shown in the Schedule shall be guilty of a contravention and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (69.88).
