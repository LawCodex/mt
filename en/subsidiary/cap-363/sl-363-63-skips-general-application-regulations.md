# Skips (General Application) Regulations (S.L. 363.63)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.63**

SKIPS (GENERAL APPLICATION) REGULATIONS 28th December, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 241 of 2000, as amended by Local Council Bye-Laws 28/9/2001, and Legal Notices 425 of 2007 and 476 of 2011.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Skips (General Application) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"Act" means the Local Government Act;

"authorised person" means any person holding a permit issued by a Local Council to use a skip or bin on wheels in terms of bye-law 4.2;

"bin on wheels" means any recipient on wheels that may be enclosed by means of a lid and which is intended for the deposit of domestic refuse but does not include a skip;

"commercial or industrial waste" means any waste or refuse, originating from any shop, factory, enterprise, or other entity operating commercially or in industry and includes bulky refuse irrespective from where it generates but does not include domestic refuse;

"domestic refuse" means household waste originating from private dwellings, including flats or residential homes but does not include commercial or industrial waste or bulky refuse;

"permit" means the permit issued to an authorised person as set out in Schedule A;

"skip" means any open or closed skip and includes any other recipient of this kind, used for the deposit of commercial or industrial waste, but does not include a bin on wheels.

*Applicability of regulations.*

3.1 These regulations shall apply to all Local Councils in relation to their locality and shall be deemed to be Bye-Laws for the purposes of article 34(2) of the Act.

3.2 Where any Local Council has made Bye-Laws regulating any matter provided for under these regulations, these regulations shall be deemed to be in addition to and not in derogation of such Bye-Laws:

Provided that in any case when such Bye-Laws provide differently from these regulations, the provisions of those Bye- Laws shall apply:

Provided further that any Local Council may make Bye- Laws in addition to these regulations to make special provision in relation to its locality.

*Placement of skips and bins on wheels. Amended by: L.N. 476 of 2011.*

4.1 No person other than the Council shall place any skip or bin on wheels in any public place within the Council’s locality boundary without a permit from the Council.

4.2 The Council may in its discretion issue a permit to authorise a person to place a skip or bin on wheels during such time and at such place and under such terms and conditions as may be stipulated in the permit.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

4.3 When a permit is granted, there shall be payable to the Council -

4.3.1 in the case of a skip, a fee of not more than one euro and sixteen cents (1.16) per day or part thereof for every skip;

4.3.2 in the case of a bin on wheels, a fee of not more than ninety-three cents (0.93) per day or part thereof for every bin on wheels.

4.4 Any person who contravenes any provision of this regulation or who fails to comply with any of the terms of the permit, shall be guilty of a contravention.

*Placement of skips and bins on wheels shall be free of danger. Added by: L.C.B.L. 28/9/ 2001.*

4.5 When placing skips or bins on wheels, a Local Council shall ensure that these do not pose any danger either to persons or vehicles.

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 28/9/ 2001.*

4.6 The application to a Local Council by a person to place a skip or bin on wheels within the locality shall be made at least one week before applicant intends to place the skip or bin on wheels.

## 5. Contraventions.

*Amended by: L.N. 476 of 2011.*

A person shall also be deemed to be guilty of a contravention if -

5.1 he causes any damage to any skip or bin on wheels or obliterates any markings printed on or affixed thereon, or scribbles or makes any mark of any sort on any skip or bin on wheels without the written consent of the Council or of the authorised person; or 5.2 after using the skip or bin on wheels he leaves its lid open or ajar, or deposits any refuse in such manner as to impede the lid from closing properly; or

5.3 he uses the skip or bin on wheels for any purpose other than that it is intended for, or deposits any waste or refuse which the skip or bin on wheels is not intended to receive; or

5.4 he deposits, throws down, drops or otherwise spills any waste or refuse outside, around or near the skip or bin on wheels; or

5.5 he removes or misplaces the skip or bin on wheels from where it is placed by the Council or by an authorised person within the space allocated for that purpose; or

5.6 he deposits waste or refuse in a skip or bin on wheels which according to the permit is intended for the exclusive use of an authorised person; or

5.7 he deposits in a bin on wheels any domestic refuse which is not contained in a closed bag, of a type and material approved by the Council; or

5.8 he deposits waste or refuse in a skip or bin on wheels other than that for which written authority has been issued by the Council; or

5.9 he deposits any commercial or industrial or domestic refuse, which has not originated from within the Council’s locality boundary;

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 28/9/ 2001.*

5.10 he deposits in any bin on wheels, waste, refuse, objects or substance which is not of a domestic nature; or deposits in any skip commercial and/or industrial waste when he is not authorised in writing by the relative Local Council to do so;

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 28/9/ 2001.*

5.11 in the case of a skip, he throws or deposits commercial and/or industrial waste or refuse which does not originate from the same locality, unless so authorised in writing by the Local Council and on registration or payment, if such is the case, of a fee as indicated in Column 2 of Schedule B;

*Added by: L.N. 476 of 2011.*

5.12 as owner or as the person providing the skip or bin on wheels failed to affix to the skip or bin on wheels, in a permanent manner and clearly legible, in Maltese and in English a updated copy of regulation 5 together with the details of the owner or provider of the skip or bin on wheels and the telephone number on which communication is possible at any time of the day.

*Registration of commercial and industrial establishments. Amended by: L.N. 476 of 2011.*

6.1 Any person who, through an activity in a locality, generates any commercial or industrial waste shall register with the Council of that locality, and shall keep such Council informed at all times of the contractor engaged by such person to collect and remove his commercial or industrial waste. Any person who fails to comply with this regulation shall be guilty of a contravention.

6.2 No person as is mentioned in regulation 6.1 shall throw or deposit commercial and, or industrial waste in the skips or bins on wheels supplied by the Council unless so authorised in writing by the said Council.

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 28/9/ 2001.*

6.3 Any person who engages in a commercial and/or industrial activity within the relative locality, which activity falls under any one of the categories of establishments listed in Column I of Schedule B, shall register with the Local Council in order to be granted authority to deposit commercial and/or industrial waste within the skips provided by the Local Council within the boundaries of the relative locality or else so that the waste may be collected by the Local Council contractor.

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 28/9/ 2001.*

6.4 For every registration made in terms of regulation 6.3 there shall be paid to the Local Council the fee as indicated in Column 2 of Schedule B.

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 28/9/ 2001.*

6.5 The Local Council may, for valid reasons given in writing, refuse to register a person in terms of regulation 6.3.

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 28/9/ 2001.*

6.6 No person listed in Column 1 of Schedule B shall throw or deposit commercial and/or industrial waste in the skips or bins on wheels provided by the Local Council or make use of the services provided by the Local Council for the collection of waste within the locality, unless registration is made together with the payment of the fee indicated in Column 2 of Schedule B.

*Offences and penalties. Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

7.1 Any person who commits any contravention under any of the provisions of regulation 5 shall, on conviction, be liable, unless a higher penalty is applicable under any other law -

7.1:1 for a first offence, to a fine (ammenda) of forty-six euro and fifty-nine cents (€46.59); and

7.1.2 for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88).

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

7.2 In the case of an offence under regulation 4, a person shall be liable on conviction to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88); and in the case of a continuing offence, to an additional fine (ammenda) of eleven euro and sixty-five cents (€11.65) for each day during which the offence continues.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

7.3 In the case of an offence under regulation 6.1 a person shall be liable to conviction to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88) and in case of a continuing offence, to an additional fine (ammenda) of eleven euro and sixty-five cents (€11.65) for each day during which the offence continues.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

7.4 In the case of contravention under regulation 6.2, a person shall be liable on conviction to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88).

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 28/9/ 2001. Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

7.5 In the case of a contravention under regulations 4.5 and 4.6, a person shall be liable on conviction to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88), and in the case of a continuing contravention, to another fine (ammenda) of eleven euro and sixty-five cents (€11.65) for each day during which the contravention continues.

*Added by: L.C.B.L. 28/9/ 2001. Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

7.6 In the case of a contravention under regulation 6.6, a person shall be liable on conviction to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-nine euro and eighty-eight cents (€69.88).

## 8. Civil damages.

The imposition of a penalty under these regulations shall be without prejudice to the right of the Council to recover any civil damages caused by the commission of an offence under these regulations.

## 9. Termination or cancellation of registration.

The Council may terminate the permit, if it is satisfied that any of the terms and conditions under which the said permit was issued has not been complied with, or if there is a substantial change in circumstances which in the opinion of the Council render it necessary to withdraw the permit. In this case the Council shall not be obliged to refund any fee paid under regulation 4.3.

## 10. Regulations not to prejudice article 34(2) of the Act.

These regulations are without prejudice to the provisions to the provisions of the proviso to article 34(2) of the Act.
