# Local Government (Procurement) Regulations (S.L. 363.186)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.186**

**LOCAL GOVERNMENT (PROCUREMENT)**

**REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 266 of 2017, as amended by Legal Notice 90 of 2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Local Government (Procurement) Regulations.

## 2.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"Director" has the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

"Procurement Guidelines" means the Procurement Guidelines issued by the Director in terms of article 80 of the Act, to be followed by the Local Councils in the execution of their functions and responsibilities under the Act and in accordance with these regulations;

## 3. Applicability of the Public Procurement Regulations.

*S.L.601.03.*

Save as otherwise provided in the Procurement Guidelines issued by the Director, as approved by the Minister, the Public Procurement Regulations shall apply to Local Councils.

## 4. Transitory Provision.

*Added by: L.N. 90 of 2018. S.L. 363.03*

The Appeals Board as established in the Local Councils (Tendering) Regulations, shall hear and decide those appeals which were lodged and presented before it, which appeals were lodged and presented until the 1st January, 2018 and which were not decided until the aforementioned date.
