# Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Safi Local Council Order (S.L. 363.88)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.88**

**DELEGATION OF ADMINISTRATION OF**

**FACILITIES TO SAFI LOCAL COUNCIL ORDER**

12th April, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 83 of 2002.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Delegation of Administration of Facilities to Safi Local Council Order.

## 2. Delegation of functions. Cap. 363.

The property described in article 3 of this Order is hereby being specified for the purposes of the delegation of functions mentioned in article 33(1)(m) of the Local Government Act, as from the date of the publication of this Order.

## 3. Description.

The property in the locality of Safi referred to in article 2 consists as follows:

(a) Sir Alexander Ball Gardens, shown edged in red on plan L.D. 73/96 attached to the Lease Agreement for the Administration and Use of Sir Alexander Ball garden at Safi by the Safi Local Council signed on the 10th March, 1999;

(b) Safi Council’s Administrative Offices shown edged in red on plan L.D. 108/97 attached to the Lease Agreement relating to property at Triq l-Iskola, Safi, signed on the 1st November, 1995;

(c) Football Ground (synthetic football pitch), shown bordered red and is indicated as Football Ground (Sand) on plan P.D. No 133/2000 attached to the Lease Agreement for the Administration and Use of Government Property by the Safi Local Council signed on the 15th February, 2001.
