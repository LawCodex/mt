# Dissolution of Sannat Local Council Order (S.L. 363.21)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.21**

**DISSOLUTION OF SANNAT LOCAL COUNCIL**

**ORDER**

16th April, 1999

LEGAL NOTICE 70 of 1999.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Dissolution of Sannat Local Council Order.

## 2. Dissolution of Sannat Local Council. Cap. 363.

The Sannat Local Council is hereby being dissolved due to a persistent non-regard to the provisions of the Local Councils Act, after formal notice dated 29 January 1999 has been given by the Minister responsible for Local Government.
