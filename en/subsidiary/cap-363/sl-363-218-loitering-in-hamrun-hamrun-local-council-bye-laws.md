# Loitering in Ħamrun (Ħamrun Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.218)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.218**

**LOITERING IN ĦAMRUN (ĦAMRUN LOCAL**

**COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS**

13th September, 2019 LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 27/09/2019.

## 1. Citation.

(1) The title of these Bye-Laws is the Loitering in Ħamrun (Ħamrun Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"the Council" means the Ħamrun Local Council;

"locality" means the locality of Ħamrun as shown in the Second Schedule to the Act;

"loitering" means the persistent presence of any person for no apparent reason;

"street" shall have the same meaning as given to it in the Act.

## 3. No loitering.

No person shall loiter within any street in Ħamrun.

## 4. Penalties.

Any person who is found loitering in any street in Ħamrun, shall be guilty of a contravention and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70), together with a further fine of not more than fifteen euro (€15) for every day during which any of these provisions of these Bye-Laws is breached.
