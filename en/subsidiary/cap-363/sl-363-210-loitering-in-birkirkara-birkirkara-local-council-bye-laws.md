# Loitering in Birkirkara (Birkirkara Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.210)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.210**

LOITERING IN BIRKIRKARA (BIRKIRKARA LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS

22nd February, 2019

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 14/02/2019.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these Bye-Laws is the Loitering in Birkirkara (Birkirkara Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

''the Act'' means the Local Government Act;

''Council'' means the Birkirkara Local Council;

''locality'' means the locality of Birkirkara as shown in the Second Schedule to the Act;

''loitering'' means the persistent presence of any person for no apparent reason;

''road'' means the same as the definition provided in the Act.

## 3. No loitering.

No person shall loiter within any road in Birkirkara.

## 4. Penalty.

Any person who is found loitering in any road in Birkirkara, shall be guilty of a contravention and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (ammenda) of sixty-five euro (€65) and to a further fine (ammenda) not exceeding fifteen euro (€15) for every day during which one of the provisions of these Bye-Laws is breached.
