# Presidents of the Regions, Mayors, Deputy Mayors, Local Councillors and Presidents of the Associations’ Allowances Regulations (S.L. 363.215)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.215**

PRESIDENTS OF THE REGIONS, MAYORS, DEPUTY MAYORS, LOCAL COUNCILLORS AND

**PRESIDENTS OF THE ASSOCIATIONS’**

**ALLOWANCES REGULATIONS**

16th July, 2019

LEGAL NOTICE 165 of 2019, as amended by Legal Notices 277 of 2019 and 109 of 2025.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Presidents of the Regions, Mayors, Deputy Mayors, Local Councillors and Presidents of the Associations’ Allowances Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"allowance" means the allowance provided for in article 32(2) of the Act;

"Association" shall have the same meaning assigned to it in the Act;

"councillor" shall have the same meaning assigned to it in the Act;

"Deputy Mayor" shall have the same meaning assigned to it in the Act;

"Mayor" shall have the same meaning assigned it in the Act;

*  S.L. 363.06.*

"Members elected on the general list" shall have the same meaning assigned to it in the Local Councils (Association) Regulations;

*  S.L. 363.06.*

"President of the Association" means the office provided for in regulation 14 of the Local Councils (Association) Regulations;

"President of the Region" means the office provided for in article 37A of the Act.

## 3. Mayor, Deputy Mayor and councillors’ allowance.

*Substituted/ Renumbered by: L.N. 277 of 2019. Substituted by: L.N. 109 of 2025.*

The allowance paid to the Mayor, Deputy Mayor and councillors shall be of three thousand and four hundred euro (€3,400) expense allowance per annum. In addition, the Deputy Mayor shall be given an additional one thousand euro (€1,000) to reflect his office.

## 4. President of the Region’s allowance.

*Renumbered by: L.N. 277 of 2019. Substituted by: L.N. 109 of 2025.*

The allowance paid to a President of the Region shall be equivalent to ninety per cent (90%) of the honorarium of a member of the House and to an additional one thousand euro (€1,000).

## 5. Chairman of the Association’s allowance.

*Renumbered by: L.N. 277 of 2019. Substituted by: L.N. 109 of 2025.*

The allowance paid to the Chairman of the Association shall be equivalent to ninety per cent (90%) of the honorarium of a member of the House and to an additional one thousand euro (€1,000).

## 6. Members elected on the general list’s allowance. 

*Renumbered by: L.N. 277 of 2019. Amended by: L.N. 109 of 2025.*

From 1st July 2019, the allowance paid to to the Members elected on the general list shall be equivalent to thirty-three per cent (33%) of the honorarium of a member of the House.

## 7. Method of payment of allowances.

*Renumbered by: L.N. 277 of 2019. Substituted by: L.N. 109 of 2025.*

The allowances referred to in these regulations shall be paid pro rata every month.
