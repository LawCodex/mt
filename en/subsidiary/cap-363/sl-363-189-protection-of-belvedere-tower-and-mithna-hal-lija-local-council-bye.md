# Protection of Belvedere Tower and Mitħna (Ħal Lija Local Council) Bye-Laws (S.L. 363.189)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 363.189**

**PROTECTION OF BELVEDERE TOWER AND**

MITĦNA (ĦAL LIJA LOCAL COUNCIL) BYE-LAWS 17th November, 2017

LOCAL COUNCIL BYE-LAW 32/09/2017

## 1. Citation.

The title of these is the Protection of Belvedere Tower and Mitħna (Ħal Lija Local Council) Bye-Laws.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Bye-Laws unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 363.*

"the Act" means the Local Government Act;

"advertisement" means any type of advertisement, including any commercial or non-commercial promotion, and includes also any other notice, notification or message printed or in writing;

"Council" means the Ħal Lija Local Council;

"locality" means the Ħal Lija locality;

"street furniture" includes any object which may be placed or fixed to the ground or placed or fixed to the wall or to any other part of the Tower and the Mitħna;

"the Mitħna" means the Mitħna devolved to the Council on the 09th April 2003 and situated in Triq San Liċinju corner with Triq Annibale Preca;

"the Tower" means the Belvedere Tower devolved to the Council on the 12th January 2011 and situated in Transfiguration Avenue.

## 3. Prohibitions.

No person shall without prior written authorisation from the Council:

(a) make any type of ties to the Tower and the Mitħna with ropes or by any other means;

(b) fix any board, banner, sticker, or advertisement to any part of the Tower and the Mitħna;

(c) fix or place any street furniture to or on the Tower and the Mitħna;

## 4. Penalties.

Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with any of the provisions of these Bye-Laws shall be guilty of a contravention and shall be liable, if found guilty:

(a) for a first contravention to a fine (ammenda) of seventy euro (€70);

(b) in the case of a continuing contravention to a fine (ammenda) of fifteen euro (€15) for every day during which the contravention continues.

## 5. Civil damages.

The imposition of a penalty under these Bye-Laws shall be without prejudice to the right of the Council to recover all civil damage cause by the commission of any contravention under these Bye-Laws, including the right to carry out the repairs at the expense of the guilty person.
