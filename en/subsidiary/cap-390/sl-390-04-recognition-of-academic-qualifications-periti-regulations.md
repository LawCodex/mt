# Recognition of Academic Qualifications (Periti) Regulations (S.L. 390.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 390.04**

RECOGNITION OF ACADEMIC QUALIFICATIONS (PERITI) REGULATIONS\*

15th February, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 39 of 2002.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Recognition of Academic Qualifications (Periti) Regulations.

## 2. Definitions.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 390.*

"Act" means the Architecture and Civil Engineering Professionals (Periti) Act;

"Board" means the Bord tal-Warrant tal-Periti, established by article 6 of the Act;

"warrant" and "warrant holder" have the same meaning as is assigned to them in article 5 of the Act.

## 3. Academic qualifications.

The academic qualifications obtained by a person who applies to the Board to be granted the warrant shall cover the basic subjects listed in the Schedule. The subjects are listed in the first column. These are described in the second column whilst the minimum duration of time dedicated to these subjects during the course of studies is listed in the third column.

\*Repealed by Article 30 of Act XIII of 2021 (Cap. 622).

## SCHEDULE

(Regulation 3)

**CORE SUBJECTS FOR PERSONS WHO APPLY FOR THE WARRANT TO**

**PRACTICE AS ARCHITECT AND CIVIL ENGINEER (PERIT)**

The applicant for the warrant to practice as an Architect and Civil Engineer (Perit) has to satisfy the Board that he has followed courses at University level over a period of not less than four years full-time or the part-time equivalent in the following areas of studies and of a duration not less than that indicated. The time allocated per subject area refers to contact hours and is to include lectures, tutorials, seminars, design sessions, site visits but excludes private study and practical experience in design offices or construction sites.
