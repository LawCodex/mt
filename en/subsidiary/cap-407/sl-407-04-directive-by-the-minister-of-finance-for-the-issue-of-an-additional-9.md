# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an Additional €9,317,493.59 6.6% Malta Government Stock 2019 (First Issue) (S.L. 407.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 407.04**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL €9,317,493.59

**6.6% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2019**

**(FIRST ISSUE)**

7th September, 1999

LEGAL NOTICE 156 of 1999, as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional €9,317,493.59 6.6% Malta Government Stock 2019 (First Issue).

## 2. .Additional issue.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in €93,174,935.94 6.6% Malta Government Stock 2019 (I) an additional issue of €9,317,493.59 6.6% Malta Government Stock 2019 (I) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6.6% Malta Government Stock 2019 (I) created by means of Legal Notice 135 of 1999 to a total amount of €102,492,429.54.
