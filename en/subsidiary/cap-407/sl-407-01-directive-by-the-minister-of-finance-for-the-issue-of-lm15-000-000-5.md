# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of Lm15,000,000 5.6% Malta Government Stock 2005 (Second Issue - 2nd Tranche) (S.L. 407.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 407.01**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF LM15,000,000 5.6% MALTA

**GOVERNMENT STOCK 2005**

**(SECOND ISSUE - 2ND TRANCHE)**

3rd August, 1999

LEGAL NOTICE 133 of 1999.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of Lm15,000,000 5.6% Malta Government Stock 2005 (Second Issue - 2nd Tranche).

## 2. Mode of issuing stock.

This stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and/or entities are eligible to apply for this Stock. Allotment will be applied and set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

(a) An application for the stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange will be made in terms of the Malta Stock Exchange Act, when the stock has been allotted.

(b) On 1st February, 2000 after the payment of the first dividend, this stock shall be integrated with the existing 5.6% Malta Government Stock 2005 (II) and shall thereafter both be deemed to be one stock.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

The sum of money to be raised by the stock shall be Lm15,000,000.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

Interest at the rate of Lm2.80 for every Lm100 stock will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 1st day of February and on the 1st day of August in every year for the duration of the stock. The first payment pro rata, covering the period from the 2nd day of September, 1999 to the 1st day of February, 2000 shall be made on the 1st day of February, 2000.

## 7. Redemption of stock.

The stock shall be redeemed at par on the 1st August, 2005.
