# Commission Agents (Licences) Regulations (S.L. 13.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 13.01**

**COMMISSION AGENTS (LICENCES)**

**REGULATIONS**

1st January, 1989 LEGAL NOTICE 115 of 1988, as amended by Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Commission Agents (Licences) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations -

*Cap. 13.*

"the Code" means the Commercial Code; and

"the Council" means the Council of the Chamber of Commerce. 3. The fee which shall be levied by the Council for the grant of a licence to act as a Commission Agent under the provisions of article 71 of the Code shall be -

*Fee to be levied. Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

(i) the sum of twenty-three euros and twenty-nine cents (23.29) which shall be payable on or before the grant of the licence; and thereafter (ii) the sum of twenty-three euros and twenty-nine cents (23.29) (hereinafter called "the annual fee") which shall be payable on the 1st January in each year during the currency of the licence. 4. If any annual fee payable under the preceding regulation on the 1st January in any year is not paid before the 1st February in that year, the Council shall, as soon after the 1st February as may be convenient, address a communication to the person whose annual fee has not so been paid drawing his attention to his failure to pay such fee, and the Council shall be deemed to have complied with this regulation if such communication be sent pre-paid, by registered post, to the usual or last known address of the person whose fee has not so been paid.

*Failure to pay fee.*

## 5. Lapse of licence.

(1) If any annual fee is not paid within thirty days after the date upon which the communication aforesaid has been sent by post, the licence to which it relates shall cease to be in force sine die, and if the person to whom it was granted wishes again to act as a Commission Agent he shall be required to apply for a new licence in accordance with the provisions of article 71 of the Code.

(2) In considering under that article any such application and the fitness of the applicant to act as a commission agent, the Council may take into consideration the previous failure of the applicant to pay the annual fee and of any explanation regarding that failure which the applicant may adduce.

(3) When a licence ceases to be in force, the fact shall be notified in the Gazette.
