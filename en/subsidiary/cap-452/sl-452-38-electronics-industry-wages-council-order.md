# Electronics Industry Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.38)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.38**

**ELECTRONICS INDUSTRY**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

14th February, 1975

LEGAL NOTICE 16 of 1975.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Electronics Industry Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

There shall be an Electronics Industry Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or part of an undertaking the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in the manufacture, repair or assembly of electronic or scientific equipment.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 141 of 2026.
