# Wage Increase (Employees) National Standard Order (S.L. 452.140)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.140**

**WAGE INCREASE (EMPLOYEES) NATIONAL**

**STANDARD ORDER**

1st January, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 290 of 2025.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Wage Increase (Employees) National Standard Order.

## 2. Cost of living increase.

(1) The wages of whole-time employees shall be increased by four euro and sixty-six cents (€4.66) per week.

(2) The hourly rate of part-time employees shall be increased by the same amount as the increase in the hourly rate of a comparable whole-time employee, calculated after including the weekly increases specified in sub-article (1), or in cases where there is no comparable category of whole-time employees at the workplace, by the hourly rate of a comparable whole-time employee in an applicable Wage Regulation Order, or in the absence of an applicable Wage Regulation Order, by one-fortieth (1/40) of the rate of increases as specified in sub-article (1).
