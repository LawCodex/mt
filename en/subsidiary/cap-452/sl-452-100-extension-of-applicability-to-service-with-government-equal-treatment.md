# Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Equal Treatment in Employment) Regulations (S.L. 452.100)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.100**

**EXTENSION OF APPLICABILITY TO SERVICE**

**WITH GOVERNMENT (EQUAL TREATMENT**

**IN EMPLOYMENT) REGULATIONS**

13th March 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 524of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Equal Treatment in Employment) Regulations.

## 2. Applicability to government. Cap. 452. S.L. 452.95

Article 28 of the Employment and Industrial Relations Act, and article 31 of the same Act, insofar as it is the empowering provision by virtue of which the Equal Treatment in Employment Regulations were issued, shall be applicable to service with the Government.
