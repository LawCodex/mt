# Travel Insurance Agencies Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.33)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.33**

**TRAVEL INSURANCE AGENCIES**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

9th February, 1968

LEGAL NOTICE 19 of 1968.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Travel and Insurance Agencies Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

There shall be a Travel and Insurance Agencies Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or part of an undertaking, the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in the provision of services relating to travel (including freight by sea or air) or insurance or estate agency and for watchmen, telephone operators and other persons employed by shipping agents, travel agents or contractors on board ships whilst in the territorial waters of Malta.

## 3. Exclusion.

The Wages Council shall not operate in respect of employees whose conditions of work are regulated by tariff made by or under the law or employees falling within the field of operation of any other Wages Council.

## 4. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 31st October, 1967.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 126 of 2026.
