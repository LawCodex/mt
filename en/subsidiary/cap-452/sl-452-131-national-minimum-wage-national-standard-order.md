# National Minimum Wage National Standard Order (S.L. 452.131)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.131**

**NATIONAL MINIMUM WAGE NATIONAL**

**STANDARD ORDER**

1st January, 2024 LEGAL NOTICE 287 of 2023.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the National Minimum Wage National Standard Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 452.*

"Act" means the Employment and Industrial Relations Act;

"Agreement" means the National Agreement on the Minimum Wage Recommendations by the Low Wage Commission signed on the 25th October 2023 between the Government, the trade unions and organisations of employers as represented on the Malta Council for Economic and Social Development.

## 3. National minimum wage.

The national minimum wage per week related to a normal working week shall be in accordance with the said Agreement as follows:

**Age eighteen (18) years and over ………………€213.54**

**Age seventeen (17) years ……………………….€206.76**

**Under seventeen (17) years ……………………..€203.92**

## 4. Part-time employees.

The national minimum wage of part-time employees shall be calculated pro rata at the same hourly rate of a comparable whole-time employee as applicable in accordance with the minimum wage of the relevant Wage Regulation Order, or in cases where a Wage Regulation Order is not applicable, at an hourly rate not below the National Standard Minimum Wage applicable, as determined in accordance with the preceding article and divided by forty (40).

## 5. Wages Council Wage Regulation Orders.

Where any Wages Council Wage Regulation Order applies to any employee, the wages payable to such employee in respect of 2024 shall not be less than those laid down by such Wages Council Wage Regulation Order applicable for 2023, adjusted for cost of living by any increases of wages declared to be generally applicable to all whole-time employees in accordance with article 8 of the Act, or by any wage increases for cost of living laid down by any national standard order applicable in respect of 2024.
