# Jewellery and Watches (Conditions of Work) Regulation Order (S.L. 452.164)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.164**

**JEWELLERY AND WATCHES (CONDITIONS OF**

**WORK) REGULATION ORDER**

30th June, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 136 of 2026.

## 1. Citation, commencement and applicability.

(1) The title of this order is the Jewellery and Watches (Conditions of Work) Regulation Order.

(2) This order shall come into force within two (2) months from its date of publication in the Gazette.

(3) The provisions established in this order shall apply to all employees in any undertaking or part of an undertaking, the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in gilding, engraving in gold or silver or in the manufacture or repair of jewellery, clocks and watches:

Provided that in the event that there is a collective agreement applicable to the class of employees covered by this order, the more favourable conditions in such agreement or in this order shall apply.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 452.*

*Cap. 252.*

"day" means a period of twenty-four (24) consecutive hours commencing from midnight (12.00a.m) of one day to midnight (12.00a.m) of the next following day;

"double-time" means double the actual time rate applicable to the employee;

"hours of work" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

"jewellery" means articles made wholly or mainly of or covered with gold, silver, platinum or other precious metals and includes diamonds or precious stones, articles of rolled gold;

"period of employment" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

*S.L. 452.87.*

"time-and-a-half" means one and a half times the actual time rate applicable to the employee;

"week" means a calendar week;

"whole-time employee" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act.

## 3. Hours of work.

The normal hours of work of the establishment shall be distributed between the times of 7:30 a.m. and 5:30 p.m. on any day of the week from Monday to Friday, both days inclusive. Hours worked on Saturday shall be compensated by an equivalent number of hours of time off in lieu.

## 5. Uniform allowance.

(1) When the employee is required by the employer to wear a uniform in the performance of his duties, such uniform shall be provided by the employer free of charge.

(2) Personal protective equipment in accordance with the Minimum Requirements for the use of Personal Protective Equipment at Work Regulations shall also be provided by the employer free of charge.

## 6. Minimum daily rest.

Whole-time employees, other than watchpersons, shall be allowed intervals for meals and rest of not less than one and a half (1½) hours in the aggregate on any day from Monday to Friday and of not less than a quarter (¼) of an hour on any Saturday.

## 11. Waiting time.

An employee shall be entitled to the payment of the minimum remuneration applicable to him for all time during which he is present on the premises of his employer or elsewhere unless he is present thereon in any of the following circumstances:

(a) without the employer’s express or implied consent;

(b) for some purpose unrelated with his work, other than that of waiting for work to be assigned to him;

(c) solely by reason of the fact that he is resident thereon;

(d) during normal meal times in a room or place in which no work is being done, and he is not waiting for any work to be assigned to him.

## 12. Other forms of leave.  S.L. 452.101.

Employees shall b entitled to the national holidays and to all public holidays with full pay, to all forms of leave in accordance with the Minimum Special Leave Entitlement Regulations and all other forms of leave that may, from time to time, be stipulated in the National Standard Orders or any regulations made under the Act.
