# Representation Fees Regulations (S.L. 452.61)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.61**

**REPRESENTATION FEES REGULATIONS**

5th August, 1986

LEGAL NOTICE 48 of 1986, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Representation Fees Regulations.

## 2. Tariff of fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

The following tariff shall apply to persons duly representing any party in proceedings before the Industrial Tribunal for cases of alleged unfair dismissal:

€

(a) for the drafting, filing and presentation

**of an introductory statement of case**

**including the filing of any documents**

**ancillary thereto or any other documents**

**required by the Industrial Tribunal**

during the same proceedings:

(i) if the statement is in respect of the

employer or of one employee:

from 34.94

to 46.59

(ii) if the statement is made jointly in

respect of two or more employees 46.59

(b) for every attendance before the

**Industrial Tribunal or at an inspection in**

faciem loci ............................................ 9.32

Provided that the total amount of fees charged under this regulation shall not exceed €93.17 in any one case.

## 3. Joint claims.

The fee laid down in regulation 2(b) shall apply to every case independently of the number of employees represented in that case.

## 4. Claims for fees.

Claims for the payment of fees for representing either party in proceedings before the Tribunal may, at the request of any party, be vetted by the Secretary of the Tribunal, or by any other officer designated to assist the Secretary, who shall certify that the sum claimed is in accordance with that taxed in the award or decision or, in the absence of that taxation, in accordance with the provisions of these regulations.

## 5. Certificate of fees.

A certificate as provided in the last preceding regulation may also be issued to any party to the proceedings.

## 6. Request for reimbursement.

The request to the Tribunal for an order of reimbursement of any amount paid in excess of that laid down in these regulations shall be made by letter addressed to the Secretary of the Tribunal, and such order shall be deemed an executive title for the purposes of paragraphs article 251(c) and (d) of the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure.

*Cap. 12.*
