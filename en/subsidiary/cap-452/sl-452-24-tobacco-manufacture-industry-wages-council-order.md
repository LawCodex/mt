# Tobacco Manufacture Industry Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.24)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.24**

**TOBACCO MANUFACTURE INDUSTRY**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

24th September, 1965

LEGAL NOTICE 49 of 1965.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Tobacco Manufacture Industry Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

There shall be a Tobacco Manufacture Industry Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or in any part of an undertaking, the activities of which consist wholly or mainly of the manufacture of cigars, cigarettes, cheroots or pipe or other tobacco and any form of processing of tobacco, whether by hand or by machine.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 25th May, 1965.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 128 of 2026.
