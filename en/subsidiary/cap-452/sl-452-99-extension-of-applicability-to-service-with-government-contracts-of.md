# Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Contracts of Service for a Fixed Term) Regulations (S.L. 452.99)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.99**

**EXTENSION OF APPLICABILITY TO SERVICE**

WITH GOVERNMENT (CONTRACTS OF SERVICE

**FOR A FIXED TERM) REGULATIONS**

15th June, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 157 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Contracts of Service for a Fixed Term) Regulations.

## 2. Applicability to government. Cap. 452. S.L. 452.81

Article 34(1) and (3) of the Employment and Industrial Relations Act and any regulations or orders made thereunder, except the provisions of regulation 7(3) of the Contracts of Service for a Fixed Term Regulations, shall be applicable to service with the Government.
