# Hotels and Clubs Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.15)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.15**

**HOTELS AND CLUBS**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

23rd September, 1955

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 529 of 1955, as amended by Legal Notices 28 of 1980 and 1 and 67 of 1989.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Hotels and Clubs Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

*Amended by: L.N. 28 of 1980; L.N. 1 of 1989. Substituted: L.N. 67 of 1989.*

The Hotels and Clubs Wages Council shall operate for all employees engaged in hotels, guest houses, holiday complexes or tourist villages, licensed holiday flats, clubs, coffee shops, bars and restaurants.

## 3. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 28 of 1980.*

(a) The term "hotel" includes any inn, boarding house, lodging house and also includes any bar, restaurant or shop run directly by the management of the hotel as part of the amenities of that hotel.

(b) The word "club" is referable to clubs of any description whatsoever, including social clubs, sports clubs and band clubs, and includes any bar, restaurant or shop either run directly by the management of the club as part of the amenities of the club, or given out under contract for the purpose of increasing those amenities.

(c) The terms ''guest houses'', ''holiday complexes or tourist villages'', and ''licensed holiday flats'' have the same meaning as is assigned to them by the Malta Travel and Tourism Services Act.

*Cap. 409.*

## 4. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of 22nd June, 1954.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 137 of 2026.
