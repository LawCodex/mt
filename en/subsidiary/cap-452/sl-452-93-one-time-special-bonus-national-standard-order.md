# One-time Special Bonus National Standard Order (S.L. 452.93)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.93**

**ONE-TIME SPECIAL BONUS NATIONAL**

**STANDARD ORDER**

5th March, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 110 of 2004, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the One-time Special Bonus National Standard Order.

## 2. One-time Special Bonus.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

A lump sum amount of €90.85, equivalent to €1.75 per week for twelve months, shall be paid by the employer, by the end of March 2004, to all employees who are on the employer’s books on Monday, 15th March, 2004 and in respect of whom social security contributions are payable.

## 3. Recovery of amount by an employer.

Such sum as is mentioned in article 2 shall be recovered by the employer by way of offsetting against funds due by such employer to Government in respect of income tax and social security contributions.
