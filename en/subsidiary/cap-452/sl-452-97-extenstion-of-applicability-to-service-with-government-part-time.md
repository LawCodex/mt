# Extenstion of Applicability to Service with Government (Part-Time Employees) Regulations (S.L. 452.97)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.97**

**EXTENSION OF APPLICABILITY TO SERVICE**

WITH GOVERNMENT (PART-TIME EMPLOYEES)

**REGULATIONS**

9th March, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 46 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Part-Time Employees) Regulations.

## 2. Applicability to government. Cap. 452.

Article 25 of the Employment and Industrial Relations Act and any regulations or orders made thereunder shall be applicable to service with the government.
