# Jewellery and Watches Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.31)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.31**

**JEWELLERY AND WATCHES**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

2nd February, 1968

LEGAL NOTICE 13 of 1968, as amended by Legal Notice 25 of 1974.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Jewellery and Watches Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

There shall be a Jewellery and Watches Wages Council which shall operate for all persons employed in any undertaking or part of an undertaking, the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in gilding, engraving in gold or silver or in the manufacture of jewellery, clocks and watches.

## 3. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 25 of 1974.*

In this order, unless the context otherwise requires -

"manufacture" includes repair;

"jewellery" means articles made wholly or mainly of or covered with gold, silver, platinum or other previous metals and includes diamonds or precious stones, articles of rolled gold.

## 4. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 14th November, 1967.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 136 of 2026.
