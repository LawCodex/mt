# Sextons and Custodians Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.70)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.70**

**SEXTONS AND CUSTODIANS**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

28th January, 1992

LEGAL NOTICE 16 of 1992.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Sextons and Custodians Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

(1) There shall be a Sextons and Custodians Wages Council which operates for sextons and custodians of churches, museums or buildings open for exhibitions to the public.

(2) In this article the word "custodian" includes, besides a person wholly employed for the custody of a church, museum or building, any person principally or mainly engaged in the cleaning, maintenance or security of such church, museum or building.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of intention to issue this Order was published in the Government Gazette of the 25th October, 1991.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 130 of 2026.
