# Agriculture and Allied Industries Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.30)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.30**

**AGRICULTURE AND ALLIED INDUSTRIES**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

5th December, 1967

LEGAL NOTICE 81 of 1967.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Agriculture and Allied Industries Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

There shall be an Agriculture and Allied Industries Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or part of an undertaking the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in any of the following -

Agriculture, horticulture, floriculture, and viticulture, the breeding and rearing of livestock, poultry, rabbits, the keeping of bees and the processing of dairy products and products of a similar nature.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 27th June, 1967.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 123 of 2026.
