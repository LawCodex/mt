# Leather Goods and Shoes Industries Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.23)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.23**

**LEATHER GOODS AND SHOES INDUSTRIES**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

17th September, 1965

LEGAL NOTICE 45 of 1965, as amended by Legal Notice 27 of 1967.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Leather Goods and Shoes Industries Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

*Amended by: L.N. 27 of 1967.*

There shall be a Leather Goods and Shoes Industries Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or in any part of an undertaking, the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in the following:

(a) the manufacture and processing of leather, leather goods, including the manufacture of handbags and gloves; and

(b) the manufacture of boots, shoes, sandals and other footwear, as well as of luggage, brief-cases, hand-bags, shopping bags and other bags of a similar nature. 3. Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 25th May, 1965.

*Date of publication of notice of intention.*

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 117 of 2026.
