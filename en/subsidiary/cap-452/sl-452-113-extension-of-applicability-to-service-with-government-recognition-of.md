# Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Recognition of Trade Unions) Regulations (S.L. 452.113)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.113**

**EXTENSION OF APPLICABILITY TO SERVICE**

**WITH GOVERNMENT (RECOGNITION OF**

**TRADE UNIONS) REGULATIONS**

9th December, 2016

LEGAL NOTICE 414 of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Recognition of Trade Unions) Regulations.

## 2. Applicability to Government. Cap. 452. S.L. 452.112

Article 48(2) of the Employment and Industrial Relations Act, in so far as it is the empowering provision by virtue of which the Recognition of Trade Unions Regulations, were issued, shall be applicable to service with the government.
