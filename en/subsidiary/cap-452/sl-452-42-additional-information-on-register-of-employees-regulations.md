# Additional Information on Register of Employees Regulations (S.L. 452.42)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.42**

**ADDITIONAL INFORMATION ON REGISTER OF**

**EMPLOYEES REGULATIONS**

9th April, 1976

LEGAL NOTICE 50 of 1976.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Additional Information on Register of Employees Regulations.

## 2. Additional information in register. Cap. 452.

The Register or Registers to be kept for the purposes of the Employment and Industrial Relations Act shall also contain the following additional information in respect of each employee :

(a) national insurance number;

(b) date of engagement;

(c) holidays and sick leave entitlements;

(d) the dates of holidays with pay and sick leave with pay, if any, granted to the employee;

(e) Work Book Number; and

(f) in the case of an employee whose conditions of employment are regulated by any wage regulation order stipulating conditions dependent on experience in the trade or previous employment, details in respect of such experience or previous employment, as the case may be, including the duration thereof.
