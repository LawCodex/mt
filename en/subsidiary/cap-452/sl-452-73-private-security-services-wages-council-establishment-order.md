# Private Security Services Wages Council (Establishment) Order (S.L. 452.73)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.73**

PRIVATE SECURITY SERVICES WAGES COUNCIL (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER\*

25th May, 1999

LEGAL NOTICE 86 of 1999.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Private Security Services Wages Council (Establishment) Order.

## 2. Establishment of Wages Council.

There shall be a Wages Council which operates for all employees who work with establishments providing private security services which include bouncers, watchmen, wardens, bodyguards, security officers and security attendants.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of intention to issue this Order was published in the Government Gazette of the 20th April, 1999.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 112 of 2026.
