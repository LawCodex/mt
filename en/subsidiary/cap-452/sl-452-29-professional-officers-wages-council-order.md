# Professional Officers Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.29)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.29**

**PROFESSIONAL OFFICERS**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

21st November, 1967

LEGAL NOTICE 76 of 1967, as amended by Legal Notices 31 of 1969 and 7 of 1970.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Professional Officers Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

*Amended by: L.N. 31 of 1969; L.N. 7 of 1970.*

There shall be a Professional Officers Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any office or undertaking or part of an undertaking the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in employment in any of the offices and establishments of the following -

(a) trade unions including associations of a similar character, chambers of professional and business people and associations of a similar character;

(b) offices of architects, engineers, legal advisers, notaries public, lotto receivers, accountants, book-keepers, auditors and consultants in any branch of engineering, architecture, law and accounting;

(c) research and scientific establishments (except laboratories attached to private hospitals);

(d) market research units, advertising agencies, establishments which receive funds from the public for investment in unit trusts or which run the management of unit trusts (except establishments carrying out wholly or mainly insurance business).

## 3. Exclusion.

This Wages Council shall not operate in respect of employees whose remuneration for work is regulated by tariff made by a law.

## 4. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 30th June, 1967.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 134 of 2026.
