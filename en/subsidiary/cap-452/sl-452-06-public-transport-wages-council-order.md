# Public Transport Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.06**

**PUBLIC TRANSPORT**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

1st August, 1952

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 377 of 1952.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Pubilc Transport Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

The Public Transport Wages Council shall operate for the employees specified hereunder employed in the conveyance of passengers by motor ominibuses licensed to ply for hire on public service routes, namely -

(a) inspectors;

(b) drivers;

(c) conductors;

(d) ticket sellers; and

(e) ticket booth attendants.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of intention of this Order was published in the Gazette of the 12th May, 1952.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 133 of 2026.
