# Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Employment Status) Regulations (S.L. 452.109)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.109**

**EXTENSION OF APPLICABILITY TO SERVICE**

**WITH GOVERNMENT (EMPOLYMENT STATUS)**

**REGULATIONS**

31st January, 2012

LEGAL NOTICE 45 of 2012.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Employment Status) Regulations.

## 2. Applicability to government. Cap. 452.

*S.L.452.108*

Article 4 of the Employment and Industrial Relations Act, in so far as it is the empowering provision by virtue of which the Employment Status National Standard Order, was issued, shall be applicable to service with the Government.
