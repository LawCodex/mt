# Paper, Plastics, Chemicals and Petroleum Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.36)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.36**

**PAPER, PLASTICS, CHEMICALS AND**

**PETROLEUM WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

6th June, 1972

LEGAL NOTICE 51 of 1972, as amended by Legal Notice 26 of 1974.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Paper, Plastics, Chemicals and Petroleum Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

*Amended by: L.N. 26 of 1974.*

There shall be a Paper, Plastics, Chemicals and Petroleum Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or part of an undertaking, the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in any one of the following -

(a) the extraction, preparation, processing, or manufacture of table salt, oils of any kind, fats, margarine and similar products, chemicals (including petroleum) or chemical products but excluding employees working in lime kilns;

(b) the manufacture of paper, packaging, or the manufacture of articles made wholly or mainly of paper, including paper bags and exercise books;

(c) the manufacture of cardboard or articles made wholly or mainly of cardboard including cardboard boxes and containers;

(d) the processing or manufacture of plastics or articles made wholly or mainly of plastics, including polythene bags, plastic baby pants, plastic aprons and other plastic garments, polythene wrapping and plastic insulation material, but excluding -

(i) luggage, brief cases, handbags, shopping bags and other bags of similar nature;

(ii) textiles made from plastic fibres;

(iii) neon signs;

(e) the manufacture of imitation jewellery, including costume jewellery.

## 3. Interpretation.

For the purpose of this Order -

''chemicals'' includes basic industrial chemicals, gases, fertilizers, natural or synthetic rubber, and petroleum but excludes gases for domestic use;

''chemical products'' includes matches, paints, varnishes, lacquers, candles, soaps, synthetic detergents, bitumen, bituminous emulsions and other articles made wholly or mainly of natural or synthetic rubber, but excludes textiles made from natural or man-made fibres, and the manufacture, repair or rebuilding and retreading of tyres;

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 116 of 2026.

''plastics'' means any material including sythetic resins that contains as an essential ingredient an organic substance of large molecular weight, is solid in its finished state and at some stage in its manufacture or in its processing into finished articles can be shaped by flow.

## 4. Saving.

This Wages Council shall not operate in respect of employees already falling within the field of operation of any wages council, other than the Paper and Plastics Products Wages Council and the Chemicals and Petroleum Wages Council.

## 5. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette on the 15th February, 1972.
