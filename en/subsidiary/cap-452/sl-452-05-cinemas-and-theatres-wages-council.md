# Cinemas and Theatres Wages Council (S.L. 452.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.05**

**CINEMAS AND THEATRES**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

29th May, 1952

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 260 of 1952.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Cinemas and Theatres Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

The Cinemas and Theatres Wages Council shall operate for all employees in cinemas and theatres, other than stage performers and musicians.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of intention of this Order was published in the Gazette of the 25th April, 1952.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 143 of 2026.
