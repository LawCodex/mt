# Hospitals and Clinics Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.34)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.34**

**HOSPITALS AND CLINICS**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

16th February, 1968

LEGAL NOTICE 21 of 1968, as amended by Legal Notices 16 of 1976 and 75 of 1977.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Hospitals and Clinics Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

*Amended by: L.N. 16 of 1976; L.N. 75 of 1977.*

(1) There shall be a Hospitals and Clinics Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any private hospital, nursing or convalescent home, dental or other clinic including dental mechanics and medical or nursing institutions or similar establishments.

(2) Unless the context otherwise requires -

"clinics" also includes ante-natal clinics, X-ray clinics and medical laboratories, and any other establishment where people go to be medically examined or to receive medical attention, but does not include any institution which is not run as a profitable concern;

"hospital" means any institute of medico-surgical treatment, any maternity home or hydropathic establishment and includes homes for old aged persons, and sanatoria, excluding those charitable institutions identified as such by the Minister responsible for social policies.

## 3. Exclusion.

This Wages Council shall not operate in respect of employees whose remuneration for service is regulated by tariff made by any other law.

## 4. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette on 27th October, 1967.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 138 of 2026.
