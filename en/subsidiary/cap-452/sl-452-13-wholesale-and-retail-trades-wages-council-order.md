# Wholesale and Retail Trades Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.13)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.13**

**WHOLESALE AND RETAIL TRADES**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

19th August, 1955

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 430 of 1955, as amended by Legal Notice 2 of 1989

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Wholesale and Retail Trades Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

The Wholesale and Retail Trades Wages Council shall operate for all employees engaged in the wholesale, retail and ancillary trades.

## 3. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 2 of 1989.*

The term "wholesale, retail and ancillary trades" means - (a) wholesale merchants, distributors, importers, exporters, commission agents, wholesale and marketing cooperatives;

(b) retailers, lending libraries, auctioneers, hairdressers, consumers’ cooperatives, photographic studios;

but excludes any shop which is run directly under the management of a hotel or of a club as part of the amenities thereof or which is given out under contract for the purpose of increasing those amenities.

## 4. Exclusion.

This Council shall not operate in respect of port workers and foremen of port workers whether employed by employers or not.

## 5. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order but with a wider field of operation was published in the Gazette of the 9th October, 1953.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 125 of 2026.
