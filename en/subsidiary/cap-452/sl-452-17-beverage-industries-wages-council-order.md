# Beverage Industries Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.17)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.17**

**BEVERAGE INDUSTRIES**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

18th November, 1957

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 702 of 1957.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Beverage Industries Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

The Beverage Induatries Wages Council shall operate for all employees in any undertaking, or in any part of an undertaking, which consists wholly or mainly in the bottling or the production of distilled spirits, wines, malt liquors, soft drinks and carbonated beverages, and of any other activity so far as it is incidental or ancillary to any such activity as aforesaid of the undertaking, including, without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing:

(a) the distilling of ethyl alcohol for all purposes, the distilling, rectifying and blending of alcoholic liquors;

(b) the production of wines, cider, perry or other fermented beverages;

(c) the production of malt or malt liquors, such as beer ale, porter and stout;

(d) the manufacture of non alcoholic beverages, such as soft drinks and carbonated mineral waters.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 31st May, 1957.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 122 of 2026.
