# Trade Union Regulations (S.L. 452.16)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.16**

**TRADE UNIONS AND EMPLOYERS’**

**ASSOCIATIONS REGULATIONS**

22nd November, 2016

LEGAL NOTICE 388 of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Trade Unions and Employers’ Associations Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation. Cap. 452.

In these regulations "the Act" means the Employment and Industrial Relations Act, and the meanings set out in article 2 of the Act shall apply to these regulations.

## 3. Application for registration.

Every application for the registration of a trade union or an employers’ association shall be in the Form No. 1 in the Schedule and shall contain the particulars set out therein.

## 4. Certificate of registration.

The certificate of registration of a trade union or an employers’ association shall be in the Form No. 2 in the Schedule.

## 5. Request for cancellation of a certificate.

Every request made by a trade union or employers’ association for cancellation of a certificate of registration shall be sent to the Registrar in the Form No. 3 in the Schedule.

## 6. Notice of intention of cancellation.

Notice of intention of cancellation of a certificate of registration shall be in the Form No. 4 in the Schedule.

## 7. Cancellation of certificate of registration.

The cancellation of a certificate of registration shall be in the Form No. 5 in the Schedule.

## 8. Alteration of rules of aregistered trade union.

Alteration of the rules of a registered trade union or a registered employers’ association may be either:

(a) partial alteration, consisting of the addition of a new rule or part of a rule or rules to the existing rules, or of the substitution of a new rule or part of a rule or rules for any of the existing rules, or of deletion of any of the existing rules or any part thereof without any substitution, or of more than one or of all of these modes; or

(b) a complete alteration consisting of the substitution of an entire set of rules for the existing set of rules.

## 9. Partial alteration of rules.

An application for the registration of a partial alteration of the rules shall be made by seven members of the trade union or employers’ association and shall be on the Form No. 6 in the Schedule and shall be accompanied by a printed copy of the existing rules marked to show where and in what way they are altered and by the following documents:

(a) if the partial alteration consists of the addition or substitution of a new rule or part of a rule or rules, one copy of such rule or part of a rule or rules shall be signed by each of the applicants;

(b) if the partial alteration consists of the deletion of any of the rules without any substitution, one copy of the rules without substitution, a copy of the resolution for such deletion shall be signed by each of the applicants. The Registrar, before registering the partial alteration of the rules, shall ascertain that the rules of the trade union or employers’ association, if altered in accordance with the proposed partial alteration, will provide for all the matters required by the Act to be provided for in the rules of a registered trade union or employers’ association.

## 10. Certificate of registration of partial alteration.

The certificate of registration of a partial alteration shall be in the Form No. 7 in the Schedule and shall be forwarded to the Secretary of the trade union or employers’ association.

## 11. Mode of application.

An application for the registration of a complete alteration of rules shall be made by seven members of the trade union or employers’ association and shall be in the Form No. 8 in the Schedule and shall be accompanied by a printed copy of the existing rules marked to show where and in what way they are altered and by a printed copy of the new rules signed by each of the applicants; and the Registrar, before registering the new set of rules, shall ascertain that it provides for all the matters which, according to the Act, are to be provided for in the rules of a registered trade union or employers’ association.

## 12. Complete alteration of the rules.

The certificate of registration of a complete alteration of the rules shall be in the Form No. 9 in the Schedule, and shall be forwarded to the Secretary attached to a copy of the new set of rules.

## 13. Dissolution of trade union.

When a registered trade union or employers’ association is dissolved, notice of the dissolution shall be given by the Secretary and the Treasurer to the Registrar in the Form No. 10 in the Schedule, and the Registrar shall, if satisfied that the dissolution has been effected in accordance with the rules of the trade union or employers’ association, register the dissolution and return a copy of the Form to the Secretary of the trade union or employers’ association, endorsed with the word "registered" and signed by him.

## 14. Statement.

The statement of the receipts, funds and expenditure of the trade union or employers’ association required under the Act shall contain, properly set out therein, all details of revenue and expenditure and shall be audited and approved at a general meeting of the trade union concerned.

## 15. Inspection.

The Secretary of every registered trade union and employers’ association shall give the Registrar all reasonable facilities for the inspection by him or by a person appointed by him for the purpose of the records showing the name, address and the trade or occupation of each member of the trade union or main activity of each member of the employers’ association and the date up to which subscriptions have been paid by each member.

## 16. Accounts.

The accounts required under article 59 of the Act shall show in detail in a property book all the effects of the trade union or employers’ association and their respective value, and shall also record all the details normally shown in the cash book, in supporting vouchers for all money received or collected by or on behalf of the trade union or the employers’ association and in filed and numbered receipts for all money paid out by or on behalf of the trade union or employers’ association.
