# Transport Equipment, Metal and Allied Industries Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.11**

**TRANSPORT EQUIPMENT, METAL AND ALLIED**

**INDUSTRIES WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

1st December, 1953

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 640 of 1953, as amended by Government Notice 362 of 1954; and Legal Notices 62 of 1964, 25 of 1969, 74 of 1976 and 64 of 1978.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Transport Equipment, Metal and Allied Industries Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operaation of Wages Council.

*Amended by: G.N. 362 of 1954; L.N. 62 of 1964; L.N, 25 of 1969; L.N. 74 of 1976; L.N. 64 of 1978.*

The Transport Equipment, Metal and Allied Industries Wages Council shall operate in respect of all employees in all factories engaged in the manufacture of transport equipment, metal and allied products, being factories engaged in any of the following:

(a) manufacture of metal products other than the manufacture of clocks and watches, scientific and optical goods, silverware and jewellery, but including the plating and polishing of metal products;

(b) manufacture of machinery;

(c) manufacture of electrical machinery;

(d) manufacture of transport equipment including the repair, rebuilding and retreading of tyres but excluding the manufacture wholly or mainly in wood of boats and motor-vehicles bodies;

(e) scrap metal works, including the cutting, sorting, crushing, breaking up or baling of metals;

(f) repairs of television sets and radios;

(g) manufacture of fishing flies.

## 3. Exclusion.

This Order shall not apply to any employees engaged in the installation, erection and fitting of transmission and distributing equipment for electric energy, gas or water, whether inside or outside buildings.

## 4. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 62 of 1964.*

For the purposes of this Order the term -

"manufacture" shall include repair; and

"factory" shall include "workshop", whether or not such workshop forms part of any establishment solely or mainly engaged in activities described in paragraphs (a) to (g) of article 2, and means any premises wherein or within the close or curtilage or precincts of which an employee is or employees are employed in manual labour in any process for or incidental to any of the following purposes, namely:

(a) the making of any article or part of any article; or

(c) the adapting for sale of any articles,
