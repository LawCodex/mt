# Hire of Cars or Private Buses Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.26)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.26**

**HIRE OF CARS OR PRIVATE BUSES**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

8th March, 1966

LEGAL NOTICE 11 of 1966, as amended by Legal Notices 68 of 1970 and 60 of 1972.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Hire of Cars or Private Buses Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

*Substituted by: L.N. 68 of 1970; L.N. 60 of 1972.*

There shall be a Hire of Cars or Private Buses Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or part of an undertaking the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in the hiring of cars or private buses, or in driving instruction.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 25th January, 1966.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 139 of 2026.
