# Fees payable to Members of Courts of Inquiry Order (S.L. 452.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.04**

**FEES PAYABLE TO MEMBERS OF COURTS OF**

**INQUIRY ORDER**

15th March, 1949

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 155 of 1949, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Fees payable to Members of Courts of Inquiry Order.

## 2. Sole member or chairman.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

A sole member constituting a Courts of Inquiry and the chairman of a Courts of Inquiry composed of more than one member, if that sole member or that chairman is not a Judge or a Magistrate or a person in the full time employment of the Government, shall be paid a fee of seven euro and thirty-four cents (7.34) for each sitting.

## 3. Other members.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

Each of the members other than the Chairman of a Courts of Inquiry composed of more than one member shall be paid a fee of two euro and forty-five cents (2.45) in respect of each sitting.

## 4. Meaning of "sitting".

For the purposes of articles 2 and 3 a sitting shall mean a conference at which one or more witnesses are heard or at which one or more of the interested parties or their representatives set out ex parte views or arguments before the Courts of Inquiry.

## 5. Fees include compensation in full.

The fees established in articles 2 and 3 shall be deemed to include compensation in full for all work of whatsoever nature including the drafting of the report or reports, performed by the Courts of Inquiry.
