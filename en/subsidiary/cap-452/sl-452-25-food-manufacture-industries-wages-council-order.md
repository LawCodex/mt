# Food Manufacture Industries Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.25)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.25**

**FOOD MANUFACTURE INDUSTRIES**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

28th September, 1965

LEGAL NOTICE 51 of 1965, as amended by Legal Notice 31 of 1970.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Food Manufacture Industries Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

*Substituted by: L.N. 31 of 1970.*

There shall be a Food Manufacture Industries Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or part of an undertaking, the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in the manufacture of foodstuffs, including bakeries, paste factories, confectioneries and ice-cream factories and of balanced fodder and other foodstuffs for animal consumption.

## 3. Exclusion.

The Wages Council shall not operate for employees in establishments covered by a wages council regulation order already in existence.

## 4. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of publication the 25th May, 1965.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 140 of 2026.
