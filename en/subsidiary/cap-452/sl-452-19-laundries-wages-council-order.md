# Laundries Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.19)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.19**

**LAUNDRIES WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

20th December, 1963

LEGAL NOTICE 42 of 1963.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Laundries Wages Council Order. 2. There shall be a Laundries Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or in any part of an undertaking the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in any of the following:

*Field of operation of Wages Council.*

The cleaning by wet or dry processes, pressing, dyeing or repairing by hand or machine apparel of any kind, linens and household furnishings, and for all employees in any laundry in a hotel where such a laundry is not run exclusively for the use of the hotel and its visitors.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 22nd November, 1963.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 135 of 2026.
