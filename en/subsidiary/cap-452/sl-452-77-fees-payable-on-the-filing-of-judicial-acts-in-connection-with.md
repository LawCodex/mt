# Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts in connection with Appeals Regulations (S.L. 452.77)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.77**

**FEES PAYABLE ON THE FILING OF JUDICIAL**

**ACTS IN CONNECTION WITH APPEALS**

**REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 103 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts in connection with Appeals Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable on appeals.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007. Cap. 452.*

The fees payable under the Employment and Industrial Relations Act on the filing of judicial acts in connection with appeals shall be one hundred and sixteen euro and forty-seven cents (116.47).
