# Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Protection of Maternity Employment) Regulations (S.L. 452.105)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.105**

**EXTENSION OF APPLICABILITY TO SERVICE**

**WITH GOVERNMENT (PROTECTION OF**

**MATERNITY (EMPLOYMENT) REGULATIONS)**

**REGULATONS**

22nd October, 2010

LEGAL NOTICE 460 of 2010.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Extension of Applicability to Service with Government (Protection of Maternity (Employment) Regulations), Regulations.

## 2. Applicability to Government. Cap. 452. S.L. 452.91

Article 10 of the Employment and Industrial Relations Act, in so far as it is the empowering provision by virtue of which the Protection of Maternity (Employment) Regulations were issued, shall be applicable to service with the Government.
