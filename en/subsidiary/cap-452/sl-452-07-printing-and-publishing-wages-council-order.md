# Printing and Publishing Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.07**

**PRINTING AND PUBLISHING**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

2nd January, 1953

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 3 of 1953.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Printing and Publishing Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

The Printing and Publishing Wages Council shall operate in respect of all employees in all establishments engaged in printing, publishing or book binding.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 115 of 2026.
