# Canning Industry Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.20)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.20**

CANNING INDUSTRY WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\* 20th December, 1963

LEGAL NOTICE 43 of 1963, as amended by Legal Notice 85 of 1968.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Canning Industry Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

*Substituted by: L.N. 85 of 1968.*

There shall be a Canning Industry Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or in any part of an undertaking, the activities of which consists wholly or mainly of the following:

(a) canning and preserving, including packing in air-tight containers, of fruits, vegetables, and fruit and vegetable juices; dehydrating and quick-freezing of fruits and vegetables; and any other process incidental thereto;

(b) manufacture of preserves, jams, jellies, pickles, sauces, or canned soups, including the canning and preserving of meat, meat products and any other form of meat processing; and the manufacture of ice;

(c) canning and preserving of fish and other sea foods, including salting, drying, smoking, curing, pickling, dehydrating, packing in airtight containers, quick-freezing and any other form of processing;

(d) cold storage of fruits, vegetables, meat, fish and other foods.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 22nd November, 1963.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 121 of 2026.
