# Seamen Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.28)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.28**

**SEAMEN WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

17th October, 1967

LEGAL NOTICE 67 of 1967.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Seamen Wages Council Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires -

"ship" includes any boat, ferry or vessel of any description whatsoever; and

"fishing vessel" means a vessel regularly operating for the purposes of the fishing trade.

## 3. Field of operation of Wages Council.

There shall be a Seamen Wages Council which shall operate for all persons employed on board ships regularly operating within the territorial waters of Malta but excluding persons employed on board fishing vessels or on foreign going ships.

## 4. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 13th June, 1967.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 132 of 2026.
