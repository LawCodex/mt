# Employees of Burdnara Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.27)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.27**

**EMPLOYEES OF BURDNARA**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

19th May, 1967

LEGAL NOTICE 33 of 1967, as amended by Legal Notice 44 of 1968.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Employees of Burdnara Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

*Substituted by: L.N. 44 of 1968.*

There shall be an Employees of Burdnara Wages Council which shall operate -

(a) for all employees of cargo clearance and burdnara in any employment relating wholly or mainly to the cargo clearance and burdnara trades, and

(b) for all employees in any undertaking or part of an undertaking engaged wholly or mainly in the clearance, haulage and transport of furniture and other household effects, and other goods or materials.

## 3. Exclusion.

*Added by: L.N. 44 of 1968.*

This Council shall not operate in respect of employees whose field of operation falls under any other Wages Council.

## 4. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 21st March, 1967.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 120 of 2026.
