# Procedure of the Labour Board Regulations (S.L. 452.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.10**

**PROCEDURE OF THE LABOUR BOARD**

**REGULATIONS**

10th July, 1953

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 386 of 1953, as amended by Emergency Ordinance VI of 1958 and Ordinance XXV of 1962.

## 1. Title.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Procedure of the Labour Board Regulations.

*Cap. 452.*

(2) The definitions set out in article 2 of the Employment and Industrial Relations Act (hereinafter referred to as "the Act") are applicable to these Regulations.

## 2. Notice of meeting.

Notice of a meeting of the Labour Board appointed as provided in the Act shall be deemed to be sufficiently given to a member of the Board if sent by post to his ordinary or last known address, but the accidental omission to give notice of a meeting to, or the non-rece ipt by, any member shall not invalidate the proceedings of any meeting.

## 3. Constitution of a meeting of the Board.

*Amended by: Em. Ord. VI of 1958; Ord. XXV of 1962.*

(a) In order to constitute a meeting of the Board there shall be present at least one of the members appointed by the Minister as being independent persons, one of the members appointed by the Minister as representing the employers, and one of the members appointed by the Minister as representing employees.

(b) When more than one independent persons have been appointed to membership of the Board, the Minister shall select therefrom and appoint a Deputy Chairman in addition to the Chairman appointed in terms of article 6(2) of the Act.

## 4. Voting.

The Chairman and each member of the Board shall have one vote:

Provided that the Chairman may if he think it desirable, and shall at the request of either the employers’ side or of the workers’ side tahe a vote by sides. In such a division the Chairman and the independent members shall not vote, but, in the event of the division resulting in a disagreement between the two sides, the question may be decided by the majority vote of the Chairman and of the independent members.
