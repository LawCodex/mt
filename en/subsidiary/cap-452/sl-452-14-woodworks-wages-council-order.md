# Woodworks Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.14)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.14**

**WOODWORKS**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

19th August, 1955

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 431 of 1955, as amended by Legal Notice 11 of 1971.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Woodworks Wages Council Order. 2. The Woodworks Wages Council shall operate for all employees engaged in establishments manufacturing furniture, fixtures and other articles wholly or mainly in wood (excluding smokers’ pipes but including boats and motor vehicles bodies, upholstery and wicker or cane work whether natural or artificial) and for employees employed by woodwork contractors in building and civil engineering.

*Field of operation of Wages Council. Amended by: L.N. 11 of 1971.*

## 3. Date of publication of notifice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of 18th June, 1954.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 124 of 2026.
