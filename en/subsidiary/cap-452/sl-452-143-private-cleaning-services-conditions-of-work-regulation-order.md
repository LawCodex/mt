# Private Cleaning Services (Conditions of Work) Regulation Order (S.L. 452.143)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.143**

**PRIVATE CLEANING SERVICES (CONDITIONS OF**

**WORK) REGULATION ORDER**

30th June, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 114 of 2026.

## 1. Citation, commencement and applicability.

(1) The title of this order is the Private Cleaning Services (Conditions of Work) Regulation Order.

(2) This order shall come into force within two (2) months from its date of publication in the Gazette.

(3) The provisions established in this order shall apply to all employees with establishments providing private cleaning services:

Provided that in the event that there is a collective agreement applicable to the class of employees covered by this order, the more favourable conditions in that agreement or in this order shall prevail.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this order, unless the context otherwise requires:

## 3. Hours of work.

(1) The minimum weekly wage for whole-time employees, other than watchpersons, shall be related to an average of forty (40) hours per week distributed over a period of thirteen (13) weeks provided that the hours of work shall not exceed forty-five (45) hours in any one week.

(2) The minimum weekly wage for watchpersons shall be related to a week of not more than fifty-two (52) hours.

## 4. Minimum wage for whole-time employees.

(1) The minimum weekly wage payable to whole-time employees other than watchpersons shall be two hundred twenty-nine euro and forty-four cents (€229.44). The minimum weekly wage payable to whole-time employees who are watchpersons shall be two hundred ninety-eight euro and twenty-seven cents (€298.27).

(2) In the case of shift workers, unless every weekly night shift is followed by one weekly day shift, such further night shift or shifts shall entitle the employee to a further remuneration amounting to not less than one-fourth of his normal rate of pay.

## 5. Uniform allowance.

(1) When the employee is required by the employer to wear a uniform in the performance of his duties, such uniform shall be provided by the employer free of any charge.

*S.L. 646.13.*

(2) Personal protective equipment according to the Minimum Requirements for the Use of Personal Protective Equipment at Work Regulations shall also be provided free of charge by the employer.

## 6. Minimum daily rest.

Whole-time employees, other than watchpersons, shall be allowed intervals for meals and rest of not less, in the aggregate, than one hour every day:

Provided that:

(a) subject to the provisions of paragraph (b), the interval or intervals shall be allowed after the first and before the last hour of the period of employment; and

(b) where the last half hour of the period of employment is allowed as a half hour of rest, the employee shall be entitled to leave the place of work as soon as such half hour commences.Minimum weekly rest. S.L. 452.87.

## 7.

All employees shall be entitled to a rest period according to the Organisation of Working Time Regulations.

## 8. Minimumovertime rates.

(1) In the case of whole-time employees, other than shift workers and watchpersons, overtime rates shall be payable as follows:

(a) for all time worked in excess of twelve (12) hours in any one (1) day........................ time-and-a-half;

(b) for all time worked in excess of forty-five (45) hours in any one (1) week.....................time-and-a-half;

(c) for all time worked in excess of an average of forty(40) hours per week distributed over thirteen (13) consecutive weeks...........................time-and-a-half;

(d) for all time worked on the first pre-determined day of rest..........................time-and-a-half;

(e) for all time worked on a Sunday, customary holiday or the second or only pre-determined day of rest of the week......................... double time.

(2) In the case of shift workers, overtime shall be payable as follows:

(a) for all time worked in excess of twelve (12) hours in any one (1) day......................time-and-a-half;

(b) for all time worked in excess of forty-five (45) hours in any one (1) week.......................time-and-a-half;

(c) for all time worked in excess of an average of forty (40) hours per week distributed over thirteen (13) consecutive weeks............................... time-and-a-half;

(d) for all time worked on the first pre-determined day of rest..........................time-and-a-half;

(e) for all time worked on the second or only pre-determined day of rest.............double time.

(3) In the case of watchpersons, overtime shall be payable as follows:

(a) for all time worked in excess of fifty-two (52) hours in any week of six (6) consecutive days.....time-and-a-half;

(b) for all time worked in excess of nine (9) hours on any day.................................time-and-a-half.

(4) Notwithstanding the provisions of sub-article (3):

(a) shift employees shall not be entitled to overtime payment for time worked in normal shift on Sundays or on a customary holiday;

(b) in the case of watchpersons, when a customary holiday falls on any day from Monday to Saturday, overtime at time-and-a-half shall be payable to them for work in excess of fifty-two (52) hours during that week.

## 9. Vacation leave. S.L. 452.87.

The employee shall be entitled to annual leave according to the Organisation of Working Time Regulations.

(1) A whole-time employee shall in every calendar year be entitled to the equivalent in hours of twelve (12) days sick leave on full pay, less an amount equal to the sum established for sickness benefit entitlement at the rate determined in accordance with the Social Security Act, and part-time employees shall have a pro rata entitlement to sick leave in hours on full pay, less an amount equal to the sum established for sickness benefit entitlement at the rate determined in accordance with the Social Security Act, which amount shall also be calculated on a pro rata basis:

## 11. Waiting time.

An employee shall be entitled to the payment of the minimum wage applicable to him for all time during which he is present at the premises of his employer or elsewhere unless he is present there in any of the following circumstances:

(a) without the employer’s express or implied consent;

(b) for some purpose not related with his work, other than that of waiting for work to be assigned to him;

(c) solely by reason that he is resident there;

(d) during normal meal times in a room or place in which no work is being carried out, and where he is not waiting for any work to be assigned to him.

## 12. Other forms of leave.   S.L. 452.101.

Employees shall be entitled to the national holidays and to all public holidays with full pay, to all forms of leave according to the Minimum Special Leave Entitlement Regulations and all other forms of leave that may, from time to time, be stipulated in the National Standard Orders or any regulations made under the Act.
