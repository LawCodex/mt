# Clay and Glass Products Wages Council Order (S.L. 452.32)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 452.32**

**CLAY AND GLASS PRODUCTS**

**WAGES COUNCIL ORDER\***

2nd February, 1968

LEGAL NOTICE 14 of 1968, as amended by Legal Notices 44 of 1970 and 49 and 62 of 1979.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Clay and Glass Products Wages Council Order.

## 2. Field of operation of Wages Council.

*Amended by: L.N. 44 of 1970; L.N. 49 of 1979; L.N. 62 of 1979.*

There shall be a Clay and Glass Products Wages Council which shall operate for all employees in any undertaking or part of an undertaking, the activities of which consist wholly or mainly in the manufacture of clay products, pottery, earthenware, china, ceramics, glass, glassware, mirrors, advertising displays and signs of whatever material made including neon signs, fibreglass, fibreglass products and ornamental stone products.

## 3. Date of publication of notice of intention.

Notice of the intention to make this Order was published in the Gazette of the 24th November, 1967.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 119 of 2026.
