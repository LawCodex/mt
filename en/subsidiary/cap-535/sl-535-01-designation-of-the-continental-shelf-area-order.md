# Designation of the Continental Shelf Area Order (S.L. 535.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 535.01**

**DESIGNATION OF THE CONTINENTAL**

**SHELF AREA ORDER**

22nd April, 1971

LEGAL NOTICES 36 of 1971 and 113 of 1974, consolidated.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is Designation of the Continental Shelf Area Order.

## 2. Indication of area.

The area described in the Schedule to this Order is by this Order designated as an area within which the rights mentioned in article 3(1) of the Continental Shelf Act are to be exercised.

*Cap. 535.*
