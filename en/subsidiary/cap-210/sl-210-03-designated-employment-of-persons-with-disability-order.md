# Designated Employment of Persons with Disability Order (S.L. 210.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 210.03**

**DESIGNATED EMPLOYMENT OF PERSONS**

**WITH DISABILITY ORDER**

10th November, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 158 of 1995.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Designated Employment of Persons with Disability Order

## 2. Designated employment.

The employment as a passenger electric lift attendant, that is to say, employment in operating and controlling an electrically driven passenger lift, shall be deemed to be a designated class of employment to which article 19 of the Persons with Disability (Employment) Act is to apply.

*Cap. 210.*
