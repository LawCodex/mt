# Standard Percentage of Employment of Persons with Disability Order (S.L. 210.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 210.02**

STANDARD PERCENTAGE OF EMPLOYMENT OF PERSONS WITH DISABILITY ORDER

10th November, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 157 of 1995.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Standard Percentage of Employment of Persons with Disability Order.

## 2. Specification of standard percentage. Cap. 210.

The standard percentage for the purpose of the Persons with Disability (Employment) Act, shall be two per cent.
