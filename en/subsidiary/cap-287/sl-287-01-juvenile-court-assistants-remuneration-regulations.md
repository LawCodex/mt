# Juvenile Court (Assistants' Remuneration) Regulations (S.L. 287.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 287.01**

**JUVENILE COURT**

(ASSISTANTS’ REMUNERATION) REGULATIONS 23rd August, 1985

LEGAL NOTICE 24 of 1985, as amended by Legal Notice 423 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Juvenile Court (Assistants’ Remuneration) Regulations.

## 2. Remuneration payable to assistants.

*Amended by: L.N. 423 of 2007. Cap. 287.*

There shall be payable to assistants of the Juvenile Court appointed under article 4 of the Juvenile Court Act, when such assistants are not in the employment of Government, a fee of nine euros and thirty-two cents (9.32) for every sitting of the said Court.
