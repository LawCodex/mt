# Innovative Technology Arrangements and Services (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 592.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 592.01**

**INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGY ARRANGEMENTS**

**AND SERVICES (FEES) REGULATIONS\***

2nd November, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 355 of 2018, as amended by Legal Notice 268 of 2023.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Innovative Technology Arrangements and Services (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Fees.

The fees prescribed in the Schedule shall be the fees to be levied in relation to the matters stated in the Schedule with reference to the certification of Innovative Technology Arrangements, Registration of Systems Auditors and Technical Administrators, and other fees for matters ancillary thereto.
