# Eco-Contribution (Form and Administrative Fee for Appeals) Regulations (S.L. 473.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 473.03**

**ECO-CONTRIBUTION (FORM AND**

**ADMINISTRATIVE FEE FOR APPEALS)**

**REGULATIONS**

5th October, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 282 of 2007, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Eco-Contribution (Form and Administrative Fee for Appeals) Regulations.

## 2. Appeal form. Cap. 473.

A person who receives and is notified of an assessment in terms of article 13A of the Eco-Contribution Act, or who is notified with a judicial act referred to in article 18(2) of the same Act, may within thirty days from the date of such notification lodge an appeal before the Appeals Board objecting to the administrative penalties so fixed. Such appeal shall be made by filing before the Appeals Board the appeal form established under the Schedule to these regulations.

## 3. Administrative fee.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

An administrative fee of fifty-eight euro and twenty-three cents (€58.23) shall be paid to the Secretary of the Appeals Board by any person lodging an appeal.
