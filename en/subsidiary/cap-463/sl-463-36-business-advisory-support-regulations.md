# Business Advisory Support Regulations (S.L. 463.36)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.36**

**BUSINESS ADVISORY**

**SUPPORT REGULATIONS**

1st July, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 423 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Business Advisory Support Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463.*

"Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"Corporation" means Malta Enterprise established under the Act;

"Guidelines" means such guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in terms of the Act;

"Small and Medium Sized Enterprise (SME) or "(SMEs)" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it by Annex I of Commission Regulation (EU) No 651/2014 of 17 June 2014;

"undertaking" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act.

## 3. Form of aid.

Where the Corporation is satisfied that an undertaking requires the services of external advisors to further develop the business, the Corporation may provide the undertaking with a cash grant as part financing of up to fifty per cent (50%) of the eligible costs incurred by the undertaking, within the terms and conditions set out in the Guidelines. Supported advisory services shall not be a continuous or periodic activity nor relate to the undertaking's usual operating costs, such as routine tax consultancy services, regular legal services or advertising.

## 4. Grants.

The Corporation shall only provide such grants in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the Guidelines.

## 5. Eligibility.

An undertaking shall only qualify for the benefits provided by these regulations if such undertaking:

(a) is carrying out an economic activity in Malta;

(b) is a small and medium sized enterprise;

(c) meets any additional criteria established in the Guidelines.
