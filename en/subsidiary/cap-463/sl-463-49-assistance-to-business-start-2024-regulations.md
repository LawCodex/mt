# Assistance to Business Start (2024) Regulations (S.L. 463.49)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.49**

**ASSISTANCE TO BUSINESS START (2024)**

**REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2024 LEGAL NOTICE 49 of 2024.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Assistance to Business Start (2024) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463.*

"Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"Corporation" means Malta Enterprise as established by article 7 of the Act;

"eligible undertaking" shall have the meaning attributed to it by the Guidelines;

"Guidelines" means such Guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in terms of the Act, establishing the parameters of implementing the aid granted by virtue of these regulations.

## 3. Assistance to eligible undertakings.

Where the Corporation is satisfied that the undertaking is an eligible undertaking as prescribed within the Guidelines, the Corporation may provide assistance to such undertakings in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the Guidelines.

## 4. Deadline for submission of applications for assistance.

(1) Applications for assistance to support eligible undertakings under these regulations shall reach the Corporation by not later than 30th October 2026.

(2) No assistance under these regulations shall be awarded by the Corporation after 31st December 2026.

## 5. Transitory provision.

In the eventuality that a new scheme is issued, the provisions in respect of which the assistance was granted prior to the issuance of the new scheme shall continue to apply thereto.
