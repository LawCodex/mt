# Get Ready Scheme Regulations (S.L. 463.26)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.26**

**GET READY SCHEME REGULATIONS**

1st September, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 358 of 2018, as amended by Legal Notice 22 of 2019.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Get Ready Scheme Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap 463.*

"the Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"the Corporation" means Malta Enterprise established under the Act;

"the Guidelines" means such guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in terms of the Act;

"Small and Medium Sized Enterprise (SME)" or "(SMEs)" has the meaning attributed to it by Annex I of Commission Regulation (EU) No 651/2014 of 17 June 2014;

"undertaking" means an undertaking as defined in the Act.

## 3. Form of aid.

Where the Corporation is satisfied that an undertaking that has a significant dependency on the British market, in terms of import and, or export, requires assistance to access advisory services, the Corporation may provide such an undertaking with a cash grant not exceeding four thousand euro (€4000) as part-financing of up to fifty per cent (50%) of the eligible costs incurred by the undertaking in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the Guidelines.

## 4. Eligibility.

An undertaking shall only qualify for the benefits provided by these regulations if such undertaking:

(a) is carrying out an economic activity in Malta;

(b) is a small enterprise that employs at least five (5) persons but not more than fifty (50) persons on full time basis;

(c) meets any additional criteria established in the Guidelines.

## 5. Deadline for submission of applications for assistance.

*Substituted: L.N. 22 of 2019.*

No assistance under these regulations shall be awarded by the Corporation to any request for support received after 28th February, 2019.
