# COVID-19 Temporary Support Measures Regulations (S.L. 463.34)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.34**

**COVID-19 TEMPORARY SUPPORT**

**MEASURES REGULATIONS**

1st April, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 345 of 2020, as amended by Legal Notices 132, 283 of 2021 and 70 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is COVID-19 Temporary Support Measures Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463.*

"the Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"the Corporation" means Malta Enterprise established under the Act;

"eligible undertakings" means an undertaking as defined in the Act that meets the parameters established in the Guidelines;

"Guidelines" means such guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time under the Act;

"Temporary Framework" means the Temporary Framework for State aid measures to support the economy in the current COVID-19 outbreak, as amended and as may be subsequently amended as published by the European Commission.

## 3. Assistance to support eligible undertakings.

Where the Corporation is satisfied that eligible undertakings require support to sustain, preserve and regenerate their economic activity, the Corporation may provide assistance as shall be further established in the Guidelines in any form allowed by the Act and in accordance with the Temporary Framework.

## 4. Utilisation of tax credits.

(1) An undertaking benefiting from tax credits in terms of these regulations and the respective Guidelines shall be entitled to deduct from the amount of income tax which is due on its chargeable income derived from its trade or business in the years of assessment following the award of the benefit an amount equivalent to the tax credit awarded.

(2) An undertaking that in any year of assessment does not utilise any tax credit awarded or deducts a value from the amount of income tax which is due on its chargeable income derived from its trade or business that is less than the tax credit awarded shall carry forward the unutilised tax credit for the following years of assessment and so on for subsequent years.

(3) Tax credits awarded in terms of these regulations and the respective Guidelines shall not give rise to a right to a refund of tax.

## 5. Deadline for submission of applications for assistance.

*Substituted by: L.N. 132 of 2021; L.N. 283 of 2021; L.N. 70 of 2022.*

Aid will be awarded to eligible undertakings under these regulations by not later than 30th June, 2022.
