# Tax Credits Supporting the Refurbishment of Hotels and Restaurants Regulations (S.L. 463.23)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.23**

TAX CREDITS SUPPORTING THE REFURBISHMENT OF HOTELS AND RESTAURANTS REGULATIONS 1st January, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 120 of 2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Tax Credits Supporting the Refurbishment of Hotels and Restaurants Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463*

"the Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"the Corporation" means Malta Enterprise established under the Act;

"the Guidelines" means such guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in terms of the Act.

## 3. Assistance to support eligible undertakings.

(1) Where the Corporation is satisfied that the undertaking is an eligible undertaking as prescribed within the Guidelines, the Corporation may provide assistance to such undertakings in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the Guidelines.

## 4. Deadline for submission of applications for assistance.

Applications for assistance under these regulations shall reach the Corporation by not later than the 30th December, 2019.
