# Start-up Finance Regulations (S.L. 463.16)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.16**

**START-UP FINANCE REGULATIONS**

2nd May, 2017

LEGAL NOTICE 158 of 2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Start-up Finance Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463*

"the Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"the Corporation" means Malta Enterprise established under the Act; and

"the Guidelines" means such guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in terms of the Act.

## 3. Assistance to support small start-ups under the Start-up Finance Scheme.

(1) Where the Corporation is satisfied that the business is classified as a small-start-up undertaking as prescribed within the Guidelines, the Corporation may provide assistance to such undertakings in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the Guidelines.

## 4. Deadline for submission of applications for assistance.

Applications for assistance under these regulations shall reach the Corporation by not later than the 31st December, 2020.
