# Business Development (2024) Regulations (S.L. 463.57)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.57**

**BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT (2024) REGULATIONS**

1st March, 2024 LEGAL NOTICE 91 of 2024.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Business Development (2024) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463.*

"Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"Corporation" means the Malta Enterprise as established by article 7 of the Act;

"eligible undertaking" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it by the Guidelines;

"Guidelines" means the Guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in accordance with the Act, establishing the parameters of implementing the aid granted by these regulations.

## 3. Form of aid.

Where the Corporation is satisfied that an undertaking carrying on or intending to carry out an activity to contribute to the economic development of Malta, requires assistance to facilitate the investment, the Corporation may support such an undertaking with a tax credit and, or a cash grant that may not exceed three hundred-thousand euro (€300,000) over a period of three (3) years in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the Guidelines.

## 4. Deadline for submission of applications for assistance.

(1) The Corporation shall establish application submission periods, which periods shall be published on the Corporation's website.

(2) No assistance shall be granted by the Corporation under these regulations after the 31st December 2030.
