# Facilitating Work Life for Individuals with Disability Regulations (S.L. 463.43)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.43**

**FACILITATING WORK LIFE FOR INDIVIDUALS**

**WITH DISABILITY REGULATIONS**

1st July 2021 LEGAL NOTICE 321 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is Facilitating Work Life for Individuals with Disability Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463.*

"Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"Corporation" means Malta Enterprise Corporation as established under the Act;

*Cap. 413.*

"Commission" means the Commission for the Rights of Persons with Disability as established under article 21 of the Equal Opportunities (Persons with Disability) Act;

"disability" means a long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairment which in interaction with various barriers may hinder one’s full and effective participation in society on an equal basis with others;

"Guidelines" means such guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in terms of the Act.

## 3. Assistance to support eligible undertakings.

Where the Corporation in collaboration with the Commission are satisfied that the undertaking is an eligible undertaking as prescribed within the Guidelines, the Corporation may provide assistance to such undertaking to facilitate the work life of its disabled employee in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the Guidelines.

## 4. Deadline for submission of applications for assistance.

(1) Applications for assistance to support eligible undertakings under these regulations shall reach the Corporation by not later than the 31st August 2023.

(2) No assistance under these regulations shall be awarded by the Corporation after the 31st December 2023.
