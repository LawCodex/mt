# Constituted Bodies Notice (S.L. 463.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.01**

**CONSTITUTED BODIES NOTICE**

19th August, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 207 of 2003.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this notice is the Constituted Bodies Notice. 2. The Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs has established that the constituted bodies which better represent the interests of the commercial sector in terms of article 6(4) of the Malta Enterprise Corporation Act are the following:

*Constituted bodies.*

*Cap. 463.*

Malta Federation of Industry

The Malta Chamber of Commerce

Association of the General Retailers and Traders Union.
