# Certify Scheme Regulations (S.L. 463.22)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.22**

**CERTIFY SCHEME REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 119 of 2018.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Certify Scheme Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463.*

"the Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"the Corporation" means Malta Enterprise established under the Act;

"the Guidelines" means such guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in terms of the Act.

## 3. Assistance to support eligible undertakings.

Where the Corporation is satisfied that the undertaking is an eligible undertaking as prescribed in the Guidelines, the Corporation may provide assistance to such undertaking in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the Guidelines.

## 4. Deadline for submission of applications.

Applications for assistance under these regulations shall reach the Corporation by not later than the 30 November 2020.
