# Family Business Support Services Regulations (S.L. 463.14)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.14**

**FAMILY BUSINESS SUPPORT SERVICES**

**REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2017

LEGAL NOTICE 429 of 2017, as amended by Legal Notice 155 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Family Business Support Service Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463.*

"the Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"the Corporation" means Malta Enterprise established under the Act;

"family business" means a business registered with the Regulator in terms of the Family Business Act and holds an updated certificate issued by the Regulator for Family Businesses;

*Cap. 565.*

"the Guidelines" means such guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in terms of the Act.

## 3. Assistance to support family businesses

*Substituted by: L.N. 155 of 2021.*

Where the Corporation is satisfied that the business is classified as a Family Business, the Corporation may provide assistance in the form of a cash grant which shall not exceed the amount of fifteen thousand euro (€15,000) per family business in any rolling three (3) year period to access advisory services on succession planning and mediation services with the terms and conditions as set out in the Guidelines.

## 4. Deadline for submission of application for assistance

*Substituted by: L.N. 155 of 2021.*

Applications for assistance under these regulations shall reach the Corporation by not later than 30th November, 2023. No assistance under these regulations shall be awarded by the Corporation after 31st December, 2023.
