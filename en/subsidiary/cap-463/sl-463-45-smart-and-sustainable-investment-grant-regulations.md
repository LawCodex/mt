# Smart and Sustainable Investment Grant Regulations (S.L. 463.45)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.45**

**SMART AND SUSTAINABLE**

**INVESTMENT GRANT REGULATIONS**

1st October 2021 LEGAL NOTICE 80 of 2022, as amended by Legal Notices 20 of 2023 and 79 of 2024.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Smart and Sustainable Investment Grant Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463.*

"Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"Corporation" means the Malta Enterprise Corporation established under the Act;

"eligible investment" means a project that shall achieve the Smart and Sustainable objectives as shall be established in the Guidelines;

"Guidelines" means the Guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in terms of the Act;

"undertaking" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act.

## 3. Assistance to support undertakings.

(1) Where the Corporation is satisfied that an undertaking is eligible within the terms that shall be prescribed within the Guidelines, the Corporation may provide assistance for the carrying out of an eligible investment.

(2) The eligible investment must be implemented within twelve (12) months from the date when funding is approved and the beneficiary must commit to maintain the investment for a minimum of three (3) years.

## 4. Form of aid.

*Amended by: L.N. 20 of 2023.*

(1) A grant of up to one hundred thousand euro (€100,000) per eligible investment project, covering up to fifty per cent (50%) of the eligible expenditure may be awarded by the Corporation.

(2) If an eligible investment project addresses sustainable development criteria as established in the Guidelines, a tax credit of up to forty thousand euro (€40,000) and covering up to seventy per cent (70%) of the eligible expenditure may be awarded by the Corporation.

## 5. Eligible expenditure. 

*Substituted by: L.N. 20 of 2023.*

(1) The Corporation may support investments and modifications in plant, machinery, equipment and technologies that lead to a quantifiable reduction in CO2 emissions as further prescribed in the Guidelines.

(2) Eligible investments may also include specialised technical services and procurement of technologies that enable the undertaking to measure parameters linked to the undertakings’ carbon footprint.

## 6. Deadline for submission of applications for assistance.

*Substituted by: L.N. 79 of 2024.*

The Corporation shall establish and publish on its website the application submission periods. No assistance shall be granted by the Corporation in accordance with these regulations after 31st December 2026.
