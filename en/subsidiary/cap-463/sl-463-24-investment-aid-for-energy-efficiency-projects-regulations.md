# Investment Aid for Energy Efficiency Projects Regulations (S.L. 463.24)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 463.24**

**INVESTMENT AID FOR ENERGY EFFICIENCY**

**PROJECTS REGULATIONS**

1st November, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 122 of 2018, as amended by Legal Notice 118 of 2019.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Investment Aid for Energy Efficiency Projects Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 463.*

"the Act" means the Malta Enterprise Act;

"the Corporation" means Malta Enterprise established under the Act;

"the Guidelines" means such guidelines as may be published by the Corporation from time to time in terms of the Act.

## 3. Assistance to support eligible undertakings under Investment Aid for Energy Efficiency Projects.

Where the Corporation is satisfied that the undertaking is an eligible undertaking as prescribed within the Guidelines, the Corporation may provide assistance to such undertakings in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the Guidelines.

## 4. Deadline for submission of applications for assistance.

*Substituted by: L.N. 118 of 2019.*

Applications for assistance under these regulations shall reach the Corporation by not later than the date established in the Guidelines.
