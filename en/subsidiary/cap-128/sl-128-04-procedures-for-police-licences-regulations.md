# Procedures for Police Licences Regulations (S.L. 128.4)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 128.04**

**PROCEDURES FOR POLICE LICENCES**

**REGULATIONS**

1st June, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 93 of 2000.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Procedures for Police Licences Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation. Cap. 128.

In these Regulations, "licence" has the same meaning as is given to in article 2 of the Police Licences Act.

## 3. Consultation by Commissioner of Police with government departments, agencies.

In processing an application for a licence the Commissioner of Police shall, where applicable, seek to request and obtain the views of Government departments or agencies for the purpose of ascertaining whether there is any objection on their part to the application and for prescribing, where necessary, the conditions under which such licences or anyone or more classes thereof may be granted renewed, transferred or cancelled.

## 4. Government departments, agencies to respond within the limit.

Government departments or agencies shall reply in writing to the Commissioner of Police by not later than thirty days from the date of receipt of such request by the Commissioner. In default, such government departments or agencies shall be deemed not to object to the application.

## 5. Extension of time limit in special circumstances.

Government departments or agencies may in special circumstances seek from the Commissioner of Police to obtain an extension of the time limit referred to in regulation 4 and if the Commissioner is satisfied that such request is justified he may grant an extension of not more than thirty days, or such other period of extension as he may deem necessary in particular cases after consulting the Department or agency involved.

## 6. Applicants to reply within time limit.

When an applicant for a licence fails to reply to a request for information by the Commissioner of Police or by a government department or agency or fails to comply with a condition imposed by the Commissioner of Police or a government department or government agency within a specified limit of time, his application shall be considered as having been withdrawn and the Commissioner of Police shall not in such circumstances take further cognisance of the said application.
