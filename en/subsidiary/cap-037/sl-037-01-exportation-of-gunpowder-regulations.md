# Exportation of Gunpowder Regulations (S.L. 37.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 37.01**

EXPORTATION OF GUNPOWDER REGULATIONS 17th December, 1909 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 250 of 1909.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Exportation of Gunpowder Regulations.

## 2. Exportation of gunpowder.

No gunpowder shall be exported from Malta except under conditions to be prescribed by the Commissioner of Police in each case.

## 3. Applications.

Applications for permission to export gunpowder lying in the government magazine should reach the Commissioner of Police at least forty-eight hours before the time fixed for the shipment of the gunpowder.
