# Exportation of Arms and Ammunition Regulations (S.L. 37.2)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 37.02**

**EXPORTATION OF ARMS AND AMMUNITION**

**REGULATIONS**

11th March, 1910 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 65 of 1910, as amended by Ordinance XVI of 1960.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Exportation of Arms and Ammunition Regulations.

## 2. Exportation of arms and ammunition.

No person shall export from Malta arms, ammunition, or other munitions of war, not required for the Armed Forces of Malta, without a special permission from the Government.

## 3. Applications.

Applications for such permission shall be addressed to the Office of the Prime Minister and shall state -

(a) the name and other particulars of the exporter;

(b) the number and quality, or, as the case may be, the quantity of the articles intended to be exported; and (c) the country to which such articles are intended to be exported.

## 4. Inspection at Customs.

*Amended by: XVI. 1960.3.*

When permission has been granted to export any such arms, ammunition or other munitions of war, the same or the packages or parcels containing the same shall be taken to the Customs House before shipment, for inspection by the Comptroller of Customs, who may order any such package or parcel to be opened by the exporter or his agent, for the purpose of verifying its contents.
