# Imported Cigarettes Regulations (S.L. 37.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 37.06**

**IMPORTED CIGARETTES REGULATIONS**

1st September, 1963 LEGAL NOTICE 22 of 1963, as amended by Act XIII of 1983 and Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Imported Cigarettes Regulations.

## 2. Imported cigarettes to have band affixed to containers.

It shall not be lawful to import into these Islands or to take out of bond any imported cigarettes unless the packet, box or container immediately enclosing the cigarettes has been sealed by means of a band or stamp issued by the Comptroller of Customs.

## 3. Method of affixing band or stamp.

The band or stamp shall be affixed in such a way that it shall not be possible to open the container without breaking it.

## 4. Bands to be issued by Comptroller of Customs.

*Amended by: XIII.1993.5; L.N. 407 of 2007.*

The bands or stamps shall be issued by the Comptroller of Customs on application by the importer of the cigarettes at the price of 0.408 of an euro per thousand bands or stamps.

## 5. Penalties.

Any person who has in his possession or in any way disposes of imported cigarettes which do not conform to the provisions of regulations 2 and 3 hereof shall be liable, on conviction, to the penalties provided for in the Customs Ordinance.

*Cap. 37.*
