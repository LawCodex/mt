# Prohibition of Importation of Obnoxious Articles Regulations (S.L. 37.8)

PROHIBITION OF IMPORTATION

*1*

OF OBNOXIOUS ARTICLES [ S.L.37.08

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 37.08**

**PROHIBITION OF IMPORTATION OF**

**OBNOXIOUS ARTICLES REGULATIONS**

12th December, 1975 LEGAL NOTICE 155 of 1975, as amended by Legal Notice 72 of 1994.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Prohibition of Importation of Obnoxious Articles Regulations.

## 2. Prohibition of importation of certain articles.

*Amended by: L.N. 72 of 1994.*

It shall not be lawful to import into Malta or to take out of bond any print, painting, photograph, film, book, card or writing or any other article whatsoever, whether similar to the above or not which is in any way threatening, offensive, libellous or seditious or which is pornographic or obscene in accordance with regulations made under article 208 of the Criminal Code:

*Cap. 9.*

Provided that the prohibition stipulated in this regulation shall not be used to justify the examination or censorship of any film imported into Malta by a person legally entitled to provide television broadcasting services in Malta when the Comptroller of Customs is satisfied that such film is being imported only for the purpose of its being broadcast on television according to the provisions of the Broadcasting Act, and of any regulations made or licence issued thereunder.

*Cap. 350.*
