# Fire Precautions at Customs Regulations (S.L. 37.7)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 37.07**

**FIRE PRECAUT IONS AT CUSTOMS**

**REGULATIONS**

7th August, 1964 LEGAL NOTICE 34 of 1964, as amended by Act XIII of 1983 and Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fire Precautions at Customs Regulations.

## 2. Prohibition smoke or to light matches or lighters.

It shall not be lawful to smoke or to light any matches or lighters in any customs shed or bonded store, or in any open area within the customs enclosure where at any time a notice to this effect is put up.

## 3. Prohibition to carry lighters.

The carrying of cigarette lighters shall not be lawful in any customs shed or bonded store.

## 4. Penalty.

*Amended by: XIII.1983.5; L.N. 407 of 2007.*

Any person who does not comply with any of the above regulations shall be liable, on conviction, to a fine not exceeding eleven euros and sixty-five cents (11.65) for each act of non-compliance.
