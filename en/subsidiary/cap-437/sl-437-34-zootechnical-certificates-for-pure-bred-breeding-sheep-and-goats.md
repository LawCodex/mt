# Zootechnical Certificates for Pure-Bred Breeding Sheep and Goats, their Semen, Ova and Embryos Regulations (S.L. 437.34)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.34**

ZOOTECHNICAL CERTIFICATES FOR PURE-BRED BREEDING SHEEP AND GOATS, THEIR SEMEN,

**OVA AND EMBRYOS REGULATIONS\***

31st October, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 328 of 2003.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Zootechnical Certificates for Pure-Bred Breeding Sheep and Goats, their Semen, Ova and Embryos Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is the implementation of European Union Commission Decision 90/258/EEC laying down the zootechnical certificates for pure-bred breeding sheep and goats, their semen, ova and embryos. This Decision finds a legal basis under European Union Council Directive 89/361/EEC, article 6.

## 2. Particulars which must be provided in the zootechnical certificate for pure-bred breeding sheep and goats.

(1) The following particulars shall be provided in the zootechnical certificate for pure-bred breeding sheep and goats - - issuing body,

\- name of flock-book, entry number in flock-book,

\- date of issue,

\- system of identification,

\- identification,

\- date of birth,

\- breed,

\- sex,

\- name and address of breeder,

\- name and address of owner,

\- pedigree:

Father Grandfather Grandmother Flock-book No. Flock-book No. Flock-book No. Mother Grandfather Grandmother Flock-book No. Flock-book No. Flock-book No. (2) The results of performance tests and the updated results, with origin, of the assessment of genetic value, on the animal itself and its parents and grandparents shall be mentioned in the certificate it they have been carried out in accordance with European Union Commission Decision 90/256/EEC.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 11 of 2024.

## 3. Indications of particulars listed in regulation 2.

The particulars referred to in regulation 2 may be indicated -

(a) in a certificate corresponding to the specimen in Schedule I;

(b) in documents accompanying the pure-bred breeding sheep or goats, in which case the competent authority shall certify that the particulars specified in regulation 2 are contained in the documents by the following formula:

''The undersigned certifies that these documents contain the particulars mentioned in Article 1 of Commission Decision 90/258/EEC.''.

## 4. Particulars to be mentioned in the zootechnical certificate for semen of pure-bred breeding sheep and goats.

The following particulars shall be mentioned in the zootechnical certificate for semen of pure-bred breeding sheep and goats:

All the data specified in regulation 2 concerning the donor ram or billygoat and, in the case of dairy breeds, his blood group or the results of a test giving equivalent scientific guarantees, information allowing identification of the semen, the date of its collection and the names and addresses of the semen collection centre and of the consignee.

## 5. Indication of particulars as provided in regulation 4.

The particulars referred to in regulation 4 may be indicated -

(a) in a certificate corresponding to the specimen in Schedule II;

(b) in documents accompanying the semen of the pure-bred breeding sheep or goat, in which case the competent authority shall certify that the particulars specified in regulation 4 are contained in the documents by the following:

''The undersigned certifies that these documents contain the particulars mentioned in Article 3 of Commission Decision 90/258/EEC.''.

## 6. Particulars which must be mentioned in the zootechnical certificate for ova of pure-bred breeding sheep and goats.

(1) The following particulars shall be mentioned in the zootechnical certificate for ova of pure-bred breeding sheep and goats:

All the data specified in regulation 2 concerning the donor ewe and nanny-goat, information allowing identification of the ovum, the date of its collection and the names and addresses of the ovum collection centre and of the consignee.

(2) Where there is more than one ovum in a straw, this must be clearly stated and all the ova must have the same parentage.

## 7. Indication of particulars as provided under regulation 6.

The particulars referred to in regulation 6 may be indicated -

(a) in a certificate corresponding to the specimen in Schedule III;

(b) in documents accompanying the ova of the pure-bred breeding sheep or goat, in which case the competent authority shall certify that the particulars specified in regulation 6 are contained in the document, by the following:

''The undersigned certifies that these documents contain the particulars mentioned in Article 5 of Commission Decision 90/258/EEC.''.

## 8. Particulars to be mentioned in the zootechnical certificate for embryos of pure-bred breeding sheep and goats.

(1) The following particulars shall be mentioned in the zootechnical certificate for embryos of pure-bred breeding sheep and goats:

All the data specified in regulation 2 concerning the donor ewe or nanny goat, and all the data specified in the first indent of regulation 4 concerning the donor ram and billygoat, information allowing identification of the embryos, date of insemination or fertilisation, date of collection and the names and addresses of the embryo collection centre and of the consignee.

(2) Where there is more than one embryo in a straw, this must be clearly stated and all the embryos must have the same parentage.

## 9. Indication of particulars referred in regulation 8.

The particulars referred to in regulation 8 may be indicated -

(a) in a certificate corresponding to the specimen in Schedule IV;

(b) in documents accompanying the embryos of the pure-bred breeding sheep or goat, in which case the competent authority shall certify that the particulars specified in regulation 8 are contained in the documents by the following:

''The undersigned certifies that these documents contain the particulars mentioned in Article 7 of Commission Decision 90/258/EEC.''.
