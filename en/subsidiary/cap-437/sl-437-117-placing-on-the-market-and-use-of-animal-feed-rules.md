# Placing on the Market and Use of Animal Feed Rules (S.L. 437.117)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.117**

PLACING ON THE MARKET AND USE OF ANIMAL

**FEED RULES**

18th February, 2025

LEGAL NOTICE 36 of 2025.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these rules is the Placing on the Market and Use of Animal Feed Rules.

(2) The scope of these rules is to implement the requirements of Regulation (EC) No 767/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 on the placing on the market and use of feed, amending European Parliament and Council Regulation (EC) No 1831/ 2003 and repealing Council Directive 79/373/EEC, Commission Directive 80/511/EEC, Council Directives 82/471/EEC, 83/228/EEC, 93/74/EEC, 93/113/EC and 96/25/EC and Commission Decision 2004/ 217/EC, as in force from time to time.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 437.*

"Act" means the Veterinary Services Act;

"competent authority" means the Department of Veterinary Services;

"Director" means the Director of Veterinary Services; and

"Regulation (EC) No 767/2009" means Regulation (EC) No 767/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 on the placing on the market and use of feed, amending European Parliament and Council Regulation (EC) No 1831/2003 and repealing Council Directive 79/373/EEC, Commission Directive 80/511/EEC, Council Directives 82/471/ EEC, 83/228/EEC, 93/74/EEC, 93/113/EC and 96/25/EC and Commission Decision 2004/217/EC, as in force from time to time.

(2) Unless the context otherwise requires, words and phrases used in these rules which are not defined herein, shall have the same meaning as assigned to them in Article 3 of the Regulation (EC) No 767/2009 and in article 2 of the Act.

## 3. Part IX of the Act.

Without prejudice to any other power or responsibility in accordance with the law, and to its application in any other manner in accordance with the law, Part IX of the Act shall also apply mutatis mutandis to these rules.

## 4. Offences, punishments and administrative penalties.

(1) Without prejudice to rule 3, any person who breaches the provisions of these rules or any obligation in accordance with these rules, or breaches a provision of Regulation (EC) No 767/ 2009, or hinders any person responsible for their implementation, shall on conviction, be liable to the punishments established in the Act.

(2) Without prejudice to the provisions of sub-rule (1), the Court may also order any person guilty of an offence, to pay for the expenses incurred by the competent authority and the Director as a result of the said offence, as well as the revocation of any permit or certification issued under these rules and the confiscation, retention or destruction or other disposal of the animals involved.
