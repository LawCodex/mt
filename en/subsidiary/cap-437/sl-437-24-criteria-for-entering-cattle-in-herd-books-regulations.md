# Criteria for Entering Cattle in Herd-Books Regulations (S.L. 437.24)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.24**

**CRITERIA FOR ENTERING CATTLE**

**IN HERD-BOOKS REGULATIONS\***

24th October, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 316 of 2003.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Criteria for Entering Cattle in Herd-Books Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to implement the provisions found under European Union Commission Decision 84/ 419/EEC laying down the criteria for entering cattle in herd-books. This Decision finds a legal basis under European Union Council Directive 77/504/EEC, article 6(1), fourth indent.

## 2. Entry in the main section of herd-book.

To qualify for entry in the main section of the herd-book of its breed an animal must -

(a) be descended from parents and grandparents entered in a herd-book of that same breed;

(b) be identified at birth according to the rules of that book;

(c) have a pedigree established in accordance with the rules of that herd-book.

## 3. Sectioning of herd-books.

The main section of a herd-book may be divided into several classes according to the animals’ merits. Only cattle meeting the criteria laid down in regulation 2 may be entered in one of those classes.

## 4. Criteria for females.

(1) A breeders’ organisation or association keeping a herd-book may decide that a female, which does not meet the criteria laid down in regulation 2, may be entered in a supplementary section of that herd-book. The female must meet the following requirements:

(a) be identified in accordance with the herd-book rules, (b) be judged to conform to the breed standard,

(c) have a minimum performance criteria as laid down by the herd-book rules.

(2) The requirements mentioned in of subregulation (1)(b) and (c) may be differentiated according to whether the female belongs to the breed although it has no known origin or was obtained from a crossing programme approved by the breeders’ organisation or association managing the herd-book.

## 5. Pure-bred females.

A female whose mother and maternal grandmother are entered in a supplementary section of the herd-book as provided for in regulation 4(1) and whose father and two grandfathers are entered in the main section of the book in accordance with the criteria laid down in regulation 2, shall be regarded as a pure-bred female and entered in the main section of the book, provided for in regulation 2.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 11 of 2024.

## 6. Criteria for imported animals.

Where a book contains several classes in the main section, an animal not originating from Malta but from a Member State shall be entered in the class of the book whose criteria it meets.
