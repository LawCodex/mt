# Animal Health and Transmissible Animal Diseases (Implementing) Rules (S.L. 437.120)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.120**

ANIMAL HEALTH AND TRANSMISSIBLE ANIMAL

**DISEASES (IMPLEMENTING) RULES**

29th April, 2025

LEGAL NOTICE 77 of 2025.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these rules is the Animal Health and Transmissible Animal Diseases (Implementing) Rules.

(2) The scope of these rules is to implement the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2016 on transmissible animal diseases and amending and repealing certain acts in the area of animal health ("Animal Health Law"), as in force from time to time.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 437.*

"Act" means the Veterinary Services Act;

"competent authority" means the Department as defined in article 2 of the Act;

''Department'' shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

"Director" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

"Regulation (EU) 2016/429" means Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2016 on transmissible animal diseases and amending and repealing certain acts in the area of animal health ("Animal Health Law"), as in force from time to time;

Provided that in these rules and as applicable according to the case, it shall also include all the delegated or implementing acts as adopted by the European Union according to Regulation (EU) 2016/429.

(2) Unless the context otherwise requires, words and phrases used in these rules which are not defined herein, shall have the same meaning as assigned to them in Article 4 of the Regulation (EU) 2016/429 and article 2 of the Act.

## 3. Competent authority.

The Department shall be the competent authority for the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2016/429.

## 4. Part IX of the Act.

Without prejudice to any other power or responsibility in accordance with the law, and to its application in any other manner in accordance with the law, Part IX of the Act shall also apply mutatis mutandis to these rules.

## 5. Offences and punishments.

(1) Without prejudice to rule 4, any person who breaches the provisions of these rules or any obligation in accordance with these rules, or breaches a provision of Regulation (EU) 2016/429, or hinders any person responsible for their implementation, shall on conviction, be liable to the punishments established in the Act.

(2) Without prejudice to the provisions of sub-rule (1), the Court may also order any person guilty of an offence, to pay for the expenses incurred by the competent authority and the Director as a result of the said offence, as well as the revocation of any permit or certification issued under these rules and the confiscation, retention or destruction or other means of disposal of the animals involved.
