# Hybrid-Breeding Pigs for Breeding Regulations (S.L. 437.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.04**

**HYBRID-BREEDING PIGS FOR BREEDING**

**REGULATIONS\***

7th October, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 282 of 2003.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Hybrid-Breeding Pigs for Breeding Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to implement the provisions found under European Union Council Directive 90/119/ EEC of hybrid breeding pigs which in turn has a legal basis in article 8 of European Union Council Directive 88/661/EEC.

## 2. Acceptance of hybrid breeding pigs.

In the territory of Malta, without prejudice to animal health rules there is no prohibition or restriction of or obstacle to -

(a) the acceptance of hybrid breeding female pigs for breeding;

(b) the acceptance of hybrid breeding male pigs for natural service;

(c) the acceptance for artificial insemination of hybrid breeding male pigs whose line has been tested for monitoring performance and assessing its genetic value;

(d) the use of the semen of the animal referred to in paragraph (e);

(e) the acceptance, for official testing, of hybrid breeding male pigs or the use of the semen of such pigs within quantitative limits necessary for the tests for monitoring their performance and assessing their genetic value to be carried out;

(f) the use of ova and embryos from hybrid breeding female pigs.

## 3. Collection, treatment and storage of semen, ova and embryos for marketing.

In the territory of Malta, without prejudice to animal health rules, for marketing, the semen, ova and embryos are collected, treated and stored by an officially approved centre or officially approved staff.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 11 of 2024.
