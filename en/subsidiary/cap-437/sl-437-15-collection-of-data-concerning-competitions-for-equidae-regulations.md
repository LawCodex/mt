# Collection of Data Concerning Competitions for Equidae Regulations (S.L. 437.15)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.15**

**COLLECTION OF DATA CONCERNING**

**COMPETITIONS FOR EQUIDAE REGULATIONS\***

24th October, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 307 of 2003.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Collection of Data Concerning Competitions for Equidae Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to implement the provisions found under European Commission Decision 92/216/ EEC on the collection of data concerning competitions for equidae as referred to in Article 4(2) of Council Directive 90/428/EEC.

## 2. Co-ordinating authority responsible for collecting data.

(1) In the territory of Malta, there shall be appointed a co-ordinating authority responsible for collecting the data concerning - (a) competitions as referred to in the first indent of article 4(2) of European Union Council Directive 90/428/ EEC organised in the territory of Malta,

(b) the criteria for the distribution of funds provided for in the third subparagraph of the second indent of the said article 4(2).

(2) The competent authority of Malta shall communicate to the European Commission the name and address of the co-ordinating authority appointed in accordance with subregulation (1).

\*These regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 83 of 2025.
