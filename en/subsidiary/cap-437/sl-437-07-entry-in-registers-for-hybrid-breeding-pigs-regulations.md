# Entry in Registers for Hybrid Breeding Pigs Regulations (S.L. 437.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.07**

**ENTRY IN REGISTERS FOR HYBRID BREEDING**

**PIGS REGULATIONS\***

7th October, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 285 of 2003.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Entry in Registers for Hybrid Breeding Pigs Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to implement the provisions found under European Union Commission Decision 89/ 505/EEC laying down the criteria governing entry in registers for hybrid breeding pigs, which in turn finds its legal basis under article 10(c) of European Union Council Directive 88/661/EEC.

## 2. Requirements for the registration of hybrid breeding pigs.

To qualify for entry in the register hybrid breeding pigs must -

(a) be identified after birth according to the rules of that register;

(b) have parentage established in accordance with the rules of that register.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 11 of 2024.
