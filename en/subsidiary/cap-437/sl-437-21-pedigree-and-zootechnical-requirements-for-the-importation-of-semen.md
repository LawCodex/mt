# Pedigree and Zootechnical Requirements for the Importation of Semen of Certain Animals Regulations (S.L. 437.21)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.21**

**PEDIGREE AND ZOOTECHNICAL**

**REQUIREMENTS FOR THE IMPORTATION OF**

**SEMEN OF CERTAIN ANIMALS REGULATIONS\***

24th October, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 313 of 2003.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Pedigree and Zootechnical Requirements for the Importation of Semen of Certain Animals Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to implement the provisions found under European Commission Decision 96/509/EC laying down pedigree and zootechnical requirements for the importation of semen of certain animals. This Decision finds a legal basis under European Union Council Directive 94/28/EC, article 5, second and third indent.

## 2. Importation of semen.

Semen as referred to in article 1 of European Union Council Directive 94/28/EC, coming from an animal which has not undergone the performance tests and genetic value assessment on the basis of the principles provided for by zootechnical rules, may only be imported within the limits of the quantities necessary for approved organisations or associations to carry out such official test.

## 3. Pedigree and zootechnical certificates.

The semen referred to in regulation 2 must be accompanied by -

(a) a pedigree and zootechnical certificate conforming to the model in Schedule I, issued by the competent authorities of the third country;

(b) a certificate conforming to the model in Schedule II, issued by the competent authorities of the territory of Malta or the Member State of destination.

The certificates mentioned in paragraphs (a) and (b) above must be presented together at the time of importation.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 11 of 2024.
