# Identification of Equidae for Breeding and Production Regulations (S.L. 437.17)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.17**

**IDENTIFICATION OF EQUIDAE FOR BREEDING**

**AND PRODUCTION REGULATIONS\***

24th October, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 309 of 2003.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Identification of Equidae for Breeding and Production Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to implement the provisions laid down under Commission Decision 2000/68/EC amending Commission Decision 93/623/EEC and establishing the identification of equidae for breeding and production. This Decision finds its legal basis under European Union Council Directive 90/427/EEC, article 8(1).

## 2. Identification number.

(1) The identification number mentioned in Section II (1) of the identification document laid down by European Commission Decision 93/623/EEC shall be the life-number of the animal, which must be maintained or a reference to which must be established whenever the competent authorities modify registration details of the animal in question.

(2) The identification number referred to in subregulation (1) shall be the identification number referred to in article 7(3) of Council Directive 90/426/EEC.

## 3. Identification document accompanying equidae for breeding.

The identification document accompanying equidae for breeding and production during their movement must contain at least the information provided for in Sections I, II, III, IV and IX of the identification document laid down by European Commission Decision 93/623/EEC.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 11 of 2024.
