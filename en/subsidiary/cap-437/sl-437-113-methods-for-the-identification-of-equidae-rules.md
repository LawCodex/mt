# Methods for the Identification of Equidae Rules (S.L. 437.113)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.113**

METHODS FOR THE IDENTIFICATION OF EQUIDAE RULES

26th May, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 75 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these rules is the Methods for the Identification of Equidae Rules.

(2) The scope of these rules is to implement the requirements of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/262 of 17 February 2015 laying down rules pursuant to Council Directives 90/ 427/EEC and 2009/156/EC as regards the methods for the identification of equidae (Equine Passport Regulation).

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 437.*

"Act" means the Veterinary Services Act;

"Department" shall have the same meaning as assigned to in the Act;

"Regulation (EU) 2015/262" means the Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/262 of 17 February 2015 laying down rules pursuant to Council Directives 90/427/EEC and 2009/156/EC as regards the methods for the identification of equidae (Equine Passport Regulation), as in force from time to time.

(2) Unless the context otherwise requires, words and phrases used in these rules which are not defined herein, shall have the same meaning as assigned to them in Regulation (EU) 2015/262 and in the Act.

## 3. Competent authority.

For the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2015/262 the competent authority shall be the Department.

## 4. Part IX of the Act.

Without prejudice to any other power or responsibility in accordance with the law and to its application in any other manner in accordance with the law, Part IX of the Act shall also apply mutatis mutandis to these rules.

## 5. Offences and punishments.

(1) Without prejudice to rule 4, any person who breaches the provisions of these rules or any obligation in accordance with these rules, or breaches a provision of Regulation (EU) 2015/262, or hinders any person responsible for their implementation, shall on conviction be liable to the punishments established in the Act.

(2) Without prejudice to the provisions of sub-rule (1), the Court may also order any person guilty of an offence, to pay for the expenses incurred by the competent authority and the Director of Veterinary Services as a result of the said offence, as well as the revocation of any permit or certification issued under these rules and the confiscation, retention or destruction or other means of disposal of the animals involved.
