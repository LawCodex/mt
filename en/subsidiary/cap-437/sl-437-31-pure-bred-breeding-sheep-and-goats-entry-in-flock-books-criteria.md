# Pure-Bred Breeding Sheep and Goats (Entry in Flock-Books Criteria) Regulations (S.L. 437.31)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 437.31**

**PURE-BRED BREEDING SHEEP AND GOATS**

**(ENTRY IN FLOCK-BOOKS CRITERIA)**

**REGULATIONS\***

31st October, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 325 of 2003.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Pure-Bred Breeding Sheep and Goats (Entry in Flock-Books Criteria) Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to implement the provisions found under European Union Commission Decision 90/ 255/EEC laying down the criteria governing entry in flock-books for pure-bred breeding sheep and goats. This Decision finds its legal basis under European Union Council Directive 89/361/EEC, article 4, second indent.

## 2. Qualifications for entry into the main section of the book.

To qualify for entry in the main section of the book of its breed a pure-bred breeding sheep or goat must -

(a) be descended from parents and grandparents entered in a flock-book of that same breed;

(b) be identified after birth according to the rules of the book;

(c) have a pedigree established in accordance with the rules of the book.

## 3. Classification according to animals’ characteristics.

The main section of the flock-book may be divided into several classes according to the animals’ characteristics. Only pure-bred breeding sheep and goats meeting the criteria laid down in regulation 2 may be entered in one of the classes.

## 4. Non-conforming animals of the female species.

(1) A breeders’ organisation or association keeping a flock-book may decide that a female which does not meet the criteria laid down in regulation 2 may be entered in a schedule to the flock-book. The female must meet the following requirements:

(a) be identified after birth in accordance with the flock-book rules,

(b) be judged to conform to the breed standard,

(c) have minimum characteristics as laid down by the flock-book rules.

(2) A female whose mother and maternal grandmother are entered in the Schedule to the flock-book as provided for in subregulation (1) and whose father and two grandfathers are entered in the main section of the book in accordance with regulation 2, is regarded as a pure-bred female and entered in the main section of the book, as provided for in regulation 2.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 11 of 2024.

(3) The requirements mentioned in the second and third indents of subregulation (1) may be differentiated according to whether the female belongs to the breed but has no known origin or was obtained from a crossing programme approved by the breeders’ organisations or associations.

## 5. Criteria laid down under regulation 2 not being kept -animals of the male species.

A breeder’s organisation or association keeping a flock-book may decide that a male which does not meet the criteria laid down in regulation 2, may be entered in an Schedule to the flock book. The male must meet the following requirements:

(a) be identified after birth in accordance with the flock-book rules;

(b) be judged to conform to the breed standard;

(c) have minimum characteristics as laid down by the flock-book rules;

(d) fulfil the conditions laid down in the Schedule.

## 6. Classification entered into section of flock-book according to characteristics.

Where a book contains several classes, a pure-bred breeding sheep or goat from another flock-book of the same breed and having specific characteristics distinguishing it from the population of the same breed in the flock-book of destination, shall be entered in such section of the book whose characteristics it meets.
