# Practice of Concurrent Professions Regulations (S.L. 464.09)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 464.09**

**PRACTICE OF CONCURRENT PROFESSIONS**

**REGULATIONS**

21st April, 2006

LEGAL NOTICE 105 of 2006, as amended by Legal Notices 273 of 2010 and 249 of 2025.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Practice of Concurrent Professions Regulations.

## 2. Concurrent practice.

*Amended by: L.N. 273 of 2010.*

(1) Any healthcare professional can practise his profession concurrently with another health care profession.

(2) In the case of healthcare professionals requesting registration in more than one register, the following procedure shall be followed:

(a) the applicant shall inform the relevant Council of any registration or pending application with another Council. Any failure to do so will result in automatic disqualification from the second application;

(b) applications for dual registration and practice will be assessed by the relevant Councils using the following criteria:

(i) potential conflict of interest,

(ii) patient and community implications,

(iii) ability to maintain satisfactory level of continuous professional development in both professions,

(iv) regulatory implications.

## 3. Criteria required for registration.

The applicant shall fulfil all the current criteria required for the registration of both health care professions.

## 4. Dual concurrent registration.

Dual concurrent registration and practice may be limited by conditions set by the relevant Council and approved by Minister.

## 5. Code of ethics and code of practice.

The successful applicant shall abide with the Code of Ethics and the Code of Practice for both health care professions.

## 6. Liability.

*Amended by: L.N.249 of 2025 Cap. 464. S.L. 464.20.*

The successful applicant shall be liable to the disciplinary action, offences and erasures as specified in the Part VII of the Health Care Professions Act and the Disciplinary Proceedings Regulations.
