# Professions Complementary to Medicine Regulations (S.L. 464.16)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 464.16**

**PROFESSIONS COMPLEMENTARY TO**

**MEDICINE REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2009

LEGAL NOTICE 277 of 2008, as amended by Legal Notice 219 of 2022.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 219 of 2022.*

The title of these regulations is the Professions Complementary to Medicine Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 219 of 2022.*

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires -

*Cap. 464.*

"the Act" means the Health Care Professions Act;

"the Council" means the Council for Professions Complementary to Medicine, established under article 26 of the Act;

## 3. Granting of licence.

A licence shall be granted by the President of Malta to those persons whose name is entered in one of registers held by the Council and on the recommendation of the Council.

## 4. Licence conditions.

Repealed by Legal Notice 219 of 2022.

## 5. Code of practice.

It shall be the duty of such persons who have been issued a licence, or who have been registered to have a licence issued to them under these regulations, to abide by the conditions and to satisfy the requirements laid down by the Code of Practice for their profession.
