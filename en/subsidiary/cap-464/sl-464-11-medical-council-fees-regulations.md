# Medical Council (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 464.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 464.11**

**MEDICAL COUNCIL (FEES) REGULATIONS**

22nd December, 2006 LEGAL NOTICE 330 of 2006, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007 and Act XIII of 2015.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Medical Council (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

The following fees shall be paid to the Medical Council:

(a) in respect of every new registration in

*Substituted by: XIII. 2015.170.*

the Medical Registers: Medical,

Dental, Specialist and Provisional ....... €51.30

(b) in respect of an annual retention fee in

the Medical or Dental Register ............ €38.50

(c) in respect of an annual retention fee in

each of the Specialist Registers ........... €51.30

(d) for every certificate issued to a medical

practitioner or dental surgeon .............. €12.90

(e) for every complete exam or part thereof

as prescribed ....................................... €1,218.20
