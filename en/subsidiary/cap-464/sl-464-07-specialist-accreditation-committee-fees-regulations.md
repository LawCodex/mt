# Specialist Accreditation Committee (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 464.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 464.07**

**SPECIALIST ACCREDITATION COMMITTEE**

**(FEES) REGULATIONS**

6th May, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 133 of 2005, as amended by Legal Notices 397 of 2005, 427 of 2007 and 238 of 2009.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Specialist Accreditation Committee (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 397 of 2005; L.N. 427 of 2007; L.N. 238 of 2009.*

A fee of one hundred euro (€100) shall be paid to the Specialist Accreditation Committee for each application for accreditation to the Specialist Medical Register.
