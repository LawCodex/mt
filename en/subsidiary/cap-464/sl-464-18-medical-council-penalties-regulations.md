# Medical Council (Penalties) Regulations (S.L. 464.18)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 464.18**

MEDICAL COUNCIL (PENALTIES) REGULATIONS\* 3rd February, 2009 LEGAL NOTICE 38 of 2009.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Medical Council (Penalties) Regulations.

## 2. Penalties.

In such cases as the Medical Council is satisfied that a charge against a practitioner has been proved, then it may impose a penalty not exceeding twenty thousand euro.

## 3. Erasure of name from register.

If a practitioner fails to pay such penalty as is imposed on him within three months from the date when the penalty is imposed, then the Medical Council shall order that the practitioner’s name to be erased from any appropriate register without further notice.

## 4. Restoration of name to register.

Where the name of a practitioner has been erased from any register for the reason mentioned in regulation 3, the practitioner’s name shall only be restored to the appropriate register when full payment of the penalty is effected and, in such case, the Medical Council shall order his name to be restored to the appropriate register with immediate effect.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 249 of 2025.
