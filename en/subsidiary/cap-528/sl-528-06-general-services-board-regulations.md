# General Services Board Regulations (S.L. 528.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 528.06**

**GENERAL SERVICES BOARD REGULATIONS**

28th February, 2014

LEGAL NOTICE 72 of 2014.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the General Services Board Regulations.

## 2. General Services Board.

There shall be a General Services Board whose functions shall be:

*Cap. 10.*

(a) to serve as an Appeals Board from decisions, relating to matters and issues arising out of the Code of Police Laws, taken by sanitary inspectors or sanitary officers of the Malta Environment and Planning Authority;

*Cap. 94. Cap. 31.*

(b) to discharge such functions as were, or may have been, vested in it by the Department of Health (Constitution) Ordinance prior to its repeal, and by the Medical and Kindred Professions Ordinance and the regulations made under any of such Ordinances;

(c) to discharge such other functions as are, or may be, vested in it by any other law in force in Malta.

## 3. Composition of the Board.

(1) The General Services Board shall be composed as follows:

(a) the Superintendent of Public Health as Chairperson;

(b) the Director General (Works and Services Department) or his representative;

(c) two specialists in Public Health Medicine to be appointed by the Minister;

(d) a Senior Medical Officer to be appointed by the Minister;

(e) the Director Environmental Health or his representative;

(f) a representative of the Chamber of Architects and Civil Engineers;

(g) a Perit appointed by the Minister;

(h) a person, not in the service of the Government, appointed by the President of Malta.

(2) There shall be a Secretary to the General Services Board, who shall be appointed by the President of Malta.

## 4. Co-option to the Board.

When upon any occasion, the General Services Board desires to obtain the advice of any person upon any particular matter or matters, the Board may co-opt such person to be a member for such meeting or meetings as may be required, and such person, while so co-opted, shall have all the rights and privileges of a member, save that he shall not be entitled to vote upon any question.

Observance of the 5. The General Services Board shall respect and apply the principles of good

principles of good administrative behaviour laid down in article 3 administrative

behaviour. of the Administrative Justice Act.

Cap. 490.
