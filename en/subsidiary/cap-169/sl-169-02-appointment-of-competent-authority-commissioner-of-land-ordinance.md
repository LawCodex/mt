15th July, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 360 of 2004.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Appointment of Competent Authority (Commissioner of Land Ordinance) Regulations.

## 2. Appointment.

Malta Industrial Parks Limited (C 28965) is designated as the competent authority for the purposes of article 2 of the Commissioner of Land Ordinance.
