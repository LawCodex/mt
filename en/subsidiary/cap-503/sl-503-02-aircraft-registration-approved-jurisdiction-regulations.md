# Aircraft Registration (Approved Jurisdiction) Regulations (S.L. 503.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 503.02**

**AIRCRAFT REGISTRATION**

**(APPROVED JURISDICTIONS) REGULATIONS**

10th May, 2011

LEGAL NOTICE 186 of 2011, as amended by Legal Notice 380 of 2018 and 72 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Aircraft Registration (Approved Jurisdiction) Regulations.

## 2. Other countries recognised as approved jurisdiction. Cap. 503.

For the purposes of the provisions of the Aircraft Registration Act, the definition "approved jurisdiction" in article 2 thereof shall include the following countries approved by the Minister:

*Substituted by: L.N. 380 of 2018; L.N. 72 of 2022.*

Albania, Algeria, Argentina, Aruba, Bahamas, Bahrain, Barbados, Bolivia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Brazil, Bermuda, British Virgin Islands, Cayman Islands, Chile, China, Croatia, Djibouti, Egypt, Georgia, Gibraltar, Guernsey, Honduras, India, Indonesia, Iraq, Isle of Man, Ivory Coast, Kosovo, Japan, Jersey, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kenya, Kuwait, Lebanon, Macedonia, Malaysia, Marshall Islands, Monaco, Montenegro, Morocco, Oman, Panama, Philippines, Qatar, Russia, Saint Lucia, San Marino, St. Kitts and Nevis, St. Vincent and the Grenadines, Saudi Arabia, Serbia, Singapore, South Africa, Syria, Tunisia, Ukraine and United Arab Emirates.
