# Commission for the Protection from Ionising and Non-Ionising Radiation Regulations (S.L. 585.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 585.04**

**COMMISSION FOR THE PROTECTION FROM**

**IONISING AND NON-IONISING RADIATION**

**REGULATIONS**

11th June, 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 242 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Commission for the Protection from Ionising and Non-Ionising Radiation Regulations.

## 2.

For the purposes of these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires -

*Cap. 585.*

"the Act" means the Nuclear Safety and Radiation Protection Act;

"the Commission" means the Commission for the Protection from Ionising and Non-Ionising Radiation.

## 3. Legal personality of the Commission for the Protection from Ionising and Non-Ionising Radiation.

The Commission shall be a body corporate having a distinct legal personality and shall be capable, subject to the provisions of the Act, of entering into contracts, of acquiring, holding and disposing of any kind of property for the purposes of its functions, of suing and being sued, and of doing all such things and entering into all such transactions as are incidental or conducive to the exercise or performance of its functions under the Act.

## 4. Exemption from liability.

The members, officers and employees of the Commission in the performance of their functions under the Act or under any other law administered by the Commission, shall not be liable for any loss or damage suffered by any person by reason of anything done or omitted to be done in good faith in the course of the administration of the Act or of any other law.
