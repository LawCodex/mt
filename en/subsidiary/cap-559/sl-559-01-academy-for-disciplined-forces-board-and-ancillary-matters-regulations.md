# Academy for Disciplined Forces Board and Ancillary Matters Regulations (S.L. 559.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 559.01**

**ACADEMY FOR DISCIPLINED FORCES BOARD**

**AND ANCILLARY MATTERS REGULATIONS**

24th August, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 288 of 2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Academy for Disciplined Forces Board and Ancillary Matters Regulations

## 2. Definitions.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 559.*

"Act" means the Academy for Disciplined Forces Act;

"Board" means the Board of the Academy for Disciplined Forces established under article 5 of the Act;

"Chairperson" means the Chairperson of the Board of the Academy for Disciplined Forces established under article 5 of the Act;

"Minister" means the Minister for Home Affairs.

## 3. Appointment of Secretary.

(1) The Minister shall appoint a person to act as a Secretary to the Board. The Secretary shall have no voting rights.

(2) The Secretary shall have the duty to convene meetings, keep minutes and also assume such other tasks as the Chairperson of the Board may assign to him.

(3) The Secretary shall be appointed for a period of three (3) years, and shall be eligible for reappointment at the end of the period of his office.

(4) If the Secretary is for any reason unable to perform the functions of his office, the Chairperson may appoint a person to assume the functions of a secretary temporarily.
