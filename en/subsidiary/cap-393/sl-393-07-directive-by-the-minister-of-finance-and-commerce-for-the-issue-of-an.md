# Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an additional Lm17,250,000 7.35% Malta Government Stock 2007 (First Issue) (S.L. 393.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 393.07**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE AND COMMERCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM17,250,000 7.35% MALTA GOVERNMENT

**STOCK 2007 (FIRST ISSUE)**

24th October, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 163 of 1997.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an additional Lm17,250,000 7.35% Malta Government Stock 2007 (First Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

(1) It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm7,500,000 7.35% Malta Government Stock 2007 (I) an additional issue of Lm17,250,000 7.35% Malta Government Stock 2007 (I) be issued thus bringing the amount of 7.35% Malta Government Stock 2007 (I) created by means of Legal Notice 146 of 1997 to a total amount of Lm24,750,000.

*S.L.393.03*

(2) The half yearly contribution to the sinking fund according to paragraph 6 of Legal Notice 146 of 1997 shall be raised from Lm75,000 to Lm247,500.

(3) All applications received by the closing date 18th October, 1997 will be accepted.
