# Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an additional Lm18,750,000 6.9% Malta Government Stock 2002 (Fourth Issue) (S.L. 393.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 393.06**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE AND COMMERCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM18,750,000 6.9% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2002 (FOURTH ISSUE)

24th October, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 162 of 1997.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an additional Lm18,750,000 6.9% Malta Government Stock 2002 (Fourth Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

(1) It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm5,000,000 6.9% Malta Government Stock 2002 (IV) an additional issue of Lm18,750,000 6.9% Malta Government Stock 2002 (IV) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6.9% Malta Government Stock 2002 (IV) created by means of Legal Notice 145 of 1997 to a total amount of Lm23,750,000.

*S.L.393.02*

(2) The half yearly contribution to the sinking fund according to paragraph 6 of Legal Notice 145 of 1997 shall be raised from Lm50,000 to Lm237,500.

(3) All applications received by the closing date 18th October, 1997 will be accepted.
