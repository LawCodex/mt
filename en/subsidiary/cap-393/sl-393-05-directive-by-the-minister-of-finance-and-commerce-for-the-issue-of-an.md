# Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an additional Lm12,500,000 6.65% Malta Government Stock 2000 (Sixth Issue) (S.L. 393.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 393.05**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE AND COMMERCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM12,500,000 6.65% MALTA GOVERNMENT

**STOCK 2000 (SIXTH ISSUE)**

24th October, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 161 of 1997.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an additional Lm12,500,000 6.65% Malta Government Stock 2000 (Sixth Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

(1) It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm5,000,000 6.65% Malta Government Stock 2000 (VI) an additional issue of Lm12,500,000 6.65% Malta Government Stock 2000 (VI) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6.65% Malta Government Stock 2000 (VI) created by means of Legal Notice 144 of 1997 to a total amount of Lm17,500,000.

*S.L.393.01*

(2) The half yearly contribution to the sinking fund according to paragraph 6 of Legal Notice 144 of 1997 shall be raised from Lm50,000 to Lm175,000.

(3) All applications received by the closing date 18th October, 1997 will be accepted.
