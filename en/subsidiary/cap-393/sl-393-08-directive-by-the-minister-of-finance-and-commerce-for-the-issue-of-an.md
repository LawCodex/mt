# Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an additional €62,310,738.41 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2013 (First Issue) (S.L. 393.08)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 393.08**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE AND COMMERCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL €62,310,738.41 7.8% MALTA GOVERNMENT

**STOCK 2013 (FIRST ISSUE)**

24th October, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 164 of 1997, as amended y Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an additional €62,310,738.41 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2013 (First Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

(1) It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in €17,470,300.49 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2013 (I) an additional issue of €62,310,738.41 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2013 (I) be issued thus bringing the amount of 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2013 (I) created by means of Legal Notice 147 of 1997 to a total amount of €79,781,038.90.

*S.L.393.04*

(2) The half yearly contribution to the sinking fund according to paragraph 6 of Legal Notice 147 of 1997 shall be raised from €174,703.00 to €797,810.39.

(3) All applications received by the closing date 18th October, 1997 will be accepted.
