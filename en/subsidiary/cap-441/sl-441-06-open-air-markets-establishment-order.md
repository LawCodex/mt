# Open Air Markets (Establishment) Order (S.L. 441.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 441.06**

OPEN AIR MARKETS (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER 29th November, 2002;

11th February, 2011 LEGAL NOTICES 361 of 2002 and 51 of 2011, consolidated.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Open Air Markets (Establishment) Order.

## 2. Establishment of open air markets. Cap. 441.

For the purposes of article 23 of the Trading Licences Act, the Minister for Economic Services has established the following open air markets in terms and for the purposes of the said Act:

(a) Ta’ Qali - Parking Area (Park Nazzjonali). Marked as Zone NWTQ 14 in the Ta’ Qali Action Plan;

(b) Open-air market at Park and Ride, Flagstone Wharf, Hornworks Ditch, Floriana.
