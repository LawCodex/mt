# Electronic Communications Service (Modification of Radio Tracking System and Services Licence) Regulations (S.L. 399.19)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 399.19**

**ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS SERVICE**

(MODIFICATION OF RADIO TRACKING SYSTEM

**AND SERVICES LICENCE) REGULATIONS**

4th March, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 48 of 2001.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Electronic Communications Service (Modification of Radio Tracking System and Services Licence) Regulations.

## 2. Definitions.

In these regulations -

*Cap. 399.*

"Act" means the Electronic Communications (Regulation) Act, and includes the First Schedule thereto;

"the Instrument of Modification" means the Instrument of Modification of the original licence issued by the Minister under article 46 of the Act on the 4th March, 2001 in respect of the said original licence, which instrument was laid on the Table of the House on the 5th March, 2001;

"the original licence" means the Licence entitled a "Licence to establish and operate a real time fixed and mobile data location and monitoring system" issued on the 5th August, 1998 by the Electronic Communications Regulator in favour of the company Datatrak Systems Limited;

"the modified licence" means the original licence as modified by the Intrument of Modification.

## 3. Modification of original licence.

The terms and conditions of the original licence shall, as from the 4th March, 2001 and for the purpose of putting into effect the provisions of the Act and of implementing the liberalisation of the Maltese electronic communications market in accordance with the Act and of implementing a new commercial and regulatory framework as has been deemed appropriate for the purpose of implementing the provisions of the Act, be modified to the terms and conditions included in the modified licence and in accordance therewith.

## 4. Relationship with Instrument of Modification.

These regulations shall complement the Instrument of Modification and nothing in these regulations shall be interpreted as prejudicing the force, effect and validity of the Instrument of Modification.
