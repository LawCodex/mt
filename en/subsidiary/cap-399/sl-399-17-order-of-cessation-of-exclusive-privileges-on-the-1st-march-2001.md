# Order of Cessation of Exclusive Privileges on the 1st March, 2001 (S.L. 399.17)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 399.17**

**ORDER OF CESSATION OF EXCLUSIVE**

**PRIVILEGES ON THE 1st MARCH, 2001**

1st March, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 46 of 2001.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Order of Cessation of Exclusive Privileges on the 1st March, 2001.

## 2. Cessation of exclusive privileges. Cap. 399.

For the purposes of article 44(3) of the Electronic Communications (Regulation) Act, the 1st March, 2001 has been appointed as the day when the following exclusive privileges of running any electronic communications systems or of providing any electronic communications services as may have been granted in or under any law, licence, authorization, contract or other instrument shall cease to exist:

All exclusive privileges to operate electronic communications systems and to provide electronic communications services granted to the company Telepage Limited by virtue of the Licence Agreements of the 11th day of March of the year one thousand nine hundred and ninety four (1994) and of the 2nd day of September of the year one thousand nine hundred and ninety eight ( 1998) and of the Agreement of the 31st day of July of the year one thousand nine hundred and ninety eight ( 1998).
