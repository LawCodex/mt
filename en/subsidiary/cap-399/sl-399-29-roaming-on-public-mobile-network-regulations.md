# Roaming on Public Mobile Network Regulations (S.L. 399.29)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 399.29**

**ROAMING ON PUBLIC MOBILE NETWORK**

**REGULATIONS\***

2nd November, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 342 of 2007, as amended by Legal Notices 182 of 2010 and 266 of 2012.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Roaming on Public Mobile Network Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 182 of 2010; L.N. 266 of 2012.*

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"the Authority" means the Malta Communications Authority, and "the Roaming Regulation" means Regulation (EU) No 531/2012 of the European Parliament and Council of 13 June 2012 on roaming on public mobile communications networks within the Union.

## 3. Designation of the Authority.

The Authority shall be the competent national authority responsible for ensuring compliance with the requirements of, and for carrying out, the tasks under the Roaming Regulation.

## 4. Non-compliance and powers of the Authority. Cap. 418.

Any non-compliance with the provisions of the Roaming Regulation shall, for the purposes of the Malta Communications Authority Act, constitute a breach of a law enforced by the Authority and without prejudice to the provisions of these regulations, the provisions of article 4 and of articles 29 to 35 of the said Act shall apply to any such breach and in empowering the Authority to ensure compliance with the Roaming Regulation .

## 5. Authority may order immediate cessation of breach.

(1) If the Authority considers that a person has acted in breach of any of the provisions of the Roaming Regulation, the Authority may issue a compliance order requiring that person to cease and, or to desist from acting in breach of the Roaming Regulation.

(2) A compliance order issued under subregulation (1) shall come into force with immediate effect.

(3) If a person fails to comply with a compliance order issued under this regulation, the Authority may, in accordance with its powers under Part VII of the Malta Communications Authority Act, take any such enforcement action as it may consider appropriate to ensure compliance.

*Cap. 418.*

## 6. Applicability of the Malta Communications Authority Act.

*Amended by: L.N. 182 of 2010; L.N. 266 of 2012. Cap. 418.*

The provisions of articles 43, 44 and 44A of the Malta Communications Authority Act shall apply to disputes between undertakings providing electronic communications networks and, or services, and to disputes between an undertaking and a consumer or end-user insofar as any such disputes relate to any issue arising under the Roaming Regulation.

\*These regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 192 of 2022.

## 7. Publicity of measures taken to ensure compliance.

The Authority may, when taking any measure to ensure compliance with these regulations and, or with the Roaming Regulation, give such publicity as it may consider appropriate in the circumstances.
