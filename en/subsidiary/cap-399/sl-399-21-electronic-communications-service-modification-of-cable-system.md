# Electronic Communications Service (Modification of Cable System Agreement and Licences) Regulations (S.L. 399.21)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 399.21**

**ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS SERVICE**

**(MODIFICATION OF CABLE SYSTEM**

**AGREEMENT AND LICENCES) REGULATIONS**

4th March, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 50 of 2001.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Electronic Communications Service (Modification of Cable System Agreement and Licences) Regulations.

## 2. Definitions.

In these regulations -

*Cap. 399.*

"Act" means the Electronic Communications (Regulation) Act, and includes the First Schedule thereto;

"the Instrument of Modification" means the Instrument of Modification of the original licence issued by the Minister under article 46 of the Act on the 4th March, 2001 in respect of the said original licence, which instrument was laid on the Table of the House on the 5th March, 2001;

"the original licence" means the agreement concluded between the Government of Malta represented by Mr George W. Borg, Secretary General and Secretary to the Cabinet, and Melita Cable TV Limited represented by Mr. Gene Schneider, Chairman, United Holdings Inc., on the third (3rd) day of June of the year one thousand nine hundred and ninety one (1991) and the Licence entitled "Licence for Malta Cable TV System" granted to Melita Cable TV Ltd according to the terms of the said Agreement and included in "Attachment A" to the said Agreement and the Licence granted by the Electronic Communications Regulator with the concurrence of the Minister responsible for electronic communications under the Electronic Communications (Regulation) Act to Melita Cable TV p.l.c. on the 6th January, 1998 with effect from the 2nd January, 1998;

*Cap. 399.*

"the modified licence" means the original licence as modified by the Instrument of Modification.

## 3. Modification of original licence.

The terms and conditions of the original licence shall, as from the 4th March, 2001 and for the purpose of putting into effect the provisions of the Act and of implementing the liberalisation of the Maltese electronic communications market in accordance with the Act and of implementing a new commercial and regulatory framework as has been deemed appropriate for the propose of implementing the provisions of the Act, be modified to the terms and conditions included in the modified licence and in accordance therewith.

## 4. Relationdhip with Instrument of Modification.

These regulations shall complement the Instrument of Modification, and nothing in these regulations shall be interpreted as prejudicing the force, effect and validity of the Instrument of Modification.
