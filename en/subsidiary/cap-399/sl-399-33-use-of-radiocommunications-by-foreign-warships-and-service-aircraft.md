# Use of Radiocommunications by Foreign Warships and Service Aircraft Regulations (S.L. 399.33)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 399.33**

USE OF RADIOCOMMUNICATIONS BY FOREIGN

**WARSHIPS AND SERVICE AIRCRAFT**

**REGULATIONS**

15th October, 2010

LEGAL NOTICE 456 of 2010.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Use of Radiocommunications by Foreign Warships and Service Aircraft Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation. Cap. 399.

(1) Any reference in these regulations to "the Act" is a reference to the Electronic Communications (Regulation) Act, and to any regulations made thereunder, unless the context otherwise requires, and subject to the provisions of subregulation (2) the provisions of article 28 of the Act shall apply to these regulations.

(2) In these regulations and unless the context otherwise requires "the Authority" means the Malta Communications Authority.

## 3. Restrictions on use of radio-communications apparatus, by foreign warships or foreign aircraft accompanying such ships.

(1) No foreign warship or foreign service aircraft accompanying such ship shall, whilst in any harbour of Malta or within three miles of Malta without permission first obtained from the Authority, use its radiocommunications apparatus.

(2) The application for such permission shall give particulars of the system, including radio frequencies, modes of emission, bandwidths, times of transmission and transmission power which the applicant desires to use.

## 4. Restrictions on foreign service aircraft not accompanying foreign warships.

(1) No foreign service aircraft not accompanying a foreign warship shall, whilst in Malta or within any harbour of Malta, or within twelve nautical miles of Malta, without the like permission as set forth in the last preceding regulation from the Authority, use its radiocommunications or electronic communications apparatus.

(2) The application for such permission shall give the particulars set forth in regulation 3(2).

## 5. Conditions on which permission to use apparatus may be granted.

The Authority may, when an application is made to it in conformity with these regulations, attach to the grant of such permission conditions -

(a) providing against interference with any other station;

(b) requiring that transmission be forthwith discontinued on a request from the Authority or from any fixed shore station;

(c) forbidding protracted signalling by the use of apparatus transmitting other than pure continuous waves.
