# Electronic Communications (Regulation) Act (Exemption) Order (S.L. 399.38)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 399.38**

ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS (REGULATION) ACT (EXEMPTION) ORDER

24th March, 2005 LEGAL NOTICE 82 of 2005, as amended by Legal Notices 434 of 2010 and 53 of 2016.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Electronic Communications (Regulation) Act (Exemption) Order.

## 2. Exemption granted to the Armed Forces of Malta.

*Amended by: L.N. 434 of 2010; L.N. 53 of 2016. Cap. 49.*

The Armed Forces of Malta are hereby being exempted from the payment of any licensing fees due under the Electronic Communications (Regulation) Act with regard to radiocommunications apparatus used in relation to defence, public security or civil protection requirements.

## 3. Exemption granted to the Executive Police.

*Added by: L.N. 53 of 2016. Cap. 399.*

The Executive Police are hereby being exempted from the payment of any licensing fees due under the Electronic Communications (Regulation) Act, with regard to radiocommunications apparatus in relation to the exigencies of defence, public security or civil protection.

## 4. Exemption granted to the Civil Protection Department.

*Added by: L.N. 53 of 2016. Cap. 399.*

The Civil Protection Department are hereby exempted from the payment of any licensing fees due under the Electronic Communications (Regulation) Act with regard to radiocommunications apparatus in relation to the exigencies of defence, public security or civil protection.
