# Radiocommunications Unserviceable Equipment) Regulations (S.L. 399.41)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 399.41**

RADIOCOMMUNICATIONS (UNSERVICEABLE EQUIPMENT) REGULATIONS

3rd October, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 246 of 2008.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Radiocommunications (Unserviceable Equipment) Regulations.

## 2. Exemption from payment of licence fees.

The licencee of radiocommunications equipment which at any time has been disposed of may file a sworn declaration with the Minister responsible for radiocommunications, hereinafter "the Minister", confirming that the said equipment has been disposed of or is about to be disposed of, and such person shall, as from the day of the filing of such declaration with the Minister and subject to the provisions of Radiocommunications (Broadcast Receiving Licence Charges) Regulations, and to his paying any arrears that may be due, be exempt from the payment of any licence fees with regard to that apparatus which may have become due during the year during which the said declaration is filed and in any subsequent year.

*S.L. 399.31*

## 3. Declaration concerning other equipment.

(1) Where the sworn declaration made in terms of regulation 2 refers to television broadcast receiving equipment, the declaration shall also include a statement declaring whether the declarant has in his possession any such other equipment and the number thereof.

(2) Any declaration that does not comply with the provisions of sub-regulation (1) shall be null and void and shall be disregarded by the Minister.

## 4. Power of Minister to refuse declaration.

(1) The Minister may refuse to act upon any declaration made in terms of these regulations where he is satisfied that such declaration is untruthful.

(2) Where the Minister refuses to act upon a declaration in terms of sub-regulation (1), he shall give the declarant the reason for his decision.
