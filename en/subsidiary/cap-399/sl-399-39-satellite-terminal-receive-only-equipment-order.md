# Satellite Terminal Receive-Only Equipment Order (S.L. 399.39)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 399.39**

**SATELLITE TERMINAL RECEIVE-ONLY**

**EQUIPMENT ORDER**

1st May, 2004 LEGAL NOTICE 194 of 2005.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Satellite Terminal Receive- Only Equipment Order.

## 2. Exemption. Cap. 49.

Satellite terminal receive-only equipment shall be exempted from the requirements of article 32 of the Electric Communications (Regulation) Act.
