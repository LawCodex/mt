# Address Register and related Location (Geospatial) Registers Regulations (S.L. 546.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 546.04**

**ADDRESS REGISTER AND RELATED LOCATION**

**(GEOSPATIAL) REGISTERS REGULATIONS**

21st April, 2026\* LEGAL NOTICE 205 of 2025.

## 1. Citation, scope and commencement.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Address Register and related Location (Geospatial) Registers Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to establish a set of registers relating to the keeping and updating of an address of an existing or planned officially identified structure in Malta, as may be required in order to ensure consistent and coherent use of such data. These regulations further provide that such registers are to be used either in conjunction with each other or as individual registers to provide official records pertinent to that specific type of geospatial location.

(3) These regulations shall come into force on such date or dates as the Minister may, by notice in the Gazette establish, and different dates may be so established for different provisions and, or different purposes of these regulations.

## 3. Office of the Registrar.

(1) There shall be established the Office of the Address Registrar, which shall be under the management of the Registrar, whose functions shall be as defined in these regulations. (2) The Registrar shall be designated as the data owner in respect of the data as processed following its inclusion in the Register and subsidiary registers.

*   Cap. 595.*

(3) The Registrar shall be the public officer holding the office of Chief Electoral Commissioner in his capacity as Head of Department in the Electoral Office for the purposes of the Public Administration Act.

(a)\* the creation and maintenance of address records, ensuring the quality and accuracy of the address records within the Register and subsidiary registers in so far as the details may be ascertained or verified on the basis of other official registers and other official data sources as the Registrar may deem fit and as may be required from time to time;

## 4. The Register.

(1) There shall be established a Register, which shall contain therein the details of all addresses in Malta, and which shall be based on data derived from the Location Base Register in accordance with the First Schedule, which shall hold records containing the necessary information required to identify any type of land, sea or air parcel including geocoding and which shall be linked to the relevant geospatial record from the official base map of Malta, and the following subsidiary registers:

(a) the Buildings Register which shall hold records of all buildings together with their entry points;

(b) the Streets Register which shall hold records of all streets which are publicly or privately-owned and which serve as thoroughfares or any other type of passageway and which are assigned a name by the Registrar;

\*Not yet in force. †Not yet in force.

(c) the Localities Register which shall hold records of all the localities, communities and sub-localities which are identified on the official base map;

(d) the Post Codes Register which shall hold records that represent defined physical areas as established from time to time by the postal services provider as mandated by the authority responsible for the regulation of communications to provide postal services in Malta;

(e) the Regions Register which shall hold the list of officially recognized geographical regions which represent a sub-division of Malta, as may be identified and included by the Registrar; and

(f) the Countries Register which shall hold records of countries and territories:

Provided that the Registrar, may from time to time, after the publication of a notice in the Gazette, establish the data elements and data types which are mandatory for each register, add new data elements and data types to the Register and subsidiary registers, and establish other subsidiary registers made up of the relevant data elements:

Provided further that data elements may not be populated with the required content as from initial implementation of the Register and subsidiary registers.

(2)\* The subsidiary registers shall be an extension of the Location Base Register establishing the Address Register, and shall contain the data elements as provided under the First to the Eighth Schedules.

(3) The Registrar shall request, at his discretion, and receive from the entities concerned, without incurring any costs, the relevant and good quality data together with its metadata, in machine-readable format from the competent data sources. The entity concerned shall comply with this request within a reasonable time-frame as established by the Registrar.

(4) New address records or changes to existing address details may be created only by the processes as established by the Registrar.

## 5. Obligation to make available and give data to Registrar.

(1) The entities requested by the Registrar to provide data relevant to the Register and subsidiary registers shall make available and give the data requested by the Registrar and shall also have the responsibility to:

(a) maintain their records and, or data elements listed in the First to the Eighth Schedules and ensure that the information shared with the Registrar is good quality data, which reflects true and updated information;

(b) facilitate the machine to machine communication of information that shall be shared or re-used on the basis of these regulations; and

(c)\* indicate, when sharing data with the Registrar, the unique identifier of every entry included in the Register and in respect of which a record is required to be kept.

(2) The sharing of data is required to:

(a) establish and maintain the Register and Subsidiary Registers, for the implementation of these regulations;

(b) facilitate the re-use and exchange of address related data to:

(i) reduce the duplication and increase efficiency; and

(ii) facilitate and improve the correctness and quality of services being delivered;

(c) assist in the identification or correction of any erroneous data held by one or more of the data sources concerned to establish an address record.

## 6. Sharing of data pertaining to planning permits and building development.

(1) Data pertaining to planning permits and building development shall be made available by the responsible authorities, in the format as shall be established by the Registrar, from time to time.

(2) The data shall be good quality data and made available upon the issuance of the permits by the authorities responsible for planning and building development.

(3) The purpose of the sharing of this data is to establish the address record at the earliest instance to serve for all the other related processes and permits relating to the said building development.

(4) The data requested by the Registrar shall be shared by the entities concerned with the Registrar without any costs incurred by the Registrar, within reasonable time-frames as established by the Registrar.

\*Not yet in force.

## 7. Sharing of data pertaining to post codes.  Cap. 418.

(1) The postal services provider, as mandated by the authority responsible for the regulation of communications established by the Malta Communications Authority Act to maintain the national list of post codes for Malta, shall be required to participate and assist the Registrar in the maintenance of the address records, and to provide good quality data in the format as shall be established by the Registrar, from time to time.

(2) The data shall be made available immediately upon:

(a) any amendment to the list of post codes; or

(b) upon the creation of a new address by the Registrar.

(3) The purpose of the sharing of this data is to establish the link between a post code and a door number or a building entry point.

(4) The data requested by the Registrar shall be shared by the entities concerned with the Registrar without any costs incurred by the Registrar, within reasonable time-frames as established by the Registrar.

## 8. Sharing of other data relating to address.

(1) The Registrar may request the sharing of good quality data by any entity in possession of data relating to the address. The requested data shall be shared in the format as established by the Registrar from time to time.

(2) The purpose of sharing of this data is to maintain the Register.

## 15. Acting in good faith.

## First Schedule — Location Base Register

The Location Base Register shall include the following data elements:

Location URI – An automatically generated numeric value that is used as the persistent and unique identifier on which the remaining information contained in the Location record can be kept and retrieved.

Location Name – A short name with a meaning or description that enables the identification of the location.

Location Type – The selection of one of the values from a controlled list as may be established from time to time by the Registrar of permitted values which link the location record to one of the subsidiary registers as explained in the Address related subsidiary registers.

\*Not yet in force.

Location Geocoding – The necessary codes and values together with the referencing system used to store the longitude, latitude and altitude as may be applicable to represent point, line or area (polygon) information.

Country – The ISO 3166 code (Countries and Territories) to identify the Country within which the location is found.

Location Version Control Number – An automated system of version control audit trail distinct identifiers linked to the original identifier used to enable the identification of changes applied to the original record and its subsequent substitutions over a period of time with a start and an end date for each version record.

## Second Schedule — Address Register

The Address Register shall include the following data elements:

Address URI – The number automatically generated by the Location Base Register and which is also used to identify the address point record when the location type is an address point. The relevant spatial information shall be available to this register accordingly.

Address Type – The classification applied to the address point from a controlled list, as established by the Registrar.

Building Entry Point – The link between the address point and the building entry point when the address is part of a multi-address structure or a building with separate units.

Building – The building that is linked to the building entry point when such a link is applicable.

Door Number – The door number identification when such a door number exists or has been allocated.

Door/Property Name – The name or other identification as prescribed by the owner or the responsible entity through which the door can be uniquely identified within the building or the street or when in combination with the door number.

Floor Level – An indication of the altitude or the floor level where the entry point of the unit is located.

Street – The link between the address point and the street which is inherited from the building entry point when such details are part of the address point record or as maintained when the Address Point is not part of a building from the Building Register.

Post Code – The post code which is linked to the address point and which is either directly maintained or inherited from the building entry point record when such a link exists.

Locality – The link between an address point and a locality which is inherited from the building details when such a link exists or is directly maintained when the address point is not within the definition of a condominium.

Region – The link between an address point and the region which is derived from the Locality details as applicable to the Address Record.

Country – The name of the country from the Countries Register.

Address Version Control Number – An automated system of version control audit trail distinct identifiers linked to the original identifier used to enable the identification of changes applied to the original record and its subsequent replacements over a time period with a start-date and end-date for each version record.

## Third Schedule — Buildings Register

The Buildings Register shall include the following data elements:

Building URI – The number automatically generated by the Location Base Register and which is also used to identify the building when the Location Type is a Building. The relevant spatial information shall be available to this register accordingly.

Building Name – The name of the building as provided by the owner or entity responsible for the building which is within the definition of a condominium according to the Condominium Act. (Cap. 398).

Building Type – The classification applied to the building from a controlled list to determine the specific features to be expected, as established by the Registrar.

Locality – The link between the building and a specific locality from the Locality Register enabling the building to inherit all the relevant details pertinent to the locality to be added to the building details.

Building Version Control Number – An automated system of version control audit trail distinct identifiers linked to the original identifier used to enable the identification of changes applied to the original record and its subsequent replacements over a time period with a start-date and end-date for each version record.

Building Entry Point URI – The number automatically generated by the Location Base Register and which is also used to identify the building entry point when the location type is a building entry point. The relevant spatial information shall be available to this register accordingly.

Building Entry Point Name – The name of the building entry point as provided by the owner or entity responsible for the building which is within the definition of the Condominium Act. (Cap. 398.)

Building Entry Point Type – The type of entry point derived from a controlled list, as established by the Registrar.

Building – The link between the entry point and the building where there is more than one entry point for the same building which may also be from different streets.

Street – The link between the entry point and the street where such access exists.

Post Code – The link between the entry point and the applicable post code.

Building Entry Point Version Control Number – An automated system of version control audit trail distinct identifiers linked to the original identifier used to enable the identification of changes applied to the original record and its subsequent replacements over a time period with a start-date and end-date for each version record.

## Fourth Schedule — Streets Register

The Streets Register shall include the following data elements:

Street URI – The number automatically generated by the Location Base Register and which is also used to identify the street when the location type is a street. The relevant spatial information shall be available to this register accordingly.

Street Name – The name of the street as officially published in the Gazette, or any other temporary name as assigned by the Registrar from time to time.

Street Type – The classification applied to the street by selecting from a controlled list to enable the differentiation between public, private, arterial, local and any other criteria into which a street may be classified.

Locality – The link between the street and a parent locality and all the locality and region details together to become an integral part of the Streets Register.

Street Version Control Number – An automated system of version control audit trail distinct identifiers linked to the original identifier used to enable the identification of changes applied to the original record and its subsequent replacements over a time period with a start-date and end-date for each version record.

## Fifth Schedule — Localities Register

The Localities Register shall include the following data elements:

Locality URI – The number automatically generated by the Location Base Register and which is also used to identify the Locality when the Location Type is a Locality. The relevant spatial information shall be available to this register accordingly.

Locality Name – The name of the Locality and which in the case of localities in the Gozo region may also for ease of identification include the Region Name to which the locality is linked.

Locality Type – The applicable classification of the selected locality from a controlled list, and which enables the distinction between parent localities and sub-localities and other types of localities as may be decided from time to time.

Parent Locality – The link between a sub-locality and its parent locality where in the case of the major localities the parent locality shall be the same identifier of the locality.

Local Council – The link established between a locality and the local council, and which is derived from the official list of Local Councils as provided by the Local Government Act. (Cap. 363.)

Region – The link established between a Locality and the Region within which it is located and which shall be derived from the Regions Register with all the pertinent information that may be derived from the Regions Register as annexed to the Localities Register.

Locality Version Control Number – An automated system of version control audit trail distinct identifiers linked to the original identifier used to enable the identification of changes applied to the original record and its subsequent replacements over a time period with a start-date and end-date for each version record.

## Sixth Schedule — Post Codes Register

The Post Codes Register shall include the following data elements:

Post Code URI – The number automatically generated by the Location Base Register and which is also used to identify the Post Code when the Location Type is a Post Code. The relevant spatial information shall be available to this register accordingly.

Post Code – The combination of locality, street and specific part of the street identifiers that make up the post code as maintained by postal services provider as authorised by the authority responsible for the regulation of communications.

Street – The details of the street, or part thereof, within which the post code is assigned and consequently all the information relating to locality and region can be amalgamated to the post code.

Post Code Version Control Number – An automated system of version control audit trail distinct identifiers linked to the original identifier used to enable the identification of changes applied to the original record and its subsequent replacements over a time period with a start-date and end-date for each version record.

## Seventh Schedule — Regions Register

The Regions Register shall include the following data elements:

Region URI – The number automatically generated by the Location Base Register and which is also used to identify the region when the location type is a region. The relevant spatial information shall be available to this register accordingly.

Region Name – The name of the Region.

Region Type – The selected classification of region which shall be derived from a controlled list of different regions and which shall be linked to officially recognised boundaries.

Country – The link established between a region and a Country in accordance with the relevant Country Register and which in most cases shall be Malta.

Region Version Control Number – An automated system of version control audit trail distinct identifiers linked to the original identifier used to enable the identification of changes applied to the original record and its subsequent replacements over a time period with a start-date and end-date for each version record.

## Eighth Schedule — Countries Register

The Countries Register shall include the following data elements:

Country URI – The number automatically generated by the Location Base Register and which is also used to identify the Country when the Location Type is a Country. The relevant spatial information shall be available to this register accordingly.

Country Name – The name of the country or territory as provided by the ISO-3166 official list.

**Alpha-2 Code – As provided by the ISO-3166 official list.**

**Alpha-3 Code – As provided by the ISO-3166 official list.**

**Numeric Code – As provided by the ISO 3166 official list.**

Country Version Control Number – An automated system of version control audit trail distinct identifiers linked to the original identifier used to enable the identification of changes applied to the original record and its subsequent replacements over a time period with a start-date and end-date for each version record.
