# Promotion of Gender Balance among Directors of Listed Companies Regulations (S.L. 456.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 456.04**

**PROMOTION OF GENDER BALANCE AMONG**

**DIRECTORS OF LISTED COMPANIES**

**REGULATIONS**

28th December, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 376 of 2024.

## 1. Citation, commencement and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Promotion of Gender Balance among Directors of Listed Companies Regulations.

(2) These regulations shall come into force on such date as the Minister responsible for equality, may by notice in the Gazette establish and different dates may be so established for different provisions and, or purposes of these regulations.

(3) The scope of these regulations is to transpose Article 10 of the Directive (EU) 2022/2381 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 November 2022 on improving the gender balance among directors of listed companies and related measures.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 456.*

"Act" means the Equality for Men and Women Act;

* Cap. 330.*

"Authority" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in article 2 of the Malta Financial Services Authority Act;

"Commission" means the National Commission for the Promotion of Equality for Men and Women appointed in accordance with article 11 of the Act and includes any officer of the Commission duly authorised by it to act on its behalf for any specific purpose under the Act;

"Directive" means Directive (EU) 2022/2381 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 November 2022 on improving the gender balance among directors of listed companies and related measures;

* Cap. 345.*

"listed company" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in article 2(1) of the Financial Markets Act.

## 3. Promotion of gender balance in listed companies.

(1) The Commission shall promote, analyse, monitor and support gender balance on boards of listed companies in order to compile a yearly report on the implementation of the Directive.

(2) The Commission shall, in order to carry out its functions in accordance with this regulation, insofar as required, be provided by the Authority with information about the gender representation on the boards of listed companies and with information regarding the measures taken with a view to achieving the applicable objectives for the purposes of gender balance on boards of listed companies, in accordance with the Directive.
