# Merchant Shipping Forms Regulations (S.L. 234.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 234.04**

**MERCHANT SHIPPING FORMS REGULATIONS**

1st June, 1973

LEGAL NOTICE 59 of 1973.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Merchant Shipping Forms Regulations.

## 2. Forms. Cap. 234.

The forms referred to in the Second Schedule to the Merchant Shipping Act shall be the forms set out in the Schedule to these regulations.
