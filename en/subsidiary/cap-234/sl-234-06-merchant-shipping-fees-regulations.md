# Merchant Shipping Fees Regulations (S.L. 234.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 234.06**

**MERCHANT SHIPPING FEES REGULATIONS**

8th June, 1973

LEGAL NOTICE 64 of 1973, as amended by Legal Notices 151 of 1977, 128 of 1992, 279 and 341 of 2002, 76 of 2003, 411 of 2007 and 41 of 2009;

Act XV of 2009; and Legal Notices 169 of 2011, 220 of 2015, 479 of 2020 and 211 of 2025.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Merchant Shipping Fees Regulations.

## 2. Fees for services, etc.

*Substituted by: L.N. 128 of 1992.*

The fees payable for the services and certificates specified in the Schedule shall be the fee specified in relation to each such service or certificate in that Schedule:

*Amended by: L.N. 279 of 2002.*

Provided that the Minister may, under such conditions as deemed appropriate, exempt any person or class of persons, or any ship or class of ships, from all or part of the fees payable in terms of these regulations.
