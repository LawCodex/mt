# Load Lines Convention Order (S.L. 234.13)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 234.13**

**LOAD LINES CONVENTION ORDER**

17th December, 1974

LEGAL NOTICES 132 of 1974, 51 of 1976, 120 of 1977, 30 of 1979 and 39 of 1986.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Load Lines Convention Order. 2. It is hereby declared that the Governments of the countries specified in the First Schedule have accepted or acceded to the International Convention on Load Lines 1966 on the date shown against each country.

*Declaration of Convention countries.*

## 3. Extention of Convention to other countries and territories.

It is hereby declared that the International Convention on Load Lines 1966 has been extended to the territories specified in the Second Schedule.
