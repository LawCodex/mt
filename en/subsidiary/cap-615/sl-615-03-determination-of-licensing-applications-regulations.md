# Determination of Licensing Applications Regulations (S.L. 615.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 615.03**

**DETERMINATION OF LICENSING**

**APPLICATIONS REGULATIONS\***

24th December, 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 498 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Determination of Licensing Applications Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:-

*Cap. 615.*

"Act" means the Real Estate Agents, Property Brokers and Property Consultants Act;

"Board" means the Licensing Board established under Part V of the Act;

"licence" means a licence issued by the Board to exercise the activity of a property broker, or real estate agent or to act as a branch manager or property consultant.

## 3. Continuance in the exercise of the trade or profession in real estate until processing of application.

Any person, exercising his trade or profession in real estate and to whom the provisions of the Act apply, who submitted an application for a licence in its completed form to the Board by the 31st December, 2021, shall not be withheld from continuing in the exercise of his trade or profession during the period in which his application is being processed and determined, unless the Board directs otherwise when the circumstances so require.

\*These Regulations have been transposed to Cap. 644, vide S.L. 644.02 and Act XXVII of 2024
