# Electronic Invoicing in Public Procurement Regulations (S.L. 601.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 601.10**

**ELECTRONIC INVOICING IN PUBLIC**

**PROCUREMENT REGULATIONS**

27th November, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 404 of 2018.

## 1. Title.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Electronic Invoicing in Public Procurement Regulations.

(2) These regulations implement certain provisions of Directive 2014/55/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16th April 2014 on electronic invoicing in public procurement.

(3) By way of derogation from sub-regulation (3), contracting authorities and contracting entities shall, by not later than 18th April 2019, adopt, publish and apply the provisions necessary to comply with the obligation contained in regulation 5 to receive and process electronic invoices.

(4) By way of derogation from sub-regulation (3), sub-central contracting authorities and contracting entities shall, by not later than the 18th April 2020, adopt, publish and apply the provisions necessary to comply with the obligation contained in regulation 5 to receive and process electronic invoices.

(5) Where Malta considers that any future update or revision of the European standard on electronic invoicing made by the European Commission does not entirely satisfy the requirements set out in Article 3(1) of Directive 2014/55/EU, it shall inform the European Commission thereof with a detailed explanation.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"contracting authorities" means the State, regional or local authorities, bodies governed by public law or associations formed by one or more such authorities or one or more such bodies governed by public law, but for the purpose of the Concession Contract Regulations only ‘contracting authorities’ shall have the meaning assigned to it in regulation 2 of those regulations;

*S.L. 601.09.*

"contracting entities" means contracting entities as defined in regulation 2 of the Public Procurement of Contracting Authorities or Entities in the fields of Defence and Security Regulations, sub-regulation (2) of regulation 2 of the Concession Contract Regulations and sub-regulation (2) of regulation 2 of the Public Procurement of Entities operating in the Water, Energy, Transport and Postal Services Sectors Regulations;

*S.L. 601.07.*

*S.L. 601.09.*

*S.L 601.05.*

"core elements of an electronic invoice" means a set of essential information components which an electronic invoice must contain in order to enable cross-border interoperability, including the necessary information to ensure legal compliance;

"electronic invoice" means an invoice that has been issued, transmitted and received in a structured electronic format which allows for its automatic and electronic processing;

"European standard on electronic invoicing" means the European standard on electronic invoicing EN 16931-1:2017 for the semantic data model of the core elements of an electronic invoice established by the European Committee for Standardization (CEN) and published through the European Commission’s Implementing Decision (EU) 2017/1870 of 16 October 2017;

"semantic data model" means a structured and logically interrelated set of terms and their meanings that specify the core elements of an electronic invoice;

"sub-central contracting authorities" means sub-central contracting authorities as defined in point (3) of Article 2(1) of Directive 2014/24/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26th February 2014 on public procurement and repealing Directive 20014/18/EC.

## 3. Scope.

(1) These regulations shall apply to electronic invoices issued as a result of the performance of contracts to which the Public Procurement of Contracting Authorities or Entities in the fields of Defence and Security Regulations, the Concession Contract Regulations, the Public Procurement Regulations or the Public Procurement of Entities operating in the Water, Energy, Transport and Postal Services Sectors Regulations apply and which are above the threshold for inclusion in the Official Journal of the European Union.

*S.L. 601.07.*

*S.L. 601.09.*

*S.L. 601.03.*

*S.L. 601.05.*

(2) These Regulations shall not apply to electronic invoices issued as a result of the performance of contracts falling within the scope of the Public Procurement of Contracting Authorities or Entities in the fields of Defence and Security Regulations, where the procurement and performance of the contract are declared to be secret or must be accompanied by special security measures in accordance with the laws, regulations or administrative provisions in force in Malta, and provided it has been determined that the essential interests concerned cannot be guaranteed by less intrusive measures.

*S.L. 601.07.*

## 4. Core elements of an electronic invoice.

The core elements of an electronic invoice are, inter alia:

(a) process and invoice identifiers;

(b) the invoice period;

(c) seller information;

(d) buyer information;

(e) payee information;

(f) seller's tax representative information;

(g) contract reference;

(h) delivery details;

(i) payment instructions;

(j) allowance or charge information;

(k) invoice line item information;

(l) invoice totals;

(m) VAT breakdown.

## 5. Receipt and processing of electronic invoices.

(1) Contracting authorities and contracting entities shall receive and process electronic invoices which comply with the European standard on electronic invoicing.

(2) These regulations are without prejudice to the right of the sender of the invoice to choose between submitting the invoice in accordance with the European standard on electronic invoicing or in paper format.

## 6. Data protection.

(1) These regulations are without prejudice to applicable European Union and national law on data protection.

(2) Subject to any provisions to the contrary in European Union or national law and without prejudice to the restrictions set out in Article 23 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016, article 5 of the Data Protection Act and any regulations made thereunder, personal data obtained for the purpose of electronic invoicing may be used only for that purpose or for purposes compatible with it.

*Cap. 586.*

(3) Without prejudice to the exemptions and restrictions set out in Article 23 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016, article 5 of the Data Protection Act and any regulations made thereunder, arrangements for the publication, for transparency and accounting purposes, of personal data gathered in connection with electronic invoicing shall be consistent with the purpose of such publication and with the principle of the protection of privacy.

*Cap. 586.*

## 7. Provisions of the Value

*Added Tax Act. Cap. 406.*

These Regulations are without prejudice to the provisions of the Value Added Tax Act.
