# Cleaner and More Energy-Efficient Road Transport Vehicles Regulations (S.L. 601.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 601.06**

**CLEANER AND MORE ENERGY-EFFICIENT**

**ROAD TRANSPORT VEHICLES REGULATIONS**

4th December, 2010

LEGAL NOTICE 175 of 2011, as amended by Legal Notices 75 of 2022 and 93 of 2026.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Cleaner and More Energy-Efficient Road Transport Vehicles Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Substituted by: L.N. 75 of 2022. Amended by: L.N. 93 of 2026.*

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"clean vehicle" means:

(a) a vehicle of category M1, M2 or N1 with a maximum tail-pipe emission expressed in CO2 g/km and real driving pollutant emissions below a percentage of the applicable emission limits as laid down in Table 2 of the Schedule; or

*S.L. 460.32.*

(b) a vehicle of category M3, N2 or N3 using alternative fuels as defined in regulation 2(1) of the Alternative Fuels Order, excluding fuels produced from high indirect land-use change-rise feed stock for which a significant expansion of the production area into land with high-carbon stock is observed in accordance with regulation 15 of the Promotion of Energy from Renewable Sources Regulations. In the case of vehicles using liquid biofuels, synthetic and paraffinic fuels, those fuels shall not be blended with convention fossil fuels;

*S.L. 545.43.*

*S.L. 601.03. S.L. 601.05.*

"contracting authorities" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in regulation 2 of the Public Procurement Regulations and regulation 2(1) of the Public Procurement of Entities Operating in the Water, Energy, Transport and Postal Services Sectors Regulations;

*S.L. 601.05.*

"contracting entities" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in regulation 2(2) of the Public Procurement of Entities operating in the Water, Energy, Transport and Postal Services Sectors Regulations;

"road transport vehicle" means a vehicle in the vehicle categories listed in Table 3 of the Schedule;

"zero-emission heavy duty vehicle" means a clean vehicle as defined in point (b) without an internal combustion engine, or with an internal combustion engine that emits less than 1g CO2/kWh as measured in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 595/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council and its implementing measures, or that emits less than 1g CO2/km as measures in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 715/2007 of the European Parliament and of the Council and its implementing measures.

## 3. Scope and applicability.

*Substituted by: L.N. 75 of 2022.*

(1) These regulations transpose Directive (EU) 2019/ 1161 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019 amending Directive 2009/33/EC on the promotion of clean and energy-efficient road transport vehicles.

(2) These regulations aim to ensure that contracting authorities and contracting entities take into account lifetime energy and environmental impacts, including energy consumption and emissions of CO2 and of certain pollutants, when procuring certain road transport vehicles with the objectives of promoting and stimulating the market for clean and energy-efficient vehicles and of improving the contribution of the transport sector to the environment, climate and energy policies of the European Union.

(3) These regulations do not apply to the vehicles referred to in point (d) of Article 2(2) and in points (a) and (b) of Article 2(3) of Regulation (EU) 2018/858 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 2018 on the approval and market surveillance of motor vehicles and their trailers, and of systems, components and separate technical units intended for such vehicles, amending Regulations (EC) No 715/2007 and (EC) No 595/2009 and repealing Directive 2007/46/EC.

## 6. Minimum procurement targets.

*Added by: L.N. 75 of 2022.*

(1) The procurement of vehicles and services referred to in regulation 4 complies with the minimum procurement targets for clean light-duty vehicles set out in Table 3 of the Schedule and for clean heavy-duty vehicles set out in Table 4 of the Schedule. Those targets are expressed as minimum percentages of clean vehicles in the total number of road transport vehicles covered by the aggregate of all contracts referred to in regulation 4, awarded between 2nd August 2021 and 31st December 2025, for the first reference period, and between 1st January 2026 and 31st December 2030, for the second reference period.

(2) For the purpose of calculating the minimum procurement targets, the date of the public procurement to be taken into account is the date of completion of the public procurement procedure, by way of awarding of the contract.

(3) Vehicles that meet the definition of clean vehicle or of zero-emission heavy-duty vehicle under regulation 2 as a result of retrofitting may be counted as clean vehicles or zero-emission heavy-duty vehicles, respectively, for the purpose of compliance with the minimum procurement targets.

(4) In the case of contracts referred to in paragraph (a) of regulation 4, the number of road transport vehicles purchased, leased, rented or hire-purchased under each contract shall be taken into account for the purpose of assessing compliance with the minimum procurement targets.

(5) In the case of contracts referred to in paragraphs (b) and (c) of regulation 4, the number of road transport vehicles to be used for the provision of the services covered by each contract shall be taken into account of the purpose of assessing compliance with the minimum procurement targets.

(6) Where new targets for the period after 1st January 2030 are not adopted, the targets set for the second reference period shall continue to apply, and shall be calculated in accordance with sub-regulations (1) to (5), over subsequent five (5) year periods.
