# Auditor General (Revision of Salary) Order (S.L. 601.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 601.02**

**AUDITOR GENERAL (REVISION OF SALARY)**

**ORDER**

1st January, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 67 of 1998, as amended by Legal Notice 410 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Auditor General (Revision of Salary) Order.

## 2. Revision of Auditor General’s salary.

*Amended by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

There shall be paid to the Auditor General the salary of €22,108.08 per annum as from the 1st January, 1998.
