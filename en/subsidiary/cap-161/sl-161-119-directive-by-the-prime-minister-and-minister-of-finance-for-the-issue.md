## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Prime Minister and Minister of Finance for the Issue of €100,000,000:

(i) 5.1% Malta Government Stock 2014(III) Fungibility Issue; or

(ii) 5% Malta Government Stock 2021(I) Fungibility Issue; or of

(iii) any combination of the above two Stocks provided the aggregate nominal value shall not exceed Euro 100,000,000.

## 2. Mode of issuing Stocks.

These Stocks shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment Policy.

All persons and, or entities are eligible to apply for these Stocks. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

(a) In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when these Stocks have been allotted, an application shall be made for the respective Stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

(b) On the 7th July 2008, after the payment of the first dividend, any amount allotted under 5.1% Malta Government Stock 2014 (III) will be integrated with the existing issue of this Stock and shall thereafter both be deemed to be one Stock.

(c) On the 9th August 2008, after the payment of the first dividend, any amount allotted under 5% Malta Government Stock 2021 (I) will be integrated with the existing issue of this Stock and shall thereafter both be deemed to be one Stock.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

The sum of money to be raised by either Stock, or of any combination of the two Stocks shall in the aggregate be Euro 100,000,000. The Accountant General reserves the right to increase the sum of money to be raised by a maximum of Euro 50,000,000.

*Rate of interest and dates of payment.*

(a) Interest at the rate of Euro 2.55 for every Euro 100 5.1% Malta Government Stock 2014 (III) will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 6th day of Janaury and the 6th day of July in every year for the duration of the Stock. (b) Interest at the rate of Euro 2.50 for every Euro 100 5% Malta Government Stock 2021 (I) will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 8th day of February and the 8th day of August in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first pro-rata payment covering the period from the 4th day of June, 2008 to the 7th day of August, 2008 (both dates included) shall be made on the 8th day of August, 2008.

*\7.*

*Redemption of Stocks.*

(a) The 5.1% Malta Government Stock 2014(III) shall be redeemed at par on the 6th July, 2014.
