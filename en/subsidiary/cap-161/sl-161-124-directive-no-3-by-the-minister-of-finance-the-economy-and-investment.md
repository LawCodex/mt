# Directive (No. 3) by the Minister of Finance, the Economy and Investment for the Issue of Euro 100,000,000 Malta Government Stocks (S.L. 161.124)

*1*

MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCKS [S.L.161.124

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 161.124**

**DIRECTIVE (No. 3) BY THE MINISTER OF**

**FINANCE, THE ECONOMY AND INVESTMENT**

**FOR THE ISSUE OF EURO 100,000,000 MALTA**

**GOVERNMENT STOCKS**

26th October, 2010

LEGAL NOTICE 465 of 2010.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive (No. 3) by the Minister of Finance, the Economy and Investment for the Issue of Euro 100,000,000 Malta Government Stocks.

## 2. Issuing of Stock.

An amount of Euro 100,000,000 Malta Government Stocks shall be issued as follows:

(a) 3.75% Malta Government Stock 2015 (VI) Fungibility issue, and

(b) 5.25% Malta Government Stock 2030 (I) Fungibility issue, or

(c) any combination of the above two Stocks which shall in the aggregate be Euro 100,000,000.

## 3. Mode of issuing Stocks.

The Stocks shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Allotment policy.

All persons and, or entities are eligible to apply for these Stocks. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 5. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

(1) In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when these Stocks have been allotted, an application shall be made for the respective Stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

(2) On the 3rd June, 2011 after the payment of the first interest, any amount allotted under 3.75% Malta Government Stock 2015 (VI) FI shall be integrated with the existing issue of this Stock and shall thereafter both be deemed to be one Stock.

(3) On the 23rd December, 2010 after the payment of the first interest, any amount allotted under 5.25% Malta Government Stock 2030 (I) FI shall be integrated with the existing issue of this Stock and shall thereafter both be deemed to be one Stock.

## 6. Sum of money to be raised.

The sum of money to be raised by either Stock, or of any combination of the two Stocks shall in the aggregate be Euro 100,000,000.

## 7. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

(1) Interest at the rate of Euro 1.875 for every Euro 100 in respect of the 3.75% Malta Government Stock 2015 (VI) shall be payable half-yearly in arrears on the 3rd day of June and the 3rd day of December in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first pro rata interest payment covering the period from the 17th day of November, 2010 to the 2nd day of June, 2011 (both dates [S.L.161.124 MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCKS

inclusive), shall be made on the 3rd day of June, 2011.

(2) Interest at the rate of Euro 2.625 for every Euro 100 in respect of the 5.25% Malta Government Stock 2030 (I) shall be payable half-yearly in arrears on the 23rd day of June and the 23rd day of December in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first pro rata interest payment covering the period from the 17th day of November, 2010 to the 22nd day of December, 2010 (both dates inclusive) shall be made on the 23rd day of December, 2010.

## 8. Redemption of Stock.

(1) The 3.75% Malta Government Stock 2015 (VI) shall be redeemed at par on the 3rd December, 2015.

(2) The 5.25% Malta Government Stock 2030 (I) shall be redeemed at par on the 23rd June, 2030.
