# Directive by the Prime Minister and Minister of Finance for the Issue of €34,940,601 4.8% Malta Government Stock 2016 (Second Issue) (S.L. 161.72)

ISSUE OF €34,940,601 4.8% MALTA

*1*

GOVERNMENT STOCK 2016 (SECOND ISSUE) [S.L.161.72

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 161.72**

**DIRECTIVE BY THE PRIME MINISTER AND**

**MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF**

**€34,940,601 4.8% MALTA GOVERNMENT**

**STOCK 2016 (SECOND ISSUE)**

17th March, 2006

LEGAL NOTICE 63 of 2006, as amended by Legal Notice 410 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Substituted by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Prime Minister and Minister of Finance for the Issue of €34,940,601 4.8% Malta Government Stock 2016 (Second Issue).

## 2. Mode of issuing Stock.

The Stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and, or entities are eligible to apply for this Stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

(a) In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when the Stock has been allotted, an application shall be made for the Stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

(b) On the 27th May 2006, after the payment of the first dividend, this stock will be integrated with the existing issue 4.8% Malta Government Stock 2016 (II) and shall thereafter both be deemed to be one Stock.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the Stock shall be €34,940,601. The Accountant General reserves the right to increase the sum of money to be raised by a maximum of €23,293,734.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €2.40 for every €100 Stock will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 26th day of May and the 26th day of November in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first pro-rata payment covering the period from the 28th day of March, 2006 to the 26th day of May, 2006 shall be made on the 26th day of May, 2006.

## 7. Redemption of Stock.

The Stock shall be redeemed at par on the 26th November, 2016.
