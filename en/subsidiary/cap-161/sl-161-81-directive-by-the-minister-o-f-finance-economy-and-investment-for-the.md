# Directive by the Minister o f Finance, Economy and Investment for the issue of €100,000,000 (S.L. 161.81)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 161.81**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE,

**ECONOMY AND INVESTMENT**

**FOR THE ISSUE OF €100,000,000**

**(I) 3.6% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2021**

**(FOURTH ISSUE); OR (II) 5.2% MALTA**

GOVERNMENT STOCK 2020 (FIRST ISSUE);

**OR OF (III) ANY COMBINATION OF THE**

**ABOVE TWO STOCKS**

16th June, 2009

LEGAL NOTICE 192 of 2009.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Directive is the Directive by the Minister of Finance, Economy and Investment for the issue of €100,000,000:

(i) 3.6% Malta Government Stock 2013 (IV) (FOURTH ISSUE) or;

(ii) 5.2% Malta Government Stock 2020 (I) (FIRST ISSUE) (Fungibility Issue) or of;

(iii) any combination of the above two Stocks which shall in the aggregate be Euro 100,000,000.

## 2. Mode of issuing Stocks.

These Stocks shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and, or entities are eligible to apply for these Stocks. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

(a) In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when these Stocks have been allotted, an application shall be made for the respective Stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

(b) On the 18th October 2009, after the payment of the first dividend, any amount allotted under 3.6% Malta Government Stock 2013 (IV) will be integrated with the existing issue of this Stock and shall thereafter both be deemed to be one Stock.

(c) On the 10th of December 2009, after the payment of the first dividend, any amount allotted under 5.2% Malta Government Stock 2020 (I) will be integrated with the existing issue of this Stock and shall thereafter both be deemed to be one Stock.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

The sum of money to be raised by either Stock, or of any combination of the two Stocks shall in the aggregate be Euro 100,000,000. The Accountant General reserves the right to increase the sum of money to be raised by a maximum of Euro 20,000,000.

*\6.*

*Rate of interest and dates of payment.*

(a) Interest at the rate of Euro 1.80 for every Euro 100, 3.6% Malta Government Stock 2013 (IV) will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 18th day of April and the 18th day of October in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first pro-rata payment covering the period from the 1st July, 2009 to the 17th day of October, 2009 (both dates inclusive) shall be made on the 18th day of October, 2009.

(b) Interest at the rate of Euro 2.60 for every Euro 100 5.2% Malta Government Stock 2020 (I) will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 10th day of June and the 10th day of December in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first pro-rata payment covering the period from the 1st July, 2009 to the 9th day of December, 2009 (both dates inclusive) shall be made on the 10th day of December, 2009.

*\7.*

*Redemption of Stocks.*

(a) The 3.6% Malta Government Stock 2013(IV) shall be redeemed at par on the 18th October, 2013.

(b) The 5.2% Malta Government Stock 2020 (I) shall be redeemed at par on the 10th June, 2020.
