# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an amount not exceeding €78,810,156.07 5.5% Malta Government Stock 2023 (First Issue) (S.L. 161.104)

ISSUE OF AN AMOUNT NOT EXCEEDING €78,810,156.07

5.5% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2023

*1*

(FIRST ISSUE) [S.L.161.104

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 161.104**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN AMOUNT NOT EXCEEDING €78,810,156.07 5.5% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2023 (FIRST ISSUE)

27th June, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 142 of 2003, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is the Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an amount not exceeding €78,810,156.07 5.5% Malta Government Stock 2023 (First Issue).

## 2. Allottment policy.

This Stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Mode of issuing Stock.

All persons and, or entities are eligible to apply for this Stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

In terms of the Malta Stock Exchange Act, when the Stock has been allotted, an application shall be made for the Stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the Stock shall not exceed €78,810,156.0 7.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €2.75,0 for every €100 Stock will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 6th day of January and on the 6th day of July in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first pro-rata payment covering the period from the 15th day of July, 2003 to the 6th day of January, 2004 shall be made on the 6th day of January, 2004.

## 7. Redemption of Stock.

The Stock shall be redeemed at par on the 6th July, 2014.
