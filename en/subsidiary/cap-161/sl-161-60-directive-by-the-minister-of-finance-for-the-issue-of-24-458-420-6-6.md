# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of €24,458,420 6.6% Malta Government Stock 2014 (S.L. 161.60)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 161.60**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF €24,458,420.68 6.6% MALTA

**GOVERNMENT STOCK 2014\***

7th September, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 167 of 2000, as amended by Legal Notice 410 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Substituted by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of €24,458,420.68 6.6% Malta Government Stock 2014.

## 2. Mode of issuing stock.

This stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and/or entities are eligible to apply for this stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on an exchange. Cap. 345.

An application for the stock to be put on the official list and for trading on a recognised stock exchange will be made in terms of the Financial Markets Act, when the stock has been allotted.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the stock shall be €24,458,420.68.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €3.30 foe every €100 stock will be payable half-yearly in arrears on the 30th day of March and on the 30th day of September in every year for the duration of the stock. The first payment covering the period from the 1st day of October, 2000 to the 30th day of March, 2001 shall be made on the 30th day of March, 2001.

## 7. Redemption of stock.

The stock shall be redeemed at par on such date between the 1st January, 2014 and 31st December, 2014 (both dates inclusive) as shall be determined by the Accountant General and notified in the Gazette.

\* This Directive has been revoked by Act XLV of 2016, but it is still partially applicable since the six-year period prescribed by law to claim funds referring to stocks that have already matured, has not yet expired.
