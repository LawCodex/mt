# Directive by the Prime Minister and Minister of Finance for the Issue of €34,940,600.98 5% Malta Government Stock 2021 Fungibility Issue (S.L. 161.108)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 161.108**

**DIRECTIVE BY THE PRIME MINISTER AND**

**MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE**

**OF €34,940,600.98 5% MALTA GOVERNMENT**

**STOCK 2021**

10th May, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 134 of 2005, as amended by Legal Notice 427 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Prime Minister and Minister of Finance for the Issue of €34,940,600.98 5% Malta Government Stock 2021 Fungibility Issue.

## 2. Mode of issuing Stock.

This Stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and, or entities are eligible to apply for this Stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

(a) In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when the Stock has been allotted, an application shall be made for the Stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

(b) On the 9th August, 2005 after the payment of the first dividend, this Stock will be integrated with the existing issue 5% Malta Government Stock 2021 and shall thereafter both be deemed to be one Stock.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the Stock shall be Lm15,000,000. The Accountant General reserves the right to increase the sum of money to be raised by a maximum of €34,940,600.98.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 427 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €2.50,0 for every €100 Stock will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 8th day of February and on the 8th day of August in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first pro rata payment covering the period from the 25th day of May, 2005 to the 8th day of August, 2005 shall be made on the 8th day of August, 2005.

## 7. Redemption of Stock.

The Stock shall be redeemed at par on the 8th August, 2021.
