# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of €69,881,201.96 6.1% Malta Government Stock 2015 (First Issue) (S.L. 161.58)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 161.58**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF €69,881,201.96 6.1% MALTA

**GOVERNMENT STOCK 2015 (FIRST ISSUE)\***

12th May, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 87 of 2000, as amended by Legal Notice 410 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Substituted by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of €69,881,201.96 6.1% Malta Government Stock 2015 (First Issue).

## 2. Mode of issuing stock.

This stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and/or entities are eligible to apply for this stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on an exchange. Cap. 345.

An application for the stock to be put on the official list and for trading on a recognised stock exchange will be made in terms of the Financial Markets Act, when the stock has been allotted.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the stock shall be €69,881,201.96.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €3.05 for every €100 stock will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 10th day of June and on the 10th day of December in every year for the duration of the stock. The first payment covering the period from the 11th day of June, 2000 to the 10th day of December, 2000 shall be made on the 10th day of December, 2000.

## 7. Redemption of stock.

The stock shall be redeemed at par on the 10th June, 2015.

\* This Directive has been revoked by Act XLV of 2016, but it is still partially applicable since the six-year period prescribed by law to claim funds referring to stocks that have already matured, has not yet expired.
