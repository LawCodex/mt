# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of €47,053.342.65 5.9% Malta Government Stock 2015 (Second Issue) (S.L. 161.63)

ISSUE OF €47,053,342.65 5.9%

*1*

MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2015 (SECOND ISSUE) [S.L.161.63

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 161.63**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF €47,053,342.65 5.9% MALTA

**GOVERNMENT STOCK 2015 (SECOND ISSUE)\***

18th March, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 68 of 2002, as amended by Legal Notice 410 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Substituted by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of €47,053,342.65 5.9% Malta Government Stock 2015 (Second Issue).

## 2. Mode of issuing stock.

This stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and/or entities are eligible to apply for this stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on an exchange. Cap. 345.

In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when the stock has been allotted, an application will be made for the stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on a recognised stock exchange.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the stock is €47,053,342.65.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €2.95 for every €100 stock will be payable half-yearly in arrears on the 9th day of April and on the 9th day of October in every year for the duration of the stock. The first payment covering the period from the 10th day of April, 2002 to the 9th day of October, 2002 shall be made on the 9th day of October, 2002.

## 7. Redemption of stock.

The stock shall be redeemed at par on the 9th October, 2015.

\* This Directive has been revoked by Act XLV of 2016, but it is still partially applicable since the six-year period prescribed by law to claim funds referring to stocks that have already matured, has not yet expired.
