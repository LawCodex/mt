# Directive by the Minister of Finance, the Economy and Investment for the Conversion of Euro 200,000,000 out of the Euro 428,885,7035. 70% Malta Government Stock 2012 (III) into Euro 200,000,000 4.30% Malta Government Stock 2016 (IV) (S.L. 161.89)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 161.89**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE, THE ECONOMY AND INVESTMENT FOR THE

CONVERSION OF EURO 200,000,000 OUT OF THE EURO 428,885,703 5.70% MALTA GOVERNMENT

**STOCK 2012 (III) INTO EURO 200,000,000 4.30%**

**MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2016 (IV)**

29th November, 2011

LEGAL NOTICE 466 of 2011.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Directive is the Directive by the Minister of Finance, the Economy and Investment for the Conversion of Euro 200,000,000 out of the Euro 428,885,703 5.70% Malta Government Stock 2012 (III) into Euro 200,000,000 4.30% Malta Government Stock 2016 (IV).

## 2. Conversion of Stock.

(1) The Minister of Finance, the Economy and Investment has declared Euro 200,000,000 out of the Euro 428,885,703 5.70% Malta Government Stock 2012 (III) which is due for repayment 30th September, 2012 to be convertible into registered stock to be issued under the provisions of the Local Loans (Registered Stock and Securities) Ordinance.

*Cap. 161.*

(2) The Minister has also authorised in terms of articles 31(b) and 32 of the said Ordinance that, for the conversion of Euro 200,000,000 out of the Euro 428,885,703 5.70% Malta Government Stock 2012 (III), a new 4.30% Malta Government Stock 2016 (IV) be created and issued as provided in this Directive.

## 3. Mode of issuing Stock.

The Stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Allottment policy.

All stockholders of 5.70% Malta Government Stock, 2012 (III) are eligible to apply for the 4.30% Malta Government Stock 2016 (IV). Allotment of this stock will be made as set out in the prospectus.

## 5. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when this Stock has been allotted, an application shall be made for this Stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

## 6. Creation and Issue of Stock for conversion.

The amount to be created and issued under this Stock shall be Euro 200,000,000.

## 7. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

(1) Interest at the rate of 2.15% for every Euro 100 in respect of the 4.30% Malta Government Stock 2016 (IV) shall be payable half-yearly in arrears on the 16th of February and the 16th of August in every year during the duration of the Stock.

(2) The first interest payment of Euro 2.15 for every Euro 100 stock covering the period from the 16th August, 2011 to 15th February, 2012 (both dates inclusive), shall be made on the 16th February, 2012.

Redemption of 8. The 4.30% Malta Government Stock 2016 (IV) shall be Stock.

redeemed at par on 16th August, 2016.
