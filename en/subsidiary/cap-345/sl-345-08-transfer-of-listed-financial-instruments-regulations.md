# Transfer of Listed Financial Instruments Regulations (S.L. 345.08)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 345.08**

**TRANSFER OF LISTED FINANCIAL**

**INSTRUMENTS REGULATIONS**

1st April, 2006 LEGAL NOTICE 287 of 2004, as amended by Act IV of 2005 and Legal Notice 335 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: IV. 2005.30.*

The title of these regulations is the Transfer of Listed Financial Instruments.

## 2. Transfer of ownership of listed financial instruments.

*Amended by: IV. 2005.30; L.N. 335 of 2007. Cap. 204. Cap.16.*

The transfer of the ownership of listed financial instruments that are transacted on a regulated market is complete for all purposes of law upon the payment, in full and final settlement, of the consideration where such payment is effected through a payment system approved and authorised by the Central Bank of Malta in accordance with article 36 of the Central Bank of Malta Act; and the provisions of article 1347 of the Civil Code shall not apply.

## 3. Transactions traded outside the regulated market.

*Amended by: L.N. 335 of 2007. S.L. 345.07*

The provisions of regulation 2 shall also apply to transactions traded outside the regulated market, in accordance with the Financial Markets Act (Off-Market Deals) Regulations, made under the Act, unless the parties thereto specifically agree otherwise.
