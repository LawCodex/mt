# Regulated Markets and Central Securities Depositories (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 345.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 345.05**

**REGULATED MARKETS AND CENTRAL**

**SECURITIES DEPOSITORIES (FEES)**

**REGULATIONS\***

1st January, 2009

LEGAL NOTICE 360 of 2008.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Regulated Markets and Central Securities Depositories (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation. Cap. 345.

In these regulations "the Act" means the Financial Markets Act and the words and expressions used shall have the same meaning as is given to them in the Act.

## 3. Fees payable by applicants for authorisation as a regulated market and by authorised regulated markets.

The fees prescribed in respect of the application for and the retention of an authorisation as a regulated market shall be as follows:

(a) €12,000 upon submission of an application for authorisation, irrespective of whether the authorisation is eventually issued or not;

(b) €25,000 as an annual supervisory fee, due on the 1st January of each year by each regulated market if it is to remain a regulated market.

## 4. Fees payable by applicants for authorisation as a central securities depositary and by authorised central securities depositories.

The fees prescribed in respect of the application for and the retention of an authorisation to provide the service of a central securities depository shall be as follows:

(a) €4,000 upon submission of an application for authorisation irrespective of whether the authorisation is eventually issued or not;

(b) €10,000 as an annual supervisory fee, due on the 1st January of each year by each person authorised to provide the service of a central securities depository, if it is to continue to provide such service.

## 5. Fees payable by persons authorised both to act as a regulated market and to provide the service of a central securities depository and by applicants for such authorisations.

The fees payable by a person wishing to apply and retain authorizations both as a regulated market and to provide the services of a central securities depository shall be as follows:

(a) €12,000 upon submission of an application for both authorisations, irrespective of whether the authorisations are eventually issued or not;

(b) €30,000 as an annual supervisory fee, due on the 1st January of each year by each person authorised both as a regulated market and to provide the service of a central securities depository, if it is to remain a regulated market and to provide the services of a central securities depository.

\*These regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 367 of 2024.

## 6. Fees payable by applicants forming part of the same group of companies.

Where the application for authorisation as a regulated market and the application for authorisation to provide the services of a central securities depository are submitted by two companies forming part of the same group and where such authorisations are held by two such companies, the fees prescribed in regulation 5 shall apply.
