## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Transfer of Rights and Liabilities of the Commissioner of Land Order.

## 2. Specified land. Cap. 169.

The land described in the Schedule to this Order is hereby specified for the purpose of article 2 of the Commissioner of Land Ordinance.

## 3. Appointed day.

The rights and liabilities in respect of the land specified in article 2 of this Order shall with effect from the dates specified in the Schedule to this Order be exercised by Malta Industrial Parks Limited (C 28965) as provided in the said article.
