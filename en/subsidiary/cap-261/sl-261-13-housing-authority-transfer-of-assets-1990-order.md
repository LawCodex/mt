# Housing Authority (Transfer of Assets) (1990) Order (S.L. 261.13)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 261.13**

HOUSING AUTHORITY (TRANSFER OF ASSETS)

**(1990) ORDER**

4th January, 1990

LEGAL NOTICES 3, 19, 36, 69, 104, 122, 144, 146, 154, 199 and 216 of 1990, consolidated.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Housing Authority (Transfer of Assets) (1990) Order.

## 2. Specified property. Cap. 261.

The property described in the Schedule is hereby specified for the purposes of article 12 of the Housing Authority Act.

## 3. Appointed day.

The property specified in article 2 shall be transferred and vest in the Housing Authority as provided in the article aforesaid with effect from the day specified in the Schedule.
