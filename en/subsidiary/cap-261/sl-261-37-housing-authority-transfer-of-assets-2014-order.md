# Housing Authority (Transfer of Assets (2014) Order (S.L. 261.37)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 261.37**

HOUSING AUTHORITY (TRANSFER OF ASSETS)

**(2014) ORDER**

14th February, 2014

LEGAL NOTICES 82, 175 (as amended by Legal Notice 266 of 2016), 258, 304, 383 and 446 of 2014, consolidated.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Housing Authority (Transfer of Assets (2014) Order.

## 2. Specified property.

The property described in the Schedule is hereby specified for the purpose of article 12 of the Housing Authority Act.

## 3. Appointed day.

The property specified in article 2 shall be transferred and vest in the Housing Authority as provided in the article aforesaid with effect from the day specified in the Schedule.
