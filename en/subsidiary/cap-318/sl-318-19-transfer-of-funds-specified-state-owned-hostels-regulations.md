# Transfer of Funds (Specified State Owned) Hostels Regulations (S.L. 318.19)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 318.19**

TRANSFER OF FUNDS (SPECIFIED STATE OWNED HOSTELS) REGULATIONS

25th April, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 112 of 2003.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Transfer of Funds (Specified State Owned Hostels) Regulations.

## 2. Transfer of funds. Cap. 318.

In terms of the provisions of article 131(4)(ii) of the Social Security Act, any rates payable to the Welfare Committee in accordance with the State-Owned Institutions and Hostels Rates Regulations in respect of the |ejtun Hostel for the Elderly as indicated in the Specified State-Owned Institutions and Hostels Regulations shall be transferred to the Director responsible for the Elderly and Community Services Department.
