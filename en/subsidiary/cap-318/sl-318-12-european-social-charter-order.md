# European Social Charter Order (S.L. 318.12)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 318.12**

**EUROPEAN SOCIAL CHARTER ORDER**

1st January, 1999 Legal Notice 204 of 1999.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the European Social Charter Order.

## 2. Extension of application. Cap. 318.

The provisions of the Social Security Act shall apply to nationals of the countries being, together with Malta, parties to the European Social Charter, and who have their ordinary residence in Malta.
