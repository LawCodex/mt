# Maximum Pensionable Income Order (S.L. 318.8)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 318.08**

**MAXIMUM PENSIONABLE INCOME ORDER**

1st January, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 5 of 2007, as amended by Legal Notice 424 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Maximum Pensionable Income Order.

## 2. Date of entry into force.

This order shall be deemed to have come into force with effect from the 1st January, 2005.

## 3. Maximum pensionable income.

*Amended by: L.N. 424 of 2007. Cap. 318.*

For the purposes of the Social Security Act and the interpretation of the expre ssion "pensionable income", the pensionable income shall in no case:

(a) exceed fifteen thousand and nine hundred and thirty-five euros and twenty-four cents (€15,935.24) per annum with effect from the 1st January, 2005; and

(b) exceed sixteen thousand and two hundred and seven euros and seventy-eight cents (€16,207.78) per annum with effect from the 7th January, 2006.
