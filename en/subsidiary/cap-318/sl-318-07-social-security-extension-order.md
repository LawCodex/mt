# Social Security (Extension) Order (S.L. 318.7)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 318.07**

**SOCIAL SECURITY (EXTENSION) ORDER**

1st January, 1987

LEGAL NOTICE 16 of 1987.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Social Security (Extension) Order.

## 2. Extension of application. Cap. 318.

The provisions of articles 19 and 30 (insofar as it applies to social assistance) of the Social Security Act, shall apply to nationals of the countries specified in the Schedule to this Order, such countries being, together with Malta, parties to the European Convention on Social and Medical Assistance.
