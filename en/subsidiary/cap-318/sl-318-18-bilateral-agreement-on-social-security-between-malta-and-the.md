# Bilateral Agreement on Social Security between Malta and the Netherlands Regulations (S.L. 318.18)

BILATERAL AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY

*1*

BETWEEN MALTA AND THE NETHERLANDS [S.L.318.18

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 318.18**

BILATERAL AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL SECURITY BETWEEN MALTA AND THE NETHERLANDS

**REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 359 of 2002.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Bilateral Agreement on Social Security between Malta and the Netherlands Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

"Agreement" means the Agreement on Social Security between the Government of Malta and the Government of the Kingdom of the Netherlands signed on 11th September, 2001, a copy of which was laid on the table of the House of Representatives on the 19th November, 2002.

## 3. Extension of applicability. Cap. 318.

The provisions of the Social Security Act shall have effect in its application with regard to the persons mentioned in the agreement, subject to such modifications and adaptations as may be required in accordance with the provisions of the said agreement, and in so far as the Social Security Act applies to -

(i) Sickness and Matemity Benefits;

(ii) Pensions in respect of Invalidity;

(iii) Pensions in respect of Retirement;

(iv) Pensions in respect of Widowhood;

(v) Benefits in respect of Unemployment; and

(vi) Children’s Allowances including allowances for children with a disability.
