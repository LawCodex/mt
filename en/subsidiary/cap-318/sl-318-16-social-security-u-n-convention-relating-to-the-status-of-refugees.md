# Social Security (U.N. Convention relating to the Status of Refugees) Order (S.L. 318.16)

SOCIAL SECURITY (U.N. CONVENTION

*1*

RELATING TO THE STATUS OF REFUGEES) [S.L.318.16

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 318.16**

**SOCIAL SECURITY (U.N. CONVENTION**

**RELATING TO THE STATUS OF REFUGEES)**

**ORDER**

1st October, 2001 Legal Notice 291 of 2001.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Social Security (U.N. Convention relating to the Status of Refugees) Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

For the purposes of this Order, the U N. Convention on the Status of Refugees refers to the 1951 United Nations Convention relating to the Status of Refugees, and the Protocol relating to the Status of Refugees of the 31st January, 1967.

## 3. Extension and application. Cap. 420.

The provisions of the Social Security Act shall apply to refugees who in terms of the provisions of the International Protection Act are accepted as refugees by the Commissioner for Refugees appointed under the Act.
