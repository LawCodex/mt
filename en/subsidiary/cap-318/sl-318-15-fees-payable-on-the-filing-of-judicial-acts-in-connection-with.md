# Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts in connection with Appeals Regulations (S.L. 318.15)

FEES PAYABLE ON THE FILING OF JUDICIAL

*1*

ACTS IN CONNECTION WITH APPEALS [S.L.318.15

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 318.15**

**FEES PAYABLE ON THE FILING OF JUDICIAL**

**ACTS IN CONNECTION WITH APPEALS**

**REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2001 Legal Notice 105 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 424 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts in connection with Appeals Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable on appeals.

*Amended by: L.N. 424 of 2007. Cap. 318.*

The fees payable under article 109(3) of the Social Security Act on the filing of judicial acts in connection with appeals shall be eleven euros and sixty-five cents (11.65).
