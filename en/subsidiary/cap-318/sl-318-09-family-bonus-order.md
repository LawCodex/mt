# Family Bonus Order (S.L. 318.09)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 318.09**

**FAMILY BONUS ORDER**

7th January, 1989

LEGAL NOTICE 93 of 1989.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Family Bonus Order.

## 2. Rate of family bonus. Cap. 318.

A family bonus equivalent to Lm1 per week shall be paid by the Director of Social Security in accordance with article 3 of this Order to the recipient of any of the allowances payable under and in accordance with articles 81 to 88 of the Social Security Act, hereinafter referred to as "the Act".

## 3. Payment of family bonus.

The family bonus referred to in article 2 of this Order shall be paid at intervals of twenty-six weeks and shall, in any calendar year, fall due as follows -

(a) as regards the first payment, on the day when the second quarterly payment of any allowance under articles 81 to 88 of the Act falls due in accordance with article 99(1)(b)(iv) of the said Act; and

(b) as regards the second payment, on the day when the fourth quarterly payment of any allowance under the aforesaid articles 81 to 88 falls due in accordance with the aforesaid subparagraph (iv).

## 4. Period covered by each payment of the family bonus.

Every payment as aforesaid shall cover any family bonus that may be payable in respect of the thirteen consecutive weeks in arrears, or part thereof, as the case may be, ending on the last day immediately prior to the day when the payment of the family bonus falls due in terms of article 3 of this Order and any family bonus that may be payable in respect of the thirteen consecutive weeks in advance, or part thereof, as the case may be, starting from the first day immediately after the day when the payment of the family bonus falls due in terms of the aforesaid article 3.

## 5. Cessation of family bonus.

Family bonus shall cease to become payable on the same day that any of the allowances payable under and in accordance with the provisions of articles 81 to 88 of the Act ceases to become payable; and for this purpose the provisions of article 99(3) of the Act shall apply in the same way as they may be applicable with respect to any of the allowances payable under the provisions of the aforesaid articles 81 to 88 with regards to the same beneficiary.
