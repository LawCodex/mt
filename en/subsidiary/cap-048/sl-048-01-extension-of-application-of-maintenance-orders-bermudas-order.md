# Extension of Application of Maintenance Orders (Bermudas) Order (S.L. 48.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 48.01**

**EXTENSION OF APPLICATION OF**

**MAINTENANCE ORDERS (BERMUDAS) ORDER**

27th July, 1925 PROCLAMATION VII of 1925.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Extension of Application of Maintenance Orders (Bermudas) Order.

## 2. Extension of applicability. Cap. 48.

The provisions of the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) Ordinance shall apply and extend to maintenance orders made by the Court of Bermuda or Somers Islands by virtue of the Proclamation which is reproduced in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE\*

(Article 2)

WHEREAS it is provided, by section 12 of the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) Ordinance, that where the Governor in Council is satisfied that reciprocal provisions have been made by the Legislature of any part of His Majesty’s Dominions outside these Islands for the enforcement, within that part, of maintenance orders made by the Court of these Islands, the Governor may, by Proclamation, extend this Ordinance to that part, and thereupon that part shall become a part of His Majesty’s Dominions to which this Ordinance extends;

AND WHEREAS I am satisfied that by virtue of the Bermuda Maintenance Orders Enforcement Act, 1922 (Bermuda Act No. 6 of 1922) the Legislature of Bermuda or Somers Islands has made such reciprocal provisions as aforesaid:

NOW THEREFORE, I, SIR WALTER NORRIS CONGREVE, do hereby proclaim that the said Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) Ordinance shall apply and extend to maintenance orders made by the Court of Bermuda or Somers Islands.

\*The Proclamation is reproduced in its original form. By virtue of the amendments, made to the parent law, by Act LVIII of 1974, the power to extend the applicability of the Ordinance outside Malta is vested in the President of Malta.
