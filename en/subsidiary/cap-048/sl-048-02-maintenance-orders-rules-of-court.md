# Maintenance Orders Rules of Court (S.L. 48.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 48.02**

**MAINTENANCE ORDERS RULES OF COURT**

28th October, 1927 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 422 of 1927, as amended by Acts LVIII of 1974 and XXIV of 1995.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is Maintenance Orders Rules of Court.

## 2. Entering of order on register.

*Amended by: XXIV.1995.362. Cap. 48.*

The Registrar of Courts to whom a copy of any order under articles 3 and 5 of the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) Ordinance, hereinafter referred to as the "Ordinance", is transmitted shall enter it in the register on the date on which he receives it.

## 3. Other orders to be entered.

*Amended by: LVIII.1974.68.*

The Registrar shall likewise enter in a register all orders under article 4, and provisional orders under article 5 of the Ordinance, sent to the President of Malta for transmission for enforcement overseas and a note thereof shall be made in the record of the suit in which any such order occurs.

## 4. Inspection of registers.

The said registers shall be open to inspection and copies of orders so registered shall be supplied upon payment of the proper fees.

## 5. Provisional orders.

*Amended by: LVIII.1974.68.*

When an order provisionally made overseas has been confirmed with or without modification under article 6 of the Ordinance or the Court has decided not to confirm it, the Registrar shall send notice thereof through the President of Malta, to the Court which had made the provisional order.

## 6. Provisional order made against defendant residing abroad.

When an order is made provisionally under article 5 of the Ordinance against a defendant who is resident overseas, the Registrar shall obtain from the applicant a personal description and, if possible, a photograph of the defendant should be added to the papers in case he disputes his identity and his present address. The name and address of the officer of the Court overseas should also be stated.

## 7. Proper officer.

The Registrar shall be the proper officer of the Court by and to whom payments are collected and paid in pursuance of the Ordinance.

## 8. Further evidence.

When a provisional order made under article 5 of the Ordinance has been remitted under sub-article (4) of that article to the Court for the purpose of taking further evidence, notice specifying the further evidence required and the time and place fixed for taking it shall be sent by the Registrar to the person on whose application the provisional order was made.
