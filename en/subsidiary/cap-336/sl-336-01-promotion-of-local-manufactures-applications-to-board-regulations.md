# Promotion of Local Manufactures (Applications to Board) Regulations (S.L. 336.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 336.01**

**PROMOTION OF LOCAL MANUFACTURES**

**(APPLICATIONS TO BOARD) REGULATIONS**

4th September, 1990 LEGAL NOTICE 136 of 1990, as amended by Legal Notice 424 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Promotion of Local Manufactures (Applications to Board) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires - "Act" means the Local Manufactures (Promotion) Act;

*Cap. 336.*

"Board" means the Local Manufactures Promotion Board set up under article 3 of the Act; and

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for industry.

## 3. Prescribed forms and fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 424 of 2007.*

(1) The prescribed forms for the application in writing from any person or organisation in connection with measures that may be taken by the Minister for the promotion of Maltese goods under the Act shall be in accordance with the form in the -

(a) First Schedule for applying to the Board for the introduction of measures to promote Maltese goods, and

(b) Second Schedule for applying to the Board for the amendment or revocation of any existing measures to promote Maltese goods,

and any reference to a prescribed form shall be construed as a reference to the appropriate form in the said Schedules.

(2) A fee of twenty-three euros and twenty-nine cents (23.29) shall be paid in respect of each application on a prescribed form submitted to the Board by manufacturers or by importers. Fees shall under no circumstances be refunded.

## 4. Powers of the Board.

(1) The Board may request such information, inspect such books and other documents and carry out or order such inspections of premises as may reasonably be considered necessary to enable it to carry out its functions.

(2) The Board may set time limits for compliance with any requests it may make in accordance with sub-regulation (1).

(3) The Board may consider an application on a prescribed form to have been withdrawn if the person making such application does not comply with any final time limit which the Board may set for the submission of any information, books or other documents connected with his application.
