# Reversion of Certain Lands Order (S.L. 432.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 432.05**

**REVERSION OF CERTAIN LANDS ORDER**

4th May, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 145 of 2018.

## 1. Citation.

(1) The title of this Order is the Reversion of Certain Lands Order.

## 2. Specified property.

The properties specified in article 3 of this Order shall revert to the person or persons to whom the land belonged, by whatsoever title, as if the acquisition by virtue of article 5 of the Building Development Areas Act\*, had never taken place with effect from the 18th June 1985, on which date the land in question was acquired by Government in virtue of the said article.

*Cap. 303. (Repealed)*

## 3. Description of specified property.

The properties to which article 2 of this Order refers is the following, that is to say:

(i) a plot of land at Kalkara having an area of circa 139 square meters, bounded on the North by the property of Family Deguara Caruana Gatto, South and East by Government Property as indicated edged in blue and marked as plot ‘A’ on plan P.D. no. 256\_87\_A\_14;

(ii) a plot of land at Kalkara having an area of circa 23 square meters, bounded on the North and West by the property of Family Deguara Caruana Gatto and East by Government property as indicated edged in blue and marked as plot ’B’ on plan P.D. no. 256\_87\_A\_14;

(iii) a plot of land at Kalkara having an area of circa 345 square meters, bounded on the East and North East by the property of Family Deguara Caruana Gatto and the North by the property of Mr Camilleri and others as indicated edged in blue and marked as plot ‘C’ on plan P.D. no. 256\_87\_A\_14;

(iv) a plot of land at Kalkara having an area of circa 1654 square meters, bounded on the North by the property of Family Deguara Caruana Gatto, East and North West by Government property as indicated edged in blue and marked as plot ‘D’ on plan P.D. no. 256\_87\_A\_14.

\* See article 10 of Act X of 1988.
