# Reversion of Certain Lands (2006) Order (S.L. 432.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 432.03**

**REVERSION OF CERTAIN LANDS (2006) ORDER**

18th April, 2006

LEGAL NOTICE 103 of 2006.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Reversion of Certain Lands (2006) Order.

## 2. Specified property.

The property specified in the Schedule shall revert to the person or persons to whom the lands belonged, by whatsoever title, as if the acquisition by virtue of article 5 of the Building Development Areas Act, had never taken place with effect from the date specified in the Schedule, on which date the lands in question were acquired by Government in virtue of the said article.

*Cap. 303.*

## SCHEDULE

(Article 2)

The property to which article 2 of this Order refers is the following, that is to say:

As from the 4th July, 1983:
