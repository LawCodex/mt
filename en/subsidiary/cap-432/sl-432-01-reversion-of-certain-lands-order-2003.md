# Reversion of Certain Lands Order (2003) (S.L. 432.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 432.01**

**REVERSION OF CERTAIN LANDS (2003) ORDER**

14th March, 2003;

11th April, 2003

LEGAL NOTICES 84, 85 and 107 of 2003.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Reversion of Certain Lands (2003) Order.

## 2. Specified property.

The property specified in the Schedule shall revert to the person or persons to whom the lands belonged, by whatsoever title, as if the acquisition by virtue of article 5 of the Building Development Areas Act, had never taken place with effect from the dates respectively specified in the Schedule, on which dates the lands in question were acquired by Government in virtue of the said article.
