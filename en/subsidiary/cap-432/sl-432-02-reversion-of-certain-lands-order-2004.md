# Reversion of Certain Lands Order (2004) (S.L. 432.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 432.02**

**REVERSION OF CERTAIN LANDS (2004) ORDER**

28th May, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 305 of 2004.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Reversion of Certain Lands (2004) Order.

## 2. Specified property.

The property specified in the Schedule shall revert to the person or persons to whom the lands belonged, by whatsoever title, as if the acquisition by virtue of article 5 of the Building Development Areas Act, had never taken place with effect from the date specified in the Schedule, on which date the lands in question were acquired by Government in virtue of the said article.

*Cap. 303.*

## SCHEDULE

(Article 2)

The property to which article 2 of this Order refers is the following, that is to say:

As from the 4th July, 1983:

(i) Portion of land at Mosta limits of Santa Margherita, namely plot 5A of area of circa 418 square metres and is bounded from North-East by a public lane, South-West and South-East by property of the Government, which plot is shown edged in red on plan P.D. 16\_2003\_B, as appearing in the attached Annex.

(ii) Portion of land at Msida limits of Swatar, of area of circa 38 square metres and is bounded from North-West and South-West by a public road, and from South-East by property of Christef Co.Ltd and others, which plot is shown edged in red on plan P.D.117\_2002\_B as appearing in the attached Annex.
