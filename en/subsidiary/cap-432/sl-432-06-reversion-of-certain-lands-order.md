# Reversion of Certain Lands Order (S.L. 432.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 432.06**

**REVERSION OF CERTAIN LANDS ORDER**

23rd December, 2025

LEGAL NOTICE 295 of 2025.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Reversion of Certain Lands Order.

## 2. Specified property.  Cap. 303. (Repealed)

The property specified in article 3 shall revert to the person to whom the land belonged, by whatsoever title, as if the acquisition by virtue of article 5 of the Building Development Areas Act had never taken place, with effect from 18th June 1985, being the date on which the land in question was acquired by the Government by virtue of the said article.

## 3. Description of specified property.

The property to which article 2 refers is the following:

A portion of land at Mosta, known as Tal-Blata l-Għolja, having an area of circa four hundred and ninety-four square metres (494m2), which portion is bounded from the East, South and West by third party property, indicated in blue and marked as plot 7\_3 on the plan P.D.234A/88/1 in the Schedule.
