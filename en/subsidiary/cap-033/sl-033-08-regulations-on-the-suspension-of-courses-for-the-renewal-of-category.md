# Regulations on the Suspension of Courses for the Renewal of Category "A", "B" and "C" Licences under the Control of Fireworks and other Explosives Regulations (S.L. 33.08)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 33.08**

**REGULATIONS ON THE SUSPENSION OF**

COURSES FOR THE RENEWAL OF CATEGORY "A", "B" AND "C" LICENCES UNDER THE THE

**CONTROL OF FIREWORKS AND OTHER**

**EXPLOSIVES REGULATIONS**

2nd July, 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 280 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Regulations on the Suspension of Courses for the Renewal of Category "A", "B" and "C" Licences under the Control of Fireworks and other Explosives Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*S.L. 33. 03.*

"regulations" means the Control of Fireworks and other Explosives Regulations.

## 3. Extension.

Notwithstanding the provisions of sub-regulations (7) and (8) of regulation 4 of the regulations, no refresher courses, oral tests, interviews, or written tests shall be held during the year 2021, and refresher courses and tests scheduled for the year 2021 shall be held in the year 2022, and those scheduled for the year 2022 shall be held in the year 2023.

## 4. Renewal.

Notwithstanding the provisions of sub-regulations (3B) and (6A) of regulation 4 of the regulations, holders of licences of Categories "A", "B" and "C" in line with regulation 3 shall have their licences renewed by an additional one year, provided that they fulfil all other conditions at law, until such time as their refresher course, oral test, interview or written test is held and results notified.
