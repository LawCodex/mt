# Ringed or Chained Pellets (Prohibition) Regulations (S.L. 33.2)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 33.02**

**RINGED OR CHAINED PELLETS (PROHIBITION)**

**REGULATIONS**

25th January, 1972 LEGAL NOTICE 4 of 1972.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Ringed or Chained Pellets (Prohibition) Regulations.

## 2. Prohibition of importation, etc., of certain explosives.

It shall not be lawful for any person to import, manufacture, store, keep, distribute or use any explosives consisting of ringed or chained pellets.

## 3. Prohibited explosives to be handed over to Commissioner of Police.

Any person who has in his possession any explosives of the type referred to in regulation 2, shall, within three days, hand over such explosives to the Commissioner of Police for their destruction.
