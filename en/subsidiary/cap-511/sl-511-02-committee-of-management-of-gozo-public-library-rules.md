# Committee of Management of Gozo Public Library Rules (S.L. 511.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 511.02**

**COMMITTEE OF MANAGEMENT OF THE**

**GOZO PUBLIC LIBRARY RULES**

14th August, 1924 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 210 of 1924, as amended by Government Notice 96 of 1925.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is Committee of Management of the Gozo Public Library Rules.

## 2. The Committee.

The Committee shall consist of a Chairman and not less than nine members t o be nominated yearly by the Mi nist er responsible for Public Libraries.

## 3. Absence of Chairman.

*Amended by: G.N. 96 of 1925.*

In the absence of the Chairman the presiding member will be elected by the members present.

The Librarian will act as Secretary.

## 4. Meetings.

The members of the Committee shall meet, at least, once every quarter and at such other times as may be deemed necessary.

## 5. Special meetings.

The Chairman may summon a special meeting and any two members may ask for one, specifying the purpose for which the meeting is to be called.

## 6. Quorum.

Business shall not be transacted unless five, at least, of the committee of management are present, exclusive of the Chairman.

## 7. Decisions by majority votes.

Every question will be decided by a majority of votes; the Chairman shall only have a casting vote.

## 8. Regulations for maintenance of order.

The Committee of Management may draw up regulations as occasions arise for the maintenance of order in the library, for the circulation of books and for other purposes connected with the management of the library.

## 9. Responsibilities of the Librarian.

The Librarian is responsible to the Committee for the care of the library, for the proper discharge of their duties by the employees under his direction and for the maintenance of order in the library.

## 10. Librarian to draw up annual report.

The Librarian shall draw up an annual report, giving particulars respecting the number and nature of -

(a) the works purchased;

(b) the works presented;

(c) the works missing or damaged beyond repair;

(d) any change of personnel; and

(e) any matters of interest relating to the Library.

## 11. List of works to be published yearly.

A list of works added to the library shall be published yearly by the Librarian in the Government Gazette.

## 12. Duties of the Librarian.

The Librarian shall keep -

(a) a general inventory;

(b) an alphabetical catalogue;

(c) a catalogue arranged by subject; and

(d) a register of the books lent and returned.

He shall also keep a catalogue of incomplete works, works missing, duplicates and an index of the works in course of publication.

## 13. Library staff.

The library staff shall consist of a Librarian and an attendant or distributor of books.
