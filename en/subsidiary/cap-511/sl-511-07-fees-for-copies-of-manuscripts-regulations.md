# Fees for Copies of Manuscripts Regulations (S.L. 511.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 511.07**

**FEES FOR COPIES OF MANUSCRIPTS**

**REGULATIONS**

19th April, 1974 LEGAL NOTICE 46 of 1974, as amended by Legal Notice 408 of 2007 and Act VII of 2011.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Fees for Copies of Manuscripts Regulations.

## 2. Fees.

There shall be charged by the Librarian for copies or extracts from records kept in any public library, and for their authentication, the fees set out in the Schedule:

Provided that the Librarian may, at his discretion, require from any person asking for any such copy or extract such deposit as the said Librarian may fix in each particular case.
