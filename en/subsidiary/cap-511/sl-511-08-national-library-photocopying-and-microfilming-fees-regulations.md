# National Library (Photocopying and Microfilming Fees) Regulations (S.L. 511.08)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 511.08**

**NATIONAL LIBRARY (PHOTOCOPYING AND**

**MICROFILMING FEES) REGULATIONS**

17th December, 2001 LEGAL NOTICE 302 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 408 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is the National Library (Photocopying and Microfilming Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 408 of 2007.*

The fees payable for photocopies and microfilming made at the National Library shall be as follows:

Photocopying

(i) for every A4 page ......................... 23c

(ii) for every A3 page ......................... 35c

Microfilming

(i) handling charge per volume ......... €5.82

(ii) for every frame ............................ 23c

(iii) duplicating microfilm from

negative to positive per film ............. €34.94
