# Application of the Malta Libraries Act Order (S.L. 511.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 511.05**

APPLICATION OF THE MALTA LIBRARIES ACT

**ORDER**

22nd February, 1974 LEGAL NOTICE 19 of 1974, as amended by Act VII of 2011.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: VII. 2011.36.*

The title of this Order is the Application of the Malta Libraries Act Order.

## 2. Application of Act.

*Amended by: VII. 2011.36.*

The provisions of the Malta Libraries Act shall apply to the Malta Public Library at Beltisseb].
