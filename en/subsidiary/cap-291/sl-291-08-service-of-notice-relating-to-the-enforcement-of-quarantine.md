# Service of Notice relating to the Enforcement of Quarantine Regulations (S.L. 291.08)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 291.08**

**SERVICE OF NOTICE RELATING TO THE**

ENFORCEMENT OF QUARANTINE REGULATIONS 12th March, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 44 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Service of Notice relating to the Enforcement of Quarantine Regulations.

## 2. Notice of the offence.

(1) A Police officer, detailed to confirm or otherwise the observance of the Enforcement of Directions relating to Quarantine Regulations, 2020, who discovers that a person has breached the conditions relating to quarantine imposed upon him, shall hand over to the offender or affix in some conspicuous place at the address of the offender a notice containing:

(i) the nature of the offence;

(ii) the place, time and date of the offence;

(iii) the signature of the Police officer handing over or affixing the notice; and

(iv) information as to payment of the penalty.

(2) Where any notice has been handed over or affixed as is provided in sub-regulation (1), the offender may, within fifteen days of the termination of his quarantine period, pay the penalty in respect of the breach in such manner as shown in that notice.

(3) Where the said penalty is not paid within the period referred to in sub-regulation (2), proceedings shall be instituted before a Commissioner for Justice in respect of the offence in question.

## 3. Scheduled offence S.L. 291.08

Any breach of the provisions of the Enforcement of Directions relating to Quarantine Regulations, shall be regarded as a scheduled offence for the purposes of the Commissioners for Justice Act.
