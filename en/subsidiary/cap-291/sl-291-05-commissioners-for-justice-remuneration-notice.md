# Commissioners for Justice (Remuneration) Notice (S.L. 291.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 291.05**

**COMMISSIONERS FOR JUSTICE**

**(REMUNERATION) NOTICE**

1st January, 2006

LEGAL NOTICE 164 of 2006, as amended by Legal Notice 423 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Notice is the Commissioners for Justice (Remuneration) Notice.

## 2. Remuneration payable to commissioners.

*Amended by: L.N. 423 of 2007.*

As from the 1st January, 2006, there shall be payable to Commissioners for Justice a remuneration at the rate of fifty-eight euros and twenty-three cents (€58.23) for each sitting, so however that the total amount shall not in one particular year exceed the sum of eleven thousand and six hundred and forty-six euros and eighty-seven cents (€11,646.87).
