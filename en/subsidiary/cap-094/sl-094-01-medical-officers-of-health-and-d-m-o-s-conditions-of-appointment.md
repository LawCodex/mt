# Medical Officers of Health and D.M.O. s Conditions of Appointment Regulations (S.L. 94.1)

MEDICAL OFFICERS OF HEALTH AND

D.M.O.’S CONDITIONS OF APPOINTMENT [ S.L.94.01

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 94.01**

**MEDICAL OFFICERS OF HEALTH AND D.M.O.’S**

CONDITIONS OF APPOINTMENT REGULATIONS 23rd June, 1944 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 251 of 1944, as amended by Emergency Ordinance I of 1959.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Medical Officers of Health and D.M.O.’s Conditions of Appointment Regulations.

## 2. Eligibility for the post of Medical Officer of Health.

*Amended by: Emerg. Ord. I of 1959.*

No candidate shall be eligible for the post of Medical Officer of Health unless he holds the permanent licence to practise the medical profession in these Islands, has practised in, or attended the practice of, a general hospital in these Islands or abroad for not less than one year to the satisfaction of the Advisory and Executive Board and has subsequently successfully undergone a course of instruction in public health approved by the Minister responsible for the Department of Health.

## 3. Eligibility for the post of District Medical Officer.

*Amended by: Emerg. Ord. I of 1959.*

No candidate shall be eligible for the post of District Medical Officer unless he holds the permanent licence to practise the medical profession in these Islands and has practised in or attended the practice of, a general hospital in these Islands or abroad for not less than one year to the satisfaction of the Advisory and Executive Board.
