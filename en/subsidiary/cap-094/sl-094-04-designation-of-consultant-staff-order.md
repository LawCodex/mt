# Designation of Consultant Staff Order (S.L. 94.4)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 94.04**

DESIGNATION OF CONSULTANT STAFF ORDER 23rd January, 1959 LEGAL NOTICE 3 of 1959.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Designation of Consultant Staff Order.

## 2. Designation of offices.

The offices, as listed in the Schedule to this Order, connected with hospital duty, have been designated to be offices entitling their substantive and permanent holders to form part of the consultant staff of Saint Luke’s Hospital for the purposes of the Department of Health (Constitution) Ordinance.

*Cap. 94.*
