# Abuses connected with Essential Commodities Regulations (S.L. 98.3)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 98.03**

**ABUSES CONNECTED WITH ESSENTIAL**

**COMMODITIES REGULATIONS**

3rd February, 1939 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 52 of 1939.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Abuses connected with Essential Commodities Regulations.

## 2. Applicability.

These Regulations shall apply to the commodities listed in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## 3. Retailers to display prices.

(1) All retailers of the commodities to which these Regulations apply shall display in a prominent place a list showing the prices of all such commodities and shall also display on a ticket affixed to each commodity the price of such commodities.

(2) The list and the tickets aforesaid shall be printed or legibly written in ink on white paper in characters not less than twelve point seven millimetres in size.

(3) Where various qualities of the same commodity are offered for sale, the price of such quality shall be shown separately.

## 4. Price control.

Commodities listed in the Schedule shall not be sold at a price higher than that shown on the price list.
