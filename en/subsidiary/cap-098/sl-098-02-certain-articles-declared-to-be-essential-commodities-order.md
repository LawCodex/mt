**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 98.02**

**CERTAIN ARTICLES DECLARED TO BE**

**ESSENTIAL COMMODITIES ORDER**

3rd February, 1939 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 51 of 1939.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Order declaring Certain Articles to be Essential Commodities.

## 2. Essential commodities. Cap. 98.

The articles listed in the Schedule to this Order have been declared essential commodities for the purpose of the Inflation of Prices (Prevention) Ordinance.
