# Returns of Essential Commodities Regulations (S.L. 98.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 98.01**

**RETURNS OF ESSENTIAL COMMODITIES**

**REGULATIONS**

3rd February, 1939 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 50 of 1939.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Returns of Essential Commodities Regulations.

## 2. Returns.

Every person owning, possessing, or concerned in the importation, sale, manufacture or production of essential commodities, shall furnish to the Chairman of the Price Control Board such returns as may, from time to time, be required by him in respect of any essential commodity held by, produced, consigned to, or under order by such person.
