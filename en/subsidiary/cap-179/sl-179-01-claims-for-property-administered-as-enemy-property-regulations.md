# Claims for Property administered as Enemy Property Regulations (S.L. 179.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 179.01**

**CLAIMS FOR PROPERTY ADMINISTERED AS**

**ENEMY PROPERTY REGULATIONS**

29th November, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 360 of 2002, as amended by Legal Notice 410 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Claims for Property administered as Enemy Property Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations -

*Cap. 179.*

"Act" means the Enemy Property Act;

"Board" means the board set up in terms of article 4 of the Act;

"Custodian" means the Custodian of Enemy Property mentioned in article 2 of the Act;

"property" means, unless otherwise indicated, both the movable and immovable property administered by the Custodian.

## 3. Claims.

*Amended by: L.N. 410 of 2007.*

(1) Any person, claiming to be a person entitled to lay claim under article 4 of the Act, shall pay a fee of two hundred and thirty-two euros and ninety-four cents (232.94) when submitting his claim to the Custodian.

(2) The Custodian shall refer all claims received by him to the Board as soon as may be after the 1st January 2003, and the Board shall ensure that, prior to the release of the property in terms of the Act, the Custodian recoups any actual expenses incurred during the course of the administration, and in addition, where the property is immovable, a five per centum administration fee calculated on the current value of the property so released, shall be due.
