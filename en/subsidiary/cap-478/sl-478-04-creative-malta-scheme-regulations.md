# Creative Malta Scheme Regulations (S.L. 478.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 478.04**

**CREATIVE MALTA SCHEME REGULATIONS**

20th December, 2022

LEGAL NOTICE 357 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Creative Malta Scheme Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 478.*

"Act" means the Malta Film Commission Act;

"applicant" means the same as the meaning assigned to it by the guidelines;

"Commission" means the Malta Film Commission established by article 3 of the Act;

"Commissioner" means the Malta Film Commissioner appointed under article 5(1) of the Act;

"Creative Malta Scheme" means the scheme set up in accordance with the guidelines which may provide assistance in terms of these regulations;

"guidelines" means such guidelines as may be published by the Commission from time to time in terms of the Act;

"project" means the same as the meaning assigned to it by the guidelines.

## 3. Assistance to support eligible applicants.

Where the Commission is satisfied that both the applicant and the project are eligible for assistance as prescribed under the guidelines, the Commission may provide assistance in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the guidelines.

## 4. Deadline for submission of applications for assistance.

(1) Applications for assistance to support any eligible applicants shall reach the Commission by the 31st October 2023.

(2) No assistance under these regulations shall be awarded by the Commission after the 31st December 2023.
