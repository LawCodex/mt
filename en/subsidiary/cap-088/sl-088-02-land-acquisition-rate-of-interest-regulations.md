# Land Acquisition (Rate of Interest) Regulations (S.L. 88.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 88.02**

**LAND ACQUISITION (RATE OF INTEREST)**

**REGULATIONS**

4th March, 2003 LEGAL NOTICE 78 of 2003.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Land Acquisition (Rate of Interest) Regulation.

## 2. Establishment of rate of interest. Cap. 88.

The minimum rate of interest for the purposes of article 22(3) of the Land Acquisition (Public Purposes) Ordinance, is hereby being established as the prevailing Term Deposit rate as published from time to time by the Central Bank of Malta, less 100 basis points.
