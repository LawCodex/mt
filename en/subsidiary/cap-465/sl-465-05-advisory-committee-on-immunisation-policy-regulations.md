# Advisory Committee on Immunisation Policy Regulations (S.L. 465.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 465.05**

ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON IMMUNISATION

**POLICY REGULATIONS**

14th September, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 253 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Advisory Committee on Immunisation Policy Regulations.

## 2. Establishment of Committee.

There shall be established by the Minister responsible for health an Advisory Committee on Immunisation Policy, hereinafter referred to as "the Committee".

## 3. Duties of the Committee.

It shall be the duty of the Committee to:

(a) promote the understanding of, and support for, the immunisation programme among professionals and the public at large;

(b) keep abreast of the latest research in vaccine development;

(c) advise the Superintendent of Public Health with respect to vaccination programme priorities in the short, medium and long term;

(d) develop written recommendations for the routine administration of vaccines;

(e) issue guidelines regarding dosages, periodicity and contraindications applicable to individual vaccines;

(f) tender advice as may be requested by the Superintendent of Public Health.

## 4. Composition of the Committee.

(1) The Committee shall be composed as follows:

(a) a chairperson to be nominated by the Superintendent of Public Health;

(b) a specialist in Public Health Medicine;

(c) a specialist in Paediatrics;

(d) a specialist in Virology;

(e) a specialist in Microbiology/Bacteriology;

(f) a specialist in Infectious Disease;

(g) a pharmacist.

(2) There shall be a Secretary to the Committee who shall be appointed by the Superintendent of Public Health.

## 5. Regulation of procedure.

The Committee shall regulate its own procedure.
