# Moratorium on Credit Facilities in Exceptional Circumstances (Extension) Regulations (S.L. 465.46)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 465.46**

**MORATORIUM ON CREDIT FACILITIES IN**

**EXCEPTIONAL CIRCUMSTANCES**

**(EXTENSION) REGULATIONS**

30th June, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 278 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Moratorium on Credit Facilities in Exceptional Circumstances (Extension) Regulations.

## 2. Extension of moratorium on credit facilities for economically vulnerable persons. S.L. 465.40.

(1) In furtherance to sub-regulations (2) and (3) of regulation 5 of the Moratorium on Credit Facilities in Exceptional Circumstances Regulations, the Minister responsible for public health, after consultation with the Minister for Finance and Financial Services, is hereby extending -

(a) the six-month (6) moratorium for a further period of six (6) months; and

(b) the period for applications from 30th June, 2020, th

to 30 September, 2020:

Provided that the extension referred to in paragraph (a) above shall require submission of an application for extension, with the period for applications ending on 30th September 2020, and shall be applicable only to borrowers who have been granted a moratorium on credit facilities between 1st March, 2020 and 30th June, 2020.

(2) The six-month (6) extension referred to in paragraph (a) above shall apply from the day following that on which the first six-month (6) period would have expired.
