# Closure of the Public Registry Order (S.L. 465.53)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 465.53**

**CLOSURE OF THE PUBLIC REGISTRY ORDER**

16th November, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 470 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Closure of the Public Registry Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this order, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Public Registry" means the Public Registry Office in Malta open for the registration of causes of preference among creditors, for the enrolment of acts requiring registration in order to have effect in regard to third parties, and for all other registrations required by law;

*Cap. 465.*

"Superintendent" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Public Health Act.

## 3. Closure of the Public Registry.

The Superintendent hereby orders the closure of the Legal Section within the Public Registry which order shall remain in force until the 23rd November of the year 2020, so however that notes for the registration of any cause of preference among creditors, notes of reference and notes for the enrolment of an act shall be received again by this section on the 24th of November of the year 2020. Provided that all legal terms for the registration of such notes, as required by any law, shall be deemed to be suspended during such time as this section is closed, and the remaining term shall start to run again as from the 24th of November of the year 2020:

Provided that the Public Registry shall remain open in those cases relating to judgments or decrees delivered by the courts of justice.
