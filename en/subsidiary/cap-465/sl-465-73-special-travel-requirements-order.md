# Special Travel Requirements Order (S.L. 465.73)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 465.73**

**SPECIAL TRAVEL REQUIREMENTS ORDER\***

9th January, 2023

LEGAL NOTICE 3 of 2023.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Special Travel Requirements Order.

## 2. Special requirements.

Any person who travels to Malta from China shall be in possession of a negative COVID-19 Rapid Antigen Test (RAT) performed not more than forty-eight (48) hours prior to his arrival in Malta:

Provided that the said requirements shall not apply to children who are eleven (11) years old or under.

## 3. Power of the Superintendent of Public Health to make exemptions.

The Superintendent of Public Health may, by means of a Government notice published in the Gazette and in accordance with those conditions which she deems necessary or appropriate, exempt any person from the requisites of this order.

\*This Order has been repealed. Vide Legal Notice 42 of 2023.
