# Repealing Regulations (S.L. 465.43)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 465.43**

**REPEALING REGULATIONS**

5th June, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 232 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Repealing Regulations.

## 2. Revocation. Cap. 465.

The orders and regulations listed hereunder, issued under the Public Health Act, are hereby being repealed:

*S.L. 465.21.*

(a) Closure of Places Open to the Public Order;

*S.L. 465. 24.*

(b) Enforcement of the Order relating to Closure of Places Open to the Public Regulations;

*S.L. 465.27.*

(c) Closure of Non-Essential Retail Outlets and Outlets Providing Non-essential Services Order;

*S.L. 465.28.*

(d) Enforcement of the order relating to Closure of Non-Essential Retail Outlets and Outlets Providing Non- Essential Services Regulations;

*S.L. 465.33.*

(e) Protection of Vulnerable Persons Order;

*S.L. 465.41.*

(f) Number of Persons in Public Spaces (Increase in Number of Persons) Order; and

*S.L. 465.38.*

(g) Closure of the Local Tribunals Order:

Provided that:

(a) any legal, judicial or administrative time-limit for the filing of any acts before the local tribunals, suspended by the above order hereby repealed, shall remain so suspended for a period of twenty (20) days from the date of coming into force of these regulations;

(b) any service of summons, suspended by the above Order hereby repealed, shall remain so suspended until a period of ten (10) days from the date of coming into force of these regulations. 3. Any act, decision or action taken before the coming into force of the Repealing Regulations, 2020 shall remain to be regulated by provisions of the regulations and orders as in force prior to the coming into force of the Repealing Regulations, 2020.

*Transitory provision.*

*L.N. 232 of 2020.*
