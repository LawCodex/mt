# Repealing Regulations (S.L. 465.70)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 465.70**

**REPEALING REGULATIONS**

10th April 2022

LEGAL NOTICE 115 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Repealing Regulations.

## 2. Revocation. Cap. 465.

The regulations listed hereunder, issued under the Public Health Act, are hereby being repealed:

*L.S. 465.51.*

(a) Number of Persons in Public Spaces Regulations; and

*L.S. 465.69.*

(b) Valid Certificate of Recognised Vaccination Against COVID-19 Requirement Regulations.

## 3. Transitory provision. L.N. 115 of 2022.

Any act, decision or action taken before the coming into force of the Repealing Regulations, 2022 shall continue to be regulated by the provisions of the regulations referred to in regulation 2 of these regulations as in force prior to the coming into force of the Repealing Regulations, 2022.
