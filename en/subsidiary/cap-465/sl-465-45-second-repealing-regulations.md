# Second Repealing Regulations (S.L. 465.45)

SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 465.45

SECOND REPEALING REGULATIONS

30th June 2020 LEGAL NOTICE 243 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Second Repealing Regulations.

## 2. Revocation.

The following orders and regulations, issued under the Public Health Act are hereby being repealed:

*S.L. 465.14.*

(i) Closure of Schools Order;

*S.L. 465.16.*

(ii) Epidemics and Infectious Disease (Suspension of legal times relating to promise of sale agreements, notarial and related matters) Order;

*S.L. 465.17.*

(iii) Epidemics and Infectious Disease (Suspension of Legal and Judicial times) Order;

*S.L. 465.20.*

(iv) Suspension of Legal Times relating to Promise of Sale Agreements, Notarial and other related matters (Epidemics and Infectious Disease) Order;

*S.L. 465.22.*

(v) Closure of Schools (Extension of Period of Closure) Order;

*S.L. 465.31*

(vi) Enforcement of the order relating to Suspension of Organised Events Regulations:

Provided that any act, decision or action taken before the coming into force of the Second Repealing Regulations, 2020 shall remain to be regulated by the Enforcement of the Order Relating to Suspension of Organized Events Regulations, 2020 as in force prior to the coming into force of the Second Repealing Regulations, 2020;

*S.L. 465.32.*

(vii) Suspension of Organised Events Order;

*S.L. 465.34*

(viii) Number of Persons in Public Spaces Order;

*S.L. 465.35*

(ix) Enforcement of the order relating to the Number of Persons in Public Spaces Regulations:

Provided that any act, decision or action taken before the coming into force of the Second Repealing Regulations, 2020 shall remain to be regulated by the Enforcement of the Order Relating to the number of persons in public spaces Regulations, 2020 as in force prior to the coming into force of the Second Repealing Regulations, 2020;

*S.L. 465.36.*

(x) Declaration of a Public Health Emergency Order.
