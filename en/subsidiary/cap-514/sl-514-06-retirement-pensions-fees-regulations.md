# Retirement Pensions (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 514.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 514.06**

RETIREMENT PENSIONS (FEES) REGULATIONS\* 1st January, 2015†

LEGAL NOTICE 10 of 2015.

## 1. Citation.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Retirement Pensions (Fees) Regulations.

*L.N. 64 of 2014.*

(2) Nothing in these regulations shall be deemed to affect the liability in respect of any fees due under the Special Funds (Fees) Regulations, 2014, prior to the coming into force of these regulations.

## 2. Interpretation. Cap. 514.

Any reference in these regulations to "the Act" is a reference to the Retirement Pensions Act, and the provisions of article 2 of the Act shall also apply to these regulations.

## 3. Payment of application and annual supervisory fees.

There shall be paid to the competent authority by any service provider, retirement scheme or retirement fund:

(a) upon submission of an application for the granting of a licence or recognition, irrespective of whether the application is eventually accepted or not, the application fee specified in the Second Column of the Schedule;

(b) on the date of the granting of a licence or recognition, the annual supervisory fee specified in the Third Column of the said Schedule;

(c) thereafter annually, upon the anniversary of such date as is mentioned in paragraph (b), the same annual supervisory fee established in the Third Column of the said Schedule.

## 4. Non-refundable fees.

None of the fees established as due in terms of these regulations shall be refundable or prorated.

\*These regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 371 of 2024. †see regulation 1(2) of these Regulations, as originally promulgated.
