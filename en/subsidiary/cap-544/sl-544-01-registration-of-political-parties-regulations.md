# Registration of Political Parties Regulations (S.L. 544.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 544.01**

**REGISTRATION OF POLITICAL PARTIES**

**REGULATIONS**

27th May, 2016 LEGAL NOTICE 204 of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Registration of Political Parties Regulations.

## 2. Form of application for the registration of a Political Party. Cap. 544

The application for the registration of a political party in terms of article 14 of the Financing of Political Parties Act shall be presented to the Electoral Commission in the form stipulated in the Schedule.
