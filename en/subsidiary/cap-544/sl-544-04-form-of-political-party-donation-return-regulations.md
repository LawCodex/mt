# Form of Political Party Donation Return Regulations (S.L. 544.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 544.04**

**FORM OF POLITICAL PARTY DONATION**

**RETURN REGULATIONS**

21st March, 2017 LEGAL NOTICE 92 of 2017.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Form of Political Party Donation Return Regulations.

## 2. Form of Political Party Donation Return. Cap. 544.

The Form of Political Party Donation Return, prepared by political parties in accordance with article 38 of the Financing of Political Parties Act shall be presented to the Electoral Commission in the form stipulated in the Schedule to these regulations.
