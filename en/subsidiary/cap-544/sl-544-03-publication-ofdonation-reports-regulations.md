# Publication ofDonation Reports Regulations (S.L. 544.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 544.03**

**PUBLICATION OF DONATION**

**REPORTS REGULATIONS**

21st March, 2017 LEGAL NOTICE 91 of 2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Publication of Donation Reports Regulations.

## 2. Format and time limit for publication.

(1) Donation reports received from political parties shall be made accessible to the public by the Electoral Commission and shall be posted on the website of the said Commission within thirty days from when they are received by the Commission.

(2) The donation reports referred to in sub-regulation (1) shall be made accessible to the public and shall be published on the website of the Commission either in the format in which they were received or in any format which is established from time to time by regulations for the filing of such reports.
