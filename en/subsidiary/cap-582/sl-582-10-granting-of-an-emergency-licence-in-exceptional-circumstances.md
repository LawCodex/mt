# Granting of an Emergency Licence in Exceptional Circumstances Regulations (S.L. 582.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 582.10**

**GRANTING OF AN EMERGENCY LICENCE IN**

**EXCEPTIONAL CIRCUMSTANCES REGULATIONS**

17th March, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 74 of 2020, as amended by Legal Notice 127 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Granting of an Emergency Licence in Exceptional Circumstances Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 582.*

"Act" means the Social Care Standards Authority Act;

"Authority" means the Social Care Standards Authority as established by virtue of article 5 of the Act;

"Chief Executive Officer" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act.

"emergency licence" means a licence issued by the Authority in exceptional circumstances for the provision of a social welfare service as defined in article 2 of the Act including for a service being provided in a particular facility or/and on a number of beds;

"exceptional circumstances" mean circumstances which justify the suspension of procedures which otherwise would have been followed, thus ensuring the mitigation of a number of difficulties.

## 3. Issuance of an emergency licence.

(1) The Authority may, at any time it deems necessary, grant an emergency licence in exceptional circumstances in order to provide a social welfare service as defined in article 2 of the Act including for a service being provided in a particular facility or/ and on a number of beds.

(2) When an emergency licence is issued, such licence may contain a number of conditions set by the Authority.

## 4. Validity of an emergency licence.

*Amended by: L.N. 127 of 2021.*

(1) The emergency licence shall only be issued for a maximum period of six (6) months but can be withdrawn at any time in the event that the exceptional circumstances cease to exist or for any other reason deemed necessary at the discretion of the Chief Executive Officer of the Authority.

(2) The emergency licence may be renewed at the discretion of the Chief Executive Officer of the Authority for such further period or periods as may be deemed necessary with any period renewed not exceeding six (6) months.
