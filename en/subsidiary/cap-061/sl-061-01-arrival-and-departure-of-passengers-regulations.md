# Arrival and Departure of Passengers Regulations (S.L. 61.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 61.01**

**ARRIVAL AND DEPARTURE OF PASSENGERS**

**REGULATIONS**

27th August, 1935 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 331 of 1935.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Arrival and Departure of Passengers Regulations.

## 2. Arrival of passengers.

Any alien arriving in these Islands as a passenger shall present his passport to the Police on board the ship and obtain a landing card. The landing card shall be shown to the Police at the gangway. Before landing, a British subject shall show his passport to the Police, at the gangway.

## 3. Passengers to show passport.

Any passenger irrespective of nationality shall show his passport to the Police at the Custom House. Aliens shall surrender their landing cards to the Police and British subjects shall have their names checked with the passengers list.

## 4. Aliens to obtain landing cards from Police.

Aliens in transit wishing to land in Malta are also required to obtain landing cards from the Police on board the ship.

## 5. Departure of passengers.

Passengers proceeding on board a ship for departure shall pass through the passport office at the Custom House and have their passports stamped with a departure stamp.

On arrival at the ship passengers shall present their passports to the Police at the top of the gangway.
