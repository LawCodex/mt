# Host Family Accommodation Regulations (S.L. 409.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 409.10**

**HOST FAMILY ACCOMMODATION**

**REGULATIONS\***

31st May, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 130 of 2002, as amended by Act XXIX of 2019.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Host Family Accommodation Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: XXIX.2019.71.*

(1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires -

*Cap. 409.*

"Act" means the Malta Travel and Tourism Services Act;

*S.L. 409.09*

"allocation agreement" has the meaning as assigned to it in the Travel Operators and Organised Excursion Operators Regulations;

"incoming tourism operation" means an operation by an incoming tourism agent and by a destination management company, and "incoming tourism operator" shall be construed accordingly;

*Cap. 605.*

"educational institution"refers to an institution as referred to in article 49 of the Education Act;

"host family" means the person in whose name the licence for the accommodation of tourists has been issued and who resides in any building, howsoever described, not being a hotel, guest house, hostel or holiday premises, which is normally used for the permanent or temporary habitation of the licensee and is used or intended to be used for the concurrent habitation of guests;

(2) Words and expressions used in these regulations and which are also used in the Act shall, unless the context otherwise requires, have the same meaning as in the Act.

## 3. Licence conditions.

(1) The Authority shall not issue a licence to a host family unless it is satisfied that:

(a) the house where the guests are to be accommodated is the residence, for the time being, of the applicant and is suitable for the concurrent habitation of guests and is not already so licensed; and

(b) the applicant and his household are of good character and are fit to receive and provide accommodation to guests in their residence.

*Cap. 327. S.L. 327.65*

(2) An applicant wishing to provide accommodation to guests registered to attend a school licensed under the Education Act and the National Minimum Conditions (Schools Teaching English as a Foreign Language) Regulations, or any other educational institution shall produce -

\*These regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 92 of 2026.

(a) documentary evidence that he has an allocation agreement with a licensed school or educational institution and that he and his household are certified as possessing a good command of the English language by a licensed school or educational institution recognised by the Ministry responsible for Education;

*Plan of premises.*

(b) a floor sketch plan together with a detailed description of:

(i) the room or rooms and their respective measurements intended for use as bedrooms by the guests and the number of beds in each bedroom; and

(ii) the bathroom or bathrooms and respective measurements intended to be used by guests; and (c) a declaration that the accommodation complies with the standard requirements as indicated in the Schedule jointly signed by the applicant and a representative of a school or educational institution.

## 4. Rooms used for the accommodation of guests.

(1) The licensee shall not use any other rooms for the accommodation of guests except those indicated in the sketch plan submitted with the application.

(2) The number of guest rooms shall not exceed four, and the number of guests, at any time, shall not exceed four:

Provided that the Authority may determine a lesser number of rooms or beds to be licensed if, in its opinion, the premises are not suitable to accommodate the maximum number of guests concurrently with the number of persons living in the house.

## 5. Declaration by applicant.

(1) The applicant shall submit a valid police conduct certificate of himself and a declaration that neither he nor any member of his household have been convicted for drug related offences or for crimes against the good order of families.

(2) The applicant shall grant the Authority the right to have access to his police record and to that of the members of his household.

## 6. Responsibilitiesfor guests staying with licensed host families.

(1) An incoming tourism operator or any other person entrusted with making the accommodation arrangements or, in the case of guests following a course of studies organised by a school or other educational institution, the Head of School or the educational institution responsible for the students’ non-academic arrangements, shall ensure that:

(a) such guests are accommodated only with host families licensed for the purpose; and

(b) where the guest is following a course of studies, the host family providing the accommodation is contracted with the school or educational institution as the case may be.

*S.L. 327.65*

(2) The provisions of Part C entitled "Non-Academic Matters" of the National Minimum Conditions (Schools Teaching English as a Foreign Language) Regulations shall, mutatis mutandis, apply to any person or body corporate that allocates guests to a host family house.

(3) Any authorised person including representations of a person, including licensed schools or educational institutions, may carry out inspections of the premises of a host family contracted with them in order to ensure compliance with the provisions of the Act and these regulations.

(4) A person holding a host family licence shall not prevent a duly authorised representative of the Authority or of the licensed school or of the educational institution from carrying out at any reasonable times, interviews and random inspections of the house in order to ensure compliance with the provisions of the Act and with these regulations.

(5) No representative of a licensed school shall give false or misleading information to the Authority about the applicant and the licensed premises.

## 7. Responsibilities of host family towards guests of minor age.

(1) An applicant who wishes to accommodate guests of minor age shall be deemed to recognise the need for and shall be bound to a higher level of care towards minors and their general well-being during their stay and shall provide an environment within the host family consonant with the age of the minor.

(2) The responsibility of the applicant in this respect shall include, but shall not be limited to -

(a) giving access at all times to the premises where such accommodation is being provided;

(b) the provision of appropriate meals to any minor accommodated in the premises.

## 8. Cancellation of licence. S.L. 327.65

The Authority may suspend or withdraw a licence as provided for in article 46 of the Act, and also if so recommended by the Monitoring Board established under the National Minimum Conditions (Schools Teaching English as a Foreign Language) Regulations.

## 9. Standard requirements for a house to qualify for a licence.

The standard requirements for a house to qualify for a host family license shall be those indicated in the Schedule.

## 10. Offences and penalties.

Any person who fails to comply with any of the provisions of these regulations shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to the fines and penalties as indicated in articles 43 and 45 of the Act.
