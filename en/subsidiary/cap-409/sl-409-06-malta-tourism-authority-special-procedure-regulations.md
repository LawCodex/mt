# Malta Tourism Authority (Special Procedure) Regulations (S.L. 409.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 409.06**

**MALTA TOURISM AUTHORITY (SPECIAL**

**PROCEDURE) REGULATIONS**

31st May, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 126 of 2002, as amended by Legal Notices 185 of 2002 and 426 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Malta Tourism Authority (Special Procedure) Regulations.

## 2. Special procedure. Cap. 409.

For the purposes of article 45 of the Malta Travel and Tourism Services Act, hereinafter referred to as "the Act", the fines that may be imposed for the offences shown in the First Column of the Schedule shall be those shown in the Second Column of the said Schedule.

## 3. Interpretation.

(1) In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires:

"certificate of classification" means the certificate issued by the Authority certifying the classification assigned to the hotel, guest house, hostel, holiday premises or catering establishment;

"register" means the record of guests kept by the keeper of a hotel, guest house, hostel and holiday premises which show the name and other particulars of every guest staying in the hostel, guest house, hostel or holiday premises;

(2) Words and expressions used in these regulations and which are also used in the Act, shall have the same meaning as in the Act. Substituted by: SCHEDULE
