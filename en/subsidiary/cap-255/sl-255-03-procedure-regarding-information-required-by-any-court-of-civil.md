## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Procedure regarding Information required by any Court of Civil Jurisdiction from the Ecclesiastical Tribunal Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation. Cap. 255.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, "Act" means the Marriage Act.

## 3. Procedure.

Where, in the execution of the provisions of the Act, any court of civil jurisdiction requires any clarification, further information or documents from the Ecclesiastical Tribunal, it shall make a decree in which it shall specify the clarification, further information or documents that it requires, and it shall order the Registrar of Courts and Tribunals to notify the same decree to the Holy See through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
