# Age-Classification of Cinematograph Films (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 542.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 542.03**

**AGE-CLASSIFICATION OF CINEMATOGRAPH**

**FILMS (FEES) REGULATIONS**

29th November, 2013 LEGAL NOTICE 427 of 2013.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Age-Classification of Cinematograph Films (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

For the purposes of these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, "Film Board" means the Film Board established by regulation 3 of the Cinema and Stage Age-Classification Regulations.

*S.L. 542.01*

## 3. Fees.

(1) The fees leviable by the Film Board, shall be at the following rates:

(a) five euro (€5) per film trailer; and

(b) one hundred euro (€100) per film.

*S.L. 542.01*

(2) Such fees shall include the issuing of the age-classification certificate provided for in the Cinema and Stage Age-Classification Regulations:

Provided that for each duplicate age-classification certificate, the Film Board shall levy an additional fee of one euro (€1).

(3) No fees shall be levied for the issue of any age-classification certificate, or of any duplicate thereof in respect of any cinematograph film imported into Malta by a diplomatic mission accredited to the Government of Malta for exhibition to the public, free of charge, at any place whether such place be licensed or not under the Cinema and Stage Age-Classification Regulations:

*S.L. 542.01*

Provided that such age-classification certificate, issued free of charge, shall not be valid for the purposes of the cinematographic film to which it refers, if such film is to be subsequently exhibited to the public against payment.
