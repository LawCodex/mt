# Prohibition of Tobacco Cultivation Order (S.L. 106.01)

SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 106.01

PROHIBITION OF TOBACCO CULTIVATION

ORDER

31st October, 1939 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 560 of 1939.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Prohibition of Tobacco Cultivation Order.

## 2. Prohibition.

It is hereby prohibited to cultivate tobacco in these Islands.
