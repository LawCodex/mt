# Types of Internal Audits Regulations (S.L. 461.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 461.02**

TYPES OF INTERNAL AUDITS REGULATIONS 8th October, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 434 of 2004.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Types of Internal Audits Regulations.

## 2. Types of internal audits.

The different types of internal audits carried out by the Directorate are the following:

(a) Financial audits;

(b) Compliance audits;

(c) Performance/Operational audits;

(d) Comprehensive audits;

(e) Value-for-money audits;

(f) Information Technology/Information Systems audits;

(g) Management audits;

(h) Program audits;

(i) European Union Joint audits;

(j) European Union Related audits;

(k) Follow-up audits;

(l) Referencing;

(m) any other types of internal audits and related assignments as may be required from time to time at the discretion of the Director.
