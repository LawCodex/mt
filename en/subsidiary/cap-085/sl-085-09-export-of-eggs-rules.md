# Export of Eggs Rules (S.L. 85.09)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 85.09**

**EXPORT OF EGGS RULES\***

6th October, 1967 LEGAL NOTICE 61 of 1967, as amended by Legal Notice 408 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is Export of Eggs Rules.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires - "Board" means the Board of Reference constituted under rule 10 of these Rules;

"Department" means the Department of Agriculture;

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Agriculture;

"Ordinance" means the Agricultural Produce (Export) Ordinance. (2) Words not otherwise defined in these Rules shall have the same meaning as is assigned to them in the Ordinance.

## 3. Notice to Director.

Any person who intends to export eggs to any country shall, not later than twenty-four hours before the proposed time of export, give to the Director a written notice in the form set out in the First Schedule:

Provided that -

(a) the Director may, at his discretion, accept such shorter notice as he may deem reasonable, and

(b) the exporter may present for inspection and eventual exportation a smaller quantity than that shown in the notice.

## 4. Eggs to be presented for inspection.

(1) Eggs in respect of which notice has been given in accordance with the provisions of rule 3 shall be presented for inspection by an inspector at the Agricultural Produce Inspection Shed at Marsa or at such other place as the Director may specify.

(2) The Director may also specify the time at which any such eggs are to be presented for inspection.

## 5. Containers.

(1) Eggs intended for export and presented for inspection shall be packed in standard containers of a capacity of 30 dozen eggs.

(2) All the eggs in any such container shall be of the same grade.

## 6. Marking of containers.

(1) On each container there shall be printed in clearly legible letters -

(a) the words "PRODUCE OF MALTA";

(b) the name of the exporter; and

(c) the grade of the eggs in accordance with the Second Schedule.

\*These Rules have been repealed by Act XXXIV of 2023.

(2) The letters of the words specified in paragraph (a) of sub-rule (1) of this rule shall be printed in not less than five centimetres in height.

## 7. Condition of eggs.

Eggs presented for inspection prior to export shall be in accordance with one of the grades specified in the Second Schedule, and shall be clean, fresh and free from any cracks, blood or meat spots, and shall be marked by the relative grade number indicated in the said Schedule.

## 8. Approval of consignment.

When an inspector has satisfied himself that a consignment of eggs conforms in all respects to the provisions of these Rules, he shall mark each container thereof with the words "PASSED FOR EXPORT" and shall provide the exporter with a certificate in the form set out in the Third Schedule.

## 9. Consignment not passed for export.

If an inspector is not satisfied that a consignment of eggs, or any part thereof, conforms in all respects to these Rules, he shall not place any mark on any of the containers not approved by him but shall provide the exporter with a notice in the form set out in the Fourth Schedule, and the exporter shall, if the inspection was carried out on premises occupied by the Government, remove forthwith any such containers therefrom unless he gives the inspector notice of reference to the Board.

## 10. Board of Reference.

(1) The Board of Reference, referred to in article 18 of the Ordinance, shall be composed of the Director, or an officer of the Department acting on behalf of the Director, as Chairman and two other members selected from a panel of ten persons engaged in the agricultural produce export trade.

(2) Such panel shall be appointed yearly by the Minister.

(3) The two members of the Board, other than the Chairman, shall be selected one by the Chairman and one by the complainant.

(4) No panel member shall sit on the Board in connection with any eggs in respect of which he shall have any direct or indirect interest.

## 11. Fees for inspection.

*Amended by: L.N. 408 of 2007.*

There shall be charged in respect of an inspection of any eggs intended for export a fee of two cents (0.02) per case of 360 eggs.

## 12. Fee for reference to Board.

*Amended by: L.N. 408 of 2007.*

Any exporter who wants to refer to the Board a decision given by an inspector shall deposit with the inspector a fee at the rate of twelve cents (0.12) per case of eggs, which deposit shall in no case be less than six euros and ninety-nine cents (6.99).
