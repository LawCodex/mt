# Eggs declared to be Agricultural Produce Order (S.L. 85.8)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 85.08**

**EGGS DECLARED TO BE AGRICULTURAL**

**PRODUCE ORDER\***

6th, October, 1967 LEGAL NOTICE 60 of 1967.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Eggs declared to be Agricultural Produce Order.

## 2. Agricultural produce. Cap. 85.

Eggs are to be considered as agricultural produce for the purposes of the Agricultural Produce (Export) Ordinance.

\*This Order has been repealed by Act XXXIV of 2023.
