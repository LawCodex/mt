# Tomatoes declared to be Agricultural Produce Order (S.L. 85.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 85.06**

**TOMATOES DECLARED TO BE AGRICULTURAL**

**PRODUCE ORDER\***

23rd March, 1967 LEGAL NOTICE 23 of 1967.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Tomatoes declared to be Agricultural Produce Order.

## 2. Agricultural produce. Cap. 85.

Tomatoes are to be considered as agricultural produce for the purposes of the Agricultural Produce (Export) Ordinance.

\*This Order has been repealed by Act XXXIV of 2023.
