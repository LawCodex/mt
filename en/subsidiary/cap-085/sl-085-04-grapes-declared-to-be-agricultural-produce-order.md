# Grapes declared to be Agricultural Produce Order (S.L. 85.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 85.04**

**GRAPES DECLARED TO BE AGRICULTURAL**

**PRODUCE ORDER\***

15th January, 1960 LEGAL NOTICE 2 of 1960.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Grapes declared to be Agricultural Produce Order.

## 2. Agricultural produce. Cap. 85.

Grapes are to be considered as agricultural produce for the purposes of the Agricultural Produce (Export) Ordinance.

\*This Order has been repealed by Act XXXIV of 2023.
