# Duty on Documents and Transfers Order (S.L. 364.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 364.10**

DUTY ON DOCUMENTS AND TRANSFERS ORDER 26th November, 2002;

1st January, 2004

LEGAL NOTICES 349 of 2002 and 113 of 2004, consolidated, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Duty on Documents and Transfers Order.

## 2. Value of concessionary rate.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

The concessionary rate of three euro and fifty cents (3.50) per one hundred euro or part thereof shall apply on the value of the first sixty-nine thousand and eight hundred and eighty-one euro and twenty cents (69,881.20) in respect of acquisition of immovable property for the purpose of establishing therein or constructing thereon the sole ordinary residence.

## 3. Concessionary rate under article 40 of the Duty on Documents and Transfers Act.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

On the acquisition of immovable property by an emphyteutical grant for a period exceeding fifty years the duty chargeable under the Duty on Documents and Transfers Act on the first three thousand and four hundred and ninety-four euro and six cents (3,494.06) of the yearly groundrent shall be reckoned at fifty per centum of the rates of duty applicable.

## 4. Exemption of divisions. Cap. 364.

No duty otherwise chargeable with duty under the provisions of the Duty on Documents and Transfers Act shall be chargeable on any deed of declaration consequent to division of companies where the draft terms of division had been forwarded to the Registrar of Companies on or prior to the 24th November, 2003 and the Registrar had published the relevant statement in the Government Gazette in terms of the provisions of the Companies Act, on or prior to the 31st December, 2003 provided a copy of the relative publication is attached to the deed of division.

*Cap. 386.*
