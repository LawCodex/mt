# Duty on Transfers by SportMalta (Exemption) Order (S.L. 364.20)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 364.20**

**DUTY ON TRANSFERS BY SPORTMALTA**

**(EXEMPTION) ORDER**

10th May, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 110 of 2024.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Duty on Transfers by SportMalta (Exemption) Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 364.*

"Act" means the Duty on Documents and Transfers Act;

* Cap. 455*

"designated property" shall have the same meaning as assigned to in article 2 of the Sports Act;

* Cap. 455.*

"SportMalta" means the body established by article 4 of the Sports Act and includes an entity established under that Act that was the predecessor or is the successor in title of SportMalta;

* Cap. 455.*

"sports association" means an association that falls within the definition of "club" as defined in the Sports Act.

## 3. Exemption.

Without prejudice to the other provisions of this order, the duty otherwise chargeable in accordance with the Act, on a transfer to which this order applies, shall not be levied.

## 4. Applicability of order.

This order shall apply to any transfer consisting of the grant of temporary emphyteusis over a designated property that has been made or shall be made by SportMalta to a sports association.
