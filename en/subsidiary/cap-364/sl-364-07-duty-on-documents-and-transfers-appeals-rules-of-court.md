# Duty on Documents and Transfers Appeals Rules of Court (S.L. 364.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 364.07**

**DUTY ON DOCUMENTS AND TRANSFERS**

**APPEALS RULES OF COURT**

5th August, 1994

LEGAL NOTICE 86 of 1994, as amended by Legal Notice 248 of 2009.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these rules is the Duty on Documents and Transfers Appeals Rules of Court.

## 2. Right of reply. Cap. 364.

(1) When an appeal is entered within the period established in article 59 of the Duty on Documents and Transfers Act, hereinafter referred to as "the Act", the opposing party shall have the right of a reply within fifteen days from the date of the notification of the act by which the appeal is made. No security for costs shall be required when the appeal is made in accordance with the provisions of article 58 of the Act.

(2) The Court shall set the appeal for hearing by not later than one month immediately following the expiration of the period of fifteen days allowed for the reply to the appeal.

## 3. Access to acts, documents.

*Amended by: L.N. 248 of 2009.*

All acts and documents received by the Court of Appeal in connection with any appeal from any decision by the said Tribunal or Board, as the case may be, in terms of article 59 of the Act shall be accessible only to the parties concerned and to their counsel and legal procurators, and such access shall be had only in the presence of the deputy registrar of the said Court. Only the said parties and their counsel and legal procurators shall have the right to obtain copies of the said acts, documents and judgement.

## 4. Keeping of acts or documents and custody of records.

The Registrar of Courts shall keep the record of the appeal in his custody to ensure the secrecy of the said record. On the conclusion of the proceedings the acts shall be bound and kept in the custody of the Registrar.

## 5. Service of acts.

All notifications required in such appeals shall always be effected by the court usher and the copies of the acts so notified shall be placed in an envelope sealed by the Registrar.

## 6. Judgements.

(1) The President of the Court of Appeal shall sign the Court judgement which shall be immediately deposited with the Registrar who shall then thereby conclude and bind the records of the proceedings.

(2) The Registrar shall, within four days from such deposit, inform the parties of such fact.

## 7. Applicability of Code of Organization and Civil Procedure. Cap. 12.

The provisions of the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure and the general rules of the Court shall apply unless these rules provide otherwise.
