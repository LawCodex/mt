# Financial Services Tribunal (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 330.08)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 330.08**

**FINANCIAL SERVICES TRIBUNAL (FEES)**

**REGULATIONS**

20th January, 2012 LEGAL NOTICE 10 of 2012, as amended by Legal Notice 62 of 2012.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Financial Services Tribunal (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Authority" means the Malta Financial Services Authority established in terms of article 3 of the Malta Financial Services Authority Act.

*Cap. 330.*

## 3. Fees payable upon the filing of an appeal.

*Amended by: L.N. 62 of 2012. Cap. 330.*

Upon the filing of an appeal to the Financial Services Tribunal established in terms of article 21 of the Malta Financial Services Authority Act there shall be paid to the Financial Services Tribunal an administrative fee as follows:

(a) a fee of two hundred and fifty euro (€250) upon an appeal from a decision of the Authority to issue a fine or other administrative penalty not exceeding two thousand and five hundred euro (€2500); and

(b) a fee of five hundred euro (€500) upon an appeal from any other decision of the Authority.

## 4. Fee payable for transfer of long-term insurance business. Cap. 403.

Upon the filing of an application for the transfer of long-term insurance business in accordance with article 57 of the Insurance Business Act, there shall be paid to the Financial Services Tribunal a fee of one thousand and five hundred euro (€1500).

## 5. Payment.

An appeal application or an application for transfer of long-term business, as applicable, filed before the Financial Services Tribunal shall not be considered unless it is accompanied by the applicable administrative fee in terms of these regulations.

## 6. Applicability.

The fees prescribed in terms of these regulations shall apply to appeals and applications for transfer of long-term business filed before the Financial Services Tribunal on or after the date of entry into force of these regulations.
