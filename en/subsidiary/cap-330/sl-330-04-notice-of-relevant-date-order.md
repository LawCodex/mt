# Notice of Relevant Date Order (S.L. 330.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 330.04**

**NOTICE OF RELEVANT DATE ORDER**

16th September, 1994 LEGAL NOTICE 115 of 1994.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Notice of Relevant Date Order.

## 2. Relevant date. Cap. 330.

The 23rd day of September, 1994 shall be the relevant date for the purposes of articles 58, 59 and 60 of the Malta Financial Services Authority Act.
