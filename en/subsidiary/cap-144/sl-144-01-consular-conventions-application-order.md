# Consular Conventions (Application) Order (S.L. 144.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 144.01**

**CONSULAR CONVENTIONS (APPLICATION)**

**ORDER**

30th January, 1956

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 69 of 1956.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Consular Conventions (Application) Order.

## 2. Applicability of articles. Cap. 144.

Articles 3 and 5 of the Consular Conventions Act shall apply in respect of France, Greece, Mexico, Norway, Sweden and the United States of America.
