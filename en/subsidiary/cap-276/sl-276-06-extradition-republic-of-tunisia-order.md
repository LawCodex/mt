# Extradition (Republic of Tunisia) Order (S.L. 276.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 276.06**

**EXTRADITION (REPUBLIC OF TUNISIA) ORDER**

\*

LEGAL NOTICE 374 of 2007, as amended by Legal Notice 46 of 2010.

## 1. Citation and commencement.

(1) The title of this Order is the Extradition (Republic of Tunisia) Order.

(2) This Order shall enter into force on such day as the Minister shall order in the Gazette after the Treaty enters into force in accordance with the terms of the said Treaty.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 46 of 2010.*

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires -

"the Minister" means the Minister responsible for justice;

*Cap. 276.*

"the relevant Act" means the Extradition Act;

"the relevant date" shall mean the date the Order enters into force;

"the requested country" means the country receiving the request for extradition;

"the requesting country" means the country making the request for extradition;

"the Treaty" means the Treaty between between the Government of Malta and the Government of the Republic of Tunisia signed on the 14th December 2006, in Malta, the terms of which are set out in the relative Treaty published in the Government Gazette dated the 13th November 2007.

## 3. Designation for the purposes of article 6 of the relevant Act.

For purposes of article 6 of the Extradition Act, the Minister has designated the Republic of Tunisia.

## 4. Relationship between the Order and the relevant Act and transitory provisions.

The provisions of the relevant Act, save where otherwise expressly indicated, shall apply to requests received or made by Malta on or after the relevant date for the return of a fugitive criminal to or from the Republic of Tunisia in pursuance of a request made under this Order, and the provisions of the relevant Act shall have effect in relation to the return under this Order of persons to, or in relation to persons returned under this Order from, the Republic of Tunisia subject to such conditions, exceptions, adaptations or modifications as are specified in this Order.

## 5. Amends article 8 of the relevant Act.

For the purposes of this Order, article 8(1)(a) of the relevant Act shall apply as if for the words "under that law" therein there were substituted the words "under Maltese law and under that law".

\*Not yet in force.

## 6. Extradition for lesser offences.

*Substituted by: L.N. 46 of 2010.*

If the request for extradition concerns several distinct offences, each offence being punishable by the requesting and the requested countries' laws by imprisonment, in the case that some offences do not meet the condition relative to the sentence's duration laid down in article 8(1)(a) of the relevant Act, extradition may also be granted for the latter offences.

## 7. Amends article 10 of the relevant Act.

*Amended by: L.N. 46 of 2010.*

For purposes of this Order, article 10 of the relevant Act shall be amended as follows:

(a) subarticle (1) of article 10 shall be substituted by the following:

"(1) A person shall not be returned under this Act to any country, or committed to or kept in custody for the purposes of such return, if it appears to the Minister or to the court of committal -

(a) that the offence of which that person is accused or was convicted is an offence of a political character or an act related to such an offence; or

(b) that the request for his return (though purporting to be made on account of an extraditable offence) is in fact made for the purpose of prosecuting or punishing him on account of his race, place of origin, nationality, political opinions, colour, religion or creed; or

(c) that he might, if returned, be prejudiced at his trial or punished, detained or restricted in his personal liberty by reason of his race, place of origin, nationality, political opinions, colour, religion or creed; or

(d) when there are serious reasons to believe that if returned, he might not enjoy the guarantees inherent in a fair trial; or

(e) that the offence of which that person is accused or was convicted was committed on Maltese territory";

(b) immediately after sub-article (3) of the relevant Act there shall be added the following:

(3A) When the description of the offence charged is altered in the course of proceedings, a person shall not be returned under this Act to any country, or committed to or kept in custody for the purpose of such return, unless provision is made by the law of that country, or by an arrangement made with that country, that the extradited person will only be dealt with insofar as the offence, under its new description, is shown to be an extraditable offence and is based on the same facts contained in the extradition request.".

## 16. Undertaking by Minister

Where the Minister has given an undertaking in connection to the temporary surrender of a person to Malta, and the undertaking includes terms that the person be kept in custody until the conclusion of any proceedings against him in Malta for an offence, a court may grant bail to the person in the proceedings only if it considers that there are exceptional circumstances which justify it.
