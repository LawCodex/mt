# Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order (S.L. 276.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 276.01**

EXTRADITION (DESIGNATED COMMONWEALTH COUNTRIES) ORDER

1st February, 1982

LEGAL NOTICE 12 of 1982.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order.

## 2. Designated Commonwealth Countries. Cap. 276.

The countries specified in the Schedule are hereby specified for the purposes of article 4 of the Extradition Act.

Canada

The Republic of Cyprus

The Gambia

Jamaica

New Zealand

Singapore
