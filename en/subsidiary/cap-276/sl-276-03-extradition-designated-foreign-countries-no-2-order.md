# Extradition (Designated Foreign Countries) (No. 2) Order (S.L. 276.3)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 276.03**

**EXTRADITION (DESIGNATED FOREIGN**

**COUNTRIES) (NO. 2) ORDER\***

17th June, 1996

LEGAL NOTICES 131 of 1996 and 31 of 2001, consolidated, as amended by Legal Notice 367 of 2010.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Extradition (Designated Foreign Countries) (No. 2) Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires -

"the Convention" means the European Convention on Extradition (hereinafter referred to as "the Convention") opened for signature in Paris on 13th December, 1957 and the terms of which are set out only in the relevant convention published in the Government Gazette dated 28th May, 1996;

*Cap. 276.*

"the relevant Act" means the Extradition Act;

"the relevant date" means the 17th June, 1996;

"scheduled countries" shall have the same meaning assigned to it by article 3(1).

## 3. Scheduled countries.

(1) Those countries listed in the Schedule (hereinafter referred to as "scheduled countries") are hereby being designated as foreign countries for the purposes of article 6 of the relevant Act and any arrangement with any such countries applicable to Malta before the relevant date by virtue of the provisions of article 30 of the said Act shall be deemed to have been revoked with effect from the relevant date.

*S.L. 276.02*

(2) The provisions of the Extradition (Designated Foreign Countries) Order, in so far as applicable to any scheduled country of before the relevant date and to the extent that they are inconsistent with the provisions of this Order, shall with effect from the relevant date no longer apply to any such country.

(3) Saving the provisions of subarticle (4), any scheduled country which before the relevant date was a designated Commonwealth country for the purposes of article 4 of the relevant Act shall on the relevant date be deemed to be no longer so designated.

(4) Notwithstanding anything contained in this Order, a scheduled country to which subarticle (3) applies shall, in respect of a territory for the external relations of which it is responsible, not being a territory listed in the Schedule, continue to be deemed a designated Commonwealth country and article 4(3) of the relevant Act shall be construed accordingly.

\*but see the Extradition (Designated Foreign Countries) Order - S.L. 276.05
