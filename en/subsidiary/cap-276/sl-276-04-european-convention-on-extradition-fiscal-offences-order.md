# European Convention on Extradition (Fiscal Offences) Order (S.L. 276.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 276.04**

**EUROPEAN CONVENTION ON EXTRADITION**

**(FISCAL OFFENCES) ORDER\***

18th February, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 32 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 368 of 2010.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the European Convention on Extradition (Fiscal Offences) Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires -

*S.L.276.03*

"the Convention" shall have the same meaning assigned to it by article 2 of the Extradition (Designated Foreign Countries) (No. 2) Order;

"the Protocol" means the Second Additional Protocol to the European Convention on Extradition done at Strasbourg on the 17th March, 1978, and the terms of which are set out in the relevant Protocol published in the Gazette dated 23rd February, 2001.

## 3. Effect of Extradition (Designated Foreign Countries) (No. 2) Order.

*S.L.276.03*

The Extradition (Designated Foreign Countries) (No. 2) Order shall have effect as regards a country listed in the Schedule with the provision set out in Article 2 of the Protocol in place of Article 5 of the Convention.

\*but see the Extradition (Designated Foreign Countries) Order - S.L. 276.05
