# Vesting Day regarding United Kingdom Lands Notice (S.L. 193.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 193.01**

**VESTING DAY REGARDING UNITED KINGDOM**

**LANDS NOTICE**

1st January, 1972

LEGAL NOTICE 66 of 1969.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Notice is Vesting Day regarding United Kingdom Lands Notice.

## 2. Department or authority of the Government of the United Kingdom. Cap. 193.

The Ministry of Public Building and Works of the Government of the United Kingdom and Northern Ireland is designated, for the purposes of article 3 of the United Kingdom Lands (Vesting) Act, a department or authority of the said Government and of any other provision thereof.
