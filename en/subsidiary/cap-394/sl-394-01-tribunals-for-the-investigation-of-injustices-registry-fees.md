# Tribunals for the Investigation of Injustices (Registry Fees) Regulations (S.L. 394.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 394.01**

**TRIBUNALS FOR THE INVESTIGATION OF**

**INJUSTICES (REGISTRY FEES) REGULATIONS**

16th May, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 64 of 1997, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Tribunal for the Investigation of Injustices (Registry Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Registry fees to be levied.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

There shall be levied by the Registrar of the Tribunal for the Investigation of Injustices the following fees:

\1. For the filing of the application ...................... €23.29 2. For the filing of the reply ................................ €23.29 3. For every photocopy, for every page of the

original .......................................................... 12 cents.
