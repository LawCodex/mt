# Purchase of Copies of Tapes (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 284.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 284.01**

**PURCHASE OF COPIES OF TAPES (FEES)**

**REGULATIONS**

1st December, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 193 of 1995, as amended by Legal Notice 423 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Purchase of Copies of Tapes (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Fee for the purchase of copies of tapes.

*Amended by: L.N. 423 of 2007.*

A fee of six euros and ninety-nine cents (6.99) shall be payable to the Registrar of Courts for the purchase of every copy of an original of a tape on which proceedings have been recorded by any electro-magnetic means, irrespective of the duration of such tape.
