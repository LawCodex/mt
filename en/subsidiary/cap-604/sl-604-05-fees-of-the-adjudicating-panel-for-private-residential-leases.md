# Fees of the Adjudicating Panel for Private Residential Leases Regulations (S.L. 604.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 604.05**

FEES OF THE ADJUDICATING PANEL FOR PRIVATE RESIDENTIAL LEASES REGULATIONS

26th March, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 105 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is Fees of the Adjudicating Panel for Private Residential Leases Regulations.

## 2. Registry fees.

The registry fees established in Tariff A annexed to these regulations shall be paid together with the filing of the relative act of procedure.

## 3. Registry fees payable to the Adjudicating Panel.

Any counter-claim contained in any act of procedure shall be treated as if it is a new claim and the fees set out in paragraphs (a) to (d) of Tariff A shall be levied on the counter claim and the reply thereto.

## 4.

The fee established in Tariff B annexed to these regulations shall be paid to the executive officer for every notification of an act of procedure, including expenses incurred in the execution of such notification:

Provided that if service is to be effected outside normal working hours, the fee shall be increased by a 100%.

## 5.

(1) All fees due shall be paid together with the filing of the relative act and the Registrar shall not accept the filing any act of procedure, which is not accompanied by the relative fee.

(2) The assessment of the Registrar on the amount of fees to be paid shall be final.
