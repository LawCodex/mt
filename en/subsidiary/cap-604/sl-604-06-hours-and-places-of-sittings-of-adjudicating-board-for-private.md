# Hours and Places of Sittings of Adjudicating Board for Private Residential Leases Regulations (S.L. 604.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 604.06**

**HOURS AND PLACES OF SITTINGS OF**

**ADJUDICATING BOARD FOR PRIVATE**

**RESIDENTIAL LEASES REGULATIONS**

26th March, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 106 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Hours and Places of Sittings of Adjudicating Board for Private Residential Leases Regulations.

## 2. Sittings.

The sittings of the Adjudicating Panel for private residential leases shall be held at the edifice of the Housing Authority in Floriana.
