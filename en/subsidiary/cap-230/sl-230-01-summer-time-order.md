# Summer Time Order (S.L. 230.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 230.01**

**SUMMER TIME ORDER**

6th April, 2001;

11th July, 2006;

6th March, 2012 LEGAL NOTICES 76 of 2001, 150 of 2006 and 84 of 2012, consolidated.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Summer Time Order.

## 2. Period of summer time.

(1) In the years 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005 and 2006, the period of summer time shall be the period beginning at two o’clock in the morning, local time, of the last Sunday in March and ending at three o’clock in the morning, local summer time, of the last Sunday in October of the same years.

(2) In the years 2007 to 2011 inclusive, the period of summer time shall begin at two o’clock in the morning, local time, of the last Sunday in March and end at three o’clock in the morning, local summer time, of the last Sunday in October of the same years, as follows:

**in 2007: Sunday 25 March and Sunday 28 October**

**in 2008: Sunday 30 March and Sunday 26 October**

**in 2009: Sunday 29 March and Sunday 25 October**

**in 2010: Sunday 28 March and Sunday 31 October**

**in 2011: Sunday 27 March and Sunday 30 October.**

(3) In the years 2012 to 2016, both inclusive, the period of summer time shall begin at two o’clock in the morning, local time, of the last Sunday in March and end at three o’clock in the morning, local summer time, of the last Sunday in October of the same years, as follows:
