# Summer Time Order (S.L. 230.3)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 230.03**

**SUMMER TIME ORDER**

17th March, 2022

LEGAL NOTICE 111 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Summer Time Order.

## 2. Period of summer time.

In the years 2022 to 2026 both inclusive, the period of summer time shall begin at two o’clock in the morning, local time, of the last Sunday in March and end at three o’clock in the morning, local summer time of the last Sunday in October of the same years, as follows:

**in 2022: Sunday 27th March and Sunday 30th October**

**in 2023: Sunday 26th March and Sunday 29th October**

**in 2024: Sunday 31st March and Sunday 27th October**

**in 2025: Sunday 30th March and Sunday 26th October**

**in 2026: Sunday 29th March and Sunday 25th October**
