# Summer Time Order (S.L. 230.2)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 230.02**

**SUMMER TIME ORDER**

23rd March, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 84 of 2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Summer Time Order.

## 2. Period of summer time.

In the years 2017 to 2021 inclusive, the period of summertime shall begin at two o’clock in the morning, local time, of the last Sunday in March and end at three o’clock in the morning, local summer time of the last Sunday in October of the same years, as follows:

in 2017: Sunday 26th March and Sunday 29th October in 2018: Sunday 25th March and Sunday 28th October in 2019: Sunday 31st March and Sunday 27th October in 2020: Sunday 29th March and Sunday 25th October in 2021: Sunday 28th March and Sunday 31th October.
