# Establishment of Sanctions and Penalties Regulations (S.L. 500.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 500.02**

**ESTABLISHMENT OF SANCTIONS**

**AND PENALTIES REGULATIONS**

26th November, 2010

LEGAL NOTICE 496 of 2010, as amended by Act XVI of 2019.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Establishment of Sanctions and Penalties Regulations.

## 2. Definitions.

*Amended by: XVI.2019.72.*

(1) For the purpose of these regulations:

*Cap. 500.*

"the Act" means the Services (Internal Market) Act;

"the body responsible for consumer protection" means the Director of Consumer Affairs designated as such in terms of article 3(1) of the Consumer Affairs Act;

*S.L. 12.19.*

"Civil Court" means the Civil Court (Commercial Section) established by article 3 of the Civil Court (Establishment of Sections) Order;

"offender" means the provider who discriminates against recipients or potential recipients of a service in terms of article 9(a) of the Act or who does not comply with the obligations under article 10(1) of the Act.

(2) Words, terms and expressions used in these regulations and which are also used in the Act, shall have, unless the context otherwise requires, the same meaning as in the Act.

## 3. Investigations, judicial proceedings and penalties. S.L. 500.01

*Substituted by: XVI.2019.72.*

In the carrying out of its functions under article 10(2) of the Act and under regulation 7 of the Designation of Various Entities Regulations, the body responsible for consumer protection shall, as it deems fit and as far as applicable, with regard to investigations, judicial proceedings and requests for the imposition of penalties, follow the procedure established in articles 12 and 12A of the Consumer Affairs Act and in Parts IIA, IIB, XII and XIIA of the Consumer Affairs Act:

*Cap. 378.*

Provided that subject to the provisions of the Consumer Affairs Act, the Civil Court shall impose penalties of not less than four hundred seventy euro (€470) and not more than forty-seven thousand euro (€47,000) on any person who fails to comply with any of the provisions of these regulations.

## 4. Right of appeal.

*Substituted by: XVI.2019.72.*

Any person who feels aggrieved by a judgement, order, penalty or measure issued by the Civil Court, may appeal before the Court of Appeal, on any point of law and, or of fact, by means of an application filed in the registry of the Court of Appeal within twenty days from the date of the judgment of the Civil Court:

*Cap. 378.*

Provided that the Court of Appeal shall appoint the case for hearing by not later than six months from the judgement of the Civil Court.
