# Recognition of the Malta Institute of Accountants Regulations (S.L. 281.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 281.06**

**RECOGNITION OF THE MALTA INSTITUTE OF**

**ACCOUNTANTS REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2020 LEGAL NOTICE 70 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Recognition of the Malta Institute of Accountants Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 281.*

"the Act" means the Accountancy Profession Act; and

"the Institute" means the Malta Institute of Accountants. 3. (1) The Minister for finance hereby recognises the Malta Institute of Accountants as an approved accountancy body for accountants in Malta.

*Recognition of approved accountancy body.*

(2) Such recognition shall be for a period of four (4) years.
