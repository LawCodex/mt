# Judicial Sales Rules of Court (S.L. 12.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 12.01**

**JUDICIAL SALES RULES OF COURT**

5th November, 1884 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 108 of 1894.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is Judicial Sales Rules of Court. 2. Whenever, with reference to a sale by auction, the court shall not have expressly dispensed with the publication, in periodical papers, of the notice referred to in articles 312, 313 and 314 of the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure, or, on ordering that publication, it shall not have pointed out the paper through which it shall be made, the Registrar shall cause the notice to be inserted only in the Government Gazette, whether that notice be signed by himself, or, in the case provided for in article 348, by an auctioner.

*Publication of notice in the Government Gazette. Cap. 12.*
