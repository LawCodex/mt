# Superior Courts (Number of Judges) Order (S.L. 12.33)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 12.33**

SUPERIOR COURTS (NUMBER OF JUDGES) ORDER\* 11th October, 2022

LEGAL NOTICE 245 of 2022.

IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred by sub-article (2) of article 6 of the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure, the President of Malta has prescribed the number of judges of the Superior Courts, besides the Chief Justice, to be twenty-six (26).

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 160 of 2023.
