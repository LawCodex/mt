# Number of Judges in the Superior Courts Order (S.L. 12.15)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 12.15**

NUMBER OF JUDGES IN THE SUPERIOR COURTS ORDER\*

25th March, 2014

LEGAL NOTICE 91 of 2014.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Number of Judges in the Superior Courts Order.

## 2. Number of judges in the superior courts.

The number of judges in the superior courts, besides the Chief Justice, shall be twenty-four.

\*This Order has been repealed by Legal Notice 246 of 2022.
