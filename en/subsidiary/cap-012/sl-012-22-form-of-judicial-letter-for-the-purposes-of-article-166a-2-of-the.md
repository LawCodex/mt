# Form of Judicial Letter for the purposes of article 166A(2) of the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure Regulation (S.L. 12.22)

FORM OF JUDICIAL LETTER FOR THE

PURPOSES OF ARTICLE 166A(2) OF THE CODE

*1*

OF ORGANIZATION AND CIVIL PROCEDURE [S.L.12.22

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 12.22**

FORM OF JUDICIAL LETTER FOR THE PURPOSES OF ARTICLE 166A(2) OF THE CODE OF

**ORGANIZATION AND CIVIL PROCEDURE**

**REGULATIONS**

1st August, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 278 of 2005.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Form of Judicial Letter for the purposes of article 166A(2) of the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure Regulations.

## 2. Notice to be given in the judicial letter.

In any judicial letter relating to article 166A, the following notice shall be printed in clear and legible letters immediately under the Court heading:

"This judicial letter is being sent under the provisions of article 166A of Chapter 12, and if you do not reply within thirty (30) days, the letter shall constitute an executive title. For this reason it would be in your interest to consult a lawyer or a legal procurator.".
