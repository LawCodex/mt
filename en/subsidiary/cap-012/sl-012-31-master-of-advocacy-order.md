# Master of Advocacy Order (S.L. 12.31)

**SUBISIDARY LEGISLATION 12.31**

**MASTER OF ADVOCACY**

6th March, 2015

LEGAL NOTICE 70 of 2015.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Master of Advocacy Order. 2. For the purposes of, and in terms of, paragraph of article81(c) of the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure, the degree of Master of Advocacy (M. Adv.) approved by the Senate of the University of Malta as required by article 81(2) of the Education Act and offered by the Faculty of Laws of the University of Malta, as published in the Gazette by the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Master of Advocacy - M.Adv. - and for the Degree of Master of Notarial Studies- M.Not.St. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Laws, is hereby prescribed as the other qualification at master’s level in terms of article 81(c) of the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure which entitles a holder thereof to sit for the examination to obtain a warrant to practice as an advocate.

*Holders of M. Adv. degree may sit for Advocate warrant examination. Cap. 12. Cap. 327. S.L. 327.508*
