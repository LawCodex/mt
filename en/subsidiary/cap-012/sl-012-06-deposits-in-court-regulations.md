# Deposits in Court Regulations (S.L. 12.6)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 12.06**

**DEPOSITS IN COURT REGULATIONS**

29th January, 1973

LEGAL NOTICE 2 of 1973, as amended by: Legal Notice 148 of 1975;

Acts XXXIX of 1976 and VIII of 1990; and Legal Notice 131 of 2014.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Deposits in Court Regulations.

## 2. Deposits in superior courts.

*Amended by: L.N. 148 of 1975; XXXIX. 1976.8; L.N. 131 of 2014.*

(1) Where any money being legal tender in Malta, is to be deposited pursuant to a schedule of deposit filed in the superior courts, the money is to be presented to the cashier in the registry of the said courts.

(2) Where any money, not being legal tender in Malta, or any gold or silver articles or any pearls or precious stones are to be deposited pursuant to a schedule of deposit filed in the said courts, they shall be placed in a safe place as directed by the Registrar Civil Courts and Tribunals or, as the case may be, at the Central Bank of Malta:

Provided that, if such a deposit is made after noon of a Saturday or of any day during the period from the sixteenth day of June of any year to the thirtieth day of September of the same year, both days included, the above money or articles shall be deposited in the registry of the said court and shall be transferred by the registrar to the appropriate place as provided for in this regulation on the next following working day.

## 3. Deposits in Courts of Magistrates.

*Amended by: XXXIX. 1976.8. VIII. 1990.3; L.N. 131 of 2014.*

Where any money, being legal tender in Malta or not, or any other effects are to be deposited pursuant to a schedule of deposit filed in the Court of Magistrates (Malta) or in the Court of Magistrates (Gozo), the provisions of regulation 2 shall apply, provided, however, that when the schedule of deposit is filed in the Courts of Magistrates (Gozo) the reference in the said regulation to the appropriate place as provided for in the said regulation shall be construed as a reference to the place as directed by the Director General Courts, Gozo, or, as the case may be, to the Central Bank of Malta.

## 4. Governor of the Central Bank to hold regular deposits of moneys not legally current in Malta, etc.

*Added by: L.N. 131 of 2014.*

The Governor of the Central Bank shall be the person who shall hold regular deposits of moneys not legally current in Malta, gold or silver articles, pearls or precious stones.

## 5. Property to be held in safe place.

*Added by: L.N. 131 of 2014.*

The property referred to in regulation 4 shall be held in a safe place and shall not be used by any person or released to any person except as directed by the Registrar Civil Courts and Tribunals.
