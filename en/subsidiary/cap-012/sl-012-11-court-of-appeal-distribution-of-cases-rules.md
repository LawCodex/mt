# Court of Appeal (Distribution of Cases) Rules (S.L. 12.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 12.11**

**COURT OF APPEAL (DISTRIBUTION OF CASES)**

**RULES**

29th April, 1994

LEGAL NOTICE 51 of 1994,as amended by Legal Notices 301 of 2018 and 33 of 2023.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is Court of Appeal (Distribution of Cases) Rules.

## 2. Distribution between the First, Second and Fourth Chambers.

*Substituted: L.N. 301 of 2018; L.N. 11 of 2023.*

The cases to be heard by the First, the Second and the Fourth Chambers of the Court of Appeal shall be distributed in as much as possible equal manner amongst those chambers unless ordered otherwise by the Chief Justice or, with regard to a particular case or cases, by one of the chambers of the Court of Appeal.

## 3. The Third Chamber.

*Added by: L.N. 33 of 2023.*

The Third Chamber of the Court of Appeal shall continue hearing all cases pending before it at the time of the coming into force of this rule and it shall also hear appeals from judgments or decrees delivered by the Chief Justice.
