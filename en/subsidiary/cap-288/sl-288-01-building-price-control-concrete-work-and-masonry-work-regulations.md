**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 288.01**

BUILDING (PRICE CONTROL) (CONCRETE WORK AND MASONRY WORK) REGULATIONS

12th August, 1980

LEGAL NOTICE 72 of 1980, as amended by Legal Notice 423 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Building (Price Control) (Concrete Work and Masonry Work) Regulations.

## 2. Establishment of price for certain works.

The prices for works specified in the first column of the Schedule shall be those established in the second column thereof. 2 [S.L.288.01 (CONCRETE WORK AND MASONRY WORK)

Reinforced concrete suspended slab; 0.15m thick,

## 2.

Masonry Work:

(a) Single Walls - Soft stone ashlar in lime

mortar in single walls 0.15m to 0.23m thick

fair faced both sides and cut to uniform height €9.32 per m2

(b) Double Walls - Soft stone ashlar in double

walls from 0.375m to 0.6m thick in lime

mortar consisting of inner and outer skin not

less than 0.15m thick well bonded together,

fair faced both sides and cut to uniform height €17.47 per m2

(c) Foundations in Masonry Work -

(i) for single walls .................................... €8.39 per m2

(ii) for double walls ................................... €15.72 per m2

Notes:

(1) The above prices apply to buildings of a height up to two storeys and include the stairwell leading to the roof thereof, parapet wall surrounding the roof, ventilators and plain projections, but do not include ornamental work such as moulded cornices and projections, moulded facias and sills, corbels, arches and balustrades.

For works above 1st floor level the above rates shall be increased by the following rates:

at 2nd floor level ....................................................... 10%

at 3rd floor level ........................................................ 15%

at 4th and 5th floor level ............................................ 171/2%

(2) All openings not exceeding 1.2m in width shall not be deducted in the calculation of areas. No charge shall be made for lintels, jambs and arrises thereof.

(3) For openings wider than 1.2m which have lintels, jambs and arrises, only the width exceeding 1.2m shall be deducted in the calculation of areas. A charge shall only be made for the lintel in excess of 1.2m.
