# Fees for Air Operators Certificates Regulations (S.L. 218.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 218.01**

**FEES FOR AIR OPERATORS’ CERTIFICATES**

**REGULATIONS**

27th December, 1991

LEGAL NOTICE 191 of 1991, as amended by Legal Notices 338 of 2002, 298 of 2003, 411 of 2007 and 291 of 2016 and Act XL of 2023.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Fees for Air Operators’ Certificates Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: Act XL of 2023.*

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires -

*Cap. 641.*

"air operator’s certificate" has the same meaning assigned to it by the Air Navigation Act;

"Director" means the Director of Civil Aviation;

*Cap. 641.*

Where any law makes references to provisions of the Air Navigation Order which were not reproduced in the Air Navigation Act, or any Act substituting the same, the matter shall be regulated by means of a regulatory instrument issued by the Director General.

## 3. Fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 338 of 2002.*

The fees established in the Schedule shall be payable by an applicant to the Director for:

*Substituted by: L.N. 291 of 2016.*

(a) the grant of an air operator’s certificate;

(b) the variation of an air operator’s certificate;

(c) the oversight of the operations of the holder of the air operator’s certificate.
