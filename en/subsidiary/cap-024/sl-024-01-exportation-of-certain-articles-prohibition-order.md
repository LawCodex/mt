# Exportation of Certain Articles (Prohibition) Order (S.L. 24.01)

SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 24.01

EXPORTATION OF CERTAIN ARTICLES

(PROHIBITION) ORDER

5th August, 1921

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 217 of 1921.

## 1.

The title of this Order is Exportation of Certain Articles Title.

(Prohibition) Order.

## 2.

The exportation of the following articles shall be Prohibition of exportation. prohibited to all destinations abroad:

ammunition;

cocaine;

explosives, other than industrial explosives;

firearms;

opium;

silver coin.
