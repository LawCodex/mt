# Unions of Equivalent Status to Civil Unions Regulations (S.L. 530.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 530.01**

**UNIONS OF EQUIVALENT STATUS TO**

**CIVIL UNIONS REGULATIONS**

20th June, 2014

LEGAL NOTICE 209 of 2014, as amended by Legal Notice 223 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Unions of Equivalent Status to Civil Unions Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 530.*

"the Act" means the Civil Unions Act;

"the Minister" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it by the Act.

## 3. List of unions of equivalent status to civil unions.

The list shown in the Schedule contains those civil unions which, in the opinion of the Minister, are unions of equivalent status to civil unions as provided for in the Act.
