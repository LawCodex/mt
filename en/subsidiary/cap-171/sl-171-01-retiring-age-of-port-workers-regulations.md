# Retiring Age of Port Workers Regulations (S.L. 171.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 171.01**

**RETIRING AGE OF PORT WORKERS**

**REGULATIONS**

1st January, 1992

LEGAL NOTICE 33 of 1974.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Retiring Age of Port Workers Regulations.

## 2. Retiring age.

As from the dates shown in the first column of the Schedule, port workers shall retire from port work when they attain the relative age shown in the second column of the said Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

(Regulation 2)

**Retiring Age**

First Column Second Column

**Date Age**

The fourth day of April, 1974 ........ 62

The fourth day of April, 1975 ........ 61

The fourth day of April, 1976 ........ Such age at which persons employed with the Government are retired from the public service
