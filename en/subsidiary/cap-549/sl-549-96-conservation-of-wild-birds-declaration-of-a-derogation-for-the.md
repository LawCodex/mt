# Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of a Derogation for the Control of Sturnus Vulgaris and Columba Livia for the Period of 2015–2016) Regulations (S.L. 549.96)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.96**

**CONSERVATION OF WILD BIRDS (DECLARATION**

*Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2020.*

**OF A DEROGATION FOR THE CONTROL OF**

STURNUS VULGARIS AND COLUMBA LIVIA FOR THE PERIOD OF 2015–2016)

10th July, 2015

LEGAL NOTICE 213 of 2015, as amended by Legal Notice 348 of 2020. 1. The title of these regulations is Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of a Derogation for the Control of Sturnus Vulgaris and Columba Livia for the Period of 2015–2016) Regulations.

*Citation. Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2020.*

## 2. Interpretation.

Terms and expressions used in these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, shall have the same meaning as given in the Conservation of Wild Birds Regulations, hereinafter referred to as "the Wild Birds Regulations".

*S.L.549.42*

## 3. Control restriction.

This derogation only applies for the control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia.

## 4. Establishment of control period.

For the purposes of the second proviso to regulation 10(6) of the Wild Birds Regulations and for the purposes of these regulations, the period for the control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia shall be from 1st September 2015 to the 31st March 2016, both days included.

## 5. Control according to set framework. S.L. 549.85

For the purposes of these regulations, the control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia shall be in accordance with the provisions of the framework laid down by the Conservation of Wild Birds (Framework for Allowing a Derogation for the Control of Birds in the Interest of Air Safety) Regulations and the Wild Birds Regulations.
