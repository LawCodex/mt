# Conservation of Wild Birds Regulations (S.L. 549.42)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.42**

CONSERVATION OF WILD BIRDS REGULATIONS 29th March, 2006 LEGAL NOTICE 79 of 2006, as amended by Legal Notices 39, 280 and 426 of 2007, 30 of 2009, 283 of 2010, 82 and 421 of 2011, 267 and 426 of 2012, 341 and 403 of 2013, 110 and 252 of 2014, 281 of 2015 69 of 2016\* 368 of 2018, 367 of 2020, 86, 107 and 255 of 2022, 150 and 209 of 2023, 299 of 2024 and 251 of 2025.

### 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Conservation of Wild Birds Regulations.

### 2. Definitions.

*Amended by: L.N. 39 of 2007; L.N. 280 of 2007; L.N. 267 of 2012; L.N. 341 of 2013; L.N. 403 of 2013; L.N. 252 of 2014; L.N. 281 of 2015; L.N. 69 of 2016; L.N. 367 of 2020; L.N. 150 of 2023; L.N. 209 of 2023. L.N. 251 of 2025. Cap. 549.*

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"the Act" means the Environment Protection Act;

"bird" means any species of naturally occurring avifauna in the wild state in the European territory of the Member States of the European Union, with the exception of Greenland;

"bird ringing scheme" means an accredited program or a scientific institution responsible for the training of bird ringers, the management of a bird-ringing database, and the facilitation of data sharing to support research, conservation, and population monitoring efforts;

"bird sanctuaries" means the areas listed in Schedule V;

"captive bred" means a bird which is hatched and bred from legally acquired parental stock in a human-controlled environment and which:

(a) in the case of a European territory bird is fitted with a closed ring or, in exceptional circumstances where the Wild Birds Regulation Unit is satisfied that the physical or behavioural properties of the bird prevent the use of a closed ring, is fitted with a uniquely numbered, unalterable microchip transponder conforming to ISO Standards 11784: 1996 (E) and 11785: 1996 (E);

(b) in the case of a non-European territory bird belonging to any of the species listed in Annex A of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97 of 9 December 1996 on the protection of species of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein, is marked in accordance with Article 66 of Commission Regulation (EC) No 865/ 2006 of 4 May 2006 laying down detailed rules concerning the implementation of Council Regulation (EC) No 338/97, and in accordance with any other applicable requirements pursuant to the Trade in Species of Fauna and Flora Regulations;

"Non-European territory bird" or "Non-European territory birds" means any bird species which, although naturally occurring in the wild state in any territory outside the territory of the Member States of the European Union, does not naturally occur in any territory of the Member States of the European Union;

"the Minister" means the minister responsible for the environment;

*S.L. 549.44*

"scientific bird-ringer" means a person who is licensed by the Wild Birds Regulation Unit to perform scientific bird ringing and geo-tagging activities in accordance with regulation 23;

"scientific bird ringing" means a research method based on the individual marking of wild birds with a view to providing data on species’ survival rates, migration, movement and dispersal patterns, population dynamics and ecological processes for conservation purposes, which involves:

(a) the capture and handling of wild birds using nets and/ or pre-recorded bird calls;

(b) the attachment of an individually marked scientific ring on a wild bird to enable individual identification and/or the attachment of a geo-tag to record its precise movements;

(c) the taking of various biometric and morphometric measurements such as size, weight, conditions of feather moult and indications of age and sex of the specimen;

(d) the recording of the scientific ring identification number and biometric information through a special scientific database accessible to scientific bird-ringers and researchers in other regions or countries; and

(e) the immediate release of the birds caught back into the wild after performing the necessary measurements and attachment of scientific rings and/or geo-tags, or after recording the necessary information from bird specimens already fitted with a scientific ring and/or geo-tag;

"to capture" means to take;

"to take" and "taking" means live-capturing of wild birds, in accordance with the provisions of these regulations;

"Wild Birds Regulation Unit" shall mean the Wild Birds Regulation Unit established within the Ministry responsible for the environment.

## PART I

### 3. Special conservation measures.

*Amended by: L.N. 39 of 2007.*

(1) The bird species referred to in Schedule I shall be the subject of special conservation measures concerning their habitat including in particular the designation of special protection areas.

(2) For the purposes of sub-regulation (1), account shall be taken of -

(a) species in danger of extinction;

(b) species vulnerable to specific changes in their habitat;

(c) species considered rare because of small populations or restricted local distribution;

(d) other species requiring particular attention for reasons of the specific nature of their habitat.

(3) Similar measures shall also be established for regularly occurring migratory species not listed in Schedule I, as regards their breeding, moulting and wintering areas and staging posts along their migration routes, bearing in mind their need for protection in the geographical sea and land area of Malta.

(4) The Ornis Committee shall make recommendations to the Minister on the adoption of measures under this regulation in accordance with the provisions of regulation 10(6)(i).

(5) The Minister shall, by notice in the Gazette, publish the sites classified as special protection areas in accordance with the provisions of this regulation, together with the maps delineating these areas.

### 4. Prohibitions.

*Amended by: L.N. 39 of 2007; L.N. 280 of 2007; L.N. 341 of 2013; L.N. 281 of 2015; L.N. 69 of 2016; L.N. 367 of 2020; L.N. 107 of 2022.*

(1) All species of naturally occurring wild birds are protected. Without prejudice to the provisions of regulations 5, 8 and 9, no person shall:

(a) hunt or attempt to hunt, take or attempt to take any bird which is protected under these regulations, other than in accordance with these regulations and with the conditions of any licence granted thereunder;

(b) hunt or attempt to hunt, take or attempt to take, destroy, damage or remove by any means, any bird nest and eggs;

(c) take the eggs in the wild of any bird which is protected under these regulations and keep these eggs even if empty;

(d) deliberately disturb any bird which is protected under these regulations during the period of breeding and rearing;

(e) be in possession or in control of or keep any specimen of any bird not listed in Schedule II and/or any specimen of any Non-European territory bird, whether alive or dead, or any recognisable parts or derivatives thereof, unless he proves that such specimen:

(i) has been declared with the Malta Environment and Planning Authority or its predecessor by the 31st May 2003 and such person is in possession of the permits, or certificates of registration by the Malta Environment and Planning Authority or its predecessor or by the Wild Birds Regulation Unit to keep such bird in accordance with regulation 21; or

(ii) is lawfully imported in accordance with regulation 6(1)(a) or 6(1)(b); or

(iii) is taken in a Member State where it was lawfully hunted or captured or otherwise lawfully acquired under the terms of Directive 2009/147/ EC of the European Parliament and of the Council on the Conservation of Wild Birds and under the legislation of that Member State; or (iv) is captive bred:

Provided that such person shall retain in his possession and for such time as he remains in possession of the specimen as provided for in paragraph (e) all licences, permits, certificates and any other documents that prove to the satisfaction of the Wild Birds Regulation Unit that such specimen was not obtained in contravention of these regulations:

Provided further that the prohibitions provided for in paragraph (e) shall not apply where the person in possession of the specimen is the Director of Veterinary Services or any other person authorized by the Director of Veterinary Services and where such possession is required for the rehabilitation, veterinary or other treatment or culling of the specimen on veterinary grounds.

(2) Deleted by Legal Notice 107 of 2022.

### 5. Hunting restrictions.

*Amended by: L.N. 39 of 2007.*

(1) Without prejudice to the provisions of regulation 9, no person shall hunt or attempt to hunt any birds other than those referred to in Schedule II:

Provided that such hunting, including falconry if practiced, as carried out in accordance with the provisions of these regulations shall be subject to any conditions and rules as may be established pursuant to regulation 10(6), in particular paragraph (a) thereof, and shall be in accordance with the principles of wise use and ecologically balanced control of the species of birds which are referred to in Schedule II and be compatible with the requirement of maintaining the population of these species, in particular as regards migratory species:

Provided further that there shall be no hunting of birds which are referred to in Schedule II during the rearing season or during the various stages of reproduction of these species.

(2) As regards migratory species which are referred to in Schedule II, there shall also be no hunting of such species during their period of reproduction or during their return to their rearing grounds.

(3) No person shall:

(a) hunt on land any bird other than those listed in Part A of Schedule II; or

(b) hunt at sea any bird other than those listed in Part B of Schedule II.

### 6. Import etc. restriction.

*Amended by: L.N. 280 of 2007. Substituted by: L.N. 281 of 2015.*

(1) Without prejudice to the provisions of regulation 9 and any other legislation, no person shall:

(a) import or export any bird specimen, whether alive or dead, or any part or derivative thereof, unless it was legally acquired in its country of origin or is captive bred, and in both cases the export of which has been authorised by the competent authorities responsible for the species’ protection in the country of origin;

(b) import or export any Non-European territory bird specimen whether alive or dead, or any part or derivative thereof unless it was legally acquired in its country of origin or is captive bred, and in all cases the export of which has been authorised by the competent authority responsible for the species’ protection in the country of origin;

(c) sell, transport for sale, keep for sale and offer for sale any live or dead specimen of a Non-European territory bird or any part or derivative thereof unless he proves that such specimen was lawfully imported in accordance with the provisions of these regulations or was captive bred;

(d) sell, transport for sale, keep for sale and offer for sale any live specimen of a bird not listed in Schedule III (a) and III (b) unless such bird is captive bred;

(e) sell, transport for sale, keep for sale and offer for sale any dead bird specimen or any part or derivative thereof of birds not listed in Schedule III (a) and III (b) unless such bird is captive bred;

(f) sell, transport for sale, keep for sale and offer for sale any live or dead bird specimen or any part or derivative thereof of birds listed in Schedule III (a) unless such birds have been lawfully killed or captured or otherwise legally acquired;

(g) sell, transport for sale, keep for sale and offer for sale any live or dead bird specimen or any part or derivative thereof of birds listed in Schedule III (b) unless authorized in accordance with the provisions of regulation 10 and such birds have been lawfully killed or captured or otherwise legally acquired.

(2) Following the granting of any authorization pursuant to sub-regulation (1)(g), the Committee established under regulation 10 shall verify at regular intervals that the conditions governing the granting of such authorization continue to be fulfilled and shall make recommendations, as necessary, to the Minister in connection therewith.

(3) Whosoever is in possession of any specimen of any bird or any Non-European territory bird, whether alive or dead, or part of any specimen, pursuant to sub-regulation (1) shall retain in his possession and for such time as he remains in possession of the specimen, as applicable, all permits, certificates and any other documents that prove to the satisfaction of the Wild Birds Regulation Unit that such specimen was not obtained in contravention of these regulations.

(4) The importation or exportation of species of birds and non- European territory birds shall be in accordance with the provisions of these regulations and without prejudice to any other existing laws and regulations.

### 7. Banned methods.

*Amended by: L.N. 39 of 2007; L.N. 368 of 2018; L.N. 367 of 2020.*

(1) Without prejudice to the provisions of regulations 8 and 9, no person shall hunt or attempt to hunt, take or attempt to take any bird by making use or be in possession of any of the following methods:

(a) snares, limes including birdlime, poisonous or stupefying bait, hooks or any similar substances or methods;

(b) any electrocuting devices;

(c) any artificial light sources, mirrors, devices for illuminating targets or any night vision devices comprising an electronic image magnifier or image converter;

(d) any gas, or any electric or electronic device, especially the use of pre-recorded bird calls;

(e) nets, including those known as ranja, mist-nets, and any type of vertical net;

(f) any kind of cage-trap, whether with a net or not;

(g) decoy live birds which are blind or mutilated;

(h) decoy live birds held by any means except those held by cotton string and swivel;

(i) decoys of protected birds;

(j) explosives; and

(k) semi-automatic or automatic weapons with a magazine capable of holding more than two rounds of ammunition:

Provided that the prohibition in sub-regulation (1) shall be without prejudice to the provisions of regulation 23(2)(f):

Provided further that the prohibition in paragraph (d) referring to electric or electronic devices shall not apply to battery-operated artificial bird decoys including motorised rotating decoys, flapping decoys and those that mimic bird movement, including flight:

Provided further that such artificial bird decoys shall not include or incorporate within or with them any pre-recorded bird calls or any other method prohibited under this regulation.

(2) No person shall sell, offer for sale, exhibit for sale or keep any device the use of which, is prohibited by sub-regulation (1):

Provided that, subject to the other provisions of these regulations and subject also to the provisions of any law for the time being in force in Malta, in as far as any device referred to in sub-regulation (1) may be used for purposes other than those to which these regulations refer, the same, offering for sale, exhibition for sale, or keeping of any such device shall not be prohibited solely on the basis of this regulation.

### 8. Live-capturing.

*Amended by: L.N. 280 of 2007; L.N. 341 of 2013.*

(1) Any live-capturing of wild birds by any means whatsoever is prohibited except by virtue of derogations authorised in terms of regulation 9.

*Substituted by: L.N. 252 of 2014. Amended by: L.N. 281 of 2015; L.N. 367 of 2020.*

(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of regulation 4(1)(a) and (e) and regulation 7, and without prejudice to the provisions of regulation 9, certain birds may be deliberately captured in accordance with the parameters established by the relevant framework regulations pertaining to live-capturing derogations and under the terms of any special live-capturing licences issued thereunder:

Provided that the licensee shall only use traditional manually-operated selective clap-nets, consisting of two parallel nets spread flat on the ground which capture birds by clapping over each other on the ground, and which are armed and operated solely by human intervention without the use of any external power means including electrical, electronic, or mechanical means:

Provided further that such live-capturing may only take place in live-capturing sites within the area clearly outlined on a site plan which is approved for that purpose by the Wild Birds Regulation Unit and registered with the Commissioner of Police, and which contains only one (1) live-capturing station per licence holder:

Provided further that copies of the site plans approved by the Wild Birds Regulation Unit may be given, or the registration or changes to the approved site plans can be effected, against an administrative fee prescribed in Schedule X.

### 9. Derogations.

*Amended by: L.N. 341 of 2013; L.N. 281 of 2015; L.N. 86 of 2022.*

(1) Derogations from the provisions of regulations 4, 5, 6, 7, 18 and 21 may be authorized where there is no other satisfactory solution, for the following reasons:

(a) in the interests of public health and safety;

(b) in the interests of air safety;

(c) to prevent serious damage to crops, livestock, forests, fisheries and water;

(d) for the protection of flora and fauna;

(e) for the purposes of research and teaching, of re-population, of re-introduction and for the breeding necessary for these purposes;

(f) to permit, under strictly supervised conditions and on a selective basis, the capture, keeping or other judicious use of certain birds in small numbers:

Provided that in authorizing any derogations, the following must be specified:

(a) the species which are subject to the derogations;

(b) the means, arrangements or methods authorized for capture or killing;

(c) the conditions of risk and the circumstances of time and place under which such derogations may be granted;

(d) the controls which will be carried out.

(2) The Ornis Committee established under regulation 10 shall make recommendations to the Minister on the authorization of such derogations where it determines that the required conditions for authorizing any such derogations are in place.

(3) In making any such recommendations, the Ornis Committee established under regulation 10 shall act in accordance with the provisions of this regulation.

(4) The Wild Birds Regulation Unit shall draw up a report on the implementation of this regulation. This report shall be submitted to the Minister for his consideration by the end of July of every year.

### 10. Ornis Committee.

*Amended by: L.N. 39 of 2007; L.N. 280 of 2007; L.N. 82 of 2011; L.N. 421 of 2011; L.N. 341 of 2013; L.N. 403 of 2013; L.N. 281 of 2015.*

(1) There shall be a committee to be known as the Ornis Committee.

(2) The Ornis Committee shall be composed of not less than nine members and not more than eleven members who shall be appointed by the Minister for a term of one year, as follows:

(a) a Chairman;

(b) the Director, or his representative, of the Environment Protection Directorate within the Environment and Resources Authority or his successor in title;

(c) three persons known for their integrity who are independent from any organization related to bird conservation and, or hunting;

(d) two members to be nominated by an organization representing persons licensed to hunt and, or, to take;

(e) two members to be nominated by an organization which represents persons knowledgeable in, or working for, the protection of avifauna;

(f) a person with knowledge of, and experience in avifauna;

(g) a person with knowledge of and experience in hunting and taking of birds:

Provided that where there is more than one organization which qualifies under regulation 2, to be appointed as is referred to in either paragraph (d) or (e) of this sub-regulation, then there shall be two members appointed in terms of each paragraph (d) and (e), in which case the two members appointed under the paragraph where there is more than one organization will be appointed as follows:

One member nominated by the organization having the most members and one member nominated by the organization having the next higher number of members.

(3) (a) The committee shall be properly constituted and may function notwithstanding any failure to nominate any or all of the members mentioned in sub-regulation (2)(d) and (e);

(b) The members referred to in sub-regulation (2)(a), (b), (c), (d) and (e) shall each have one vote;

(c) The organizations referred to in sub-regulation (2)(d) and (e) shall hold one vote per organization;

(d) The other members of the Committee shall have no vote;

(e) The Minister shall appoint a person to act as Secretary of the Committee.

(4) The quorum of the Ornis Committee shall consist of the Chairman and not less than half the number of voting members, provided that where any members representing the organizations referred to in sub-regulation (2)(d) and (e) of are not nominated, the Chairman shall act on his own, after consulting with the persons mentioned in sub-regulation (2)(c).

(5) Subject to the provisions of sub-regulation (4), the Chairman shall have an original vote and, where the votes are equally divided, a second or casting vote.

(6) The functions of the Ornis Committee shall be to -

(a) recommend to the Minister, for the species referred to in Schedule II and in accordance with the provisions of regulation 5, the open seasons during which hunting may be allowed; such recommendation shall be submitted to the Minister at least four weeks prior to the proposed start of the open season during which hunting may be allowed:

Provided that in exceptional circumstances determined in conjunction with the Minister, the recommendation shall be submitted to the Minister at least two weeks prior to the proposed start of the open season during which hunting may be allowed;

(b) recommend to the Minister, in accordance with the provisions of applicable regulations, and without prejudice to the provisions of regulations 8 and 9, the open season during which taking may be allowed; such recommendation shall be submitted to the Minister at least four weeks prior to the proposed start of the open season during which taking may be allowed:

Provided that in exceptional circumstances determined in conjunction with the Minister, the recommendation shall be submitted to the Minister at least two weeks prior to the proposed start of the open season during which taking may be allowed;

(c) make recommendations to the Minister on the authorization of derogations from the provisions of regulations 4, 5, 6, 7, 18 and 21 where necessary in accordance with the provisions of regulation 9 and subject to the conditions specified therein:

Provided that following any such decisions the Ornis Committee shall verify at regular intervals that the conditions governing the granting of such authorization or authorisations continue to be fulfilled;

(d) make recommendations to the Minister on the establishment of policies and conditions in relation to the renewal of licences for the hunting of birds and the issuing of new licences for the hunting of birds, in accordance with the provisions of these regulations;

(e) make recommendations to the Minister on the establishment of policies and conditions on the confirmation of licences to take and on the issuing of new licences to take as may be necessary in application of regulations 8 and 9, and in accordance with the provisions of these regulations;

(f) make recommendations to the Minister on the establishment of policies relating to the enforcement of regulations;

(g) make recommendations to the Minister on plans for the management of areas for the hunting and taking of birds in accordance with the provisions of these regulations;

(h) make recommendations to the Minister on the establishment of policies relating to awareness programmes associated with any matter concerning these regulations;

(i) recommend to the Minister the adoption of special measures for the conservation of the bird species referred to in Schedule I and for regularly occurring migratory species not included in Schedule I in accordance with the provisions of regulation 3, taking into account their protection requirements in the geographical sea and land area of Malta;

(j) recommend to the Minister, as may be necessary, the adoption of national lists of species in danger of extinction or particularly endangered species, taking into account their geographical distribution;

(k) recommend to the Minister, as may be necessary, the drawing up of listings and ecological descriptions of areas particularly important to migratory species on their migratory routes and as wintering and nesting grounds;

(l) recommend to the Minister, as may be necessary, listings of data on the population levels of migratory species as shown by ringing;

(m) recommend to the Minister, as may be necessary, ecological methods for preventing any type of damage caused by birds;

(n) determine, as necessary, the role of certain species as indicators of pollution and make recommendations to the Minister in connection therewith;

(o) study, where necessary, the adverse effect of chemical pollution on population levels of bird species and make recommendations to the Minister in connection therewith;

(p) recommend measures to the Minister to control and monitor the introduction of any bird species not occurring naturally in the wild state in the European territory of the Member States of the European Union;

(q) recommend and, or commission, as necessary, regular studies and, or assessments relating to the status of the birds;

(r) prepare reports related to any matter arising from these regulations;

(s) recommend to the Minister the revision of any parameters of any hunting or taking season at any time on the basis of latest scientific advice concerning conservation status and maintenance of the population of bird species at satisfactory level, and or on the basis of the assessment of any other factors the Committee deems relevant:

Provided that in exercise of its functions, the Ornis committee may draw upon assistance of the Wild Birds Regulation Unit where this is required:

Provided further that the Committee may, with the consent of the Minister, delegate specific functions prescribed in this sub-regulation to the Wild Birds Regulation Unit:

Provided further that should there a be a period when the Committee is not constituted, the Minister may, by notice in the Gazette, temporarily delegate the functions of the Committee to the Wild Birds Regulation Unit until such time that the new Committee is appointed:

Provided further that as regards paragraphs (a), (b) and (c), the Minister shall, after having given due consideration to the recommendations submitted by the Committee and exercising his own discretion, by notice in the Gazette -

(i) establish the open seasons during which hunting shall be allowed;

(ii) establish the open seasons during which taking shall be allowed;

(iii) decide on whether or not to authorize derogations from the provisions of regulations 4, 5, 6, 7, 18 and 21 and the conditions under which any such derogations are to be exercised:

So however that if, when in acting under this proviso, there exists substantial divergence between the recommendations of the Committee and the decision or decisions taken by the Minister, the Minister shall state in writing to the Committee the reasons for his decision within three weeks from the publication of such decisions in the Gazette.

(7) The Ornis Committee may, with the Minister’s consent, delegate to the Wild Birds Regulation Unit any of such functions as are referred to in sub-regulation (6):

Provided that in exercising any of the functions so delegated, the Wild Birds Regulation Unit shall act in accordance with the provisions of these regulations.

(8) The Ornis Committee shall inform the Minister about its deliberations and decisions and shall transmit to him a copy of the minutes of the meetings.

### 11. Wild Birds Regulation Unit.

*Substituted by: L.N. 341 of 2013. Amended by: L.N. 281 of 2015; L.N. 69 of 2016; L.N. 86 of 2022.*

(1) The Wild Birds Regulation Unit shall be established by the Minister and shall be charged with implementing the recommendations of the Ornis Committee and Minister’s decisions concerning implementation of these regulations. The Wild Birds Regulation Unit shall also act as Secretariat to the Committee established under regulation 10 and shall assist this Committee in the performance and in the carrying out of its functions:

Provided that in exercising any of its functions under these regulations, the Wild Birds Regulation Unit shall act in accordance with the provisions of these regulations.

### 19. Time restrictions.

*Amended by: L.N. 252 of 2014; L.N. 281 of 2015.*

(1) No person shall, between two hours after sunset and two hours before sunrise of the next following day, hunt or attempt to hunt, take or attempt to take any bird by any means, or carry a fire-arm which is loaded, or out of its case.

(2) No person shall hunt or attempt to hunt any bird by any means on Sundays and Public Holidays after 1 o’clock in the afternoon (1.00 p.m).

(3) The hunting of birds on land shall be permitted between the 1st September of any given year and the 31st January of the following year, between two hours before sunrise and two hours after sunset on any day between Monday and Saturday, and between two hours before sunrise and 1 o’clock in the afternoon (1:00 pm) on Sundays and Public Holidays:

Provided that hunting of birds on land between the 15th September and the 7th October of any year shall not be permitted from Monday to Saturday between 7:00 pm and two hours before sunrise of the following day.

(4) The hunting of birds at sea shall be permitted between the 1st October of any given year and the 31st January of the following year:

Provided that hunting at sea between the 1st October and 7th of October of any given year shall not be permitted from Monday to Saturday between 7:00 pm and two hours before sunrise of the following day:

Provided further that hunting of birds at sea between 8th October of any given year and 31st January of the following year shall be allowed between two hours before sunrise and two hours after sunset on any day between Monday and Saturday, and between two hours before sunrise and 1 o’clock in the afternoon (1:00 p.m) on Sundays and public holidays.

(5) The Minister may, by means of notice in the Gazette, revise the parameters of the hunting season in any given year at any time on the basis of relevant latest scientific advice concerning conservation status and maintenance of the population of bird species at satisfactory level, or any other reason which the Minister deems to be of sufficient gravity:

Provided that, in its recommendation as found in regulation 10(6)(s), the Ornis Committee may also consider any reason which it deems to be of sufficient gravity.

### 20. Space restrictions.

*Amended by: L.N. 252 of 2014; L.N. 367 of 2020.*

No person shall:

(a) keep or confine any bird whatsoever in any cage or other receptacle which is less than 250mm in height, 250mm in length and 150mm in breadth, and which, considering the size of the bird, is not large enough to permit the bird to stretch its wings freely:

Provided that this paragraph shall not apply to poultry or to the keeping or confinement of any bird -

(i) while such bird is undergoing treatment by a veterinary surgeon;

(ii) during the open live-capturing season when the cage should not be smaller than the dimensions provided for in the relevant framework regulations pertaining to any live-capturing derogations for that species for which a particular derogation applies;

(b) promote, permit, assist or take part in any event whatsoever in the course of which captive birds are liberated for the purpose of being shot at immediately on their release.

### 23. Scientific bird ringing.

*Amended by: L.N. 341 of 2013. Substituted by: L.N. 367 of 2020. Amended by: L.N. 86 of 2022; L.N. 150 of 2023; L.N. 251 of 2025.*

(1) Notwithstanding the provisions of regulation 4(1)(a) and 4(1)(e), scientific bird-ringers may capture any bird for the purposes of scientific bird ringing and/or geo-tagging:

Provided that without prejudice to any research Framework Regulations, a person who is not licensed as a bird-ringer in accordance with regulation 23(2)(a), may capture certain birds for the purpose of geo-tagging, followed by their immediate release back into the wild, insofar as that person satisfies the following requirements:

(a) the submission of an application to the Wild Birds Regulation Unit, not later than two (2) months prior to the commencement of the geo-tagging activity, which application must be accompanied by the following information:

(i) a detailed explanation of the objectives of the proposed research and the reasons thereof;

(ii) the alternatives considered;

(iii) the proposed quantity per species to be captured for the purpose of geo-tagging;

(iv) the proposed methodology;

(v) the location of the sites where geo-tagging is proposed to be carried out in the format prescribed in paragraph (c) of sub-regulation (3);

(vi) details of the persons who will be entrusted with handling birds and the management of the geo-tags’ software, including data collection and analysis;

(vii) the proposed research dates for geo-tagging;

(viii) the proposed duration of the data collection and analysis; and

(ix) the date when the report on the outcome of the research is expected to be submitted to the Wild Birds Regulation Unit;

(b) the submission, together with the application referred to in paragraph (a), of a signed declaration, either:

(i) by the applicant, confirming that he has proven experience in bird-handling and affixing geo-tags on avian species, including knowledge of the geo-tags’ software, data collection, management and analysis if the data will also be managed by the same applicant, supported by a biography that lists his previous ornithological research conducted by means of geo-tagging, including the research period, outcome of the research and a link to each publication in peer-reviewed scientific journals; or

(ii) by the applicant’s trainer, who has proven experience in bird-handling and affixing geo-tags on avian species, as supported by the trainer’s biography, which shall be submitted together with the declaration and shall contain all the information specified in sub-paragraph (i), confirming that he has trained the applicant who is knowledgeable about all aspects related to geo-tagging, including affixing of geo-tags on the target avian species proposed in the application referred to in paragraph (a) and, if the data will also be managed by the same applicant, a confirmation that he is conversant with the geo-tags’ software, including data collection, management and analysis;

(c) the inclusion, in the proposed methodology as referred to in paragraph (a) of sub-regulation (1), of a bird-ringing component as provided for in sub-regulation (2), for all species not listed in Schedule II:

Provided that if the geo-tagging activity involves species listed in Schedule II, the proposed methodology should preferably also include a bird-ringing component as provided for in sub-regulation (2);

(d) the recommendations and conditions of the Ornis Committee, in accordance with regulation 10(6); and (e) the conditions specified in the licence issued for the purpose of geo-tagging by the Wild Birds Regulation Unit, including reporting obligations and the submission of a detailed report on the outcome of the geo-tagging activity to the Wild Birds Regulation Unit:

Provided that the Wild Birds Regulation Unit shall present the report as referred to in paragraph (e) to the Ornis Committee in terms of the proviso to regulation 10(6)(c):

Provided further that in the event that the applicant fails to submit the report as referred to in paragraph (e) within the deadline stipulated by the Wild Birds Regulation Unit in the licence conditions, the applicant shall be rendered ineligible to apply for any other research permit or renewal.

(2) Scientific bird-ringers may only capture birds subject to the following conditions:

(a) no person shall act as a scientific bird-ringer without a licence from the Wild Birds Regulation Unit issued to him in accordance with the provisions of these regulations;

(b) no person other than a licensed scientific bird-ringer shall capture any bird for the purpose of scientific bird ringing;

(c) scientific bird-ringers shall not capture any bird from the wild for any purpose whatsoever other than for the purpose of research;

(d) a scientific bird-ringer shall only use rings approved for this purpose by a bird ringing scheme;

(e) a scientific bird-ringer may capture any bird for research purposes at any time throughout the year and inside any site:

Provided that the sites where bird ringing and geo-tagging will be practiced shall be pre-notified to the Wild Birds Regulation Unit in accordance with regulation 23(3)(c);

(f) without prejudice to regulation 9 and notwithstanding the provisions of regulation 7(1)(d), (e) and (f), a scientific bird-ringer who captures a bird exclusively for scientific bird ringing purposes may, for this purpose, also use pre-recorded bird calls, nets, and live-decoys of specimens pertaining to the targeted species, including their mules and hybrids;

(g) a scientific bird-ringer shall immediately release birds caught into the wild after performing the necessary measurements and attachment of rings and/or geo-tags, or after reading of the necessary information from the previously fitted rings and/or geo-tags in the event of recapture of bird specimens previously ringed or fitted with a geo-tag;

(h) scientific bird-ringers who are also in possession of a hunting licence shall not engage in scientific bird ringing activities at the same time as practicing hunting;

(i) before the scientific bird-ringer leaves the bird ringing and, or geo-tagging site, all nets, including mist-nets, shall either be removed, furled or completely covered in such a way that no bird or other fauna can get caught.

(3) In order to be eligible for the licence mentioned in sub-regulation 23(2)(a) an applicant shall, by the date of application:

(a) have obtained a training certificate, which is granted after the applicant has successfully completed the necessary training and gained field experience with a qualified trainer who is part of a bird ringing scheme;

(b) have the approval for a licence from a bird ringing scheme; and

(c) submit A4 site plans to the Wild Birds Regulation Unit showing the precise boundary of the sites delineated in red ink where scientific bird ringing and geo-tagging is intended to be carried out.

(4) The bird ringing scheme shall, on an annual basis and by not later than 1st February of each year, submit to the Wild Birds Regulation Unit a written report on bird ringing and geo-tagging activities performed during the previous calendar year, as follows:

(a) the number of birds per species fitted with a scientific ring and/or geo-tag during the reporting year; and

(b) any other information on bird ringing and geo-tagging activities as may be required by the Wild Birds Regulation Unit.

### 24. Bird sanctuaries.

*Amended by: L.N. 30 of 2009; L.N. 367 of 2020.*

Except as provided for in regulation 23, no person:

(a) shall hunt, injure, take or try to hunt, injure or take any bird in a bird sanctuary;

(b) shall, at any time and by any means whatsoever, have in his possession or under his control any bird, dead or alive, or part of a bird, in a bird sanctuary;

(c) shall have in his possession or under his control, any fire-arm, ammunition, decoys, net or any other means to hunt, or take any bird in a bird sanctuary:

Provided that the foregoing shall not apply to any person licensed to hunt or to take in accordance with the provisions of these regulations, who, while on a moving vehicle, is carrying a fire-arm which is unloaded and without any cartridges in the magazine, and inside its case, or who has nets for taking birds while passing through the main roads of a bird sanctuary:

Provided further that the Minister may, in accordance with the provisions of regulation 9(1)(b), by notice in the Gazette, authorize a derogation from the provisions of this regulation in respect to items (8) and (9) of Schedule V, in the interests of air safety. In cases of urgency, the Minister may act without the recommendation of the Ornis Committee in terms of these regulations.

### 25. Demolition of hides.

*Amended by: L.N. 341 of 2013.*

The Wild Birds Regulation Unit may order the owner, tenant or other person who is making use of, or is in possession of land on which any hide or other hiding place intended to be used for shooting or taking of birds has been constructed or built, to demolish, within such time as the Wild Birds Regulation Unit may determine, any such hide or other hiding place if it has been so constructed or built in a bird sanctuary, or within the limits indicated in regulation 18(2)(a).

### 26. Exclusion of clay-pigeon shooting.

Nothing in these regulations is intended to affect clay-pigeon shooting organized by shooting clubs registered with the Commissioner and held according to a licence and conditions laid down by the Commissioner, provided that all participants are in possession of a licence to carry a fire-arm.

### 27A. Special procedures.

*Added by: L.N. 341 of 2013. Amended by: L.N. 281 of 2015. Substituted by L.N. 86 of 2022.*

(1)Notwithstanding anything contained in these regulations for the trial and punishments of offences and subject to the provisions of this regulation and of Schedule VIII, where the Wild Birds Regulation Unit and, or, the Executive Police believe that a person has committed an offence against these regulations as found in Schedule VIII, the Executive Police shall confiscate the corpus delicti as specified in Schedule VIII and shall inform the Wild Birds Regulation Unit which shall, within thirty (30) days from such information, give notice in writing to such person describing the offence of which the person is accused, including the penalty points for each offence in terms of Schedule VIII, the steps to be taken to remedy the offence and the administrative penalty which he is required to pay in respect of that offence:

*Cap. 9.*

Provided that this regulation shall be without prejudice to the powers granted to the Executive Police under the Criminal Code:

Provided further that any person served with the notice who feels aggrieved by a decision of the Wild Birds Regulation Unit under this sub-regulation, may appeal to the Administrative Review Tribunal established under the Administrative Justice Act for a revocation or modification of such administrative penalty, and the return of the corpus delicti confiscated in terms of this regulation.

*Cap. 490.*

(2) If at any time during a two (2) year period a person accumulates in aggregate twelve (12) penalty points or more as specified in Schedule VIII, the offence or offences committed shall be punishable in terms of sub-regulation 11(9) and the immediate confiscation of the corpus delicti as specified in Schedule VIII:

Provided that the accumulated points shall revert back to zero and the two (2) year period shall be reset if the licensee does not commit other offences listed in Schedule VIII within two (2) years from the date of last offence:

Provided further that in all cases, following reversion to zero points and resetting of the two (2) year period, the licensee shall remain subject to accumulation of penalty points for offences listed in Schedule VIII:

Provided further that, should the licensee appeal to the Administrative Review Tribunal and until the Tribunal decides whether or not to revoke or modify the administrative penalty, the two (2) year period shall continue to apply and the penalty points related to the offence in question shall remain valid and shall be added to any penalty points that the accused may have subsequently accumulated, and shall only be deducted from his total if he is acquitted of the charges brought against him.

(3) Where a notice under this regulation has been given, the person named in the notice may, within twenty-one (21) days of the service of the notice, accept responsibility for the offence specified in the notice and within the same period, remedy the offence and pay the penalty indicated in the notice:

Provided that where the person to whom notice is given under sub-regulation (1) does not accept or, having accepted such responsibility, fails to remedy the offence within the time aforesaid, ordinary criminal proceedings shall be taken against him in accordance with regulation 27. The two (2) year period shall continue to apply and the penalty points relating to the offence in question shall remain valid and shall be added to any penalty points that the accused may have accumulated, and shall only be deducted from his total if he is acquitted of the charges brought against him.

(4) The provisions of this regulation shall not apply in any case where the offence listed in Schedule VIII is accompanied by any offence which is not listed in the same Schedule including where the offence is committed in conjunction with any other offence under these regulations, with the exception of the administrative offences listed in Schedule VIII, and in such circumstances the provisions of regulation 27 shall automatically apply.

(5) Where a person holding a special licence issued in terms of the relevant Framework Regulations has committed an offence in terms of this regulation during the period of a derogation, the special licence obtained shall not be suspended:

Provided that when such person accumulates the twelve (12) penalty points, in terms of the provisions of sub-regulation (2), during the period of a derogation or is charged or accused under regulation 27 in lieu of this regulation, such special licence shall be automatically revoked.

## PART III

### 28. Omissions by responsible persons.

Where any person or body responsible for the performance of these regulations fails to perform any tasks or functions as are assigned to it under these regulations within the prescribed time period, if any, the Minister may proceed to take action as he may deem expedient, without prejudice to the possibility of making any charges upon the performance by such person or body of the tasks or functions assigned to it.

### 29. Saving of provisions of Protection of Birds and Wild Rabbit Regulations.

*Amended by: L.N. 39 of 2007; L.N. 341 of 2013; L.N. 341 of 2013. S.L. 549.90*

The definition of "close season" and the provisions regulating the wild rabbit in the Protection of Birds and Wild Rabbit Regulations (revoked by these regulations) are hereby saved:

Provided that, with the exception of regulation 2B(6), (7) and (8) of the Protection of Birds and Wild Rabbit Regulations, where in any provision regulating the wild rabbit in the Protection of Birds and Wild Rabbit Regulations, there is a reference to the Ornis Committee, any such reference shall be construed as a reference to the Wild Birds Regulation Unit:

Provided also that notwithstanding its repeal -

(a) anything that has lawfully been done by virtue of the Protection of Birds and Wild Rabbit Regulations shall remain in force, except insofar as it is inconsistent with any of the provisions of these regulations;

(b) any liability to any penalty under any of the provisions of the Protection of Birds and Wild Rabbit Regulations shall continue to subsist notwithstanding the repeal of the said regulations.

### 30. Other licences.

*Added by: L.N. 341 of 2013.*

The provisions and conditions laid down in these regulations shall not exempt any person from the requirement of obtaining:

(a) an Autumn live-capturing licence for the capturing of relevant species during a potential Autumn live-capturing season; and, or

(b) a Spring Hunting Licence for the hunting of the relevant species during a potential spring hunting season;

(c) any other license as may be required under these regulations.

### 31. Conservation of Wild Birds Fund.

*Added by: L.N. 69 of 2016. Amended by: L.N. 368 of 2018.*

(1) The Minister shall set up a fund, hereinafter referred to as the Conservation of Wild Birds Fund.

(2) The Conservation of Wild Birds Fund shall be used to finance activities, projects, programmes and schemes related to the implementation of the aims and objectives of these regulations, including activities organised by non-governmental organizations.

(3) The Conservation of Wild Birds Fund shall be administered by the Wild Birds Regulation Unit.

(4) There shall be paid into the Conservation of Wild Birds Fund:

(a) any sums appropriated by Parliament for the purpose;

(b) any donations or grants made to the Conservation of Wild Birds Fund by individuals or institutions;

(c) sums received by the Wild Birds Regulation Unit for the purpose of being placed in the Conservation of Wild Birds Fund;

(d) such other sums or monies as may from time to time be provided by, or collected under this or any other law or regulations.

(5) The Conservation of Wild Birds Fund shall keep a proper account of its revenue and expenditure and the Wild Birds Regulation Unit shall, every financial year, deliver to the Minister a copy of its balance sheet together with a report of its activities during the previous financial year.

*Cap. 123. Cap. 364.*

(6) The revenue of the Conservation of Wild Birds Fund shall not be subject to tax under the Income Tax Act and the Conservation of Wild Birds Fund shall not be liable to tax under the Duty on Documents and Transfers Act.

(7) The Wild Birds Regulation Unit may make proposals, for endorsement by the Minister, concerning the procedures to be followed in the functioning of the Conservation of Wild Birds Fund.
