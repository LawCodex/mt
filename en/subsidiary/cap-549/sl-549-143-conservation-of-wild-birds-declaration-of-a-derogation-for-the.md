# Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of a Derogation for the Control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia) Regulations (S.L. 549.143)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.143**

CONSERVATION OF WILD BIRDS (DECLARATION OF A DEROGATION FOR THE CONTROL OF

**STURNUS VULGARIS AND COLUMBA LIVIA)**

**REGULATIONS**

21st May, 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 218 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of a Derogation for the Control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

Words and expressions used in these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, shall have the same meaning as assigned to them in the Conservation of Wild Birds Regulations, hereinafter referred to as "the Wild Birds Regulations".

*S.L. 549. 42.*

## 3. Control restriction.

This derogation only applies for the control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia.

## 4. Establishment of control period.

For the purposes of the second proviso to regulation 10(6) of the Wild Birds Regulations and for the purposes of these regulations, the period for the control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia shall be from 1st July 2021 to the 30th June 2022, both days included.

## 5. Control according to set framework. S.L. 549. 85.

For the purposes of these regulations, the control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia shall be in accordance with the provisions of the framework laid down by the Conservation of Wild Birds (Framework for Allowing a Derogation for the Control of Birds in the Interest of Air Safety) Regulations and the Wild Birds Regulations.
