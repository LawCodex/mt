# Smoking Control on Beaches Regulations (S.L. 549.177)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.177**

SMOKING CONTROL ON BEACHES REGULATIONS 1st January, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 204 of 2025.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Smoking Control on Beaches Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*  Cap. 549.*

"Authority" means the Environment and Resources Authority established by article 6 of the Environment Protection Act;

"beach" means any beach as delineated in the Schedule;

* S.L. 315.10.*

"herbal product for smoking" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Manufacture, Presentation and Sale of Tobacco and Related Products Regulations;

* S.L. 315.09.*

"tobacco devices" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Products and Smoking Devices (Simulating Cigarettes or Tobacco) (Control) Regulations;

"tobacco product" means products that may be consumed and consist, even partly, of tobacco, whether genetically modified or otherwise, and includes any tobacco devices.

## 3. Prohibition of smoking on beaches.

It shall not be lawful for any person to consume and, or use a tobacco product or any herbal product for smoking on any beach delineated in the Schedule:

Provided that such prohibition shall not apply in those areas designated as smoking areas and physically delineated on site by the Authority.

## 4. Control and enforcement.

The Authority, the Department of Health, the Malta Tourism Authority, the Local Enforcement System Agency and the Executive Police shall collaborate and cooperate in enforcing these regulations.

## 5. Offences and punishments.

Any person who contravenes the provisions of these regulations shall be guilty of an offence and shall on conviction be liable to a fine (multa) of one hundred fifty euro (€150).
