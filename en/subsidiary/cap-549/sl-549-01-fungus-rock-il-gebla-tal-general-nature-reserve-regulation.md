# Fungus Rock (il-Ġebla tal-Ġeneral) Nature Reserve Regulation (S.L. 549.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.01**

**FUNGUS ROCK (IL-{EBLA TAL-{ENERAL)**

**NATURE RESERVE REGULATIONS**

14th February, 1992 LEGAL NOTICE 22 of 1992, as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fungus Rock (il-{ebla tal-{eneral) Nature Reserve Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations "flora and fauna" are to be construed as meaning all plants and animals, resident or migratory, species of which exist in the wild state on Fungus Rock (il-{ebla tal-{eneral).

## 3. Establishing Fungus Rock as a nature rcscrve.

All the surface area of Fungus Rock (il-{ebla tal-{eneral) shall be a nature reserve.

## 4. Prohibition of killing etc., of flaura and fauna from nature reserve.

(1) The killing, capture, collection, trapping, maintaining in captivity, taxidermy, commercial exploitation, as well as picking and hunting of any species of flora and fauna in or from the Fungus Rock (il-{ebla tal-{eneral) Nature Reserve is prohibited.

(2) The Minister may remove any such prohibition as referred in subregulation (1) for the purpose of scientific study in particular cases.

## 5. Access to nature reserve.

(1) Climbing and access to the plateau surface of the Fungus Rock (il-{ebla tal-{eneral) Nature Reserve is prohibited.

(2) Climbing and access to the plateau surface of the Fungus Rock (il-{ebla tal-{eneral) Nature Reserve shall be by permission to be granted by the Minister for educational and scientific purposes, and in granting such permission the Minister may impose such conditions as he may deem appropriate.

## 6. Offences and penalties.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2006.*

Any person who contravenes any of the provisions of these regulations or any condition of a permission granted under the last preceding regulation, shall be liable on conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to a fine (multa) not exceeding one thousand and one hundred and sixty-four euro and sixty-nine cents (€1,164.69) or to both such imprisonment and fine.
