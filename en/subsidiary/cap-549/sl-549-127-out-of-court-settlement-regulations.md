# Out of Court Settlement Regulations (S.L. 549.127)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.127**

**OUT OF COURT SETTLEMENT REGULATIONS**

22nd March, 2019

LEGAL NOTICE 49 of 2019.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Out of Court Settlement Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 549.*

"the Act" means the Environment Protection Act;

"the Authority" has the same meaning assigned to it under the Act;

"electrical and electronic equipment" or "EEE" has the same meaning as assigned to it under the Waste Management (Electrical and Electronic Equipment) Regulations;

*S.L. 549.89.*

*S.L. 549.89.*

"waste electrical and electronic equipment" or "WEEE" has the same meaning as assigned to it under the Waste Management (Electrical and Electronic Equipment) Regulations.

## 3. Out of court settlement.

(1) When the Authority believes that a person has committed an offence against any provision of the Act or regulations made thereunder, the Authority may enter into an out of court settlement agreement in writing with the offender, whereby the offender pays an amount equivalent to a fine that would be due by way of a penalty, and upon the signing of the said agreement by both parties, all criminal liability of the offender under the said Act and regulations, with regard to the offences to which the out of court settlement agreement has been entered, shall be extinguished:

Provided that payment of the penalty under the out of court settlement agreement and the carrying out of any imposed action, shall not extinguish any civil liability to make good damages to any person, or to the Authority, or to the environment in terms of environmental liability.

(2) The agreement shall contain:

(a) a description of the offence;

(b) the steps to be taken by the offender to remedy the offence; and

(c) the penalty due by the offender in respect of the offence as specified in the Schedule.

(3) Where the offender does not observe the out of court settlement agreement put forward by the Authority within the stipulated time frame, the Authority shall proceed to institute criminal proceedings against the offender:

Provided that the time frame stipulated by the Authority in such out of court settlement agreements, shall not be less than fifteen (15) days and not more than sixty (60) days.

## 4. Administrative Settlements Fund.

(1) In accordance with the provisions of sub-article (9) of article 32 of the Act, the Authority hereby establishes the Administrative Settlements Fund.

(2) There shall be paid into the Administrative Settlements Fund any amounts received by the Authority as:

(a) out of court settlements;

(b) administrative fines; and

*S.L. 549.72*

(c) any penalties applicable in terms of the Daily Penalties (Environment) Regulations.

## 5. Management of revenue generated under these regulations.

Without prejudice to any other regulations made under the Act, all the revenue generated under these regulations shall be deposited in the Administrative Settlements Fund:

Provided that any revenue generated through any out of court settlement agreement, in relation to offences in terms of the Waste Management (End of Life Vehicles) Regulations, the Waste Management (Packaging and Packaging Waste) Regulations, the Waste Management (Waste Batteries and Accumulators) Regulations and the Waste Management (Electrical and Electronic Equipment) Regulations shall be deposited into the Waste Management Fund as established in the Waste Regulations.
