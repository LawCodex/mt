# Environment Protection Act (Transfer of Assets) Regulations (S.L. 549.114)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.114**

**ENVIRONMENT PROTECTION ACT**

**(TRANSFER OF ASSETS)**

**REGULATIONS**

21st April, 2017 L-AVVIŻ LEGALI 112 of 2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Environment Protection Act (Transfer of Assets) Regulations.

## 2. Specified property. Cap. 549

The property described in the Schedule is hereby specified for the purpose of article 38 of the Environment Protection Act.

## 3. Appointed day.

The property specified in regulation 2 of these regulations shall be transferred and vest in the Environment and Resources Authority as provided in the article aforesaid with effect from the day specified in the Schedule.
