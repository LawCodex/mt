# Environment and Planning Appeals (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 549.105)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.105**

**ENVIRONMENT AND PLANNING APPEALS**

**(FEES) REGULATIONS**

4th April, 2016\*

LEGAL NOTICE 112 of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Environment and Planning Appeals (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 551.*

"the Act" means the Environment and Planning Review Tribunal Act;

"appeal" means an appeal made to the Environment and Planning Review Tribunal established by the Act;

*Cap. 552.*

"development permission" has the same meaning as assigned to it in the Development Planning Act;

"the Environment and Resources Authority” has the same meaning as assigned to it in the Environment Protection Act;

*Cap. 549.*

"environmental NGOs" means non-governmental organizations promoting environmental protection and which are registered under the Voluntary Organisations Act;

*Cap. 492.*

*S.L. 504.103*

"external consultee" has the same meaning as assigned to it in regulation 2 of the Development Planning (Procedure for Applications and their Determination) Regulations, or any other regulations which may from time to time succeed the said regulations;

*Cap. 552.*

"the Planning Authority" has the same meaning assigned to the term "Authority" in article 2 of the Development Planning Act;

"Tribunal " means the Environment and Planning Review Tribunal.

(2) In these regulations any expression defined by the Act shall have the same meaning as it has in the Act.

## 3. Appeal from decision of the Planning Authority. Cap. 552

Where an appeal is made in respect of a decision of the Planning Authority, from which an appeal lies under any provision of the Development Planing Act, the application of the appeal shall be accompanied by the appropriate fee as set out in Schedule 1 and by an administrative fee as set in Schedule 2.

## 4. Appeal from decision of the Environment and Resources Authority. Cap. 549.

Where an appeal is made in respect of a decision of the Environment and Resources Authority, from which an appeal lies under any provision of the Environment Protection Act, the application of the appeal shall be accompanied by the appropriate fee as set out in Schedule 1 and by an administrative fee as set in Schedule 2.

\* see regulation 1(2) of these Regulations, as originally promulgated.

## 5. Appeal to be null and void.

An appeal which is not accompanied by the appropriate fee as provided in regulations 3 and 4 shall be null and void only in those cases where the appellant fails to pay such fee within such time following the lodging of an appeal as the Tribunal may establish.

## 6. Refunds.

Where an appeal is upheld, the Tribunal may order that the fee paid according to Schedule 1, or part thereof, shall be refunded to the appellant.

## 7. Appeals by interested persons, or environmental NGOs, or external consultees.

When any interested person, or an environmental NGO, or an external consultee has lodged an appeal before the Tribunal, and the Tribunal finds in favour of any such appellant, the appeal fee paid by the said appellant shall be reimbursed to the said appellant in equal proportions by the parties cast and, for the purposes of this regulation, the Tribunal decision shall be an executive title in like manner as if it were an award of arbitrators referred to in article 253(d) of the Code or Organization and Civil Procedure for all purposes of that Code.

*Cap. 12.*
