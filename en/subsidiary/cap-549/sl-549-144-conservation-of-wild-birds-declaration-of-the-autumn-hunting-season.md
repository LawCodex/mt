# Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of the Autumn Hunting Season for Turtle Dove) Regulations (S.L. 549.144)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.144**

**CONSERVATION OF WILD BIRDS**

**(DECLARATION OF THE AUTUMN HUNTING**

**SEASON FOR TURTLE DOVE) REGULATIONS**

31st August, 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 341 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of the Autumn Hunting Season for Turtle Dove) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

Words and expressions used in these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, shall have the same meaning as assigned to them in the Conservation of Wild Birds Regulations, hereinafter referred to as "the Wild Birds Regulations".

*S.L. 549. 42.*

## 3. Establishing of hunting period.

Notwithstanding the provisions of regulation 19(3) of the Wild Birds Regulations, the Autumn Hunting season for Turtle Dove (Streptopelia turtur) on land shall only be between the 1st September and the 30th September of any given year, both dates included:

Provided that the provisions of regulation 19(3) of the Wild Birds Regulations shall remain applicable to the hunting of Turtle Dove, save as is otherwise inconsistent with this provision.

## 4. Hunting conditions.

(1) The overall bag limit for the Autumn Hunting Season for Turtle Dove shall be 500.

(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 1(a) of Schedule IV of the Wild Birds Regulations, any Turtle Dove hunted during September shall be reported immediately through the telephonic game reporting system or through the game reporting mobile app.

(3) The Autumn Hunting Season for Turtle Dove may be terminated prior to the 30th September of each year if the quota established in sub-regulation (1) has been reached.

(4) In the event that the quota has been reached, the Minister shall order, by notice in the Gazette or any other notice, that the Autumn Hunting Season for Turtle Dove is to terminate immediately. All licensees shall be informed immediately of such termination by means of a short message service (SMS) to the number registered with the Wild Birds Regulation Unit and no further hunting of Turtle Dove may be permitted therefrom.

## 5. Offences and penalties.

Any person who fails to comply with any provision of these regulations shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to the punishments established under regulation 27(3) or regulation 27A of the Wild Birds Regulations, as applicable.
