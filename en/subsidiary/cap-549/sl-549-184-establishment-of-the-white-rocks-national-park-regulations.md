# Establishment of the White Rocks National Park Regulations (S.L. 549.184)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.184**

**ESTABLISHMENT OF THE WHITE ROCKS**

**NATIONAL PARK REGULATIONS**

31st July, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 209 of 2026.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Establishment of the White Rocks National Park Regulations.

(2) The purpose of these regulations is to establish the White Rocks as a national park.

## 2. Interpretation.

For the purposes of these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 549.*

"Act" means the Environment Protection Act;

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for the environment;

"Park" means the White Rocks National Park as delineated in the Schedule.

## 3. Establishment of the White Rocks National Park.

The Minister hereby establishes the White Rocks National Park as delineated in the site map provided in the Schedule.

## 4. Agricultural and other legitimate uses.

The designation of the Park shall not of itself prejudice lawful existing agricultural activity, access rights, servitudes, permits or authorisations, provided that the exercise of such rights remains subject to these regulations and any other applicable law.

## 5. Applicability of other legal measures.

These regulations shall be without prejudice to any stricter protection, prohibition, restriction, permit requirement, authorisation, enforcement power or conservation measure provided for under the Act or any other applicable law.
