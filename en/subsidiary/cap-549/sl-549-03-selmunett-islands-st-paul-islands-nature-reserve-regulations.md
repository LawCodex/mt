# Selmunett Islands (St.Paul Islands) Nature Reserve Regulations (S.L. 549.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.03**

**SELMUNETT ISLANDS (ST. PAUL ISLANDS)**

**NATURE RESERVE REGULATIONS**

2nd March, 1993 LEGAL NOTICE 25 of 1993, as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Sehnunett Islands (St. Paul Islands) Nature Reserve Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations "flora and fauna" are to be construed as meaning all plants and animals, resident or migratory, species of which exist in the wild state on Selmunett Islands, also known as St. Paul Islands.

## 3. Establishing Selmunett Islands (St. Paul Islands) as a nature reserve.

All the surface area of Selmunett Islands (St. Paul Islands) shall be a protected nature reserve.

## 4. Prohibition of killing etc., of flora and fauna on nature reserve.

(1) The killing, capture, collection, trapping, maintaining in captivity, taxidermy, commercial exploitation, as well as picking and hunting of any species of flora and fauna in or from the Selmunett Islands (St. Paul Islands) Nature Reserve is prohibited.

(2) The Minister may remove such prohibition as referred to in subregulation (1) for the purpose of scientific study in particular cases.

## 5. Access to nature reserve.

Access to the Selmunett Islands (St. Paul Islands) is only permissible between sunrise and sunset and then only for swimming, berthing of sea craft and walking along designated footpaths. All other activities, such as camping and barbecues, are prohibited.

## 6. Offences and penalties.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

Any person who contravenes any of the provisions of these regulations or any condition of a permission granted under regulation 4, shall be liable on conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months, or to a fine (multa) not exceeding one thousand and one hundred and sixty-four euro and sixty-nine cents (€1,164.69), or to both such imprisonment and fine.
