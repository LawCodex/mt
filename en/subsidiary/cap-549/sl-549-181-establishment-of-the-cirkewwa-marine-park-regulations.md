# Establishment of the Ċirkewwa Marine Park Regulations (S.L. 549.181)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.181**

**ESTABLISHMENT OF THE ĊIRKEWWA MARINE**

**PARK REGULATIONS**

26th February, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 51 of 2026.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Establishment of the Ċirkewwa Marine Park Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to establish the Ċirkewwa Marine Park located in the limits of Iċ-Ċirkewwa and Il- Qammieħ, lying within the special protection area known as Żona fil- Baħar madwar Għawdex.

## 2. Interpretation.

For the purpose of these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 549.*

"Act" means the Environment Protection Act;

"Marine Park" means the Ċirkewwa Marine Park;

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for the environment.

## 3. Establishment of the Marine Park.

The Minister hereby declares the establishment of the Marine Park in the Northeast of Malta, as delineated in the site map in the Schedule. The Marine Park shall serve to promote and conserve the ecological and natural features while facilitating the sustainable use of the Marine Park's resources for recreation and other appropriate activities.
