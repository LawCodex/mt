# Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration on a Derogation for a 2020 Spring Hunting Season for Quail) Regulations (S.L. 549.133)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.133**

**CONSERVATION OF WILD BIRDS (DECLARATION**

ON A DEROGATION FOR A 2020 SPRING HUNTING

**SEASON FOR QUAIL) REGULATIONS**

9th April, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 140 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration on a Derogation for a 2020 Spring Hunting Season for Quail) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

Terms and expressions used in these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, shall have the same meaning as given to them in the Conservation of Wild Birds Regulations, hereinafter referred to as the "the Wild Birds Regulations".

*S.L. 549.42.*

## 3. Establishment of Spring hunting season.

For the purposes of the fourth proviso to sub-regulation (6) of regulation 10 of the Wild Birds Regulations and for the purposes of these regulations, the period for the hunting of Quail in Spring 2020, hereinafter in these regulations referred to as "the Spring hunting season", shall be from the 10th April to the 30th April, 2020, both dates included, from two (2) hours before sunrise until noon:

Provided that the provisions of these regulations shall cease to have effect and no hunting activity shall be permitted in the event that the Minister orders, by notice in the Gazette or any other notice, that the Spring hunting season shall terminate immediately or at a date as specified by the Minister.

## 4. Hunting according to set framework.

For the purposes of these regulations, the Spring hunting season shall be in accordance with the provisions of the framework laid down by the Framework for Allowing a Derogation Opening a Spring Hunting Season for Turtle Dove and Quail Regulations.

*S.L. 549.57.*

## 5. Special licence and quotas.

(1) The Spring Hunting Licence shall set the overall bag limit for the Spring hunting season at five-thousand (5,000) Quail as the total number of birds which may be hunted under the authority of all issued licences taken together.

(2) The Spring Hunting Licence shall cease to have effect in the event that the Minister orders, by notice in the Gazette or any other notice, that the Spring hunting season shall terminate immediately or at a date as specified by the Minister.
