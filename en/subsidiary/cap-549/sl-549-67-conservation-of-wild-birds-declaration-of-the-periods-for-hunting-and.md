# Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of the periods for Hunting and Taking for 2011) Regulations (S.L. 549.67)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.67**

**CONSERVATION OF WILD BIRDS**

**(DECLARATION OF THE PERIODS**

**FOR HUNTING AND TAKING FOR 2011)**

**REGULATIONS**

16th August, 2011

LEGAL NOTICE 331 of 2011.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of the periods for Hunting and Taking for 2011) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

Terms and expressions used in these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, shall have the same meaning as given to them in the Conservation of Wild Birds Regulations, hereinafter referred to as the "Wild Birds Regulations".

*S.L. 549.42*

## 3. Establishing of periods.

For the purposes of establishing the period for the hunting and taking of wild birds under regulation 10(6)(i), (ii) and (iii) of the Wild Birds Regulations, the Prime Minister has established the following dates:

(a) the hunting of birds on land shall be permitted between the 1st September 2011 and the 31st January 2012, betwen two hours before sunrise and two hours after sunset on any day between Monday and Saturday, and between two hours before sunrise and 1 o’ clock in the afternoon (1.00 p.m) on Sundays and Public Holidays:

Provided that hunting on land between the 15th September 2011 and the 30th September 2011 shall not be permitted from Monday to Saturday after 3.00 pm and two hours before sunrise of the next following day.

(b) the hunting of birds at sea shall be permitted between the 1st October 2011 and the 31st January 2012.

## 4. Applicability of the taking and shooting of wild rabbit.

The applicable times for the taking and shooting of wild rabbits between the 15th September 2011 and the 30th September 2011 shall be as established in the proviso to regulation 3(a).
