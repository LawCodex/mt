# Nomination of Competent Authority Order (S.L. 549.19)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.19**

**NOMINATION OF COMPETENT AUTHORITY**

**ORDER**

3rd May, 2002;

28th June, 2002 LEGAL NOTICES 334 of 2001, 107 and 172 of 2002, and 211 of 2012, consolidated, as amended by Act XXV of 2015.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Nomination of Competent Authority Order.

## 2. Environment and Resources Authority nominated as competent authority for the purposes of the Environment Protection Act. Cap. 549.

The Environment and Resources Authority is hereby nominated as the competent authority for the purposes of the Environment Protection Act, to carry out the functions of the competent authority under the said Act:

Sohowever that the Minister responsible for the protection of the environment may by regulations issued under the said Act establish that another person or body shall be responsible for the performance of such regulations.

## 3. Regulator for Energy and Water Services established as the competent authority in Regulations relating to indirect discharges.

The Regulator for Energy and Water Services is hereby established as the competent authority with respect to indirect discharges referred to in the following regulations made under the Environment Protection Act:

(a) the Pollution caused by Certain Dangerous Substances discharged into the Aquatic Environment Regulations;

*Amended by: XXV. 2015.41. Cap. 549. S.L. 549.10 S.L. 549.12 S.L. 549.13*

(b) the Limit Values and Quality Objectives for Hexachlo-rocyclohexane Discharges Regulations;

(c) the Limit Values and Quality Objectives for Mercury Discharges by Sectors other than the Chlor-Alkali Electrolysis Industry Regulations;

*S.L. 549.14*

(d) the Limit Values and Quality Objectives for Mercury Discharges by the Chlor-Alkali Electrolysis Industry Regulations;

*S.L. 549.15*

(e) the Limit Values and Quality Objectives for Cadmium Discharges Regulations; and

*S.L. 549.17*

(f) the Limit Values and Quality Objectives for Discharges of Certain Dangerous Substances into the Aquatic Environment Regulations.

## 4. Regulator for Energy and Water Services to carry out functions of competent authority.

*Amended by: XXV. 2015.41. S.L. 549.21*

The Regulator for Energy and Water Services in consultation with the Ministry responsible for Health is to carry out the functions of the competent authority under the Quality required of Surface Water Intended for the Abstraction of Drinking Water Regulations.

## 5. Malta Resources Authority to be competent authority. S.L. 423.50

The Malta Resources Authority is to carry out the functions of the competent authority under the European Union Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading System for Stationary Installations Regulations.
