# Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of a Derogation for the Control of Sturnus vulgaris) Regulations (S.L. 549.86)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.86**

CONSERVATION OF WILD BIRDS (DECLARATION OF A DEROGATION FOR THE CONTROL OF

**STURNUS VULGARIS) REGULATIONS**

30th August, 2013

LEGAL NOTICE 256 of 2013.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of a Derogation for the Control of Sturnus vulgaris) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

Terms and expressions used in these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, shall have the same meaning as given in the Conservation of Wild Birds Regulations, hereinafter referred to as "the Wild Birds Regulations".

*S.L.549.42*

## 3. Control restrictions.

This derogation only applies for the control of Sturnus vulgaris.

## 4. Establishment of control period.

For the purposes of the second proviso to regulation 10(6) of the Wild Birds Regulations and for the purposes of these regulations, the period for the control of Sturnus vulgaris, shall be from 1st September 2013 to the 31st May 2014, both days included.

## 5. Control according to set framework.

For the purposes of these regulations, the control of Sturnus vulgaris shall be in accordance with the provisions of the framework laid down by the Conservation of Wild Birds (Framework for Allowing a Derogation for the Control of Birds in the Interest of Air Safety) Regulations and the Wild Birds Regulations.
