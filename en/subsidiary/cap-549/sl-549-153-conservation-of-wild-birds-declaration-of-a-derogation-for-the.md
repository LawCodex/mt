# Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of a Derogation for the Control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia) Regulations (S.L. 549.153)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.153**

CONSERVATION OF WILD BIRDS (DECLARATION OF A DEROGATION FOR THE CONTROL OF

**STURNUS VULGARIS AND COLUMBA LIVIA)**

**REGULATIONS**

16th December, 2022

LEGAL NOTICE 354 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Conservation of Wild Birds (Declaration of a Derogation for the Control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

Unless the context otherwise requires, words and expressions used in these regulations shall have the same meaning as assigned to them in the Conservation of Wild Birds Regulations, hereinafter referred to as "the Wild Birds Regulations".

*S.L. 549.42.*

## 3. Control restrictions.

This derogation shall only apply for the control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia.

## 4. Establishment of control period.

The period for the control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia shall be from the date of publication of these regulations until the 30th June 2023, both days included.

## 5. Control accordingto set framework. S.L. 549.85.

The control of Sturnus vulgaris and Columba livia shall be in accordance with the provisions of the framework laid down by the Conservation of Wild Birds (Framework for Allowing a Derogation for the Control of Birds in the Interest of Air Safety) Regulations and the Wild Birds Regulations.
