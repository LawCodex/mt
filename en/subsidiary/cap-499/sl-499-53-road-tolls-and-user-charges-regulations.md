# Road Tolls and User Charges Regulations (S.L. 499.53)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 499.53**

**ROAD TOLLS AND USER CHARGES**

**REGULATIONS\***

16th October, 2008 LEGAL NOTICE 260 of 2008.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Road Tolls and User Charges Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Directive 1999/62/EC" means Directive 1999/62/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17th June, 1999 on the charging of heavy goods vehicles for the use of certain infrastructures as amended by Directive 2006/38/EC of 17th May, 2006, and by Council Directive 2006/103/EC of 20th November, 2006.

## 3. Applicability.

The provisions of Directive 1999/62/EC shall be applicable whenever a toll and user charge system is established on any road in Malta.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 338 of 2024.
