# Gozo Passenger and Goods Services (Fares) Regulations (S.L. 499.75)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 499.75**

GOZO PASSENGER AND GOODS SERVICES (FARES) REGULATIONS

1st August, 2025

LEGAL NOTICE 163 of 2025.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Gozo Passenger and Goods Services (Fares) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"authorised officer" means an officer of the scheduled ro-ro passenger ships services operator so contracted by Government to operate such services or a public official appointed by the Ministry responsible for transport;

*  Cap. 627.*

"disability" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the United Nations Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act;

"person with disability" means any person in possession of a specific identification document or other documentation acknowledging such disability; and

"ro-ro passenger ship" means a ship with facilities to enable road vehicles to roll on and roll off the vessel, and carrying more than twelve (12) passengers.

## 3. Passenger and vehicle fares.

(1) The fares payable by passengers and vehicles for the journey from Ċirkewwa or from any other port in Malta to Mġarr, Gozo, or vice versa, with ro-ro passenger ships, shall be those listed in the First Schedule.

(2) The fares payable in respect to any incentive scheme for the journey from Ċirkewwa or from any other port in Malta to Mġarr, Gozo, or vice versa, with ro-ro passenger ships, shall be those listed in the Second and Fourth Schedules.

## 4. Commercial vehicle fares.

The fares payable on commercial vehicles and, or other vehicles falling within the parameters listed in the Third Schedule for the journey from Ċirkewwa or from any other port in Malta to Mġarr, Gozo, or vice versa, with ro-ro passenger ships shall be those listed in the said Schedule.

## 5. Identity of Gozo resident and Maltese resident senior citizen.

A passenger may be requested to produce his legally valid identification document or Maltese resident senior citizen status at the time of the issuing of the ticket, or at any other time of boarding the vessel, or during the trip, so as to establish his identity as a Gozo permanent resident or Maltese resident senior citizen respectively.

## 6. Production of ticket.

A passenger may be requested to produce his ticket on demand by an authorised officer in order to verify and inspect such ticket and its validity, and the entitlement thereof in the case of a subsidised fare, both before boarding the vessel and, or on board such vessel.

## 7. Commercial loads.

Loads on commercial vehicles are not to exceed the registered capacity of the vehicle and, or an axle-weight in excess of thirteen (13) tons.
