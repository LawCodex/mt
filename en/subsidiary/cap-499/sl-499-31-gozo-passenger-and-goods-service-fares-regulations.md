# Gozo Passenger and Goods Service (Fares) Regulations (S.L. 499.31)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 499.31**

**GOZO PASSENGER AND GOODS SERVICE**

**(FARES) REGULATIONS\***

1st June, 2004 LEGAL NOTICE 314 of 2004, as amended by Legal Notices 425 of 2007, 426 of 2012 and 22 of 2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Gozo Passenger and Goods Service (Fares) Regulations.

## 2. Passenger and vehicle fares.

(1) The fares payable by passengers and vehicles for the journey from Ċirkewwa or Sa Maison, Malta to Mġarr, Gozo, or vice-versa, shall be those shown in the First Schedule.

(2) The fares payable in respect to any incentive scheme for the journey from Ċirkewwa, Malta, to Mġarr, Gozo, or vice-versa, shall be those shown in the Second and Third Schedules.

## 3. Commercial vehicle fares.

The fares payable on commercial and, or other vehicles falling within the parameters given in the Fourth Schedule for the journey from Ċirkewwa or Sa Maison, Malta, to Mġarr, Gozo, or vice-versa, shall be those shown in the said Schedule.

## 4. Identity of Gozo resident and senior citizen.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2012.*

A commuter may be requested to produce his legally valid identification document or "Karta Anzjan" at the time of the issuing of the ticket, or at any other time of boarding the vessel or during the trip, so as to establish his identity as a Gozo resident or senior citizen respectively.

## 5. Production of ticket.

A commuter may be requested to produce his ticket, on demand by an authorised officer, in order to check and inspect the tickets and their validity, and the entitlement thereof in the case of subsidised fares, both before boarding the vessel and, or on board the said vessel:

Provided that for the purposes of these regulations, an authorised officer shall mean an officer of the ferry operator so contracted by Government to carry out these services or a public official so appointed by the Ministry for Competitiveness and Communications.

## 6. Commercial loads.

Loads on commercial vehicles are not to exceed the registered capacity of the vehicle and, or an axle-weight in excess of 13 tons.

\*These regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 163 of 2025.

**Gozo by Night Scheme**

The Gozo by Night Scheme provides for the sale of a "group ticket" which may be purchased solely from the ticket booth at Ċirkewwa, Malta.

The ticket costs €9.32 for a car (under 5.3 metres in length) together with a driver and 3 accompanying passengers, or €6.99 for a maximum of 4 persons traveling on foot. Ticket holders will be entitled to a return passage boarding frorn Mġarr, Gozo between 22.00 hours of the same day and 04.30 hours of the following day. This fare also applies when such passengers cross over to Malta with the Passenger only Night Service during the period covered by the winter schedule.

The same fare will apply even if the number of passengers in the group is less than 4. The tickets are valid between Monday and Thursday only (excluding public holidays). There will be no refund for the unused tickets. If passengers return separately from the group, a full fare has to be paid.

The scheme may be terminated at the Company’s discretion.
