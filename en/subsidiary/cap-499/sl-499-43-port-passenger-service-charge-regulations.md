# Port (Passenger Service Charge) Regulations (S.L. 499.43)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 499.43**

**PORT (PASSENGER SERVICE CHARGE)**

**REGULATIONS**

1st March, 2007 LEGAL NOTICE 230 of 2006, as amended by Legal Notices 425 of 2007 and 192 of 2008; and Act XV of 2009.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Port (Passenger Service Charge) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 192 of 2008; XV. 2009.49. Cap. 499.*

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Authority" means the Authority for Transport in Malta established under the Authority for Transport in Malta;

"operator of passenger facilities" means the person who for the time being has the management of passenger operations.

## 3. Charges by an operator of passenger facilities.

*Amended by: L.N. 192 of 2008.*

All charges payable in respect of services rendered by an operator of passenger facilities in connection with passenger operations shall be those as determined or approved, from time to time, by the Authority.

## 4. Exemption.

*Amended by: L.N. 192 of 2008.*

These regulations shall not apply to any child under four years of age.
