# Blue Grotto and Neighbouring Caves Boats Service (Fares) Regulations (S.L. 499.46)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 499.46**

**BLUE GROTTO AND NEIGHBOURING CAVES**

**BOATS SERVICE (FARES) REGULATIONS**

28th November, 2006 LEGAL NOTICE 289 of 2006, as amended by Legal Notices 424 of 2007 and 260 of 2023.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Blue Grotto and Neighbouring Caves Boats Service (Fares) Regulations.

## 2. Tariff of fares.

The fares payable by passengers for the journey from Wied iż-Żurrieq to the Blue Grotto and neighbouring caves and vice-versa shall be those shown in the Schedule.

## 3. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 260 of 2023.*

In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires:

"Blue Grotto commercial vessels operators" means licensed commercial vessel operators who provide boat transport services from Wied iż-Żurrieq to the Blue Grotto and neighbouring caves and vice-versa;

"disability" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the United Nations Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act;

*Cap. 627.*

"neighbouring caves" means Cat’s Cave, Filfla Cave, Reflection Cave, Blue Grotto, Circle Cave, Elephant Foot, Honeymoon Cave and Window Cave.

"persons with disability" means any person in possession of a specific identification document or other documentation acknowledging such disability;

"ticket" means a printed coupon issued as stipulated in regulation 4;

## 4. Payment of fare and issue of ticket.

(1) There shall be set up a central office by the Blue Grotto commercial vessels operators, which shall be responsible for the sale and issue of tickets to passengers and the collection of the relative fares prior to embarkation on any such boat:

Provided that passengers may purchase a ticket from any person authorised by the said central office.

(2) The maximum waiting time for each trip, as computed from the time the first passenger boards a boat, shall be fifteen minutes.

(3) On payment of the relative fare and prior to departure, there shall be issued to passengers a ticket which shall contain the following information:

(a) the fare paid;

(b) the date of issue of the ticket; and

(c) the duration of the tour as being of approximately twenty-five minutes.

## 5. Production of ticket.

A passenger may be requested to produce his ticket on demand by an authorised officer, in order to check and inspect the ticket and its validity, both before boarding the boat and on board the said boat. Such authorised officer should wear an appropriate identification tag:

Provided that for the purposes of these regulations, an authorised officer shall mean an officer so appointed by the Authority for Transport in Malta.
