# Civil Aviation (Restriction of Flying) Regulations (S.L. 499.21)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 499.21**

**CIVIL AVIATION (RESTRICTION OF FLYING)**

**REGULATIONS**

31st May, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 122 of 2002, as amended by Legal Notices 411 of 2007 and 160 of 2011 and Act XL of 2023.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Civil Aviation (Restriction of Flying) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 160 of 2011; Act XL of 2023. Cap. 641*

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"aircraft" shall have the meaning assigned to it in article 2 of the Air Navigation Act;

"Director General" means the Director General responsible for Civil Aviation in Malta and, to the extent of the authority given, any person authorised in that behalf by the Director General;

"owner" in relation to an aircraft includes any person by whom the aircraft is hired at the time of the offence.

## 3. Prohibition of flying for aircraft spotting.

*Amended by: L.N. 160 of 2011.*

Aircraft in support of fishing operations in the Mediterranean Sea shall not take off from, or land in, Malta throughout each of the months of May, June and July of each year.

## 4. Powers of the Director General.

*Amended by: L.N. 160 of 2011.*

If it appears to the Director General that an aircraft is intended or likely to be flown in support of fishing operations, the Director General shall have the right to prohibit the take-off from, or landing in Malta of that aircraft during the month of June of each year.

## 5. Penalties.

*Amended by: L.N. 411 of 2007.*

An aircraft owner who contravenes regulation 3 shall be liable on conviction to a fine (multa) not exceeding two thousand and three hundred and twenty-nine euro and thirty-seven cents (2,329.37) or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both such fine and imprisonment.
