# Maximum Residue Levels of Pesticides in Produce of Plant Origin Regulations (S.L. 430.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 430.02**

MAXIMUM RESIDUE LEVELS OF PESTICIDES IN

**PRODUCE OF PLANT AND ANIMAL ORIGIN**

**(IMPLEMENTATION OF EC REGULATION)**

**REGULATIONS**

13th January, 2009

LEGAL NOTICE 14 of 2009.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Maximum Residue Levels of Pesticides in Produce of Plant and Animal Origin (Implementation of EC Regulation) Regulations.

## 2. Implementation.

These regulations implement the provisions of Articles 30, 31, 34 and 38 of Regulation (EC) No 396/2005 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 February 2005 on maximum residue levels of pesticides in or on food and feed of plant and animal origin and amending Council Directive 91/414/EEC.

## 3. Establishment of the competent authority.

The Malta Standards Authority is the competent authority within the meaning of Article 38 of Regulation (EC) No. 396/2005.

## 4. Penalties.

The sanctions applicable for infringement of the provisions of Regulation (EC) No. 396/2005 shall be those provided for in article 9 of the Pesticides Control Act.

*Cap. 430.*
