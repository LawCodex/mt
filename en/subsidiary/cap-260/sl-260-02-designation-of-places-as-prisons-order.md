# Designation of Places as Prisons Order (S.L. 260.2)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 260.02**

**DESIGNATION OF PLACES AS PRISONS ORDER**

25th February, 1966

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 166 of 1966 and LEGAL NOTICES 71 of 1980, 43 of 1987, 24 of 1993, 50 of 1995, 38 of 1997 and 29 of 2000, consolidated and 105 of 2019 and 223 of 2026.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Designation of Places as Prisons Order.

## 2. Designation of places as prisons. Cap. 260.

The places mentioned in the Schedule are hereby declared to be a prison for the purposes of the Prisons Act.

## SCHEDULE

*Amended by: L.N. 105 of 2019; L.N. 223 of 2026.*

(Article 2)

## 1. G.N. 166 of 1966.

The Police Headquarters at Floriana.

## 2. L.N. 71 of 1980.

The lock-up in the building housing the Law Courts at Valletta.

## 3. L.N. 43 of 1987.

The lock-up at Police General Headquarters at Floriana. 4. The lock-up at Paola Police Station.

## 5. L.N. 24 of 1993. L.N. 50 of 1995.

St Michael Ward at St Luke’s Hospital in Guardamangia. 6. The former Primary School at Mtaħleb.

## 7. L.N. 38 of 1997. L.N. 29 of 2000.

Forensic Unit at Mount Carmel Hospital.

## 8.

Block D, Zone B, Ħal Far Detention Centre.
