# Oath of Office (Prisons Officers) Regulations (S.L. 260.5)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 260.05**

**OATH OF OFFICE (PRISONS OFFICERS)**

**REGULATIONS**

25th June, 2013

LEGAL NOTICE 162 of 2013, as amended by Legal Notice 116 of 2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Oath of Office (Prisons Officers) Regulations.

## 2. Oath of office for newly appointed prisons officers.

(1) Before entering upon duties on appointment, all new or re-admitted prisons officers shall take the oath of office contained in the Schedule.

(2) The oath shall be taken and signed by all officers before a person duly empowered by law to administer an oath or before the Director.

## 3. Oath of office for serving prison officers.

*Amended by: L.N. 116 of 2017.*

(1) All prisons officers in the service on the date of coming into force of these regulations shall, by not later the 31st July 2017, take the oath of office contained in the Schedule.

(2) The oath shall be taken and signed by all officers before a person duly empowered by law to administer an oath or before the Director.

## SCHEDULE — Oath of office of Prisons Officers

"I ............... solemnly swear/affirm that I will bear true faith and allegiance to the people of the Republic of Malta and its Constitution and I will faithfully serve the said Republic during my service in the Prisons, as a Prisons Officer and I will subject myself to all Codes, Acts, Ordinances, orders and regulations relating to the Prisons now in force or which may from time to time be in force and will discharge all the duties of a Prisons Officer according to law, without fear or favour, affection or ill will. (So help me God)".
