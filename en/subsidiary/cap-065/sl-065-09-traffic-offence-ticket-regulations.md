# Traffic Offence Ticket Regulations (S.L. 65.09)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 65.09**

**TRAFFIC OFFENCE TICKET REGULATIONS**

29th May, 1981 LEGAL NOTICE 52 of 1981, as amended by Legal Notice 184 of 1999.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Traffic Offence Ticket Regulations.

## 2. Form of notice.

*Amended by: L.N. 184 of 1999.*

The notice to be handed over to the owner or driver, or to be affixed to the windscreen of, a motor vehicle used in the commission of such an offence as is referred to in the First and Second Schedules to the Traffic Regulation Ordinance shall contain the following:

*Cap. 65.*

(a) general description of the offence;

(b) particulars of the motor vehicle used in the commission of the offence;

(c) place, time and date of the offence;

(d) signature of the Police officer handing over or affixing the notice;

(e) information as to payment of fine (multa).
