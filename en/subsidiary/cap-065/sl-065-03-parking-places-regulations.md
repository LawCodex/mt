# Parking Places Regulations (S.L. 65.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 65.03**

**PARKING PLACES REGULATIONS**

12th May, 1961 LEGAL NOTICE 25 of 1961.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Parking Places Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires -

"agent" means the person employed by a contractor for the management of a scheduled parking place;

"car park attendant" means any person employed by a contractor for the purpose of regulating the parking of cars in a scheduled parking place;

"contractor" means the person to whom a scheduled parking place has been allocated by the Government;

"scheduled parking place" means a parking place shown in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## 3. Car park attendants.

(1) In every scheduled parking place there must be at least one uniformed attendant on duty during such times of the day and the night as the Commissioner of Police, with the advice of the Traffic Control Board, may direct.

(2) Any additional attendant or attendants shall also be uniformed while on duty.

## 4. Uniforms.

The uniforms to be worn by attendants and by any other employee shall be as approved by the Commissioner of Police with the advice of the Traffic Control Board.

## 5. Fees chargeable.

(1) There shall be charged a fee for any parking period not exceeding twelve hours, or an alternative parking fee which shall allow a motorist to park one particular car any number of times during any one day.

(2) The fees chargeable under sub-regulation (1) of this regulation shall not exceed those fixed from time to time by the Commissioner of Police with the advice of the Traffic Control Board; such fees shall be published in the Government Gazette, and shall be prominently displayed on a notice board in the scheduled parking place:

Provided that such fees may be fixed either generally or for particular scheduled parking places.

(3) There shall be no reservation of parking space for any reason whatsoever.

## 6. Payment of fee.

No person driving a car into a scheduled parking place shall refuse to pay the appropriate fee contemplated in regulation 5 immediately alter having parked the car therein.

## 7. Tariff of fees.

The tariff of fees shall be displayed conspicuously at the entrance to any scheduled parking place.

## 8. Tickets.

A ticket shall be given to the person parking a car in a scheduled parking place showing the fee paid and the registration number of the relative car.

## 9. Obstruction of carriageway.

No person may park a car in any of the carriageways inside a scheduled parking place or obstruct in any other way such carriageways.

## 10. Responsibility of contractor or his agent.

(1) The contractor and his agent, if any, shall be responsible to ensure that the provisions of regulation 9 are strictly complied with.

(2) The contractor shall be responsible for any acts or omissions on the part of the car park attendants during the discharge of their duties.

## 11. Drivers to obey Police orders or directives given by contractor or his employee.

Every person driving in or out of, or parking a car in, a scheduled parking place shall comply with any Police orders or with any directives given to him by the contractor or his agent or by car park attendants employed therein.

## 12. Applicability of regulation 65 of the Motor Vehicles Regulations.

The provisions of regulation 65 of the Motor Vehicles Regulations, shall, in so far as they are not incompatible with these Regulations, apply to car park attendants on duty at a scheduled parking place.

## SCHEDULE

(Regulation 2)

The car park adjacent to the Commonwealth Air Force Memorial at Floriana.
