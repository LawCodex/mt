## PART I — PRELIMINARY

### 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Motor Vehicles (Weights, Dimensions and Equipment) Regulations.

### 3. Classification of passenger vehicles.

*Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

Passenger vehicles shall be classified in the following categories:

**CATEGORY M**

**Motor vehicles with at least four wheels used**

**for the carriage of passengers**

\- M1 - Vehicles used for the carriage of passengers and comprising no more than eight seats in addition to the driver’s seat;

\- M2 - Vehicles used for the carriage of passengers, comprising more than eight seats in addition to the driver’s seat, and having a maximum mass not exceeding 5 tonnes;

\- M3 - Vehicles used for the carriage of passengers, comprising more than eight seats in addition to the driver’s seat, and having a maximum mass exceeding 5 tonnes.

### 4. Classification of goods vehicles.

*Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

Goods vehicles shall be classified in the following categories:

**CATEGORY N**

**Motor vehicles with at least four wheels used**

**for the carriage of goods**

\- N1 - Vehicles used for the carriage of goods and having a maximum mass not exceeding 3.5 tonnes; - N2 - Vehicles used for the carriage of goods and having a maximum mass exceeding 3.5 tonnes but not exceeding 12 tonnes;

\- N3 - Vehicles used for the carriage of goods and having a maximum mass exceeding 12 tonnes.

In the case of a towing vehicle designed to be coupled to a semi-trailer or centre-axle trailer, the mass to be considered for classifying the vehicle is the mass of the tractor vehicle in running order, increased by the mass corresponding to the maximum static vertical load transferred to the tractor vehicle by the semi-trailer or centre-axle trailer and, where applicable, by the maximum mass of the tractor vehicle’s own load.

### 5. Classification of trailers.

*Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

Trailers shall be classified as follows:

**CATEGORY O**

**Trailers (including semi-trailers)**

\- O1 - Trailers with a maximum mass not exceeding 0.75 tonnes;

\- O2 - Trailers with a maximum mass exceeding 0.75 tonnes but not exceeding 3.5 tonnes;

\- O3 - Trailers with a maximum mass exceeding 3.5 tonnes but not exceeding 10 tonnes;

\- O4 - Trailers with a maximum mass exceeding 10 tonnes.

In the case of a semi-trailer or centre-axle trailer, the maximum mass to be considered for classifying the trailer corresponds to the static vertical load transmitted to the ground by the axle or axles of the semi-trailer or centre-axle trailer when coupled to the towing vehicle and carrying its maximum load.

### 6. Conflict. S.L. 65.11

If anything contained in these regulations is inconsistent or in conflict with the Motor Vehicles Regulations, the present regulations shall prevail, and to the extent of these inconsistencies or conflicts the Motor Vehicles Regulations shall be deemed null and void.

(Omitted under the Statute Law Revision Act).

## PART II

MAXIMUM AUTHORISED DIMENSIONS IN NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL TRAFFIC AND THE MAXIMUM

**AUTHORISED WEIGHTS IN INTERNATIONAL TRAFFIC**

### 8. Goods vehicles and trailers.

*Amended by: L.N. 348 of 2005; L.N. 422 of 2016.*

Goods vehicles in categories N2 and N3 and their trailers in categories O3 and O4 registered in Malta shall comply with limit values in the First Schedule and with any other restrictions on maximum authorized dimensions and, or weights of vehicles in specified areas or on specified roads.

### 9. Conformity with First Schedule requirements.

*Amended by: L.N. 422 of 2016; L.N. 397 of 2021.*

(1) No vehicle or vehicle combination shall be allowed to circulate in Malta for the national transport of goods in Malta if it is not in conformity with the characteristics set out in points 1.1, 1.2, 1.4 to 1.8, 4.2 and 4.4 of the First Schedule.

(2) The maximum lengths laid down in point 1.1 of the First Schedule, subject where applicable to regulation 12B, and the maximum distance laid down in point 1.6 of the First Schedule, may be exceeded by 15cm for vehicles or vehicle combinations engaged in the transport of 45-foot containers or 45-foot swap bodies, empty or loaded, provided that the road transport of the container or swap body in question is part of an intermodal transport operation.

(3) The maximum authorised weights of alternatively fuelled or zero-emission vehicles shall be those set out in points 2.2.1, 2.2.2, 2.2.3, 2.2.4, 2.3.1, 2.3.2 and 2.4 of the First Schedule.

(4) Alternatively fuelled or zero-emission vehicles shall also comply with the maximum authorised weight limits set out in point 3 of the First Schedule.

(5) Articulated vehicles put into circulation before 1 January 1991 which do not comply with the specifications contained in points 1.6 and 4.4 of the First Schedule shall be deemed to comply with such specifications for the purposes of regulation 11 u 12 respectively if they do not exceed a total length of 15.50m.

### 10. Exceptions.

The Commissioner of Police in consultation with the Authority may, in special cases to be decided on a case-by-case basis, grant permission for the circulation in Malta of vehicles or vehicle combinations for the national transport of goods which are not in conformity with the characteristics set out in 1.3, 2, 3, 4.1 and 4.3 of the First Schedule.

### 11. International transport of vehicles and trailers.

*Amended by: L.N. 348 of 2005; L.N. 422 of 2016.*

Passenger transport vehicles (categories M2 and M3 and their trailers in categories O3 and O4) registered in another State and circulating temporarily in Malta as part of an international journey or on cabotage operations shall comply with the limit values established in the First Schedule and with any other restrictions on maximum authorized dimensions and, or weights of vehicles in specified areas or on specified roads.

### 12. Conformity with specifications.

*Amended by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

Vehicles registered in Malta which are used exclusively in Malta for hire or reward, shall comply with specifications established from time to time by the Authority.

### 12A. Vehicles or vehicle combinations equipped with aerodynamic devices.

*Added by: L.N. 422 of 2016. Amended by: L.N. 397 of 2021.*

(1) Vehicles or vehicle combinations which are equipped with aerodynamic devices meeting the requirements laid down in paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Fifth Schedule, and which comply with Directive 2007/46/EC, may exceed the maximum lengths provided for in point 1.1 of the First Schedule, to allow the addition of such devices to the rear of vehicles or vehicle combinations. Vehicles or vehicle combinations equipped with such devices shall comply with point 1.5 of the First Schedule, and any exceeding of the maximum lengths shall not result in an increase in the loading length of those vehicles or vehicle combinations.

(2) This regulation shall be deemed to have come into force on 15 April 2020.

### 12B. Vehicles or vehicle combinations may exceed the maximum lengths.

*Added by: L.N. 422 of 2016. Amended by: L.N. 397 of 2021.*

(1) Vehicles or vehicle combinations which fulfil the requirements laid down in paragraph 1 of the Sixth Schedule and which comply with Directive 2007/46/EC may exceed the maximum lengths laid down in point 1.1 of the First Schedule provided that their cabs deliver improved aerodynamic performance, energy efficiency and safety performance. Vehicles or vehicle combinations equipped with such cabs shall comply with point 1.5 of the First Schedule and any exceeding of the maximum lengths shall not result in an increase in the load capacity of those vehicles.

(2) This regulation shall be deemed to have come into force on 1 September 2020.

### 13. Temporary exceptions.

The Commissioner of Police in consultation with the Authority may allow vehicles or vehicle combinations incorporating new technologies or new concepts which cannot comply with one or more requirements of these regulations to carry out certain local transport operations for a trial period.

### 15. Prohibitive road signs to prevail.

The dimension and weight limits established in this Part of these regulations applicable to vehicles for circulation on the public highway shall apply without prejudice to any prohibitive road signs placed in order to safeguard certain road infrastructures, that are not suitable for long and heavy vehicles, from damage in accordance with Traffic Signs and Markings Regulations.

## PART III

INSTALLATION AND USE OF SPEED LIMITATION DEVICES 16. (1) The scope of this Part is the implementation of Council Directive 92/6/EEC on the installation and use of speed limitation devices for certain categories of motor vehicles in the Community as amended by Directive 2002/85/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council.

*Applicability. Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

(2) This Part applies to N3, N2, M3 and M2 category vehicles that are intended for use on the road, having at least four wheels and a maximum design speed exceeding 25 kph.

### 17. Exceptions.

*Amended by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

The requirements of this Part shall not apply to the following:

(a) motor vehicles used by armed forces, civil defence, fire and other emergency services and forces responsible for maintaining public order;

(b) goods vehicles whose maximum design speed is lower than 90 kph and passenger vehicles whose maximum design speed is lower than 100 kph;

(c) motor vehicles used for scientific tests on roads; and (d) motor vehicles used only for public services in urban areas.

### 18. Goods vehicles.

*Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

The following goods vehicles shall be equipped with a fully operational speed limitation device:

(a) N3 category vehicles first registered in Malta or registered in a Member State on or after 1st January, 1988;

(b) N2 category vehicles first registered in Malta on or after the 1st January, 2005 to operate both nationally and internationally or registered in a Member State and circulating on the public highway;

(c) N2 category vehicles having a maximum mass of more than 7.5 tonnes registered in Malta on or after the 1st January, 2005 to operate only in Malta;

(d) N2 category vehicles having a maximum mass of more than 3.5 tonnes but not exceeding 7.5 tonnes to be first registered in Malta on or after the 1st January, 2008 to operate only in Malta.

### 19. Diesel engined goods vehicles.

*Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

N2 category vehicles with a diesel engine first registered in Malta between 1st October, 2001 and the 31st December, 2004 shall be equipped with a fully operational speed limitation device by the -

(a) 1st January, 2006 if they are registered to be used for both national and international transport, and

(b) 1st January, 2007 if they are registered to be used only for national transport.

### 20. Maximum speed limitation for goods vehicles.

*Amended by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

The maximum speed limitation for such goods vehicles shall be set at 90 kph on the speed limitation device.

### 21. Maximum speed limitation for nationally operating N3 dangerous goods vehicles.

*Amended by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

The maximum speed limitation for vehicles registered and used exclusively in Malta for the carriage of dangerous goods shall be set at 90 kph on the speed limitation device.

### 22. Passenger vehicles.

*Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

The following passenger vehicles shall be required to be equipped with a fully operational speed limitation device:

(a) M3 category vehicles with a maximum mass of more than 10 tonnes first registered in Malta or registered in a Member State on or after the 1st January, 1988;

(b) M3 category vehicles that are first registered in Malta or registered in a Member State on or after the 1st January, 2005;

(c) M2 category vehicles that are first registered in Malta on or after the 1st January, 2005 to operate both nationally and internationally or registered in a Member State and circulating on the public highway;

(d) M2 category vehicles to be first registered in Malta on or after 1st January 2008 to operate only in Malta.

### 23. Diesel engined passenger vehicles.

*Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

M3 category vehicles with a maximum mass of more than 5 tonnes but not exceeding 10 tons and having a diesel engine and M2 category vehicles having a diesel engine first registered on or after the 1st October, 2001 shall be fitted with a speed limitation device by the -

(a) 1st January, 2006 if they are registered to be used for both national and international transport, and

(b) 1st January, 2007 if they are registered to be used only for national transport.

### 24. Maximum speed limitation for M3 passenger transport vehicles.

The maximum speed limitation for such passenger transport vehicles shall be set at 100 kph on the speed limitation device.

### 25. Technical requirements.

*Substituted by: L.N. 348 of 2005. S.L. 427.23*

Speed limitation devices shall satisfy the technical requirements laid down in the Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) Regulations. However, all vehicles covered by this Part of the regulations and registered before the 1st January, 2005 may continue to be equipped with speed limitation devices which satisfy the technical requirements laid down by the competent authorities in Member States.

### 26. Installation of devices.

Speed limitation devices shall be installed by workshops or bodies approved by the Authority or by the competent authority of an Agreement State.

## PART IV — INSTALLATION AND USE OF RECORDING EQUIPMENT — (TACHOGRAPHS)

### 27. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 348 of 2005; L.N. 345 of 2007.*

This Part shall apply to motor vehicles that are in categories M2, M3, N2 and N3 and are registered:

(a) in a Member State and are circulating on the public highway in Malta; or

(b) in Malta for international operation.

### 28. Exemptions.

*Amended by: L.N. 348 of 2005.*

Motor vehicles performing services listed in the Third Schedule shall be exempt from the application of regulation 30.

### 29. Installation and use of recording equipment.

*Amended by: L.N. 348 of 2005. Substituted by: L.N. 345 of 2007.*

(1) All motor vehicles referred to in regulation 27 put into service for the first time before 1st May 2006 shall be fitted with recording equipment installed in accordance with the Council Regulation on recording equipment and which comply with either Annex I or Annex IB and with Annex II to the said Regulation.

(2) All motor vehicles referred to in regulation 27 put into service for the first time on the 1st May 2006 or after that date shall be fitted with recording equipment installed in accordance with the Council Regulation on recording equipment and which comply with Annex IB and Annex II to that Regulation.

### 30. Use of vehicles.

*Amended by: L.N. 348 of 2005. Substituted by: L.N. 345 of 2007.*

No person shall use, or cause or permit to be used, a vehicle referred to in regulation 27 unless that vehicle is fitted with recording equipment in accordance with the preceding regulation.

### 31. Issuing of memory cards.

*Substituted by: L.N. 345 of 2007.*

The Authority shall be responsible for the issue of:

(a) a driver card to a driver who has his normal residence in Malta,

(b) a company data card to an undertaking which owns vehicles fitted with recording equipment,

(c) a test station card to fitters or workshops authorised to install and repair recording equipment, and

(d) a control card to an authorised inspecting officer.

### 32. Cards issued to fitters or workshops.

(1) Test station cards issued to approved workshops or fitters shall only be valid for a period not exceeding one year.

*Substituted by: L.N. 345 of 2007.*

(2) Where a card issued to an approved workshop or fitter is to be extended, is damaged, malfunctions, is lost or is stolen, the Authority shall issue a replacement card within five working days of receiving a detailed request to that effect.

### 33. Approval of fitters and workshops.

(1) Recording equipment may only be installed on vehicles, repaired or removed therefrom by fitters or workshops approved for that purpose by the Authority.

(2) The Authority shall, after consultation with the Minister, lay down procedures and conditions to be adopted by approved fitters and workshops.

(3) The Authority may withdraw approval from any fitter or workshop for failure to comply with these procedures and conditions.

(4) An authorised inspecting officer may enter the premises of an approved fitter or workshop for the installation, repair and inspection of recording equipment and inspect any document, record book or equipment being kept at the premises.

### 34. Resale, etc., of recording equipment.

The Authority shall take any measures necessary to ensure that the resale or decommissioning of recording equipment cannot detract, in particular, from the satisfactory application of these regulations.

## PART VI — MINIMUM TREAD DEPTH OF TYRES

### 64. Minimum tread depth of tyres.

The minimum tread depth in the main grooves of tyres used on vehicles in Category M1, N1 and O1 and O2 shall be 1.6 mm when circulating on the public highway in Malta.

### 65. Circulation on public highways.

No vehicle in Category M1, N1, O1 and O2 shall be allowed to circulate on the public highway if the tread depth of any of its tyres is less than the minimum depth stipulated in the previous regulation.

### 66. Exclusions.

The Minister may by regulation exclude from the application of the previous regulation certain vehicles which are declared to be of historical value and used infrequently on the public highway, that were originally equipped with tyres which, when new, had a tread depth of less than 1.6 mm.

**CONDITIONS RELATING TO EQUIVALENCE BETWEEN CERTAIN**

**NON-AIR SUSPENSION SYSTEMS AND AIR SUSPENSION**

**FOR VEHICLE DRIVING AXLE(S)**

\1. DEFINITION OF AIR SUSPENSION

A suspension system is considered to be air suspended if at least 75% of the spring effect is caused by the air spring.

\2. EQUIVALENCE TO AIR SUSPENSION

A suspension recognized as being equivalent to air suspension must conform to the following:

2.1. during free transient low frequency vertical oscillation of the sprung mass above a driving axle or bogie, the measured frequency and damping with the suspension carrying its maximum load must fall within the limits defined in points 2.2 to 2.5;

2.2. each axle must be fitted with hydraulic dampers. On tandem axle bogies, the dampers must be positioned to minimize the oscillation of the bogies;

2.3. the mean damping ratio D must be more than 20% of critical damping for the suspension in its normal conditions with hydraulic dampers in place and operating;

2.4. the damping ratio D of the suspension with all hydraulic dampers removed or incapacitated must be not more than 50% of D;

2.5. the frequency of the sprung mass above the driving axle or bogie in a free transient vertical oscillation must not be higher than 2,0 Hz;

2.6. the frequency and damping of the suspension are given in paragraph 3. The test procedures for measuring the frequency and damping are laid down in paragraph 4.
