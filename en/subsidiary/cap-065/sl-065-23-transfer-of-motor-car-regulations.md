# Transfer of Motor-Car Regulations (S.L. 65.23)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 65.23**

**TRANSFER OF MOTOR-CAR REGULATIONS**

1st December, 2003 LEGAL NOTICE 381 of 2003.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Transfer of Motor-Car Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 65.*

''Ordinance'' means the Traffic Regulation Ordinance;

''owner'' means the person in whose name a motor-car is licensed;

''vehicle'' includes any carriage, car, motor-car, omnibus, or other means of transport of any class or description intended for the conveyance or carriage of persons, corpses or goods.

## 3. Withholding of transfer of vehicle.

Where any fine is awarded by a court of criminal jurisdiction, or where a Commissioner for Justice has made an order for the payment of a penalty under the provisions of the Commissioners for Justice Act, in connection with any offence under the Ordinance committed by the use of any motor car or vehicle, and any such fine or penalty, as the case may be, has not been paid, the Authority shall withhold from effecting and registering the transfer of the road licence in respect of that motor-car or vehicle from the owner to any other person, until such time as the payment of such fine or penalty is effected.

*Cap. 291.*
