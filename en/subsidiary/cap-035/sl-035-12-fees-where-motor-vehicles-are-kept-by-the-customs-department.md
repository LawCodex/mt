# Fees where Motor Vehicles are kept by the Customs Department Regulations (S.L. 35.12)

FEES WHERE MOTOR VEHICLES ARE KEPT

*1*

BY THE CUSTOMS DEPARTMENT [S.L.35.12

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.12**

**FEES WHERE MOTOR VEHICLES ARE KEPT**

BY THE CUSTOMS DEPARTMENT REGULATIONS 14th February, 1989 LEGAL NOTICE 16 of 1989, as amended by Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees where Motor Vehicles are kept by the Customs Department Regulations.

## 2. Fees for keeping of motor vehicles.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

A fee of one euro and sixteen cents (1.16) for each day or part thereof shall be charged by the Comptroller of Customs where a motor vehicle is kept by the Customs Department:

Provided that such fee shall be increased to two euros and thirty-three cents (2.33) per vehicle per day or part thereof after the lapse of thirty days:

Provided further that in the case of motor cycles the fees chargeable under this regulation shall be of one fourth of the amount chargeable as aforesaid.

## 3. Power of Minister of Finance.

It shall be lawful for the Minister of Finance to reduce or remit any fees due in virtue of these regulations.
