# Fees payable for the Issue of Excise Stamps or Bands under Part C of the Sixth Schedule to the Excise Duty Act Regulations (S.L. 35.32)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.32**

**FEES PAYABLE FOR THE ISSUE OF EXCISE**

**STAMPS OR BANDS UNDER PART C OF THE**

## SIXTH SCHEDULE TO THE EXCISE DUTY ACT — REGULATIONS

17th December, 2013

LEGAL NOTICE 438 of 2013, as amended by Legal Notice 259 of 2021.

## 1. Citation and commencement.

*Amended by: L.N. 259 of 2021.*

(1) The title of these regulations is the Fees payable for the Issue of Excise Stamps or Bands under Part C of the Sixth Schedule to the Excise Duty Act Regulations.

(2) (a) The provisions of these regulations with respect to the fees chargeable, in accordance with regulation 2, for packets containing cigarettes referred to in Part C of the Sixth Schedule to the Excise Duty Act shall come into force on the date of publication of these regulations.

(b) The provisions of these regulations with respect to the fees chargeable, in accordance with regulation 2, for packets containing hand rolling tobacco or other smoking tobacco referred to in Part C of the Sixth Schedule to the Excise Duty Act shall come into force on such date as the Minister may, by notice in the Gazette, establish.

## 2. Fees payable for the Issue of Stamps or Bands under Part C of the Sixth Schedule to the Excise Duty Act.

*Substituted by: L.N. 259 of 2021. Cap. 382.*

The fee payable to the Commissioner for the issue of excise stamps or bands under the provisions of Part C of the Sixth Schedule to the Excise Duty Act shall be, for every excise stamp or band:

(a) €0.0049 in the case of Wet Glue Banderol for cigarettes;

(b) €0.01545 in the case of roll-your-own tobacco; and (c) €0.05 in the case of water pipe tobacco (also known as shisha tobacco).
