# Health Inspectorate Services (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 35.27)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.27**

**HEALTH INSPECTORATE SERVICES (FEES)**

**REGULATIONS**

18th March, 2004 LEGAL NOTICE 134 of 2004, as amended by Legal Notice 407 of 2007 and Act XIII of 2015.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Health Inspectorate Services (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires - ''Superintendent'' means the Superintendent of Public Health;

*Cap. 449.*

''food'' shall have the same meaning as is assigned to it in the Food Safety Act.

## 3. Fees due.

(1) The fees specified in the Schedule shall be levied by the Superintendent of Public Health for the services listed therein.

(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of subregulation (1), the Superintendent may at any time refuse to offer any service listed in the said Schedule.

(3) The fees prescribed in the Schedule are without prejudice to any other fees that may be due for any other services rendered by the Health Inspectorate.
