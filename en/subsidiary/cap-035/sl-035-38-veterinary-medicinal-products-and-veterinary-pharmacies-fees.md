# Veterinary Medicinal Products and Veterinary Pharmacies (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 35.38)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.38**

**VETERINARY MEDICINAL PRODUCTS AND**

**VETERINARY PHARMACIES (FEES)**

**REGULATIONS**

10th February, 2025

LEGAL NOTICE 336 of 2024.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Veterinary Medicinal Products and Veterinary Pharmacies (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires:

"Director" means the Director for Veterinary Services;

* S.L. 437.108.*

"GMP" means good manufacturing practice as defined in the Good Manufacturing Practice for Veterinary Medicinal Products Rules.

## 3. Fees.

The fees specified in the Schedule shall be payable to the Director for services listed in the said Schedule.
