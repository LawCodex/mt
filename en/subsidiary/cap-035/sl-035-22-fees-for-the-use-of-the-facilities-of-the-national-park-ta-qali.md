# Fees for the Use of the Facilities of the National Park - Ta’ Qali - Regulations (S.L. 35.22)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.22**

**FEES FOR THE USE OF THE FACILITES OF THE**

**NATIONAL PARK - TA’ QALI - REGULATIONS\***

16th February, 2010 LEGAL NOTICE 85 of 2010.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees for the Use of the Facilities of the National Park - Ta’ Qali - Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires -

"the Minister" means the Minister responsible for national parks, and "Ministry" shall be construed accordingly;

"Park" means the National Park - Ta’ Qali, as shown on the site-plan in the Second Schedule;

"the Park Unit" means the Park Unit National Park and Countryside Restoration - Ta’ Qali, responsible for the management and administration of the Park within the Ministry.

## 3. Charging of fees and other expenses.

(1) There shall be charged and collected by the Park Unit for use of facilities at the Park for cultural and other activities or events, the fees shown in the First Schedule.

(2) Any other expenses, including the provision of water, electricity and telephone services and the attendance of extra staff during such activities or events, shall be charged separately and reimbursed to the Park Unit.

(3) The fees indicated in the First Schedule shall be charged on a daily basis, provided that if the facilities are used for four to seven consecutive days, the fee shall be reduced by 10%, while there shall be a reduction of 20% if the facilities are used for eight day or more.

## 4. Bank guarantee.

The organisers of any event held at the Park may be requested to give a bank guarantee as security for the activity or event. The determination of the amount of the guarantee shall be at the discretion of the Park Unit.

## 5. Exemption from obligation to pay fees.

(1) The Minister may exempt charitable entities from paying the full fees as prescribed in these regulations.

*Cap. 492.*

(2) Non-government organisations registered under the Voluntary Organisations Act shall pay fifty per cent of the fees prescribed in these regulations.

## 6. Prohibition of certain events.

The Minister may prohibit the holding of certain events at the Park.

## 7. Minister’s discretion to establish fees in other areas within the Park.

The Minister shall have the discretion to establish the fee for activities and events held in areas other than those described in the First Schedule, but which are also within the boundaries of the Park.

\*These regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 34 of 2026.

## 8. Other permits.

The authorisation to hold an activity or event at the Park does not exempt the applicant from obtaining all the necessary official permits for the holding of the said activity or event.
