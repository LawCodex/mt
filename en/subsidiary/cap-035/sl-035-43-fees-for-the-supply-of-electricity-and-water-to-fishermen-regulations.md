# Fees for the Supply of Electricity and Water to Fishermen Regulations (S.L. 35.43)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.43**

**FEES FOR THE SUPPLY OF ELECTRICITY AND**

**WATER TO FISHERMEN REGULATIONS**

31st July, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 208 of 2026.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fees for the Supply of Electricity and Water to Fishermen Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 425.*

"Act" means the Fisheries Conservation and Management Act;

"Director" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act.

## 3. Fees.

(1) The fees listed in the Schedule shall be paid to the Director by any person making use of systems indicated by the Director for the supply of electricity and water, as may be specified by the Director by Government notice published in the Gazette.

(2) The systems indicated by the Director are intended primarily for the use of fishing vessels registered in accordance with the Act:

Provided that any other vessel may make use of the said systems, provided that the fees applicable to them are paid in accordance with these regulations.
