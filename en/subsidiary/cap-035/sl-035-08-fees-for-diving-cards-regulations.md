# Fees for Diving Cards Regulations (S.L. 35.8)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.08**

**FEES FOR DIVING CARDS REGULATIONS**

21st March, 1980 LEGAL NOTICE 31 of 1980, as amended by Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees for Diving Cards Regulations.

## 2. Charging of fee.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

There shall be payable to the Superintendent of Public Health, in respect of the first issue and on every subsequent yearly renewal of a diving card, a fee of two euros and thirty-three cents (2.33).

## 3. Duty on documents.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

There shall be affixed on every application for the granting of a diving card, a duty on documents of seventeen cents (0.17).

## 4. Validity of card.

Every card shall be valid up to the thirty-first day of December of any year in which it is issued or renewed.
