# Fees for the Use of the Hard Standing Facility Regulations (S.L. 35.45)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.45**

**FEES FOR THE USE OF THE HARD STANDING**

**FACILITY REGULATIONS**

4th August, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 213 of 2026.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fees for the Use of the Hard Standing Facility Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

* Cap. 425.*

"Director" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Fisheries Conservation and Management Act;

"Hard Standing Facility" means the facility under the management of the Director located in Marsaxlokk or any other facility as may be indicated by the said Director from time to time by means of a Government notice in the Gazette.

*    Cap. 425.*

(2) Unless the context otherwise requires, words and phrases used in these regulations which are not defined in these regulations shall have the same meaning as assigned to them in the Fisheries Conservation and Management Act.

## 3. Hard Standing Facility.

The use of the Hard Standing Facility is subject to the payment of the fees provided in these regulations.

## 4. Fees to be paid.

(1) Fees shall be due and paid to the Director in accordance with sub-regulations (2) and (3).

(2) The minimum fee due for every vessel entry into the Hard Standing Facility shall be seventy-five euro (€75), which fee shall be due in full irrespective of the amount of time that the vessel remains in the Hard Standing Facility.

(3) The following fees shall be paid to the Director for the services listed in the tables of the Schedule before the vessel is removed from the Hard Standing Facility:

Provided that where the amount due in accordance with these regulations exceeds the amount of seventy-five euro (€75) due and paid in accordance with sub-regulation (2), the said amount paid shall be credited against the total amount due as established in the Schedule.
