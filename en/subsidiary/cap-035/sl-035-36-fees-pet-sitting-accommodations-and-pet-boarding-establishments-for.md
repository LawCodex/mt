# Fees (Pet Sitting Accommodations and Pet Boarding Establishments for Cats and Dogs) Regulations (S.L. 35.36)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.36**

FEES (PET SITTING ACCOMMODATIONS AND PET BOARDING ACCOMMODATION ESTABLISHMENTS FOR CATS AND DOGS) REGULATIONS

28th August, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 150 of 2024.

## 1. Citation.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Fees (Pet Sitting Accommodations and Pet Boarding Establishments for Cats and Dogs) Regulations.

## 2. Fees.

The fees listed in the Schedule shall be payable to the Director responsible for Animal Welfare.

## Schedule

(regulation 2)

The applicable fees for the licensing of Pet Boarding accommodation establishments:

(a) First registration for a Pet Boarding €800

accommodation establishment

(b) Renewal of the licence of a Pet €200

Boarding accommodation establishment holding

ten (10) animals or less

(c) Renewal of the licence of a Pet €400

Boarding accommodation establishment holding

more than ten (10) animals

The applicable fees for the registration of Pet Sitting accommodations:

(a) First registration for a Pet Sitting €400

accommodation;

(b) Renewal of the registration of a Pet €100

Sitting accommodation
