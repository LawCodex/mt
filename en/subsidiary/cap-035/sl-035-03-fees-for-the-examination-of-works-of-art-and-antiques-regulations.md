# Fees for the Examination of Works of Art and Antiques Regulations (S.L. 35.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.03**

**FEES FOR THE EXAMINATION OF WORKS OF**

**ART AND ANTIQUES REGULATIONS**

16th January, 1968 LEGAL NOTICE 4 of 1968, as amended by Legal Notices 104 of 1981 and 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Fees for the Examination of Works of Art and Antiques Regulations.

## 2. Fees for examination of works of art and of antiques.

*Substituted by: L.N. 104 of 1981. Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

Where any work of art or any antique is examined by the Director of the Museum or his representative for the purpose of determining its age and/or its artistic value there shall be charged for such examination and paid to the Department of Museums an ad valorem fee of five per cent of its value as accepted by the Director of the Museum, together with such expenses as may be necessary in connection with such examination:

Provided that the fee so charged shall never be less than two euros and thirty-three cents (2.33).
