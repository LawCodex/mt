# Fees for the Censorship of Dramatic and other Stage Productions Regulations (S.L. 35.5)

FEES FOR THE CENSORSHIP OF DRAMATIC

*1*

AND OTHER STAGE PRODUCTIONS [S.L.35.05

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.05**

FEES FOR THE CENSORSHIP OF DRAMATIC AND OTHER STAGE PRODUCTIONS REGULATIONS 4th May, 1973 LEGAL NOTICE 45 of 1973, as amended by Legal Notices 95 of 1981, 210 of 1999 and 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees for the Censorship of Dramatic and other Stage Productions Regulations.

## 2. Fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

(1) The fees specified in the Schedule shall be leviable by the Commissioner of Police in respect of the examination of any script by censors, for the purposes of the provisions of regulation 42 of the Cinema and Stage Regulations.

*S.L. 10.17*

(2) Such fees shall include the censorship certificate provided for in regulation 46 of the regulations referred to in sub-regulation (1):

Provided that for each duplicate censorship certificate, the Commissioner of Police shall levy an additional fee of fifty-eight cents (0.58).

## SCHEDULE

*Substituted by: L.N. 95 of 1981; L.N. 210 of 1999; L.N. 407 of 2007.*

(Regulation 2

## 1.

The fees leviable by the Commissioner of Police in respect of the examination of stage productions in Maltese or any foreign language shall be at eleven euros and sixty-five cents (L11.65) per production.
