# Private Clinics (Prices for Blood and Blood Products) Regulations (S.L. 35.26)

*1*

(PRICES FOR BLOOD AND BLOOD PRODUCTS) [S.L.35.26

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.26**

PRIVATE CLINICS (PRICES FOR BLOOD AND

**BLOOD PRODUCTS) REGULATIONS**

21st November, 2003 LEGAL NOTICE 360 of 2003, as amended by Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Private Clinics (Prices for Blood and Blood Products) Regulations.

## 2. Fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

The following fees shall be charged by the Superintendent of Public Health for the supply of a bag of:

(a) Red Blood Cell concentrate ................... €88.52 (b) Single Donor Platelets ........................... €211.97 (c) Fresh frozen plasma .............................. €67.55 (d) Pooled Platelets .................................... €97.63
