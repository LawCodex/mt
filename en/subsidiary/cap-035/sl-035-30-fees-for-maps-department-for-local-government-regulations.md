# Fees for Maps (Department for Local Government) Regulations (S.L. 35.30)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.30**

**FEES FOR MAPS (DEPARTMENT FOR LOCAL**

**GOVERNMENT) REGULATIONS**

30th Marchr, 2007 LEGAL NOTICE 83 of 2007, as amended by Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fees for Maps (Department for Local Government) Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

There shall be charged by the Department for Local Government in respect of each map of AO or A1 size issued by the said Department a fee as follows:

(a) for each map supplied to a Local

**Council after the completion of the**

relative inventory No Charge (b) for each extra map requested by the

Local Council €39.60 (c) for each map requested by Government

Departments or Government entities €110.16 (d) for each map requested by a private

entity €200.33.
