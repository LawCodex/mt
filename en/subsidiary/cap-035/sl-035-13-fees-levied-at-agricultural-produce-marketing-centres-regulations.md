# Fees levied at Agricultural Produce Marketing Centres Regulations (S.L. 35.13)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.13**

**FEES LEVIED AT AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE**

**MARKETING CENTRES REGULATIONS\***

1st June, 1989 LEGAL NOTICE 54 of 1989, as amended by Legal Notices 407 of 2007 and 210 of 2015.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees levied at Agricultural Produce Marketing Centres Regulations.

## 2. Fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007; L.N. 210 of 2015.*

(1) The Director of Agriculture and Fisheries shall charge, for the use of the facilities provided by his Department at the agricultural produce marketing centres at Malta and Gozo, a fee to be paid as follows:

(a) by purchasers, a fee of six cents (0.06) for every six euro and ninety-nine cents (6.99) of sales shown on any sales voucher;

(b) by the pitkali or co-operative societies acting as pitkali, a fee of two cents for every six euros and ninety-nine cents (6.99) on every voucher relative to sales carried out through such pitkal or such co-operative;

(c) by the farmers, a fee of six cents (0.06) for every six euro and ninety-nine cents (6.99) of sales shown on any farmer’s voucher.

(2) (a) In all the cases referred to in sub-regulation (1)(a) and (c), the minimum fee shall be six cents (0.06).

(b) In all the cases referred to in sub-regulation (1)(b), the minimum fee shall be two cents (0.02).

\*These regulations have been repealed by Act XXXIV of 2023.
