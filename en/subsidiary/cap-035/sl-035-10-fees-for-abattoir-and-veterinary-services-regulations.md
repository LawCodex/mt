# Fees for Abattoir and Veterinary Services Regulations (S.L. 35.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.10**

**FEES FOR ABATTOIR AND VETERINARY**

**SERVICES REGULATIONS**

8th August, 1980 LEGAL NOTICE 68 of 1986, as amended by Legal Notices 74 of 1998 and 407 of 2007; and Act XIII of 2015.

### 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees for Abattoir and Veterinary Services Regulations.

### 2. Fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 74 of 1998.*

The fees specified in the Schedule shall be paid to the Director of Veterinary Services in the several matters to which they are applicable.
