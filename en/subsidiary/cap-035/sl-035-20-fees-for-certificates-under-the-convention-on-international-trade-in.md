## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees for Certificates under the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

The terms "Convention", "export", "import" and "re-export" used in these regulations shall be construed as having the same meaning as is given to them in the Trade in Species of Fauna and Flora Regulations, hereinafter referred to as "the Convention regulations".

*S.L. 348.01*

## 3. Fee for the issue of permits or certificates.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

(1) A fee of eleven euros and sixty-five cents (11.65) shall be payable to the Director for the Protection of the Environment on application for the issue of a permit or certificate which is required in terms of the Convention and of the provisions of regulation 6 of the Convention regulations by -

(a) an exporter with regard to an export or re-export permit or certificate; and

(b) an importer with regard to an import permit or certificate.

(2) The said Director shall refuse to issue any permit or certificate required by the Convention and by the Convention regulations until such fee is paid.
