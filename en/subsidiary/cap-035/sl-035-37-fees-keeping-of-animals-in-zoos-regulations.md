# Fees (Keeping of Animals in Zoos) Regulations (S.L. 35.37)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.37**

**FEES (KEEPING OF ANIMALS IN ZOOS)**

**REGULATIONS**

27th October, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 200 of 2024.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fees (Keeping of Animals in Zoos) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation. S.L. 439.29.

The definitions contained in the Keeping of Animals in Zoos Regulations shall where applicable, apply to these regulations.

## 3. Fees payable. S.L. 439.29.

The fees payable to the Director for Veterinary Services for the issue or renewal of a licence under the provisions of the Keeping of Animals in Zoos Regulations shall be the following:

Opening of a new zoo €500

Renewal of a zoo licence €100

*    S.L. 439.29.*

Together with the licensing fee, both the applicant and the licence holder shall be subject to a contribution fee payable to the Animal Welfare Awareness and Research Fund as established in the Keeping of Animals in Zoos Regulations as follows:

Upon application of a new zoo €1,500

Renewal of a zoo licence €400
