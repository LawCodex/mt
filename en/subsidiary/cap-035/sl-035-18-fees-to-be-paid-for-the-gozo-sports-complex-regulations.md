# Fees to be paid for the Gozo Sports Complex Regulations (S.L. 35.18)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 35.18**

**FEES TO BE PAID FOR THE GOZO SPORTS**

**COMPLEX REGULATIONS**

1st August, 1995 LEGAL NOTICE 120 of 1995, as amended by Legal Notices 407 of 2007 and 51of 2010.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees to be paid for the Gozo Sports Complex Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires - "Complex" means the Gozo Sports Complex;

"Director" means the Director for Customer Services at the Ministry for Gozo;

*Cap. 252.*

"public holiday" shall have the same meaning as is assigned to it by the National Holidays and other Public Holidays Act.

## 3. Fees for use of Complex.

(1) There shall be charged by the Director the fees listed in the Schedule for the use of the facilities at the Gozo Sports Complex.

(2) No fees shall be charged for use by individuals on Sundays and public holidays for the holding of sports activities at the Complex.

## 4. Free use.

Notwithstanding the preceding provisions of these Regulations the Director may reduce or remit any fee payable to the Complex by schools and sports associations and clubs when the activity takes place for the purpose of promoting physical education and sports.

## 5. Advertising.

Hoardings for the purpose of advertising shall be approved by the Director prior to fixing in the main hall.
