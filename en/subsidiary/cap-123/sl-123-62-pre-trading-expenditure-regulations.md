# Pre-Trading Expenditure Regulations (S.L. 123.62)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.62**

**PRE-TRADING EXPENDITURE REGULATIONS**

19th April, 2002 LEGAL NOTICE 88 of 2002.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Pre-Trading Expenditure Regulations.

## 2. Prescribed categories of expenditure. Cap. 123.

The following categories of expenditure are prescribed for the purposes of the provisions of article 14(3) of the Income Tax Act:

(a) staff training

(b) salaries and, or wages

(c) advertising.
