## 3. Special tax status.

(1) An individual, as duly represented by an authorised registered mandatary, may apply to the Commissioner for special tax status under these rules, in such form as the Commissioner may require and by paying a non-refundable administrative fee upon application. Special tax status shall be categorised as follows:

(a) global resident status;

(b) EU, EEA, Swiss resident status;

(c) retired pensioner status;

(d) UN pensioner status.

(2) The non-refundable administrative fee mentioned in sub-rule (1) shall amount to eight thousand and five hundred euro (€8,500) for special tax statuses granted in accordance with such rule.

(3) Where it is established that the individual mentioned in sub-rule (1) qualifies as a beneficiary, the Commissioner shall determine in writing that such individual is granted special tax status under these rules:

Provided that without prejudice to the powers of the Commissioner, including for the avoidance of doubt, that of determining at any time that any such special tax status is no longer applicable in line with the provisions of regulation 6, such special tax status shall apply for a term of five (5) years:

Provided further that any granting of special tax status made before or up to 31st December 2026, including for the avoidance of doubt, any applications for the granting of such status received up to the same date, shall continue to apply until 31st December 2031:

Provided further that any special tax status granted in accordance with sub-rule (1) may, at the option of the applicant, be renewed for further periods of five (5) years. Any application for renewal shall be submitted to the Commissioner, in such form and accompanied by such documents as the Commissioner may require and by paying a non-refundable administrative fee of two thousand five hundred euro (€2,500) upon application and such renewal shall not be unreasonably withheld by the Commissioner.

(4) Following the death of a beneficiary, the special tax status granted in accordance with sub-rule (1) shall be granted to a dependant of such deceased beneficiary who has inherited the property that was the primary residence of such beneficiary, or who rents a qualifying rented property immediately after the death of the said beneficiary and satisfies all the other requirements established in rule 4. Such status shall only be transferred once the said dependant provides proof to the Commissioner that all the requirements of rule 4 are satisfied in such manner as the Minister may determine after consultation with the Commissioner.

(5) An individual who has been granted special tax status in accordance with sub-rule (1) shall notify the Commissioner through his authorised registered mandatary of a change in the dependants of such individual by not later than four (4) weeks from the date when the change occurs. Such notification is to be made on such form as may be required by the Commissioner:

Provided that where such notification is not made within the time specified, the person responsible for such event shall be charged an administrative penalty of five thousand euro (€5,000).

## 4. Beneficiary.

A beneficiary is an individual who proves to the satisfaction of the Commissioner that:

(a) he is a third country national and is not a long-term resident, Maltese, EEA or Swiss national, if such individual is applying for special tax status in accordance with rule 3(1)(a);

(b) he is an EU, EEA or Swiss national but is not a Maltese national or a permanent resident of Malta, if such individual is applying for special tax status in accordance with rule 3(1)(b);

(c) he is not a Maltese national, a long-term resident or permanent resident of Malta, if such individual is applying for special tax status in accordance with rule 3(1)(c) or (d);

(d) he is not a person who benefits under the Residents Scheme Regulations, the High Net Worth Individuals - EU/EEA/Swiss Nationals Rules, the High Net Worth Individuals - Non-EU/EEA/Swiss Nationals Rules, the Qualifying Employment in Innovation and Creativity (Personal Tax) Rules, the Highly Qualified Persons Rules, the Senior Employees of Family Offices, Back Offices and Treasury Management Operations Tax Rules, or the Tax Treatment of Highly Skilled Individuals Rules, Qualifying Employment in Aviation (Personal Tax) Rules and Qualifying Employment in Maritime Activities and the Servicing of Offshore Oil and Gas Industry Activities (Personal Tax) Rules:

Provided that no individual shall benefit under multiple special tax status categories under rule 3(1);

(e) he holds a qualifying property holding;

(f) he is in receipt of stable and regular resources which are sufficient to maintain himself and his dependants without recourse to the social assistance system in Malta;

(g) he is in receipt of a pension, as supported by documentary evidence, all of which is received in Malta and constitutes at least seventy-five per cent (75%) of the beneficiary’s chargeable income, if such individual is applying for special tax status in terms of rule 3(1)(c);

(h) he is in receipt of a United Nations pension or a Widows’ and, or Widowers’ Benefit, of which at least forty per cent (40%) is received in Malta, if such individual is applying for special tax status in accordance with rule 3(1)(d);

(i) he is in possession of a valid travel document;

(j) he is in possession of sickness insurance in respect of all risks across the whole of the European Union normally covered for Maltese nationals, for himself and his dependants;

(k) he is not domiciled in Malta and that he does not, within five (5) years from the date of application in place, intend to establish his domicile in Malta;

(l) he can adequately communicate in one of the official languages of Malta; and

(m) he is a fit and proper person.

## 5. 15% rate.

(1) In these rules and in article 56(23) of the Act, the rate of fifteen cents (0.15) for every euro shall apply to any income arising outside Malta, including income arising outside Malta and received in Malta during the whole of the year in which the special tax status was granted, in the year immediately preceding the year of assessment which is received in Malta by the beneficiary, the beneficiary’s spouse and children referred to in paragraphs (b) and (d) of the definition of the term "dependant" in rule 2, with the possibility to claim relief of double taxation in accordance with article 74(a) and (b) of the Act:

Provided that the minimum amount of tax payable in terms of these rules and article 56(23) of the Act, in respect of the income arising outside Malta of the persons who have been granted special tax status in terms of rule 3(1)(a) and (b) shall be thirty-five thousand euro (€35,000) for any year of assessment. Such minimum amount is payable in full in both the year when the special tax status was granted and in the year when the individual ceases to possess the said special tax status:

Provided further that the minimum amount of tax payable in terms of these rules and article 56(23) of the Act in respect of any year of assessment by persons who have been granted special tax status in accordance with rule 3(1)(c) shall be fifteen thousand euro (€15,000) per year of assessment. Such minimum amounts are payable in full in both the year when the special tax status was granted and in the year when the individual ceases to possess the said special tax status.

(2) In the case of a beneficiary who has been granted special tax status in accordance with rule 3(1)(d), such beneficiary’s United Nations pension income or Widows’ and, or Widowers’ Benefit, as the case may be, received in Malta following the granting of the special tax status, shall be exempt from income tax. Tax shall be charged at the rate of fifteen cents (0.15) for every euro on any income, excluding the United Nations pension or Widows’ and, or Widowers’ Benefit arising outside Malta in the year immediately preceding the year of assessment in which it is received in Malta by the beneficiary, the beneficiary’s spouse and children referred to in paragraphs (b) and (d) of the definition of the term "dependant" in rule 2, following the granting of the special tax status, with the possibility to claim relief of double taxation under article 74(a) and (b) of the Act:

Provided that the minimum amount of tax payable in accordance with these rules and article 56(23) of the Act in respect of the income arising outside Malta, excluding United Nations pension income or Widows’ and, or Widowers’ Benefit of the persons referred to in this sub-rule shall be twenty thousand euro (€20,000) in respect of the beneficiary.

(3) Income of a beneficiary, the beneficiary’s spouse and children referred to in paragraphs (b) and (d) of the definition of the term "dependant" in rule 2 that is not chargeable to tax under these rules at the rate referred to in sub-rule (1) shall be charged as separate income at the rate of thirty-five cents (0.35) for every euro.

(4) Notwithstanding any provision of the Act:

(a) subject to the provisions of paragraph (b), the minimum tax referred to in the provisos to sub-rule (1) shall be payable by not later than the 30th April of the year immediately preceding the relevant year of assessment. Such payment shall be accompanied by a return submitted to the Commissioner that provides proof that all the requirements of rule 4 continue to be satisfied. The said return, which shall be in such form as the Commissioner may determine, does not need to be submitted in the year in which the special tax status is granted;

(b) in the case of the year in which the special tax status is granted, where it is evident that the special tax status shall not be granted before the 30th April, the minimum tax referred to in the provisos to sub-rule (1) is to be paid before the special tax status is granted;

(c) any tax paid in accordance with the provisions of sub-rule (1) shall not be refundable.

(5) (a) Notwithstanding any provision in the Act, an individual who falls under the definition of "long-term resident" or "permanent resident of Malta" as defined in rule 2 shall be taxable on any income accruing in or derived from Malta or otherwise, and whether received in Malta or otherwise in respect of income mentioned in article 4 of the Act and subject to tax at the rates mentioned in article 56 of the Act.

(b) In the case where the individual referred to in paragraph (a) is a beneficiary or dependant, the relevant authorised registered mandatary shall:

(i) enquire and obtain information from the relevant beneficiary as to whether such beneficiary or any of his dependants falls under the said definition of "long-term resident" or "permanent resident of Malta" as at the 31st December of each year from the year of the appointed date onwards; and

(ii) notify the Commissioner of any individual that is a beneficiary or dependant that falls under the said definition of "long-term resident" or "permanent resident of Malta" by not later than the 30th April following the 31st December referred to in sub-paragraph (i) on such form as the Commissioner may determine. Where the authorised registered mandatary shall not be able to obtain such information, he is to notify the Commissioner by the said 30th April of this state of affairs and provide proof that at least two (2) attempts at enquiry were made. Where such notifications shall not be made within the time specified, the authorised registered mandatary shall be charged an administrative penalty of ten thousand euro (€10,000).

## 6. Cessation of special tax status.

(1) An individual shall, with immediate effect from the beginning of the relative year of assessment, cease to possess special tax status under these rules:

(a) if the individual becomes a Maltese national;

(b) if at any time, after the appointed day, such individual does not hold a qualifying property holding, including in the case where the said individual lets or sublets the qualifying property holding;

(c) if at any time, after the appointed day, such individual becomes a long-term resident or permanent resident of Malta;

(d) in cases where a beneficiary was granted special tax status in terms of rule 3(1)(c), if the individual fails to receive in Malta all the pension indicated in the documentary evidence submitted to the Commissioner according to rule 4(g);

(e) in cases where a beneficiary was granted special tax status in terms of rule 3(1)(d), if the individual fails to receive in Malta at least forty percent (40%) of the United Nations pension or Widows’ and, or Widowers’ Benefit as indicated in the documentary evidence submitted to the Commissioner according to rule 4(h);

(f) if after the appointed day, the individual does not have private medical insurance in respect of all risks normally covered for Maltese nationals for himself and his dependants;

(g) if the individual’s stay is not in the public interest;

(h) if the individual stays in any other jurisdiction for more than one hundred and eighty-three (183) days in a calendar year;

(i) if the individual ceases to be duly represented by an authorised registered mandatary;

(j) if any dependants, including additional dependants are determined to be not fit and proper;

(k) if the beneficiary fails to effect payment of the minimum amount of tax payable in terms of rule 5(1) or 5(2) or fails to effect payment within the time limit imposed in accordance with rule 5(4);

(l) if the beneficiary fails to submit the return in accordance with rule 5(5); or

(m) if the individual fails to possess any criteria required in accordance with rule 4.

(2) An individual who ceases to possess special tax status for any of the reasons specified in sub-rule (1) shall notify the Commissioner through his authorised registered mandatory of such fact by not later than four (4) weeks from the date when he becomes aware of such fact. This notification is to be made on such form as may be required by the Commissioner. Where such notification is not made within the time specified, the individual responsible under this paragraph shall be charged an administrative penalty of five thousand euro (€5,000).

(3) An individual shall with immediate effect, cease to possess special tax status from the relative year of assessment in accordance with these rules if he notifies the Commissioner through his authorised registered mandatory of his intention not to remain in possession of such special tax status on such form as the Commissioner may require.

(4) An individual shall, with effect from the beginning of the relative year of assessment, cease to possess special tax status under these rules if he is in breach of the Act and, or of the Income Tax Management Act.

(5) The Minister shall have the power to pardon any failure to satisfy any one of the conditions in sub-rule (1) if:

(a) such failure was due to unforeseen circumstances which are beyond the control of the individual;

(b) such individual notifies the Minister of such failure; and

(c) such individual has exercised his best efforts to remedy the failure.

## 8. Production of information and documents.

For the purpose of ascertaining an individual’s entitlement to rights acquired in accordance with these rules and ensuring the proper application of these rules, the Commissioner may require that individual or the relevant authorised registered mandatary to produce, within the time indicated by the Commissioner in that request, such information and documents as the Commissioner may consider necessary, including certifications and declarations.

## 9. Issue of an assessment.  Cap. 372.

If an individual benefits under these rules where such individual was not so entitled, the Commissioner may issue an assessment in terms of article 31 of the Income Tax Management Act.

## 10. Return.  Cap. 372.

The return required in terms of article 10 of the Income Tax Management Act shall, in the case of a beneficiary in accordance with these rules, include such form as the Commissioner may require.

## 11. Authorisation.

(1) An individual shall authorise a person who is an authorised registered mandatary to act on his behalf in respect of all applications, correspondence, submissions, filings, declarations and notifications contemplated in accordance with these rules. Such acts shall be submitted by the said authorised registered mandatary.

(2) The authorisation referred to in sub-rule (1) shall be made in such form as the Commissioner may require.

(3) Unless the Commissioner specifically authorises in writing, a person may not authorise more than one (1) authorised mandatary.

(4) Registration as an authorised registered mandatary with the Commissioner shall be made by filling in and submitting to the Commissioner such form as the Commissioner may require.

(5) Registration as an authorised registered mandatary in accordance with these rules shall be subject to such terms and conditions as the Commissioner may impose from time to time.

(6) Acceptance of registration in accordance with these rules shall be notified by the Commissioner to the person requesting such registration.

(7) When a person who has been registered with the Commissioner desires to cancel such registration, he shall make a request in such form as the Commissioner may require.

## 12. Compliance monitoring.

The Commissioner may give notice in writing to any authorised registered mandatary to furnish him, within a reasonable time stated in such notice, not being less than twenty (20) days, such information as may be necessary in order to verify that such authorised registered mandatary is complying with the obligations established in these rules. Such requests shall be sent to that authorised registered mandatary either by electronic means to his verified email address or sent by post to the authorised registered mandatary’s registered address. Where any of the information requested is not submitted to the Commissioner within the time limit specified in the notice, the authorised registered mandatary shall be liable to an administrative penalty of not less than five hundred euro (€500) and not exceeding nineteen thousand, two hundred and fifty euro (€19,250):

Provided that where the Commissioner is satisfied that the default in respect of which the administrative penalties have been imposed were justifiable, the Commissioner may remit such administrative penalties in whole or in part as he may think fit. In every case the Commissioner shall inform the said authorised registered mandatary in writing:

Provided further that where an authorised registered mandatary repeatedly fails to carry out a function required under these rules, he shall cease to be an authorised registered mandatary, in such manner and form as the Commissioner may determine. For the purposes of this paragraph, "repeatedly" means more than two (2) failures of carrying out functions required under these rules.
