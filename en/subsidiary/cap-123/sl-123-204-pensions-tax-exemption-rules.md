# Pensions (Tax Exemption) Rules (S.L. 123.204)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.204**

**PENSIONS (TAX EXEMPTION) RULES**

4th March 2022

LEGAL NOTICE 98 of 2022, as amended by Legal Notices 220 of 2022, 48 of 2023, 5 and 356 of 2024 and 53 of 2026.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these rules is the Pensions (Tax Exemption) Rules.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Substituted by: L.N. 220 of 2022.*

In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 123.*

"Act" means the Income Tax Act;

"pension income" means pension income derived by an individual who is at least sixty-one (61) years old in the year in which he receives the said income and which would be taxable, were it not for these rules, according to the provisions of article 4(1)(d) of the Act.

## 3. Tax exemption.

*Substituted by: L.N. 220 of 2022; L.N. 48 of 2023. Amended by: L.N. 5 of 2024; L.N. 356 of 2024; L.N. 53 of 2026.*

Pension income derived by an individual on or after 1 January 2022 shall be partially or fully exempt from tax as follows:

Applicability of the exemption Amount exempt

Pension income derived in Twenty per cent (20%), the year immediately preceding thebut not exceeding two year of assessment 2023 thousand, eight hundred and sixty-four euro (€2,864)

Pension income derived in Forty per cent (40%), the year immediately preceding thebut not exceeding five year of assessment 2024 thousand, nine hundred and eighty-seven euro (€5,987) Pension income derived in Sixty per cent (60%), the year immediately preceding thebut not exceeding nine year of assessment 2025 thousand, seven hundred and thirty-two euro (€9,732)

Pension income derived in Eighty per cent (80%), the year immediately preceding thebut not exceeding thirteen year of assessment 2026 thousand, three hundred and nine euro (€13,309);

Pension income derived in One hundred per cent the year immediately preceding the(100%), but not exceeding year of assessment 2027 and inthirty-seven thousand, one subsequent years hundred and four euro (€37,104)

## 4. Tax rebate.

*Added by: L.N. 53 of 2026.*

In the case of an individual who, in the year of assessment 2027 or any subsequent year, benefits from the exemption granted under rule 3 and who is chargeable to tax at the rates specified in article 56(1)(a)(i) of the Act, a tax rebate shall be granted as a set-off against the tax on the chargeable income, as follows:

Tax rebate = (chargeable income less 15,000) multiplied by 15%:

Provided that such rebate shall not exceed €540:

Provided further that any negative result shall not be taken into account and any rebate granted under this rule may not give rise to a refund or be available to be carried forward.
