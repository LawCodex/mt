# Agricultural Subsidies (Income Tax Exemption) Rules (S.L. 123.91)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.91**

**AGRICULTURAL SUBSIDIES (INCOME TAX**

**EXEMPTION) RULES**

1st January, 2005 LEGAL NOTICE 52 of 2006.

## 1. Title and commencement.

The title of these regulations is the Agricultural Subsidies (Income Tax Exemption) Rules, and they shall be deemed to have come into force as from the year of assessment commencing on the 1st of January, 2005.

## 2. Exemption. Cap. 123.

The subsidies that are exempt from income tax in accordance with the provisions of article 12(1)(r) of the Income Tax Act are the following:

(a) subsidies granted under the Social Market Policy Programme for Maltese Agriculture (SMPPMA) in the following production sectors:

(i) fresh fruit;

(ii) fresh vegetables;

(iii) fruit and vegetables for processing;

(iv) wine;

(v) pigmeat;

(vi) poultry meat;

(vii) eggs;

(viii) milk;

(b) European Union farm subsidies granted under the Common Agricultural Policy (CAP):

(i) aid to producers of tomatoes for processing;

(ii) sheep premium;

(iii) slaughter premium (bovines);

(iv) veal premium;

(v) special beef premium;

(vi) aid on arable crops;

(vii) aid to producer organisations and producer groups;

(viii) aid on olive oil production;

(ix) set-aside aid;

(x) milk premium;

(c) aid measures under the Rural Development Programme (RDP):

(i) aid under the pre-accession Rural Stewardship Scheme for Rubble Walls;

(ii) aid under the pre-accession Rural Stewardship Scheme for Producer Groups;

(iii) aid under the RDP’s Producer Group measure;

(iv) aid under the RDP’s Agri-environment measures;

(v) aid under the RDP’s Less Favoured Areas measure;

(vi) aid under the RDP’s Ad Hoc measure;

(vii) aid under the RDP’s Meeting Standards measure;

(viii) aid under the Structural Funds measure on Investments in Agricultural Holdings;

(ix) aid under the Structural Funds measure on Marketing and Processing of Agricultural Products.
