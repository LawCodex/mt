# Income Tax Exemption Order (S.L. 123.21)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.21**

**INCOME TAX EXEMPTION ORDER**

2nd April, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 78 of 2002, as amended by Legal Notices 181 of 2015, 120 of 2022 and 19 of 2024.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Income Tax Exemption Order. 2. The allowances and benefits, as listed in the Schedule to this Order, and any other non-contributory allowances and benefits, payable under the Social Security Act, are exempt from the payment of income tax.

*Exemption. Amended by: L.N. 181 of 2015. Cap. 318.*
