# Fiscal Exemptions (Institutions of the European Communities) Rules (S.L. 123.75)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.75**

**FISCAL EXEMPTIONS (INSTITUTIONS OF THE**

**EUROPEAN COMMUNTIES) RULES**

1st May, 2004 LEGAL NOTICE 289 of 2004.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these rules is the Fiscal Exemptions (Institutions of the European Communities) Rules.

## 2. Institutions of the European Communities to which these regulations shall apply.

The provisions of these regulations shall apply to the:

(a) European Parliament

(b) Council of the European Union

(c) European Commission

(d) European Court of Justice of the European Communities

(e) European Court of Auditors

(f) other bodies:

(i) European Central Bank

(ii) European Investment Bank

(iii) European Ombudsman

(iv) Any other body or organisation of the European Communities as may be approved by the Minister of Foreign Affairs and Investment Promotion in concurrence with the Minister of Finance from time to time.

## 3. Fiscal exemptions.

The aforesaid Institutions of the European Communities shall be entitled to the exemptions from taxes and duties as specified herein:

*Cap. 123.*

(a) with reference to the Income Tax Act, in terms of article 12(2) thereof;

*Cap. 368.*

(b) with reference to the Motor Vehicles Registration and Licensing Act, in terms of article 19(2) thereof.
