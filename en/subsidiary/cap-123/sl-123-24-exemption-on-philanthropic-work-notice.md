# Exemption on Philanthropic Work Notice (S.L. 123.24)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.24**

INCOME TAX EXEMPTION ON PHILANTHROPIC

**WORK NOTICE**

GOVERNMENT NOTICES 455 of 1976, 142 of 1977, 561 of 1983, 698 of 1985, 326 of 1987, 666 of 1988, and LEGAL NOTICES 182 of 1991, 64 of 1994, 76 of 1996, 68 of 1997, 47 of 1998, 95 of 2000, 9 of 2001, 87 of 2002, 23, 256 of 2003, 187, 415, 513 of 2004, 52, 234 of 2005, 235 of 2006, 61, 305 of 2007, 288 of 2008, 338 of 2009, 277 of 2011, 71 of 2012, 67, 398 of 2013, 272 of 2014, 65 341 of 2017, 224 of 2018 and 167 of 2019, 177 of 2021, 62 of 2024 and 229 of 2026 consolidated.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Notice is the Income Tax Exemption on Philanthropic Work Notice.

## 2. Approved institutions. Cap. 123.

The institutions and organizations as listed in the Schedule to this Notice are to be considered exempt from income tax for the purposes of article 12(1)(e) of the Income Tax Act.
