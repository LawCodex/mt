# Deduction (Childcare Fees) Rules (S.L. 123.121)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.121**

**DEDUCTION (CHILDCARE FEES) RULES**

1st January, 2010

LEGAL NOTICE 466 of 2010,as amended by Legal Notice 341 of 2018.

## 1. Citation and commencement.

(1) The title of these rules is the Deduction (Childcare Fees) Rules.

(2) These rules shall come into force as from the year of assessment 2011.

## 2. Conditions applicable.

*Amended by: L.N. 341 of 2018. Cap. 123.*

The deduction that may be claimed by an individual in terms of article 14C of the Income Tax Act, hereinafter referred to as "the Act", shall only be allowed where:

(a) a fee is payable after deducting any subsidies or rebates that may be received for the childcare service; and

(b) the childcare centre is registered or otherwise approved by the by the entity responsible for regulating childcare centres; and

(c) the payment and the details of the individual making the claim are confirmed by information provided, in such format and content as determined by the Commissioner, by the person running the childcare centre.

## 3. Information.

*Amended by: L.N. 341 of 2018.*

The entity responsible for regulating childcare centres shall provide the Commissioner, for the purposes of the administration of article 14C of the Act and of these rules, with the informationin its possession regarding the childcare centres as the Commissioner may require.
