# Recognised Pension Commutation (Exemption) Rules (S.L. 123.171)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.171**

**RECOGNISED PENSION COMMUTATION**

**(EXEMPTION) RULES**

1st January, 2016

LEGAL NOTICE 262 of 2016.

## 1. Citation and commencement.

(1) The title of these rules is the Recognised Pension Commutation (Exemption) Rules.

(2) These rules shall come into force with effect from the year of assessment 2017.

## 2. Interpretation.

For the purposes of these rules:

*Cap. 123.*

"the Act" means the Income Tax Act;

"the Commissioner" means the Commissioner for Revenue;

*Cap. 514.*

"recognised retirement scheme" means a retirement scheme licensed, registered or otherwise authorised under the Retirement Pensions Act, and which qualifies as a Recognised Overseas Pension Scheme in accordance with the Finance Act 2004 of the Parliament of the United Kingdom;

*Cap. 514.*

"retirement date" means the date when the payment of retirement benefits commences, as prescribed in terms of the Retirement Pensions Act, including any regulations or Pension Rules issued thereunder;

"retirement scheme administrator" means an entity appointed to perform duties in connection with the overall operation and the ordinary and day-to-day operations of a recognised retirement scheme and which is so licensed, registered or otherwise authorised under the Retirement Pensions Act, including any regulations or Pension Rules issued thereunder;

*Cap. 514.*

"total pension" means the total value of the assets held by a recognised retirement scheme for a member and which are available to provide retirement benefits to the member, such value being determined on the member’s retirement date.

## 3. Exemption.

(1) The total amount of capital sum received by way of commutation of pension by a member of a recognised retirement scheme which is exempt from the tax under the provisions of article 12(1)(h) of the Act shall amount to a maximum of thirty percent (30%) of the total pension.

(2) The provisions of sub-rule (1) shall apply irrespective of whether the said capital sum is paid as one lump sum or in a series of tranches within one year from retirement date as prescribed under the Retirement Pensions Act, including any regulations or Pension Rules issued thereunder.

*Cap. 514.*

## 4. Information.

The retirement scheme administrator shall provide the Commissioner with the information in its possession regarding sums paid to a member of a recognised retirement scheme as the Commissioner may require for the purpose of administration of these rules.
