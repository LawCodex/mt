# Tax Credit (Construction Waste Recycling) Rules (S.L. 123.186)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.186**

**TAX CREDIT**

**(CONSTRUCTION WASTE RECYCLING) RULES**

28th June, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 209 of 2018,as amended by Legal Notice 47 of 2019.

## 1. Title.

The title of these rules is Tax Credit (Construction Waste Recycling) Rules.

## 2. Definitions.

*Amended: L.N. 47 of 2019.*

In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:

"authorised person" means a person who is in possession of a permit issued by the Environment and Resources Authority for the acceptance in his quarry of construction and demolition material from third parties;

"qualifying services" means the acceptance by an authorised person of construction and demolition material from third parties at a fee not exceeding eight euro (€8) per tonne;

"relevant year" means the calendar year 2017, 2018 or 2019. 3. (1) An authorised person who, during a relevant year, provides qualifying services, may claim a tax credit equivalent to 25% of the gross fees paid to him for the provision of those services:

*Tax credit.*

Provided that the tax credit shall not exceed the tax chargeable on the income derived by the authorised person during that year from the provision of those services.

(2) The claim for a tax credit in accordance with these rules shall be made in such manner and on such form as the Commissioner may approve and subject to the production of such documents or other evidence as the Commissioner may require.
