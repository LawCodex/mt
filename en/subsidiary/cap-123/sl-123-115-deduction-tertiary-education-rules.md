# Deduction (Tertiary Education) Rules (S.L. 123.115)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.115**

**DEDUCTION (TERTIARY EDUCATION) RULES**

1st January, 2011

LEGAL NOTICE 427 of 2010.

## 1. Citation and commencement.

(1) The title of these rules is the Deduction (Tertiary Education) Rules.

(2) These rules shall come into force as from the year of assessment 2011, in respect of fees paid as from 1st January 2010.

## 2. Deduction. Cap. 123.

(1) Where a deduction is claimed by an individual in terms of article 14F of the Income Tax Act, the said deduction shall, subject to the provisions of these rules, be allowed against his income for the year in which he has successfully completed his studies:

Provided that the deduction to be allowed shall be the amount of fees actually paid or ten thousand euro (€10,000), whichever is the lesser:

Provided further that, to the extent that the deduction cannot be wholly set off against the income of the individual or of his spouse, where applicable, for the aforesaid year, it shall be carried forward and set off against the income for subsequent years in succession.

(2) The deduction shall only be allowed where the individual has not received or will not receive any form of compensation regarding the fees paid for the said studies from the Government or from any other Government or private entity.

(3) No deduction with respect to tertiary studies may be claimed under these rules if the individual claims or has claimed any deduction or tax credit for any year in respect of the said tertiary studies.

## 3. Documentation.

The deduction in accordance with rule 2 shall only be allowed where the individual claiming it submits to the Commissioner, in the first year the claim is made, together with the income tax return for the year, the certificate relative to the completed program of studies awarded by the relative institution, such certificate being confirmed as of a tertiary education level by the Malta Qualifications Recognition Information Centre.
