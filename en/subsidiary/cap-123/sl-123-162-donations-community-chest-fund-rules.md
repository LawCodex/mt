# Donations (Community Chest Fund) Rules (S.L. 123.162)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.162**

DONATIONS (COMMUNITY CHEST FUND) RULES 28th November, 2014

LEGAL NOTICE 412 of 2014, as amended by Legal Notices 2 of 2016, 2, and 343 of 2017, 321 of 2018, 325 of 2019, 63 of 2021, 103 of 2022 and 62 of 2023.

## 1. Citation and commencement.

(1) The title of these rules is the Donations (Community Chest Fund) Rules.

(2) These rules shall be deemed to have come into force as from the year of assessment 2015.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 2 of 2016; L.N. 2 of 2017.; L.N. 342 of 2017; L.N. 321 of 2018; L.N. 325 of 2019; L.N. 63 of 2021; L.N. 103 of 2022; L.N. 62 of 2023.*

(1) These rules shall apply only to companies as defined in the Act.

(2) Repealed by Legal Notice 62 of 2023.

## 3. Deduction for donations.

*Amended by: L.N. 2 of 2016.*

(1) Where a company makes a cash donation of not less than two thousand euro (€2000) to the Malta Community Chest Fund, such donation may be claimed as a deduction against its income for the relative year of assessment, provided that a relevant signed certificate in respect of such donation has been issued by the secretary of the Malta Community Chest Fund and is attached to and submitted together with the income tax return for the year.

(2) The certificate referred to in sub-rule (1) shall show the date of the donation, the name and company registration number of the donor and the value of the donation.

(3) The deduction as aforesaid shall only be allowed where the Malta Community Chest Fund has forwarded the details of all qualifying donations to the Commissioner by the 30th June of the year following that in which the donations were made.
