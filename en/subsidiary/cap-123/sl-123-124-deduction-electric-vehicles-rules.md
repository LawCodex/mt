# Deduction (Electric Vehicles) Rules (S.L. 123.124)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.124**

**DEDUCTION (ELECTRIC VEHICLES) RULES**

1st January, 2011

LEGAL NOTICE 13 of 2011, as amended by Legal Notice 231 of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these rules is the Deduction (Electric Vehicles) Rules.

## 2. Definitions.

*Amended by: L.N. 231 of 2016.*

In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:

"electric vehicle" means a vehicle that derives its motive power exclusively from an electric motor;

"hybrid vehicle" means a vehicle having at least two different energy converters and two different energy storage systems for the purpose of vehicle propulsion;

"qualifying expenditure" means expenditure of a capital nature incurred on the acquisition of new electric or hybrid vehicles;

"qualifying person" means a company that carries on a trade or business.

## 3. Deduction.

*Amended by: L.N. 231 of 2016.*

(1) Where a qualifying person incurs qualifying expenditure in the year preceding the year of assessment, a deduction shall be allowed against the said person’s income charged to tax for such year of assessment. The said deduction shall be equivalent to 150% of the cost incurred in the case of an electric vehicle and 125% of the cost incurred in the case of a hybrid vehicle.

(2) The total deduction claimed under these rules as aforesaid shall not exceed forty thousand euro (€40,000) in the case of an electric vehicle and thirty thousand euro (€30,000) in the case of a hybrid vehicle.

(3) Where a deduction is claimed under these rules, no deduction in respect of wear and tear may be claimed in respect of the same electrical vehicle.

(4) Any grant or other benefit received from the Government in respect of a vehicle to which these rules apply shall be deducted from the cost incurred for the purpose of calculating the deduction allowable under this rule.
