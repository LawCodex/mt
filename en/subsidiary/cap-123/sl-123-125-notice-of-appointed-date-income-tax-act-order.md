# Notice of Appointed Date (Income Tax Act) Order (S.L. 123.125)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 123.125**

**NOTICE OF APPOINTED DATE**

**(INCOME TAX ACT) ORDER**

1st January, 2011

LEGAL NOTICE 102 of 2011.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Notice of Appointed Date (Income Tax Act) Order.

## 2. Notice of appointed day.

It is hereby notified that the Minister of Finance, the Economy and Investment has appointed the 1st January, 2011 to be the date referred to in the subarticle (2) of article 15 of the Income Tax Act.
