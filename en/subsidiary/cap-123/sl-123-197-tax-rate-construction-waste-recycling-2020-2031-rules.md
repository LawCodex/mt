## 1. Citation.

The title of these rules is the Tax Exemption (Construction Waste Recycling) (2020-2031) Rules.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 301 of 2023; L.N. 29 of 2025; L.N. 25 of 2026; L.N. 210 of 2026.*

In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 123.*

"Act" means the Income Tax Act;

"authorised person" means a person who is in possession of a valid permit issued by the Environment and Resources Authority for the acceptance of construction and demolition material from third parties at his quarry and, or to accept and carry out recycling or recovery in his establishment or undertaking;

"qualifying services" means the acceptance by an authorised person of construction and demolition material from third parties, at a fee of not exceeding sixteen euro (€16) per tonne, net of value added tax;

"relevant year" means any calendar year from 2020 to 2031, both years inclusive;

"total fee" means the total amount that an authorised person charges or receives, directly or indirectly, for the provision of qualifying services, including any other payment, by whatever name or description, including but not limited to, a service fee, an administrative charge, a processing fee, a surcharge, or any other charge of any kind imposed in connection with the same service, but excluding value added tax.

## 3. Exemption from tax on income from qualifying services.

*Substituted by: L.N. 218 of 2020; L.N. 284 of 2020. Amended by: L.N. 210 of 2026.*

Income derived by an authorised person from qualifying services during a relevant year shall be exempt from the tax that would otherwise be chargeable on that income subject to the following conditions:

*  *

(a) the income in question is declared in a tax return filed in accordance with article 10 of the Income Cap. 372. Tax Management Act for the respective year of assessment; and

(b) an amount of tax is paid equivalent to five cents (€0.05) on every euro of that income ; and

(c) the tax on the chargeable income of the authorised person for the respective year of assessment shall be calculated at the rate or rates that would apply if that income included also the income exempted by these rules; and

(d) the authorised person produces such documents or other evidence as the Commissioner may require;

(e) the total fee charged or received by the authorised person for the qualifying services shall not exceed sixteen euro (€16) per tonne, net of value added tax, and no additional payment of any kind, by whatever name or description, shall be charged or paid, directly or indirectly, to or by third parties in connection with such service; and

(f) the authorised person shall not close his quarry or cease accepting construction and demolition material from third parties without a valid reason. Breach of 4. Where an authorised person, without a valid reason, conditions.

breaches the conditions established in paragraphs (e) or (f) of rule 3, the Added by:

L.N. 210 of 2026. following provisions shall apply:

(a) where the breach relates to the condition established in paragraph (e) of rule 3, the tax exemption granted by these rules pursuant to paragraph (b) of rule 3 shall be deemed not to have been applicable for the relevant year in which the breach occurred, and all income derived from qualifying services during that year shall instead be subject to tax in accordance with the Act as if the exemption had not applied;

(b) the Commissioner shall issue a notice of assessment to the authorised person for the recovery of the tax due under paragraph (a), and the authorised person shall pay that amount due within thirty (30) days of being notified of such notice;

(c) where the breach relates to the condition established in paragraph (f) of rule 3, the tax exemption granted by these rules shall be suspended with immediate effect from the date of the unauthorised closure and the exemption shall not be reapplied unless and until the quarry is reopened.

## 5. Power of inspection.

*Added by: L.N. 210 of 2026.*

(1) The Commissioner, or any person authorised by him in writing for that purpose shall have the power at all reasonable times to:

(a) enter and inspect any quarry and, or any recycling or recovery establishment or undertaking operated by an authorised person;

(b) examine, take copies of, or extract data or other information relating to the qualifying services or the fees charged by such authorised person from any book, record, account or document.

(2) Every authorised person shall provide all reasonable facilities and assistance to the Commissioner or to any person authorised by him in order to exercise the powers conferred by this rule.
