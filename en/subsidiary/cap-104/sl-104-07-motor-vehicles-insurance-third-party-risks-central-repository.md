**REGULATIONS**

5th August, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 188 of 2008.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Motor Vehicles Insurance (Third-Party Risks) (Central Repository) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 104.*

"the Ordinance" means the Motor Vehicles Insurance (Third- Party Risks) Ordinance.

## 3. Central Repository.

The Central Repository referred to in article 9F of the Ordinance, shall be the Motor Insurance Information Centre set up under the Motor Vehicle (Insurance Information Centre) Regulations.

*S.L. 104.06*
