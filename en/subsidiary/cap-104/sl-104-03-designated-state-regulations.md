# Designated State Regulations (S.L. 104.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 104.03**

**DESIGNATED STATE REGULATIONS**

1st March, 2004;

1st May, 2004 LEGAL NOTICE 93 of 2004.

## 1. Title and commencement.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Designated State Regulations.

(2) These regulations shall come into force on the 1st March, 2004 when these refer to a Member State of the European Union, and on the 1st May, 2004 when these refer to a Member State of the European Economic Area.

## 2. Scope of regulations.

The scope of these regulations is to list such States as are to be listed by regulations in terms of the definition "designated State" in article 2 of the Motor Vehicles Insurance (Third Party Risks) Ordinance.

*Cap. 104.*

## 3. Definition of Designated State.

For the purposes of these Regulations, a designated State shall mean -

*Cap. 460.*

(a) a Member State of the European Union in terms of the European Union Act, and

(b) a Member State of the European Economic Area that is a contracting party to the Agreement signed at Oporto on the 2nd May, 1992 and the Protocol adjusting that agreement, signed in Brussels on 17th March, 1993.
