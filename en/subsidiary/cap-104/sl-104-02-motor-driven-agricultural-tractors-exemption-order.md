# Motor Driven Agricultural Tractors Exemption Order (S.L. 104.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 104.02**

**MOTOR DRIVEN AGRICULTURAL TRACTORS**

**EXEMPTION ORDER**

30th December, 1957 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 809 of 1957.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Motor Driven Agricultural Tractors Exemption Order.

## 2. Exemption.

Any kind of motor-driven agricultural tractor or machine used and intended solely for agricultural purposes is exempted from the provisions of article 3 of the Motor Vehicles (Third Party Risks) Ordinance, provided it is incapable of developing a speed exceeding eight miles per hour and is so constructed as to be controlled not by a driver riding on the tractor or machine proper but a person on foot.

*Cap. 104.*
