# Widows' and Orphans' Pensions (Cross-Border Payments) Regulations (S.L. 58.1)

WIDOWS’ AND ORPHANS’ PENSIONS

*1*

(CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS) [S.L.58.01

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 58.01**

**WIDOWS’ AND ORPHANS’ PENSIONS**

**(CROSS-BORDER PAYMENTS) REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2004 LEGAL NOTICE 195 of 2004.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Widows’ and Orphans Pensions (Cross-Border Payments) Regulations.

## 2. Cross-border payments. Cap. 58.

Pensions, gratuities and allowances payable under the Widows’ and Orphans’ Pensions Act to any person shall also be payable to the person entitled thereto in other Member States of the European Community net of any taxes and transaction charges in accordance with Article 5 of Council Directive 98/49/EC of the 29th June, 1998 on safeguarding the supplementary pension rights of employed and self-employed persons moving within the Community.
