# Deferment of Excise Duty payable during the months of April and May 2020 Regulations (S.L. 382.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 382.03**

DEFERMENT OF EXCISE DUTY PAYABLE DURING

**THE MONTHS OF APRIL AND MAY 2020**

**REGULATIONS**

21st April, 2020

**LEGAL NOTICE 147 of 2020, as amended by Act VII of 2022.**

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Deferment of Excise Duty payable during the months of April and May 2020 Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 382.*

"the Act" means the Excise Duty Act; and

"duty" means the duty imposed by the Act;

## 3. Duty payable in April 2020 to be payable in June 2020 and duty payable in May 2020 to be payable in July 2020.

The duty on all excisable goods and services payable on various dates during the month of April 2020 according to the Act, is hereby being declared to be payable sixty (60) days later, but not later than the 28th June 2020, and the duty on all excisable goods and services payable on various dates during the month of May 2020 according to the Act, is hereby being declared to be payable sixty (60) days later, but not later than the 26th July 2020.
