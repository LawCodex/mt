# Television Broadcasting Services (Antennae) Regulations (S.L. 350.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 350.02**

**TELEVISION BROADCASTING SERVICES**

**(ANTENNAE) REGULATIONS**

1st July, 1997 LEGAL NOTICE 71 of 1998, as amended by Legal Notice 65 of 1999.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Television Broadcasting Services (Antennae) Regulations.

## 2. Validity of these regulations in relation to the Act. Cap. 350.

These regulations shall prevail over the provisions of paragraph 1(h) of the First Schedule and paragraph 16 of Part C of the Fourth Schedule to the Broadcasting Act, insofar as the said paragraphs regulate antenna systems or the siting of antenna systems for television broadcasting services. The remaining provisions of the First and Fourth Schedules shall continue to apply to television broadcasting services in respect of matters not specified in these regulations.

## 3. Antennae for television broadcasting services.

*Amended by: L.N. 65 of 1999.*

(1) The provisions of paragraph 1(h) of the First Schedule and of paragraph 16 of Part C of the Fourth Schedule to the Broadcasting Act shall not apply to television broadcasting services.

*Cap. 350.*

(2) Subject to the provisions of subregulation (3), television broadcasting services shall make use of such antenna systems and sites for antenna systems as the Broadcasting Authority, after consultation with the Director of Wireless Telegraphy, may in writing approve.

(3) These regulations shall not dispense any person from obtaining any permit that may be required by law from the Planning Authority, the Minister responsible for Wireless Telegraphy or from any other person or body, to erect or operate any antenna system.
