# Satellite Television Broadcasting Licence (Empowerment) Order (S.L. 350.13)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 350.13**

**SATELLITE TELEVISION BROADCASTING**

**LICENCE (EMPOWERMENT) ORDER**

9th February, 2007 LEGAL NOTICES 19 of 2007 and 175 of 2008, consolidated and Act XVI of 2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Satellite Television Broadcasting Licence (Empowerment) Order.

## 2. Issue of licence to Modern Investment Company Limited.

Repealed by Act XVI of 2018.

## 3. Issue of licence to BuzzTV Limited.

The Minister for Infrastructure, Transport and Communications hereby empowers the Broadcasting Authority to issue on behalf of Government a licence to the company BuzzTV Limited to broadcast, via satellite, a television programme service. BuzzTV Limited is a company registered in Malta under company registration number C44481.
