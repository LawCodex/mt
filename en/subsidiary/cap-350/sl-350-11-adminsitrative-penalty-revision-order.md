# Adminsitrative Penalty (Revision) Order (S.L. 350.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 350.11**

ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTY (REVISION) ORDER 1st September, 2005 LEGAL NOTICE 302 of 2005, as amended by Legal Notice 424 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Administrative Penalty (Revision) Order.

## 2. Interpretation. Cap. 350.

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires, "the Act" means the Broadcasting Act.

## 3. Revision of administrative penalty.

*Amended by: L.N. 424 of 2007.*

The amount of the administrative penalty of eleven thousand and six hundred and forty-six euros (11,646) established in the first proviso to article 41(4)(c) of the Act is hereby being prescribed by this Order to be read and construed as being an amount of thirty-four thousand and nine hundred and forty euros (34,940).
