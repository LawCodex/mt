# Dubbing of Tapes Regulations (S.L. 350.08)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 350.08**

**DUBBING OF TAPES REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2001 LEGAL NOTICE 245 of 2000, as amended by Legal Notice 424 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Dubbing of Tapes Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) For the purposes of these regulations:

"Authority" means the Broadcasting Authority established by article 118 of the Constitution of Malta;

"complainant" means an affected third party who applies to the Authority to seek effective compliance by a broadcaster with the provisions of the Broadcasting Act.

*Cap. 350.*

(2) Words and phrases used in these regulations shall have the same meaning as is assigned to them in the Broadcasting Act.

## 3. Duties of a broadcaster.

A broadcaster shall provide any person feeling aggrieved by the content of a broadcast, with a copy of such broadcast within one day from such person’s request.

## 4. Fees to be levied by broadcaster.

*Amended by: L.N. 424 of 2007.*

A broadcaster shall levy the following fees from a complainant for the granting of a copy of any broadcast:

(a) in the case of a radio programme -

(i) a fee of twenty-three euros (23) for a broadcast not exceeding one hour, which fee shall cover all costs including the cost of the provision of an audio cassette;

(ii) an additional fee of five euros and fifty cents (5.50) for an additional one hour or part thereof; and

(b) in the case of a television programme -

(i) a fee of thirty-four euros and fifty cents (34.50) for a broadcast not exceeding one hour, which fee shall cover all costs including the cost of the provision of a video cassette;

(ii) an additional fee of five euros and fifty cents (5.50) for an additional one hour or part thereof. 5. Should the broadcaster fail to provide a copy of such tape as aforesaid within the period specified in regulation 3, the Authority shall provide a copy of such broadcast and the fees mentioned in regulation 4 shall be levied by the Authority.

*Failure by broadcaster to provide copy of broadcast.*

## 6. Power of the Authority.

The provisions of these regulations are without prejudice to the powers of the Authority under article 21A(3) of the Broadcasting Act.

*Cap. 350.*
