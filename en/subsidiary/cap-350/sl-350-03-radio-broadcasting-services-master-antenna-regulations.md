# Radio Broadcasting Services (Master Antenna) Regulations (S.L. 350.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 350.03**

**RADIO BROADCASTING SERVICES**

**(MASTER ANTENNA) REGULATIONS**

1st April, 1999 LEGAL NOTICE 63 of 1999, as amended by Legal Notice 424 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Radio Broadcasting Services (Master Antenna) Regulations.

## 2. Duties of the Broadcasting Authority.

The Broadcasting Authority shall provide from the master antenna, transmission and other services connected therewith, such as lighting for common areas (excluding electricity consumption by the nation-wide broadcasting stations), security services, periodical surveys, and the maintenance of the master antenna as well as surveys and maintenance of the steel tower, buildings, site and other reasonable requirements.

## 3. Annual fee for services.

*Amended by: L.N. 424 of 2007.*

A nation-wide radio broadcasting service shall pay for the services hereabove referred to, an annual fee of four thousand and six hundred and fifty-eight euros (4,658) payable to the Authority in advance.

## 4. Power of Authority in default of payment.

Should a nation-wide broadcasting service fail to pay the said annual fee and remains in arrears for a period of fifteen days from being called upon by the Authority to effect payment, then the Authority shall, on the lapse of such term, withhold all transmission facilities to the defaulting nation-wide broadcasting service and this until payment is effected.

## 5. Reconnection fee.

*Amended by: L.N. 424 of 2007.*

A reconnection fee of one thousand and one hundred and sixty-four euros (1,164) shall be paid to the Authority by any defaulting broadcasting service.

## 6. Non-liability for damages.

The Broadcasting Authority shall in no manner be liable for damages which may be sustained by any nation-wide broadcasting service as a result of any interruption or suspension of transmissions from the master antenna.
