# Fees payable on the filing of Judicial Acts in connection with Appeals Regulations (S.L. 350.09)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 350.09**

**FEES PAYABLE ON THE FILING OF JUDICIAL**

**ACTS IN CONNECTION WITH APPEALS**

**REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2001 LEGAL NOTICE 106 of 2001, as amended by Legal notice 424 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts in connection with Appeals Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable on appeals.

*Amended by: L.N. 424 of 2007. Cap. 350.*

The fees payable under article 16(9) of the Broadcasting Act on the filing of judicial acts in connection with appeals shall be three hundred and forty-nine euros (349).
