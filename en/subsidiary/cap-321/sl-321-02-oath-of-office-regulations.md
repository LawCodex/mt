# Oath of Office Regulations (S.L. 321.2)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 321.02**

**OATH OF OFFICE REGULATIONS**

26th November, 1993

LEGAL NOTICE 162 of 1993.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Oath of Office Regulations.

## 2. Oaths of allegiance and of office.

Any person authorised to practice as engineer with the authority of the Minister responsible for infrastructure on the recommendation of the Engineering Board granted by warrant shall, before entering upon the exercise of his profession, take before the Court of Appeal, in a public sitting, the oath of allegiance according to the form set forth in article 10 of the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure and the oath of office in the following terms:

*Cap. 12.*

I, .........................., do swear that I will faithfully and with all honesty and exactness perform the duties of Engineer to the best of my knowledge and ability. So help me God.
