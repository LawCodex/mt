# Application for the Issue of a Warrant Regulations (S.L. 321.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 321.01**

**APPLICATION FOR THE ISSUE OF A WARRANT**

**REGULATIONS**

1st September, 1990

LEGAL NOTICE 131 of 1990, as amended by Legal Notices 119 of 1997, 424 of 2007 and 101 of 2010 and Act XXI of 2020.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Application for the Issue of a Warrant Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 101 of 2010; XXI of 2020.*

In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires- "Act" means the Inġiniera Act;

*Cap. 321.*

"Board" means the Board tal-Inġiniera established by article 6 of the Act;

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for infrastructure;

"warrant" means a warrant to practice the profession of engineer issued by the Board in terms of article 3 of the Act; and "warrant holder" shall be construed accordingly.

## 3. Prescribed form and fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 119 of 1997; L.N. 424 of 2007; L.N. 101 of 2010.*

(1) The prescribed form for the application in writing from any person for the issue of a warrant shall be in accordance with the form set out in the Schedule, and any reference to a prescribed form shall be construed as a reference to the form in the said Schedule:

Provided that applications for a warrant may also be submitted by electronic means on a form approved by the Board.

(2) The prescribed form together with all relative original documents, or copies thereof, as may be required by the Board, shall be submitted to the Secretary thereof by the applicant personally, or by electronic means, who shall also produce adequate documents of identification and nationality.

(3) A fee of fifteen euro (€15.00) shall be paid by a professional engineer to the Board in respect of the issue of an original warrant, and a fee of five euro (€5.00) shall be paid in respect of the issue of each copy of the original warrant.

(4) A fee of fifty euro (€50.00) shall be paid to the Board by a person holding foreign professional engineering qualifications acceptable to the Board, in respect of the issue or renewal of a special licence.

## 4. Powers of the Board.

(1) The Board may request such information and inspect such projects, schemes and other documents as may reasonably be considered necessary to enable it to carry out its functions.

(2) The Board may set time limits for compliance with any requests it may make in accordance with sub-regulation (1).

(3) The Board may consider an application on a prescribed form to have been withdrawn if the applicant does not comply with any final time limit which the Board may set for the submission of any information relative to his professional and academic qualifications referred to in sub-regulation (1).
