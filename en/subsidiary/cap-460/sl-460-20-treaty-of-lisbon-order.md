# Treaty of Lisbon Order (S.L. 460.20)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.20**

**TREATY OF LISBON ORDER**

1st February, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 42 of 2008.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Treaty of Lisbon Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 460.*

''the Act'' means the European Union Act;

''the Specified Treaty'' means the Treaty between the Kingdom of Belgium, the Republic of Bulgaria, the Czech Republic, the Kingdom of Denmark, the Federal Republic of Germany, the Republic of Estonia, the Hellenic Republic, the Kingdom of Spain, the French Republic, Ireland, the Italian Republic, the Republic of Cyprus, the Republic of Latvia, the Republic of Lithuania, the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, the Republic of Hungary, the Republic of Malta, the Kingdom of the Netherlands, the Republic of Austria, the Republic of Poland, the Portuguese Republic, Romania, the Republic of Slovenia, the Slovak Republic, the Republic of Finland, the Kingdom of Sweden, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland amending the Treaty on European Union and the Treaty establishing the European Community, signed at Lisbon on 13 December 2007;

''the Treaty'' has the same meaning as that given in the Act.

## 3. The Specified Treaty shall be considered as one with the Treaty.

For the purposes of article 2(2) of the Act it is hereby declared that the Specified Treaty shall be considered as one with the Treaty immediately upon the coming into force of the Specified Treaty between all the signatory states thereof as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 6 of the Specified Treaty.
