# European Union Act (Approval of Treaty Amendment Decision) Order (S.L. 460.28)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.28**

**EUROPEAN UNION ACT (APPROVAL OF**

**TREATY AMENDMENT DECISION) ORDER**

9th October, 2012

LEGAL NOTICE 334 of 2012.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the European Union Act (Approval of Treaty Amendment Decision) Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 460.*

"the Act" means the European Union Act;

"the Treaty" has the same meaning as is assigned to it in the Act;

"the Specified Decision" means the European Council Decision of 25 March 2011 amending Article 136 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union with regard to a stability mechanism for Member States whose currency is the euro (Council Decision 2011/199/EU).

## 3. Specified Decision to be regarded as one with the Treaty.

For the purposes of article 2(2) of the Act, it is hereby declared that the Specified Decision shall be regarded as one with the Treaty, while stating the Government’s understanding that where the Treaty refers to financial stability of the Euro Area, this should be interpreted to mean the Euro Area as a whole or one of its Member States in isolation whatever its size.
