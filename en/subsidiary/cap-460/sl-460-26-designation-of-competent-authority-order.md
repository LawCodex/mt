# Designation of Competent Authority Order (S.L. 460.26)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.26**

**DESIGNATION OF COMPETENT AUTHORITY**

**ORDER**

20th April, 2012 LEGAL NOTICE 121 of 2012.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of this Order is the Designation of Competent Authority Order.

(2) The purpose of this Order is to implement the provisions of Regulation (EU) No 211/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 February 2011 on the citizens’ initiative, hereinafter referred to as "the Regulation".

## 2. Competent authority.

The Electoral Commission, established by article 60 of the Constitution of Malta, is hereby designated as the competent authority for the purposes of the Regulation.
