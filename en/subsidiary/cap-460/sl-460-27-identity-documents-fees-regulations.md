# Identity Documents (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 460.27)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.27**

**IDENTITY DOCUMENTS (FEES) REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2013\* LEGAL NOTICE 309 of 2012.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Identity Documents (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable.

The fees specified in the Schedule shall be payable to the authorised officer as designated by the Prime Minister as the person authorised to issue identity documents or to any person delegated for the purpose.
