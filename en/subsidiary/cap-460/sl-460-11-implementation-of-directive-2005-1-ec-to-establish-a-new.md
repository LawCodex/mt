# Implementation of Directive 2005/1/EC (to establish a new organisational structure for financial services committees) Order (S.L. 460.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.11**

**IMPLEMENTATION OF DIRECTIVE 2005/1/EC**

**(TO ESTABLISH A NEW ORGANIZATIONAL**

**STRUCTURE FOR FINANCIAL SERVICES**

**COMMITTEES) ORDER**

26th April. 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 124 of 2005.

## 1. Citation and application.

(1) The title of this Order is the Implementation of Directive 2005/1/EC (to establish a new organisational structure for financial services committees) Order.

(2) This Directive established a new organisational structure for financial services committees.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Order:

"the Directive" means the provisions of Council Directive 2005/ 1/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2005 amending Council Directives 73/239/EEC, 85/611/EEC, 91/ 675/EEC, 92/49/EEC and 93/6/EEC and Directives 94/19/EC, 98/ 78/EC, 2000/12/EC, 2001/34/EC, 2002/83/EC and 2002/87.

## 3. Directive to have effect.

The Directive is adopted as part of the laws of Malta and shall have effect in Malta accordingly.
