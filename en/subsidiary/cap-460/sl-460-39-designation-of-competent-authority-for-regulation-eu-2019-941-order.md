# Designation of Competent Authority for Regulation (EU) 2019/941 Order (S.L. 460.39)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.39**

**DESIGNATION OF COMPETENT AUTHORITY FOR**

**REGULATION (EU) 2019/941 ORDER**

5th March, 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 70 of 2021.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of this order is Designation of Competent Authority for Regulation (EU) 2019/941 Order.

(2) The scope of this order is to designate the competent authority for Malta under Regulation (EU) 2019/941 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 June 2019 on risk-preparedness in the electricity sector and repealing Directive 2005/89/EC.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this order, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Regulation" means Regulation (EU) 2019/941 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 June 2019 on risk-preparedness in the electricity sector and repealing Directive 2005/89/EC.

## 3. Competent authority.

The Ministry responsible for energy is hereby designated as the competent authority for the purposes of the Regulation.

## 4. Powers of the competent authority.

The competent authority shall be empowered to delegate the operational tasks regarding risk-preparedness planning and risk management set out in the Regulation to other bodies, in line with Article 3(3) of the Regulation.
