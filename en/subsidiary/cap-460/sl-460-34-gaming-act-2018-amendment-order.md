# Gaming Act, 2018 (Amendment) Order (S.L. 460.34)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.34**

**GAMING ACT (AMENDMENT), 2018**

**ORDER**

28th June, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 204 of 2018.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is Gaming Act (Amendment), 2018 Order, and this Order shall be read and construed as one with the Gaming Act, 2018, hereinafter referred to as "the principal Act".

*Act XVI of 2018.*

## 2. Amends article 1 of the principal Act

Paragraph (a) of sub-article (2) of article 1 of the principal Act shall be substituted by the following new paragraph:

"(a) The provisions of this Act shall, without prejudice to paragraph (b), come into force on the 1st August, 2018.".

## 3. Amends article 57 of the principal Act

Article 57 of the principal Act shall be amended as follows:

(a) the proviso to sub-article (4) thereof shall be substituted by the following:

"Provided that in so far as these provisions apply to gaming provided by means of distance communication, such provisions shall only remain in force until 31st July, 2018."; and

(b) in paragraph (b) of sub-article (5) thereof, for the words "after 1 July 2018," there shall be substituted the words "after 1st August 2018,".
