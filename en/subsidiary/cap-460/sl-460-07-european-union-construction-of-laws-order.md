# European Union (Construction of Laws) Order (S.L. 460.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.07**

**EUROPEAN UNION**

**(CONSTRUCTION OF LAWS) ORDER**

1st May, 2004\*

LEGAL NOTICE 248 of 2004.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the European Union (Construction of Laws) Order.

## 2. Interpretation of laws.

Where in accordance with the Treaty certain rights accorded to citizens or persons established in a Member State of the European Union are also to be accorded to citizens or persons established in a State party to the agreement establishing the European Economic Area or to citizens of or persons established in Switzerland, then to the extent that such rights are to be so accorded, the terms Member State, citizen of a Member State or person established in a M ember State, in any law, shall be construed as being a reference also to a State party to the agreement establishing the European Economic Area and to Switzerland, to a citizen of such State party and to Switzerland and to a person established in such State party or in Switzerland.

\*See article 1(2) of this Order, as originally promulgated.
