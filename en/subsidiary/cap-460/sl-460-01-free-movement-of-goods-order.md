# Free Movement of Goods Order (S.L. 460.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.01**

**FREE MOVEMENT OF GOODS ORDER**

1st May, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 43 of 2004.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Free Movement of Goods Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

''Member State'' means a state party to the Treaty.

## 3. Free movement of goods.

No provision of any law shall impede the importation, marketing and use of products legally produced and marketed in a Member State other than Malta, provided that such products ensure an equivalent level of protection as those that may legally be produced and marketed in Malta.
