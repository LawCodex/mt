# Designation of Competent Authority for Regulation (EU) No 2017/1938 Order (S.L. 460.38)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.38**

**DESIGNATION OF COMPETENT AUTHORITY FOR**

**REGULATION (EU) 2017/1938 ORDER**

5th March,2021

LEGAL NOTICE 69 of 2021.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of this order is the Designation of Competent Authority for Regulation (EU) No 2017/1938 Order.

(2) The purpose of this order is to designate the competent authority for Malta under Regulation (EU) 2017/1938 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2017 concerning measures to safeguard the security of gas supply and repealing Regulation (EU) No 994/2010.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this order, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Regulation" means Regulation (EU) 2017/1938 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2017 concerning measures to safeguard the security of gas supply and repealing Regulation (EU) No 994/2010

## 3. Competent authority.

The Ministry responsible for energy is hereby designated as the competent authority for the purposes of the Regulation.

## 4. Powers of the competent authority.

The competent authority shall be empowered to delegate specific tasks set out in the Regulation to other bodies, in line with Article 3(2) of the Regulation.
