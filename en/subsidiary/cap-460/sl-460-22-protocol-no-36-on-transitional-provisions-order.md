# Protocol (No. 36) on Transitional Provisions Order (S.L. 460.22)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.22**

**PROTOCOL (No. 36) ON TRANSITIONAL**

**PROVISIONS ORDER**

19th October, 2010

LEGAL NOTICE 459 of 2010.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Protocol (No. 36) on Transitional Provisions Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 460.*

''the Act” means the European Union Act;

''the Protocol'' means Protocol (No. 36) on Transitional Provisions annexed to the Treaty on European Union, the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community.

## 3. Protocol to be deemed as one with the Treaties.

For the purposes of article 2(2) of the Act, it is hereby declared that the Protocol shall be deemed as one with the Treaties above-mentioned as soon as the Protocol comes into force for all the countries which are signatories thereto, as provided in the second paragraph of the Preamble to the Protocol.
