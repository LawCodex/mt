# Ratification of the Convention on the Elaboration of a European Pharmacopoeia Order (S.L. 460.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 460.10**

RATIFICATION OF THE CONVENTION ON THE ELABORATION OF A EUROPEAN

**PHARMACOPOEIA ORDER**

12th April, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 104 of 2005.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Ratification of the Convention on the Elaboration of a European Pharmacopoeia Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 460.*

"the Act" means the European Union Act;

"the Convention" means the Convention listed in the Schedule;

"the Treaty" has the same meaning as is given to the expression in article 2(1) of the Act.

## 3. Ratification of the specified Treaty to be regarded as one with the Treaty.

The Convention in the Schedule shall be regarded as one with the Treaty.

## SCHEDULE

(Article 3)

## 1.

The Convention on the Elaboration of a European Pharmacopoeia.
