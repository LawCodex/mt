# Prices payable for the Acquisition of Graves and Grave Sites Order (S.L. 17.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 17.02**

**PRICES PAYABLE FOR THE ACQUISITION OF**

**GRAVES AND GRAVE SITES ORDER**

17th September, 1974;

22nd May, 1978;

1st January, 1993;

1st July, 1997;

21st November, 2003 LEGAL NOTICE 96 of 1974, as amended by Act XIII of 1983, LEGAL NOTICE 50 of 1978, as amended by Act XIII of 1983, and LEGAL NOTICES 115 of 1992, 93 of 1997, 363 of 2003, 204 of 2004, 227 of 2005, and 228 and 377 of 2007, consolidated, and as amended by Legal Notices 407 of 2007, 327 of 2012, 32 of 2013 and 425 of 2016.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Prices payable for the Acquisition of Graves and Grave Sites Order.

## 2. Price for grave site.

*Amended by: XIII. 1983.5; L.N. 407 of 2007.*

The price payable for the acquisition of a grave site shall be fifty-eight euro and twenty-three cents (58.23).

## 3. Price for graves.

*Amended by: XIII. 1983.5; L.N. 377 of 2007; L.N. 407 of 2007; L.N. 327 of 2012. Substituted by: L.N. 425 of 2016.*

The price payable for the acquisition of a grave in any Government cemetery (inclusive of the consideration for the site) shall be €8,000:

Provided that the provisions of this article shall not apply to the price payable for the acquisition of a constructed grave in any Government cemetery, inclusive of the consideration for the grant of the site, in favour of applicants who had already entered into a preliminary agreement with the Government prior to the coming into force of this Order.\*

## 3A. Payment of dues.

*Added by: L.N. 32 of 2013.*

Any person who is informed of his entitlement for the acquisition of a grave at the Santa Marija Cemetery, Xewkija, Gozo, will be required to pay half the amount due on the signing of the promise of acquisition, with the remaining dues to be paid on the signing of the contract of acquisition.

## 4. Payment of dues.

*Added by: L.N. 327 of 2012. Amended and re-numbered by: L.N. 425 of 2016.*

Any person who is informed of his entitlement for the acquisition of a grave will be required to pay half the amount due in article 3 on the signing of the promise of acquisition, with the remaining dues to be paid on the signing of the contract of acquisition.
