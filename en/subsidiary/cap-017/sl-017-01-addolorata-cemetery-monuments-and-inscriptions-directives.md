# Addolorata Cemetery (Monuments and Inscriptions) Directives (S.L. 17.1)

ADDOLORATA CEMETERY

*1*

(MONUMENTS AND INSCRIPTIONS) [ S.L.17.01

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 17.01**

**ADDOLORATA CEMETERY**

(MONUMENTS AND INSCRIPTIONS) DIRECTIVES 5th August, 1902;

13th May, 1921;

4th October, 1921 GOVERNMENT NOTICES 185 of 1902, 146 of 1921 and 278 of 1921, consolidated.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Directives is Addolorata Cemetery (Monuments and Inscriptions) Directives.

## 2. Inspection of monument, etc.

(1) Persons who desire to erect monuments or make inscriptions in the Addolorata Cemetery shall, besides submitting the design for approval by the board according to the provisions of article 9 of the Burials Ordinance, give notice at the office of Public Works on the completion of the monument or other work, to enable the board to inspect such monument, etc., previous to their erection at the cemetery.

*Cap. 17.*

(2) The drafts and designs aforesaid may be presented on any working day at the office of the Director of Public Works.

(3) Applicants shall state, in their application, the name of the person or firm to whom the work is to be entrusted.
