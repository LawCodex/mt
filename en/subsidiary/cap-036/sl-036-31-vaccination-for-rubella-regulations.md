# Vaccination for Rubella Regulations (S.L. 36.31)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 36.31**

**VACCINATION FOR RUBELLA REGULATIONS**

2nd May, 1989

LEGAL NOTICE 50 of 1989.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Vaccination for Rubella Regulations.

## 2. Immunization against rubella.

(1) The parent or other person having the custody of any female child who has attained ten years of age but has not attained thirteen years of age, and who has not yet been vaccinated against rubella, commonly known as german measles, shall cause the child to be vaccinated.

(2) A certificate of vaccination against rubella, issued by the Department of Health, shall prove that the child has been vaccinated.
