**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 36.25**

**PROHIBITION OF IMPORTATION OF ANIMALS**

**AND FEEDING STUFF REGULATIONS**

29th August, 1970

LEGAL NOTICE 85 of 1970, as amended by Legal Notices 99 of 1970, 53 and 69 of 1971, 88 of 1973 and 64 of 1974.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Prohibition of Importation of Animals and Feeding Stuff Regulations.

## 2. Prohibition of importation of animals and feeding stuff.

(1) No person shall import into Malta any animal, carcass, fodder, litter or dung, whether by means of any ship or by means of any aircraft, from any country set out in the Schedule to these Regulations, or any animal, carcass, fodder, litter or dung conveyed by a ship or aircraft which has called at any such country, unless the Chief Government Medical Officer and Superintendent of Public Health is satisfied that such animal, carcass, fodder, litter or dung is free from contamination or infection.

(2) In this regulation "fodder" includes any feeding stuff.
