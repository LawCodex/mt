# Use of Wrapping Paper Order (S.L. 36.01)

SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 36.01

USE OF WRAPPING PAPER ORDER

24th June, 1916

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 139 of 1916.

## 1.

The title of this Order is Use of Wrapping Paper Order. Title.

## 2.

The wrapping of foodstuffs in unclean, used or printed Wrapping of foodstuffs. paper is prohibited.
