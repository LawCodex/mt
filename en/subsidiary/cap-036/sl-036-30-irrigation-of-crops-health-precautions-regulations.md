# Irrigation of Crops (Health Precautions) Regulations (S.L. 36.30)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 36.30**

IRRIGATION OF CROPS (HEALTH PRECAUTIONS) REGULATIONS

16th September, 1988

LEGAL NOTICE 71 of 1988.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Irrigation of Crops (Health Precautions) Regulations.

## 2. Prohibition of irrigation with sewage.

No person may irrigate any field, where crops intended for human consumption are, or may be, cultivated, with any sewage or sewage contaminated water.

## 3. Seizure and destruction.

It shall be lawful for the Superintendent of Public Health to order the seizure and/or destruction of crops which have been irrigated with sewage or sewage contaminated water.

## 4. Saving.

The provisions of regulations 2 and 3 of these Regulations shall not apply to any field irrigated with effluent from sewage which has been treated and purified at a government-run sewage treatment plant in respect of such crops which the Superintendent of Public Health has approved could be cultivated in such field.
