# Prohibition of Importation of Food and other goods Regulations (S.L. 36.24)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 36.24**

**PROHIBITION OF IMPORTATION OF FOOD**

**AND OTHER GOODS REGULATIONS**

29th August, 1970

LEGAL NOTICE 84 of 1970, as amended by Legal Notices 101 of 1970, 54 and 70 of 1971, 87 of 1973 and 63 of 1974.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Prohibition of Importation of Food and other goods Regulations.

## 2. Prohibition of importation of food and other goods.

(1) No person shall import into Malta any food, fish or bird whether by means of any ship or by means of any aircraft, from any country set out in the Schedule to these Regulations, or any food, fish or bird conveyed by a ship or aircraft which has called at any such country, unless the Chief Medical Officer and Superintendent of Public Health is satisfied that such food, fish or bird is free or has been rendered free from contamination or infection.

(2) In this regulation ''food'' includes any article used for food or drink by man, any article to be used in the composition or preparation of food and any flavouring matter or condiment.

(3) The provisions of sub-regulation (1) of this regulation shall apply to second hand or used clothing, rags, hay or straw.
