# Aquaculture Regulations (S.L. 36.34)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 36.34**

**AQUACULTURE REGULATIONS\***

29th May, 1990

LEGAL NOTICE 73 of 1990.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Aquaculture Regulations. 2. In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires -

*Interpretation.*

"Director" means the Director of Agriculture and Fisheries and includes any officer of the department duly authorised by the Director to act on his behalf;

"to fatten" means to keep fish from the fingerling stage to market size;

"to rear" means to breed and rear up to the fingerling stage and includes the selling of broodstock;

"to keep" includes to rear and to fatten;

"fish" includes fin fish, molluscs and crustaceans, whether of fresh or of sea-water.

## 3. Licence to keep, rear or fatten fish.

No person shall keep, rear or fatten farmed fish, whether as owner, part-owner or assistant, unless he holds a licence issued to him.

## 4. Form of application.

A licence as provided for in regulation 3 may be granted by the Director after consideration of the application made on the appropriate form issued by him.

## 5. Fish farms.

No person shall keep, rear or fatten fish in any place unless that place is licensed as a fish farm for any or all of those specific purposes.

## 6. Requirements of licence.

No licence for a fish farm shall be issued unless the requirements laid down in the form of application are satisfied.

## 7. Revocation of licence.

The Director may at any time revoke a licence if the place licensed is found lacking in any of the conditions mentioned in the licence, or if in the opinion of the Director there is a risk of the spread of disease.

## 8. Validity of licences.

Licences shall be valid for one calendar year, and may be renewed from year to year.

## 9. Distance between farms.

The minimum distance between any two fish farms shall be one kilometre, provided that the Director may establish a greater distance at his discretion according to the particular circumstances of each case.

## 10. Extensions and alterations.

No extensions or alterations in any licensed place may be carried out unless previously authorised by the Director.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Act XXXIV of 2023.

## 11. Movement of fish.

No person, whether licensed or not, shall move or cause any live fish or live fish products to be moved from a licensed place to any other place unless he has obtained permission to do so in writing from the Director.

## 12. Imports.

No person shall import live fish or live fish products destined for farming unless he has obtained permission to do so in writing from the Director. A health certificate from the point of origin may be required.

## 13. Inspection.

The Director may inspect licensed premises at any time in order to ensure that the licence conditions are being complied with.

## 14. Requirements under any other law.

A licensee under these Regulations shall not be exempt from complying with the requirements of any other law or regulation which may be in force from time to time.
