# Milk Boiling Regulations (S.L. 36.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 36.03**

**MILK BOILING REGULATIONS**

23rd March, 1923 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 51 of 1923.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Milk Boiling Regulations. 2. (1) No one shall, without the special permission in writing of the Superintendent of Public Health, keep, sell or supply, or allow to be kept, sold or supplied, in any hotel, restaurant, coffee shop or other shop, milk which has not been previously boiled.

*Prohibition of keeping of unboiled milk.*

(2) In any case in which milk is kept in contravention of this regulation, the onus of proof that any such milk was not intended to be sold or supplied, shall lie on the accused.

## 3. Posting of regulations.

Owners or other persons responsible for the management of hotels, restaurants, coffee shops or any other shops where milk is kept, sold or supplied, shall keep posted up in a conspicuous position in each room a notice, obtainable free of charge from the Department of Health, reproducing these Regulations.
