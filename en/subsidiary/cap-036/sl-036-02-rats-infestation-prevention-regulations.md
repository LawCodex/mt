# Rats Infestation (Prevention) Regulations (S.L. 36.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 36.02**

**RATS INFESTATION (PREVENTION)**

**REGULATIONS**

12th December, 1919 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 516 of 1919.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Rats Infestation (Prevention) Regulations.

## 2. Prohibition of accumulation of rags, etc.

The owner or occupier of any land, dwelling or other building shall not keep or allow to be kept on them any accumulation of rags, tins, boxes, bottles and other articles that may afford harbourage for rats, and shall take reasonable and proper precautions to prevent such dwelling or other building becoming infested by rats.

## 3. Duty to keep walls, etc., in good state of repair.

It shall be the duty of the owner of any building to keep in a good state of repair the walls and floors of basements and the floors of yards and of ground-floors, with a view to prevent rats and mice burrowing into and forming runs in such buildings.

## 4. Applicability of regulations. Cap. 10.

These Regulations may also be applied, at the discretion of the Superintendent of Public Health, to premises licensed under article 130 of the Code of Police Laws.
