# Notification of Tuberculosis by Medical Practitioners Order (S.L. 36.15)

NOTIFICATION OF TUBERCULOSIS

*1*

BY MEDICAL PRACTITIONERS [ S.L.36.15

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 36.15**

NOTIFICATION OF TUBERCULOSIS BY MEDICAL PRACTITIONERS ORDER

30th July, 1949 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 481 of 1949.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Notification of Tuberculosis by Medical Practitioners Order.

## 2. Notification. Cap. 36.

The provisions of article 7 of the Prevention of Disease Ordinance, relating to the duty of medical practitioners to notify disease, shall apply to all forms of tuberculosis as they apply to tubercular phthisis under article 10(2) of the said Ordinance.
