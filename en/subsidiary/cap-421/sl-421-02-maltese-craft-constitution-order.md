# Maltese Craft (Constitution) Order (S.L. 421.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 421.02**

**MALTESE CRAFT (CONSTITUTION) ORDER**

28th June, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 158 of 2001.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Maltese Craft (Constitution) Order.

## 2. Declaration.

It is hereby being declared that any art, skill, trade or industry mentioned in the Schedule is a Maltese craft for the purposes of article 3 of the Malta Crafts Council Act.

*Cap. 421.*
