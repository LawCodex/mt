# Portomaso Extension II, St Julians (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.26)

*1*

SPECIAL AREA) [S.L. 246.26 SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.26

**PORTOMASO EXTENSION II, ST JULIANS**

**(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) ORDER**

21st October, 2022

LEGAL NOTICE 260 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Portomaso Extension II, St Julians (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of special area.

Portomaso Extension II, St Julians consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Act. Cap. 246.

Portomaso Extension II, St Julians shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
