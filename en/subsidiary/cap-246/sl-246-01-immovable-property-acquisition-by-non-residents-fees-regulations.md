# Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 246.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.01**

IMMOVABLE PROPERTY (ACQUISITION BY NON- RESIDENTS) (FEES) REGULATIONS

23rd January, 1979

LEGAL NOTICE 17 of 1979, as amended by Legal Notices 1 of 1988 and 411 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Charging of fee.

*Amended by: L.N. 1 of 1988; L.N. 411 of 2007.*

(1) There shall be charged to any non-resident person, not being a citizen of Malta, a fee of €232.94 in respect of any permit granted by the Minister under the provisions of -

(a) article 5(1)(b), or

(b) article 5(2),

*Cap. 246.*

of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act, for the acquisition of any immovable property by such person.

(2) The provision of subregulation (1) shall have effect only in respect of permits granted on or after the day immediately following the date of the coming into effect of these regulations.

## 3. Place and time of payment of fee.

Any fee charged under regulation 2 shall be paid to the Secretary of the Ministry responsible for finance on the date of issue of the relative permit.

## 4. Fee paid not refundable.

No fee paid under these regulations shall, for any reason whatsoever, be refundable.
