# Mistra Heights Project (Special Designated Area) Order (S.L. 246.22)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.22**

**MISTRA HEIGHTS PROJECT**

**(SPECIAL DESIGNATED AREA) ORDER**

6th September, 2019

LEGAL NOTICE 226 of 2019.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is Mistra Heights Project (Special Designated Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

The Mistra Heights Project, consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Special Designated Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non- Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Act. Cap. 246.

The Mistra Heights Project shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
