# Pender Place and Mercury House Site, Extension VI, St. Julian’s (Designated Special Areas) Order (S.L. 246.25)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.25**

**PENDER PLACE AND MERCURY HOUSE SITE,**

**EXTENSION VI, ST JULIAN’S (DESIGNATED**

**SPECIAL AREAS) ORDER**

24th June, 2022

LEGAL NOTICE 195 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Pender Place and Mercury House Site, Extension VI, St. Julian’s (Designated Special Areas) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Areas.

Pender Place and Mercury House Site, Extension VI, St. Julian’s, consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Act. Cap. 246.

Pender Place and Mercury House Zone, Extension VI, St. Julian’s shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
