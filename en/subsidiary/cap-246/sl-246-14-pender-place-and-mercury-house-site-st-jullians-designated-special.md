# Pender Place and Mercury House Site, St Jullians (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.14)

*1*

ST. JULIANS (DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) [S.L.246.14

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.14**

**PENDER PLACE AND MERCURY HOUSE SITE,**

**ST. JULIANS (DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA)**

**ORDER**

3rd August, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 210 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Pender Place and Mercury House Site, St. Julian’s (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

Pender Place and Mercury House site, St. Julian’s consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this Order, is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to First Schedule of the Act. Cap. 246.

Pender Place and Mercury House Zone, St. Julian’s shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
