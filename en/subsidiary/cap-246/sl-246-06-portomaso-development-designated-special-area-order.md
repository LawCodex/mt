# Portomaso Development (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.06**

**PORTOMASO DEVELOPMENT**

**(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) ORDER**

3rd August, 1999

LEGAL NOTICE 137 of 1999, as amended by Legal Notice 411 of 2007.

### 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Portomaso Development (Designated Special Area) Order.

### 2. Designation of Special Area.

Portomaso Development situated on a plot of land at Spinola, Designation of St. Julians, Malta, shown outlined in blue in a plan marked as Document Special Area B annexed to a deed in the records of the Assistant Notary to Government, Doctor Anthony Attard, by Deed No. 83 dated 19th May, 1964 (hereinafter referred to as the Special Area) is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Designation of Special Areas) Regulations.

*S.L.246.02*

### 3. Non-resident persons to whom this Order applies. Cap. 246.

(1) This Order shall apply to non-resident persons falling under the definition in paragraph ( a ) of the definition of "non-resident person" in article 2 of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act (hereinafter in this Order referred to as "the Act") and to non-resident persons falling under paragraph (b) of the said definition but only where none of the shares or interests in such body of persons are issued or transferable by delivery to bearer:

*Cap. 233.*

Provided that the interest or shares in any such entity may be held and registered in the name of a nominee, resident in Malta, provided such nominee obtains permission under such conditions as the Minister may deem appropriate to issue in accordance with article 17(2) of the Exchange Control Act.

(2) Where the non-resident person is a body of persons as defined in subarticle (1), it shall be a condition of the permit that the shares or interests in the said body of persons shall not be transferable to any person inter vivos without the permit in writing by the Minister and where the shares or interests are held by a nominee, a new permission under the Exchange Control Act shall be obtained prior to the transfer of any share or interest in the body of persons to new beneficial interests and such a change in beneficial interests shall be deemed to be a transfer of the registered shares or interests for the purpose of the Duty on Documents and Transfers Act, and shall pay to the Minister the sum that would be due as duty under that Act and notwithstanding anything contained in the said Act.

*Cap. 233.*

*Cap. 364.*

(3) For the purposes of a permit as is referred to in subarticle (2), an application shall be made on behalf of the person to whom such transfer is intended to be made, giving such details or other documentation relative to such person as apply in respect of the original shareholders as prescribed under this Order. Such permit shall only be granted upon the payment of the fee referred to in article 7.

### 4. Immovable property to which Order applies.

(1) This Order applies to the immovable property marked on the plan referred to in article 2.

(2) Each residential unit or any other immovable property being part of the designated property may be acquired or transferred under any title but may not be acquired, transferred or held under any timeshare scheme or other similar system of multi-property.

### 5. Application.

(1) An application for the acquisition by a non-resident person of any immovable property in the special area or for the transfer of shares in a body of persons holding such property, shall be made in the form as shown in the Schedule, and shall contain such particulars and be accompanied by such documents as are prescribed in the said form.

(2) Applications may be made in relation to more than one unit of immovable property in the special area, but a separate fee as prescribed in article 3(3) shall be payable in respect of each single unit.

### 6. Permissible uses.

(1) The immovable property to which this Order applies may, subject to the provisions of this article, be put to any use permissible by law.

(2) The provisions of subarticle (1) shall not be considered as exempting from the provision of any law requiring any permit for a change in land use, for the exercise of any trade or profession, or generally to make any particular use of the immovable property.

(3) Notwithstanding the other provisions of this article where the immovable property is put to residential use (whether such use is long term or temporary) it may only be used by the owner, his friends or family, and where the owner is a body of persons by the shareholders, directors or employees of the company, and may be leased to third parties.

### 7. Fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 411 of 2007.*

The fees payable under this order shall be €232.94 for each unit acquired by a non-resident referred to in article 3(1).
