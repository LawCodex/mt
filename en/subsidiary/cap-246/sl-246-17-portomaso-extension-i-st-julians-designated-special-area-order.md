# Portomaso Extension I, St Julians (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.17)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.17**

**PORTOMASO EXTENSION I, ST JULIANS**

**(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) ORDER**

16th May, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 133 of 2008.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Portomaso Extension I, St Julians (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

Portomaso Extension I, St Julians consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this Order, is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Act. Cap. 246.

Portomaso Extension I, St Julians shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
