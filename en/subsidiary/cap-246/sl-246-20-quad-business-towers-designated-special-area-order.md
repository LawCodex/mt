# Quad Business Towers (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.20)

*1*

(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) [S.L. 246.20 SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.20

**THE QUAD BUSINESS TOWERS (DESIGNATED**

**SPECIAL AREA) ORDER**

6th December, 2017 LEGAL NOTICE 349 of 2017.

## 1. Title.

The title of this order is the Quad Business Towers (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

The Quad Business Towers, Mrieħel consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents).

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Act. Cap. 246

The Quad Business Towers, Mrieħel shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
