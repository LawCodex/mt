# The Quad Business Towers, Mrieħel, Extension I (Special Designated Area) Order (S.L. 246.23)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.23**

**THE QUAD BUSINESS TOWERS, MRIEĦEL,**

**EXTENSION I (SPECIAL DESIGNATED AREA)**

**ORDER**

26th November, 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 459 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is The Quad Business Towers, Mrieħel, Extension I (Special Designated Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

The Quad Business Towers, Mrieħel, Extension I, consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Special Designated Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Amends the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act. Cap. 246.

The Quad Business Towers, Mrieħel, Extension I shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
