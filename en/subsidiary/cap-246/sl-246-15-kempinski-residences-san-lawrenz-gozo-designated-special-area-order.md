# Kempinski Residences, San Lawrenz, Gozo (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.15)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.15**

KEMPINSKI RESIDENCES, SAN LAWRENZ, GOZO (DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) ORDER

16th May, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 131 of 2008.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Kempinski Residences, San Lawrenz, Gozo (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

Kempinski Residences, San Lawrenz, Gozo consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this Order, is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Act. Cap. 246.

Kempinski Residences, San Lawrenz, Gozo shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
