# Fort Chambray (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.03**

FORT CHAMBRAY (DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) ORDER

17th September, 1993

LEGAL NOTICE 124 of 1993, as amended by Legal Notices 411 of 2007 and 205 of 2024.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Fort Chambray (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

*Amended by: L.N. 205 of 2024.*

Fort Chambray in the limits of Għajnsielem in the Island of Gozo, consisting of immovable property which is defined as the "Emphyteutical Land" in the deed in the records of Notary Public Dr. Vincent Miceli dated 24th January 2005, as shown delineated in red on a plan indicated as "Property Division number two hundred and fifty-four underscore two thousand and four" attached as document marked "X" to the said deed is hereby designted as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Designation of Special Areas) Regulations.

*S.L.246.02*
