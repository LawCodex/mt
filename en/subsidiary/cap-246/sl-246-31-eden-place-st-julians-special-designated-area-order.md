# Eden Place, St Julians (Special Designated Area) Order (S.L. 246.31)

*1*

(SPECIAL DESIGNATED AREA) [S.L. 246.31] SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.31

EDEN PLACE, ST JULIANS (SPECIAL DESIGNATED AREA) ORDER

16th January, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 7 of 2026.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is Eden Place, St Julians (Special Designated Area) Order.

## 2. Special designated area.    Cap. 246.

Eden Place, St Julians consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Special Designated Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act. Cap. 246.

Eden Place, St Julians shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
