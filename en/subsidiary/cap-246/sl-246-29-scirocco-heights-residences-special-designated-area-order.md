# Scirocco Heights Residences (Special Designated Area) Order (S.L. 246.29)

*1*

(SPECIAL DESIGNATED AREA) [S.L. 246.29

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.29**

**SCIROCCO HEIGHTS RESIDENCES (SPECIAL**

**DESIGNATED AREA) ORDER**

27th June, 2025

LEGAL NOTICE 111 of 2025.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Scirocco Heights Residences (Special Designated Area) Order.

## 2. Special designated area.    Cap. 246.

Scirocco Heights Residences consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Special Designated Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non- Residents) Act.

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act. Cap. 246.

Scirocco Heights Residences shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
