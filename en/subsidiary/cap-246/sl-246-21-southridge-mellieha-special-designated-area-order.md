# Southridge, Mellieħa (Special Designated Area) Order (S.L. 246.21)

*1*

(SPECIAL DESIGNATED AREA) [S.L. 246.21 SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.21

**SOUTHRIDGE, MELLIEĦA**

**(SPECIAL DESIGNATED AREA) ORDER**

5th June, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 181 of 2018.

## 1. Title.

The title of this order is the Southridge, Mellieħa (Special Designated Area) order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area. Cap. 246.

Southridge, Mellieħa consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this Order, is hereby designated as a Special Designated Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents).

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Act. Cap. 246.

Southridge, Mellieħa shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
