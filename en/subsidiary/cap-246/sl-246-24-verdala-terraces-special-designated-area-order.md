# Verdala Terraces (Special Designated Area) Order (S.L. 246.24)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.24**

**VERDALA TERRACES (SPECIAL DESIGNATED**

**AREA) ORDER**

17th June 2022

LEGAL NOTICE 175 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is Verdala Terraces (Special Designated Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

Verdala Terraces, consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Special Designated Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Amends the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act. Cap. 246.

Verdala Terraces shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
