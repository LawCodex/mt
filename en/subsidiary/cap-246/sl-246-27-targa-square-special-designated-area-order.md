# Tarġa Square (Special Designated Area) Order (S.L. 246.27)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.27**

TARĠA SQUARE (SPECIAL DESIGNATED AREA)

**ORDER**

21st February 2023

LEGAL NOTICE 39 of 2023.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is Tarġa Square (Special Designated Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of special area.

Tarġa Square, consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Special Designated Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Act. Cap. 246.

Tarġa Square shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
