# Ta' Monita Residence, Marsascala (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.13)

*1*

(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) [S.L.246.13

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.13**

**TA’ MONITA RESIDENCE, MARSASCALA**

**(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) ORDER**

10th July, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 181 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Ta’ Monita Residence, Marsascala (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

Ta’ Monita Residence, Marsascala consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this Order, is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to First Schedule of the Act. Cap. 246.

Ta’ Monita Residence, Marsascala shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
