# Fort Cambridge Zone, Tigne (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.12)

*1*

(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) [S.L.246.12

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.12**

**FORT CAMBRIDGE ZONE, TIGNÈ**

**(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) ORDER**

15th June, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 159 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Fort Cambridge Zone, Tignè (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

Fort Cambridge Zone, Tignè consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this Order, is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to First Schedule of the Act. Cap. 246.

Fort Cambridge Zone, Tignè shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
