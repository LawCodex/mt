# Madliena Village Complex (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.10**

**MADLIENA VILLAGE COMPLEX**

**(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) ORDER**

29th December, 2006

LEGAL NOTICE 335 of 2006.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Madliena Village Complex (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

Madliena Village Complex consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this Order, is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to First Schedule of the Act. Cap. 246

Madliena Village Complex shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
