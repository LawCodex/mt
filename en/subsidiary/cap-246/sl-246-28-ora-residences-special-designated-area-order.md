# ORA Residences (Special Designated Area) Order (S.L. 246.28)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.28**

**THE ORA RESIDENCES (SPECIAL DESIGNATED**

**AREA) ORDER**

7th December 2023

LEGAL NOTICE 285 of 2023.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the ORA Residences (Special Designated Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of special area.     Cap. 246.

The ORA Residences, consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Special Designated Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Act.  Cap. 246.

The ORA Residences shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
