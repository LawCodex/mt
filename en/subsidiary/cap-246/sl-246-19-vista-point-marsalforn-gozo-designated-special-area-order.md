# Vista Point, Marsalforn, Gozo (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.19)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.19**

**VISTA POINT, MARSALFORN, GOZO**

**(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) ORDER**

28th September, 2012

LEGAL NOTICE 314 of 2012.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Vista Point, Marsalforn, Gozo (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

Vista Point, Marsalforn, Gozo consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this Order, is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

## 3. Addition to First Schedule to the Act. Cap. 246.

Vista Point, Marsalforn, Gozo, shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
