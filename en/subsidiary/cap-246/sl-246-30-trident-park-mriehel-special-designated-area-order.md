# Trident Park, Mrieħel (Special Designated Area) Order (S.L. 246.30)

*1*

(SPECIAL DESIGNATED AREA) [S.L. 246.30 SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.30

TRIDENT PARK, MRIEĦEL (SPECIAL DESIGNATED AREA) ORDER

15th July, 2025

LEGAL NOTICE 138 of 2025.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Trident Park, Mrieħel (Special Designated Area) Order.

## 2. Special designated area.    Cap. 246.

Trident Park, Mrieħel consisting of immovable property situated in the area bordered in red in the Land Registry Plan annexed to this order, is hereby designated as a Special Designated Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act. Cap. 246.

Trident Park, Mrieħel shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
