## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Pender Place and Mercury House Site, Extensions I, II, III, IV and V, St Julians (Designated Special Areas) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Areas.

Pender Place and Mercury House Site, Extensions I, II, III, IV and V, St. Julians consisting of immovable properties situated in the areas bordered in red in the Land Registry Plans annexed to this Order, are hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purpos es of the Immovable Property (Acquis iti on by Non- Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to the First Schedule of the Act. Cap. 246

Pender Place and Mercury House Zone, Extensions I, II, III, IV and V, St. Julians shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
