# Tas-Sellum Residence (Mellieha Project) (Designated Special Area) Order (S.L. 246.9)

TAS-SELLUM RESIDENCE (MELLIE}A PROJECT)

*1*

(DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) [S.L.246.09

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.09**

TAS-SELLUM RESIDENCE (MELLIE}A PROJECT) (DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA) ORDER

27th May, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 156 of 2005.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is Tas-Sellum Residence (Mellie]a Project) (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

Tas-Sellum Residence (Mellie]a Project) consisting of the immovable property as per Plan marked as Document "X" annexed to a deed in the records of Notary Doctor of Laws Sandra Bugeja by Deed No. 246 of the twenty-ninth (29th) September of the year two thousand and four (2004), is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to First Schedule of the Act. Cap. 246.

Tas-Sellum Residence (Mellie]a Project) shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.
