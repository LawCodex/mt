# SmartCity (designated Secial Area) Order (S.L. 246.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 246.11**

**SMARTCITY (DESIGNATED SPECIAL AREA)**

**ORDER**

20th April, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 109 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the SmartCity (Designated Special Area) Order.

## 2. Designation of Special Area.

SmartCity consisting of the immovable property defined as the Site in the deed of Emphyteutical Grant to be signed between the Government of Malta and SmartCity (Malta) Limited TECOM Investments FZ-LLC and the Malta Maritime Authority approved by the House of Representatives on the 20th March 2007 is hereby designated as a Designated Special Area for the purposes of the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non-Residents) Act.

*Cap. 246.*

## 3. Addition to First Schedule of the Act. Cap. 246.

SmartCity shall be added to the List of Scheduled Zones in the First Schedule to the Immovable Property (Acquisition by Non- Residents) Act.
