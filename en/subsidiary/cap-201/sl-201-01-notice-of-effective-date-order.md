# Notice of Effective Date Order (S.L. 201.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 201.01**

**NOTICE OF EFFECTIVE DATE ORDER**

18th February, 1993

LEGAL NOTICE 58 of 1993.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Notice of Effective Date Order. 2. The 18th day of February, 1993 is established as the effective date for the purposes of article 12 of the Mortmain Act.

*Effective date. Cap. 201.*
