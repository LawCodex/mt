# Definition of "Eligible Assets" in the External Transactions Act Notice (S.L. 233.9)

DEFINITION OF "ELIGIBLE ASSETS"

*1*

IN THE EXTERNAL TRANSACTIONS ACT [S.L.233.09

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 233.09**

**DEFINITION OF "ELIGIBLE ASSETS" IN THE**

**EXTERNAL TRANSACTIONS ACT NOTICE**

20th April, 2007

LEGAL NOTICES 111 and 125 of 2007, consolidated.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Notice is the Definition of "Eligible Assets" in the External Transactions Act Notice.

## 2. Definition of "eligible assets". Cap. 233.

For the purposes of article 2(1) of the External Transactions Act, the definition "eligible assets" referred to in the said subarticle of the said Act shall include:

(a) deposits denominated in Maltese lira and euro, and currency notes denominated in Maltese lira and euro; and

(b) securities denominated in Maltese lira and euro with a primary listing on the Malta Stock Exchange.
