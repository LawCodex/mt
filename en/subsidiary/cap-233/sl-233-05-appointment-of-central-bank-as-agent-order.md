# Appointment of Central Bank as Agent Order (S.L. 233.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 233.05**

**APPOINTMENT OF CENTRAL BANK AS AGENT**

**ORDER**

1st January, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 428 of 2003.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Appointment of Central Bank as Agent Order.

## 2. Appointment of Central Bank as agent. Cap. 233.

The Central Bank of Malta has been appointed by the Minister of Finance to act as agent for the said Minister for the purposes of all the provisions of the External Transactions Act, with effect from 1st January, 2004.
