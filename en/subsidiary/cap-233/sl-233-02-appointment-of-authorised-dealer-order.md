# Appointment of Authorised Dealer Order (S.L. 233.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 233.02**

APPOINTMENT OF AUTHORISED DEALER ORDER 31st December, 1999

LEGAL NOTICE 225 of 1999.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Appointment of Authorised Dealer Order.

## 2. Appointment of authorised dealer. Cap. 233.

The Malta Financial Services Authority, Attard, has been appointed by the Minister of Finance to be an authorised dealer for the purposes of articles 17, 18 and 32 of the External Transactions Act, with effect from 1st January, 2000.
