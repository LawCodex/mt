# Planning Commission (Types of Applications) Regulations (S.L. 552.20)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 552.20**

**PLANNING COMMISSION (TYPES OF**

**APPLICATIONS) REGULATIONS**

1st April, 2016

LEGAL NOTICE 110 of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Planning Commission (Types of Applications) Regulations.

## 2. Planning Commission.

(1) There shall be a division of the Planning Commission to be known as the "Planning Commission (Development Permissions)" and a division of the Planning Commission to be known as the "Planning Commission (Regularisation Permissions)". These two divisions of the Planning Commission shall deal with different types of development applications as specified in sub-regulations (2) and (3).

(2) The Planning Commission (Development Permissions) shall deal with all types of development permission applications excluding applications submitted to regularise existing development in terms of regulations promulgated by the Parliamentary Secretary responsible for Planning and the Simplification of Administrative Processes for this purpose.

(3) The Planning Commission (Regularisation Permissions) shall deal with the types of application submitted in terms of regulations promulgated by the Parliamentary Secretary responsible for Planning and the Simplification of Administrative Processes for this purpose.

## 3. Applicability.

The provisions of these regulations shall be applicable to those development permission applications which would as yet be undecided on the 4th of April 2016.

## 4. Transitory provision.

In the interim period between the 4th of April 2016 and the date of the coming into force of regulations for the regularisation of existing development, the Planning Commission (Development Permissions) may delegate any other type of development permission application to be dealt with by the Development Commission (Regularisation Permissions) to ensure an efficient process for the determination of development applications.
