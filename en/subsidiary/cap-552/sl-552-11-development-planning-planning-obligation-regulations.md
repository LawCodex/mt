# Development Planning (Planning Obligation) Regulations (S.L. 552.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 552.11**

**DEVELOPMENT PLANNING**

**(PLANNING OBLIGATION) REGULATIONS**

1st December, 2009

LEGAL NOTICE 347 of 2009.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Development Planning (Planning Obligation) Regulations.

## 2. No imposition of planning obligation. Cap. 552.

No planning obligation imposing a payment, as indicated in article 77(1)(b) of the Development Planning Act, may be imposed on an applicant for a development permission in respect of a development of a national or strategic significance or a development affecting matters of national security.
