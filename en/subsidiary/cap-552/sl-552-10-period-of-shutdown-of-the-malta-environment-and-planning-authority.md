# Period of Shutdown of the Malta Environment and Planning Authority Offices Regulations (S.L. 552.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 552.10**

**PERIOD OF SHUTDOWN OF THE PLANNING**

**AUTHORITY OFFICES REGULATIONS**

15th December, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 4 of 2008.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Period of Shutdown of the Planning Authority Offices Regulations.

## 2. Definitions. Cap. 552.

"The Act" means the Development Planning Act.

## 3. Period of shutdown.

(1) The period of shutdown of the Planning Authority Offices shall be from the 23rd December of one year till the 1st January of the next following year.

(2) The period mentioned in subregulation (1) shall not be considered as forming part of the original or extended period mentioned in article 74(1) or of the period mentioned in article 71 of the Act.
