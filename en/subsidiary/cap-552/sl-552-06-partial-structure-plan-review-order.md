# Partial Structure Plan Review Order (S.L. 552.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 552.06**

**PARTIAL STRUCTURE PLAN REVIEW ORDER**

23rd February, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 35 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Partial Structure Plan Review Order.

## 2. Coming into force of Partial Structure Plan Review.

The Partial Structure Plan Review as resolved by the House of Representatives in sitting No. 427 of the 26th July, 2006 shall be deemed to have come into force on the 15th February, 2007.
