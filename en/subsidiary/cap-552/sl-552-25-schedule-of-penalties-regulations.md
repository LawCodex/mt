# Schedule of Penalties Regulations (S.L. 552.25)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 552.25**

## SCHEDULE OF PENALTIES REGULATIONS

24th November, 2012\* LEGAL NOTICE 277 of 2012, as amended by Legal Notice 288 of 2013.

## 1. Citation and applicability.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Schedule of Penalties Regulations.

(2) These regulations shall apply to those applications submitted on or after the date of coming into force of these regulations\*.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 549. Cap. 552.*

"the Act" means the Environment Protection Act or the Development Planning Act, as the case may require;

*Cap. 552. Cap. 549.*

"the Authority" means the Planning Authority established under the Development Planning Act and, or the Environment and Resources Authority established under the Environment Protection Act, as the case may be;

*S.L. 552.12*

"DPF" means the development permission fee in accordance with the Development Planning (Fees) Regulations, applicable on an application relating to the illegal development undertaken, or relating only to the illegal part in the case of an application covering a number of developments;

"ODZ" means Outside Development Zone and means development outside the boundary of a temporary provisions scheme or outside the boundary for development in a local plan;

"protected area" means an area which is protected under the provisions of the Act or any regulation made thereunder.

(2) Development under these regulations shall exclude minor amendments and development notification orders without notification.

(3) In these regulations any expression defined by the Act has the same meaning as it has in the Act.

## 3. Schedule of penalties. Cap. 504.

Where the Authority gives notice in writing to a person in accordance with article 93(1) of the Environment and Development Planning Act, the penalty which is to be notified to such person shall be in accordance with the schedules to these regulations in terms of the following regulations.

## 4. Development not in accordance with a development permission.

(1) Where the offence consists in failure to comply with approved plans or conditions or other obligations imposed by a development permission, unless such offence falls under Schedule C, the penalties shall be in accordance with Schedule A.

\*see sub-regulation (2) of regulation 1 of these regulations, as originally promulgated.

(2) The penalties mentioned in Schedule A shall be paid upon the decision of a development planning application indicating the need for sanctioning the offence, and shall be increased by a further 50% if such offence was not indicated by the applicant when the said application was submitted.

(3) The penalty shall be calculated in relation to the DPF, as shown in Schedule A.

## 5. Development without a development permission.

(1) Where the offence consists of development carried out without a development permission, unless such offence falls under Schedule C, the penalties shall be in accordance with Schedule B.

(2) The penalties mentioned in Schedule B shall be paid upon the decision of a development planning application indicating the need for sanctioning the offence, and shall be increased by 50% if such offence was not indicated by the applicant when the said application was submitted.

(3) The penalty shall be calculated in relation to the DPF, as shown in Schedule B.

## 6. Minor offences.

The following offences shall be considered as minor offences for the purposes of these regulations and shall, unless such property is within a protected area, be regulated by the penalties mentioned in Schedule C:

(a) an extension to a change of use not exceeding 25 square metres, changes to size, style, form and other similar elements of the facade, excluding timber balconies, where such changes alter materially the character or context of the building and its surroundings but does not result in a totally different facade, and

(b) addition of 36 square metres to a building, provided that such an addition would not result in the creation of an additional dwelling unit;

(c) a development notification order with notification. 7. The offences mentioned in Schedule D shall be regulated by the penalties mentioned therein.
