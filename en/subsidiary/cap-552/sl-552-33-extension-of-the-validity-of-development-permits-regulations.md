# Extension of the Validity of Development Permits Regulations (S.L. 552.33)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 552.33**

EXTENSION OF THE VALIDITY OF DEVELOPMENT PERMITS REGULATIONS

10th November, 2023

LEGAL NOTICE 263 of 2023, as amended by Legal Notice 66 of 2025.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Extension of the Validity of Development Permits Regulations.

## 2.    Cap. 552.

Unless the context otherwise requires, the words and expressions used in these regulations shall have the same meaning as that assigned to them in the Development Planning Act.

## 3. Extension of the validity of development permits.

*Amended by: L.N. 66 of 2025.*

Any development permit which on the date of coming into force of these regulations, is still within its period of validity but which is due to expire by, or on any date earlier to the 31st December 2026, shall have its period of validity extended by an additional three (3) years:

Provided that any development permit, which expired between the 1st of January 2023 and the 9th of November 2023, both dates included, shall be deemed to be valid until 31st December 2026:

Provided further that any development permit which expired between the 1st of January 2025 and the 1st of April 2025, both dates included, shall be deemed to be valid until the 31st December 2028.
