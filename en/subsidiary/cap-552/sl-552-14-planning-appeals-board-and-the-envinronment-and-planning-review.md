# Planning Appeals Board and the Envinronment and Planning Review Tribunal (Transitory Provisions) Regulations (S.L. 552.14)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 552.14**

**PLANNING APPEALS BOARD AND THE**

**ENVIRONMENT AND PLANNING REVIEW**

**TRIBUNAL (TRANSITORY PROVISIONS)**

**REGULATIONS**

25th January, 2011

LEGAL NOTICE 27 of 2011

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Planning Appeals Board and the Environment and Planning Review Tribunal (Transitory Provisions) Regulations.

## 2. Appeals to be dealt with by the Planning Appeals Board or the Environment and Planning Review Tribunal.

(1) The Planning Appeals Board shall deal with those appeals pending before it and which, before the 31st December 2010, have been deferred for a decision.

(2) The Environment and Planning Review Tribunal shall deal with:

(a) appeals submitted before the Planning Appeals Board and which, before the 31st December 2010, have not yet been deferred for a decision;

(b) appeals which have been re-appointed after the delivery of a preliminary decision by the Planning Appeals Board;

(c) appeals which have been re-appointed after a decision of the Court of Appeal; and

*Cap. 504.*

(d) all appeals submitted in accordance with the provisions of the Environment and Development Planning Act.
