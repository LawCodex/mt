# Extension of the Validity of Development Permits Regulations (S.L. 552.30)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 552.30**

EXTENSION OF THE VALIDITY OF DEVELOPMENT

**PERMITS REGULATIONS**

27th March, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 110 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is Extension of the Validity of Development Permits Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, the words and expressions used in these regulations shall have the same meaning as that assigned to them in the Development Planning Act.

*Cap. 552.*

## 3. Extension of the validity of development permits.

Any development permit which, on the date of entry into force of these regulations, is still within its period of validity but which is due to expire by, or any date earlier to, the 31st December 2022, shall have its remaining period of validity extended by an additional three (3) years.
