# Co-operative Societies (Levying of Fees) Regulations (S.L. 442.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 442.05**

**CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES**

**(LEVYING OF FEES) REGULATIONS**

1st October, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 198 of 2003 as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Co-operative Societies (Levying of Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Definitions. Cap. 442.

The words used in these regulations shall have the same meaning as in the Co-operative Societies Act, hereinafter referred to as “the Act”.

## 3. Power to levy fees.

The fees prescribed in the Schedule shall be the fees to be levied in relation to the inspection or issue of documents, certificates, copies or extracts under the Act.
