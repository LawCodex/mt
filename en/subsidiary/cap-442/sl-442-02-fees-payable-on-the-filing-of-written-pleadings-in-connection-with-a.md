# Fees payable on the filing of Written Pleadings in connection with a reference to the Court of Appeal Regulations (S.L. 442.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 442.02**

**FEES PAYABLE ON THE FILING OF WRITTEN**

**PLEADINGS IN CONNECTION WITH A**

**REFERENCE TO THE COURT OF APPEAL**

**REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 104 of 2001 as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Fees payable on the filing of Written Pleadings in connection with a reference to the Court of Appeal Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable on appeals.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007. Cap. 442.*

The fees payable under article 110(3) of the Co-operative Societies Act on the filing of written pleadings in connection with a reference to the Court of Appeal shall be one hundred and sixteen euro and forty-seven cents (€116.47).
