# Co-operative Societies (Rate of Dividend on Share Capital) Regulations (S.L. 442.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 442.01**

CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES (RATE OF DIVIDEND

**ON SHARE CAPITAL) REGULATIONS**

9th March, 1990

LEGAL NOTICE 35 of 1990.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Co-operative Societies (Rate of Dividend on Share Capital) Regulations.

## 2. Rate of dividend on share capital.

No registered co-operative society shall pay a dividend on paid up share capital exceeding five per centum per annum without the sanction of the Co-operatives Board.
