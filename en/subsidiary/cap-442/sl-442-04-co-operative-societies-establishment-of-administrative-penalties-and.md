# Co-operative Societies (Establishment of Administrative Penalties and Sanctions) Regulations (S.L. 442.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 442.04**

**ESTABLISHMENT OF ADMINISTRATIVE**

**PENALTIES AND SANCTIONS REGULATIONS**

1st December, 2003

LEGAL NOTICE 115 of 2003 as amended by Legal Notices 426 of 2007 426 of 2012, 215 of 2020 and 329 of 2021.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Co-operative Societies (Establishment of Administrative Penalties and Sanctions) Regulations.

## 2. Definitions. Cap. 442.

Words used in these regulations shall have the same meaning as in the Co-operative Societies Act, hereinafter referred to as “the Act”.

## 3. Civil debt constituting an executive title.

*Substituted by: L.N. 329 of 2021. Cap. 12.*

The Board shall have the power to impose administrative penalties and sanctions in accordance with the Schedule and such administrative penalty or sanction shall be payable to the Board as a civil debt constituting an executive title for the purposes of Title VII of Part I of the Second Book of the Code of Organization and Civil Procedure as if the payment of the amount of the fine had been ordered by a judgement of a court of civil jurisdiction.

## 4. Authority to publicize.

The Board shall also have the authority to publish in daily newspapers and in the broadcasting media the names of those cooperative societies which have become subject to administrative penalties and sanctions imposed on them by the same Board as provided for in these regulations and in the Act:

Provided that the Board shall use this authority only to give public notice of such contravention, and during such time as the said contravention persists.

## 5. Preclusion of dual proceedings.

(1) The taking of proceedings for the use and application of administrative penalties and sanctions by the Board with respect to a contravention as outlined in these regulations shall preclude the taking of criminal proceedings against the same person in respect of the same contravention.

(2) The provisions of these regulations shall not apply in those cases where criminal proceedings are commenced in respect of a contravention included in the Schedule.

## 6. Suspension of administrative penalties and sanctions.

*Substituted by: L.N. 329 of 2021. Cap. 465.*

The Board may, in exceptional circumstances that a public health emergency is declared or regulations come into force in accordance with article 26 of the Public Health Act, during the period when the public health emergency is in force or the regulations are in force suspend the period when it imposes penalties and administrative sanctions established in the Schedule resulting from non-compliance with articles 48, 64(1) and 65(1) of the Act up to a maximum of seventy-five (75) days until the public health emergency is lifted by the Superintendent of Public Health or the regulations are no longer in force.
