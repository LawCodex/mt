# Extension of Applicable Period Order (S.L. 62.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 62.01**

**EXTENSION OF APPLICABLE PERIOD ORDER**

23rd July, 2013

LEGAL NOTICE 218 of 2013.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Extension of Applicable Period Order.

## 2. Extension of applicable period. Cap. 62.

The Minister for Finance has specified that the applicable period in accordance with article 4(3)(a) of the Revenue (Safeguard) Act be extended from the 31st January, 2013 to the end of April, 2013.
