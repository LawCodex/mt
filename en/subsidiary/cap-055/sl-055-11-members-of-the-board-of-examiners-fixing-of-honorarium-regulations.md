# Members of the Board of Examiners (Fixing of Honorarium) Regulations (S.L. 55.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 55.11**

**MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF EXAMINERS**

**(FIXING OF HONORARIUM) REGULATIONS**

23rd June 2023

LEGAL NOTICE 120 of 2023.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Members of the Board of Examiners (Fixing of Honorarium) Regulations.

## 2. Honorarium to be paid to members of the Board of Examiners. Cap. 55.

With effect from the year 2023, the member appointed by the Notarial Council as well as the two (2) members appointed by the Minister responsible for notarial affairs to serve on the Board of Examiners established in accordance with article 7 of the Notarial Profession and Notarial Archives Act shall each receive an honorarium amounting to two thousand four hundred and twenty-five euro (€2,425) for each year during which they shall serve as members of the Board.
