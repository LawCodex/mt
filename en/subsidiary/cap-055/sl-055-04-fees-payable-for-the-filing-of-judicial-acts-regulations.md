# Fees payable for the Filing of Judicial Acts Regulations (S.L. 55.4)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 55.04**

**FEES PAYABLE FOR THE FILING OF JUDICIAL**

**ACTS REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2001 LEGAL NOTICE 100 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 408 of 2007 and Act XIII of 2015.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Fees payable for the Filing of Judicial Acts Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable for the filing of judicial acts.

*Amended by: L.N. 408 of 2007. Substituted by: XIII. 2015.28. Cap. 55.*

The fees payable under article 112A(1) of the Notarial Profession and Notarial Archives Act for the filing of judicial acts shall be one hundred and twenty euro (€120).
