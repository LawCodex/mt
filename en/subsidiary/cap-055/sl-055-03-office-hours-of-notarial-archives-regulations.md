# Office Hours of Notarial Archives Regulations (S.L. 55.3)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 55.03**

**OFFICE HOURS OF NOTARIAL ARCHIVES**

**REGULATIONS**

19th December, 2008 LEGAL NOTICE 337 of 2008.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Office Hours of Notarial Archives Regulations.

## 2. Opening days and hours.

The days and hours on which the Notarial Archives in Malta and Gozo at No. 2, Mikiel Anton Vassalli Street, Valletta and No. 128 Racecourse Street, Victoria, Gozo respectively, shall be open to the public, saving on those days when the office of the Notary to Government is closed, are detailed in the Schedule to these regulations.

## SCHEDULE

(Regulation 2)

**Malta**

From 1st January to 31st December:

Monday to Friday - 7.30a.m. to 3.00p.m.

**Gozo**

From 1st October to 15th June:

Monday to Friday - 7.30a.m. to 12.30p.m.

1.30p.m. to 4.45p.m.

From 16th June to 30th September:

Monday to Friday - 7.15a.m. to 1.00p.m
