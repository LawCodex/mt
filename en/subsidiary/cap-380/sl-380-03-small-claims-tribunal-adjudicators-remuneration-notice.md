# Small Claims Tribunal (Adjudicators' Remuneration) Notice (S.L. 380.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 380.03**

**SMALL CLAIMS TRIBUNAL**

**(ADJUDICATORS’ REMUNERATION) NOTICE**

1st April, 2015

LEGAL NOTICE 139 of 2015.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Notice is the Small Claims Tribunal (Adjudicators’ Remuneration) Notice.

## 2. Adjudicators’ remuneration.

The remuneration payable to adjudicators of the Small Claims Tribunal as from the 1st April, 2015 shall be at the rate of fifty euro (€50) per case disposed of, whether decided upon, ceded or withdrawn
