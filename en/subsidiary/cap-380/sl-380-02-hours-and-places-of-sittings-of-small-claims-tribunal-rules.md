# Hours and Places of Sittings of Small Claims Tribunal Rules (S.L. 380.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 380.02**

**HOURS AND PLACES OF SITTINGS**

**OF SMALL CLAIMS TRIBUNAL RULES**

1st January, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 252 of 2000.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these rules is the Hours and Places of Sittings of Small Claims Tribunal Rules.

## 2. Sittings.

(1) The sittings of the Small Claims Tribunal shall be held at the edifice of the Courts of Justice of Malta in Valletta and of the Court of Magistrates (Gozo) in Victoria, Gozo, or at such other place as the Minister of Justice may from time to time, designate by Order in the Gazette.

(2) All sittings shall be held on weekdays, excluding public holidays, and shall not start earlier than 12.00 p.m., without the specific consent of the Registrar.
