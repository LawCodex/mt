24th January, 2014

LEGAL NOTICE 28 of 2014.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fees payable to the National Statistics Office for the Processing of Data Requests Regulations.

## 2. Requests made by the Office of the Prime Minister and by the Ministry for Finance.

These regulations shall not apply to requests made by the Office of the Prime Minister and by the Ministry for Finance, as well as Departments falling under the said Office of the Prime Minister and Ministry for Finance.
