# Register of Applicants for Employment (Part Two) Order (S.L. 594.14)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 594.14**

REGISTER OF APPLICANTS FOR EMPLOYMENT

**(PART TWO) ORDER**

23rd May, 1980 LEGAL NOTICE 52 of 1980, as amended by Legal Notice 64 of 1988.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Register of Applicants for Employment (Part Two) Order.

## 2. Persons who may register under Part Two of the REgister of Applicants for Employment.

*Amended by: L.N. 64 of 1988.*

The classes of persons who may register in Part Two of the Register of Applicants for Employment shall be:

(i) persons in gainful occupation;

(ii) persons who, without a good and sufficient cause, terminate employment or reject an opportunity of employment.

## 3. Separate sections of the Register.

*Amended by: L.N. 64 of 1988.*

Each of the classes of persons mentioned in article 2(i) and (ii) shall be registered in separate sections of the Register.
