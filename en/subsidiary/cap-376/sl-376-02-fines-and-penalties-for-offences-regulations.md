# Fines and Penalties for Offences Regulations (S.L. 376.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 376.02**

**FINES AND PENALTIES FOR OFFENCES**

**REGULATIONS**

29th November, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 357 of 2002, as amended by Legal Notices 386 of 2003 and 425 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fines and Penalties for Offences Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires - "Act" means the Financial Institutions Act;

*Cap. 376.*

"competent authority" means the body nominated under article 12(1) of the Act; and

"Financial Institutions Directive" means any directive issued by the competent authority under article 13(2) of the Act;

*Cap. 330.*

"Tribunal for Financial Services" means the Financial Services Tribunal established under article 21(1) of the Malta Financial Services Authority Act.

## 3. Penalty.

(1) Any person found guilty of an offence for having contravened a provision of the Act appearing in the first column of the First Schedule shall be liable to the payment of the corresponding fine (multa) laid down in the second column of the said Schedule or to the corresponding period of imprisonment laid down in the third column thereof or to both such fine and imprisonment.

(2) Without prejudice to any regulatory provisions of the Act and to any regulatory provision under any other law, for any failure by any person to comply with a provision of the Act appearing in the first column of the Second Schedule consisting in the corresponding act or omission described in the second column thereof, the competent authority may impose by way of an administrative penalty, the corresponding sum appearing in the third column of the said Schedule.

## 4. Appeal.

(1) Any person may appeal to the Financial Services Tribunal against any decision of the competent authority imposing upon him an administrative penalty in accordance with the provisions of regulation 3(2).

(2) Subject to the provisions of subregulation (3), the provisions of article 21 of the Act shall apply mutatis mutandis to any appeal made under this regulation.

(3) Any appeal under this regulation shall, in all cases, suspend the execution of the decision appealed from and the decision of the Financial Services Tribunal on any such appeal shall be final.
