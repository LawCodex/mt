# Inspection of Milch Animals Regulations (S.L. 449.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 449.04**

INSPECTION OF MILCH ANIMALS REGULATIONS 1st May, 1978 LEGAL NOTICE 92 of 1976.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Inspection of Milch Animals Regulations.

## 2. Power of inspection.

It shall be lawful for an inspector and/or for the Sanitary Authority to inspect any farm, where milch animals are kept, for the purpose of -

(a) ascertaining the state of health of the said animals;

(b) issuing instructions on the treatment of the said animals, if necessary; and

(c) taking all steps to prevent the spread of disease to animal or man.

## 3. Information by owner and by veterinary surgeon.

(1) The owner of milch animals shall, when he suspects that any of his animals is sick, ascertain, without any delay, the cause of the illness and shall, if such cause consists of any of the diseases set out in the Food Safety Act, or set out in any order made thereunder by the Minister, inform the Superintendent of Public Health within twenty-four hours of such ascertainment.

*Cap. 449.*

(2) Any veterinary surgeon who becomes aware that any milch animal is suffering from any of the diseases referred to in sub-regulation (1) shall immediately inform the Superintendent of Public Health accordingly.

## 4. Steps by Superintendent of Public Health.

The Superintendent of Public Health, on receiving any information as is referred to in regulation 3, shall take any such steps as he may deem necessary.
