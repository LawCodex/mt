# Food to be sold by Net Weight Direction (S.L. 449.01)

SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 449.01

FOOD TO BE SOLD BY NET WEIGHT

DIRECTION

10th August, 1934 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 324 of 1934.

*\1.*

*Title.*

The title of this Direction is Food to be sold by Net Weight Direction.

*\2.*

*Sale of food.*

The sale of all articles of food commonly sold by weight shall be made by the net weight.
