# Kava-Kava Regulations (S.L. 449.37)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 449.37**

**KAVA-KAVA REGULATIONS**

30th January, 2003 LEGAL NOTICE 40 of 2004.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Kava-Kava Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

"Kava-Kava" means a plant, or any part of or an extract from a plant, belonging to the species Piper methysticum;

"food" includes:

*S.L. 449.36*

(a) food supplements, as defined in the Food Supplements Regulations, and

(b) non-alcoholic and alcoholic beverages,

*Cap. 458.*

but shall not include medicinal products, as defined under the Medicines Act.

## 3. Prohibition of sale of food consisting of or containing Kava-Kava.

No person shall manufacture, sell or import into Malta any food consisting of, or containing, Kava-Kava.
