# Erucic Acid Regulations (S.L. 449.24)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 449.24**

**ERUCIC ACID REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2001 LEGAL NOTICE 247 of 2000.

*Title.*

1.1 The title of these regulations is the Erucic Acid Regulations.

*Scope.*

2.1 These regulations shall apply to:

(a) oils, fats and mixtures thereof intended as such for human consumption;

(b) compound foodstuffs to which oils, fats or mixtures thereof have been added and the overall fat content of which exceeds 5%.

*Maximum levels of erucic acid.*

3.1 The products mentioned in regulation 2 shall contain not more than 5% erucic acid, calculated on the total level of fatty acids in the fat component.
