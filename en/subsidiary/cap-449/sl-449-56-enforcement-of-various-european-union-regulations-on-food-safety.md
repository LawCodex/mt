# Enforcement of various European Union Regulations on Food Safety Regulations (S.L. 449.56)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 449.56**

ENFORCEMENT OF VARIOUS EUROPEAN UNION REGULATIONS ON FOOD SAFETY REGULATIONS 16th September, 2008 LEGAL NOTICE 226 of 2008,as amended by Legal Notice 103 of 2018.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Enforcement of various European Union Regulations on Food Safety Regulations.

## 2. Definition. Cap. 449.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, "Act" mean the Food Safety Act.

## 4. Offences and penalties.

*Substituted by: L.N.103 of 2018.*

(1)Any person who violates the provisions of the regulations of the European Union to which these regulations apply shall be guilty of an offence against these regulations and the provisions of the Act concerning the violation of the provisions of regulations made under the Act shall apply to the violation of the said regulations of the European Union.

*Cap. 449.*

(2) The penalties mentioned in Article 29 of Regulation (EU) No. 2015/2283 refer to those as given in article 43(1), (2), (3)(a) and (b) of Food Safety Act.
