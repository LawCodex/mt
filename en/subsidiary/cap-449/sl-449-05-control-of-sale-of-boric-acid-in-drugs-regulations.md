# Control of Sale of Boric Acid in Drugs Regulations (S.L. 449.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 449.05**

**CONTROL OF SALE OF BORIC ACID IN**

**DRUGS REGULATIONS**

1st May, 1978 LEGAL NOTICE 118 of 1976.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Control of Sale of Boric Acid in Drugs Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, "to sell" includes to offer, expose, advertise or keep for sale.

## 3. Control of importation, etc.

No person may import, manufacture or sell any medicinal preparation, intended for internal use, containing boric acid or sodium borate.

## 4. Label when for external use.

No person may sell any medicinal preparation containing boric acid or sodium borate, intended for external use, unless the container bears a label, clearly legible, with the words "POISON- For external use only. Not to be used on skin of infants and children under three years of age".
