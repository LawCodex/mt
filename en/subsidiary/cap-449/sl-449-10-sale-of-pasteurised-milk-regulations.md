# Sale of Pasteurised Milk Regulations (S.L. 449.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 449.10**

SALE OF PASTEURISED MILK REGULATIONS 1st May, 1978 LEGAL NOTICE 148 of 1977.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Sale of Pasteurised Milk Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations "to sell" includes to offer, expose, advertise or keep for sale and to supply by way of compensation or otherwise.

## 3. Limitation of sale of raw milk.

No person may sell for human consumption any raw milk other than to the Manager of the Malta Dairy Products.

## 4. Sale of milk other than pasteurised.

Saving the provision of regulation 3, no person may sell any milk other than pasteurised milk:

Provided that this prohibition shall not apply to the selling of preserved milk in sealed and appropriately labelled containers.

## 5. When milk is deemed to be pasteurised.

For the purpose of pasteurisation, milk is deemed to have been so treated when it has been heated -

(a) to a temperature of not less than 71.7° of the Celsius Scale (161° F) and kept at that temperature for a period of not less than fifteen seconds and thereafter cooled to a temperature of not more than 10 degrees of the Celsius Scale (50° F) immediately after heat treatment; or

(b) to a temperature of not less than 62.8° of the Celsius Scale (145° F) and kept at that temperature for a period of not less than thirty minutes, and thereafter cooled to a temperature of not more than 10° of the Celsius Scale (50° F) immediately after heat treatment and which has not been so treated more than once.

## 6. Non-applicability of regulations.

Nothing in these regulations shall apply to ovine milk.
