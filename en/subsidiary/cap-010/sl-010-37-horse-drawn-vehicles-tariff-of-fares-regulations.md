# Horse-drawn Vehicles (Tariff of Fares) Regulations (S.L. 10.37)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 10.37**

**HORSE-DRAWN VEHICLES**

**(TARIFF OF FARES) REGULATIONS**

27th September, 1988 LEGAL NOTICE 75 of 1988, as amended by Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

### 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Horse-drawn Vehicles (Tariff of Fares) Regulations.

### 2. Hire by time.

Licensed horse-drawn vehicles (karrozzini) shall be hired by time which shall commence when the cab is called off the stand or street.

### 3. Fares.

*Substituted by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

The following fares shall be charged by time for the hiring of a horse-drawn cab per trip:

Minimum fare (covering the first half-hour or

## part thereof) .......... six euros and ninety-nine cents (6.99) — For each subsequent half-hour or part thereofone euro and

seventy-five cents (1.75).

### 4. Definition of "trip".

The term "trip" means the transportation of a number of passengers not exceeding four in number on an excursion or from one destination to another:

Provided that for the purposes of this regulation, two children between the ages of three and ten years shall be considered as one person.

### 5. Copy of tariff of fares.

The driver of each horse-drawn cab shall post up a copy of the tariff of fares in his vehicle in a place where it can be easily seen by the passengers.
