# Addolorata Cemetery Regulations (S.L. 10.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 10.04**

**ADDOLORATA CEMETERY REGULATIONS**

29th April, 1910;

11th February, 1916;

9th November, 1933 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 107 of 1910, as amended by Act XIII of 1983, GOVERNMENT NOTICE 29 of 1916, as amended by Act XIII of 1983, and GOVERNMENT NOTICE 467 of 1933, as amended by Government Notice 167 of 1935, consolidated, and as amended by Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

**Preliminary**

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Addolorata Cemetery Regulations.

**Cemetery regulations**

## 2. Opening hours of cemetery.

*Amended by: G.N. 167 of 1935.*

The Addolorata Cemetery shall be open to the public on any day from 7.00a.m to noon and from 2.00p.m to sunset; no interments shall take place out of these hours and no person shall be allowed to enter or remain in the cemetery except for urgent reasons.

## 3. Prohibition of vehicles.

No vehicle shall be allowed to enter the cemetery except the hearse, the parish priest’s vehicle, and other vehicles carrying materials for use in the cemetery; in special cases the Superintendent of Public Health or the Capuchin Father in charge may grant permission for visiting the cemetery in a conveyance.

## 4. Works or repairs.

Except in very urgent cases, no works or repairs connected with graves and chapels shall be allowed between the 20th of October and the 10th of November of each year.

## 5. Prohibitions.

It is forbidden to pluck flowers, to smoke, or to carry fire arms in the cemetery: provided that the latter prohibition shall not apply in the case of funerals of members of the Armed Forces of Malta.

**Watching of corpses**

## 6. Watching of corpses.

Corpses which are conveyed to the cemetery before the lapse of twenty-four hours from death shall be kept in the mortuary annexed to the cemetery and shall be watched by a person duly approved by the Superintendent of Public Health.

## 7. Fee payable.

*Amended by: XIII. 1983.5; L.N. 407 of 2007. Cap. 10.*

A fee of twenty-nine cents (0.29) for such watch shall be charged on application for the permission referred to in article 143 of the Code of Police Laws.

**Removal of corpses**

## 8. Removal of corpses.

Except in cases of absolute necessity, the removal of corpses from graves to the charnel-house in the cemetery shall take place during the first seven days of any of the months of January, April, July or October, during such hours as shall be fixed by the Superintendent of Public Health.

## 9. Fee for supervision.

*Amended by: XIII. 1983.5; L.N. 407 of 2007.*

The removals referred to in regulation 8 of these Regulations shall be carried out under the supervision of an officer appointed by the Superintendent of Public Health; and a fee of twelve cents (0.12) shall be charged for such supervision if the removal is effected on the days specified in regulation 8, and of forty cents (0.40) on other days.

## 10. Persons appointed for removal.

*Amended by: XIII. 1983.5; L.N. 407 of 2007.*

No persons other than those appointed by the Superintendent of Public Health shall be employed in carrying out any such removal, and a fee of forty-seven cents (0.47) shall be charged for such service.

## 11. Single fee.

A single fee for supervision shall be charged collectively on all removals taking place during the same day.
