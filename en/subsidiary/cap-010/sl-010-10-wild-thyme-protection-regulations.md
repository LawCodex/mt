# Wild Thyme Protection Regulations (S.L. 10.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 10.10**

**WILD THYME (PROTECTION) REGULATIONS**

23rd March, 1932 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 85 of 1932.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Wild Thyme (Protection) Regulations.

## 2. Prohibition.

It shall not be lawful to destroy, cut down or uproot shrubs of wild thyme or savoury (Thymus Capitatus) known in Maltese as Sag]tar or to sell, purchase, use or keep such shrubs for any purpose.

## 3. Permission in certain cases.

The Director of Agriculture may, however, give permission in writing for the cutting down, uprooting and removal of wild thyme, whenever the land is required for reclamation or to be otherwise industrially exploited, or in order to reduce the number of the shrub where it overgrows.
