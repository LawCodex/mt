# Video Film Shows Regulations (S.L. 10.34)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 10.34**

**VIDEO FILM SHOWS REGULATIONS**

24th August, 1984 LEGAL NOTICE 43 of 1984, as amended by Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Video Film Shows Regulations.

## 2. Licence.

It shall not be lawful to hold in any public place or in any place open to the public any show by means of a video film projector, without a licence from the Commissioner of Police.

## 3. Licence fees.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

For the issue or renewal of any licence under these Regulations there shall be payable to the Commissioner of Police the following fees:

(a) in respect of shows by video film

projector held in any premises

licensed as a hotel, restaurant or

catering establishment ............... €116.47 per annum;

(b) in respect of shows by video film

projector held in any trade or

business premises, other than a

hotel, restaurant or catering

establishment, or in respect of

any public place or a place open

to the public .............................. €23.29 per annum. 4. The provisions of the Police Licences Regulations shall apply to any licence issued or renewed in accordance with these Regulations.

*Applicability of Police Licences Regulations. S.L. 128.01.*

## 5. Applicability of Value

*Added Tax Act. Cap. 406.*

Any video film show held in premises licensed under regulation 3(a) is exempt from tax if held in accordance with the provisions of the Value Added Tax Act. Any other video film show is chargeable to tax as provided in that Act.

## 6. Offences and penalties.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007.*

(1) Any person who contravenes the provisions of these regulations other than regulation 4, shall commit an offence and shall on conviction be liable for each offence to a fine (multa) not exceeding one thousand and one hundred and sixty-four euros and sixty-nine cents (1,164.69).

*S.L.128.01.*

(2) Any person who is found guilty of a contravention against any of the provisions of the Police Licences Regulations, made applicable to these regulations by regulation 4, shall on conviction be liable to the penalties laid down in those regulations.
