# Applications for Holding Musical Bands Order (S.L. 10.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 10.01**

APPLICATIONS FOR HOLDING MUSICAL BANDS ORDER

3rd February, 1898 POLICE NOTICE dated 3rd February, 1898.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Applications for Holding Musical Bands Order.

## 2. Application.

Applications for fixing poles in streets, squares and public thoroughfares on the occasions of public festivals, for musical bands, or for firing petards will not be considered unless the same are received at the relative Police Station as follows:

(a) for fixing poles .........................two clear weeks;

(b) for musical bands ......................three clear days; and (c) for firing petards........................ten clear days,

before the date of the required permit.

## 3. Prohibition of firing of petards.

No permits for the firing of petards in streets, squares and public thoroughfares will be issued.
