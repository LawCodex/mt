# Sports Grounds (Prices of Admission) Regulations (S.L. 10.32)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 10.32**

**SPORTS GROUND (PRICES OF ADMISSION)**

**REGULATIONS**

4th January, 1978 LEGAL NOTICE 1 of 1978 as amended by: Legal Notice 112 of 1978;

Act XIII of 1983; and Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Sports Ground (Prices of Admission) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: L.N. 112 of 1978.*

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise require, sports ground means -

(a) the Stadium, G\\ira;

(b) Manoel Island Football Ground, G\\ira;

(c) Schreiber Ground, Paola; and

(d) the Silver Jubilee Ground at Gozo.

## 3. Maximum admission price.

The price that may be charged for admission to any sports ground shown in the first column of the Schedule, inclusive of any duty payable thereon under the Value Added Tax Act, in respect of any football match, shall not exceed that shown in respect thereof in the second column of that Schedule:

*Cap. 406.*

Provided that the Commissioner of Police may authorize in writing an increase of the said prices, or of any of the said prices, either on a regular basis or in respect of special occasions.

## 4. Penalties.

*Amended by: XIII. 1983.5; L.N. 407 of 2007.*

Any person who sells, issues or releases any ticket for admission to a sports ground at a price higher than that as is provided in regulation 3 shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable, on conviction, to a fine (multa) not exceeding two hundred and thirty-two euros and ninety-four cents (232.94) in respect of each offence.
