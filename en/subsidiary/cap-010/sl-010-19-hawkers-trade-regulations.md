# Hawkers Trade Regulations (S.L. 10.19)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 10.19**

**HAWKERS’ TRADE REGULATIONS**

1st January, 1938 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 1 of 1938.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Regulation is Hawkers’ Trade Regulation. 2. It shall not be lawful, except with the special permission of the Commissioner of Police for any licensed hawker or a itinerant vendor to carry on any trade or business at any time during which it would be unlawful to keep a shop open for carrying on that class of trade or business.

*Carrying on of trade.*
