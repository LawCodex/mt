# Fees (Lifelong Learning Courses) Regulations (S.L. 605.03)

**FEES (LIFELONG LEARNING COURSES)**

## 1. Citation.

*Amended by: L.N. 216 of 2013.*

The title of these regulations is the Fees (Lifelong Learning Courses) Regulations.

## 2. Fees chargeable for Lifelong Learning.

*Amended by: L.N. 374 of 2009; L.N. 396 of 2010. Substituted by: L.N. 216 of 2013. Amended by: L.N. 336 of 2013; L.N. 103 of 2024.*

The fees chargeable to -

(a) Maltese citizens; or

(b) citizens of other Member States of the European Union who are entitled to equal treatment to Maltese citizens; or

(c) citizens of any other country who are entitled to equal treatment to Maltese citizens in matters related to employment by virtue of the application to that country of EU legislation and treaty provisions dealing with the free movement of workers; or

(d) any other persons who are entitled to equal treatment to Maltese citizens in terms of the law or by virtue of EU legislation and treaty provisions, on account of their family relationship with persons mentioned in paragraphs (a), (b) or (c); or

(e) third country nationals who have been granted long-term resident status in Malta under regulation 4 of the Status of Long-Term Residents (Third Country Nationals) Regulations, or who have been granted a residence permit under regulation 18(3) thereof, together with family members of such third country nationals who have been granted a residence permit under the Family Reunification Regulations,

## 3. Persons eligible to apply for any course offered by the Directorate for Research, Lifelong Learning and Employability.

*Amended by: L.N. 374 of 2009; L.N. 396 of 2010. Substituted by: L.N. 103 of 2024.*

(1) Foreign nationals who are not officially registered as residents of Malta are not eligible to apply for any course offered by the Directorate for Research, Lifelong Learning and Employability.

(2) Without prejudice to regulation 2, foreign nationals applying for a course shall present documents attesting that they are officially residing in Malta in accordance with national or European Union law.

(3) Third-country nationals may not request an authorisation to remain in Malta on the basis of having applied for, or are following any course offered by the Directorate for Research, Lifelong Learning and Employability.
