# National Curriculum Framework Regulations (S.L. 605.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 605.07**

**NATIONAL CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK**

**REGULATIONS**

22nd February, 2013

LEGAL NOTICE 69 of 2013, as amended by Act XXIX of 2019.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the National Curriculum Framework Regulations.

## 2. National Curriculum Framework.

*Amended by XXIX.2019.71. Cap. 605.*

The National Curriculum Framework of studies for all schools in Malta for the purposes of in article 5(b) and (d) of the Education Act shall, with effect from 22nd February 2013, be that set out in the document entitled "A National Curriculum Framework for All" published by the Ministry responsible for Education in December 2012.

## 3. Implementation.

The implementation of the National Curriculum Framework shall proceed in stages according to guidelines to be adopted by the Minister responsible for Education.
