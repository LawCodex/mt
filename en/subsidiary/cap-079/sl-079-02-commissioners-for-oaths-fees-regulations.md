# Commissioners for Oaths (Fees) Regulations (S.L. 79.2)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 79.02**

**COMMISSIONERS FOR OATHS (FEES)**

**REGULATIONS**

15th May, 1989 LEGAL NOTICE 49 of 1989, as amended by Legal Notice 408 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Commissioners for Oaths (Fees) Regulations.

## 2. Fees.

A fee of one euro and sixteen cents (1.16) shall be levied by commissioners for oaths for the swearing of affidavits and attestations of signatures unless another fee is otherwise prescribed under any other law.
