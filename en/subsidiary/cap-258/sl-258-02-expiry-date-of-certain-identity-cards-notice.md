# Expiry Date of Certain Identity Cards Notice (S.L. 258.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 258.02**

**EXPIRY DATE OF CERTAIN IDENTITY CARDS**

**NOTICE**

12th March, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 123 of 2004.

## 1. Title.

The title of this notice is the Expiry Date of Certain Identity Cards Notice.

## 2. Expiry date. S.L. 258.01

For the purposes of the proviso to regulation 3(1) of the Identity Cards (Issue and Validity) Regulations, the 1st April, 2004 has been established as the date on which all paper based plastic encapsulated identity cards issued in terms of the Identity Card Act before the 1st April, 2002 shall cease to be valid.

*Kap. 258.*
