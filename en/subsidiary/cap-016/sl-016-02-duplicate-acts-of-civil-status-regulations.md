# Duplicate Acts of Civil Status Regulations (S.L. 16.2)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 16.02**

**DUPLICATE ACTS OF CIVIL STATUS**

**REGULATIONS**

14th September, 1979 LEGAL NOTICE 92 of 1979.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Duplicate Acts of Civil Status Regulations.

## 2. Preparation of duplicate acts.

(1) It shall be the duty of the Director of Public Registry to prepare duplicate acts of civil status of all such original acts deposited in the Public Registry in Malta or Gozo which are destroyed or damaged through fire or wear and tear.

(2) The preparation of the civil acts mentioned in sub-regulation (1) of this regulation shall be carried out by reference to the register kept at the Public Registry.

## 3. Authentication of acts.

The duplicate acts so prepared shall be authenticated by the Director of Public Registry or by such other person delegated by him for this purpose by affixing his signature at the foot of the said acts.

## 4. Applicability of the provisions of the Civil Code. Cap. 16.

The provisions of the Civil Code relating to the formulation of acts of civil status shall apply to duplicate acts prepared under these Regulations.
