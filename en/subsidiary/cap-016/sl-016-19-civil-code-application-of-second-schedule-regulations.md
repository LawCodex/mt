# Civil Code (Application of Second Schedule) Regulations (S.L. 16.19)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 16.19**

**CIVIL CODE (APPLICATION OF SECOND**

## SCHEDULE) REGULATIONS

10th March, 2023

LEGAL NOTICE 57 of 2023.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Civil Code (Application of Second Schedule) Regulations.

## 2. Scope.

It is hereby provided for the avoidance of doubt that organisations formed in pursuance of the provisions of article 7A of the Drug Dependence (Treatment not Imprisonment) Act shall be regulated by the provisions of the Second Schedule to the Civil Code insofar as those provisions apply to associations, to such extent as provided in the Responsible Use of Cannabis (Licensing of Organisations) Regulations.
