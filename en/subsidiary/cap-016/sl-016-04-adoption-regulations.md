# Adoption Regulations (S.L. 16.4)

SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 16.04

ADOPTION REGULATIONS

1st March, 1995 LEGAL NOTICE 15 of 1995,as amended by Act II of 2019.

*\1.*

*Title. 2. Matters prescribed for the purpose of various articles of the Civil Code. Cap. 16. Amended by: II.2019.6.*

The title of these Regulations is Adoption Regulations. The following matters are prescribed for the purposes of the provisions of the Civil Code hereinafter specified:

(a) for the purposes of article 116(2), the prescribed authority therein referred to shall be the Chief Executive Officer of the Social Care Standards Authority. The Chief Executive Officer shall in turn submit a social report to the court in the interest of the person to be adopted, and the court shall decide on the application for an adoption after it has taken cognizance of the report;

(b) for the purposes of article 118(2), the prescribed manner therein referred to shall be a reference to the number which distinguishes the application filed in favour of the person whose identity is not known to the consenting party and a reference to the date on which that application was filed;

(c) for the purposes of article 118(2)(b), the class of person who may attest a document of the kind referred to in that sub-article shall be any class of person holding an office which, according to the law of Malta or of the place where the document is attested, empowers such person to attest documents at the place where the document is executed;

(d) for the purposes of article 119(2), the certificate of a registered medical practitioner shall be required in all cases except where the applicant or one of the applicants is the mother or the father of the person to be adopted or where the Court deems proper to exempt the applicant from such a requirement;

(e) for the purposes of article 120, the person who may be appointed by the Court as special curator of a minor shall be a person who has attained the age of thirty-five years and is, by reason of his experience or office, a fit and proper person to act as such a curator.
