# European Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Decisions concerning Custody of Children and on the Restoration of Custody of Children Order (S.L. 410.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 410.02**

**EUROPEAN CONVENTION ON THE**

**RECOGNITION AND ENFORCEMENT OF**

**DECISIONS CONCERNING CUSTODY OF**

**CHILDREN AND ON THE RESTORATION OF**

**CUSTODY OF CHILDREN ORDER**

25th July, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 131 of 2000.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the European Convention on Recognition and Enforcement of Decisions concerning Custody of Children and on the Restoration of Custody of Children Order.

## 2. Designated countries. Cap. 410.

(a) The countries specified in the Schedule are hereby specified for the purposes of article 15 of the Child Abduction and Custody Act.

(b) The date specified in the Schedule with respect to each country specified therein shall be the date on which the European Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Decisions concerning Custody of Children and on the Restoration of Custody of Children which was signed in Luxembourg on the 20th May, 1980 has come into force between Malta and that country.
