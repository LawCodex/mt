# Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction Order (S.L. 410.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 410.01**

**CONVENTION ON THE CIVIL ASPECTS OF**

INTERNATIONAL CHILD ABDUCTION ORDER 25th July, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 130 of 2000, as amended by Legal Notices 235 of 2000, 119 and 198 of 2001, 56, 253 and 380 of 2002, 62, 148, 295 and 351 of 2003, 336 and 526 of 2004, 39 and 115 of 2005, 209 of 2009, 334 of 2011 and 230 of 2016, 212 of 2019, 5 of 2023 and 30 of 2024.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction Order.

## 2. Designated countries. Cap. 410.

(a) The countries specified in the Schedule are hereby specified for the purposes of article 4 of the Child Abduction and Custody Act.

(b) The date specified in the Schedule with respect to each country specified therein shall be the date on which the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction which was signed at The Hague on the 25th October, 1980 has come into force between Malta and that country.
