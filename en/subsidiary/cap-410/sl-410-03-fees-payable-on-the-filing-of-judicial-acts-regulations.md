# Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts Regulations (S.L. 410.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 410.03**

**FEES PAYABLE ON THE FILING OF JUDICIAL**

**ACTS REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 116 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fees payable on the Filing of Judicial Acts Regulations.

## 2. Fees payable on filing of judicial acts.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007. Cap. 410.*

The fees payable under article 26(3) of the Child Abduction and Custody Act on the filing of judicial acts shall be eleven euro and sixty-five cents (€11.65).
