# Control of Water Pumps and Wells Order (S.L. 545.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 545.02**

CONTROL OF WATER PUMPS AND WELLS ORDER 26th March, 1946

PROCLAMATIONS V of 1946 and II of 1947, consolidated.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Control of Water Pumps and Wells Order.

## 2. Declaration of Malta as water controlled area.

The whole area of the Island of Malta is hereby declared to be a water-controlled area with effect from the 8th day April, 1946.

## 3. Declaration of Gozo as water controlled area.

The whole area of the Island of Gozo is hereby declared to be a water-controlled area with effect from the 11th day February, 1947.
