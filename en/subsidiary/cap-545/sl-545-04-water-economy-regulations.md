# Water Economy Regulations (S.L. 545.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 545.04**

**WATER ECONOMY REGULATIONS**

29th July, 1952

GOVERNMENT NOTICES 371 of 1952 and 402 of 1956, consolidated.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Water Economy Regulations.

## 2. Cutting off of water supply.

The supply of water from public standpipes shall be cut off every evening for a continuous period of eight hours.

## 3. Prohibition of use of hoses.

The use of hoses or mechanical apparatus for washing vehicles is prohibited.

## 4. Prohibition of use of water supplied from Government piped supplies for washing motor cars.

The use of water supplied to any tenement from Government piped supplies for washing motor cars or other vehicles is prohibited.
