# Applicable Rate in lieu of the Proxy for the Market Price Regulations (S.L. 545.40)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 545.40**

**APPLICABLE RATE IN LIEU OF THE PROXY FOR**

**THE MARKET PRICE REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2023

LEGAL NOTICE 63 of 2023.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Applicable Rate in lieu of the Proxy for the Market Price Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to determine the rate to be paid by the distribution system operator in lieu of the proxy for the market price for the year 2023 in view of the international energy crisis due to the Ukraine conflict.

## 2. Interpretation. Cap. 545. S.L. 545.34.

The definitions in the Regulator for Energy and Water Services Act and in the Electricity Regulations shall apply.

## 3. Rate.

The rate to be paid by the distribution system operator in lieu of the proxy for the market price for the year 2023 shall be of eight cents and eighty-five mils (€0.08,85) per kWh.
