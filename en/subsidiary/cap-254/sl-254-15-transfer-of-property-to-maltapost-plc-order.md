# Transfer of Property to Maltapost plc Order (S.L. 254.15)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 254.15**

TRANSFER OF PROPERTY TO MALTAPOST P.L.C.

**ORDER**

18th February, 2011

LEGAL NOTICE 53 of 2011.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Transfer of Property to Maltapost plc Order.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Order:

*Cap. 254.*

"the Act" means the Postal Services Act;

"the Commissioner" means the Commissioner of Land;

"the company" means Maltapost plc;

*Cap. 386.*

"Maltapost plc" means the public limited company registered in terms of the Companies Act and which is licensed under article 8(1) of the Act to provide postal services in Malta.

## 3. Transfer of premises to Maltapost plc.

The premises consisting of the building and land at site 305, Triq Ħal Qormi, Marsa, owned by the Government and currently being used by Maltapost plc, marked as Site A on the Plan in the Schedule, shall be transferred by title of sale by the Commissioner to the company under a contract of sale entered into between the Commissioner and the company.
