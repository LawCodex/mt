# Post Office Leases Order (S.L. 254.16)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 254.16**

**POST OFFICE LEASES ORDER**

31st August, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 289 of 2018

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Post Office Leases Order. 2. In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Interpretation.*

*Cap. 254.*

"the Act" means the Postal Services Act;

"company" means the company called "MaltaPost p.l.c." which holds a licence under article 7 of the Act to provide the postal services under the Postal Services (Designation of Universal Service Providers) Order and the MaltaPost p.l.c Licence (Modification) Regulations (revoked).

*S.L. 254.13.*

*S.L. 254.14.*

## 3. Premises listed in First and Second Schedules.

(1) The premises listed in the First Schedule are premises which were, on the 1st May, 1998 held by Government under title of lease, and are still held under title of lease by Government, and used as a Post Office.

(2) The premises in the Second Schedule are premises owned by Government and which on the 1st May, 1998 were used as a Post Office.

## 4. Validity of transfer of leases.

(1) The title of lease of the premises listed in the First Schedule which has been vested in the company as from the 1st May, 1998, shall remain so vested in the company until the 31st May, 2020 or for such time as the said company holds a licence to provide the postal services mentioned in article 8 (1) of the Act, whichever is the shorter.

(2) The transfer of the titles of lease referred to in sub-article (1) may be extended for further periods up to the 31st May, 2028.

## 5. Validity of leases of premises.

The premises listed in the Second Schedule which have been leased to the company as from the 1st May, 1998, shall remain so leased to the company for such time as the said company holds a licence to provide the postal services mentioned in article 8 (1) of the Act.
