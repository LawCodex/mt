# Central Bank of Malta Act(Appointment of Competent Authority on Interchange Fees for Card-Based Payment Transactions) Regulations (S.L. 204.7)

CENTRAL BANK OF MALTA ACT (APPOINTMENT OF

COMPETENT AUTHORITY ON INTERCHANGE FEES

*1*

FOR CARD-BASED PAYMENT TRANSACTIONS) [S.L.204.07

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 204.07**

CENTRAL BANK OF MALTA ACT (APPOINTMENT OF COMPETENT AUTHORITY ON INTERCHANGE FEES FOR CARD-BASED PAYMENT

**TRANSACTIONS) REGULATIONS**

9th October, 2015

LEGAL NOTICE 326 of 2015, as amended by Legal Notice 220 of 2016.

## 1. Citation and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Central Bank of Malta Act (Appointment of Competent Authority on Interchange Fees for Card-Based Payment Transactions) Regulations.

(2) The scope of these regulations is to implement the provisions of Regulation (EU) No 2015/751 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2015 on interchange fees for card-based payment transactions hereinafter referred to as "the Regulation".

## 2. Appointment of competent authority.

The Minister for Finance hereby appoints the Central Bank of Malta as the competent authority for the purposes of Article 13 of the Regulation to ensure the enforcement of the Regulation, and to monitor effectively compliance with the Regulation, including to counter attempts by the payment service providers to circumvent the Regulation and to take all necessary measures to ensure such compliance.

## 3. Imposition of sanctions.

*Added by: L.N. 220 of 2016.*

The Minister for Finance further empowers the Central Bank of Malta, for the purposes of Article 14 of the Regulation, to issue and enforce sanctions where any person contravenes or fails to comply with the provisions of the Regulation, and to take all necessary measures to ensure that they are applied.

## 4. Complaint and redress procedures.

*Added by: L.N. 220 of 2016. Cap. 330.*

The Minister for Finance hereby designates the Financial Services Tribunal established under article 21 of the Malta Financial Services Authority Act as the competent tribunal to hear and adjudicate on any complaint or redress procedure instigated by, or arising between, any payee and, or its payment service provider in respect of disputes arising pursuant to the application of the provisions of the Regulation.
