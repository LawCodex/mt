# Registration Area Order (1991) (S.L. 296.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 296.07**

**REGISTRATION AREA ORDER (1991)**

1st July, 1991

LEGAL NOTICE 73 of 1991.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Registration Area Order (1991).

## 2. Declaration of registration areas.

The areas shown outlined with a non continuous thick line on the maps contained in the Schedule hereto are declared, as from the 1st July, 1991, to be registration areas for the purposes of article 10 of the Land Registration Act.
