# Registration Area Order (2000) (S.L. 296.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 296.10**

**REGISTRATION AREA ORDER (2000)**

1st September, 2000

LEGAL NOTICE 144 of 2000.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Registration Area Order (2000).

## 2. Declaration of registration areas.

The area shown outlined with a non continuous thick line on the map contained in the Schedule hereto is declared, as from the 1st September, 2000, to be a registration area for the purposes of article 10 of the Land Registration Act.

*Cap. 296.*
