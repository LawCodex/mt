# Registration Area Order (1986) (S.L. 296.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 296.05**

**REGISTRATION AREA ORDER (1986)**

1st April, 1986

LEGAL NOTICES 11, 21 and 70 of 1986, consolidated.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Registration Area Order (1986).

## 2. Declaration of registration areas.

The areas shown outlined with a non continuous thick line on the maps contained in the Schedule hereto are declared, as from the date mentioned therein, to be registration areas for the purposes of article 10 of the Land Registration Act.
