# Compulsory Registration Area and Voluntary Registration Rules (S.L. 296.13)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 296.13**

**COMPULSORY REGISTRATION AREA AND**

**VOLUNTARY REGISTRATION RULES**

10th May, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 111 of 2024.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Compulsory Registration Area and Voluntary Registration Rules.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 296.*

"Act" means the Land Registration Act;

"compulsory registration area" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

"Land Registrar" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

"registrar" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

"registration area" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in the Act;

"voluntary registration" means the registration of a title to land in accordance with the provisions of article 17 of the Act.

## 3. Compulsory registration area.

Any property situated outside a registration area and has been registered with any title by the Land Registrar, shall be considered to be in a compulsory registration area with effect from 31st March 2022.

## 4. Voluntary registration.

(1) In case a registration affects part of a property which is considered as one (1) unit, that part of the property which does not fall in a registration area shall be considered for voluntary registration by the registrar.

(2) A property which does not fall in a registration area but enjoys common rights which are registered with the property of third parties, shall be considered for voluntary registration by the registrar.
