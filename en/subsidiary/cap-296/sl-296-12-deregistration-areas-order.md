# Deregistration Areas Order (S.L. 296.12)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 296.12**

**DEREGISTRATION AREAS ORDER**

11th February 2022

LEGAL NOTICE 73 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this order is the Deregistration Areas Order. 2. In this order, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Interpretation.*

*Cap. 296.*

"Act" means the Land Registration Act.

## 3. Declaration of deregistration areas.

The areas shown outlined with a non-continuous thick line on the map for Gozo numbered G48 contained in the Schedule no longer form part of a registration area for the purposes of article 10 of the Act.

## 4. Cancellation of registered areas.

As provided in sub-article (1) of article 16B all registrations made in the said area shall be deemed canceled and ineffective except for those registrations that led to a guaranteed title in accordance with articles 21 to 25 of the Act, both articles included.

## 5. Cancellation of cautions, hypothecs, privileges and other rights.

Any caution, hypothec, privilege or other right in connection with a registration canceled in accordance with sub-article (2) of article 16B shall also be deemed to be canceled and ineffective. SCHEDULE
