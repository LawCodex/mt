# Registration Area Order (S.L. 296.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 296.11**

**REGISTRATION AREA ORDER**

1st July 2019 LEGAL NOTICE 142 of 2019.

## 1. Title.

The title of this order is the Registration Area Order.

## 2. Declaration of Registration Area.

The areas shown outlined with a non-continuous thick line on the maps for Gozo numbered G44 to G48 and from Malta numbered 90 to 92 contained in the Schedule hereto are declared to be registration areas for the purposes of article 10 of the Land Registration Act.

*Cap. 296.*
