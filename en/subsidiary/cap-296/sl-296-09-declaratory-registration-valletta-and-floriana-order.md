# Declaratory Registration (Valletta and Floriana) Order (S.L. 296.09)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 296.09**

**DECLARATORY REGISTRATION**

**(VALLETTA AND FLORIANA) ORDER**

1st October, 1997

LEGAL NOTICE 129 of 1997.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Declaratory Registration (Valletta and Floriana) Order.

## 2. Declaration of Declaratory Registration Area.

The Valletta and Floriana areas shown outlined with a non continuous thick line on the map marked X contained in the Schedule hereto attached are designated to be Declaratory Registration Areas for the purposes of article 15 of the Land Registration Act.

*Cap. 296.*

## 3. Limitation. Cap. 296.

This order shall apply only to all the land, which in terms of the Land Registration Act includes building or any other construction or works:

(a) owned by the Government, by entities in which Government has a controlling interest or by parastatal bodies; and/or

(b) situated within the street blocks marked A, B, C and D on the map marked Y contained in the Schedule hereto attached, which land shall be registerable at the Land Registry by the owners or administrators of such land, by not later than the 30th January, 1998.
