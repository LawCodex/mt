# Value Added Tax (Rate of Interest) Regulations (S.L. 406.19)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 406.19**

**VALUE ADDED TAX (RATE OF INTEREST)**

**REGULATIONS**

1st January, 2014\*

LEGAL NOTICE 52 of 2014, as amended by Legal Notices 303 of 2019 and 227 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Value Added Tax (Rate of Interest) Regulations.

## 2. Rate of interest.

*Amended by: L.N. 303 of 2019; L.N. 227 of 2022.*

The interest applicable in terms of article 21(4) of the Value Added Tax Act shall be at the rate of zero point six per cent (0.6%):

*Cap. 406.*

Provided that for balances of tax which became payable by taxable persons before 1st September 2022, interest at the rate of zero point three, three per cent (0.33%) shall continue to apply.

\*see regulation 1(2) of these Regulations as originally promulgated.
