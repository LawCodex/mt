# Value Added Tax (Letting of Premises and Sites for Parking Vehicles) Regulations (S.L. 406.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 406.05**

VALUE ADDED TAX (LETTING OF PREMISES AND SITES FOR PARKING VEHICLES) REGULATIONS

1st January, 1999

LEGAL NOTICE 88 of 1999.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Value Added Tax (Letting of Premises and Sites for Parking Vehicles) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

(1) In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires -

*Cap. 406.*

"the Act" means the Value Added Tax Act;

"premises and sites for parking vehicles" means any premises or sites for parking vehicles where a fee is charged for such parking, but shall not include any public property where a car park attendant licensed as such in terms of the Motor Vehicles Regulations is in attendance.

*S.L. 65.11*

(2) Other terms and expressions shall have the meaning assigned to them in the Act.

## 3. Parking areas.

Premises and sites for parking vehicles shall be treated as parking areas for the purposes of subparagraph (b) of paragraph (1) of item 1 of Part Two of the Fourth Schedule to the Act.
