# Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an Additional €139,762,403.91 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2018 (First Issue) (S.L. 401.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 401.06**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE AND COMMERCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL €139,762,403.91 7.8% MALTA GOVERNMENT

**STOCK 2018 (FIRST ISSUE)**

24th July, 1998

LEGAL NOTICE 177 of 1998, as anended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an additional €139,762,403.91 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2018 (First Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

(1) It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in €23,293,733.99 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2018 (I) an additional issue of €139,762,403.91 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2018 (I) be issued thus bringing the amount of 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2018 (I) created by means of Legal Notice 161 of 1998 to a total amount of €163,056,137.90.

*S.L.401.03*

(2) The half yearly contribution to the sinking fund according to paragraph 6 of Legal Notice 161 of 1998 shall be raised from €232,937.34 to €1,630,561.38.
