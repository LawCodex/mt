# Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of €23,293,733.99 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2018 (First Issue) (S.L. 401.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 401.03**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE AND COMMERCE FOR THE ISSUE OF €23,293,733.99

**7.8% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2018**

**(FIRST ISSUE)**

19th June, 1998

LEGAL NOTICE 161 of 1998, as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of €23,293,733.99 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2018 (First Issue).

## 2. Mode of issuing stock.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The stock shall be issued at the price of €100 percent and shall be allotted by application in multiples of €100. Applications for the allotment of stock shall open on Wednesday, 1st July, 1998 at 8.30 a.m. and shall close at 12.00 noon on Wednesday, 15th July, 1998 or earlier at the discretion of the Accountant General.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and/or entities are eligible to apply. Allotment will be on a first-applied first-accepted basis with preference being given to non-banking applicants.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

An application for the stock to be put on the official list and trading on the Malta Stock Exchange will be made in terms of the Malta Stock Exchange Act, when the stock has been allotted.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the stock shall be €23,293,733.99.

## 6. Rate of contribution to sinking fund.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

On closing date, one part out of every three of the total balance standing to the credit of the sinking funds of the three stocks being redeemed as per Treasury Notices published in the Government Gazette of 12th June, 1998 will be transferred to the Sinking Fund 7.8% Malta Government Stock 2018 (I) established for the purpose of redeeming the stock. A further half-yearly appropriation of not less than €23,293,733.99 out of the general revenue and assets of the Government of Malta shall be made as a contribution to this same sinking fund. The first contribution shall be made during the first six months of the year 1999.

## 7. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €3.90 for every €100 stock shall be payable in arrear half-yearly on the 15th January and the 15th of July of every year during the currency of the stock. The first payment covering the period from the 16th July, 1998 to the 15th January, 1999 shall be made on the 15th day of January, 1999.

## 8. Redemption of stock.

The stock shall be redeemed at par on the 15th July, 2018.
