# Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an Additional Lm15,851,800 6.8% Malta Government Stock 2004 (Second Issue) (S.L. 401.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 401.04**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE AND COMMERCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM15,851,800 6.8% MALTA GOVERNMENT

**STOCK 2004 (SECOND ISSUE)**

24th July, 1998

LEGAL NOTICE 175 of 1998.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance and Commerce for the Issue of an additional Lm15,851,800 6.8% Malta Government Stock 2004 (Second Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

(1) It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm10,000,000 6.8% Malta Government Stock 2004 (II) an additional issue of Lm15,851,800 6.8% Malta Government Stock 2004 (II) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6.8% Malta Government Stock 2004 (II) created by means of Legal Notice 159 of 1998 to a total amount of Lm25,851,800.

*S.L.401.01*

(2) The half yearly contribution to the sinking fund according to paragraph 6 of Legal Notice 159 of 1998 shall be raised from Lm100,000 to Lm258,518.
