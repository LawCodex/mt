# Electronic Trust Services Notification and Fees Regulations (S.L. 426.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 426.03**

**ELECTRONIC TRUST SERVICES**

**NOTIFICATION AND FEES REGULATIONS**

5th July, 2016

LEGAL NOTICE 248 of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Electronic Trust Services Notification and Fees Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation. Cap. 426.

(1) Unless provided otherwise in these regulations, the definitions provided for in the Electronic Commerce Act and in Regulation Number 910/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 2014 on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market and repealing Directive 1999/93/EC, shall apply.

(2) In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 426.*

"Act" means the Electronic Commerce Act;

"Authority" means the Malta Communications Authority; and "Regulation" means Regulation number 910/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 2014 on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market and repealing Directive 1999/93/ EC.

## 3. Notification.

In accordance with the provisions of the Regulation, a person established in Malta who intends to provide qualified trust services shall, before providing such services, submit a notification to the Authority in accordance with the provisions of Article 21 of the Regulation and with any notification form and process that the Authority may from time to time establish.

## 4. Fees payable.

A person who has submitted a notification to the Authority in accordance with regulation 3 shall pay to the Authority the following fees:

(a) upon the submission of the notification an one-off fee of two thousand and eight hundred euro (€2,800) payable to the Authority; and

(b) an annual fee of two thousand euro (€2,000) payable on the anniversary of the notification under paragraph (a).
