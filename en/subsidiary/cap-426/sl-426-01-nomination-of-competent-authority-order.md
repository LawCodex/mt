# Nomination of Competent Authority Order (S.L. 426.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 426.01**

**NOMINATION OF COMPETENT AUTHORITY**

**ORDER**

15th September, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 326 of 2005.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Nomination of Competent Authority Order.

## 2. Competent authority. Cap. 426.

In accordance with article 25(3) of the Electronic Commerce Act, the Malta Communications Authority is hereby designated as the competent authority responsible for monitoring and ensuring compliance with the said Act.
