## 1. Citation and interpretation.

*Substituted by: L.N. 278 of 2024.*

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Diploma in European Studies - Dip. Eur. Studies - under the auspices of the Institute for European Studies.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - “the Board” means the Board of the Institute for European Studies;

“the Course” means the programme of study leading to the Diploma in European Studies - Dip. Eur. Studies;

“the Diploma” means the Diploma in European Studies - Dip. Eur. Studies;

“the Institute” means the Institute for European Studies; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2019.Applicability.

## 2.

These bye-laws shall apply to courses commencing in October 2010 or later.

## 3. Requiremeuts for admission.

*Amended by: L.N. 71 of 2016.*

(1) To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants must satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admission Regulations of the University.

(2) Applicants admitted under regulation 6(c) of the Admission Regulations must satisfy the Board, through an interview, that they have reached the academic standard required to follow the course with profit.

(3) The interviewing board appointed by the Board shall be composed of at least three members.

## 4. Degree in European Studies or degree with substantive European Studies content.

Applicants who are in possession of a degree in European Studies or who are in possession of a degree with a substantive European Studies content shall not be admitted into the course.

## 5. Course duration.

The course shall extend over a period of two semesters of full-time study or the equivalent in part-time study.

## 6. Programme of study.

*Substituted by: L.N. 278 of 2024.*

The Course shall consist of a number of taught study-units to which a total of 60 ECTS credits are assigned.

## 7. Classification of the Award.

The Final Weighted Average Mark for the purpose of the classification of the Diploma shall be based on the results obtained in the Course, all credits being weighted equally.

## 8. Special provisions for part-time courses.

The provisions of the foregoing bye-laws shall apply mutatis mutandis to courses followed on a part-time basis, and subject to such changes as may reasonably be deemed by the Board as necessary due to the part-time nature of the course.
