# Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Centre for Environmental Education and Research (S.L. 327.735)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.735**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY – PH.D. –UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE CENTRE FOR ENVIRONMENTAL EDUCATION AND RESEARCH

11th September, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 219 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Centre for Environmental Education and Research.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires –

"the Board" means the Board of the Centre for Environmental Education and Research;

“the Centre” means the Centre for Environmental Education and Research;

"the Degree" means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D.; and

"the Principal Regulations" means the Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Centre shall be in possession of a Master’s degree in Education for Sustainable Development or in any other area deemed relevant by the Board.

## 4.

Applicants may also be asked to demonstrate competence in those skills required by the nature of the proposed research.

## 5. Centre Doctoral Committee

The Board shall recommend to Senate the appointment of a Centre Doctoral Committee, composed as follows:

**The Chair of the Centre or delegate, as Chair**

The Director of the Centre, or delegate, and

A resident academic.

The Centre Doctoral Committee may co-opt other resident academics from other Faculties, Institutes or Centres, if deemed necessary.

## 6. Taught Study- Units

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during their studies, and/or to satisfactorily undertake tasks recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 7. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 8. Repeal

The Bye-Laws of 2018 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – Degree Regulations, 2008 for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Centre for Environmental Education and Research, published as Legal Notice 424 of 2018, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
