# Fees (International School of English) Regulations (S.L. 327.39)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.39**

**FEES (INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL OF ENGLISH)**

**REGULATIONS**

1st January, 1994

LEGAL NOTICE 87 of 1993, as amended by Legal Notices 424 of 2007 and 371 of 2009.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Fees (International School of English) Regulations.

## 2. Fees chargeable at the International School of English.

*Amended by: L.N. 371 of 2009.*

The fees chargeable to persons who are not citizens of a Member State of the European Union following any course in the International School of English shall be those fees appearing in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

*Substituted by: L.N. 424 of 2007. Amended by: L.N. 371 of 2009.*

(Regulation 2)

Standard Course (three weeks’ duration) €140.00

Short Course (two weeks’ duration) €116.50
