# Bye-Laws for the Diploma in Arabic under the auspices of the Faculty of Arts (S.L. 327.292)

DIPLOMA IN ARABIC UNDER THE AUSPICES

*1*

OF THE FACULTY OF ARTS [S.L.327.292

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.292**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DIPLOMA IN ARABIC**

**UNDER THE AUSPICES OF**

**THE FACULTY OF ARTS**

22nd July, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 263 of 2005.

## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Diploma in Arabic under the auspices of the Faculty of Arts.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the course" means the programme of studies leading to the Diploma in Arabic; and

"the Diploma" means the Diploma in Arabic.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2004 or later.

## 3. Requirements for admission.

To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants must satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admission Regulations of the University.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over a period of two years of part-time study.

## 5. Course programme.

The course shall consist of a number of taught study-units to which 60 credits shall be assigned as follows:

First Year 30 credits

Second Year 30 credits.

## 6. Classification of the Award.

(1) The Final Weighted Average Mark for the purpose of the classification of the Diploma shall be based on the results obtained in each year of the course, all credits being weighted equally.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Diploma shall be published in a list in alphabetical order classified as follows:

Passed with Distinction

Passed.
