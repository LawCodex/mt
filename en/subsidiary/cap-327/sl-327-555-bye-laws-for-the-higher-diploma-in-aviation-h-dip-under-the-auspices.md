# Bye-Laws for the Higher Diploma in Aviation - H.Dip. - under the auspices of the Institute of Aerospace Technologies (S.L. 327.555)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.555**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE HIGHER DIPLOMA IN**

**AVIATION - H. DIP. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF**

**THE INSTITUTE OF AEROSPACE**

**TECHNOLOGIES\***

7th October, 2016

LEGAL NOTICE 329 of 2016, as amended by Legal Notices 322 of 2017.

## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Higher Diploma in Aviation - H.Dip. - under the auspices of the Institute of Aerospace Technologies.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Institute of Aerospace Technologies;

"the Board of Studies" means the Board of Studies appointed by Senate for the course, on the recommendation of the Board;

"the Certificate" means the Certificate in Aviation;

"the course" means the programme of study leading to the Higher Diploma in Aviation - H.Dip.;

"the Diploma" means the Diploma in Aviation; and

"the Higher Diploma" means the Higher Diploma in Aviation - H. Dip.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 322 of 2017.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2017 or later.

## 3. Special requirements for admission.

*Amended by: L.N. 322 of 2017.*

(1) To be registered as regular students in the Course, applicants shall, apart from satisfying the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admissions Regulations, be in possession of a pass at Advanced Matriculation Level at Grade C or better in Physics.

(2) If not already in possession of an EASA/FAA-issued Air Transport Pilot License, applicants must be enrolled on a pilot training course leading to such a qualification, or equivalent, within six months of the start of the course.

(3) If applicants have other qualifications considered to be sufficient, they may be exempted by the University Admissions Board, on the advice of the Institute Admissions Committee, from the whole or part of these special course requirements.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over a period of three semesters of full-time study or the equivalent in part-time study, which may include the summer period.

## 5. Course programme.

(1) The course shall comprise taught study-units to which a total of 90 ECTS credits shall be assigned.

\*These bye-laws have been repealed by Legal Notice 185 of 2022.

(2) Students who successfully complete a prescribed set of study-units totalling 30 ECTS credits and opt not to proceed with the course, or having proceeded do not successfully complete the course, shall be entitled to the Certificate.

(3) Students who successfully complete a prescribed set of study-units totalling 60 ECTS credits and opt not to proceed with the course, or having proceeded do not successfully complete the course, shall be entitled to the Diploma.

(4) Students shall be eligible for the award of the Higher Diploma if they obtain the 90 ECTS credits as outlined in the programme of study for the course.

## 6. Publication of programme of study.

The programme of study shall be published after approval by Senate, normally not less than eight months prior to the commencement of the course.

## 7. Classification of the Awards.

(1) The overall evaluation of each student’s performance in the course for the purpose of the classification of the Diploma and the Higher Diploma shall be based on the results obtained, all credits awarded being weighted equally.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Diploma and Higher Diploma shall be published in a list in alphabetical order classified as follows:

Pass with Distinction

Pass with Merit

Pass.

(3) The Certificate shall be awarded unclassified.

## 8. Special provisions for part-time courses.

The provisions of the foregoing bye-laws shall apply mutatis mutandis to courses followed on a part-time basis, and subject to such changes as may reasonably be deemed by the Board of Studies as necessary due to the part-time nature of the course.
