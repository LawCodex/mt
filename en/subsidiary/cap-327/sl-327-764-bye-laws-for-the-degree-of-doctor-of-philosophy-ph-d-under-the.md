# Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Institute of Anglo-Italian Studies (S.L. 327.764)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.764**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF

**THE INSTITUTE OF ANGLO-ITALIAN STUDIES**

24th March, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 67 of 2026.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Institute of Anglo-Italian Studies.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - “the Board” means the Board of the Institute of Anglo-Italian Studies;

“the Degree” means the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D.;

“the Institute” means the Institute of Anglo-Italian Studies; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 April 2026 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

(1) Applicants for the Degree offered by the Institute shall be in possession of at least a Pass with Merit (or equivalent) classification in a Master’s degree in English Literature, or Italian Literature, or Comparative Literature, or in any area deemed relevant by the Board. In the case of applicants who hold a Master’s degree from this or another university, where it is not possible to determine the equivalence of a Pass with Merit, the Board may determine ad hoc procedures in order to establish such equivalence.

(2) The Board may also consider applications from applicants in possession of a Master’s degree with a Pass classification in the same area of study or in a closely related area, subject to the approval of the Board.

## 4. Taught Study-Units

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during the course of their studies, and/or to satisfactorily undertake tasks recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 5. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the theses for the Degree.
