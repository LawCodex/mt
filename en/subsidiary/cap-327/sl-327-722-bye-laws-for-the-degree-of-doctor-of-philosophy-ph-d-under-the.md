# Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Economics, Management and Accountancy (S.L. 327.722)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.722**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY – PH.D. – UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE FACULTY OF ECONOMICS, MANAGEMENT

**AND ACCOUNTANCY**

16th July, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 159 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation.

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Economics, Management and Accountancy.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires –

“the Board” means the Board of the Faculty of Economics, Management and Accountancy;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D.;

“the Faculty” means the Faculty of Economics, Management and Accountancy; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission.

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Faculty shall be in possession of one of the following degrees:

(a) a Master's degree of this or another university in one of the following areas of study: Accountancy, Banking, Economics, Finance, Insurance, Management, Marketing, Public Policy, Tourism or in a related area of study as approved by the Faculty Doctoral Committee; or

(b) the Master of Business Administration degree of this or another university awarded with at least Merit, provided that the Board may consider applications from applicants in possession of a Pass classification who also possess experience or knowledge which, in the opinion of the Board, enables them to successfully pursue doctoral studies; or

(c) a Bachelor's degree obtained with First Class Honours or Second Class Honours (Upper Division) of this or another university in the areas of Accountancy, Banking, Economics, Finance, Insurance, Management, Marketing, Public Policy, Tourism or in a related area of study as approved by the Faculty Doctoral Committee,

provided that in all cases mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (c) of this bye-law, the Faculty Doctoral Committee:

(i) ascertains that the applicant has a very strong background in the areas of study related to the proposed area of research; and

(ii) submits a clearly motivated recommendation for acceptance to the Board for eventual consideration by the Doctoral Academic Committee and Senate. 4. When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during the course of their studies, and/or to satisfactorily undertake tasks recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

*Taught Study- Units.*

## 5. ThesisPresentation and Format.

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 6. Repeal.

The Bye Laws of 2010 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – Degree Regulations, 2008, for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Faculty of Economics, Management and Accountancy, published as Legal Notice 209 of 2010 shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.

## 7.

The Bye Laws of 2012 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – Degree Regulations, 2008, for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Institute for Tourism, Travel and Culture, published as Legal Notice 400 of 2012 shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
