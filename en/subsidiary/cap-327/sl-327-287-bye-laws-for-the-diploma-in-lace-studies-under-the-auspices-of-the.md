## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these Bye-Laws is the Bye-Laws for the Diploma in Lace Studies under the auspices of the Institute for Islands and Small States.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Institute for Islands and Small States;

"the Board of Studies" means the Board of Studies appointed by Senate for the course; and

"the course" means the programme of studies leading to the Diploma in Lace Studies.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 256 of 2018.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2018 or later.

## 3. Special course requirements for admission.

To be registered as regular students in the Course, applicants shall:

*Amended by: L.N. 71 of 2016; L.N. 256 of 2018.*

(a) satisfy the general requirements for admission as regular students as specified in the Admission Regulations of the University; or

(b) satisfy the provisions of regulation 6 (c) of the Admissions Regulations, 2016, and be in possession of qualifications and experience (as evidenced in a Curriculum Vitae) that would satisfy the Board that they have reached the academic standard required to follow the Course with profit.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over a period of two years of part-time study.

## 5. Course programme.

(1) The programme of studies shall consist of:

(a) a number of taught study-units covering historical, cultural, technical and economic aspects of lace making, to which 48 credits shall be assigned (24 credits in each year); and

(b) a study-unit comprising an individual practical project, to which 12 credits shall be assigned.

(2) Students who obtain the 24 credits assigned to the study-units and the 6 credits assigned to a practical project and opt not to proceed to the second year of the Diploma programme shall be entitled to the Certificate in Lace Studies.
