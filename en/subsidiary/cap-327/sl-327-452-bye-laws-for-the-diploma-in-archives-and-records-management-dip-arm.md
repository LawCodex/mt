## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Diploma in Archives and Records Management - Dip. ARM - under the auspices of the Faculty of Media and Knowledge Sciences.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Faculty of Media and Knowledge Sciences;

"the course" means the programme of study leading to the Diploma in Archives and Records Management - Dip. ARM;

"the Diploma" means the Diploma in Archives and Records Management - Dip. ARM; and

*S.L. 327.236*

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2013 or later.

## 3. Requirements for admission.

To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants shall satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admission Regulations.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over a period of one year of full-time study or two years of part-time study.

## 5. Programme of study.

The programme of study shall consist of study-units to which 60 ECTS credits shall be assigned, including synoptic study-units, a practicum and a long essay.

## 6. Publication of programme of study.

The Board shall publish the programme of study in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Principal Regulations and as approved by Senate, normally not less than eight months before the commencement of each Course. Subject to changes that the Board may make, with the approval of Senate, students shall follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated in the programme of study.
