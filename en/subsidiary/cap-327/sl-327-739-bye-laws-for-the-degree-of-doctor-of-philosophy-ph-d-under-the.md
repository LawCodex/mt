# Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. -under the auspices of the Institute of Linguistics and Language Technology (S.L. 327.739)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.739**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF

**THE INSTITUTE OF LINGUISTICS AND**

**LANGUAGE TECHNOLOGY**

11th September, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 223 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. -under the auspices of the Institute of Linguistics and Language Technology.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

“the Board” means the Board of the Institute of Linguistics and Language Technology;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D.;

“the Institute” means the Institute of Linguistics and Language Technology; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Institute shall be in possession of one of the following degrees:

(a) a Master’s degree in any area of study related to the proposed area of research; or

(b) a Bachelor’s degree obtained with First Class Honours in an area of study deemed by the Board as strongly related to the proposed area of research; or

(c) qualifications which the Board deems to be appropriate and comparable to the degrees mentioned in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) of this bye-law.

## 4. Institute Doctoral Committee

The Board shall recommend to Senate the appointment of an Institute Doctoral Committee composed as follows:

**The Chair of the Institute or delegate as Chair**

The Director of the Institute or delegate

Two resident academics of the Institute.

## 5. Taught Study- Units

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during the first part of their studies, as recommended by the Board, after approval from Senate.

## 6. Transfer Assessment Board

The Transfer Assessment Board shall be composed of the Chair of the Institute or delegate as Chair, and two examiners, one of whom shall be the Principal Supervisor and the other a member of the Institute or of another faculty/institute/centre with expertise in the area.

## 7. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 8. Repeal

The Bye-Laws of 2010 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations 2008 for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Institute of Linguistics, published as Legal Notice 299 of 2010 shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
