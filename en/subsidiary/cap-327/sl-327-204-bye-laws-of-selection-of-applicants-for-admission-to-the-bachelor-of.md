## 1. Citation.

The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws of 2011 Selection of Applicants for Admission to the Bachelor of Science (Honours) (Physiotherapy) Degree Course under the auspices of the Faculty of Health Sciences.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 307 of 2013; L.N. 324 of 2014.*

These bye-laws shall apply to courses commencing in October 2014 or later.

## 3. Selection of applicants. S.L. 327.269

Whenever the number of students that may be admitted to the Physiotherapy area of study is limited by Senate in terms of the provisions of bye-law 10 of the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours), and the number of eligible applicants for this area of study exceeds the number of places available, the selection of applicants shall be made according to the criteria listed in bye-law 4 of these bye-laws.

(3) A grade obtained in Physics taken at Advanced Level, shall, for the purpose of selection, be equated to the following grades at Intermediate level:

Grade D at Advanced Level equated to Grade C at Intermediate Level

Grade C at Advanced Level equated to Grade B at Intermediate Level

Grade B at Advanced Level equated to Grade A at Intermediate Level

Grade A at Advanced Level equated to Grade A at Intermediate Level.

(4) When applicants present more than one pass in Biology or Physics, the best grade shall be taken into consideration.

(5) For the purpose of selection, applicants who fully satisfy the general and the special course requirements by the 31 August preceding the commencement of the course shall be considered first.
