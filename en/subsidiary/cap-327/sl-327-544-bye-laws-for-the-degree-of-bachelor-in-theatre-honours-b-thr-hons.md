# Bye-Laws for the Degree of Bachelor in Theatre (Honours) – B.Thr.(Hons) - under the auspices of the School of Performing Arts (S.L. 327.544)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.544**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF BACHELOR IN**

**THEATRE (HONOURS) – B. THEATRE (HONS) -**

**UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE SCHOOL OF**

**PERFORMING ARTS**

26th May, 2016

LEGAL NOTICE 197 of 2016, as amended by Legal Notice 160 of 2021.

## 1. Citation and interpretation.

*Substituted by: L.N. 160 of 2021.*

(1)These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws of 2021 in terms of the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2019 for the degree of Bachelor in Theatre (Honours) - B.Theatre (Hons) - under the auspices of the School of Performing Arts.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - “the Board” means the Board of the School of Performing Arts;

“the Course” means the programme of study leading to the degree of Bachelor in Theatre (Honours) - B. Theatre (Hons);

“the Degree” means the degree of Bachelor in Theatre (Honours) - B. Theatre (Hons);

“the Diploma” means the Diploma in Theatre; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2019.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 160 of 2021.*

These bye-laws shall be apply from 22nd February, 2021 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for admission.

(1) To be registered as regular students in the Course, applicants must satisfy the general entry requirements for admission as specified in the Admissions Regulations of the University.

(2) Applicants admitted as "Adult Learners" under regulation 6(c) of the Admissions Regulations must be in possession of those qualifications and/or experience that would satisfy the Board that they have reached the academic standard required to follow the Course with profit. Such applicants shall be required to submit with their application a letter written in English giving their motivation for applying for this course, together with a copy of their Curriculum Vitae.

(3) The eligibility of applicants referred to in paragraph (2) of this bye-law shall be decided by the Board following an interview conducted by a board appointed for the purpose.

(4) The interviewing board appointed by the Board shall be composed of at least three members.
