# Statute regarding Immovable Property over which the University of Malta has a Claim or Title at Law (S.L. 327.585)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.585**

**STATUTE REGARDING IMMOVABLE PROPERTY**

OVER WHICH THE UNIVERSITY OF MALTA HAS

**A CLAIM OR TITLE AT LAW**

12th December, 2017 LEGAL NOTICE 365 of 2017,as amendedv by Act XXIX of 2019.

## 1. Interpretation.

For the purposes of this Statute, ‘third party’ shall mean any physical and/or legal person, but shall not include any legal person of which the University of Malta (hereinafter ‘University’) is a shareholder and/or founder.

## 2. Citation.

This Statute may be cited as the ‘Statute regarding Immovable Property over which the University of Malta has a Claim or Title at Law’.

## 3. Field of Application.

This Statute shall be applicable from 1 January 2018 onwards.

## 4. Objective and General Rule.

*Amended by: XXIX.2019.71.*

Immovable property over which the University has a claim or a title at law shall be solely and exclusively used for educational purposes and for support services associated therewith in line with the University’s ission deriving from the Education Act (Chapter 605 of the Laws of Malta).

Furthermore, insofar as any alienation, transfer, sale, exchange, hypothecation, burdening or other encumbering of, and insofar as any assignment of rights, obligations and/or title relating to, such property is concerned, the University and/or any of its Principal Officers shall be precluded, both ipso facto and ipso jure, from alienating, transferring, selling, hypothecating or otherwise burdening and/or encumbering such property and/or from assigning any rights, obligations and/or title hereto to third parties to further such third parties’ private, commercial, financial and/or profit making interests to the detriment of any above-mentioned educational purposes and associated support services in line with University’s overarching mission deriving from the Education Act.

## 5. Exemption.

The Council may resolve to allow an exemption to clause 4. Provided that such decision shall be taken only if all members of Council are present and vote unanimously to such effect.

Provided further that the objective criteria and the reasons upon which Council has based its decision shall be explicitly and expressly minuted within the official documents of Council.

## 6. Amendment.

A unanimous vote shall be required for the purposes of any Amendment to this Statute.

For the avoidance of doubt, Council may not and shall not, at any time whatsoever and in any manner whatsoever, delegate all or any of its powers emanating from this Statute.
