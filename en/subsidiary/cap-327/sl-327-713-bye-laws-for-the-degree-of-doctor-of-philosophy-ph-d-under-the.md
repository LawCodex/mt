# Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Dental Surgery (S.L. 327.713)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.713**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY – PH.D. –UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE FACULTY OF DENTAL SURGERY

6th February, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 25 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Dental Surgery.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires –

“the Board” means the Board of the Faculty of Dental Surgery;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D.;

“the Faculty” means the Faculty of Dental Surgery; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 February 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Faculty shall be in possession of one of the following degrees:

(a) a Master's degree in a related area of Dental Surgery of this or another University or qualifications of comparable standard recognised for this purpose by the Senate; or

(b) a Bachelor’s degree obtained at First Class Honours or Second Class Honours (Upper Division), provided that the Faculty Doctoral Committee ascertains that the applicant has a very strong background in the area of study related to the proposed area of research. This being the case, the Faculty Doctoral Committee shall submit a clearly motivated recommendation for acceptance to the Board for eventual consideration by the Doctoral Academic Committee and Senate.

## 4.

Applicants may also be asked to demonstrate competence in those skills required by the nature of the proposed research.

## 5. Faculty Doctoral Committee

The Board shall recommend to Senate the appointment of a Faculty Doctoral Committee composed as follows:

The Dean of the Faculty or delegate (Chair)

3 senior members of staff, representative of every department within the Faculty

2 additional members of staff, who may be co-opted from the resident academics of the Faculty.

## 6. Taught Study- Units

Where the Senate, on the recommendation of the Board, deems it necessary, it may require applicants to successfully complete a number of study-units under those conditions it may impose.

## 7. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 8. Repeal

The Bye Laws of 2010 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – Degree Regulations, 2008, for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Dental Surgery, published as Legal Notice 211 of 2010, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1st February 2024.
