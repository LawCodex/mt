## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Medical Biochemistry - B.Sc. (Hons) Med. Biochem. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Medicine and Surgery and the Faculty of Science.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board of Studies" means the Board of Studies appointed by Senate for the course, on the recommendations of the Boards of the Faculty of Medicine and Surgery and of the Faculty of Science;

"the course" means the programme of study leading to the degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Medical Biochemistry - B.Sc. (Hons) Med. Biochem.;

"the Degree" means the degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Medical Biochemistry - B.Sc. (Hons) Med. Biochem; and

*S.L. 327.236*

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2017 or later.

## 3. Special course requirements for admission.

To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants shall:

(a) satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admissions Regulations; and

(b) be in possession of two passes at Advanced Matriculation Level at grade C or better in Biology and Chemistry; and

(c) be in possession of a pass at Intermediate Matriculation Level in one of the following subjects:

**Computing or Physics or Pure Mathematics.**

## 4. Number of applicants.

*Renumbered by: L.N. 303 of 2022.*

The Board of Studies shall recommend to Senate the number of applicants that may be allowed to register on the course, depending on the availability of resources. The number of available places shall be announced at the time of the call for applications. Senate shall approve bye-laws for the selection of applicants into the course.

## 5. 2 Course duration.

*Renumbered by: L.N. 303 of 2022.*

The course shall extend over a period of three years of full-time study.

## 6. Programme of study.

*Renumbered by: L.N. 303 of 2022.*

The Board of Studies shall publish the programme of study before the commencement of each course. Subject to changes that the Board of Studies may, with the approval of Senate, be constrained to make, students are bound to follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated in the programme of study for the Degree, provided that the programme shall be drawn up in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Principal Regulations.

## 7. Registration.

*Renumbered by: L.N. 303 of 2022.*

Students shall be required to register and pass in study-units to which a total of 180 ECTS credits are assigned.

## 8. Classification of the Degree.

*Renumbered by: L.N. 303 of 2022.*

The Final Weighted Average Mark for the classification of the Degree shall be based on the results obtained in the three years of the Course, all study-units being weighted equally.

## 10. Publication of Degree classification.

*Renumbered by: L.N. 303 of 2022.*

The Degree classification shall be published according to the provisions of the Principal Regulations.
