# Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Institute of Maltese Studies (S.L. 327.740)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.740**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE INSTITUTE OF MALTESE STUDIES

11th September, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 224 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Institute of Maltese Studies.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

“the Board” means the Board of the Institute of Maltese Studies;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D.;

“the Institute” means the Institute of Maltese Studies; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Institute shall be in possession of a Master’s degree of this or another university, normally in the same or in a related area of study as that applied for.

## 4.

Applicants may also be asked to demonstrate competence in those skills required by the nature of the proposed research.

## 5. Taught Study- Units

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during their studies, and/or to satisfactorily undertake tasks recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 6. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 7. Repeal

The Bye Laws of 2010 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2008 for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Institute of Maltese Studies, published as Legal Notice 302 of 2010, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
