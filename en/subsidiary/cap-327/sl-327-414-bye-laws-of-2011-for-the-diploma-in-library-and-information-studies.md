## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws of 2011 for the Diploma in Library and Information Studies - Dip. LIS - under the auspices of the Faculty of Media and Knowledge Sciences.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Faculty of Media and Knowledge Sciences;

"the course" means the programme of study leading to the Diploma in Library and Information Studies - Dip. LIS;

"the Diploma" means the Diploma in Library and Information Studies - Dip. LIS; and

*S.L. 327.236*

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 44 of 2013.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2012 or later.

## 3. Special course requirements for admission.

*Amended by: L.N. 44 of 2013; L.N. 71 of 2016.*

(1) To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants shall satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admission Regulations.

(2) Applicants applying for admission in terms of regulation 6(c) of the Admission Regulations shall preferably have at least one year’s experience in librarianship and shall be currently working as librarians as evidenced by testimonials from an employer.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over a period of one year of full-time study or two years of part-time study.

## 5. Course programme.

The course shall consist of study-units to which 60 credits are assigned, including synoptic study-units, a practicum and a long essay.

## 6. Publication of programme of study.

The Board shall publish the programme of study in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Principal Regulations and as approved by Senate, before the commencement of each course. Subject to changes that the Board may make, with the approval of Senate, students shall follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated in the programme for the Diploma.
