# Bye-Laws for the Pre-Tertiary Certificate in the Baroque Culture of the Maltese Islands under the auspices of the International Institute for Baroque Studies (S.L. 327.656)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.656**

BYE-LAWS FOR THE PRE-TERTIARY CERTIFICATE IN THE BAROQUE CULTURE OF THE MALTESE

**ISLANDS UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE**

**INTERNATIONAL INSTITUTE FOR BAROQUE**

**STUDIES**

28th August, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 350 of 2020.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Pre-Tertiary Certificate in the Baroque Culture of the Maltese Islands under the auspices of the International Institute for Baroque Studies.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

"the Board" means the Board of the International Institute for Baroque Studies;

"the Board of Studies" means the Board of Studies appointed for the Course;

“the Certificate” means the Pre-Tertiary Certificate in the Baroque Culture of the Maltese Islands; and

"the Course" means the programme of study leading to the Pre- Tertiary Certificate in the Baroque Culture of the Maltese Islands.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2020 or later.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

The Course shall be open to applicants who:

(a) possess technical background and/or experience relevant to the area; and

(b) pass an admission test set by the Board of Studies. 4. The Course shall extend over two semesters of part-time study.

*Course Duration*

## 5. Programme of Study

(1) The programme of study shall consist of study-units to which 30 ECTS credits shall be assigned.

(2) The Board shall publish the programme of study before the commencement of each Course, following approval by Senate. Subject to changes that the Board may, with the approval of Senate, be constrained to make, students are bound to follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated in the programme of study.

## 6. Award of the Certificate

(1) The Certificate shall be awarded to students who obtain all the credits indicated in bye-law 5.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Certificate shall be published in a list in alphabetical order.
