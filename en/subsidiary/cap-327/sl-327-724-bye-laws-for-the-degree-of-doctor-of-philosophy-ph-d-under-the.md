# Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Laws (S.L. 327.724)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.724**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF

**THE FACULTY OF LAWS**

16th July, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 161 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation.

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Laws.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

“the Board” means the Board of the Faculty of Laws;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D.;

“the Faculty” means the Faculty of Laws; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission.

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Faculty must be in possession of one of the following degrees:

(a) a Master’s degree in Law, including a research component of at least 30 ECTS credits or a degree which the Board considers as equivalent awarded by another university recognised for this purpose by the Senate; or

(b) an LL.D. degree; applicants are also required to demonstrate to the satisfaction of the Faculty Doctoral Committee that they are capable of conducting original research of the highest quality; the Board may also require applicants to submit for its perusal copies of their previous work and/or publications.

## 4. Taught Study- Units.

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during the course of their studies recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 5. Faculty Doctoral Committee.

The Board shall recommend to Senate the appointment of a Faculty Doctoral Committee composed as follows:

The Dean of the Faculty or delegate (Chair)

3 – 5 Heads of Department

## 6. ThesisPresentation and Format.

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 7.

Theses shall consist of between 80,000 and 100,000 words, excluding the list of references, footnotes, endnotes, appendices, abstract, and extended summary.

## 8. Repeal.

The Bye-Laws of 2009 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - degree Regulations, 2008 for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Laws, published as Legal Notice 298 of 2008, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
