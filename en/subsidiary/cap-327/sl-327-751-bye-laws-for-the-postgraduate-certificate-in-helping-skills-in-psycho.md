# Bye-Laws for the Postgraduate Certificate in Helping Skills in Psycho-Social Settings - P.G. Cert. - under the auspices of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing (S.L. 327.751)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.751**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE POSTGRADUATE**

**CERTIFICATE IN HELPING SKILLS IN PSYCHO-**

**SOCIAL SETTINGS - P.G. CERT. - UNDER THE**

**AUSPICES OF THE FACULTY FOR SOCIAL**

**WELLBEING**

5th November, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 292 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Postgraduate Certificate in Helping Skills in Psycho-Social Settings - P.G. Cert. - under the auspices of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires –

“the Board” means the Board of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing;

“the Certificate” means the Postgraduate Certificate in Helping Skills in Psycho-Social Settings - P.G. Cert.;

“the Course” means the programme of study leading to the PostgraduateCertificate in Helping Skills in Psycho-Social Settings - P.G. Cert.; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the General Regulations for the University Postgraduate Awards, 2021.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in February 2025 or later.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

The Course shall be open to applicants who are working in a psycho-social setting and are in possession of a Bachelor’s degree from this University, or another higher education institution recognized by Senate, in an area deemed relevant by the Board.

## 4. Course Duration

(1) The Course shall extend over one semester of full-time study or the equivalent in part-time study.

(2) Eligible applicants may register as visiting students for individual study-units and obtain credit for them. Subject to approval by the Board, credits awarded for any such study-units may be transferred to the Course, provided that not more than seven years have elapsed from the successful completion of the study-unit. When credit for more than one study-unit is being transferred to the Course, then the seven years shall commence from the date of the last credit awarded.

## 5. Programme of Study

The programme of study shall consist of a number of study-units to which 30 ECTS credits shall be assigned.

## 6.

The programme of study shall be published by the Board after approval by the Senate, normally not less than eight months before the commencement of the Course.

## 7. Result of the Award

(1) The Certificate shall be awarded to students who obtain all the credits indicated in bye-law 5.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Certificate shall be published in a list in alphabetical order.

## 8. Special Provision for Part-Time Courses

The provisions of the foregoing bye-laws shall apply mutatis mutandis to courses followed on a part-time basis, and subject to such changes as may reasonably be deemed by the Board as necessary due to the part-time nature of the Course.
