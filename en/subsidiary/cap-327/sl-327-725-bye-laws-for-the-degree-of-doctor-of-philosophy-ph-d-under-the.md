# Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Science (S.L. 327.725)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.725**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF

**THE FACULTY OF SCIENCE**

16th July, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 162 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Science.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires –

“the Board” means the Board of the Faculty of Science;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D.;

“the Faculty” means the Faculty of Science; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

(1) Applicants for the Degree offered by the Faculty shall be in possession of one of the following degrees, obtained in the last ten years prior to registration:

(a) a Master’s degree in an area of study related to the proposed area of research; or

(b) a Bachelor degree obtained with at least Second Class Honours (Upper Division) in an area of study related to the proposed area of research; or

(c) qualifications which the Board deems comparable to the degrees indicated in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) of this bye-law.

(2) Applicants in possession of one of the degrees indicated in paragraph (1) of this bye-law obtained more than ten years prior to their application may apply and are required to produce sufficient proof of their competence to carry out research at the required level.

## 4. Taught Study- Units

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during the first part of their studies, and/or to satisfactorily undertake tasks recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 5. Faculty Doctoral Committee

The Board shall recommend to Senate the appointment of a Faculty Doctoral Committee composed as follows:

**The Dean of the Faculty, or delegate (Chair)**

3 - 4 Heads of Department or their delegates, providing that each member shall be qualified up to Ph.D. level.

## 6. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 7. Repeal

The Bye-Laws of 2009 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - degree Regulations, 2008 for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Science, published as Legal Notice 312 of 2009, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
