## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Master of Architecture - M.Arch. - under the auspices of the Faculty for the Built Environment.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires – "the Board" means the Board of the Faculty for the Built Environment;

"the Course" means the programme of study leading to the degree of Master of Architecture - M.Arch.;

"the Degree" means the degree of Master of Architecture - M.Arch.;

"the Postgraduate Diploma" means the Postgraduate Diploma in Architecture - P.G. Dip.;

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Postgraduate Awards, 2008; and

"the Schedule" means the list of thematic study-units and the credit value assigned to each, which applicants are expected to have covered as part of their first cycle degree programme.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 76 of 2016; L.N. 8 of 2017; L.N. 328 of 2018; L.N. 21 of 2021.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2020 or later.

*Substituted by: L.N. 76 of 2016.*

(a) a first cycle degree in Built Environment Studies from this University, with at least at Second Class (Honours); or

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over four semesters of full-time study.

## 5. Programmes of study.

*Amended by: L.N. 21 of 2021.*

(1) The programme of study shall comprise study-units to which a total of 120 ECTS credits are assigned, of which not less than 45 ECTS credits are assigned to the Final Project Workshop and Dissertation study-unit. It shall include study-units grouped by themes (two main themes each assigned 25 ECTS credits and one secondary theme assigned 10 ECTS credits), and 15 ECTS credits assigned to synoptic project-based workshops.

(2) The programme of study shall particularly address the topics of architectural design, history and theory of architecture, fine arts, urban design and planning, environmental performances, the construction process and costs, and structural behaviour.

(3) The programme of study shall be published after approval by Senate on the recommendation of the Board, normally not less than eight months prior to the commencement of the Course.

## 6. Assessment and progress.

*Amended by: L.N. 8 of 2017.*

(1) The assessment of each taught study-unit shall be completed by the end of the semester in which the teaching of the study-unit is held.

(2) (i) Students who in any academic year fail in the assessment of not more than 20 ECTS credits shall be given the opportunity to re-sit the failed assessments during the September supplementary examination session,

provided that if a student fails in two-thirds or more of the assignments associated with the Design Workshops held in the first year of studies, corresponding to work undertaken over a significant portion of the academic year, the Board may decide that a re-assessment during the following September supplementary session is not a viable option for the student, and may require that the student re-submits the failed assignments during an additional year, before being admitted to the second year of studies; and

(ii) Students in the second year of the course shall only be given the opportunity to re-sit the failed assessments of taught study-units assigned not more than 20 ECTS credits during the September supplementary examination session.

(3) Students who fail more than the number of credits permitted under paragraph (2) of this bye-law, or students who after re-assessment fail to obtain credit for any study-unit, shall be required to withdraw from the course.

## 7. Final Project Workshop.

Students shall be required to complete the Final Project Workshop and write a dissertation in accordance with the guidelines issued by the Board.

## 8. Work on the Final Project Workshop.

*Amended by: L.N. 8 of 2017. S.L. 327.328*

(2) The Board of Examiners for the Final Project Workshop shall be composed of at least seven members, comprising the Dean as Chairman and including members of staff with expertise in the disciplines relevant to the theme of the Final Project Workshop, as approved by Senate.

(3) Work on the Final Project Workshop shall be undertaken during the third and fourth semesters of the course. Students shall be assigned a number of supervisors who shall provide guidance and advice, in conformity with the main themes of the Final Project Workshop.

(4) The Final Project Workshop shall comprise a synoptic design project, presented by each student via drawings, written documents, audio-visual material and an oral presentation.

(5) The theme for the Final Project Workshop shall be submitted to the Board for approval by the deadline indicated by the Board. Students may undertake similar themes for the Final Project Workshop, provided that they shall be required to submit for assessment individual project proposals.

## 9. Dissertation.

*Amended by: L.N. 8 of 2017; L.N. 328 of 2018.*

(1) The dissertation shall be of approximately 15,000 words in accordance with the guidelines issued by the Board. Each student shall be assigned a supervisor who shall provide guidance and advice on a regular basis during the period of study.

(2) A provisional title for the dissertation and a detailed research proposal shall be submitted to the Board for approval by the deadline indicated by the Board.

(3) The assessment of the dissertation shall include an oral examination. The process of assessment of the dissertation shall be as defined in the Procedures for Boards of Examiners of Master Dissertations (assigned less than 60 ECTS credits), approved by Senate.

(4) The Board of Examiners shall be composed of at least four members, including the Chairman and the supervisor, as approved by Senate, provided that in cases where the Head of Department is also the supervisor, he shall not chair but another member shall be appointed as chairman; in cases where the Board deems it appropriate, a chairman other than the Head of Department may be appointed.

## 10. Supervisor.

(1) Students shall be required to have regular contact with their supervisors, not less than once a month.

(2) Students shall be required to make an interim presentation to the relative Board of Examiners, mid-way through the programme of both the Dissertation and Final Project Workshop, so that it may be ascertained that each student is making satisfactory progress. ## 11. Classification of the Award.

The Final Weighted Average Mark for the purpose of the classification of the Degree shall be based on the results obtained in all the components of the programme of study, all credits being weighted equally.

## 12. Classification Board.

*Added by: L.N. 8 of 2017. S.L. 327.328*

The Award Classification Board shall be composed of at least seven members of staff, including the Dean, or delegate, as chairman, appointed by the Board. The classification procedures shall otherwise conform to the General Regulations for University Postgraduate Awards.

## 13. Visiting External Examiner.

*Added by: L.N. 8 of 2017.*

A Visiting External Examiner shall be appointed to review the taught component of the course as well as the Final Project Workshop and dissertation, in accordance with the applicable procedures.
