## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-laws for the Postgraduate Certificate in English Language Teaching - P.G.Cert. ELT - under the auspices of the Centre for English Language Proficiency and the Faculty of Education.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board of Studies" means the Board of Studies appointed by Senate for the course, on the recommendations of the Boards of the Centre for English Language Proficiency and the Faculty of Education;

"the course" means the programme of study leading to the Postgraduate Certificate in English Language Teaching - P.G.Cert. ELT;

"the Postgraduate Certificate" means the Postgraduate Certificate in English Language Teaching - P.G.Cert. ELT; and

*S.L. 327.328*

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Postgraduate Awards.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2016 or later.

## 3. Requirements for admission.

To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants must be in possession of a Bachelor’s degree from the University of Malta or from another recognised higher education institution, in an area of study deemed relevant by the Board of Studies, provided that they are also in possession of a Malta Qualifications Framework Level 4 qualification in English or an IELTS grade 7 or equivalent.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over one semester of full-time study.

## 5. Programme of study.

(1) The programme of study shall comprise taught study-units to which a total of 30 ECTS credits are assigned.

(2) The programme of study shall be published by the Board of Studies after approval by Senate normally not less than eight months prior to the commencement of the course.

## 6. Assessment and progress.

(1) Students who fail in the assessment of not more than 10 ECTS credits for taught study-units shall be given the opportunity P.G.Cert. ELT - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE CENTRE FOR ENGLISH [S.L.327.564 LANGUAGE PROFICIENCY AND THE FACULTY OF EDUCATION

to re-sit the failed assessments during the September supplementary examination session.

*2*

(2) Students who fail in more than the number of credits permitted under paragraph (1) of this bye-law, or students who after re-assessment fail to obtain the credits for any study-unit, shall be deemed to have failed the course.

## 7. Result of the Award.

(1) The Certificate shall be awarded to students who obtain all the credits indicated in bye-law 5.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Certificate shall be published in a list in alphabetical order.
