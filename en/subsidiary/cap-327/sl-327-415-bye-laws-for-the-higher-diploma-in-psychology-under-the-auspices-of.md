# Bye-Laws for the Higher Diploma in Psychology under the auspices of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing (S.L. 327.415)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.415**

**BYE-LAWS IN TERMS OF THE GENERAL**

**REGULATIONS FOR UNIVERSITY**

**UNDERGRADUATE AWARDS, 2004 FOR THE**

**HIGHER DIPLOMA IN PSYCHOLOGY - H. DIP. -**

**UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE FACULTY FOR**

**SOCIAL WELLBEING**

9th December, 2011

LEGAL NOTICE 470 of 2011, as amended by Legal Notice 46 of 2013 226 of 2018 and 219 of 2019.

## 1. Citation and interpretation.

*Substituted by: L.N. 226 of 2018.*

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws of 2018 in terms of the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2004 for the Higher Diploma in Psychology - H. Dip. -under the auspices of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - ''the Board'' means the Board of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing;

''the Course'' means the programme of study leading to the Higher Diploma in Psychology - H. Dip.; and

''the Higher Diploma'' means the Higher Diploma in Psychology - H. Dip.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 46 of 2013; L.N. 219 of 2019. Requirements for admission. Substituted by: L.N. 46 of 2013; L.N. 226 of 2018. Amended by: L.N. 219 of 2019.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2020 or later.

3.\* To be registered as regular students in the Course, applicants shall be in possession of one of the following qualifications obtained from this or another University:

(a) a Bachelor degree in Psychology obtained with at least Category II; or

(b) a Bachelor degree with Psychology as a main area of study obtained with at least Category II and a minimum average mark of 60% in the Psychology area of study, provided that at least 70 ECTS credits in Psychology include those study-units indicated in the programme of study as compulsory for entry into the Course.

*Course duration. Substituted by: L.N. 226 of 2018.*

4.† The course shall extend over a period of one year of full-time study or two years of part-time study.

\* Applicable from 1st May, 2018 † Applicable from 1st May, 2018

*Programme of study.*

5.\* The programme of study shall consist of study-units to which 60 credits shall be assigned.

*Classification of the Award.*

6.† (1) The Final Weighted Average Mark for the classification of the Higher Diploma shall be based on the results obtained in the course, all credits being weighted equally.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Higher Diploma shall be published in a list in alphabetical order classified as follows:

Passed with Distinction

Passed with Merit

Passed.
