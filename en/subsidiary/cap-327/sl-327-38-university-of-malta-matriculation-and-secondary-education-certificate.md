## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the University of Malta Matriculation and Secondary Education Certificate Examination Fees Regulations.

## 2. Fees to be charged.

The fees to be charged for the Matriculation Advanced Level Examinations, Practical Examinations, Intermediate Examinations, Secondary Education Certificate Examinations and the registration fees chargeable therefor shall be those set out in the Schedule.
