# Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Theology (S.L. 327.727)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.727**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF

**THE FACULTY OF THEOLOGY**

16th July, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 164 of 2024, as amended by Legal Notice 69 of 2026.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Theology.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires –

“the Board” means the Board of the Faculty of Theology;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D.;

“the Faculty” means the Faculty of Theology; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

*Amended by: L.N. 69 of 2026.*

(1) Applicants for the Degree offered by the Faculty shall be in possession of one of the following degrees:

(a) a Licentiate classified with at least Magna cum Laude or a Master’s degree obtained with at least Pass with Merit, in an area of study offered by the Faculty; or

(b) degrees comparable to those indicated in sub-paragraph (a) of this bye-law awarded by a university recognised by Senate for this purpose.

(2) The Board may also consider applications from applicants in possession of a Master’s degree with a Pass classification in the same area of study or in a related area or with qualifications at Master's level or higher in any discipline if they also possess experience or knowledge which, in the opinion of the Board, will enable them to pursue doctoral studies successfully.

## 4.

Applicants are normally required to have a thorough reading knowledge of languages of primary and secondary sources.

## 5. Areas of Study

The areas of study offered for the Degree shall be in an area of Theology, Religious Studies, Ethics, or other areas related to Theology.

## 6. Taught Study- Units

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during the first part of their studies, and/or to satisfactorily undertake tasks recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 7. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 8. Repeal

The Bye Laws of 2010 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - degree Regulations, 2008 for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Theology, published as Legal Notice 214 of 2010, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
