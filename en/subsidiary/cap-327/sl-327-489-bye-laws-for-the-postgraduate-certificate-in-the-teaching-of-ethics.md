## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Postgraduate Certificate in the Teaching of Ethics in Schools under the auspices of the Faculty of Education.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Faculty of Education;

"the Certificate" means the Postgraduate Certificate in the Teaching of Ethics in Schools;

"the course" means the programme of study leading to the Postgraduate Certificate in the Teaching of Ethics in Schools; and "the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Postgraduate Awards.

*S.L. 327.328*

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 228 of 2018.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in February 2019.

## 3. Requirements for admission.

*Amended by: L.N. 228 of 2018. Substituted by: L.N. 171 of 2023.*

To be registered as regular students in the Course, applicants shall be in possession of one of the following qualifications:

(a) the Master in Teaching and Learning; or

(b) the degree of Bachelor of Education (Honours); or

(c) a first cycle degree together with a Postgraduate Certificate in Education; or

(d) a first cycle and/or second cycle degree or higher and a permanent teacher’s warrant; or

(e) any other recognized teaching qualification and a permanent teacher’s warrant.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over a period of two semesters of part-time study.

## 5. Programme of study.

The Programme of Study shall consist of a number of study-units to which 30 ECTS credits shall be assigned.

## 6. Publication of programme of study.

The Board shall publish the programme of study drawn up in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Principal Regulations and approved by Senate. Subject to changes that the Board may, with the approval of Senate, be constrained to make, OF ETHICS IN SCHOOLS UNDER THE AUSPICES [S.L.327.489 OF THE FACULTY OF EDUCATION

students are bound to follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated in the programme of study.

*2*

## 7. Result of the Award.

(1) The Certificate shall be awarded to students who obtain all the credits indicated in bye-law 5.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Certificate shall be published in a list in alphabetical order.
