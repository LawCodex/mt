# Bye-Laws for the Diploma in Gozo Studies under the auspices of the Institute of Islands and Small States (S.L. 327.443)

*1*

THE INSTITUTE OF ISLANDS AND SMALL STATES [S.L.327.443

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.443**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DIPLOMA IN GOZO**

**STUDIES UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE**

INSTITUTE OF ISLANDS AND SMALL STATES 29th January, 2013

LEGAL NOTICE 43 of 2013, as amended by Legal Notice 71 of 2016.

## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Diploma in Gozo Studies under the auspices of the Institute of Islands and Small States.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Institute for Islands and Small States;

"the Board of Studies" means the Board of Studies appointed by Senate for the Course, on the recommendation of the Board;

"the course" means the programme of study leading to the Diploma in Gozo Studies; and

"the Diploma" means the Diploma in Gozo Studies.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2012 or later.

## 3. Requirements for admission.

*Amended by: L.N. 71 of 2016.*

(1) To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants shall satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admission Regulations of the University.

(2) Applicants admitted under regulation 6(c) of the Admission Regulations must be in possession of those qualifications and/or experience that would satisfy the Board of Studies that they have reached the academic standard required to follow the course with profit. The eligibility of such applicants shall be decided by the Board of Studies, following an interview conducted by an interviewing board appointed for the purpose.

(3) The interviewing board appointed by the Board shall be composed of at least three persons.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over a period of four semesters of part-time study.

## 5. Course programme.

The course shall consist of a number of taught study-units to which 60 ECTS credits shall be assigned.

## 6. Publication of programme of study.

The programme of study shall be published after approval by Senate, normally not less than eight months prior to the commencement of the course.

## 7. Classification of the Award.

(1) The Final Weighted Average Mark of each student’s performance in the Course for the purpose of the classification of the Diploma shall be based on the results obtained, all credits earned being weighted equally.
