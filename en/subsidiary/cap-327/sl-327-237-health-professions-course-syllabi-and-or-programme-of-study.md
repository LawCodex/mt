# Health Professions Course Syllabi and/or Programme of Study Regulations (S.L. 327.237)

HEALTH PROFESSIONS COURSE SYLLABI

*1*

AND/OR PROGRAMME OF STUDY [S.L.327.237

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.237**

HEALTH PROFESSIONS COURSE SYLLABI AND/

**OR PROGRAMME OF STUDY REGULATIONS**

17th June, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 215 of 2005, as amended by Legal Notice 247 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Health Professions Course Syllabi and/or Programme of Study Regulations.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 247 of 2021.*

These regulations shall be applicable to courses commencing from 15th May, 2021 onwards.

## 3. Course syllabi and/ or programmes of study.

The syllabi and/or programmes of study related to courses leading to an award that gives access to the professions of:

*Substituted by: L.N. 247 of 2021.*

(a) Medical Doctor

(b) Dental Surgeon

(c) Pharmacist

(d) Nurse; and

(e) Midwife

shall conform with the minimum training requirements established by the relative European Union Directive 2005/36/EC and with any other Maltese legislation governing the granting of permission to practise the relevant profession in Malta.
