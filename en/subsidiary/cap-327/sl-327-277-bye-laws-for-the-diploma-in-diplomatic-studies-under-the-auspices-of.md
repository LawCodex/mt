**STUDIES**

14th June, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 207 of 2005, as amended by Legal Notice 111 of 2008.

## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Diploma in Diplomatic Studies under the auspices of the Mediterranean Academy of Diplomatic Studies.

(2) In these bye- laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Mediterranean Academy of Diplomatic Studies;

"the course" means the programme of studies leading to the Diploma in Diplomatic Studies; and

"the Diploma" means the Diploma in Diplomatic Studies.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 111 of 2008.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses starting in October 2008 or later.

## 3. Requirements for admission.

To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants must satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admission Regulations of the University.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over two years of part-time study. 5. The course shall consist of taught study-units to which a total of 60 credits are assigned.

*Course programme. Substituted by: L.N. 111 of 2008.*

## 6. Progress.

*Substituted by: L.N. 111 of 2008.*

Students who obtain the 30 credits assigned to Year 1 and opt not to continue to Year 2 of the course or, who fail to obtain the Diploma, shall be eligible for the award of the Certificate in Diplomatic Studies.

## 7. Classification of the Award.

The names of students who qualify for the award of the Diploma shall be published in a list in alphabetical order classified as follows:
