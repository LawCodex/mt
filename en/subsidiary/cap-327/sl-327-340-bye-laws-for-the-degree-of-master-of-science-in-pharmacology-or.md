## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Master of Science in Pharmacology or Clinical Pharmacology under the auspices of the Faculty of Medicine and Surgery.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Faculty of Medicine and Surgery;

"the course" means the programme of study leading to the degree of Master of Science in Pharmacology or Clinical Pharmacology;

"the Degree" means the degree of Master of Science in Pharmacology or Clinical Pharmacology; and

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Postgraduate Awards.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 487 of 2012; L.N. 123 of 2014.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 24th January 2014 onwards.

## 3. Areas of study.

(1) The course shall be offered mainly by research in any area of Pharmacology or Clinical Pharmacology.

(2) Subject to the availability of resources, the Board may recommend to Senate the number of students to be admitted to the Course and the areas of study in which it can offer expert guidance and supervision. The areas offered and the maximum number of students to be admitted shall be announced at the time of the call for applications for admission to the course.

(3) If there are more eligible applicants than the number of places available, applicants shall be selected after an interview by a board appointed for the purpose. The board shall select applicants using the following criteria:

(a) the class of the degree;

(b) experience in the area of the proposed study;

(c) the research proposal; and

(d) their performance during an interview.

## 4. Requirements for admission.

*Amended by: L.N. 487 of 2012; L.N. 123 of 2014.*

(1) The course shall be open to applicants in possession of:

(a) the degree of Doctor of Medicine and Surgery or the degree of Bachelor of Dental Surgery; or

(b) the degree of Master of Dental Surgery awarded from July 2018 onwards; or

(c) a Bachelor degree in Pharmacy or in a Health or Life Science related area of study as deemed suitable by the Board, with at least Second Class Honours; ore

(d) the degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) (Pharmaceutical science) with a classification of second Class Honours or better.

(2) The admission of applicants under paragraph (c) of this bye-law with Third Class Honours, who have obtained other qualifications, including relevant experience following the first cycle degree, may be made conditional on the results of an assessment as the Board may deem appropriate.

## 5. Course duration.

The course shall extend over three semesters of full-time study or the equivalent in part-time study.

## 6. Programme of study.

The programme of study shall comprise study-units to which a total of 90 credits are assigned, of which 30 credits are assigned to taught study-units and 60 credits are assigned to a dissertation study-unit.

## 7. Publication of programme of study.

The programme of study shall be published after approval by Senate, normally not less than eight months prior to the commencement of the course.

## 8. Assessment and progress.

(1) The assessment of each taught study-unit shall be completed by the end of the semester in which the teaching of the study-unit is held.

(2) Students who fail in the assessment of not more than 10 credits for taught study-units shall be given the opportunity to re-sit the failed assessments during the September supplementary examination session.

(3) Students who fail in more than 10 credits or students who after reassessment fail to obtain the credits for any study-unit, shall be deemed to have failed the course.

## 9. Dissertation.

(1) Students shall be required to submit an individual dissertation of approximately 40,000 words in accordance with the guidelines issued by the Board.

(2) Work on the dissertation may be undertaken throughout the course after approval of the proposal. Each student shall be assigned a supervisor who shall provide guidance and advice on a regular basis during the period of study.

## 11. Classification of the Award.

The Final Weighted Average Mark for the purpose of the classification of the Degree shall be based on the results obtained in all components of the programme of study, all credits being weighted equally.

## 12. Special provisions for part-time courses.

The provisions of the foregoing bye-laws shall apply mutatis mutandis to courses followed on a part-time basis, and subject to such changes as may reasonably be deemed by the Board as necessary due to the part-time nature of the course.
