# Bye-Laws for the Certificate of Finance in International Trade - COFIT - under the auspices of the Faculty of Economics, Management and Accountancy (S.L. 327.462)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.462**

BYE-LAWS FOR THE CERTIFICATE OF FINANCE

**IN INTERNATIONAL TRADE - COFIT - UNDER**

**THE AUSPICES OF THE FACULTY OF**

**ECONOMICS, MANAGEMENT AND**

**ACCOUNTANCY**

1st October, 2013

LEGAL NOTICE 290 of 2013, as amended by Legal Notices 41 of 2015 and 71 of 2016.

## 1. Citation and interpretation.

*Substituted by: L.N. 41 of 2015.*

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Certificate of Finance in International Trade - COFIT - under the auspices of the Faculty of Economics, Management and Accountancy.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Faculty of Economics, Management and Accountancy;

"the Certificate" means the Certificate of Finance in International Trade - COFIT;

"the course" means the programme of study leading to the Certificate of Finance in International Trade - COFIT; and

*S.L. 327.236*

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 41 of 2015.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in February 2015 or later.

## 3. Requirements for admission.

*Amended by: L.N. 71 of 2016.*

(1) To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants must satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admission Regulations of the University.

(2) Applicants admitted under regulation 6(c) of the Admission Regulations must be in possession of those qualifications and/or experience that would satisfy the Board that they have reached the academic standard required to follow the Course with profit. Such applicants shall be required to submit with their application a letter written in English giving their motivation for applying for this course together with a copy of their curriculum vitae (C.V.).

(3) Applicants who completed their secondary or higher education overseas must be in possession of a certificate of proficiency in English at the level required by the University.

(4) Admission to the course may, with the approval of Senate, be restricted to applicants who are nominated by FIMBank and the International Factors Group provided that such applicants are eligible for admission in terms of paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) of this bye-law.

## 4. Course duration.

*Substituted by: L.N. 41 of 2015.*

The course shall extend over a period of one semester of study.

## 5. Course programme.

The course shall consist of a number of study-units to which 30 ECTScredits shall be assigned.

## 6. Publication of programme of study.

The Board shall publish the programme of study drawn up in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Principal Regulations and approved by Senate, normally not less than eight months prior to the commencement of the course. Subject to changes that the Board may, with the approval of Senate, be constrained to make, students are bound to follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated in the programme of study.

## 7. Result of the Award.

(1) The Certificate shall be awarded to students who obtain all the credits indicated in bye-law 5.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Certificate shall be published in a list in alphabetical order.
