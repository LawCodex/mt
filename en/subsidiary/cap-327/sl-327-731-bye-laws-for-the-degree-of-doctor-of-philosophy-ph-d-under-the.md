# Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Centre for Biomedical Cybernetics (S.L. 327.731)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.731**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY – PH.D. – UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE CENTRE FOR BIOMEDICAL CYBERNETICS

11th September, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 215 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Centre for Biomedical Cybernetics.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires –

"the Board" means the Board of the Centre for Biomedical Cybernetics;

“the Centre” means the Centre for Biomedical Cybernetics;

"the Degree" means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D.; and

"the Principal Regulations" means the Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Centre shall be open to applicants in possession of one of the following qualifications:

(a) a Master’s degree in an area of study relevant to that applied for; or

(b) the Master of Dental Surgery degree; or

(c) the Doctor of Medicine and Surgery degree; or

(d) a Bachelor’s degree in an area of study relevant to that applied for, obtained at First Class Honours or Second Class Honours (Upper Division); or

(e) a qualification deemed by Senate, on the recommendation of the Board, to be equivalent or higher to the qualifications listed in paragraphs (a) to (d) of this bye-law.

## 4.

Applicants may also be asked to demonstrate competence in those skills required by the nature of the proposed research.

## 5. Centre Doctoral Committee

The Board shall recommend to Senate the appointment of a Centre Doctoral Committee, composed as follows:

The Director of the Centre, or delegate, as Chair and

**3 members who shall be resident academics.**

The Centre Doctoral Committee may co-opt other members from other Faculties/Institutes/Centres, if deemed necessary.

## 6. Taught Study- Units

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during their studies, and/or to satisfactorily undertake tasks recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 7. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 8. Repeal

The Bye-Laws of 2015 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – Degree Regulations, 2008 for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Centre for Biomedical Cybernetics, published as Legal Notice 324 of 2015, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
