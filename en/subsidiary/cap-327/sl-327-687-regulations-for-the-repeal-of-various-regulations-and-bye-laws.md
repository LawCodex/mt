# Regulations for the Repeal of Various Regulations and Bye-Laws (S.L. 327.687)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.687**

**REGULATIONS FOR THE REPEAL OF VARIOUS**

**REGULATIONS AND BYE-LAWS**

1st July 2022 LEGAL NOTICE 185 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

These regulations may be cited as the Regulations for the Repeal of Various Regulations and Bye-Laws.

## 2. Applicability.

These regulations shall be applicable as from 1st July, 2022.

## 3. Repeal.

The following regulations and bye-laws are hereby being repealed:
