# Bye-Laws for the Certificate in Proof-Reading:Maltese under the auspices of the Faculty of Arts (S.L. 327.308)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.308**

BYE-LAWS FOR THE CERTIFICATE IN PROOF- READING: MALTESE - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE FACULTY OF ARTS

30th May, 2006

LEGAL NOTICE 120 of 2006.

## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Certificate in Proof-Reading: Maltese - under the auspices of the Faculty of Arts.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Faculty of Arts;

"the Certificate" means the Certificate in Proof-Reading:

Maltese;

"the course" means the programme of studies leading to the Certificate in Proof-Reading: Maltese; and

*S.L. 327.236*

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall be applicable to courses commencing in February 2006 or later.

## 3. Special course requirements for admission.

(a) To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants must satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admission Regulations of the University.

(b) Applicants must also be in possession of a pass at Grade C or better in Maltese at Advanced Matriculation level or equivalent.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over a period of one year of part-time study.

## 5. Course programme.

The course shall consist of a number of study-units to which 30 credits shall be assigned as follows: 24 credits for taught study-units and 6 credits for a project.

## 6. Publication of programme of study.

The Board shall publish the programme of study drawn up in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Principal Regulations and approved by Senate, at the commencement of each course. Subject to changes that the Board may, with the approval of Senate, be constrained to make, students are bound to follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated in the programme of study.

## 7. Result of the Award.

(1) The Certificate shall be awarded to students who obtain all the credits indicated in bye-law 5.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Certificate shall be published in a list in alphabetical order.
