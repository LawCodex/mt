# Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Institute of Earth Systems (S.L. 327.733)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.733**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. UNDER THE AUSPICES OF

**THE INSTITUTE OF EARTH SYSTEMS**

11th September, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 217 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Institute of Earth Systems.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

“the Board” means the Board of the Institute of Earth Systems;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D.;

“the Institute” means the Institute of Earth Systems; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

(1) Applicants for the Degree offered by the Institute shall be in possession of one of the following degrees:

(a) a Master’s degree in Earth, Rural, Life and/or Natural Sciences or Geography, or Humanities, or Environmental Planning and/or Environmental Management; and/or

(b) a Bachelor’s degree obtained with First Class Honours in the same areas of study mentioned in sub-paragraph (a) of this bye-law.

(2) Under exceptional circumstances, the Board may favourably consider an applicant with a Second Class Honours (Upper Division) classification in the event that such applicant has authored relevant research papers in reputable journals.

## 4.

Applicants may also be asked to demonstrate competence in those skills required by the nature of the proposed research.

## 5. Institute Doctoral Committee

The Board shall recommend to Senate the appointment of an Institute Doctoral Committee composed as follows:

**The Director of the Institute or delegate as Chair**

The Coordinator of the Division not represented by the Director

Another two members selected from among the members of the Board of the Institute or from the Institute's resident academics.

## 6. Taught Study- Units

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during the course of their studies, and/or to satisfactorily undertake tasks recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 7. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 8. Repeal

The Bye-Laws of 2010 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2008 for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Institute of Earth Systems, published as Legal Notice 298 of 2010, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
