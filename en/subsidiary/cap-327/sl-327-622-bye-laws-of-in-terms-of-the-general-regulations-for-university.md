# Bye-Laws of in terms of the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards,for the Certificate in the Practice of Cooperative Societies under the auspices of the Centre for Labour Studies (S.L. 327.622)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.622**

**BYE-LAWS IN TERMS OF THEGENERAL**

**REGULATIONS FOR UNIVERSITY**

**UNDERGRADUATE AWARDS FOR THE**

CERTIFICATE IN THE PRACTICE OF COOPERATIVE SOCIETIES UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE CENTRE FOR LABOUR STUDIES\*

2nd April, 2019

LEGAL NOTICE 52 of 2019.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws of in terms of the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards,for the Certificate in the Practice of Cooperative Societies under the auspices of the Centre for Labour Studies.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

“the Board” means the Board of the Centre for Labour Studies;

“the Certificate” means the Certificate in the Practice of Cooperative Societies;

“the Course” means the programme of study leading to the Certificate in the Practice of Cooperative Societies; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2004.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2019 or later.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

(1) To be registered as regular students in the Course, applicants shall satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admissions Regulations of the University.

(2) Applicants admitted as “Adult Learners” under paragraph (c) of regulation 6 of the Admissions Regulations must be in possession of those qualifications and experience that would satisfy the interviewing board, through an interview, that they have reached the academic standard required to follow the Course with profit. Such applicants shall be required to submit with their application a letter written in English giving their motivation for joining the Course, together with a copy of their Curriculum Vitae.

(3) Applicants admitted in terms of paragraph (2) of this bye-law shall be in possession of a pass in an English language proficiency test as approved by the University before being admitted to the Course, provided that applicants in possession of a Secondary Education Certificate pass at Grade 3 or higher in English Language or a pass at the same standard in a comparable qualification, shall be exempt from this requirement.

\*These bye-laws have been repealed by Legal Notice 185 of 2022.

(4) The interviewing board appointed by the Board shall be composed of at least three members.

## 4. Course Duration

The Course shall extend over a period of two semesters of part-time study.

## 5. Programme of Study

The programme of study shall comprise study-units to which a total of 30 ECTS credits are assigned.

## 6.

Subject to changes that the Board may, with the approval of Senate, be constrained to make, students are bound to follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated in the programme of study for the Course.

## 7.

The programme of study shall be published after approval by Senate, normally not less than eight months prior to the commencement of the Course.

## 8. Result of the Award

(1) The Certificate shall be awarded to students who obtain all the credits indicated in bye-law 5.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Certificate shall be published in a list in alphabetical order.
