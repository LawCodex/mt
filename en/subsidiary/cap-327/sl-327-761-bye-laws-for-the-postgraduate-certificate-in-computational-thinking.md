# Bye-Laws for the Postgraduate Certificate in Computational Thinking and Coding for Primary Educators - P.G. Cert. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Education (S.L. 327.761)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.761**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE POSTGRADUATE**

**CERTIFICATE IN COMPUTATIONAL THINKING**

**AND CODING FOR PRIMARY EDUCATORS - P.G.**

CERT. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE FACULTY

**OF EDUCATION**

21st January, 2026

LEGAL NOTICE 18 of 2026.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Postgraduate Certificate in Computational Thinking and Coding for Primary Educators - P.G. Cert. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Education.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

“the Board” means the Board of the Faculty of Education;

“the Certificate” means the Postgraduate Certificate in Computational Thinking and Coding for Primary Educators - P.G. Cert.;

“the Course” means the programme of study leading to the Postgraduate Certificate in Computational Thinking and Coding for Primary Educators - P.G. Cert.; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the General Regulations for University Postgraduate Awards, 2021.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2025 or later.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

Applicants may be registered as students on the Course if they are in possession of:

(a) the degree of Bachelor of Education (Honours); or

(b) a first cycle degree together with a Postgraduate Certificate in Education; or

(c) a first cycle and/or second cycle degree or higher and a permanent teacher’s warrant; or

(d) any other recognised teaching qualification and a permanent teacher’s warrant; or

(e) any other recognised qualification in education that allows one to be employed in schools.

## 4. Course Duration

The Course shall extend over one semester of full-time study or two semesters of part-time study.

## 5. Programme of Study

The programme of study shall consist of a number of study-units to which 30 ECTS credits shall be assigned.

## 6.

The programme of study shall be published after approval by Senate, normally not less than eight months prior to the commencement of the Course.

## 7. Result of the Award

(1) The Certificate shall be awarded to students who obtain all the credits indicated in bye-law 5 with a Final Average Weighted Mark of at least 50%.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Certificate shall be published in a list in alphabetical order.
