## 1. Citation and interpretation.

*Substituted by: L.N. 245 of 2021.*

(1)These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Earth Systems - B.Sc. (Hons) - under the auspices of the Institute of Earth Systems.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Institute of Earth Systems;

"the Course" means the programme of study leading to the degree ofBachelor of Science (Honours) in Earth Systems - B.Sc. (Hons);

"the Degree" means the degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in EarthSystems - B.Sc. (Hons);

"the Diploma" means the Diploma in Earth Systems;

"the Higher Diploma" means the Higher Diploma in Earth Systems; and

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2019.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 245 of 2021.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable as from 15 May, 2021 onwards except for bye-law 7 which shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2021 or later.

## 3. Special course requirements for admission.

To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants shall, apart from satisfying the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admission Regulations, be in possession of an Intermediate Level pass at grade C or better in at least one of the following subjects: Environmental Science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Pure Mathematics, Applied Mathematics (Mechanics), Geography and Sociology.

## 4. Course duration.

*Substituted by: L.N. 275 of 2024.*

The Course shall extend over a period of three years of full-time study or the equivalent in part-time study.

## 5. Programme of study.

The Board shall publish the programme of study before the commencement of each course. Subject to changes that the Board may, with the approval of Senate, be constrained to make, students are bound to follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated in the programme of study for the Degree, provided that the programme of study shall be drawn up in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Principal Regulations.

## 7. Diploma.

Students who successfully complete study-units to which not less than 60 credits are assigned and opt not to proceed with the course or, having proceeded, do not successfully complete the course, shall be entitled to the Diploma.

## 8.

*Added by: L.N. 245 of 2021.*

Students who successfully complete study-units to which not less than 120 credits are assigned and opt not to proceed with the Course or, having proceeded, do not successfully complete the Course, shall be entitled to the Higher Diploma.

## 9. Classification of the Awards.

*Renumbered/ Substituted by: L.N. 245 of 2021.*

(1) For the purpose of the classification of the Diploma and the Higher Diploma, the overall evaluation of each student's performance in the Course shall be based on the results obtained in all the study-units of each programme as appropriate, with all credits awarded being weighted equally.

(2) For the purpose of the classification of the Degree, the overall evaluation of the performance of every student in the Course shall be based on the results obtained in all the study-units of the three years of the course, which shall be weighted as follows:

First Year Average Mark 30%

Second Year Average Mark 35%

Third Year Average Mark 35%.

## 10. Provisions for part-time courses

*Added by: L.N. 275 of 2024.*

The provisions of the foregoing bye-laws shall apply mutatis mutandis to courses followed on a part-time basis, and subject to such changes as may reasonably be deemed by the Board as necessary due to the part-time nature of the Course.

## 11.

The Course may be offered on a flexible part-time basis with the following special provisions in terms of the provisions outlined in the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2019 applicable with the necessary changes needed (mutatis mutandis) owing to the part-time nature of the Course:

(a) During each academic year, students shall be allowed to register for a number of study-units as agreed with the Course Coordinator, to which not less than 12 ECTS credits are assigned, provided that registration for the study-units included in the programme of study for each Course year (60 ECTS credits) is spread over not more than three academic years;

(b) In order to progress regularly from one Course year to the next, the 60 ECTS credits of each Course year must be completed in not more than three academic years;

(c) Students who are absent for the assessment of a study-unit, or fail in the assessment of a study-unit, shall, if eligible, be required to take the assessment of any number of such incomplete study-units at the September assessment session or at the next available opportunity;
