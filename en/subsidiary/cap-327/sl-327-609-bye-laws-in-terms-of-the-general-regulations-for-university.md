# Bye-Laws in terms of the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2004 for the Certificate for Graduates of Non-EU/EEA Nursing Programmes, under the auspices of the Faculty of Health Sciences (S.L. 327.609)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.609**

**BYE-LAWS IN TERMS OF THE GENERAL**

**REGULATIONS FOR UNIVERSITY**

**UNDERGRADUATE AWARDS, 2004 FOR THE**

CERTIFICATE FOR GRADUATES OF NON-EU/EEA NURSING PROGRAMMES UNDER THE AUSPICES

**OF THE FACULTY OF HEALTH SCIENCES**

9th November, 2018

LEGAL NOTICE 369 of 2018, as amended by Legal Notice 308 of 2019, 361 of 2022 and 169 of 2026.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws in terms of the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2004 for the Certificate for Graduates of Non-EU/EEA Nursing Programmes, under the auspices of the Faculty of Health Sciences.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

"the Board" means the Board of the Faculty of Health Sciences;

"the Board of Studies" means the Board of Studies appointed for the Course;

"the Course" means the programme of study leading to the Certificate for Graduates of Non-EU/EEA Nursing Programmes;

"the Certificate" means the Certificate for Graduates of Non-EU/ EEA Nursing Programmes; and

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2004.

## 2. Applicability

*Amended by: L.N. 308 of 2019.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2019 or later.

## 3. Requirements for Admission 

*Substituted by: L.N. 361 of 2022. Amended by: L.N. 169 of 2026.*

(1) The Course shall be open to applicants in possession of:

(a) a qualification in Nursing deemed suitable by the Board of Studies and recognized at least at Level 5 on the Malta and European Qualifications Frameworks; or

(b) a professional qualification deemed by Senate to be comparable to the qualification indicated in sub-paragraph (a) of this bye-law.

(c) Repealed by Legal Notice 169 of 2026.

(2) In addition to the qualifications listed in paragraph (1) of this bye-law, applicants shall be required to provide evidence that they are in possession of a valid licence to practice as a nurse awarded by the relevant regulatory council of the country from where the qualification referred to in paragraph (1) was awarded.

## 4.

Senate, on the advice of the Board, may limit the number of students that may be admitted to the Course depending on the availability of resources. The Board shall announce the number of available places at the time of the call for applications.

## 5.

Qualified applicants shall be accepted on a first-come first-served basis (in order of receipt of application and full documentation) up to any maximum number of students as approved by Senate.

## 6.

*Substituted by: L.N. 308 of 2019.*

Once accepted to the Course, students are also required to undergo an occupational health assessment so as to ascertain that they are fit for the duties related to their proposed studies. Students who do not fulfil this requirement may be barred from progressing with their studies. Failure to disclose information about a physical or mental health problem that could affect patient safety is considered to be a serious breach of the University Suitability to Practise Regulations.

## 7. Course Duration

The Course shall extend over a period of one semester of full-time study or the equivalent in part-time studies.

## 8. Programme of Study 

*Substituted by: L.N. 361 of 2022; L.N. 169 of 2026.*

The Course shall consist of a number of study-units to which 30 ECTS credits shall be assigned.

## 9.

Students are bound to follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated by the Board of Studies, which shall be tasked to ensure that gaps in the training received by the students in the Non- EU/EEA nursing programmes are addressed and that students received adequate training to ensure compliance with the EU directive for Nurse Education (Directive 2005/36/EC) and thus, are eligible for registration with the relevant regulatory Council/s.

## 10. Result of the Award

(1) The Certificate shall be awarded to students who obtain all the credits indicated in bye-law 8.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Certificate shall be published in a list in alphabetical order.
