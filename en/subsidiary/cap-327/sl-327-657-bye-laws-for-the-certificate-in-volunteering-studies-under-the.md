# Bye-Laws for the Certificate in Volunteering Studies under the auspices of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing (S.L. 327.657)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.657**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE CERTIFICATE IN**

VOLUNTEERING STUDIES UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE FACULTY FOR SOCIAL WELLBEING 28th August, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 356 of 2020, as amended by Legal Notice 240 of 2025.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Certificate in Volunteering Studies under the auspices of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

“the Board” means the Board of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing;

“the Board of Studies” means the Board of Studies appointed by Senate for the Course, on the recommendation of the Board;

“the Course” means the programme of study leading to the Certificate in Volunteering Studies; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2019.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable for courses commencing in October 2020 or later.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

*Amended by: L.N. 240 of 2025.*

(1) To be registered as regular students in the Course, applicants shall satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admissions Regulations.

(2) Applicants admitted as “Adult Learners” under paragraph (c) of regulation 6 of the Admissions Regulations shall be called for an interview conducted by an interviewing board composed of at least three persons appointed for the purpose. Applicants are required to pass the interview.

## 4. Course Duration

The Course shall extend over a period of one year of part-time study.

## 5. Course Programme

*Substituted by: L.N. 240 of 2025.*

The Course shall consist of a number of taught study-units to which at least 30 ECTS credits shall be assigned.

## 6.

The programme of study shall be published after approval by Senate, normally not less than eight months prior to the commencement of the Course.
