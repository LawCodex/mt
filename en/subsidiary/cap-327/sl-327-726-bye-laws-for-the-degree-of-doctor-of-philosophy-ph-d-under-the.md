# Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy-– Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing (S.L. 327.726)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.726**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF

**THE FACULTY FOR SOCIAL WELLBEING**

16th July, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 163 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy-– Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires –

“the Board” means the Board of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D.;

“the Faculty” means the Faculty for Social Wellbeing; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Faculty shall be in possession of one of the following degrees:

(a) a Master’s degree normally in the same area of studies applied for; or

(b) a Bachelor’s degree obtained with First Class Honours or Second Class Honours (Upper Division) with a very strong background in the area of study related to the proposed area of research. 4. When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during the course of their studies, and/or to satisfactorily undertake tasks recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

*Taught Study- Units*

## 5. Faculty Doctoral Committee

The Board shall recommend to Senate the appointment of a Faculty Doctoral Committee composed as follows:

The Dean of the Faculty or delegate (Chair)

3 – 4 Heads of Department

## 6. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 7. Repeal

The Bye-Laws of 2013 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2008 for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty for Social Wellbeing, published as Legal Notice 383 of 2013, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
