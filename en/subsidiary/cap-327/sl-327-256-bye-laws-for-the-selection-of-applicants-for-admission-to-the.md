# Bye-Laws for the Selection of Applicants for Admission to the Preparatory Course for the Diploma in Health Science under the auspices of the Institute of Health Care (S.L. 327.256)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.256**

BYE-LAWS FOR THE SELECTION OF APPLICANTS FOR ADMISSION TO THE PREPARATORY COURSE FOR THE DIPLOMA IN HEALTH SCIENCE

**UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE INSTITUTE OF**

**HEALTH CARE\***

31st May, 2005

LEGAL NOTICE 161 of 2005.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Selection of Applicants for Admission to the Preparatory Course for the Diploma in Health Science under the auspices of the Institute of Health Care.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall apply to the Nursing Studies area of study in the preparatory courses leading to admission to the Diploma in Health Science held under the auspices of the Institute of Health Care commencing in October 2004 or later.

## 3. Selection of applicants. S.L. 327.261

Whenever the number of students that may be admitted to the Nursing Studies area of study is limited by Senate in terms of the provisions of bye-law 9 of the Bye-Laws for the Diploma in Health Science, and the number of eligible applicants for this area of study exceeds the number of places available, the selection of applicants shall be made according to the criteria listed in bye-law 4.

## 4. Criteria for selection.

(1) Applicants for the Nursing Studies area of study who satisfy the entry requirements as laid down in the bye-laws for the course shall be awarded points for their qualifications in Maltese, English Language, Mathematics, one Science subject and the best result in two other subjects, obtained at any of the indicated levels. (2) If applicants present more than one pass in a subject, only the best grade shall be taken into consideration. Subjects may only be offered at one level.

(3) For the purpose of selection, only qualifications obtained by the 31st August preceding the commencement of the course shall be taken into consideration.

(4) If the applicants falling in the last eligible group with the same number of points are such that the total number of applicants to be admitted exceeds the number of places established by Senate, then the applicants in that group shall be interviewed by an ad hoc board appointed by the Board of the Institute of Health Care in order to fill the remaining place/s. The ad hoc board may consider additional qualifications to those indicated in paragraph (1).
