# Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Health Sciences (S.L. 327.723)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.723**

BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE FACULTY OF

**HEALTH SCIENCES**

16th July, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 160 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Health Sciences.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

“the Board” means the Board of the Faculty of Health Sciences;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D.;

“the Faculty” means the Faculty of Health Sciences; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admissions

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Faculty shall be in possession of one of the following degrees:

(a) a Master’s degree in the same area of study or in a related area of study obtained subsequent to the completion of a first cycle degree or a comparable qualification; or

(b) a Bachelor’s degree in a related area of study obtained with at least Second Class Honours (Upper Division); or

(c) a degree qualification comparable to the degrees specified in paragraphs (a) and (b), as approved by Senate on the recommendation of the Board.

## 4.

Applicants in possession of the degrees indicated in paragraphs (b) and (c) of bye-law 3 shall have successfully completed a dissertation to which not less than 10 ECTS credits are assigned.

## 5. Taught Study- Units.

Where the Board deems it necessary, it may require applicants to successfully complete a number of study-units during the course of their studies under those conditions it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 6. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 7. Repeal.

The Bye-Laws of 2014 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. – Degree Regulations, 2008 for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Health Sciences, published as Legal Notice 210 of 2010, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from the 1 October 2024.
