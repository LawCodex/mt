## 1. Citation and interpretation.

*Substituted by: L.N. 41 of 2013; L.N. 452 of 2014.*

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Pharmaceutical Technology - B.Sc. (Hons) Pharm. Tech. - under the auspices of the Faculty of Medicine and Surgery.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the Board" means the Board of the Faculty of Medicine and Surgery;

"the course" means the programme of study leading to the Degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Pharmaceutical Technology - B.Sc. (Hons) Pharm. Tech.;

"the Degree" means the Degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Pharmaceutical Technology - B.Sc. (Hons) Pharm. Tech.; and

*S.L. 327.236*

"the Principal Regulations" means the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: L.N. 41 of 2013; L.N. 452 of 2014; L.N. 233 of 2019.*

These bye-laws shall be applicable as from October 2019 onwards.

*Amended by: L.N. 233 of 2019.*

## 4. Number of applicants that may be allowed to register on the course.

The Board shall recommend to Senate the number of applicants that may be allowed to register on the course, depending on the availability of resources. The number of available places shall be announced at the time of the call for applications. Senate shall approve bye-laws for the selection of applicants to be admitted into the course.

## 5. Course duration.

*Substituted by: L.N. 23 of 2024.*

The Course shall extend over a period of three years of full-time study or the equivalent in part-time study.

## 6. Programme of study.

The Board shall publish the programme of study at the commencement of each course. Subject to changes that the Board may, with the approval of Senate, be constrained to make, students are bound to follow the study-units and obtain the credits as indicated in the programme of study for the Degree, provided that the programme shall be drawn up in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Principal Regulations.

## 7. Credits.

Students shall be required to register and pass in study-units to which a total of 180 credits are assigned.

## 8. Classification of the Degree.

The Final Weighted Average Mark for the classification of the Degree shall be based on the results obtained in the three years of the course, all study-units being weighted equally.

## 9. Provisions for part-time courses

*Added by: L.N. 23 of 2024.*

The provisions of the foregoing bye-laws shall apply mutatis mutandis to Courses followed on a part-time basis, and subject to such changes as may reasonably be deemed by the Board as necessary due to the part-time nature of the Course.

## 10.

*Added by: L.N. 23 of 2024.*

The Course may be offered on a flexible part-time basis with the following special provisions in terms of the provisions outlined in the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2019 applicable with the necessary changes needed (mutatis mutandis) owing to the part-time nature of the Course:

(a) During each academic year, students shall be allowed to register for a number of study-units as agreed with the Course Coordinator, to which not less than 12 ECTS credits are assigned, provided that registration for the study-units included in the programme of study for each Course year (60 ECTS credits) is spread over not more than three academic years.

(b) In order to progress regularly from one Course year to the next, the 60 ECTS credits assigned to the study-units of each Course year must be completed in not more than three academic years.

(d) If necessary, students may be allowed to refer the assessment of the study-units to the next academic year, provided that not more than 12 ECTS credits may be referred from one Course year to the next.

(e) The assessment of study-units that may be referred to the next academic year shall be considered as additional to the minimum number of credits (12 ECTS credits) that students are obliged to register for during any academic year.

(i) Students registered on the flexible part-time programme must obtain the approval of the Course Coordinator before they are registered on the study-units they wish to follow in any academic year.

(j) Subject to approval by the Board, students registered on the Course may be allowed to change the mode of attendance from full-time to flexible part-time or vice versa at the beginning of the second year of the regular programme of study. Changes in mode of attendance in any subsequent year of the Course may be considered by the Board if the Course programme permits.

(k) Notwithstanding the provisions in the General Regulations for University Undergraduate Awards, 2019 owing to the nature of the Course when offered on a flexible part-time basis that allows for a longer duration than normal, students registered on such a flexible part-time Course programme shall not be entitled to ever repeat a Course year in its entirety, nor to an extension year after each Course year.
