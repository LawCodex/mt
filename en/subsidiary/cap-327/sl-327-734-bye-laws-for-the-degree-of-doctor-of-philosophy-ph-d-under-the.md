# Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Centre for English Language Proficiency (S.L. 327.734)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.734**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

*Substituted by: L.N. 330 of 2024.*

PHILOSOPHY – PH.D. – UNDER THE AUSPICES OF

**THE CENTRE FOR ACADEMIC LITERACIES AND**

**ENGLISH COMMUNICATION SKILLS**

11th September, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 218 of 2024, as amended by Legal Notices 330 of 2024 and 245 of 2025.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

*Substituted by: L.N. 330 of 2024.*

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Centre for Academic Literacies and English Communication Skills.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires –

“the Board” means the Board of the Centre for Academic Literacies and English Communication Skills;

“the Centre” means the Centre for Academic Literacies and English Communication Skills;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D.; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

*Substituted by: L.N. 245 of 2025.*

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Centre shall be in possession of one of the following degrees:

(a) a Master’s degree in the same area of study or in a related area; or

(b) a Bachelor’s degree obtained with First Class Honours or Second Class Honours (Upper Division) in the same area of study or in a related area; or

(c) a qualification deemed by Senate, on the recommendation of the Board, to be equivalent or higher to the qualifications listed in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this bye-law and possessing experience or knowledge which, in the opinion of the Board, will enable them to pursue doctoral studies successfully.

## 4.

Applicants may also be asked to demonstrate competence in those skills required by the nature of the proposed research.

## 5. Centre Doctoral Committee

The Board shall recommend to Senate the appointment of a Centre Doctoral Committee, composed as follows:

**The Director of the Centre, or delegate, as Chair**

**3 members who shall be resident academics.**

The Centre Doctoral Committee may co-opt other members from other Faculties/Institutes/Centres, possessing academic doctoral qualifications, if deemed necessary.

## 6. Taught Study- Units

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during the course of their studies, and/or to satisfactorily undertake tasks recommended by the Board, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 7. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 8. Repeal

The Bye-Laws of 2016 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – Degree Regulations, 2008, for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy – Ph.D. – under the auspices of the Centre for English Language Proficiency, published as Legal Notice 377 of 2016, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
