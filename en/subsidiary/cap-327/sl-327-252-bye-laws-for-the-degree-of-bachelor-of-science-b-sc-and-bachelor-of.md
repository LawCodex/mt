## 4. Course duration.

*Substituted by: L.N. 173 of 2023.*

(1) The Course leading to the degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in one main area of study, which may be taken with one subsidiary area, shall extend over a period of three years of full-time study or the equivalent in part-time study.

(2) The Course leading to the degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in two subjects as main areas of study shall extend over a period of four years of full-time study or the equivalent in part-time study.

## 5. Areas of study.

*Amended by: L.N. 408 of 2013; L.N. 198 of 2018.*

(1) The degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) in one main area of study may be offered in any area of study that falls within the responsibility of the Faculty, as approved by the Board.

(2) \*The degree of Bachelor of Science (Honours) may also be offered either in two main areas of study from the areas listed in (a), or in one main area of study from option (a) and one main area of study from option (b), as approved by the Board:

(a) Biology, Chemistry, Computational Physics, Geosciences, Mathematics, Physics, Statistics and Operations Research;

(b) Banking and Finance, Computing Science, Philosophy. (3) †Subject to approval by Senate, the Board may also allow any of the areas of study indicated in sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (2) of this bye-law to be offered jointly with other areas of study which fall under the responsibility of other academic entities within the University, provided that in such instances, applicants shall be required to be in possession of those qualifications relevant to their chosen second main area of study as indicated in the bye-laws for the degree in the area of study offered under the auspices of the academic entity responsible for the discipline.

## 8. Course programme for the Degree in one main area of study.

*Amended by: L.N. 408 of 2013; L.N. 292 of 2016; L.N. 198 of 2018.*

(1) The course shall consist of study-units to which 180 credits are assigned.

(2) The course may be offered either in one area of study as a main area, or in one main area of study with a subsidiary area as approved by the Board, provided that not less than 110 credits shall be assigned to the main area of study.

(3) Programmes of study for the areas that may be taken as subsidiary areas shall be those published by the boards of the academic entities responsible for them.

(4) \*The third year of the Course shall include a dissertation to which not less than 10 credits and not more than 20 credits shall be assigned:

provided that in the case of Mathematics and Computational Physics, the dissertation may be substituted by one or more additional taught study-units.

(5) Students who successfully complete study-units to which 60 ECTS credits are assigned and opt not to proceed with the Course or, having proceeded, do not successfully complete the Course, shall be entitled to the Diploma.

(6) Students who successfully complete study-units to which 120 ECTS credits are assigned and opt not to proceed with the Course or, having proceeded, do not successfully complete the Course, shall be entitled to the Higher Diploma.

## 9. Course programme for the Degree in two main areas of study.

The course shall consist of two parts, namely, Part One comprising the first three years and Part Two comprising the fourth year.

## 12. Award of the grade CP.

*Substituted by: L.N. 292 of 2016.*

(1) In a course programme for the Degree in two main areas of study, students who fail in any study-unit, except a compulsory study-unit that is declared to be non-compensatable in a Programme of Study, with a mark of not less than 35% in the unit and whose current Year Average Mark in the Programme of Study for the area of study to which the study-unit is assigned is at least 50%, shall be awarded the grade CP and shall be awarded credit for the unit by compensation and shall not be required to be reassessed.

(2) Students who successfully complete study-units to which 60 ECTS credits are assigned and opt not to proceed with the Course or, having proceeded, do not successfully complete the Course, shall be entitled to the Diploma.

(3) Students who successfully complete study-units to which 120 ECTS credits are assigned and opt not to proceed with the Course or, having proceeded, do not successfully complete the Course, shall be entitled to the Higher Diploma.

## 13. Classification of the Awards. Re-numbered by: L.N. 23 of 2012.

*Substituted by: L.N. 292 of 2016.*

The Final Weighted Average Mark for the classification of the Diploma, the Higher Diploma and the Degree, shall be based on the results obtained in each year of the Course, all years being weighted equally.

## 14. Provisions for Part- Time Courses.

*Added by: L.N. 173 of 2023.*

The provisions of the foregoing bye-laws shall apply mutatis mutandis to courses followed on a part-time basis, and subject to such changes as may reasonably be deemed by the Board as necessary due to the part-time nature of the Course.
