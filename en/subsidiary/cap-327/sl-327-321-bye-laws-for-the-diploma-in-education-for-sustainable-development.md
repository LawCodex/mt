# Bye-Laws for the Diploma in Education for Sustainable Development under the auspices of the Faculty of Education (S.L. 327.321)

DIPLOMA IN EDUCATION FOR SUSTAINABLE

DEVELOPMENT UNDER THE AUSPICES

*1*

OF THE FACULTY OF EDUCATION [S.L.327.321

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.321**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DIPLOMA IN EDUCATION**

FOR SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE FACULTY OF EDUCATION 23rd November, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 386 of 2007.

## 1. Citation and interpretation.

(1) The title of these bye-laws is the Bye-Laws for the Diploma in Education for Sustainable Development under the auspices of the Faculty of Education.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires - "the course" means the course of studies leading to the Diploma in Education for Sustainable Development; and

"the Diploma" means the Diploma in Education for Sustainable Development.

## 2. Applicability.

These bye-laws shall apply to courses commencing in October 2007 or later.

## 3. Requirements for admission.

To be registered as regular students in the course, applicants must satisfy the general requirements for admission as specified in the Admission Regulations of the University.

## 4. Course duration.

The course shall extend over a period of two years of part-time study.

## 5. Course programme.

The course shall consist of:

(a) a number of taught study-units to which 42 credits shall be assigned;

(b) a study-unit comprising a supervised individual project to which 10 credits shall be assigned; and

(c) one study-unit comprising a practicum to which 8 credits shall be assigned.

## 6. Classification of the Award.

(1) The Final Weighted Average Mark for the purpose of the classification of the Diploma shall be based on the results obtained in each year of the course, all credits being weighted equally.

(2) The names of students who qualify for the award of the Diploma shall be published in a list in alphabetical order classified as follows:

Passed with Distinction

Passed.
