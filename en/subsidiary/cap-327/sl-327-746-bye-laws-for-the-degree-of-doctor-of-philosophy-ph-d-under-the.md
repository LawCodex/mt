# Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Mediterranean Institute (S.L. 327.746)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.746**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF

**THE MEDITERRANEAN INSTITUTE**

8th October, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 260 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Mediterranean Institute.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires –

“the Board” means the Board of the Mediterranean Institute;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D.;

“the Institute” means the Mediterranean Institute; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Institute shall be in possession of a Master’s degree or equivalent.

## 4.

Applicants may be asked to demonstrate competence in those skills required by the nature of the proposed research.

## 5. Institute Doctoral Committee

The Board shall recommend to Senate the appointment of an Institute Doctoral Committee composed as follows:

the Chair of the Institute or delegate (as Chair)

the Director of the Institute or delegate

a member of the Institute Board.

## 6. Taught Study- Units

When the Board deems it necessary, it may require students to successfully complete a number of study-units during the course of their studies, under those conditions that it may impose, after approval by Senate.

## 7. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 8.

The minimum number of words for the thesis shall be 80,000 words.

## 9. Repeal

The Bye-Laws of 2009 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2008 for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Mediterranean Institute, published as Legal Notice 310 of 2009, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
