# Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Centre for Molecular Medicine and Biobanking (S.L. 327.730)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 327.730**

**BYE-LAWS FOR THE DEGREE OF DOCTOR OF**

PHILOSOPHY - PH.D. - UNDER THE AUSPICES OF THE CENTRE FOR MOLECULAR MEDICINE AND

**BIOBANKING**

11th September, 2024

LEGAL NOTICE 214 of 2024.

## 1. Citation and Interpretation

(1) These bye-laws may be cited as the Bye-Laws for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Centre for Molecular Medicine and Biobanking.

(2) In these bye-laws, unless the context otherwise requires -

“the Board” means the Board of the Centre for Molecular Medicine and Biobanking;

“the Centre” means the Centre for Molecular Medicine and Biobanking;

“the Degree” means the degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D.; and

“the Principal Regulations” means the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2023.

## 2. Applicability

These bye-laws shall be applicable from 1 October 2024 onwards.

## 3. Requirements for Admission

Applicants for the Degree offered by the Centre shall be in possession of one of the following degrees:

(a) a Master’s degree, normally of this or another University, in an area of studies relevant to that applied for; or

(b) a Bachelor’s degree, obtained with at least Second Class Honours (Upper Division), normally of this or another University, in an area of studies relevant to that applied for; or

(c) qualifications which the Board deems comparable to the degrees indicated in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this bye-law.

## 4.

Applicants may also be asked to demonstrate competence in those skills required by the nature of the proposed research.

## 5. Taught Study- Units/Technical Experience

The Board may require students to audit a number of relevant study-units and to undertake additional tasks as deemed necessary in order to improve their scientific and technical background during the course of their studies.

## 6. ThesisPresentation and Format

The Board shall approve and publish guidelines on the format and presentation of the thesis for the Degree.

## 7. Repeal

The Bye-Laws of 2021 in terms of the Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - Degree Regulations, 2008 for the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy - Ph.D. - under the auspices of the Centre for Molecular Medicine and Biobanking, published as Legal Notice 25 of 2021, shall be deemed to cease to be in force as from 1 October 2024.
