# Forms of Stock Books for Spirits Order (S.L. 41.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 41.01**

**FORMS OF STOCK BOOKS FOR SPIRITS ORDER**

18th May, 1937 NOTICE dated 18th May, 1937.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Forms of Stock Books for Spirits Order.

## 2. Stock books.

The stock books to be kept by dealers or retailers of spirits shall be in the form as detailed in the Schedule to this Order.
