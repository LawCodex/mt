# Suspension of Conditions regarding the Renewal of Target Shooter Licence A Special Regulations (S.L. 480.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 480.07**

**SUSPENSION OF CONDITIONS REGARDING THE**

**RENEWAL OF TARGET SHOOTER LICENCE A**

**SPECIAL REGULATIONS**

5th February, 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 42 of 2021.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Suspension of Conditions regarding the Renewal of Target Shooter Licence A Special Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 480.*

"Act" means the Arms Act;

*S.L. 480. 02.*

"Regulations" means the Arms Licensing Regulations.

## 3. Renewal of Target Shooter Licence A Special.

A holder of a Target Shooter Licence A Special shall have his licence renewed for the year 2021 even if he does not meet the requirement indicated in regulation 21A(6) of the Regulations, provided that he meets all the other requirements at law.
