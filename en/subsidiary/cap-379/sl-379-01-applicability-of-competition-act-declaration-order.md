# Applicability of Competition Act (Declaration) Order (S.L. 379.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 379.01**

**APPLICABILITY OF COMPETITION ACT**

**(DECLARATION) ORDER**

7th February, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 16 of 1995.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Applicability of Competition Act (Declaration) Order.

## 2. Applicability of Competition Act. Cap. 379.

The provisions of the Competition Act shall apply to the following departments, corporations, companies or partnerships, in which the Government, directly or indirectly, has a controlling interest:

Bank of Valletta Ltd.

Hotel Management Services Co. Ltd.

Kalaxlokk Co. Ltd.

Konkos Co. Ltd.

Kordin Grain Terminal Co. Ltd.

Malta Gantry Manufacturing Ltd.

Malta Government Investments Co. Ltd.

Malta University Services Co. Ltd.

Malta Win Containers Ltd.

Mediterranean Oilfield Services Co. Ltd.

Mid-Med Bank Ltd.

Promotion and Trading Co. Ltd.
