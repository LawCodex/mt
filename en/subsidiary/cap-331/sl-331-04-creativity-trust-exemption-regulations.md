# Creativity Trust (Exemption) Regulations (S.L. 331.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 331.04**

**CREATIVITY TRUST (EXEMPTION)**

**REGULATIONS**

15th January, 2013

LEGAL NOTICE 22 of 2013.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Creativity Trust (Exemption) Regulations.

## 2. Exemption. S.L. 444.02 Cap. 331.

The Trustees of the trust known as the Creativity Trust, established by the Creativity Trust Order, shall be exempt from the requirements of articles 43 and 43A of the Trusts and Trustees Act in accordance with article 57(1)(g) of the same Act.
