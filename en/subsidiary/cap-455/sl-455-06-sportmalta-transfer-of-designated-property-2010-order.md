# SportMalta (Transfer of Designated Property) (2010) Order (S.L. 455.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 455.06**

**SPORTMALTA (TRANSFER OF DESIGNATED**

**PROPERTY) ORDER (2010)**

1st January, 2010

LEGAL NOTICES 66, 86, 122, 489, 490, 491, 492 and 493 of 2010, consolidated, and as amended by Act VI of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

The title of this order is the SportMalta (Transfer of Designated Property) Order (2010).

## 2. Designated Property.

The properties described in the Schedule are hereby being designated for the purpose of the enjoyment, possession, use and management of all rights and obligations performing to such designated property in terms of article 16 of the Sports Act.

*Cap. 455.*

## 3. Appointed Day.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

The designated properties referred to in article 2 shall be transferred and vest in SportMalta as provided in the article aforesaid with effect from the dates specified in the Schedule.
