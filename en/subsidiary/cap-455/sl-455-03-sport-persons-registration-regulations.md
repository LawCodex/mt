# Sport Persons (Registration) Regulations (S.L. 455.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 455.03**

**SPORTS PERSONS**

**(REGISTRATION) REGULATIONS**

12th February, 2008

LEGAL NOTICE 63 of 2008, as amended by Act VI of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Sport Persons (Registration) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

(1) In these regulations unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap 455.*

"the Act" means the Sports Act;

"applicant" means a sport association or national sport association, a federation or national federation, a club, sport school or person who applies with SportMalta for registration in the Register;

"application form" means the application form set out in the First Schedule hereof;

"statute" means the statute or act of constitution of an applicant or registered person.

(2) Subject to the provisions of sub-regulation (1), terms and expressions used in these regulations shall, unless the context otherwise requires, have the meaning assigned to them in the Act.

## 3. Register of Sport Persons.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

SportMalta shall keep the Register of Sport Persons provided for in Part VII of the Act, and it may consist of such parts as SportMalta may from time to time determine for the purpose of distinguishing the nature of the registered person.

## 4. Application to register.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

(1) No person shall be registered in the appropriate part of the Register unless an application form is submitted to SportMalta.

(2) An applicant shall apply for registration by submitting to SportMalta the application form, addressed to the Chairman of SportMalta, which shall be accompanied by all such other documents required by the Act and these regulations.

## 5. Alterations to be notified.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

(1) A registered person, other than an individual, shall notify SportMalta of any of the following:-

(a) any amendment or change in its Statute;

(b) any change in its management, administration or representation;

(c) any change in its official address or office.

(2) Where any of the above events occur, it shall be the duty of the person or official entrusted with the legal representation of the registered person to deliver to SportMalta, within twenty days from its occurrence:

(a) a copy of the amendment or change in the Statute of the registered person, together with an updated and revised copy of the Statute;

(b) a return of any change in its official address or office;

(c) a return of any change in the membership and officials of the Committee, Board or SportMalta of the registered person, specifying the name and residence of the newly appointed persons.

## 6. Annual accounts.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

(1) every registered person shall, by not later than the end of October of each current year, deliver to SportMalta a copy of the accounts for the financial year ending not later than the 31st December of the immediately preceding year together with the auditor’s report referred to in sub-regulation (2). Such accounts shall comply as to form and content with the provisions of the Second Schedule hereof. Such accounts shall give a true and fair view of the assets, liabilities, operations, financial position and profit or loss of the registered person, and shall be signed by at least one official of the registered person or in the case of an individual registered person by that individual.

*Auditor’s report.*

(2) The accounts referred to in the immediately preceding sub-regulation shall be audited by an auditor in accordance with the provisions of the Statute of the registered person.

## 7. Data and information to be provided to SportMalta.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

(1) SportMalta may from time to time require any applicant or registered person to provide SportMalta with data and information relating to athletes, coaches, officials and facilities of such applicant or registered person and in the case of a national association or federation or other registered person, data and information relating to any organization registered or affiliated thereto and their officials as SportMalta may require for the purpose of performing its functions under the Act.

*Annual return. Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

(2) Every registered person shall by not later than the end of October of any current year make and deliver to SportMalta a signed return in the form set out in the Third Schedule.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*
