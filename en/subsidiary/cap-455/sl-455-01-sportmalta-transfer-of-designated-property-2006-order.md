# SportMalta (Transfer of Designated Property) (2006) Order (S.L. 455.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 455.01**

**SPORTMALTA (TRANSFER OF DESIGNATED**

**PROPERTY) ORDER (2006)**

15th October, 2006

LEGAL NOTICE 265 of 2006, as amended by Act VI of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

The title of this order is the SportMalta (Transfer of Designated Property) Order (2006).

## 2. Designated Property.

The property described in article 4 is hereby being designated for the purpose of the enjoyment, possession, use and management of all rights and obligations performing to such designated property in terms of article 16 of the Sports Act.

*Cap. 455.*

## 3. Appointed Day.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

The designated property referred to in article 2 shall be transferred and vest in SportMalta as provided in the article aforesaid with effect from the 15th October, 2006.

## 4. Description of designated property.

The property referred to in article 2 is the following, that is to say:

A plot of land at Luqa having an area of approximately 3,738 square metres, bounded on the South by Triq Xmun Azzopardi, on the West by Triq San Tumas and on the North East by Triq Ġużeppi Ellul, indicated edged red on P.D.2006\_592.
