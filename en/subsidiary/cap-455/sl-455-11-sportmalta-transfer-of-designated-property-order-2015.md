# SportMalta (Transfer of Designated Property) Order (2015) (S.L. 455.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 455.11**

**SPORTMALTA (TRANSFER OF DESIGNATED**

**PROPERTY) ORDER (2015)**

1st November, 2014

LEGAL NOTICE 62 of 2015, as amended by Act VI of 2016.

## 1. Citation.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

The title of this order is the SportMalta (Transfer of Designated Property) Order (2015).

## 2. Designated Property.

The properties described in the Schedule are hereby being designated for the purpose of the enjoyment, possession, use and management of all rights and obligations performing to such designated property in terms of article 16 of the Sports Act.

*Cap. 455.*

## 3. Appointed Day.

*Amended by: VI. 2016.7.*

The designated properties referred to in article 2 shall be transferred and vest in SportMalta as provided in the article aforesaid with effect from the dates specified in the Schedule.

2 S.L.455.11] (TRANSFER OF DESIGNATED PROPERTY)

SCHEDULE L.N. 62 of 2015. As from 1st November, 2015:

A plot of land (Water polo pitch and club) in Valletta, having an area of approximately 3240 sq m, bounded by the East, West and South by Government property as indicated edged in red on P.D. 56\_99\_B and subject to a right of access to third parties on foreshore and bastion lights.
