# Applicability of the Companies Act to Offshore Companies formed and registered under the Commercial Partnerships Ordinance and the Malta Financial Services Authority Act Order (S.L. 386.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 386.07**

**APPLICABILITY OF THE COMPANIES ACT**

**TO OFFSHORE COMPANIES FORMED AND**

**REGISTERED UNDER THE COMMERCIAL**

**PARTNERSHIPS ORDINANCE AND THE MALTA**

FINANCIAL SERVICES AUTHORITY ACT ORDER\* 1st January, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 270 of 2003.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Applicability of the Companies Act to Offshore Companies formed and registered under the Commercial Partnerships Ordinance and the Malta Financial Services Authority Act Order.

## 2. Applicability to offshore companies.

The provisions of the Companies Act shall, as from the 1st January, 2004, commence to apply to offshore companies formed and registered under the Commercial Partnerships Ordinance and the Malta Financial Services Centre Act as retained in force by article 107(4) of the Special Funds (Regulation) Act, 2002, (transitory provision in Part V of the last mentioned Act amending the Malta Financial Services Authority Act, Cap. 330), and subject to the provisions of the Companies Act (Applicability to Offshore Companies) Regulations.

*S.L. 386.06*

\*These regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 69 of 2024.
