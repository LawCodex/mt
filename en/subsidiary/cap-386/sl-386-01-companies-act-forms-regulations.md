# Companies Act (Forms) Regulations (S.L. 386.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 386.01**

**COMPANIES ACT (FORMS) REGULATIONS**

1st January, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 101 of 1996, as amended by Legal Notices 116 of 1997, 214 and 425 of 2007, 401 of 2011, 358 of 2014, 51 of 2022, 92 and 287 of 2025.

### 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Companies Act (Forms) Regulations.

### 2. Use of forms. Cap. 386.

The following forms prescribed in the Schedule shall be the forms to be used under the Companies Act.

(iv) does not have any assets in excess of five thousand euro (€5,000); and
