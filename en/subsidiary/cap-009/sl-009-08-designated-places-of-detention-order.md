# Designated Places of Detention Order (S.L. 9.08)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 9.08**

**DESIGNATED PLACES OF DETENTION ORDER**

1st January, 2004 LEGAL NOTICE 437 of 2003.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is the Designated Places of Detention Order.

## 2. Designation of places of detention. Cap. 9.

The places mentioned in the Schedule are hereby declared to be a place of detention for the purposes of the Criminal Code.

## SCHEDULE

(Article 2)

## 1.

The lock-up at Police General Headquarters at Floriana. 2. The lock-up in the building housing the Law Courts at Valletta.

## 3.

The lock-up in the building housing the administrative section of the Police Department at Victoria, Gozo.
