# Service of Summons of Jurors Regulations (S.L. 9.26)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 9.26**

**SERVICE OF SUMMONS**

**OF JURORS REGULATIONS**

23rd October, 2020

LEGAL NOTICE 405 of 2020.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Service of Summons of Jurors Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 9.*

"Code" means the Criminal Code;

"Court" means the Criminal Court;

"Registrar" means the Registrar, Criminal Courts and Tribunals.

"summons" means the summons of jurors issues in accordance with article 608 of the Code.

## 3. Service by post.

The service of the summons shall, unless the Court otherwise orders, be made through registered post, and there shall be an application for advice of delivery when it is posted.

## 4. The summons shall be enclosed in an envelope.

The summons to be served by post as aforementioned shall be enclosed in an envelope upon which the words "Summons of Jurors" shall be written in a clearly visible place.

## 5. Duties of the executive officers of the courts.

When the service is to be effected by post, the executive officers of the courts shall:

(a) prepare a postal document necessary for that service and present the relative envelope with the summons enclosed inside it in the postal office;

(b) note in a registry specifically held for such purpose, the particulars of the summons, the date of the summons, the number given by the postal authorities to the envelope in which the summons is enclosed for postage, and, if the service would have taken place, the date of such service and the date of return of the advice of delivery;

(c) make a certificate where it is stated whether the summons took place or not, and if the summons did not take place, the reason why it did not take place. In the certificate, there shall also be the particulars of the notified person or the person who refused service depending on the case;

(d) inform the Registrar whether the service took place or not, and if it took place, the date of such service.
