# Payment of Fees to Jurors Regulations (S.L. 9.6)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 9.06**

**PAYMENT OF FEES TO JURORS REGULATIONS**

1st May, 1993 LEGAL NOTICE 52 of 1993, as amended by Legal Notices 407 of 2007 and 105 of 2016.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Payment of Fees to Jurors Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these Regulations -

*Cap. 9.*

''juror'' means a person who is sworn in as a juror in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Code and includes a foreman of jurors, a supplementary juror and a special juror sworn as aforesaid;

''registrar'' means the Registrar of the superior courts;

''day of service'' means a day during which a juror has given his services and includes a Saturday, Sunday or other public holiday when the jurors are not allowed to return home as provided in sub-article (2) of article 471 of the Criminal Code.

*Cap. 9.*

## 3. Payment of fees to jurors not in receipt of salary or wages.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007. Substituted by: L.N. 105 of 2016.*

The registrar shall, as soon as may be after every trial by jury, pay to a juror a fee of thirty euro (€30) for every day of service, or part of such day, in of which the said juror was not in receipt of any salary or wages from his employment when the trial does not extend beyond fifteen working days and forty euro (€40) for every additional day of service, or part of such day, as above mentioned beyond the said fifteen working days.

## 4. Payment of fees to jurors in receipt of salary or wages.

*Amended by: L.N. 407 of 2007. Substituted by: L.N. 105 of 2016.*

The registrar shall, as soon as may be after every trial by jury, pay to a juror a fee of ten euro (€10) for every day of service, or part of such day, in of which the said juror was in receipt of - (a) any salary or wages from his employment; or

(b) a pension; or

(c) if the juror was unemployed; or

(d) if the juror was a housewife.
