# Communicable Diseases and Conditions Regulations (S.L. 9.10)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 9.10**

**COMMUNICABLE DISEASES AND CONDITIONS**

**REGULATIONS**

17th May, 2005 LEGAL NOTICE 137 of 2005.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Communicable Diseases and Conditions Regulations.

## 2. Specification of diseases or conditions. Cap. 9.

The following diseases or conditions are specified as diseases or conditions to which article 244A of the Criminal Code applies:

(a) Human Immunodeficiency Virus Infection (HIV);

(b) Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS);

(c) Hepatitis B infection;

(d) Hepatitis C infection;

(e) Tuberculosis.
