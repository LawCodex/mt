# Property held under the Criminal Code Notice (S.L. 9.7)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 9.07**

PROPERTY HELD UNDER THE CRIMINAL CODE

**NOTICE**

31st May, 2002 LEGAL NOTICE 121 of 2002.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Notice is Property held under the Criminal Code Notice.

## 2. Designated property and persons.

The following persons shall hold, on behalf of the Registrar of the Criminal Courts, the property designated next to their names:

The Commissioner of Police Arms and ammunition, including parts thereof;

The Brigadier of the Armed Explosives, detonators, Forces dangerous chemicals and other substances or combustible materials;

The Director of Health Dangerous drugs;

The Director of Museums Articles of a historical or artistic value;

The Governor of the Central Monies, financial certificates, Bank gold, silver, gemstones and other precious articles.

## 3. Property to be held in a safe place.

Property held by such persons shall be held in a safe place by the persons designated and shall not be used by any person or released to any person except as directed by the Registrar of the Criminal Courts.

## 4. Firearms, etc., forfeited in favour of the Government. Cap. 9.

The Commissioner of Police shall be the person to whom firearms, ammunition, explosives or other dangerous substances forfeited in favour of the Government in terms of the Criminal Code shall be consigned by the registrar in order to be disposed of.
