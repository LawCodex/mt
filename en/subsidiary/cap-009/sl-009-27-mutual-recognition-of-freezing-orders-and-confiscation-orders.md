# Mutual Recognition of Freezing Orders and Confiscation Orders Regulations (S.L. 9.27)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 9.27**

**MUTUAL RECOGNITION OF FREEZING ORDERS**

**AND CONFISCATION ORDERS REGULATIONS**

23th April, 2021

LEGAL NOTICE 180 of 2021.

## 1. Title and scope.

(1) The title of these regulations is the Mutual Recognition of Freezing Orders and Confiscation Orders Regulations.

(2) These regulations provide for the necessary provisions required to implement Regulation (EU) 2018/1805 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 November 2018 on the mutual recognition of freezing orders and confiscation orders, hereinafter referred to as "the Regulation".

## 2. Designation of competent authorities.

(1) When Malta is the ‘issuing State’ for purposes of the Regulation, the courts of criminal jurisdiction and the Asset Recovery Bureau shall be designated as competent issuing authorities in respect of freezing orders and confiscation orders:

Provided that the Attorney General shall be the validating authority for purposes of the Regulation.

(2) When Malta is the ‘executing State’ for purposes of the Regulation, the Asset Recovery Bureau shall be designated as the competent executing authority.

## 3. Transmission of freezing orders and confiscation orders.

(1) A freezing certificate transmitted to the Asset Recovery Bureau shall be accompanied by the original freezing order and a translation of the freezing certificate.

(2) A confiscation certificate transmitted to the Asset Recovery Bureau shall be accompanied by the original confiscation order and a translation of the confiscation certificate.
