# Fees for Drugs Certificates Regulations (S.L. 101.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 101.04**

FEES FOR DRUGS CERTIFICATES REGULATIONS 1st January, 1998 LEGAL NOTICE 147 of 1998, as amended by Legal Notice 409 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Fees for Drugs Certificates Regulations.

## 2. Levy of certificate fee.

There shall be paid to the Superintendent of Public Health, in respect of the issue or renewal of a certificate shown under Column 1 of the Schedule, the fee shown under Column 2 of the same Schedule.

## 3. Other licences, etc.

(1) Any certificate issued under the provisions of these regulations shall lapse on the thirty-first day of December of each year.

(2) No certificate issued under the provisions of these regulations shall be deemed to exempt from the requirement of any other licence, permit or authority imposed by any other provision of the Dangerous Drugs Ordinance or by any Regulations or other law in force for the time being.

*Cap. 101.*
