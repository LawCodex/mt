# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm5,000,000 6% Malta Government Stock 1998 (Fifth Issue) (S.L. 384.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 384.02**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM5,000,000 6%

**MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 1998**

**(FIFTH ISSUE)**

11th July, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 97 of 1995.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm5,000,000 6% Malta Government Stock 1998 (Fifth Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

(1) It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm5,000,000 6% Malta Government Stock 1998 (V) an additional issue of Lm5,000,000 6% Malta Government Stock 1998 (V) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6% Malta Government Stock 1998 (V) created by means of Legal Notice 85 of 1995 to a total amount of Lm10,000,000.

*S.L.161.46*

(2) This additional amount shall be allotted to those applicants who have already applied to subscribe to this stock by the closing date as announced in Legal Notice 85 of 1995.

*S.L.161.46*
