# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm2,500,000 6.9% Malta Government Stock 2002 (Third Issue) (S.L. 384.08)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 384.08**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM2,500,000 6.9% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2002

**(THIRD ISSUE)**

29th September, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 137 of 1995.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm2,500,000 6.9% Malta Government Stock 2002 (Third Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

(1) It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm10,000,000 6.9% Malta Government Stock 2002 (III) an additional issue of Lm2,500,000 6.9% Malta Government Stock 2002 (III) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6.9% Malta Government Stock 2002 (III) created by means of Legal Notice 116 of 1995 to a total amount of Lm12,500,000.

*S.L.384.05*

(2) This additional amount shall be allotted to those applicants who have already applied to subscribe to this stock by the 26th September, 1995.
