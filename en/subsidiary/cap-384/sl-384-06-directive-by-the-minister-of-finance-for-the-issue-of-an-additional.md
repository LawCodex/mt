# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm7,600,000 6% Malta Government Stock 1998 (Sixth Issue) (S.L. 384.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 384.06**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM7,600,000 6%

**MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 1998**

**(SIXTH ISSUE)**

29th September, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 135 of 1995.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm7,600,000 6% Malta Government Stock 1998 (Sixth Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

(1) It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm10,000,000 6% Malta Government Stock 1998 (VI) an additional issue of Lm7,600,000 6% Malta Government Stock 1998 (VI) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6% Malta Government Stock 1998 (VI) created by means of Legal Notice 114 of 1995 to a total amount of Lm17,600,000.

*S.L.384.03*

(2) This additional amount shall be allotted to those applicants who have already applied to subscribe to this stock by the 26th September, 1995.
