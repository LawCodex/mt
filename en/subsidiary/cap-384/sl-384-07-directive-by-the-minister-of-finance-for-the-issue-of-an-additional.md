# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm5,500,000 6.5% Malta Government Stock 2000 (Fourth Issue) (S.L. 384.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 384.07**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM5,500,000 6.5% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2000

**(FOURTH ISSUE)**

29th September, 1995

LEGAL NOTICE 136 of 1995.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm5,500,000 6.5% Malta Government Stock 2000 (Fourth Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

(1) It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm5,000,000 6.5% Malta Government Stock 2000 (IV) an additional issue of Lm5,500,000 6.5% Malta Government Stock 2000 (IV) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6.5% Malta Government Stock 2000 (IV) created by means of Legal Notice 115 of 1995 to a total amount of Lm10,500,000.

*S.L.384.04*

(2) This additional amount shall be allotted to those applicants who have already applied to subscribe to this stock by the 26th September, 1995.
