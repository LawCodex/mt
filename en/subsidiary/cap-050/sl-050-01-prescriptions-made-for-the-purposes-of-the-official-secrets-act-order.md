# Prescriptions made for the purposes of the Official Secrets Act Order (S.L. 50.1)

*1*

OF THE OFFICIAL SECRETS ACT [ S.L.50.01

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 50.01**

**PRESCRIPTIONS MADE FOR THE PURPOSES OF**

**THE OFFICIAL SECRETS ACT ORDER**

26th July, 1996 LEGAL NOTICE 143 of 1996.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Prescriptions made for the purposes of the Official Secrets Act Order.

## 2. Prescribed bodies.

The bodies which are set out in the first column of the First Schedule to this Order, and the classes of members or employees of those bodies which are set out in the second column of that Schedule, are hereby prescribed for the purposes of article 2(1)(e) -definition of "public servant" - of the Official Secrets Act.

*Cap. 50.*

## 3. Prescribed offices.

The offices which are set out in the first column of the Second Schedule to this Order, and the classes of employees of the holders of those offices which are set out in the second column of that Schedule, are hereby prescribed for the purposes of article 2(1)(f) - definition of "public servant" - of the Official Secrets Act.

*Cap. 50.*

## 4. Further prescribed bodies.

The bodies which are set out in the first column of the Third Schedule to this Order are hereby prescribed for the purposes of article 12(5) and/or article 13(9) of the Act as set out in the second column of that Schedule, so as to enable them, in the case of article 12(5), to give official authorisation for, or to impose official restrictions on, disclosures or, in the case of article 13(9), to give official directions for the return or disposal of documents).
