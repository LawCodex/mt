## 4. Composition of the Council.

(1) The Council shall be composed of the following members:

(a) a Chairperson with proficient knowledge, competence and experience in the agricultural sector, appointed by the Minister;

(b) the Director of Agriculture, ex officio;

(c) one (1) person representing the Permanent Secretary in the Ministry responsible for agriculture, ex officio;

(d) ten (10) persons, who shall be sectorial leading representatives, each with professional qualifications or technical expertise, knowledge and, or experience in different individual agricultural sectors, appointed by the Minister as follows:

(i) one (1) representative of the dairy livestock sector;

(ii) one (1) representative of the swine sector;

(iii) one (1) representative of the sheep or goat sector;

(iv) one (1) representative of the poultry sector;

(v) one (1) representative of the rabbit sector;

(vi) one (1) representative of the apiculture sector;

(vii) one (1) representative of the vine sector;

(viii) one (1) representative of the tomato processing sector;

(ix) one (1) representative of the fruit and vegetable sector; and

## 5. Functions of the Council.

(1) The functions of the Council shall be:

(a) to promote participation and dialogue amongst all the stakeholders on issues relating to or in connection with sustainable development of the agricultural and the livestock sector;

(b) to improve and enhance the communication between the stakeholders of the agricultural and the livestock breeding sectors and the Government;

(c) to identify issues that affect stakeholders of the agricultural sector and proposes to the Government strategies that address such;

(d) to propose sustainable and feasible projects to implement the objectives of governmental policies in the agricultural and livestock breeding sectors and recommend measures to facilitate and further the achievement of any targets set;

(e) upon the request by the Government, to draw up, examine and make recommendations on legislative proposals REGULATIONS [S.L. 117.45 relating to the objectives of the Council;

(f) upon the request of the Minister, to make recommendations and submit opinions on any other matter arising in relation to the functions of the Ministry responsible for agriculture;

(g) to make recommendations to the Government regarding policies relating to the agricultural sector and rural development; and

(h) to perform any other functions as may be assigned to it by the Minister under the provisions of these regulations or any other law.

(2) Without prejudice to the provisions of sub-regulation (1), in the discharge of its objectives and functions, the Council shall adopt national perspectives and strategies.

## 6. Proceedings of the Council.

(1) The Council may act and perform its functions notwithstanding any absence or vacancy amongst its members, provided that there is a quorum of at least six (6) members of the Council:

Provided that a quorum shall not be present in the absence of the Chairperson or designated Deputy Chairperson.

(2) In the exercise of its functions and, or its deliberations on specific issues or cases, the Council may request the input of persons engaged in the public sector and, or public entities. The Council may also request appropriate expertise from, or collaboration with, third parties, as it may deem appropriate.

## 7. Ad hoc committees.

(1) The Council shall appoint ad hoc committees to serve as platforms for various sectors in agriculture whereby they may raise any issues, concerns or suggestions which may be discussed by the Council during its meetings.

(2) The Council shall inform the Minister of the appointment of each ad hoc committee.

(3) The Council may, when it deems it necessary, inform the Minister of the issues raised by the appointed committees.

(4) The chairperson of each committee shall be the sectorial leading representative most relevant or the most closely related to the matter or issue to be discussed by the committee. Where the matter or issue to be discussed affects more than one (1) agricultural sector, the Council shall determine which sectorial leading representative is to chair the particular committee.

(5) The chairperson of each committee shall convene the first meeting by not later than one (1) calendar month following the date of appointment of the committee in accordance with sub-regulation (1).

(6) Subject to the provisions of these regulations, the committees shall regulate their own proceedings:

## 8. Secretary to the Council.

(1) The Secretary to the Council appointed in accordance with regulation 4(8) shall hold office for a period of three (3) years, under such terms and conditions as the Council, with the approval of the Minister, may establish.

(2) The Secretary shall, inter alia, be responsible for:

(a) the convening of meetings of the Council and the keeping of minutes of the meetings thereof;

(b) the keeping of records of the members present during the meeting, the absent members and the excused members ;

(c) the provision of administrative and secretarial services required by the Council and any ad hoc committees;

(d) ensuring that all information that may be required by the Council and any ad hoc committees to effectively carry out their functions is made available to them;

(e) the provision of information and carrying out preparatory work for the reports of the Council and any ad hoc committees;

(f) ensuring that office services are provided for the running of the Council and any ad hoc committees; and

(g) exercising any other responsibility as may be delegated by the respective Chairpersons on behalf of the Council and any ad hoc committees.

## 9. Meetings.

(1) The Council shall meet on a regular basis as the Chairperson may deem necessary, provided that the Council shall hold not less than one (1) meeting every quarter in every calendar year.

(2) The Council shall hold a meeting with the Minister not less than once (1) every quarter in every calendar year.

## 10. Annual report.

By not later than six (6) weeks after the end of each calendar year or following instructions issued by the Minister, the REGULATIONS [S.L. 117.45 Council shall deliver to the Minister an annual report detailing all the activities undertaken by the Council and any ad hoc committees appointed in accordance with regulation 7 during the said calendar year. Such report shall include, inter alia, a general report on the proceedings of and developments made by the Council and any ad hoc committees during the period to which it relates in respect of matters falling within the scope of the Council.

## 11. Relations between the Minister and the Council.

The Minister may, from time to time, issue directives in writing to the Council as he may deem appropriate, being directives consistent with the provisions of these regulations and any other regulations made under the Act so as to give effect to the provisions of these regulations and, or for the better carrying out of any of the provisions of these regulations. The Council shall, as soon as practicable, give effect to all such directives and conduct its affairs accordingly.
