# Control of Re-exports of Supplies and Services Regulations (S.L. 117.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 117.06**

**CONTROL OF RE-EXPORTS OF SUPPLIES AND**

**SERVICES REGULATIONS**

28th March, 1956 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 180 of 1956, as amended by Government Notices 389 and 784 of 1957; and Legal Notices 25 of 1959, 13 of 1961 and 409 of 2007.

### 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is Control of Re-exports of Supplies and Services Regulations.

### 2. Prohibition.

The transhipment in Malta of any goods specified in the First Schedule which are in the course of shipment on a through bill of lading or otherwise -

(a) from any country specified in the Second Schedule; or (b) from any other country if the Comptroller of Customs is satisfied that a person who is resident in any country specified in the Second Schedule has acted as a principal in the consignment of the goods,

to any country specified in the Third Schedule is prohibited unless the goods are accompanied by a valid transit authorisation certificate issued by the appropriate authority of the country from which the goods were originally exported, in the form of the Fourth Schedule.

### 3. Description of goods.

The importer of any goods referred to in regulation 2 or his agent, shall, before transhipment, in making due entry of the goods in terms of the Customs Ordinance and of the regulations made thereunder, describe such goods in accordance with their designation in the First Schedule, giving the relative quantity and/ or unit of measure or weight and deliver to the Comptroller of Customs the aforesaid transit authorisation certificate.

*Cap. 37.*

### 4. Valid transit authorisation certificate.

No master or person in charge of a vessel shall receive on board in the course of transhipment any goods referred to in regulation 2 unless they are accompanied by a valid transit authorisation certificate in the form of the Fourth Schedule.

### 5. Offences and penalties.

*Amended by: L.N. 409 of 2007.*

(1) Any person failing to comply with any of these Regulations shall be guilty of an offence.

(2) Any person alleged to have committed an offence in sub-regulation (1) shall be tried by the Court of Magistrates (Malta) or the Court of Magistrates (Gozo), and shall on conviction be liable to imprisonment for a period not exceeding six months or to a fine (multa) not exceeding one thousand and one hundred and sixty-four euros and sixty-nine cents (1,164.69), or to both such imprisonment and fine.

(3) When any person is convicted of an offence under sub-regulation (1), the court shall order the forfeiture of the goods to which the offence relates, whether or not the owner thereof is a party to the proceedings.
