# Price Control of Fish Regulations (S.L. 117.12)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 117.12**

**PRICE CONTROL OF FISH REGULATIONS\***

15th July, 1957

GOVERNMENT NOTICE 394 of 1957, as amended by Government Notice 298 of 1958 and Legal Notice 116 of 1974.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Regulations is Price Control of Fish Regulations.

## 2. Applicability.

*Amended by: G.N. 298 of 1958; L.N. 116 of 1974. S.L. 117.15.*

(1) The Control of Sale of Commodities Regulations shall, but subject as hereunder, apply to fresh fish, whether locally caught or imported.

(2) The Director of Agriculture and Fisheries may, in respect of such fish, dispense with any specified requirements of the said regulations.

## 3. Director to fix prices.

*Substituted by: L.N. 116 of 1974.*

Notwithstanding the provisions of any other regulations, the Director of Agriculture and Fisheries may, from time to time, fix the maximum, minimum or actual price, or the maximum margin of profit, at which any type of fish may be sold, by an order published in the Gazette.

## 4. Maximum, minimum or actual prices of fish to be sold wholesale.

*Substituted by: L.N. 116 of 1974.*

The maximum, minimum or actual prices at which any type of fish may be sold wholesale by fishermen or pitkali shall be such as may be fixed by the Director of Agriculture and Fisheries from time to time by a notice to be published in the Gazette.

\*These Regulations have been repealed by Act XXXIV of 2023.
