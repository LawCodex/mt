# Importation Control Regulations (S.L. 117.14)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 117.14**

**IMPORTATION CONTROL REGULATIONS**

1st May, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 242 of 2004, as amended by Legal Notices 341 of 2004, 230 of 2005, 49 of 2006 and 409 of 2007.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Importation Control Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires -

"goods" means used and unused goods;

"importation" means the bringing of goods into Malta from any other country, by whatever means the carriage is effected;

"Minister" means the Minister responsible for trade and includes, to the extent of the authority given, any person authorized by the Minister in that behalf for any purpose of these regulations.

## 3. Importations subject to an authorization or import licence.

(1) Unless a person is in possession of a valid authorisation or import licence issued by the Minister, he shall not:

(a) transfer into Malta any goods from any Member State of the European Union, which are listed in the First Schedule;

(b) import any goods, originating in any country outside the European Union, which are listed in the Second Schedule;

(c) import any goods listed in the Third or Fourth Schedules, originating in the countries indicated by the country codes appearing in the fourth column of the respective schedules.

(2) For the purposes of subregulation (1), the country codes refer to those countries listed in the Fifth Schedule.

## 4. Granting of authorizations or import licences.

(1) An authorization or import licence granted by the Minister in pursuance of these regulations may be:

(a) limited so as to expire on a specified date unless renewed;

(b) subject to or without conditions;

(c) annulled, suspended, modified or revoked by the Minister.

(2) Nothing in these regulations shall be taken to prohibit the importation of any goods under the authority of an authorization or licence granted by the Minister, provided that all conditions attaching to the authorization or licence are complied with.

## 5. Importations under special circumstances.

(1) Without prejudice to the provisions of these regulations, no person may import any goods originating from any country, if such importation is not in conformity with a common position or joint action adopted by the Council of the European Union, or a binding resolution of the Security Council of the United Nations or a decision of the Organisation for the Security and Co-operation in Europe.

(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of these regulations, the importation of any goods manufactured, duplicated or otherwise reproduced or copied in violation of the rights of copyright enjoyed by any person and protected by Maltese law is hereby prohibited and no authorization or licence shall be granted for such importation.

## 6. Applications for authorizations or licences.

Applications for the grant of authorizations or licences for the importation of goods for the purposes of these regulations shall be made in such form and contain such particulars as the Minister may, from time to time, determine.

## 7. Authorization or licence to cease to have effect.

An authorization or licence issued under these regulations shall cease to have effect and any goods purported to have been imported thereunder shall be deemed to have been imported without an authorization or licence -

(a) if the goods to which the authorization or licence relates are not shipped within such date as may be specified to that effect in the authorization or licence, or, if no such date has been specified, within eight months from the date of the issue of the authorization or licence; or

(b) if the goods originate in or are consigned from a country other than that shown on the authorization or licence; or

(c) if the authorization or licence is used by a person or firm other than the one shown on the authorization or licence; or

(d) if the quantity of the goods imported exceeds that shown on the authorization or licence.

## 8. Penalties.

*Amended by: L.N. 409 of 2007.*

Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with these regulations shall be guilty of an offence and shall, on conviction, be liable -

(a) to a fine (multa) of not less than two hundred and thirty-two euros and ninety-four cents (232.94) but not exceeding one thousand and one hundred and sixty-four euros and sixty-nine cents (1,164.69) or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to both such fine and such imprisonment, when the value of the goods which have been imported, or in respect of the importation of which an attempt or a preparatory act has been made, does not exceed one thousand and one hundred and sixty-four euros and sixty-nine cents (1,164.69); or

(b) to a fine (multa) of not less than four hundred and sixty-five euros and eighty-seven cents (465.87) but not exceeding two thousand and three hundred and twenty-nine euros and thirty-seven cents (2,329.37) or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both such fine and such imprisonment, when the value of the goods which have been imported, or in respect of the importation of which an attempt or a preparatory act has been made, exceeds one thousand and one hundred and sixty-four euros and sixty-nine cents (1,164.69),

and in all cases the Court shall moreover order the forfeiture of the goods to which the offence relates, even though the owner thereof may not be a party to the proceedings.

## 9. False statements, etc.

*Amended by: L.N. 409 of 2007.*

If, for the purposes of obtaining an authorization or licence under these regulations, any person makes any statement or furnishes any document or information which to his knowledge is false in a material particular or recklessly makes any statement which is false in material particular, he shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable, on conviction, to a fine (multa) not exceeding one thousand and one hundred and sixty-four euros and sixty-nine cents (1,164.69) or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to both such fine and such imprisonment; and any authorization or licence which may have been granted in connection with the application for which the false statement was made or the false document or information was furnished shall be void as from the time when the authorization or licence was granted.
