# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the issue of €69,881,201.96 with the option to increase the offer to an amount not exceeding €93,174,935.94 6.25% Malta Government Stock 2011 (Second Issue) (S.L. 428.03)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 428.03**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF €69,881,201.96 WITH THE OPTION TO INCREASE THE OFFER TO AN AMOUNT NOT

**EXCEEDING €93,174,935.94 6.25% MALTA**

**GOVERNMENT STOCK 2011**

**SECOND ISSUE**

10th July, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 168 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the issue of €69,881,201.96 with an option to increase the offer to an amount not exceeding €93,174,935.94 6.25% Malta Government Stock 2011.

## 2. Mode of issuing stock.

This stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and, or entities are eligible to apply for this stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Styock Exchange. Cap. 345.

In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when the stock has been allotted, an application will be made for the stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the stock should not exceed €93,174,935.94.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €3.125 for every €100 stock will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 1st day of February and on the 1st day of August in every year for the duration of the stock. The first payment covering the period from the 2nd day of August, 2001 to the 1st day of February, 2002 shall be made on the 1st day of February, 2002.

## 7. Redemption of stock.

The last payment covering the period from the 2nd day of August, 2010 to the 1st day of February, 2011 shall be made on the redemption date 1st February, 2011.
