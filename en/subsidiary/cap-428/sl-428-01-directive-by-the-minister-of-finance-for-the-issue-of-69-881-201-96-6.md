# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the issue of €69,881,201.96 6.65% Malta Government Stock 2016 (First Issue) (S.L. 428.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 428.01**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF €69,881,201.96 6.65% MALTA

**GOVERNMENT STOCK 2016**

**FIRST ISSUE**

2nd March, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 43 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the issue of €69,881,201.96 6.65% Malta Government Stock 2016.

## 2. Mode of issuing stock.

The stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and, or entities are eligible to apply for this stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Styock Exchange. Cap. 345.

An application for the stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange will be made in terms of the Financial Markets Act, when the stock has been allotted.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the stock shall be €69,881,201.96

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €3.325 for every €100 stock will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 28th day of March and on the 28th day of September in every year for the duration of the stock. The first payment covering the period from the 29th day of March, 2001 to the 28th day of September, 2001 shall be made on the 28th day of September, 2001.

## 7. Redemption of stock.

The stock shall be redeemed at par on such date between the 1st January, 2016 and the 31st December, 2016 (both dates inclusive) as shall be determined by the Accountant General and notified in the Gazette.
