# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an amount not exceeding €69,881,201.96 5.9% Malta Government Stock 2009 (Third Issue) Fungibility Issue (S.L. 428.05)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 428.05**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN AMOUNT NOT EXCEEDING

**€69,881,201.96 5.9% MALTA GOVERNMENT**

**STOCK 2009**

**THIRD ISSUE**

26th October, 2001

LEGAL NOTICE 248 of 2001, as amended by Legal Notice 426 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the issue of an amount not exceeding €69,881,201.96 5.9% Malta Government Stock 2009.

## 2. Mode of issuing stock.

This stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 3. Allotment policy.

All persons and, or entities are eligible to apply for this stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

(a) In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when the stock has been allotted, an application will be made for the stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

(b) On the 30th March, 2002 after the payment of the first dividend, this stock shall be integrated with the 5.9% Malta Government Stock (III) and shall thereafter be deemed to be one stock.

## 5. Sum of money to be raised.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

The sum of money to be raised by the stock should not exceed €69,881,201.96.

## 6. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

*Amended by: L.N. 426 of 2007.*

Interest at the rate of €2.95 for every €100 stock will be payable half-yearly in arrear on the 30th day of March and on the 30th day of September in every year for the duration of the stock. The first pro rata payment covering the period from the 20th day of November, 2001 to the 30th day of March, 2002 shall be made on the 30th day of March, 2002.

## 7. Redemption of stock.

The last pro rata payment covering the period from the 1st day of October, 2008 to the 27th day of March, 2009 shall be made on the redemption date 27th March, 2009.
