# Carriage of Goods by Sea (Exemption) Regulations (S.L. 283.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 283.01**

CARRIAGE OF GOODS BY SEA (EXEMPTION)

**REGULATIONS**

12th September, 1980

LEGAL NOTICE 80 of 1980.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Carriage of Goods by Sea (Exemption) Regulations.

## 2. Exemptions. Cap. 283.

The provisions of the Carriage of Goods by Sea (Regulation) Act shall not apply to:

bulk cargo;

goods landed at Malta for oncarriage or transhipment to another country;

live stock;

fish from fishing vessels.
