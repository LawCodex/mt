# Disestablishment of Air Traffic Control Corps and Airport Company Order (S.L. 220.09)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 220.09**

**DISESTABLISHMENT OF AIR TRAFFIC**

**CONTROL CORPS AND AIRPORT COMPANY**

**ORDER**

1st May, 1998 LEGAL NOTICE 112 of 1998.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Disestablishment of Air Traffic Control Corps and Airport Company Order.

## 2. Disestablishment of Air Traffic Control Corps and Airport Company. Cap. 220.

It is hereby declared that the Air Traffic Control Corps and the Airport Company of the Armed Forces of Malta are hereby disestablished and are henceforth no longer Corps for the purpose of the Malta Armed Forces Act and regulations made thereunder.
