## 1. Title.

The title of this Order is Employment for the purposes of the Pensions Ordinance Order.

## 2. Designation in terms of article 8A of the Pensions Ordinance. Cap. 93.

The companies and, or entities listed in the Schedule to this Order are being designated as companies and, or entities with which an officer or employee who terminates Government service may take up full time employment in terms and for the purposes of article 8A of the Pensions Ordinance .
