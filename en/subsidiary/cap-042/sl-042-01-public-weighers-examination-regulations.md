# Public Weighers Examination Regulations (S.L. 42.1)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 42.01**

**PUBLIC WEIGHERS’ EXAMINATION**

**REGULATIONS**

18th April, 1935 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 145 of 1935, as amended by: Legal Notices 49 of 1974 and 13 of 1977; Act XIII of 1983; and Legal Notice 407 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Added by: L.N. 49 of 1974.*

The title of these regulations is the Public Weighers’ Examination Regulations.

## 2. Examinations.

*Substituted by: L.N. 49 of 1974. Amended by: XIII. 1983.5; L.N. 407 of 2007.*

(1) Applicants for a licence to act as a public weigher shall be examined in the subjects as detailed in the Schedule and shall undergo a practical test in the work of the public weighers.

(2) No person shall be admitted to such examination unless - (a) he is above eighteen years of age on the day of the examination;

(b) he is of good conduct; and

(c) he produces satisfactory evidence that he has obtained six months’ experience under an authorised public weigher in the management of the steelyard and balance.

(3) Applications accompanied by certificates referred to in sub-regulation (2)(a), (b) and (c) should be addressed to the Director of Eduction.

(4) An admission fee of fifty-eight cents (0.58) shall be paid by every candidate before being admitted to the examination.
