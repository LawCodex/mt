# Directive by the Minister of Finance, the Economy and Investment for the Issue of Euro 30,000,000 Malta Government Stocks (S.L. 502.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 502.01**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE, THE ECONOMY AND INVESTMENT FOR THE ISSUE OF EURO 30,000,000 MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCKS 16th July, 2010

LEGAL NOTICE 361 of 2010.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance, the Economy and Investment for the Issue of Euro 30,000,000 Malta Government Stocks.

## 2. Issuing of Stock.

An amount of Euro 30,000,000 Malta Government Stocks shall be issued in Floating Rate Malta Government Stock 2013 (VI) linked to the six-month Euribor.

## 3. Mode of issuing Stocks.

The Stock shall be issued as set out in the prospectus.

## 4. Allotment policy.

All persons and, or entities are eligible to apply for this Stock. Allotment will be applied as set out in the prospectus.

## 5. Trading on the Malta Stock Exchange. Cap. 345.

In terms of the Financial Markets Act, when this Stock has been allotted, an application shall be made for this Stock to be placed on the official list and for trading on the Malta Stock Exchange.

## 6. Sum of money to be raised.

The sum of money to be raised shall be Euro 30,000,000.

## 7. Rate of interest and dates of payment.

Interest shall be paid at the floating rate linked to the six-month Euribor in effect on the applicable interest determination date, plus a spread of 45 basis points (bps) calculated on Day Count Fraction (Actual/360) for every Euro 100. The spread of 45bps is fixed and applicable until the maturity of the Stock. Interest shall be payable half yearly in arrears on the 11th of February and 11th of August in every year for the duration of the Stock. The first payment covering the period from the 11th August, 2010 to the 10th February, 2011 (both days inclusive) shall be made on the 11th day of February, 2011.

## 8. Redemption of Stock.

The Floating Rate Malta Government Stock 2013 (VI) linked to the six-month Euribor shall be redeemed at par on the 11th August, 2013.
