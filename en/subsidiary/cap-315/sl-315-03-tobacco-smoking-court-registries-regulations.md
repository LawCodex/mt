# Tobacco Smoking (Court Registries) Regulations (S.L. 315.3)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 315.03**

**TOBACCO SMOKING (COURT REGISTRIES)**

**REGULATIONS**

1st November, 2002

LEGAL NOTICE 325 of 2002, as amended by Legal Notice 424 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Tobacco Smoking (Court Registries) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations -

''tobacco product'' means any product manufactured, even partly, from tobacco and intended for use by smoking, but does neither include smokeless tobacco which is intended for use by mastication, and including nasal and oral snuff, nor any medicine that is sold or supplied wholly or principally for use as an aid in giving up smoking.

## 3. Prohibition of tobacco smoking in Court registries.

No person shall at any time smoke any tobacco product within the precincts of the registries of the Superior Courts, Civil or Criminal, and of the Inferior Courts in Malta, and of the Court of Magistrates (Gozo).

## 4. Offences and penalties.

*Amended by: L.N. 424 of 2007.*

Any person who contravenes the provisions of these regulations shall be guilty of an offence and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine (multa) of not less than eleven euros and sixty-five cents (11.65) and not exceeding one hundred and sixteen euros and forty-seven cents (116.47).
