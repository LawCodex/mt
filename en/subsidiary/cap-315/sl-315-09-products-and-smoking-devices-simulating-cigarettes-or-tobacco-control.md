## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Products and Smoking Devices (Simulating Cigarettes or Tobacco) (Control) Regulations.

## 2. Interpretation.

In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires:

*Cap. 315.*

"the Act" means the Tobacco (Smoking Control) Act;

"tobacco devices" includes any product bearing the name cigarette or tobacco which is intended as a substitute to a conventional tobacco product or smoking requisite and includes any non-nicotine device, but excludes any pharmaceutical nicotine delivery devices.

## 3. Tobacco devices to comply with the Act and regulations.

All tobacco devices are to comply with the provisions of the Act and any regulations made there under in s o far as advertising and smoking in public places are concerned.
