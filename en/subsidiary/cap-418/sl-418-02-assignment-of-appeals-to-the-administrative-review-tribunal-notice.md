# Assignment of Appeals to the Administrative Review Tribunal Notice (S.L. 418.02)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 418.02**

**ASSIGNMENT OF APPEALS TO THE**

**ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW TRIBUNAL**

**NOTICE**

26th August, 2014

LEGAL NOTICE 297 of 2014.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Notice is the Assignment of Appeals to the Administrative Review Tribunal Notice.

## 2. Assignment of appeals.

The Administrative Review Tribunal is hereby assigned the hearing and determination of all the appeals which, on the 1st June 2012, were pending for a decision before the Telecommunications Appeals Board and before the Communications Appeals Board.
