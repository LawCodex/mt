# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm2,250,000 6.3% Malta Government Stock 1999 (Fifth Issue) (S.L. 388.15)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 388.15**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM2,250,000 6.3% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 1999 (FIFTH

**ISSUE)**

8th October, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 187 of 1996.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm2,250,000 6.3% Malta Government Stock 1999 (Fifth Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm3,000,000 6.3% Malta Government Stock 1999 (V) an additional issue of Lm2,250,000 6.3% Malta Government Stock 1999 (V) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6.3% Malta Government Stock 1999 (V) created by means of Legal Notice 175 of 1996 to a total amount of Lm5,250,000.

*S.L.388.13*
