# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm1,500,000 6.3% Malta Government Stock 1999 (Third Issue) (S.L. 388.04)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 388.04**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM1,500,000 6.3% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 1999

**(THIRD ISSUE)**

3rd May, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 80 of 1996.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm1,500,000 6.3% Malta Government Stock 1999 (Third Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm7,500,000 6.3% Malta Government Stock 1999 (III) an additional issue of Lm1,500,000 6.3% Malta Government Stock 1999 (III) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6.3% Malta Government Stock 1999 (III) created by means of Legal Notice 56 of 1996 to a total amount of Lm9,000,000.

*S.L.388.01*
