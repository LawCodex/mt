# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional €6,988,120.20 7.5% Malta Government Stock 2011 (First Issue) (S.L. 388.16)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 388.16**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL €6,988,120.20

**7.5% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2011**

**(FIRST ISSUE)**

8th October, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 188 of 1996, as amended by Legal Notice 425 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional €6,988,120.20 7.5% Malta Government Stock 2011 (First Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

*Amended by: L.N. 425 of 2007.*

It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in €27,952,480.78 7.5% M alta Government Stock 2011 (I) an additional issue of €6,988,120.20 7.5% Malta Government Stock 2011 (I) be issued thus bringing the amount of 7.5% Malta Government Stock 2011 (I) created by means of Legal Notice 176 of 1996 to a total amount of €34,940,600.98.
