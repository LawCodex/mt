# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm9,250,000 7.25% Malta Government Stock 2006 (Second Issue) (S.L. 388.06)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 388.06**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM9,250,000

**7.25% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2006**

**(SECOND ISSUE)**

3rd May, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 82 of 1996.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm9,250,000 7.25% Malta Government Stock 2006 (Second Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm10,000,000 7.25% Malta Government Stock 2006 (II) an additional issue of Lm9,250,000 7.25% Malta Government Stock 2006 (II) be issued thus bringing the amount of 7.25% Malta Government Stock 2006 (II) created by means of Legal Notice 58 of 1996 to a total amount of Lm19,250,000.

*S.L.388.03*
