# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm5,000,000 7.25% Malta Government Stock 2006 (Third Issue) (S.L. 388.12)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 388.12**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM5,000,000

**7.25% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2006**

**(THIRD ISSUE)**

30th July, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 146 of 1996.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm5,000,000 7.25% Malta Government Stock 2006 (Third Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm10,000,000 7.25% Malta Government Stock 2006 (III) an additional issue of Lm5,000,000 7.25% Malta Government Stock 2006 (III) be issued thus bringing the amount of 7.25% Malta Government Stock 2006 (III) created by means of Legal Notice 127 of 1996 to a total amount of Lm15,000,000.

*S.L.388.09*
