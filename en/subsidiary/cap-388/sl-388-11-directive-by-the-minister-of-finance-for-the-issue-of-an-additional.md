# Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm3,500,000 6.75% Malta Government Stock 2001 (Fourth Issue) (S.L. 388.11)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 388.11**

DIRECTIVE BY THE MINISTER OF FINANCE FOR THE ISSUE OF AN ADDITIONAL LM3,500,000

**6.75% MALTA GOVERNMENT STOCK 2001**

**(FOURTH ISSUE)**

30th July, 1996

LEGAL NOTICE 145 of 1996.

## 1. Title.

The title of this Directive is Directive by the Minister of Finance for the Issue of an additional Lm3,500,000 6.75% Malta Government Stock 2001 (Fourth Issue).

## 2. Additional issue.

It is hereby authorised that due to oversubscription in Lm4,000,000 6.75% Malta Government Stock 2001 (IV) an additional issue of Lm3,500,000 6.75% Malta Government Stock 2001 (IV) be issued thus bringing the amount of 6.75% Malta Government Stock 2001 (IV) created by means of Legal Notice 126 of 1996 to a total amount of Lm7,500,000.

*S.L.388.08*
