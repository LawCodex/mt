# Immigration Appeals Board (Division) Regulations (S.L. 217.13)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 217.13**

**IMMIGRATION APPEALS BOARD (DIVISION)**

**REGULATIONS**

1st July, 2011

LEGAL NOTICE 246 of 2011, as amended by Legal Notices 82 of 2023 and 169 of 2025.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Immigration Appeals Board (Division) Regulations.

## 2. Divisions of the Immigration Appeals Board.

*Substituted by: L.N. 82 of 2023; L.N. 169 of 2025.*

The number of Divisions of the Immigration Appeals Board shall be determined by the Minister responsible for immigration on the basis of the number of cases to be adjudicated:

Provided that one of the divisions shall operate on a full-time basis.

## 3. Assignment of cases between divisions.

*Substituted by: L.N. 82 of 2023. L.N. 169 of 2025.*

The assignment of cases between the divisions of the Immigration Appeals Board shall be made by the Secretary of the division operating on a full-time basis, in such a manner that all divisions shall, as far as possible, be assigned an equal number of cases.

## 4. Remuneration.

*Added by: L.N. 169 of 2025.*

The remuneration paid to members of the Board shall be in accordance with the applicable directives and guidelines issued by the Government from time to time.
