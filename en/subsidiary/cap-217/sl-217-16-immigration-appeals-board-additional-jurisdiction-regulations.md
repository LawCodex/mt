# Immigration Appeals Board (Additional Jurisdiction) Regulations (S.L. 217.16)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 217.16**

**IMMIGRATION APPEALS BOARD**

**(ADDITIONAL JURISDICTION) REGULATIONS**

6th January, 2012

LEGAL NOTICE 2 of 2012, as amended by Legal Notice 133 of 2017.

## 1. Title.

The title of these regulations is the Immigration Appeals Board (Additional Jurisdiction) Regulations.

## 2. Competence to hear appeals relating to refusal, annulment or revocation of visa.

The Immigration Appeals Board has the competence to hear appeals related to the refusal, annulment or revocation of the visa with reference to the provisions of paragraph (3) of Article 32 and paragraph (7) of Article 34 of the Regulation (EC) No. 810/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 establishing a Community Code on Visas (Visa Code).
