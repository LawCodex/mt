# Credit Institutions (Reorganisation and Winding-up) Regulations (S.L. 371.12)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 371.12**

CREDIT INSTITUTIONS (REORGANISATION AND WINDING-UP) REGULATIONS

1st May, 2004

LEGAL NOTICE 228 of 2004, as amended by Legal Notices 298 of 2015 and 149 of 2016.

### 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Credit Institutions (Reorganisation and Winding-up) Regulations.

### 2. Scope.

*Amended by: L.N. 298 of 2015; L.N. 149 of 2016.*

(1) These regulations apply to Maltese credit institutions and their branches located in other Member States or EEA States and Part IV of these regulations applies to branches present in Malta of credit institutions authorised in another Member State. These regulations shall not apply to those branches of credit institutions that are situated in a Member State or an EEA State in which they have their head offices.

(2) In the event of application of the resolution tools and exercise of the resolution powers provided for in the Act and any relevant regulations made thereunder transposing the BRRD, these regulations shall also apply to the financial institutions, firms and parent undertakings falling within the scope of the BRRD in the event of application of the resolution tools and exercise of the resolution powers provided for in the said Directive.

(3) The provisions of these regulations do not apply to post office giro institutions or to the Central Bank of Malta or to any central bank of any other Member State or EEA State.

(4) The institutions listed in the Schedule shall not be considered European credit institutions for the purposes of these regulations, and the provisions of these regulations do not apply to them.

(5) The provisions of these regulations relating specifically to the branches of a credit institution not having a head office within a Member State or an EEA State apply only where such institution has branches in at least two States being either a Member State or and EEA State.

(6) Where a credit institution is either under resolution or where the conditions for resolution have been determined by the Resolution Committee to be met, normal insolvency proceedings shall not be commenced except at the initiative of the Resolution Committee, and a decision placing a credit institution into normal insolvency proceedings shall be taken only with the consent of the Resolution Committee as provided for in regulation 86 of the Recovery and Resolution Regulations made under the Malta Financial Services Authority Act.

*S.L. 330.09 Cap. 330.*

(7) These regulations shall only apply to reorganisation measures or winding-up proceedings adopted immediately after the date of coming into force of these regulations.

## PART I - REORGANISATION MEASURES

### 4. Adoption of reorganisation measures in Malta. Cap. 386. Cap. 383.

(1) The Court shall, upon receipt of an application made in terms of the Companies Act, or the Controlled Companies (Procedure for Liquidation) Act, and the Act, decide on the implementation of reorganisation measures with respect to a Maltese credit institution including its branches established in host States.

(2) Reorganisation measures adopted by the Court in respect of a Maltese credit institution including its branches in any other host State shall, unless expressly provided otherwise in these regulations, be applied in terms of the Companies Act, the Controlled Companies (Procedure for Liquidation) Act and the Act as appropriate.

*Cap. 386. Cap. 383.*

(3) Upon reorganisation measures taken pursuant to these regulations becoming effective in Malta they shall be immediately fully effective in any other Member State or EEA State without any further formalities, including formalities as against third parties in any Member State or EEA States, even if the legislation of such host State applicable to such third parties, does not provide for such reorganisation measures, or alternatively makes their implementation subject to conditions which are not fulfilled.

(4) Nothing in these regulations shall prejudice the powers of the competent authority in relation to reorganisation measures with respect to a Maltese credit institution including its branches established in host States, where provided for in article 29 of the Act.

### 7. Publication of Court decision on reorganisation measures in interests of third parties right of redress.

(1) Where a decision on reorganisation measures adopted by the Court is subject to a right of redress and its implementation is likely to affect the rights of third parties, upon the Court’s taking such decision, the administrator or any other person appointed by the Court in relation to such measure, shall at the earliest opportunity publish an extract from the Court’s decision in -

(a) the Malta Government Gazette;

(b) any two Maltese newspapers, provided that one extract is published in the English language and the other is published in the Maltese language;

(c) the Official Journal of the European Communities, provided that third party rights in any host State are capable of being affected by the reorganisation measures; and

(d) two national newspapers in each host State, provided that third party rights in the host State are capable of being affected by the reorganisation measures.

(2) The publication made pursuant to subregulation (1) shall at least specify -

(a) the purpose and legal basis of the Court’s decision taken;

(b) the time limits for lodging requests for redress to the Court;

(c) a clearly understandable indication of the date of expiry of the time limit for lodging requests for redress to the Court; and

*Cap. 386.*

(d) the details of Court and that it is competent to hear the grievances of persons stipulated under the Companies Act or any other applicable law.

(3) The information set out in subregulation (1) shall be published in English or in both English and Maltese as well as, where applicable, in an official language of the host State in which the information is published.

(4) The reorganisation measures apply regardless of the provisions concerning publication set out in subregulations (1) and (2) and are fully effective against creditors, unless otherwise provided by law or by the administrative or judicial authorities.

## PART II - WINDING-UP PROCEEDINGS

### 10. Adoption of winding-up proceedings in Malta. Cap. 386. Cap. 383.

(1) The Court shall, upon receipt of an application in terms of the Companies Act or the Controlled Companies (Procedure for Liquidation) Act and the Act, decide on opening of winding-up proceedings in respect of a Maltese credit institution, including its branches established in any Member State or EEA State.

(2) The Court’s decision concerning the dissolution and winding-up of a Maltese credit institution including its branches in any host State shall be recognised within the territory of any other Member State or EEA State without any further formalities and shall be effective there as soon as the Court’s decision is effective in Malta.

(3) (a) The Court shall, upon receipt of an application for any winding-up proceedings, inform the competent authority regarding this request in order to obtain its views on the adoption of such measures and the practical effects which they may have.

(b) Where the court makes a decision, order or appointment of any of the following kinds -

*Cap. 386.*

(i) a winding-up order under article 219 of the Companies Act; or

*Cap. 386.*

(ii) the appointment of a provisional administrator under article 228 of the Companies Act,

it must immediately inform the competent authority, or cause the competent authority to be informed without delay of the decision, order or appointment which has been made.

(4) Where the competent authority is informed of a decision, order or appointment in accordance with this regulation, the competent authority shall in turn, without delay and by any means available inform the foreign authority in all Member States or EEA States that the decision, order or appointment has been made and the possible effects that of a decision, order or appointment of that kind may have.

*Cap. 386.*

(5) Where an arrangement has been concluded pursuant to article 291 of the Companies Act, any person appointed by the Maltese credit institution or creditors shall inform the competent authority of the arrangement which has been approved.

*Cap. 386.*

(6) Where a liquidator is appointed as mentioned in article 230 of the Companies Act the liquidator must inform the competent authority of his appointment.

*Cap. 386.*

(7) In the case of a member’s voluntary winding-up, the liquidator shall in article 272 of the Companies Act inform the competent authority if he is of the opinion that the Maltese credit institution will not be able to pay its debts within the period stated in the declaration of solvency.

(8) The duties provided for in this regulation are supplemental to and without prejudice to the duties of the Registrar of Courts in articles 224 and 264 of the Companies Act.

*Cap. 386.*

(9) Nothing in these regulations shall prejudice the duties and powers of the competent authority in relation to winding-up proceedings with respect to a Maltese credit institution including its branches established in host States, where provided for in the Act or in the Controlled Companies (Procedure for Liquidation) Act.

*Cap. 383.*

### 11. Applicable law in the winding-up of a Maltese credit institution.

(1) Subject to the exceptions expressly provided for in these regulations, the decision to dissolve and wind up a Maltese credit institution as well as its method of winding-up and dissolution, shall be made in accordance with the general provisions of Part V of the Companies Act and in particular with article 29 of the Act and the provisions of the Controlled Companies (Procedures for Liquidation) Act.

*Cap. 386. Cap. 383.*

(2) In relation to a Maltese credit institution, the law of Malta shall be the applicable law and shall determine in particular:

(a) the assets subject to administration and the treatment of assets acquired by the credit institution after the opening of winding-up proceedings;

(b) the respective powers of the Maltese credit institution and the liquidator;

(c) the conditions under which set-off may be invoked;

(d) the effects of the winding-up proceedings on current contracts to which the Maltese credit institution is party;

(e) the effects of the winding-up proceedings brought by individual creditors, with the exception of lawsuits pending as provided for in these regulations;

(f) the claims which are to be lodged against the Maltese credit institution and the treatment of claims arising after the opening of winding-up proceedings;

(g) the rules governing the lodging, verification and admission of claims;

(h) the rules governing the distribution of proceeds from the realisation of assets, the ranking of claims, the rights of creditors who have obtained partial satisfaction after the opening of the winding-up order by virtue of a right in rem or through set-off;

(i) the conditions for and the effects of the closure of winding-up proceedings, in particular by composition;

(j) the rights of creditors after the closure of the winding-up proceedings;

(k) who is to bear the cost and expenses incurred in the winding-up proceedings; and

(l) the rules relating to the voidness, violability or unenforceability of legal acts detrimental to all the creditors.

### 12. Consultation with competent authority before voluntary winding-up.

(1) Before any voluntary winding-up decision is taken by the board of directors or other governing bodies of a credit institution these said governing bodies shall consult the competent authority in the most appropriate form.

(2) The commencement of the voluntary winding-up of a credit institution shall not preclude the adoption of reorganisation measures or the opening of winding-up proceedings by the Court.

### 13. Withdrawal of a credit institution’s authorisation. Cap. 386.

(1) Where the Court decides upon the opening of winding-up proceedings either in the absence of reorganisation measures or in terms of article 329B(12) of the Companies Act following the failure of reorganisation measures, the authorisation of the credit institution shall be withdrawn by the competent authority in terms of the provisions of article 9 of the Act.

(2) In the event of the withdrawal of authorisation the competent authority shall, in terms of article 9(7) of the Act inform the foreign authority of the host State so as to enable it to take appropriate measures to prevent the credit institution concerned from initiating further transactions within its territory and to safeguard the interests of depositors.

(3) The withdrawal of authorisation provided for in this regulation shall not prevent the person or persons entrusted with the winding-up from carrying on some of the credit institution’s activities insofar as that is necessary or appropriate for the purposes of winding-up:

Provided that, such activities shall be carried under the supervision of the competent authority.

### 14. Publication of decision to open winding-up procedures by liquidator or Court.

(1) The liquidator or any other person appointed for this purpose by the Court shall at the earliest opportunity make their decision to open winding-up proceedings public by publishing an extract from the decision ordering the winding-up proceedings in:

(a) the Malta Government Gazette;

(b) any two Maltese newspapers, provided that one extract is published in the English language and the other is published in the Maltese language;

(c) the Official Journal of the European Communities, provided that third party rights in any host State are capable of being affected by the winding-up proceedings; and

(d) two national newspapers in each host State, provided that third party rights in the host State are capable of being affected by the winding-up proceedings.

(2) The publication made pursuant to subregulation (1)(a) to (d), shall at least specify:

(a) that winding-up proceedings have commenced in relation to a Maltese credit institution;

(b) the applicable time limits for the winding-up proceedings, if any;

(c) the penalties laid down in regards to any time limits, if any;

(d) the full address of the body or authority empowered to accept claims or observations relating to claims and the other measures laid down; and

(e) as to whether creditors whose claims are preferential or secured in rem need lodge their claims.

(3) The information set out in subregulation (1) shall be published in English or in both English and Maltese.

(4) A form shall also be used bearing, in all the official languages of the European Union, the heading ''Invitation to lodge a claim. Time limits to be observed.''.

(5) The duties provided for in this regulation are without prejudice to the duties of the Registrar of Courts in article 401 of the Companies Act and to the duties of the Controller in article 4 of the Controlled Companies (Procedure for Liquidation) Act.

*Cap. 386. Cap. 383.*

### 15. Information to known creditors.

(1) When winding-up proceedings have commenced, the liquidator shall without delay individually inform all known creditors wheresoever resident or domiciled, in writing:

(a) that winding-up proceedings have commenced in relation to a Maltese credit institution;

(b) of the applicable time limits for the winding-up proceedings, if any;

(c) of the penalties laid down in regards to any time limits, if any;

(d) of the body or authority empowered to accept claims or observations relating to claims and the other measures laid down; and

(e) as to whether creditors whose claims are preferential or secured in rem need lodge their claims.

*Cap. 386.*

(2) Where the liquidator shall in terms of the Companies Act advise creditors as to inter alia the date and venue of any creditors meeting to which the addressee may be entitled to attend such notice in subregulation (1) shall be given without prejudice the provisions of article 296 of the Companies Act.

(3) The information set out in subregulation (1) shall be issued in English or in both English and Maltese.

(4) A form shall also be used bearing, in all the official languages of the European Union, the heading ''Invitation to lodge a claim. Time limits to be observed.''.

*Cap. 383.*

(5) The provisions of this regulation are without prejudice to the duties of the Controller in article 4 of the Controlled Companies (Procedure for Liquidation) Act.

### 16. Reports to creditors.

(1) A liquidator shall send a written report setting out the position in particular as regards the progress of the winding-up once every twelve months, beginning from the date when the liquidator’s appointment has effect, to every known creditor whosesoever resident or domiciled.

(2) Where the official receiver is acting as a liquidator the first such report to creditors is to contain the information required in terms of article 227 of the Companies Act.

*Cap. 386.*

(3) The requirement in subregulation (1) does not apply where a liquidator is required by order of the Court to send reports to creditors at intervals which are more frequent than those required by this regulation.

*Cap. 386. Cap. 383.*

(4) The duties provided for in this regulation are without prejudice to article 234 of the Companies Act and where applicable are supplemental to the duties of the Controller under the Controlled Companies (Procedure for Liquidation) Act.

### 17. Honouring of obligations.

(1) Where an obligation has been honoured for the benefit of a credit institution which is not a legal person and which is the subject of winding-up proceedings opened in another Member State or EEA State when it should have been honoured for the benefit of the liquidator in those proceedings, the person honouring the obligation shall be deemed to have discharged it if he was unaware of the opening of proceedings.

(2) Where such an obligation is honoured before the publication provided for in regulation 14 has been effected, the person honouring the obligation shall be presumed, in the absence of proof to the contrary, to have been unaware of the opening of winding-up proceedings.

(3) Where the obligation is honoured after the publication provided for in regulation 14 has been effected, the person honouring the obligation shall be presumed, in the absence of proof to the contrary, to have been aware of the opening of proceedings.

### 18. Right of creditors, etc., to lodge claims.

(1) Any creditor, including the public authorities of a Member State or a EEA State, being domiciled in or having a normal place of residence or head-office in a Member State or EEA State other than Malta, shall have all the rights provided to creditors in Part V of the Companies Act to lodge claims or to submit written observations relating to claims in relation to a Maltese credit institution being wound up.

*Cap. 386.*

(2) The claims of any creditor who is domiciled in or having a normal place of residence or head offices in any Member State or EEA States other than Malta, shall be treated in the same way as any creditor resident or domiciled or having a head offices in Malta and accorded the same ranking as claims of an equivalent nature which may be lodged by creditors having their domiciles, normal places of residence, or head offices in Malta.

(3) When lodging a claim a creditor shall send copies of supporting documents, if any, and shall indicate:

(b) the date on which it arose and its amount;

## PART III – PROVISIONS COMMON TO REORGANISATION — AND WINDING-UP PROCEEDINGS

### 20. Employment contracts and relationships.

The effects of a reorganisation measure or winding-up proceedings of a credit institution on employment contracts and employment relationships are determined in accordance with the laws of the Member State or EEA State to which that contract or relationship is subject.

### 21. Contracts in connection with immovable property.

The effects of a reorganisation measure or winding-up proceedings of a credit institution on a contract conferring the right to make use of or acquire immovable property situated within the territory of a Member State or EEA State are to be governed in accordance with the law of that Member State or EEA State.

### 22. Registrable rights.

Rights of a credit institution in respect of immovable property, a ship or an aircraft subject to registration in a public register shall be governed solely by the law of the Member State or EEA State under the authority of which the register is kept.

### 23. Third parties rights in rem.

(1) The adoption of reorganisation measures or the opening of winding-up proceedings of a Maltese credit institution shall not affect the rights in rem of creditors or third parties in respect of tangible or intangible, movable or immovable assets including both specific assets and collections of indefinite assets as a whole which change from time to time belonging to the credit institution concerned which are situated within the territory of a Member State or EEA State at the time of the opening of such measures or proceedings.

(2) The rights in rem referred to in subregulation (1) shall in particular mean -

(a) the right to transfer assets or have them transferred and to obtain satisfaction from the proceeds of or the income from those assets, in particular by virtue of a lien, mortgage or hypothec;

(b) the exclusive right to have a claim met, in particular a right guaranteed by a lien in respect of the claim or by assignment of the claim by way of a guarantee;

(c) the right to demand the assets from, and, or to require restitution, by anyone having possession or use of them contrary to the wishes of the party so entitled;

(d) a right in rem to the beneficial use of assets.

(3) A right, recorded in a public register and enforceable against third parties, under which a right in rem within the meaning of subregulation (1) may be obtained, is also to be treated as a right in rem for the purposes of this regulation.

(4) Subregulation (1) does not preclude actions for voidness, voidability or unenforceability of legal acts detrimental to creditors referred to in regulation 11(2)(l).

### 24. Reservation of title agreement.

(1) The commencement of reorganisation measures or winding-up proceedings in relation to a Maltese credit institution purchasing an asset shall not affect the seller’s rights based on a reservation of title where at the time of the commencement of such measures or proceedings the asset is situated within the territory of another Member State or EEA State.

(2) The commencement of reorganisation or winding-up proceedings against a Maltese credit institution selling an asset, after delivery of the asset, shall not constitute grounds for rescinding or terminating the sale and shall not prevent the purchaser from acquiring title where at the time of the opening of such measure or proceedings the asset sold is situated within the territory of a Member State or EEA State in which such measures or proceedings were commenced.

(3) Subregulations (1) and (2) do not preclude actions for voidness, voidability or unenforceability of legal acts detrimental to creditors referred to in regulation 11(2)(l).

### 25. Creditor’s rights to set off.

(1) The adoption of reorganisation measures or the opening of winding-up proceedings shall not affect the rights of creditors to demand the set-off of their claims against the claims of the Maltese credit institution where such a set-off is permitted by the law of the Member State or EEA State which is applicable to the claim of the Maltese credit institution.

(2) This regulation does not preclude actions for voidness, voidability or unenforceability of legal acts detrimental to creditors referred to in regulation 11(2)(l), above.

### 26. Lex rei sitae.

The enforcement of proprietary rights in instruments or other rights in such instruments the existence or transfer of which presupposes their recording in a register, an account or a centralised deposit system held or located in a Member State or EEA State shall be governed by the law of the Member State or EEA State where the register, account, or centralised deposit system in which those rights are recorded is held or located.

### 27. Netting agreements.

*Substituted by: L.N. 298 of 2015. Cap. 459. S.L. 330.09*

Pursuant to the provisions of the Set-Off and Netting on Insolvency Act, and without prejudice to regulations 68 and 71 of the Recovery and Resolution Regulations, netting agreements shall be governed solely by the law of the contract which governs such agreements.

### 28. Repurchase agreements.

*Substituted by: L.N. 298 of 2015. S.L. 330.09*

Without prejudice to regulations 68 and 71 of the Recovery and Resolution Regulations, and to regulation 26 of these regulations, repurchase agreements shall be governed solely by the law of the contract which governs such agreements.

### 29. Regulated markets.

Without prejudice to regulation 26, transactions carried out in the context of a regulated market are to be governed solely in accordance with the law of the contract which governs such transactions.

### 30. Proof of liquidators’ appointment.

(1) The administrator or liquidator’s appointment shall be evidenced by a certified copy of the original decision appointing him or her or by any other certificate issued by the administrative or judicial authority of the home State attesting to the due appointment of the administrator or liquidator.

(2) A translation into the official language or one of the official languages of the Member State or EEA State within the territory of which the administrator or liquidator wishes to act may be required. No legislation or other similar formality shall be required.

(3) Administrators and liquidators shall be entitled to exercise within the territory of any Member State or EEA State all the powers which they are entitled to exercise within the territory of the home State. They may also appoint persons to assist or, where appropriate, represent them in the course of the reorganisation measure or winding-up proceedings, in particular in host States and, specifically in order to help overcome any difficulties encountered by creditors in the host State.

(4) In exercising their powers, administrators and liquidators shall comply with the law of the Member State or EEA State within the territory of which they wish to take action, in particular with regard to procedures for the realisation of assets and the provision of information to employees. Those powers may not include the use of force or the right to rule on legal proceedings or disputes.

### 31. Detrimental acts.

(1) Regulation 11 shall not apply as regards the rules relating to the voidness, voidability or unenforceability of legal acts detrimental to the creditors as a whole, where the beneficiary of these acts provides proof that:

(a) the act detrimental to the creditors as a whole is subject to the law of a Member State or EEA State other than the home State, and

(b) that law does not allow any means of challenging that act in the case in point.

(2) Where a reorganisation measure decided on by a judicial authority provides for rules relating to the voidness, voidability or unenforceability of legal acts detrimental to the creditors as a whole performed before adoption of the measure, regulation 4(2) shall not apply in the cases provided for in this regulation.

### 32. Protection of third party purchasers.

Where, by an act concluded after the adoption of a reorganisation measure or the opening of winding-up proceedings, a credit institution disposes, for consideration of -

(a) an immovable asset situated within the territory of a Member State or EEA State;

(b) a ship or an aircraft subject to registration in a public register kept under the authority of a Member State or EEA State; or

(c) instruments or rights in such instruments the existence or transfer of which presupposes their being recorded in a register, an account or a centralised deposit system held or located in a Member State or EEA State,

the validity of that act shall be governed by the law of the Member State or EEA State within whose territory the immovable asset is situated or under whose authority the register, account or system is kept, as the case may be.

### 33. Pending lawsuits.

The effects of reorganisation measures or winding-up proceedings on a pending lawsuit concerning an asset or a right of which the Maltese credit institution has been divested shall be governed solely in accordance with the law of the Member State or EEA State in which the lawsuit is pending.

### 34. Registration in a public register.

(1) In order to secure the rights of interested third parties, an administrator or liquidator may request that a reorganisation measure or the decision to open winding-up proceedings be registered in the registry of companies and any other public register by whatever name kept in Malta or any other Member State or EEA State.

(2) The costs of registration are to be regarded as costs and expenses incurred in the winding-up proceedings or reorganisation measure.

### 35. Professional secrecy.

*Amended by: L.N. 298 of 2015.*

All persons required to receive or divulge information in connection with the information or consultation procedures laid down in these regulations shall be bound by professional secrecy, in accordance with the rules and conditions laid down in the Banking Act, the Malta Financial Services Authority Act and the Banking Act (Supervisory Review) Regulations, with the exception of any judicial authorities to which existing national provisions apply:

*S.L. 330.09*

Provided that this regulation shall not apply where regulation 84 of the Recovery and Resolution Regulations applies, and accordingly the confidentiality provisions of such regulations shall apply.

PART IV - REORGANISATION MEASURES AND WINDING-UP PROCEEDINGS IN RESPECT OF EUROPEAN CREDIT

**INSTITUTIONS EFFECTIVE IN MALTA**

### 36. Adoption of reorganisation measures and winding-up proceedings of a European credit institution. Cap. 386.

(1) The commencement of a reorganisation measure or winding-up proceedings of a European credit institution has effect in Malta as if it were part of the Companies Act, when such is in relation to:

(a) a branch of a European credit institution;

(b) any property or other assets of the European credit institution; or

(c) any debt or liability of such European credit institution.

(2) Subject to subregulation (4), the administrator or liquidator or persons appointed to assist, or where appropriate, represent the administrators and liquidators may exercise in Malta, in relation to a European credit institution which is subject to a reorganisation measure or winding-up proceedings, any function which, pursuant to that measure or order, he is entitled to exercise in relation to a European credit institution.

(3) The appointment of the administrator and liquidator shall be evidenced -

(a) by a certified copy of the order or decision by a judicial or administrative authority competent in the home State for reorganisation or winding-up proceedings by or under which the administrator or liquidator was appointed; or

(b) by any other certificate issued by the judicial or administrative authority which has jurisdiction in relation to a reorganisation measure or winding-up proceedings,

and, where necessary accompanied by a certified translation of that order, decision or certificate, as the case may be.

(4) In exercising the functions of the kind mentioned in subregulation (2), the administrator or the liquidator -

(a) may not take any action which would constitute an unlawful use of force in Malta;

(b) may not rule on any dispute arising from a matter falling within Parts I, II and III of these regulations; and

(c) notwithstanding the way in which functions may be exercised in the home State of the European credit institution, shall act in accordance with the laws or rules as to procedure which are effective in Malta.

### 37. European credit institutions or branches of European credit institutions located in Malta.

*Substituted by: L.N. 298 of 2015; L.N. 149 of 2016. Cap. 386. Cap. 386.*

(1) From the date of coming into force of these regulations\*, the Court may not, in relation to a European credit institution or any branch of a European credit institution -

(a) make a winding-up order pursuant to article 224 of the Companies Act; or

(b) adopt reorganisation measures or perform any act ancillary to the adoption of such measures in terms of Part VI of the Companies Act.

(2) Sub-regulation (1)(a) does not prevent -

(a) the Court from making a winding-up order after the coming into force of these regulations in relation to a European credit institution or any branch of a European credit institution if -

(i) a provisional administrator was appointed in relation to the European credit institution or any branch of a European credit institution before the coming into force of these regulations; and (ii) the appointment continues in force until immediately before that winding-up order is made;

(b) the winding-up of a European credit institution or any branch of a European credit institution after the coming into force of these regulations pursuant to a winding-up order which was made, and has not been discharged, before that date.

(3) Sub-regulation (1)(b) does not prevent a provisional administrator of a European credit institution appointed before the coming into force of these regulations, from acting in relation to such institution after that date.

## PART V - INTERPRETATION

### 38. Interpretation.

(1) The purpose of these regulations is to implement the provisions of Directive 2001⁄24⁄EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4th April, 2001 on the reorganisation and winding-up of credit institutions, and shall be interpreted and applied accordingly.

(2) In the event that there is a conflict between any of these regulations and the provisions of the Directive mentioned in subregulation (1), the provisions of the Directive shall prevail.
