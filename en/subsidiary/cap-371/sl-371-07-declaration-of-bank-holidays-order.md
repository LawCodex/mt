# Declaration of Bank Holidays Order (S.L. 371.07)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 371.07**

**DECLARATION OF BANK HOLIDAYS ORDER**

10th September, 2004

LEGAL NOTICES 406 of 2004, 371 of 2005, 333 of 2006, 352 of 2007, 91, 103 of 2008, 20, 340 of 2009, 6, 507 of 2011, 51, 423 of 2013, 477 of 2014, 380 of 2015, 348 of 2016, 383 of 2017, 435 of 2018, 331 of 2019, 486 of 2020, 473 of 2021 and 316 of 2022, 269 of 2023, 359 of 2024 and 280 of 2025. consolidated.

## 1. Citation.

The title of this Order is the Declaration of Bank Holidays Order.

## 2. Bank holidays.
