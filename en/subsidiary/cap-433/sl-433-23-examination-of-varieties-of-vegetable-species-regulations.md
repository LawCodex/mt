# Examination of Varieties of Vegetable Species Regulations (S.L. 433.23)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 433.23**

**EXAMINATION OF VARIETIES OF**

**VEGETABLE SPECIES REGULATIONS\***

5th April, 2007

LEGAL NOTICE 87 of 2007, as amended by Legal Notices 322 of 2007, 271 of 2008, 78 of 2010, 20 of 2011, 54 of 2012, 321 of 2013, 201 of 2014. 376 of 2015, 254 of 2016, 128 of 2017, 312 of 2018, 230 of 2019, 159 of 2020, 403 of 2021 and 271 of 2022.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Examination of Varieties of Vegetable Species Regulations.

## 2. Scope.

*Amended by: L.N. 201 of 2014. Substituted by: L.N. 312 of 2018.*

The scope of these regulations is to:

(a) ensure that varieties of species of vegetables which shall be included in the national catalogue of such varieties in Malta shall comply with internationally recognised guidelines as regards the characteristics to be covered as a minimum by the examination and the minimum conditions for examining such varieties.

(b) transpose the provisions of:

(i) Commission Directive 2003/91/EC of 6th October, 2003, as regards the minimum characteristics and the minimum conditions for examining certain varieties of vegetable species, as amended by Commission Directive 2006/ 127/EC of 7 December 2006;

(ii) Commission Directive 2007/49/EC of 26 July 2007;

(iii) Commission Directive 2008/83/EC of 13 August 2008;

(iv) Commission Directive 2009/97/EC of 3 August 2009;

(v) Commission Directive 2010/46/EU of 2 July 2010;

(vi) Commission Implementing Directive 2011/68/ EU of 1 July 2011;

(vii) Commission Directive 2012/44/EU;

(viii) Commission Directive 2013/57/EU amending Commission Directive 2003/91/EC setting out implementing measures for the purposes of Article 7 of Council Directive 2002/55/EC, as regards the minimum characteristics to be examined and the minimum conditions for examining certain varieties of vegetable species;

\*These regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 144 of 2023.

(ix) Commission Implementing Directive 2014/105/ EU of 4 December 2014 amending Directives 2003/90/EC and 2003/91/EC setting out implementing measures for the purposes of Article 7 of Council Directive 2002/53/EC and Article 7 of Council Directive 2002/55/EC respectively, as regards the characteristics to be covered as a minimum by the examination and the minimum conditions for examining certain varieties of agricultural plant species and vegetable species;

(x) Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2015/ 1168 of 15 July 2015 amending Directives 2003/90/EC and 2003/91/EC setting out implementing measures for the purposes of Article 7 of Council Directive 2002/53/EC and Article 7 of Council Directive 2002/55/EC respectively, as regards the characteristics to be covered as a minimum by the examination and the minimum conditions for examining certain varieties of agricultural plant species and vegetable species;

(xi) Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2016/ 1914 of 31 October 2016 amending Directives 2003/90/EC and 2003/91/EC setting out implementing measures for the purposes of Article 7 of Council Directive 2002/53/EC and Article 7 of Council Directive 2002/55/EC respectively, as regards the characteristics to be covered as a minimum by the examination and the minimum conditions for examining certain varieties of agricultural plant species and vegetable species;

(xii) Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2018/ 100 amending Commission Directive 2003/90/EC setting out implementing measures for the purposes Article 7 of Council Directive 2002/53/EC as regards the minimum characteristics to be examined and the minimum conditions for examining certain varieties of agricultural plant species; and

(xiii) as made reference to by Commission Directive 2009/145/EC of 26 November 2009;

## 3. Conditions for examination of varieties of vegetables. S.L. 433.30

*Amended by: L.N.312 of 2018*

(1) Varieties of vegetable species shall be officially accepted in the national catalogue within the meaning of regulation 6(1) of the Seeds of Agricultural Plants and Vegetable Regulations after fulfilling the requirements, as regards distinctness, uniformity and stability, set out hereunder:

(a) the species listed in Schedule I shall comply with the conditions laid down in the "Protocols for distinctness, uniformity and stability tests" of the Administrative Council of the Community Plant Variety Office (hereafter referred to as the "CPVO") which are relevant for the particular species;

(b) the species listed in Schedule II shall comply with the "Guidelines for the conduct of tests for distinctness, homogeneity and stability" of the International Union for the Protection of New Varieties of Plants (hereafter referred to as "UPOV") which are relevant for the particular species.

(2) All the varietal characteristics within the meaning of sub-regulation (1)(a) and any characteristics which are marked by an asterisk (\*) in the test guidelines referred to in sub-regulation (1)(b) shall be used, provided that observation of a characteristic is not rendered impossible by the expression of any other characteristics and provided that the expression of a characteristic is not prevented by the environmental conditions under which the test is conducted.

(3) It shall be ensured that in respect of the species listed in Schedules I and II, the minimum requirements for carrying out the examinations in respect of trial design and growing conditions, as laid down in the test guidelines referred to in these Schedules, are fulfilled at the time of the examinations.
