# Cattle Breeding Rules (S.L. 97.01)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 97.01**

**CATTLE BREEDING RULES\***

20th January, 1939 GOVERNMENT NOTICE 38 of 1939, as amended by Legal Notice 408 of 2007.

## 1. Title.

The title of these Rules is Cattle Breeding Rules.

## 2. Application for licence.

Applications for a licence to keep a bull for stud purposes shall contain the following particulars -

(a) the name, surname and address of the owner;

(b) the age and breed of the bull;

(c) the place where the bull is kept;

(d) the date on which the application is made and the signature or mark of applicants.

## 3. Conditions to refuse, suspend or withdraw licence.

The Director of Agriculture may refuse, suspend or withdraw any licence in any of the following cases:

(a) if, in his opinion, a bull is unfit for service;

(b) if, in his opinion, the bull or the premises where the bull is kept, are not maintained in a sufficiently clean condition;

(c) if the district where the bull is kept is already sufficiently provided with service bulls, or

*Cap. 97.*

(d) if the provisions of the Cattle Breeding Ordinance or any of these Rules, have not been observed.

## 4. Validity of licence.

All licences shall be valid until the 30th June of each year. 5. Applications for renewal of a licence shall be made at least a fortnight before the date of the expiration of the licence.

*Renewal of licence.*

The licence is personal to the owner.

## 6. Owner of licensed bull to keep service book.

The owner of any licensed bull shall keep a service book for every bull so licensed and shall record therein the following particulars:

(a) name, surname and address of the owner of any cow served by the bull;

(b) the breed of the cow so served;

(c) the date of service.

Such book shall be kept available for inspection by the Director of Agriculture and by any other officer acting on his behalf.

## 7. Fee for service.

*Amended by: L.N. 408 of 2007.*

No fee for service shall exceed thirty-five cents (0.35) for each service.

\*These Rules have been repealed by Act XXXIV of 2023.
