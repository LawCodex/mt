## PART 2 — Provisions relating to New Zealand benefits — Article 5

**Effect of residence in Malta**

\1. A person shall be considered, for the purpose of an application, to be ordinarily resident and present in New Zealand on the date that he or she applies for a New Zealand benefit under this Agreement if, on that date, he or she:

(a) is ordinarily resident in Malta, and present in either Malta or New Zealand; and

(b) has the intention of remaining resident in Malta for at least 26 weeks; and

(c) has had a continuous period of New Zealand working age residence of at least 1 year.

\2. For the purpose of payment of any New Zealand benefit under this Agreement, a person shall be considered to be ordinarily resident in, and not absent from, New Zealand, if he or she:

(a) is ordinarily resident in Malta, and present in Malta or New Zealand; and

(b) has had a continuous period of New Zealand working age residence of at least 1 year.

\3. For the purposes of this Article:

(a) if a person who is ordinarily resident in Malta is temporarily absent from Malta for a period that does not exceed 26 weeks, that absence shall be considered as not interrupting the person’s ordinary residence or presence in Malta;

(b) if a person who is ordinarily resident in Malta is temporarily absent from Malta for a period that exceeds 26 weeks, that person’s ordinary residence in Malta shall be considered to have ceased on the date of departure from Malta;

(c) a person is considered to be ordinarily resident in Malta on the date of the person’s departure from New Zealand if:

(i) the person leaves New Zealand with the intention of becoming and remaining resident in Malta for at least 26 weeks; and

(ii) the person begins to be resident in Malta within 26 weeks of that departure.

\4. For the purposes of subparagraphs (a) and (b) of paragraph 3 of this Article, a period when a person is temporarily in New Zealand shall not be considered a period when the person is temporarily absent from Malta.

## PART 3 — Provisions relating to Maltese benefits — Article 11

**Totalisation for Maltese Benefits**

\1. Where this Agreement applies to a person and there is a period of Maltese contributions that is:

(a) less than the period necessary to give a person entitlement to the benefit claimed under the legislation of Malta; and

(b) equal to or greater than the minimum period mentioned in paragraph 3 of this Article for that benefit;

then any period of New Zealand working age residence by that person shall be deemed to be a period of Maltese contributions.

\2. For the purposes of this Article, where a period of Maltese contributions and a period of New Zealand working age residence overlap, only the period of Maltese contributions shall be taken into account.

\3. The minimum period of Maltese contributions to be taken into consideration for the purposes of paragraph 1 of this Article shall be 52 paid contributions valid for assessment of the contribution average assessment under the Social Security Act (Cap 318).

**Article 12**

**Calculation of Maltese Benefits**

\1. Where a Maltese benefit is payable by Malta to a person by virtue of this Agreement the rate of that benefit shall be calculated as follows:

\2. For the purposes of paragraph 1 of this Article:

(a) the "Theoretical Rate of Maltese Benefit" (TR) is the applicable rate of Maltese benefit attributable to a person as assessed under the provisions of the Social Security Act (Cap 318) taking into consideration the Maltese contribution period and the New Zealand working age residence as totalised under Article 12 of this Agreement;

(b) the "Reckonable Contributions" (RC) is the number of Maltese contributions on which the Maltese benefit would have been calculated under the Social Security Act (Cap 318) in the absence of this Agreement; and

(c) the "Totalised Contributions" (TC) is the aggregate number of Maltese contributions and the New Zealand working age residence as totalised under Article 12 of this Agreement.

\3. Any additional allowances or bonuses that are applicable to certain Maltese benefits covered by this Agreement that are payable under the legislation of Malta, shall be calculated in the same manner as that indicated in paragraph 1 of this Article, as the case may require.

\4. Any amount of Maltese benefit payable calculated in accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article shall be rounded up to the nearest whole euro cent.

### Section 1

Exchange of Information on Changes in Circumstances and General Adjustments of Benefit Rates of Mutual Clients

#### 1.

Reason for Information Matching Programme

Under the legislation of New Zealand the rate of benefit paid in New Zealand is generally dependent on the rate of payment made by Malta. A change in circumstances for a person who receives both a Maltese and a New Zealand benefit can affect the continuing entitlement to the rate of the New Zealand benefit.

Each Liaison Agency will notify the other about any change in circumstances for a mutual client, resident in that country, so that the other Liaison Agency may review the continuing entitlement or rate of payment.

#### 2.

Frequency of Information Exchange

Exchanges of information for changes in circumstances will occur on an ad hoc basis as soon as practicably possible following either Liaison Agency becoming aware of a mutual client’s change of circumstances.

General adjustments to benefit rates will be advised by each Liaison Agency to the 14 [L.S.318.23 BEJN MALTA U NEW ZEALAND

other as soon as practicably possible.

For New Zealand the annual general adjustment to benefit rates occurs in April. For Malta the general adjustment of benefit rates occurs in January.

#### 3.

Type of Information Exchanged

Each Liaison Agency will exchange information about change of circumstances relating to mutual benefit clients as specified in section 7(4)(a) and (b) of the Administrative Arrangement. The exchange of information about client changes in circumstances will be ongoing but on an ad hoc basis as they arise.

Information about adjustments to benefit rates as specified in sections 7(7) and 7(8) of the Administrative Arrangement will also be exchanged between the liaison agencies.

Where a change in circumstances has occurred for a mutual client, or where either Liaison Agency notifies details of adjustments to benefit rates, the relevant information will be transmitted to the other Liaison Agency on a liaison form as soon as practicably possible to the other Liaison Agency.

#### 4.

Method of Collecting Information

The generating Liaison Agency will obtain the information specified in section 7 of the Administrative Arrangement from its own database or client information retention system. In relation to changes in circumstances the information will be collected from the client as they advise the change to the relevant Liaison Agency.

#### 5.

Method of Conveying Information

Personal information will be exchanged between the liaison agencies by airmail through registered mail or via equivalent air courier service for:

• Client changes in circumstances

• Adjustment of benefit rates

Application forms and any accompanying documentation containing personal information will be sent by one Liaison Agency to the other Liaison Agency by registered mail or via equivalent air courier service.

#### 6.

Use of the Information received from the other Party

Information received on liaison forms will be used to reassess the client’s entitlement. Appropriate adjustments will be made to the liaison agencies’ computer payment systems and client core information system on the basis of the information received from the other Liaison Agency.

#### 7.

Update of the client’s record in New Zealand

Prior to any changes being made under paragraph 6 the New Zealand Liaison Agency will check the client’s record to ensure that compliance requirements have been met with section 19D of the Social Welfare (Transitional Provisions) Act 1990 of New Zealand. Where the client’s record indicates there has not been compliance with section 19D of the Social Welfare (Transitional Provisions) Act 1990 no update of the client’s record will take place until this requirement is met.

Subject to compliance with section 19D of the Social Welfare (Transitional Provisions) Act 1990 the New Zealand Liaison Agency will undertake a 100% manual check of the client’s:

• name

• client reference number for New Zealand

• client reference numbers for Malta

• date of birth

Where the information matches the New Zealand Liaison Agency will update the client record manually. The client may be able to seek a review under section 10A of the Social Security Act 1964.

#### 8.

Update of the client’s record in Malta

The Malta Liaison Agency, before updating its records, will check the client’s: • name

• client reference numbers for Malta

• client reference for New Zealand

• date of birth

Where the information matches that submitted by the New Zealand Liaison Agency, the Malta Liaison Agency will update the records manually. The client may be able to seek a review in respect of the adverse action created by this process in terms of the provisions of Article 108 of the Social Security Act (Cap 318).

#### 9.

Destruction of Information Matching Data

In accordance with New Zealand Privacy Act 1992 liaison forms from Malta will be destroyed within 60 working days from the date of their receipt in New Zealand, unless a discrepancy is found and a decision is made to take adverse action about that. In that case, the forms will be destroyed either after 1 year if no adverse action is subsequently taken or, if adverse action is taken, as soon as practicable after the forms are no longer needed for the purpose of taking action against an identifiable individual.

In accordance with the Malta Data Protection Act 2001, liaison forms from New Zealand will not be kept for a period longer than is necessary. In this regard, forms from New Zealand will be destroyed within 60 working days from the date of their receipt in Malta, unless:

(a) a discrepancy is found; or

(b) a decision is made to take action in regard to the discrepancy; or

(c) further investigations and clarifications are required on the matter.

In such cases, depending on the action to be taken, the forms will be destroyed as soon as practicable after all issues are resolved and the forms are no longer needed to deliver the service as appropriate to an identifiable individual.

Neither Liaison Agency will re-use or re-assign their benefit reference numbers to another person.

This Supplementary Administrative Arrangement will commence on the same day the Agreement and Administrative Arrangement enters into force and will operate for the duration of the Agreement.
