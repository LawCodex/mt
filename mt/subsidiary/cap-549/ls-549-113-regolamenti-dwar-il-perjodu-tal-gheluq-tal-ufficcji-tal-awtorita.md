# Regolamenti dwar il-Perjodu tal-Għeluq tal-Uffiċċji tal-Awtorità għall-Ambjent u r-Riżorsi (S.L. 549.113)

**SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION 549.113**

**PERIOD OF SHUTDOWN OF THE**

ENVIRONMENT AND RESOURCES AUTHORITY

**OFFICES REGULATIONS**

21st April, 2017 LEGAL NOTICE 111 of 2017.

## 1. Citation.

The title of these regulations is the Period of Shutdown of the Environment and Resources Authority Offices Regulations.

## 2. Period of Shutdown.

(1) The period of shutdown of the Environment and Resources Authority Offices shall be from the 24th December of one year till the 1st January of the next following year.

(2) The period mentioned in sub-regulation (1) shall suspend any time-frame prescribed by any regulation under the Act.
