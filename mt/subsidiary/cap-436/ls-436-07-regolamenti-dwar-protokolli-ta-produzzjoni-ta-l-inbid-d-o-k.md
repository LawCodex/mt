# Regolamenti dwar Protokolli ta' Produzzjoni ta' l-Inbid D.O.K (S.L. 436.07)

**LEĠISLAZZJONI SUSSIDJARJA 436.07**

**REGOLAMENTI DWAR PROTOKOLLI**

**TA’ PRODUZZJONI TA’ L-INBID D.O.K.**

1 ta’ Mejju, 2007 L-AVVIŻ LEGALI 416 tal-2007, kif emendat bl-Avviżi Legali 168 tal- 2011 u 214 tal-2026.

## 1. Titolu.

It-titolu ta’ dawn ir-regolamenti hu Regolamenti dwar Protokolli ta’ Produzzjoni ta’ l-Inbid D.O.K.

## 2. Tifsir.

*Emendat: A.L. 214 tal-2026.*

F’dawn ir-regolamenti, kemm il-darba dawn ma jkunux diġà mfissra fl-Att, għandhom japplikaw it-tifsiriet li ġejjin:

*Kap. 436.*

"l-Att" tfisser l-Att dwar l-Inbid;

"bittija" tfisser kontenitur tal-injam tas-siġra tal-ballut li tkun tesa’ mhux aktar minn ħames mitt (500) litru;

"barrique" tfisser bittija tal-injam tas-siġra tal-ballut u tkun tesa bejn 225 u 250 litru;

"Direttur" ifisser Direttur responsabli għall-Agrikoltura;

"Ministru" ifisser Ministru responsabbli għall-Agrikoltura;

"novello" tfisser prodotti tal-inbid ħomor jew Rosé maħdumin minn għeneb li ngħata proċess ta’ maċerazzjoni karbonika fejn il-proċess ta’ maċerazzjoni tal-għenieqed sħaħ sar fl-ambjent mingħajr ossiġenu għal perjodu minimu ta’ tlett ijiem;

*L.S. 436.05*

"ir-Regolamenti dwar id-Denominazzjoni" tfisser ir-Regolamenti dwar id-Denominazzjoni ta’ l-Oriġini u ta’ Indikazzjonijiet Ġeografiċi;

"Taqsima" tfisser l-uffiċċju responsabbli mill-Vitikultura u l- Enoloġija.

## 3. Kompetenza.

It-Taqsima qegħda b’dan tingħata s-setgħa bħala l-uffiċċju kompetenti biex tinforza dawn ir-regolamenti u biex tagħti ċ-ċertifikazzjoni għall-inbejjed D.O.K. ordnati.

## 4. ~ertifikazzjoni ta’ l-inbid.

(1) Iċ-ċertifikazzjoni tiġi mogħtija mit-Taqsima wara talba uffiċjali li għandha ssir bil-miktub, mil-produttur konċernat, kif stipulat fir-regolament 17(8) tar-Regolamenti dwar id- Denominazzjoni u wara li t-Taqsima tkun għamlet il-kontrolli tekniċi u ammistrattivi meħtieġa biex tivverifika li l-inbejjed prodotti jikkonformaw mal-Protokoll rilevanti.

(2) Għandu jitwaqqaf bord ad hoc mit-Taqsima biex tipproċessa r-riżultati tal-analiżi kimiċi, fiżiċi u organolettiċi stipulati fir-regolamenti 13 u 17 tar-Regolamenti dwar id- Denominazzjoni u biex jiċċertifika li l-karatteristiċi jkunu jaqblu mal-protokolli tal-produzzjoni rispettivi. Fil-każ li jkun hemm appell kif stipulat fir-regolament 17(6) tar-Regolamenti dwar id- Denominazzjoni, il-bord għandu jinkludi rappreżentant min-naħa tal-appellant.

(3) Inbid jiġi ċertifikat bħala inbid D.O.K. "MALTA" jekk il-protokoll fl-Iskeda I jiġi osservat kollu kemm hu.

(4) Inbid jiġi ċertifikat bħala inbid D.O.K. "GOZO" jekk il-protokoll fl-Iskeda II jiġi osservat kollu kemm hu.

## 5. Parteċipazzjoni fl-iskema ta’ kwalità.

*Emendat: A.L. 214 tal-2026.*

(1) Għassara interessati f’din l-iskema ta’ kwalità tad- D.O.K. jistgħu japplikaw fit-Taqsima sa mhux aktar tard mit-30 ta’ Mejju.

(2) It-Taqsima tirreġistra lil applikant wara li jkun ħallas dritt nominali li jiġi stabbilit mit-Taqsima kull sena, u li jitħallas lid- Direttur.

(3) L-applikazzjoni msemmija fis-subregolament (1) għandha tiġġedded kull sena flimkien mal-ħlas tad-dritt għall-parteċipazzjoni msemmi fis-subregolament (2).

(4) Għassara li ma jkunux reġistrati fl-iskema ta’ kwalità fil-ħin skont ma hemm fis-subregolament (1) ma jkunx eleġibbli għal proċess ta’ ċertifikazzjoni.

(5) L-għassara għandhom iħallsu dritt, li jiġi stabbilit kull sena mit-Taqsima, għal kull kwantità ta’ inbid li jiġi ppreżentat għaċ-ċertifikazzjoni biex jagħmel tajjeb għall-ispejjes tal-analiżi.

(6) Lista ta’ għassara liċenzjati u dawk li rreġistraw taħt din l-iskema ta’ kwalità skont is-subregolament (1) għandha tiġi ppubblikata fil-Gazzetta u tkun aċċessibli għall-pubbliku.

(7) Imħassar bl-Avviż Legali 214 tal-2026.

(8) B’żieda mal-applikazzjoni skont is-subregolament (1), jekk ma tkunx saret id-dikjarazzjoni, kull kontenitur fiss li jinsab fil-post tal-għassar jew f’xi kantina tal-għassar applikant, għandu jiġu dikjarat għand it-Taqsima bid-dettalji eżatti ta’ kemm dawn ikunu jesgħu flimkien ma’ numru ta’ identifikazzjoni u indikazzjoni tal-pożizzjoni eżatta tagħhom permezz ta’ pjanta ta’ perit li jagħmel fi proporzjon fiss għad-daqs reali. In-numri ta’ identifikazzjoni fuq il-kontenituri għandhom ikunu fissi u li ma jitħassrux. Il-post jew btieħi fejn tankijiet mobbli jiħu maħżunin għandhom jiġu mgħarrfa lit-Taqsima fuq pjanta ta’ perit fi proporzjon fiss għad-daqs reali. Jekk ikun hemm xi tibdil dan għandu jiġi minnufih avżat lit- Taqsima permezz ta’ ittra reġistrata.

## 6. Teħid ta’ kampjuni.

(1) Il-kampjuni għall-analiżi skont ir-regolament 17(1) tar- Regolamenti dwar id-Denominazzjoni jittieħdu mit-Taqsima skond il-proċeduri tagħha nnifisha wara li tkun saret talba bil-miktub mill-għassar konċernat skont ir-regolament 4(1) ta’ dawn ir-regolamenti.

(2) Il-kampjuni għandhom jittieħdu fil-preżenza tal-għassar jew tar-rappreżentant tiegħu u għandu jsir notament ukoll fir-reġistru rilevanti tal-għassar kontrassenjat mill-persuna li tkun ħadet il-kampjun.

(3) Il-kampjuni għandhom jittieħdu mingħajr ebda ħlas filwaqt li tingħata rċevuta uffiċjali.

(4) Il-kampjuni għandhom jiġu ssiġillati kif imiss u kampjun minnhom għandu jitħalla għand l-għassar li qiegħed japplika għaċ-ċertifikazzjoni.

(5) Minkejja r-regolament 17(1) tar-Regolamenti dwar id- Denominazzjoni, il-kampjuni jistgħu jittieħdu fil-fażi tal-ibbottiljar f’każijiet ġustifikati fejn it-Taqsima tkun ġiet sodisfatta li kampjuni li ttieħdu qabel din il-fażi jistgħu jikkompromettu l-karatteristiċi tal-kampjun innifsu.

5 - Method of cultivation.

The method of cultivation, the planting distances and the pruning systems shall be quality oriented and shall not change the characteristics of the grapes and wines. Any practice for overproduction is strictly forbidden. Nevertheless, the practice of rescue irrigation is considered essential for quality and is thus allowed.

5.1 Planting density.

For new plantings and re-plantings of grape varieties, carried out on a date subsequent to the entry into force of this protocol, the minimum planting density shall be of 4,000 vines per hectare.

5.1.1 In the case of vines, planted using the training form of the traditional bush method (alberello), the minimum planting density may be of 2,500 vines per hectare.

5.2 Training forms.

For new plantings and re-plantings of grape varieties, carried out on a date subsequent to the entry into force of this protocol, the training forms shall be of the Vertical Shoot Positioning (VSP) type or the traditional bush method (alberello).

5.3 Pruning systems.

For new plantings and re-plantings, carried out on a date subsequent to the entry into force of this protocol, the pruning systems to be adopted shall be limited to Cane and, or Spur pruning, for example "Guyot" or "Cordone Speronato" or mixed pruning methods of the two.

7 - Vinification protocols.

7.1 Region of vinification.

Vinification operations, any compulsory ageing, ageing in barriques, bottling and ageing in bottles shall be carried out within the territory of the region of production referred to in heading 2. For the production of PDO "MALTA" wines in all typologies the above operations may also be carried out in the following regions in close proximity:

(a) Malta - Harbour area

(b) Gozo.

7.2 Production.

The typologies listed in heading 1 shall be produced in conformity with European Community and National laws.

7.3 Methods of grape processing.

The typologies listed in heading 1 may bear on their label the following terms and any expression resulting from a translation thereof:

• "Maceration pelliculaire" or "Cold Maceration". These may be used exclusively for white wines in the case of the pre-fermentation skin contact of the crushed white grapes for a minimum period of 4 hours prior to pressing and fermentation with the aim of increasing varietal flavour.

• "Fermented in barriques". These may be used to describe white wines of which a minimum 45% has completely undergone the fermentation process in barriques.

• "Barrel fermented" or "Fermented in oak barrels". These may be used to describe white wines of which a minimum of 60% has completely undergone the fermentation process in barrels.

• "Maceration Carbonique" or "Macerazione Carbonica" or "Carbonic Maceration". These may be used exclusively for red or rosé wines in the case of the maceration of whole grape clusters under an anaerobic atmosphere for a minimum period of 3 days.

• "Late Harvest" or "Vendemmia Tardiva" or "Vendange Tardive". These may be used exclusively for wines, 100% of which is obtained from grapes which have been left on the vine to undergo a partial drying process resulting in the concentration of grape sugars to a minimum natural alcoholic strength of 13.5% vol.

• "Passito". This may be used exclusively for wines 100% of which is obtained from grapes which have undergone a drying process, in whole or in part, on the plant or after harvest, resulting in the concentration of grape sugars to a minimum natural potential alcoholic strength of 18.0% vol. and of which the minimum actual alcoholic strength is 12.0% vol. Notwithstanding the EU regulations on oenological practices, the maximum limit of volatile acidity for the "Passito" typology is of 30 milliequivalents per litre and the maximum limit of total sulphur dioxide for this wine can be up to 300mg per litre if the wine has a sugar content greater than or equal to 45g/l.

• "Imqadded". This may be used exclusively for wines 100% of which is obtained from grapes which have undergone a drying process, in whole or in part, on the plant or after harvest, resulting in the concentration of grape sugars to a minimum natural potential alcoholic strength of 14.0% vol. and of which the minimum actual alcoholic strength is 12.5% vol. Notwithstanding the EU regulations on oenological practices, the maximum limit of volatile acidity for the "Imqadded" typology is of 25 milliequivalents per litre and the maximum limit of total sulphur dioxide for this wine can be up to 300mg per litre if the wine has a sugar content greater than or equal to 45g/l.

7.4 Methods of Vinification and Ageing.

The typologies listed in heading 1 may bear on their label the following terms and any expression resulting from a translation thereof:

• "Saignée". This may be used to describe Rosé wines of which a minimum 75% has been produced by draining must from crushed red grapes within the first 48 hours of the start of the alcoholic fermentation process.

• "Elevé sur lies" or "Aged on lees". These may be used to describe White or Rosé wines that have been left in contact with the yeast lees resulting from the alcoholic fermentation for a minimum period of 4 months from the start of the alcoholic fermentation.

• "Matured in oak Barriques". These may be used to describe red wines of which a minimum of 75% has been stored in barriques for at least 6 months. In the case of white wines this mention may be used to describe white wines of which a minimum 45% has been stored in barriques for at least 8 weeks.

• "Barrel Matured" or "Matured in oak Barrels" or "Oak Aged". These may be used to describe red wines of which a minimum of 85% has been stored in barrels for at least 6 months or red wines of which a minimum 75% has been stored in barriques for at least 6 months. In the case of white wines this mention may be used to describe white wines of which a minimum 50% has been stored in barrels for at least 8 weeks or white wines of which a minimum 45% has been stored in barriques for at least 8 weeks.

7.5 Special Mentions.

Subject to complying with the criteria described hereunder in this item, the typologies listed in heading 1 may bear on their label the following terms:

• "Superior"

This mention may be allowed on the label to describe the wine typologies listed in heading 1 and defined in heading 8 put up for consumption, which have not undergone any form of enrichment and which have:

(a) For the White and Rosé wine typologies, a minimum natural alcoholic content and a minimum total alcoholic content in the wine put up for consumption of 12.0% vol.

(b) For the Red wine typologies, a minimum natural alcoholic content and a minimum total alcoholic content in the wine put up for consumption of 12.5% vol.

• "Riżerva"

This mention may be allowed on the label to describe the wine typologies put up for consumption listed in heading 1 and defined in heading 8, which have not undergone any form of enrichment and which, depending on the style of the producer, after a period of ageing in wooden recipients, comply with the following additional criteria:

(A) For wines of the White typologies:

(a) The minimum natural alcoholic content of the wine shall be 12.0% vol.;

(b) The minimum total alcoholic content of the wine put up for consumption shall be 12.0% vol.;

(c) Wines cannot be commercialised before 24 months have elapsed from the 1st of September of the vintage year;

(d) Wines shall be aged for a minimum of 6 months in bottle before commercial release.

(B) For wines of the Red typologies:

(a) The minimum natural alcoholic content of the wine shall be 12.5% vol.;

(b) The minimum total alcoholic content of the wine put up for consumption shall be 12.5% vol.;

(c) Wines cannot be commercialised before 24 months have elapsed from the 1st of September of the vintage year;

(d) Wines shall have a minimum ageing of 12 months in oak barrels;

(e) Wines shall be aged for a minimum of 6 months in bottle before commercial release.

In the case of the mention "Riżerva" it is obligatory to also specify the vintage year on the label.

7.5.1 When applicable under the criteria given in item 7.5, the special mentions "Superior" or "Riżerva" are to be positioned on the label directly below the D.O.K. mention as described in heading 9.

7.5.2 When applicable under the criteria given in item 7.5, the special mention "Riżerva" may be replaced by "Reserve".

7.6 Vineyards or Estate mention.

7.6.1 Vineyards mention

The use of additional topographic indications which make reference to the vineyards from which 100% of the grapes used to make the wine originate is allowed on condition that:

• All the vineyard parcels are located within the topographical indication used;

• The topographic indication shall refer to an area which is no larger than any of the local council boundaries as indicated in Annex I;

• The vineyards shall be registered to the list of vineyards in such a way that it can be identified separately under the topographical name used;

• The grapes harvested from the named vineyards are subject to a specific annual production declaration and vinified separately;

• The whole extent of the vineyard parcels making up the named Vineyards mention have a productive area of not less than one hectare of grapes of the same typology;

• The grapes and the relative wines produced from the named vineyards are registered separately in the obligatory winery registers.

7.6.2 Estate mention

The use of the mention of an Estate name which makes reference to the vineyards from which 100% of the grapes used to make the wine originate is allowed on condition that:

• The whole extent of the vineyard parcels making up the named estate have a productive area of not less than one hectare of grapes of the same typology;

• All the vineyard parcels making up the named estate shall be within a distance not exceeding one hundred meters, inclusive of pathways and, or public roads;

• The vineyard parcels making up the named estate shall be under the full control of the wine making entity through direct ownership or a long term lease and evidence of the above shall be provided to the regulating body;

• The vineyard parcels making up the named estate shall be registered by the wine producing entity to the list of vineyards in such a way that it can be identified separately under the estate name used;

• The grapes harvested from the named estate are subject to a specific annual harvest declaration and vinified separately;

• The grapes and the relative wines produced from the named estate are registered separately in the obligatory winery registers.

7.6.3 The optional additional mention "Single Vineyard" shall be reserved for quality wines that satisfy all the conditions for an Estate mention or a Vineyards mention, described in items 7.6.1 and 7.6.2, only where the vineyard parcels making up the Estate or named Vineyards are contiguous to each other and may only be separated by pathways and, or public roads.

7.7 Oenological practices and processes.

For the elaboration of wines eligible for the mention of "Late Harvest" or "Vendemmia Tardiva" or "Vendange Tardive" or "Imqadded" or "Passito", as described in item 7.3, any form of enrichment of the must or wine shall be prohibited except for the eventual possible addition, in the case of wines eligible for the mentions "Imqadded" and "Passito", even after the 30th November of each year, of grapes of the same harvest with a maximum sugar concentration of 60%.

Also for the elaboration of wines eligible for the mention of "Superior" and "Riżerva", as described in item 7.5, any form of enrichment shall be strictly prohibited.

5 - Method of cultivation.

The method of cultivation, the planting distances and the pruning systems shall be quality oriented and shall not change the characteristics of the grapes and wines. Any practice for overproduction is strictly forbidden. Nevertheless, the practice of rescue irrigation is considered essential for quality and is, thus, allowed.

5.1 Planting density.

For new plantings and re-plantings of grape varieties, carried out on a date subsequent to the coming into force of this protocol, the minimum planting density shall be of 4,000 vines per hectare.

5.1.1 In the case of vines, planted using the training form of the traditional bush method (alberello), the minimum planting density may be of 2,500 vines per hectare.

5.2 Training forms.

For new plantings and re-plantings of grape varieties, carried out on a date subsequent to the coming into force of this protocol, the training forms shall be of the Vertical Shoot Positioning (VSP) type or the traditional bush method (alberello).

5.3 Pruning systems.

For new plantings and re-plantings, carried out on a date subsequent to the coming into force of this protocol, the pruning systems to be adopted shall be limited to Cane and, or Spur pruning, for example "Guyot" or "Cordone Speronato" or mixed pruning methods of the two.

The typologies referred to in heading 1 may bear on their label the following terms and any expression resulting from a translation thereof:

• "Fermented in barriques". These may be used to describe white wines of which a minimum 45% has completely undergone the fermentation process in barriques.

• "Barrel fermented" or "Fermented in oak barrels". These may be used to describe white wines of which a minimum of 60% has completely undergone the fermentation process in barrels.

• "Maceration Carbonique" or "Macerazione Carbonica" or "Carbonic Maceration". These may be used exclusively for red or rosé wines in the case of the maceration of whole grape clusters under an anaerobic atmosphere for a minimum period of 3 days.

• "Late Harvest" or "Vendemmia Tardiva" or "Vendange Tardive". These may be used exclusively for wines, 100% of which is obtained from grapes which have been left on the vine to undergo a partial drying process resulting in the concentration of grape sugars to a minimum natural alcoholic strength of 13.5% vol.

• "Passito". This may be used exclusively for wines 100% of which is obtained from grapes which have undergone a drying process, in whole or in part, on the plant or after harvest, resulting in the concentration of grape sugars to a minimum natural potential alcoholic strength of 18.0% vol. and of which the minimum actual alcoholic strength is 12.0% vol. Notwithstanding the EU regulations on oenological practices, the maximum limit of volatile acidity for the "Passito" typology is of 30 milliequivalents per litre and the maximum limit of total sulphur dioxide for this wine can be up to 300mg per litre if the wine has a sugar content greater than or equal to 45g/l.

• "Imqadded". This may be used exclusively for wines 100% of which is obtained from grapes which have undergone a drying process, in whole or in part, on the plant or after harvest, resulting in the concentration of grape sugars to a minimum natural potential alcoholic strength of 14.0% vol. and of which the minimum actual alcoholic strength is 12.5% vol. Notwithstanding the EU regulations on oenological practices, the maximum limit of volatile acidity for the "Imqadded" typology is of 25 milliequivalents per litre and the maximum limit of total sulphur dioxide for this wine can be up to 300mg per litre if the wine has a sugar content greater than or equal to 45g/l.

7.4 Methods of Vinification and Ageing.

The typologies referred to in heading 1 may bear on their label the following terms and any expression resulting from a translation of them:

• "Saignée". This may be used to describe Rosé wines of which a minimum 75% has been produced by draining must from crushed red grapes within the first 48 hours of the start of the alcoholic fermentation process.

• "Eleve sur lies" or "Aged on lees". These may be used to describe White or Rosé wines that have been left in contact with the yeast lees resulting from the alcoholic fermentation for a minimum period of 4 months from the start of the alcoholic fermentation.

• "Matured in oak Barriques". These may be used to describe red wines of which a minimum of 75% has been stored in barriques for at least 6 months. In the case of white wines this mention may be used to describe white wines of which a minimum 45% has been stored in barriques for at least 8 weeks.

• "Barrel Matured", or "Matured in oak Barrels" or "Oak Aged". These may be used to describe red wines of which a minimum of 85% has been stored in barrels for at least 6 months or red wines of which a minimum 75% has been stored in barriques for at least 6 months. In the case of white wines this mention may be used to describe white wines of which a minimum 50% has been stored in barrels for at least 8 weeks or white wines of which a minimum 45% has been stored in barriques for at least 8 weeks.

7.5 Special Mentions.

Subject to complying with the criteria described hereunder in this item, the typologies listed in heading 1 may bear on their label the following terms:

• "Superior"

This mention may be allowed on the label to describe the wine typologies put up for consumption referred to in heading 1 and defined in heading 8, which have not undergone any form of enrichment and which have:

(a) For the White and Rosé wine typologies, a minimum natural alcoholic content and a minimum total alcoholic content in the wine put up for consumption of 12.0% vol.

(b) For the Red wine typologies, a minimum natural alcoholic content and a minimum total alcoholic content in the wine put up for consumption of 12.5% vol.

• "Riżerva"

This mention may be allowed on the label to describe the wine typologies of products other than sparkling wines put up for consumption referred to in heading 1 and defined in heading 8, which have not undergone any form of enrichment and which, depending on the style of the producer, after a period of ageing in wooden recipients, comply with the following additional criteria:

(A) For wines of the White typologies:

(a) The minimum natural alcoholic content of the wine shall be 12.0% vol.;

(b) The minimum total alcoholic content of the wine put up for consumption shall be 12.0% vol.;

(c) Wines cannot be commercialised before 24 months have elapsed from the 1st of September of the vintage year;

(d) Wines shall be aged for a minimum of 6 months in bottle before commercial release.

(B) For wines of the Red typologies:

(a) The minimum natural alcoholic content of the wine shall be 12.5% vol.;

(b) The minimum total alcoholic content of the wine put up for consumption shall be 12.5% vol.;

(c) Wines cannot be commercialised before 24 months have elapsed from the 1st of September of the vintage year;

(d) Wines shall have a minimum ageing of 12 months in oak barrels;

(e) Wines shall be aged for a minimum of 6 months in bottle before commercial release.

In the case of the mention "Riżerva" it is obligatory to also specify the vintage year on the label.

7.5.1 When applicable under the criteria provided in item 7.5, the special mentions "Superior" or "Riżerva" shall be positioned on the label directly below the D.O.K. mention as described in heading 9.

7.5.2 When applicable under the criteria given provided in item 7.5, the special mention "Riżerva" may be replaced by "Reserve".

7.6 Vineyards or Estate mention.

7.6.1 Vineyards mention.

The use of additional topographic indications which make reference to the vineyards from which 100% of the grapes used to make the wine originate is allowed on condition that:

• All the vineyard parcels are located within the topographical indication used;

• The topographic indication shall refer to an area which is no larger than any of the local council boundaries as indicated in Annex I;

• The vineyards shall be registered to the list of vineyards in such a way that it can be identified separately under the topographical name used;

• The grapes harvested from the named vineyards are subject to a specific annual production declaration and vinified separately;

• The whole extent of the vineyard parcels making up the named Vineyards mention have a productive area of not less than 1 hectare of grapes of the same typology;

• The grapes and the relative wines produced from the named vineyards are registered separately in the obligatory winery registers.

7.6.2 Estate mention.

The use of the mention of an Estate name which makes reference to the vineyards from which 100% of the grapes used to make the wine originate is allowed on condition that:

• The whole extent of the vineyard parcels making up the named estate have a productive area of not less than 1 hectare of grapes of the same typology;

• All the vineyard parcels making up the named estate shall be within a distance not exceeding one hundred meters, inclusive of pathways and, or public roads;

• The vineyard parcels making up the named estate must be under the full control of the wine making entity through direct ownership or a long term lease and evidence of the above must be provided to the regulating body;

• The vineyard parcels making up the named estate shall be registered by the wine producing entity to the list of vineyards in such a way that it can be identified separately under the estate name used;

• The grapes harvested from the named estate are subject to a specific annual harvest declaration and vinified separately;

• The grapes and the relative wines produced from the named estate are registered separately in the obligatory winery registers.

7.6.3 The optional additional mention "Single Vineyard" shall be reserved for quality wines that satisfy all the conditions for an Estate mention or a Vineyards mention, described in items 7.6.1 and 7.6.2, only where the vineyard parcels making up the Estate or named Vineyards are contiguous to each other and may only be separated by pathways and/or public roads.

7.7 Oenological practices and processes.

For the elaboration of wines eligible for the mention of "Late Harvest" or "Vendemmia Tardiva" or "Vendange Tardive" or "Imqadded" or "Passito", as described in item 7.3, any form of enrichment of the must or wine shall be prohibited except for the eventual possible addition, in the case of wines eligible for the mentions "Imqadded" and "Passito", even after the 30th November of each year, of grapes of the same harvest with a maximum sugar concentration of 60%.

Also for the elaboration of wines eligible for the mention of "Superior" and "Riżerva", as described in item 7.5, any form of enrichment shall be strictly prohibited.
